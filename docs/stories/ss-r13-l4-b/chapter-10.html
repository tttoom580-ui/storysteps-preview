包裝間的粉塵

Dust in the Packing Room

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The warehouse visit began with an apology from the assistant manager. He admitted that a bargain transport contract had led the company to use an old packing room beside a grain store. A maintenance worker warned that fine dust in the air could become critical near an open flame. A careless visitor called it a mere inconvenience. Then a small motor made a sharp sound and stopped; no explosion occurred, but everyone understood the risk. Yingrong insisted that the room remain closed until an electrician inspected it. The manager congratulated her on being cautious, then tried to change the subject to sales. She replied that safety was not a favor and could not be exchanged for a better price. A proper resolution would need written repairs. The worker used a bandage for a small cut, but the inspector said that personal care could not replace safer machinery. The supervisor agreed to forbid open flames and to install a protected switch before work resumed.

倉庫訪查以副經理的道歉開始。他承認公司為了便宜的運輸合約，把包裝間設在舊糧倉旁。維修工人警告，空氣中的細粉塵若碰到明火，可能變得非常危險。粗心訪客把它當成微不足道的小麻煩。接著一臺小馬達發出尖響並停止；沒有爆炸，但每個人都理解了風險。映蓉堅持在電工檢查前先關閉房間。經理恭喜她謹慎，接著想把話題轉向銷售。她回答，安全不是人情，也不能換成更好的價格。適當的解決方式需要書面修繕紀錄。 工人用繃帶包住小割傷，但檢查員說個人處理不能取代更安全的機器。主管同意禁止明火，並在復工前安裝有防護的開關。

The packing records showed a long chain of changes. A settler family had once owned the old storehouse, and its stone walls were older than the town's modern roads. A photograph of an ancient tomb found nearby hung in the corridor, part of the district's local history exhibition. None of this history proved the quality of a seed. Yingrong asked the clerk to illustrate the movement of the seventh lot on a simple map: arrival, drying, repacking, and shipment. The clerk marked two dates that did not match the labels. Fang Dingchuan looked suspiciously at the list, then admitted he had never been shown that page. He refused to accuse his manager before speaking with him. Yingrong accepted that limit and asked only for a signed copy. A museum label called the warehouse part of the town's trading civilization, but Yingrong knew history could not excuse today's hazards. Fang gave the manager a suspicious look when he saw that the newer date had been written over an older mark.

包裝紀錄顯示一連串改動。早年一個移居家庭擁有這座舊倉，石牆的年代比鎮上的現代道路還久。走廊掛著附近古墓的照片，是地方史展覽的一部分。這些歷史都不能證明種子品質。映蓉請職員用簡單地圖標出第七批貨的移動：入庫、烘乾、重新包裝與出貨。職員圈出兩個與標籤不符的日期。方定川狐疑地看著名單，接著承認從未看過這一頁。他拒絕在和經理談話前指控上司。映蓉接受這個界線，只要求取得簽名影本。 博物館標籤稱倉庫是小鎮商業文明的一部分，但映蓉知道歷史不能替今天的危險開脫。方定川看到新日期蓋過舊記號時，狐疑地看了經理一眼。

A pregnant warehouse worker approached Yingrong while the manager was away. She had complained that masks and breaks were inadequate, but had been forbidden to speak to inspectors without a supervisor present. She did not want her name published. Yingrong gave her a telephone number for a local labor adviser and asked whether she needed help arranging a safe conversation. The woman agreed, on the condition that the shop not speak for her. Yingrong remembered how a surgeon had once explained her father's operation without asking him what he wanted. Her father had been too ill to object, and the memory still troubled her. Now she asked the worker what choice would help her most and wrote no name on the public sheet.

經理離開時，一名懷孕的倉庫女工走近映蓉。她曾抱怨口罩和休息時間不足，卻被禁止在主管不在場時向檢查員說話。她不希望自己的名字被公開。映蓉給她地方勞動顧問的電話，問是否需要協助安排安全談話。女工同意，但條件是種子行不可替她發言。映蓉想起父親過去開刀時，一位外科醫師曾向家屬解釋手術，卻沒先問父親想要什麼。父親當時病得無法反對，那段記憶至今仍困擾她。這次映蓉先問女工什麼選擇對她最有幫助，沒有在公開表格上寫下名字。

At the town library, Xiao-an asked whether a website could hold copies of the test results. The librarian showed him a slow computer connected through a telephone line. It could display pages from an agricultural research center, but many farmers had no computer at home. They decided that the website might be a useful extra reference, not the only place to publish essential safety information. A paper bulletin, a telephone message, and the seed shop counter would remain necessary. Xiao-an printed a page describing safe grain-storage practices. The librarian warned that online information needed checking just like any printed leaflet. A convincing design could carry an old mistake as easily as a new fact.

在鎮立圖書館，小安問能不能把測試結果放到網站。圖書館員給他看一臺透過電話線連線、速度很慢的電腦。它能顯示農業研究中心的網頁，但許多農民家裡沒有電腦。她們決定，網站可作額外參考，不可成為公布重要安全資訊的唯一管道。紙本公告、電話訊息與種子行櫃檯仍然必要。小安印下一頁安全穀物儲存方法。圖書館員提醒，網路資訊和印刷傳單一樣都要核對。漂亮設計可以傳遞新事實，也可以傳遞舊錯誤。

On returning home, Yingrong drafted two letters. The first asked Golden Field to preserve the seventh lot's packing records and inspect the dangerous motor. The second explained to customers why the shop had halted sales but would continue accepting claims. Mother read both and crossed out a line that suggested the firm had deliberately deceived farmers. There was evidence of mislabeling, not yet of intent. Yingrong thanked her for the correction. Their aim was neither a revolution nor a bargain at any price. It was a way to restore trust without pretending damage had not occurred. When they locked the door, Xiao-an placed the red safety notice above the trays. The work of finding out what happened would continue, but no one needed to stand in a dusty room to prove it. A prosperous business, she thought, should be able to show how it keeps its workers safe.

回家後，映蓉草擬兩封信。第一封要求金田保存第七批貨的包裝紀錄，並檢查危險馬達。第二封向顧客解釋為何停售，卻仍接受申訴。母親看完兩封信，劃掉一句暗示公司故意欺騙農民的話。目前有錯標證據，還沒有主觀故意的證據。映蓉謝謝她修正。她們的目標既不是革命，也不是不計代價的交易，而是不假裝損失不存在、仍能重建信任的方法。鎖門時，小安把紅色安全公告貼在淺盤上方。追查還會繼續，但沒有人需要站在滿是粉塵的房間裡才能證明它。 她心想，真正繁榮的企業應能說清楚如何維護工人安全。
