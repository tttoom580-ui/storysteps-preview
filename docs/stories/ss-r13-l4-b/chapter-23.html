公開田間示範

The Open Field Demonstration

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 23 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The district hosted a public demonstration at Fengzhi's farm when the spring trials reached their first reliable stage. Volunteers helped assemble signs and carry small tables, while Yingrong checked that no one would trample the rows. A biology teacher explained the growth of roots and leaves without suggesting that every green plant would become a profitable crop. The financial officer showed the cost of seed, labor, water, and transport separately. The numbers frustrated some visitors, who had expected a simple success story. Fengzhi said she was grateful for the explanation precisely because it included loss. Her first field had failed, but the new rows gave her enough evidence to plan the next planting with more confidence. She spoke for herself, without a merchant writing her words.

春季試種達到第一個可靠階段時，區公所在鳳枝的農田舉辦公開示範。志工幫忙組裝招牌、搬小桌，映蓉確認沒有人踩到試種行。生物老師解釋根和葉的生長，沒有暗示每棵綠苗都會變成有利可圖的作物。財務人員分別列出種子、人工、用水和運輸成本。部分來客原本期待簡單的成功故事，看到數字有些挫折。鳳枝說，她正因解釋包含損失而感激。第一塊田雖失敗，新試種行卻提供足夠證據，讓她更有把握規劃下次播種。她親自發言，沒有商人替她寫稿。

A shipment of fresh cargo arrived with samples from two nearby farms. Yingrong asked the farmers to distribute them only after the labels had been checked and consent for the public demonstration confirmed. One young guardian had brought a child who asked about a mysterious plum tree near the field. Pan said he could describe the tree's likely variety but could not name it with certainty from a single fruit. The child laughed and asked whether even teachers were allowed not to know. 'Especially teachers,' Pan answered. The visitors seemed to appreciate that reply. A psychologist attending with the school explained that people often trusted confident voices too quickly when they felt afraid. Careful uncertainty could be a form of respect, not weakness.

一批新貨運抵現場，包含附近兩家農場的樣本。映蓉請農民等標籤核對、公開展示取得同意後再分發。一名年輕監護人帶來的孩子問田旁神祕李樹是什麼品種。潘維說，他可以描述可能品種，卻不能只看一顆果實就確定名字。孩子笑問，連老師也可以說不知道嗎？「尤其是老師。」潘維回答。來客似乎很欣賞這句話。隨學校來的一位心理學家解釋，人害怕時常太快信任有自信的聲音。謹慎地承認不確定，有時是尊重，不是軟弱。

The company representative presented a correction notice acknowledging that the seventh lot had been exposed to moisture during repacking. It described failures in labeling and storage, along with a schedule for handling compensation. It did not accuse individual warehouse workers of deliberate destruction. Yingrong read the notice aloud, then invited questions. One visitor demanded equal compensation regardless of documented loss. Fengzhi answered that equality meant fair access to the same review process, not pretending every field had the same damage. The explanation was tolerable to some and disappointing to others. Yingrong asked the officer to repeat the appeals procedure and post it on the district board. A public correction mattered only if people could use it after the meeting ended.

公司代表公布更正公告，承認第七批貨在重新包裝時受潮。公告描述了標籤與保存失誤，並列出處理賠償的時間表，沒有指控個別倉庫工人故意破壞。映蓉朗讀公告，接著開放提問。一名來客要求不論損失證據，每人都拿相同賠償。鳳枝回答，平等意味每人都能進入同樣公平的審查程序，而不是假裝每片田的損害一樣。有些人接受這個說明，有些人失望。映蓉請公所人員再說明申訴程序，張貼到區公告欄。公開更正只有在會後仍可被使用時才真正有意義。

In the afternoon, Mother led her first paid lesson under a canvas shade. She taught participants how to judge whether a paper packet had become too damp and when to seek a professional test. She did not pretend to know every variety. A retired volunteer wrote the steps on a board while Xiao-an photographed the labeled examples. Yingrong watched from the back and felt appreciation for Mother's long experience, now shared without exhausting her. Mother stopped after the agreed hour, even though more people had questions. She referred them to the shop's next scheduled session. The boundary surprised several neighbors. It delighted Yingrong, who knew the older woman had spent decades answering every knock on the door as if rest were a violation of duty. She warned that alcohol used for cleaning tools must be stored safely away from children. Mother chose to devote only the agreed hour to teaching and to leave the remaining questions for next month.

下午，母親在帆布遮蔭下教她第一堂有薪課。她教學員判斷紙袋是否受潮過度、何時應尋求專業測試，沒有假裝認識每種品種。退休志工把步驟寫上板子，小安拍下有標籤的例子。映蓉在後方看著，對母親長久經驗充滿感謝，如今這份經驗能被分享而不耗盡她。母親在約定的一小時後準時停止，雖然還有人想問。她請大家等下一堂預定課。這個界線令幾位鄰居驚訝，映蓉卻很開心。母親幾十年來總把每次敲門當成不能拒絕的責任。 她提醒，用來清潔工具的酒精必須安全存放、遠離兒童。母親選擇只投入約定的一小時教學，把其餘問題留待下個月。

When the crowd left, the field rows remained uneven. Some plants were strong, others small, and a few places were empty. Yingrong gathered the records and took a final photograph of the site before anyone removed the signs. She knew the farm would not become profitable through one demonstration. The shop had lost money during the closures, the company still owed payments, and the old property sale was unfinished. Yet the evidence was stronger, the labor arrangements fairer, and the next planting less uncertain. Fengzhi walked beside her to the gate and said the seventh packet had not given her a miracle. It had given her a question she could finally answer. Yingrong thought that was enough for one afternoon.

人群散去後，田行仍長得參差不齊。有些植物強壯，有些矮小，還有幾處空著。映蓉收起紀錄，在大家拆招牌前拍下場地的最後一張照片。她知道，一場示範不會讓農場突然獲利。停業使店損失金錢，公司仍欠賠款，舊產權出售也未完成。但證據更強，勞動安排更公平，下次播種也少一點不確定。鳳枝陪她走到田門口，說第七號紙袋沒有帶來奇蹟，卻帶來一個終於能回答的問題。映蓉覺得，對這個下午而言，已經足夠。
