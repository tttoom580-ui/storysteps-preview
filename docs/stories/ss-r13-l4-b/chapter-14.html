母親的借款

The Mother's Loan

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 14 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mother's old loan came due in November. Yingrong had known there was a debt, but not that her brother had signed as a second guarantor. The bank officer played an audio recording of a previous meeting after Mother asked to hear it. The recording was poor, yet it showed she had understood the payment schedule. Yingrong did not criticize her for borrowing money to repair the roof. The bank's manner was harsh, but the figures were not mysterious: their income could not cover the remaining payments without changes. Mother said she deserved a retirement that did not depend on asking her daughter for money. Yingrong agreed and asked for a written statement of all options, including a smaller repayment plan. They could not conquer the debt by refusing to read it. The bank officer asked the family to verify each input in the repayment table before using the total.

母親的舊貸款在十一月到期。映蓉知道有債務，卻不知道兄弟曾擔任第二保證人。母親要求重聽以前的會議，銀行職員播放錄音。音質不好，但能證明她當時理解付款期程。映蓉沒有責怪她借錢修屋頂。銀行態度嚴厲，數字卻不神祕：若不改變現況，收入無法支付剩餘款項。母親說，她值得一個不必一直向女兒要錢的退休生活。映蓉同意，請銀行提供包含縮小還款額的所有選項書面資料。她們不能靠拒絕讀債務，就把它征服。 銀行職員請家人使用總額之前，先確認還款表的每一筆輸入資料。

The brother, Jianping, arrived on the afternoon train. He was angry that his name had not appeared on the public shop records even though his savings were at risk. Yingrong admitted she should have asked sooner. He brought a proposal to sell the storage building, frame the remaining shop as a fashionable gift store, and use the proceeds to settle the loan. His plan was cunning in a practical way: it would cut costs quickly but also remove the dry space needed for seed testing. They argued without shouting. Mother said both children had tried to help her in different ways, and neither should speak as if she had disappeared from the room. She was the borrower, the owner, and the person who would decide whether to retire.

兄弟建平搭下午火車來。他生氣自己的存款面臨風險，名字卻沒出現在店裡的公開紀錄。映蓉承認應該更早問他。他帶來一個提案：賣掉儲物建築，把剩餘店面改成時髦禮品店，用所得償還貸款。他的計畫實際又精明，能快速減少成本，卻也會失去種子檢驗需要的乾燥空間。兄妹爭論，但沒有咆哮。母親說，兩個孩子用不同方式幫她，卻都不該當她已經不在房間裡。她是借款人、店主，也是有權決定是否退休的人。

Pan accompanied them to the town archive, where a thin book of agricultural literature described a peculiar storage method used by local growers. An elderly woman demonstrated how jars had been sealed with clay and pickled leaves to keep insects away. She warned that the method was not suitable for every seed. Jianping found the encounter more interesting than he expected. He asked whether the old building could serve another purpose without becoming a public museum. The archivist suggested renting only its dry upper room to the seed shop while selling the ground floor to a craft worker. This would still require a legal survey and a willing buyer. Yingrong was delighted by a third possibility, but she kept the idea in her notebook until the figures were checked.

潘維陪他們到鎮上檔案室，一本薄薄的農業文獻描述本地農戶使用的特殊保存方法。一位長者示範如何用黏土封罐，以及以醃漬葉片防蟲。她提醒這方法不適合每種種子。建平對這次相遇比預期更感興趣。他問老建物是否能有其他用途，不一定變成公共博物館。檔案員建議只把乾燥的樓上租給種子行，樓下則賣給工藝工作者。這仍需要合法測量和願意購買的人。映蓉很高興出現第三種可能，但在數字核對前只把它記進筆記。

That evening Mother told Yingrong about the surgery she had undergone years before. During recovery she had feared being useless, and keeping the shop open had become her way of proving she still mattered. The confession made Yingrong sympathetic, but it did not persuade her that Mother should continue lifting sacks. 'Retirement is not a punishment,' she said. 'It may be the first time your work belongs to you.' Mother looked away and asked whether Yingrong would be happier if the seed shop closed. Yingrong answered that she wanted the freedom to choose, not the virtue of being a dutiful daughter at any cost. They made tea together. Neither woman could solve the other's future, and both were beginning to understand that truth. Mother said she would not describe her years at the counter as torture, but she wanted permission to feel tired without apologizing.

那晚，母親談起多年前接受的手術。復原期間她害怕自己變得無用，繼續開店便成了證明自己仍有價值的方式。這段告白讓映蓉很同情她，但不代表母親應繼續搬麻袋。「退休不是處罰。」她說。「也許那是妳第一次能真正替自己使用時間。」母親別開眼，問映蓉如果店關了是否更快樂。映蓉回答，她想保有選擇自由，不是想不計代價扮演孝順女兒。兩人一起泡茶。沒有誰能替對方解決未來，但她們都逐漸明白這件事。 母親說，她不會把多年站櫃檯形容成折磨，卻希望自己疲倦時不必道歉。

Before sleeping, Yingrong reviewed the bank's figures and the possible building plan. A handwritten note from her father lay inside a drawer, beside a small drawing he had made of a seed pod. He had described the work as a delight, yet he had never written that the shop must last forever. Yingrong placed the note back where she found it. Tomorrow they would identify the survey costs, the buyer's nationality requirements if any, and what part of the property could legally be divided. She would not turn a complicated family decision into a tale of cruel children and a saintly mother. The facts were more ordinary: people who loved one another had made different promises at different times, and now had to decide which promises they could still keep.

睡前，映蓉重新看銀行數字和可能的建物方案。抽屜裡有父親留下的手寫便條，旁邊畫著一顆種莢。他曾說這份工作令人快樂，卻從沒寫下店必須永遠存在。映蓉把便條放回原處。明天她們要確認測量費、買方是否有國籍方面的限制，以及產權哪些部分依法能分割。她不想把複雜家事寫成殘忍子女和聖人母親的故事。事實更平凡：相愛的人在不同時候許下不同承諾，如今必須決定哪些承諾仍做得到。
