A Carton with Two Codes

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The warehouse manager sent a letter admitting that two shipments had been given the same printed code. Golden Field had begun to expand its business too quickly after a change in management, and its dominant distributor insisted that old sacks be reused. It was not yet clear whether damaged seeds were mixed with good ones. Yingrong studied the letter beside her dusty ledger and considered the consequence: every farmer who had trusted that code might now be uncertain about a planting decision. Pan explained that seeds of the same breed could still respond differently to storage. A few years earlier, a local paper had praised the company's eventual growth as evidence of modern capitalism. Now the story was less grand. The company had saved paper but lost track of what the paper meant.

倉庫經理寄信承認，兩批貨被印上相同批號。金田在更換管理層後擴張得太快，主導物流的經銷商堅持重用舊袋。是否有受損種子混入好貨，仍不清楚。映蓉把信放在積灰的帳簿旁研究，想著後果：每位相信那個批號的農民，如今都可能對播種決定失去把握。潘維解釋，同一品種的種子也會因保存條件不同而有不同反應。幾年前，地方報紙曾稱讚公司最終成長是現代資本主義的證明。現在故事沒那麼宏偉了。公司省下紙張，卻弄丟紙張代表的意義。

A worker named He Meilan arrived with a notebook of repacking dates. She had copied them during her breaks because the original schedule kept changing. There was no dramatic evidence of an invasion or a criminal plan, she said. She only knew that the instructions had been revised after the rain. Yingrong asked permission before making a copy; the worker might need the notebook for her job. Meilan wanted the records to circulate among the farmers, but not her home address. They agreed to remove personal details and mark the document as the worker's account rather than an official report. A small indication on page three showed that the seventh lot had stood near a leaking wall for two nights. That was an important lead, not a complete explanation.

一名叫何美蘭的工人帶著重新包裝日期的筆記來了。她在休息時間抄錄，因為原來班表一直改動。她說，這裡沒有入侵陰謀或犯罪計畫的戲劇性證據；她只知道下雨之後指示被修改。映蓉取得同意才影印筆記，因為女工工作上也可能需要。美蘭希望紀錄能在農民間流通，但不要公開自家地址。她們同意刪去私人資料，並標明這是員工記述，不是正式報告。第三頁一個小記號顯示，第七批貨曾在漏水牆旁放了兩夜。那是重要線索，還不是完整解釋。

Fang Dingchuan came to negotiate a temporary settlement. He offered replacement packets and asked Yingrong to stop displaying the disputed date until the company could investigate. Mother liked the offer; farmers needed seed more urgently than arguments. Yingrong said persuasion could not replace the evidence already collected. They could accept safe replacements without agreeing to conceal a known error. Fang was silent for a long time. Then he said his manager considered anyone questioning the label system a rebel against the firm. 'A company is not an army,' Yingrong replied. 'People can question a procedure without wishing for its defeat.' They agreed to submit the lot record to the district laboratory and discuss refunds separately. No one signed a waiver that day.

方定川來協商暫時和解。他提出替換紙袋，請映蓉在公司調查前停止展示有爭議的日期。母親喜歡這提案；農民比起爭辯，更急著拿到種子。映蓉說，說服不能取代已收集的證據。她們可以接受安全替代品，卻不能因此答應掩蓋已知錯誤。方定川沉默很久，才說經理把質疑標籤制度的人都當成公司的叛徒。「公司不是軍隊。」映蓉回答。「人可以質疑程序，卻不必希望公司失敗。」她們同意把批號紀錄送區域檢驗室，退款則另外討論。那天沒人簽下放棄權利的文件。

Pan visited with a retired engineer who had once helped install a solar pump. The man compared the warehouse records to an old telegraph message: each part had to arrive in order, or the meaning changed. Xiao-an laughed when the engineer's coat brushed his neck and gave him an accidental tickle. The old man pretended to be offended, then showed how he had restored a wrecked pump after a flood. Yingrong did not need the pump for her trial, but the story reminded her to inspect a whole chain, not a single broken object. She asked the engineer to examine the warehouse's ventilation plan as a favor to the workers, without entering the unsafe building. The problems of dust, moisture, and reporting were connected even if different people had first noticed them.

潘維帶來一位退休工程師，他過去曾協助安裝太陽能水泵。老人把倉庫紀錄比成舊式電報：每個部分都要依序到達，意思才不會改變。他的外套擦過小安脖子，讓小安癢得笑出來。老人假裝被冒犯，接著說明自己如何在洪水後修好毀損的水泵。映蓉的試驗不需要那臺泵，但這故事提醒她，要檢查整條流程，不只看一件壞掉的物品。她請工程師不進危險建物，只從圖面協助工人檢視通風。粉塵、濕度與通報看似不同，實際上互相連結。

Before closing, Yingrong placed the manager's letter, Meilan's account, and the laboratory request in separate folders. Mother watched the arrangement and said the process looked like a marathon, not a quick race to sell seed. Yingrong agreed. The burden would be long, and the eventual answer might disappoint everyone. Still, the shop had something it had lacked at the start: a common record that customers could examine without depending on the loudest speaker. Outside, small shoots had appeared in the first of Fengzhi's field rows. Their shapes were graceful and uncertain. Yingrong resisted the desire to call them proof of success. She wrote the date and weather beside each one and left the next line empty for tomorrow.

關門前，映蓉把經理的信、美蘭的記述和檢驗申請分開裝進文件夾。母親看著這個動作，說整件事像馬拉松，不像趕著賣種子的短跑。映蓉同意。負擔會持續很久，最後答案也可能讓所有人失望。至少店裡擁有開始時缺少的東西：顧客能親自檢查、不必依賴最大聲者的共同紀錄。門外，鳳枝第一批田間試驗行裡出現小芽，姿態優雅，結果仍難以預料。映蓉忍住稱它們為成功證據的衝動，只在每株旁記下日期與天氣，把明天的一行留白。
