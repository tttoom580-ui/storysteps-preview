A Claim for the Damaged Lot

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 22 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Golden Field invited customers to a meeting about compensation. An economist from the county explained why a cheap packet might become costly if a whole field failed. She used a simple curve to show how transport costs rose when each farmer ordered only a few bags. Her advice was constructive and economical: share delivery routes where possible, but do not force anyone to buy an unsafe lot. A merchant in the audience said the idea had merit. Another asked whether the company would cease selling the disputed seed. Fang Dingchuan answered that sales had already stopped pending verification. Yingrong was pleased by the clear statement. It did not solve the past damage, but it prevented the firm from treating a known concern as merely a public-relations problem.

金田邀請顧客參加補償會議。一名縣府經濟學家解釋，一包看似便宜的種子若讓整片田失敗，代價可能非常高。她用簡單曲線顯示，每位農民只訂幾包時運費如何上升。她給出具建設性且經濟實惠的建議：能合併的配送路線就合併，但不能強迫任何人購買不安全批次。一名商人說這有道理，另一人問公司是否會停止販售爭議種子。方定川回答，在查核完成前早已停售。映蓉很欣慰聽到清楚說明。它不能解決過去損失，至少避免公司把已知疑慮只當公關問題。

A lawyer explained the enforcement procedure for a written settlement. Farmers could accept payment without giving up unrelated rights, but they had to read the terms carefully. One man threatened to sue everyone present if his claim was not paid by sunset. Fengzhi asked him to take a moderate position and let the adviser explain the deadlines first. The room became calmer. Yingrong showed the results of her shop's tests and the exact dates of each report. She was not associated with the insurer, and she said so openly. An officer from the district added that the final compensation amount depended on documented losses, not public anger. The meeting was slow because people needed answers that could survive after the shouting stopped.

律師解釋書面和解的執行程序。農民可以接受付款而不放棄無關權利，但必須仔細讀條款。一名男子威脅若日落前不獲賠，就要告在場所有人。鳳枝請他採取比較溫和的立場，先讓顧問說明期限。房間安靜些。映蓉展示店裡的試驗結果和每份報告的確切日期。她不是保險方的關係人，也清楚說明這點。區公所人員補充，最終補償額取決於文件化的損失，不是公眾怒氣。會議很慢，因為大家需要的是吵鬧結束後仍站得住的答案。

During the break, a child at the window pointed to a cat stretching its claw toward a potted plant. The plant had been damaged by a pest, and Xiao-an showed the child how to inspect leaves without tearing them. The child's guardian, carrying an infant, asked whether pesticides were necessary. Yingrong explained that the right answer depended on the pest, the crop, and the risks of any treatment. She would not recommend chemicals based on a glimpse through glass. The guardian seemed relieved by the refusal to guess. Xiao-an offered a kettle of warm water so the family could rest before the long journey home. It was a delightful pause in an uncomfortable day. Nobody needed to be convinced that every problem had an instant solution. At another table, a farmer used a hand mill to grind dry grain and demonstrate how storage affected its texture.

休息時，一名孩子指著窗邊的貓，牠正伸出爪子碰盆栽。植物受了害蟲侵害，小安示範如何不撕裂葉片就能檢查。抱著嬰兒的監護人問是否需要農藥。映蓉解釋，要看害蟲、作物和治療風險，不能只隔著玻璃瞥一眼就推薦化學藥品。監護人似乎因她拒絕猜測而鬆口氣。小安端來一壺溫水，讓家人長途返家前休息。這是在不舒服的一天裡令人愉快的短暫停頓。沒有人需要相信每個問題都能瞬間解決。 另一桌上，農民用手磨磨碎乾穀，示範保存方式如何影響質地。

The meeting resumed with a discussion of the farmers who had no receipts. One elderly man explained that his parents had died when he was young and he had grown up as an orphan, learning trade by working for neighbors. He rarely kept paperwork. Yingrong suggested checking delivery records and independent witnesses instead of dismissing his case immediately. The lawyer said such evidence could be considered, though it might take longer to verify. A company clerk demonstrated an invention for numbering new sacks automatically. Yingrong asked whether its numbers could be traced to the original batch, not merely stamped on the surface. The clerk said they would test that connection. A new device was useful only if it solved the weakness of the old process.

會議繼續討論沒有收據的農民。一名老人說自己幼年喪父母，作為孤兒在鄰居家幫工學會交易，很少保留紙張。映蓉建議先查配送紀錄和獨立證人，不要立即駁回。律師說可以考慮這些證據，但核對可能比較久。一名公司職員展示會自動在新袋上編碼的發明。映蓉問，號碼是否能追溯原始批次，而不只是印在表面。職員答應測試這條連結。新裝置只有真正解決舊流程弱點，才算有用。

By evening, the parties had agreed on a schedule for reviewing claims, not on a single amount for everyone. Fang Dingchuan thanked Yingrong for keeping the evidence organized even when his company disliked it. She answered that the same standard should apply to her own shop. If she ever mislabeled a packet, customers deserved to know. The economist smiled and called that an economical form of trust: it cost effort before trouble, but far less than a lost season afterward. Mother walked beside Yingrong on the way home, using her cane when the street turned steep. She did not ask to return to full-time work. Instead she asked whether the teaching room had enough chairs for her first class next month.

傍晚，各方同意了申訴審查時間表，沒有硬定每人相同的補償額。方定川感謝映蓉整理證據，即使公司並不喜歡那些內容。她回答，同一標準也應適用自家店；若自己錯標，顧客同樣有知情權。經濟學家微笑，稱那是成本較低的信任：事前要多費心，但遠比事後失去一季收成便宜。回家路上，母親在陡坡處拄著手杖走在映蓉旁邊。她沒有要求回來全職工作，而是問下個月授課的房間是否有足夠椅子。
