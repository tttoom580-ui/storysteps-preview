溫室圖

The Greenhouse Diagram

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Friday, Yingrong accompanied Pan to a small greenhouse behind the district school. The annual garden attraction had recently drawn visitors from nearby towns, but this week its door stood open for an experimental seed test. A mechanic named Lu had repaired the fan after the motor overheated. He showed Yingrong a diagram of the airflow and an old maintenance manual. The fan could strengthen circulation without drying the trays if they adjusted the vents carefully. Yingrong drew a draft of the setup and marked every place where temperatures might differ. She was hesitant to call the result essential evidence until the equipment held steady for several hours. Pan agreed that a machine did not become reliable just because a trained person had touched it. Her hesitation came from the risk of drawing conclusions from an unstable fan.

星期五，映蓉陪潘維到區立學校後方的小溫室。每年的園藝景點最近吸引了附近城鎮的訪客，但這週敞開大門供實驗種子檢驗使用。一位姓陸的技師修好了過熱的風扇馬達。他拿氣流圖和舊維修手冊給映蓉看。只要小心調整通風口，風扇就能加強循環，又不至於吹乾淺盤。映蓉畫了一份配置草圖，標出各處可能出現溫差的位置。她不願在設備穩定運轉數小時前，就稱結果是關鍵證據。潘維同意，一台機器不會只因受過訓練的人碰過就自動可靠。 她的猶豫來自風扇尚未穩定，貿然下結論可能有風險。

An agriculture official arrived with a visitor who introduced herself as a former diplomat working on seed exchange between regions. She greeted the staff in several languages, then asked permission before photographing any student's work. The visitor explained that a magnificent landscape in a brochure could hide exhausted soil. Seeds moved across borders through many routes, and each new environment mattered. She pointed to a satellite image of the river valley, where the bare slopes looked different from fields shaded by trees. Yingrong did not pretend the image could show individual seeds. It could help them choose sites for later field tests. When a child asked whether a germ could destroy their trays, the official explained how clean tools reduced contamination. That warning belonged beside the temperature readings, not beneath a picture of healthy crops.

一位農業官員帶著訪客來到現場。訪客介紹自己曾任外交官，如今協助各地交換種子。她用幾種語言向工作人員問候，拍學生作品前也先徵得同意。她解釋，宣傳冊裡壯麗的地景也可能掩蓋疲乏的土壤。種子沿著許多路線跨越邊界，每個新環境都會影響結果。她指向一張河谷衛星影像，裸露山坡和樹蔭下的田地顯得不同。映蓉不假裝影像能看到個別種子，但它可以幫忙挑選日後田間試驗的地點。孩子問病菌是否會毀掉淺盤，官員便解釋清潔工具如何降低污染。這個警告應和溫度紀錄放在一起，而不是藏在健康作物照片後面。

Around noon, a sudden wind sent loose plastic covers lifting from the greenhouse roof. Children began to flee toward the gate, but Lu asked them to stop at a safe point while he switched off the fan. One cover was caught on a beam. No one climbed up to retrieve it in the wind. Yingrong helped loosen the cord on a lower frame and moved the exposed trays to a dry bench. An adult who had come for recreation offered to carry the notebooks. Xiao-an thanked him but insisted on checking each label before moving it. When the wind passed, Pan found that two trays had changed position. They wrote down the interruption rather than pretending the trial had remained perfect. An outstanding result would mean little if they forgot how it had been obtained.

中午前後，一陣強風把溫室屋頂鬆動的塑膠布吹了起來。孩子們開始往大門逃跑，陸技師請他們先到安全位置停下，自己關掉風扇。一片塑膠布卡在樑上。沒有人冒著大風爬上去取它。映蓉鬆開下方支架的繩子，把暴露在外的淺盤移到乾燥工作檯。一位來休閒參觀的成年人主動幫忙搬筆記。小安道謝，但堅持移動前先核對每個標籤。風停後，潘維發現兩盤的位置改變了。他們把中斷情形記下，而不是假裝試驗一直完美。即使得到傑出的結果，若忘了它如何取得，也沒有太大意義。

The former diplomat listened to Yingrong describe the seventh packet without promising to find a buyer. She suggested one question: who had printed the labels at the wholesale depot? That detail seemed dull beside pictures of unusual crops, yet the labels were the only objects shared by all the affected farmers. Yingrong telephoned the depot. A clerk said their records were stored in boxes and would require a written request. She drafted a short letter naming the lots and explaining why the information was needed. Mother had always resolved such matters by speaking directly to someone she knew. Yingrong did not reject that experience; she asked Mother to review the letter for the correct contact name. They could use both a personal connection and a traceable record.

前外交官聽映蓉講述第七號紙袋，沒有承諾幫她找買家。她建議先問一個問題：批發倉的標籤是誰印的？和奇特作物照片相比，這細節很無趣，但標籤是所有受影響農民共同接觸的物品。映蓉打電話到批發倉。職員說紀錄收在箱子裡，必須書面提出申請。她草擬了一封短信，列出批號並說明為何需要資料。母親向來靠直接找熟人解決這些事。映蓉沒有否定這種經驗，而是請母親核對信裡聯絡人的名字。她們可以同時利用人脈與可追查的文字紀錄。

By sunset, the new trays showed uneven but visible roots. Pan resisted the temptation to celebrate. He placed two clean markers beside the strongest samples and asked Xiao-an to copy the humidity readings for the day's final hour. Yingrong drew a second diagram showing which benches had been moved during the wind. She would need that correction before making comparisons. A school child offered a brightly colored greeting card with pictures of green fields. It showed the horizon beneath a clear sky, nothing like the wet valley outside. Yingrong pinned the card near the door anyway. It was a picture of hope, not a prediction. Her practical plan remained harder: follow the labels, repeat the test, and say exactly what the evidence could support.

日落時，新淺盤長出參差不齊但看得見的根。潘維忍住慶祝的衝動，在生長最好的樣本旁放了兩個乾淨標記，請小安抄下最後一小時的濕度讀數。映蓉畫了第二張圖，標示強風期間哪些工作檯曾移動。她在做比較前必須先修正這項資訊。一名學生送她一張色彩鮮豔的問候卡，上面畫著綠色田野。清澈天空下的地平線和外面潮濕的河谷完全不同。映蓉仍把卡片釘在門邊。那是希望的圖像，不是預測。她實際的計畫仍然困難：追查標籤、重複試驗，以及清楚說出證據真正能支持什麼。
