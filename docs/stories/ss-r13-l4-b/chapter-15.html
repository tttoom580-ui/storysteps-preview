The Crack in the Wall

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 15 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Heavy rain struck the valley before the property survey. A crack appeared in the old storage wall, and water ran across the floor toward the seed sacks. Yingrong called the mechanic and a building inspector rather than asking workers to carry everything through the wet corridor. Their first analysis was simple: the wall's formation and the ground's slope were allowing water to collect. The inspector used measuring tools to mark the crack's width and a monitor to record humidity. He said the construction was older than its most recent repairs, and they should not overlook hidden damage. Mother wanted to enter and rescue the last sealed boxes. Yingrong refused until the inspector confirmed an appropriate route. A minimum loss of inventory was better than an avoidable injury.

產權測量前，大雨襲擊河谷。舊儲物間的牆上出現裂縫，水沿地板流向種子麻袋。映蓉找來技師和建築檢查員，沒有要求工人穿過濕滑走廊搬走所有東西。第一輪分析很簡單：牆體構造和地面坡度讓水積在一起。檢查員用量測工具標出裂縫寬度，並用儀器監測濕度。他說，建築比最近的修補還舊，不能忽略隱藏損傷。母親想進去搶救最後幾箱封口貨。映蓉拒絕，直到檢查員確認適當通道為止。少損失些庫存，比可避免的受傷更重要。

A dispute began when the landlord insisted that the tenant had caused the damage by storing heavy sacks. Yingrong asked for photographs taken before the last storm and receipts for earlier repairs. The landlord said such detailed measures were unnecessary; the wall had endured for decades. Pan noted the contrast between survival and safety. A building could stand for a long time and still be unsafe on a particular day. Xiao-an found the old repair receipt in a drawer. It recorded the same crack, patched with a material intended for temporary use. The inspector said the new damage had potential to spread, but he would not call collapse certain without further examination. They closed the room and moved the records to a dry part of the house. That choice cost money, but it did not depend on winning the argument first.

房東堅持裂縫是租戶堆放沉重麻袋造成，雙方發生爭執。映蓉要求看上次暴雨前的照片和早先維修收據。房東說沒必要這麼細，牆已撐過幾十年。潘維提醒，存在很久和今天安全是不同的事。小安在抽屜找到舊修繕收據，上面記載同一條裂縫曾用臨時材料補過。檢查員說新損傷有擴大的可能，但進一步檢查前不能確定會倒塌。她們封閉房間，把紀錄搬到屋內乾燥區。這個選擇要花錢，卻不必先贏得爭論才能執行。

The landlord's lawyer proposed a settlement in which the shop would accept the wall as its responsibility in exchange for a reduced rent. Yingrong said that was not appropriate until the cause was known. She did not want to conquer the landlord in court; she wanted a safe building and a fair account of repair costs. Mother felt the pressure more painfully. Every day of closure reduced sales, and the bank did not stop asking for payment. She took an aspirin for a headache and sat down. Yingrong urged her to rest, while Xiao-an checked whether an empty room at the school could hold a few sealed samples. Pan offered a temporary table with strict storage conditions. Participation in the trial would continue only where the inspector allowed it.

房東的律師提出和解：店同意承擔牆體責任，換取減租。映蓉說，在查明原因前不適合這樣決定。她不想在法庭上擊敗房東，只想要安全建物和公平的修繕成本。母親承受壓力更深。每停業一天就少一筆收入，銀行卻不會停止催款。她為頭痛吃了阿斯匹靈，坐下休息。映蓉勸她休息，小安則去確認學校有沒有空房可放少量封口樣本。潘維提供一張臨時桌子，但附上嚴格保存條件。試驗只會在檢查員允許的場所繼續。

Two farmers visited with offers to help carry supplies. They were noble gestures, but Yingrong would not tolerate untrained people entering the restricted space. She thanked them and asked instead for transport from the safe entrance. They agreed. The next day the group moved only inspected items, recording each box's condition and destination. Mother stayed at the counter with the customer list, not because she was incapable, but because she had chosen not to lift loads. The work took longer than anyone hoped. By the end, the shop had saved the reference papers and most of its dry inventory. Several bags were too damp to sell. Yingrong marked them clearly rather than trying in vain to recover their purchase price through customers.

兩名農民主動來幫忙搬物資，出發點很高尚，但映蓉不能容忍未受訓的人進入管制區。她道謝，請他們改從安全入口協助運送。他們答應。隔天，團隊只搬經檢查的物品，記下每箱狀況和目的地。母親留在櫃檯處理顧客名單，不是因為她無能，而是她選擇不再搬重物。工作比任何人希望的都慢。最後，店保住參考文件與多數乾庫存，幾袋種子卻已潮到不能賣。映蓉清楚標記，而不是徒勞地想藉顧客把進貨成本收回。

The property survey was postponed until the building was stable. Jianping called to say he understood why selling quickly might not be possible. Yingrong thanked him without pretending that a delay solved the debt. Mother listened from her chair, her face tired but calm. She asked for the inspector's report and read the main points herself. The report did not tell her whether to keep the business; it told her the conditions under which keeping it could be safe. Yingrong saw a difference she had missed as a younger woman: independence sometimes meant accepting limits imposed by facts, not by family authority. She placed the damp sacks under a red notice and locked the unsafe door. The next chapter of the shop would have to begin somewhere dry.

產權測量延期到建物穩定之後。建平打電話說，他理解為何不能急著出售。映蓉道謝，卻沒有假裝延後就能解決債務。母親坐在椅子上聽，臉色疲憊但平靜。她要求拿檢查報告，親自讀了主要內容。報告不會告訴她是否要繼續做生意，只說明在什麼條件下繼續才安全。映蓉看見年輕時忽略的差別：獨立有時意味接受事實帶來的限制，而不是接受家人的權威。她把濕麻袋放在紅色告示下，鎖上危險的門。這家店的下一章，必須從乾燥的地方開始。
