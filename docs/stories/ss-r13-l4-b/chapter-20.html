冬季研習

The Winter Workshop

1998，長堤種子行的第七號紙袋 · 第 20 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The agricultural school invited the shop to a workshop on seed handling. Several students were preparing for graduation, while an amateur sculptor offered to make wooden markers for trial rows. Yingrong welcomed the art but asked that every marker remain readable in rain. A nutrition teacher explained how protein and other nutrients made certain crops useful in family meals; the point was not to promise that one vegetable could prevent disaster. A professional inspector described the storage inspection procedure in careful terms. Some listeners yawned after a long day, but the inspector's thorough checklist gave farmers questions they could use. Yingrong liked an event where experts could speak without turning the audience into passive admirers. Participants were free to disagree and ask for evidence. The teacher emphasized that nutritious food depended on a balanced diet, not a single fashionable crop.

農業學校邀請種子行參加種子處理研習。幾名學生準備畢業，一位業餘雕塑家願意製作田間木標記。映蓉歡迎藝術創作，但要求每個標記在雨中也要清楚可讀。營養老師解釋蛋白質與其他營養素如何使某些作物適合家庭餐食，並非保證單一蔬菜能防止災難。專業檢查員詳細說明保存檢查程序。有些聽眾工作一整天後打呵欠，但完整檢查表讓農民得到可以使用的提問。映蓉喜歡專家能說明，又不把聽眾當被動崇拜者的活動。參與者有權不同意，也能要求證據。 老師強調，營養豐富的食物仍要配合均衡飲食，不能只靠一種時髦作物。

The workshop included a small exhibition about rebuilding farms after past floods. One panel showed a damaged field site split by a drainage ditch, while another displayed an old sculpture made from broken equipment. The images were moving, but Yingrong wanted the revision dates printed beneath them. Historical photographs could teach people, yet they could also hide changes in land use. A farmer asked whether Golden Field had repaired its packing room. Fang Dingchuan, who had come voluntarily, said a new motor was installed but the building was still awaiting an independent inspection. Yingrong thanked him for the specific answer. It would have been easier to announce a successful repair, but a clear unresolved step was more useful to everyone. He promised to furnish an inspection summary and warned that bypassing a guard could cause a fatal accident.

研習附設一個關於歷次洪水後重建農田的小展。一面展示板有被排水溝切開的受損田地，另一面放著以壞設備製作的舊雕塑。畫面令人感動，映蓉卻希望下方印上修訂日期。歷史照片可以教人，也可能掩蓋土地使用改變。一名農民問金田是否已修好包裝間。自願到場的方定川說，新馬達已安裝，但建築仍等待獨立檢查。映蓉感謝他回答具體。宣布修好比較簡單，但清楚說出尚未完成的步驟，對大家更有用。 他承諾提供檢查摘要，並警告繞過防護裝置可能導致致命事故。

During a break, a librarian showed the group three books: a technical handbook, a history of local charity, and a romance novel set among farmers. Xiao-an laughed at the last title, but Yingrong said stories also shaped how people imagined work. She had once thought every business story had to end with a single heroic owner saving everyone else. The damaged seed packets had taught her otherwise. Each customer, worker, and supplier carried some part of the outcome, though not equal power or equal responsibility. The librarian suggested collecting ordinary accounts of successful and failed planting seasons. Yingrong agreed if contributors could review their own words before publication. A story could be truthful without giving a person more credit or blame than evidence supported. Yingrong needed time to absorb that lesson rather than dodge the difficult questions in the records.

休息時，圖書館員展示三本書：技術手冊、地方慈善史，以及一本以農民為背景的愛情小說。小安看見最後一本書名笑了，映蓉卻說，故事也會塑造人們想像工作的方式。她曾以為每個生意故事都要由一名英雄店主拯救所有人作結。受損紙袋讓她知道並非如此。每位顧客、工人與供應商都承擔部分結果，只是權力和責任並不相等。圖書館員建議收集一般農民成功與失敗的播種紀錄。映蓉同意，前提是提供者能在出版前審閱自己的話。真實的故事不必給任何人超出證據的功勞或責任。 映蓉需要時間吸收這個教訓，而不是迴避紀錄裡的難題。

The day's final exercise asked participants to decide whether to sell a batch with incomplete test records. Groups argued over price, timing, and the possibility of later loss. Yingrong was pleased that nobody proposed hiding the uncertainty. One student suggested selling the batch only after a clear retest, while another preferred to stop distribution entirely. The instructor explained that real decisions needed to account for the crop, the buyer, and the available alternatives. There was no single answer for every circumstance. Mother attended quietly from the back and filled in her own worksheet. When asked whether she would run the shop next year, she said she was still deciding. The room accepted her answer without asking her to justify it.

最後的練習要學員決定，是否販售檢驗紀錄不完整的批次。各組討論價格、時間和未來損失的可能。映蓉很欣慰，沒有人提議隱瞞不確定性。一名學生建議清楚重測後才販售，另一人偏好完全停止配送。講師解釋，真實決策要考慮作物、買方與可選替代方案，不存在適用所有情況的單一答案。母親安靜坐在後排，自己填了一張練習單。有人問她明年是否繼續經營，她說還在決定。全場接受這個回答，沒有要求她為此辯解。

On the journey home, the train passed fields waiting for spring. Mother said the workshop had been tiring but worthwhile. She had learned that her experience could become teaching material without requiring her to keep the shop forever. Yingrong asked whether she might prefer occasional paid lessons to daily counter work. Mother said perhaps, if she could choose which days and stop when she wished. They agreed to explore the idea after reviewing the debt plan. The next morning the post office building passed its roof inspection, and Yingrong received permission to fit out the front room. She set the opening date for a month later, leaving enough time to move inventory safely and inform customers. The new season was close, but she refused to hurry it into danger. Before departure, Mother asked the instructor to send a copy of the safety checklist to the new shop.

回程火車經過等待春天的田地。母親說研習很累，卻值得。她發現自己的經驗能成為教材，不一定非要永遠守著店。映蓉問，她會不會比較喜歡偶爾領薪授課，而不是天天站櫃檯。母親說也許，只要能自己選日期，也能隨時停止。她們同意在看完債務方案後再討論。隔天，舊郵局建物通過屋頂檢查，映蓉獲准整理前房。她把開幕日訂在一個月後，留足時間安全搬貨並通知顧客。新季節已近，但她不願為趕時間再冒險。 出發前，母親請講師把安全檢查表寄一份到新店。
