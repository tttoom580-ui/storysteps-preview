馬達聲下的人聲

A Voice Beneath the Motor

把海風留在最後一格 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The old cinema garage smelled of oil, bacon from the café next door, and damp carpet. Eli had brought a motor from a smaller projector so they could test the film without pulling on its weak edges. Nia placed a flashlight under the repair table and studied the dust around a sealed box. Someone had written “audio” on it, but the handwriting did not match the film labels. A technician named Jo arrived with a heavy sack of cables and a microphone. She was a former student of Eli's and had asked to help because her grandfather once worked near the floodgate. Nia explained the research rules before letting Jo touch a single item. Everybody would know whose material they were handling.

老戲院的車庫裡有機油味、隔壁咖啡館飄來的培根香，以及潮濕地毯的氣味。以安帶來一顆小型放映機馬達，讓他們能在不拉扯脆弱片邊的情況下測試。夏黎把手電筒放到工作桌底下，檢查封口盒四周的灰塵。有人在盒上寫著「聲音」，字跡卻與片卷標籤不同。技師小喬提著一大袋纜線和一支麥克風抵達。她曾是以安的學生，祖父又在舊防洪閘附近工作，因此主動請求協助。夏黎先解釋研究規則，才讓她碰任何物件；每個人都必須知道自己接觸的是誰的材料。

Jo opened the box and found a short sound recording on a separate tape. Its paper sleeve looked fake, as if somebody had copied an official design at home. Nia did not complain about the poor label; she recorded it as another clue. The tape began with steam from a machine, then footsteps and a voice asking whether the river gate was ready. The speaker's words were hard to pronounce through the noise. Eli adjusted the speed, but the sound remained unclear. Nia felt her jaw tighten. She wanted the voice to say something useful so badly that almost any answer would have satisfied her. Instead, she stopped playback and asked Jo to write down only what each listener heard independently.

小喬打開盒子，發現一卷分開保存的錄音帶。紙套看起來像假貨，彷彿有人在家模仿官方格式。夏黎沒有抱怨標籤粗糙，而是把它記成另一條線索。錄音先出現機器蒸汽聲，接著是腳步聲和一句詢問：「河閘準備好了嗎？」噪音使說話者的發音很難辨識。以安調整播放速度，聲音仍不清楚。夏黎感到下顎繃緊。她太希望那人說出有用的話，以至於幾乎任何答案都能讓她滿足。最後她停止播放，請小喬讓每個聆聽者先獨立記下真正聽見的字句。

The next day, an elderly worker came to listen. He had a cough and asked for warm water with a little peanut paste because swallowing dry food hurt his throat. He remembered a considerable amount of noise during the gate test, but not the exact conversation. He was careful about responsibility: “If you put my guess into the film as a fact, people will think I was there at every moment.” Nia promised not to do that. She also explained that the project would not engage him in an interview unless he wished to take part. The worker chose to describe the noise, not the people. His limited account still helped them separate a machine sound from a possible warning bell.

隔天，一名年長工人前來試聽。他有些咳嗽，要求一杯加少量花生醬的溫水，因為乾食物會讓他的喉嚨難受。他記得閘門測試時有相當大的聲響，卻不記得確切對話。他很在意責任問題：「如果你把我的猜測當事實放進電影，大家會以為我每個時刻都在現場。」夏黎答應不會這樣做，也說明除非他願意，團隊不會讓他參與正式訪談。工人選擇只談聲音，不談人物。這份範圍有限的證詞仍然有用，因為它幫助團隊區分機械聲與可能存在的警示鐘聲。

A second tape lay beneath the first, wrapped in paper that had gone brown with age. It contained a woman's voice describing ripe fruit at the market and the way children played near the water before the storm. Then she stopped, and a violent burst of static covered the next sentence. Nia could hear sorrow in the pause but refused to invent words. She asked Jo to save both the damaged and cleaned versions. When Jo offered to remove every hiss, Nia shook her head. Too much cleaning might erase the low sounds that showed where the recording had been made. Repair was not the same as making the past pretty.

第一卷錄音帶下方還壓著第二卷，外面包的紙因年代久遠而變褐。裡面是一名婦女談論市場成熟水果的聲音，以及暴風雨前孩子如何在水邊遊玩。她突然停下，一陣猛烈的雜訊蓋過下一句。夏黎在停頓中聽見悲傷，卻拒絕替她編造語句。她請小喬同時保存破損原聲與清理後的版本。小喬提議除去所有嘶嘶聲，夏黎搖頭。過度清理也許會刪掉能顯示錄音地點的低頻聲響。修復不等於把過去打扮得漂亮。

At lunch, they shared bread and oranges in the garage. Nia told the team about a childhood memory of watching her father fix a radio. He used to say that a loose wire could make a truthful speaker sound like a liar. Eli smiled and added that a clean recording could also carry a dishonest message. Their opinions were not in conflict. One concerned the instrument; the other concerned the speaker. Jo wrote this difference above the workbench and pinned the two audio labels beneath it. For the first time, the group felt less like three people collecting mysteries and more like a team setting standards for its own work.

午餐時，三人在車庫分享麵包和橘子。夏黎提起童年看父親修收音機的回憶。父親常說，接觸不良的電線能讓說真話的人聽起來像在說謊。以安笑著補充，乾淨的錄音也可能傳遞不誠實的訊息。兩人的看法並不衝突：一個關乎器材，另一個關乎說話者。小喬把這項區別寫在工作檯上方，兩張錄音標籤則釘在下面。這是三人第一次感到，自己不只是蒐集謎團的人，更是一個正在替工作訂下標準的團隊。

Before they left, Alma visited with a small bag of peaches. She listened to the recording once and said that the woman's voice might be hers, but she would not confirm it without hearing the original tape at normal speed. Nia liked that answer. She had expected their first week to end with a dramatic discovery. Instead, it ended with a list of uncertain sounds and an agreement about how to handle them. On the wall, Jo drew three columns: heard directly, supported by records, and still unknown. Eli placed the tape in its case, and Nia signed the record. She could not yet show the public a finished film. She could, however, show how they were learning to listen.

離開前，艾瑪帶著一小袋桃子來訪。她聽完錄音後說，婦女的聲音可能是自己，但在以正常速度聽過原始錄音前，她不會確認。夏黎喜歡這個回答。她原以為第一週會以戲劇性的發現結束，結果只得到一張不確定聲音的清單，以及對處理方式的共識。小喬在牆上畫了三欄：「親耳聽見」、「有紀錄支持」和「仍待查明」。以安把錄音帶放回盒子，夏黎在紀錄上簽名。她還不能向大眾放映完成的影片，但至少能說明團隊正在如何學習聆聽。
