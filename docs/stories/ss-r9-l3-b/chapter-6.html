封蠟上的年份

The Wax Seal

把海風留在最後一格 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Tuesday, the professor arrived with a box of records sealed in old wax. “Do not assume that a seal proves a document is true,” he said. “It may only prove that nobody opened the box recently.” Inside were notes from an emergency exercise held beside the river. The papers mentioned a tropical storm, a closed road, and a visiting politician who promised better warning systems. Nia noticed a line about a missile alert: the town had once used the same alarm for military practice and flood tests. That could explain why witnesses remembered different sounds. She made a summary of each document before deciding which ones mattered. A dull administrative note could be more useful than a dramatic picture.

星期二，教授帶來一盒以舊封蠟封住的紀錄。「不要假設有封印就代表文件真實，」他說，「它也許只表示最近沒人打開盒子。」裡面是河邊緊急演習的筆記，提到熱帶風暴、封閉道路，以及一名承諾改善預警系統的來訪政治人物。夏黎注意到一行飛彈警報的記載：這座城以前曾把同一套警報用在軍事練習和防洪測試上。這可能解釋證人為何記得不同聲響。她先替每份文件寫摘要，再判斷重要性。平淡的行政筆記，可能比戲劇性的畫面更有用。

Jo opened a laboratory kit that measured moisture and bacteria on the edge of the film. The numbers were low enough for cautious treatment, but the repair room still needed better air flow. Eli warned that opening every tin at once could multiply the damage if the room became damp. Nia bumped her elbow against a shelf while moving the boxes, sending a cloud of dust into the light. “I thought the archive would give me answers,” she said. “Anyhow, it keeps teaching me new questions.” Eli laughed, then helped her carry the heavy shelf away from the wall. Nothing about his help suggested that he expected to make decisions for her.

小喬打開實驗工具組，測量底片邊緣的濕氣和細菌。數值容許他們謹慎處理，但修復室仍需要改善通風。以安警告，如果房間變潮，一次打開所有鐵盒可能使損害倍增。夏黎搬盒子時手肘撞到架子，一片灰塵在燈光中揚起。「我原以為檔案會給我答案，」她說，「總之，它反而一直教我提出新問題。」以安笑了，幫她把沉重架子移開牆邊。他的協助完全沒有讓人覺得他想替她做決定。

The exercise records introduced a teenage volunteer called Mina, who had counted warning bells along the shore. A drawing in the margin showed a technique for distinguishing two kinds of alarm by the pause between rings. Nia checked the recording and found a similar pause, but there was still a problem: the tape could have been edited later. The professor suggested contacting Mina, now a retired teacher. He reminded them that every research resource came with a human cost. Calling someone unexpectedly about a frightening flood might upset them. Nia wrote a polite invitation that explained the project and made refusal easy. She would not turn an old fear into a sudden demand.

演習紀錄提到一名叫米娜的青少年志工，她曾在海岸計算警鐘次數。頁邊畫著一種辨識兩類警報的技巧，方法是看兩次鐘聲之間停頓多久。夏黎在錄音裡找到相似停頓，但問題仍在：錄音帶可能後來被剪輯。教授建議聯絡如今已退休當老師的米娜，同時提醒每種研究資源都有人的代價。突然打電話詢問嚇人的洪水往事，可能讓當事人難受。夏黎於是寫了一封禮貌邀請，說明計畫，也讓拒絕變得容易。她不會把舊日恐懼變成突然施加的要求。

The bump against the shelf had also knocked an empty label onto the floor. Jo picked it up and found a faint date on its back. They photographed the label before putting it in a clean cover. A small accident had revealed a useful mark, but Nia refused to praise carelessness as a research technique. Next time they would move the boxes more safely.

撞上架子的那一下，也把一張空標籤震落地面。小喬撿起來，發現背面有淡淡日期。大家先拍照，再把標籤放入乾淨封套。小意外讓他們看見有用記號，但夏黎不會把粗心說成研究技巧。下次他們一定會用更安全的方法搬盒子。

A sheet of safety instructions had gone stale with age; the paper smelled sour and the ink broke when touched. The creator of the rules had listed food, water, and first aid as an inner necessity of every rescue plan. Eli pointed out that the gate operators had been trained to protect machinery before checking whether workers were safe. “That is not proof they followed the order,” he said. “It tells us what the system expected.” The distinction settled the uneasy feeling in Nia's stomach. Their job was not to find one suspect as quickly as possible. It was to understand which choices the system allowed, which it punished, and what people actually did.

一張安全說明因年久變得陳腐，紙張散發酸味，墨跡碰了就剝落。制定規範的人把食物、飲水與急救列為救援計畫的核心必需品。以安指出，閘門操作員接受的訓練是先保護機器，才確認工人是否安全。「這不證明他們真的照做，」他說，「只說明制度期待他們做什麼。」這項區分讓夏黎胃裡的不安稍微安定。他們的任務不是盡快找到一名嫌疑人，而是理解制度允許什麼、懲罰什麼，以及人們實際做了什麼。

At the evening workshop, Nia asked the volunteers to name one example of excellence in preservation. Jo said “a clean picture,” the professor said “an accurate source,” and Eli said “a useful repair that can be repeated.” Nia added a fourth answer: “The person whose memory we use still has a choice.” She was no longer trying to win a contest for the best film. She was building a process that other people could question. Outside, the harbor lights reflected on the windows like lines of moving fire. Alma called to say that Mina had received the invitation and wanted to meet, but only in daylight. Nia accepted the condition immediately.

晚間工作坊上，夏黎請志工各舉一個保存工作中「卓越」的例子。小喬說「乾淨畫面」，教授說「準確來源」，以安說「可被他人重做的有效修復」。夏黎加上第四個答案：「被使用記憶的人，仍然保有選擇權。」她不再只想贏得最佳影片的競賽，而是在建立一套別人也能質疑的程序。窗外港燈映在玻璃上，像一條條移動的火線。艾瑪打電話說，米娜已收到邀請，願意見面，但只在白天。夏黎立刻接受這項條件。

When the others left, Nia checked the archive log one more time. She could not confirm who had applied the wax seal, and the record did not name a single person who had approved the old exercise. Those absences mattered. She marked them clearly and saved an untouched scan beside her working notes. As she turned off the lamp, Eli asked whether she had eaten dinner. She had forgotten. They walked to a small noodle shop, where the owner closed early on stormy evenings. There, without discussing the film for ten minutes, they discovered that they both liked quiet books and disliked crowds. Nia was surprised by how welcome an ordinary conversation felt.

其他人離開後，夏黎又檢查了一次檔案紀錄。她無法確認誰封上的蠟，也找不到批准舊演習的單一責任人。這些空缺同樣重要。她清楚註記，並把未修改掃描檔與工作筆記分開保存。正要關燈時，以安問她吃過晚餐沒。她居然忘了。兩人走到一家逢暴風雨就提早關門的麵店，刻意有十分鐘不談底片。他們發現彼此都喜歡安靜的書，也不喜歡擁擠場合。夏黎驚訝地發現，如此普通的聊天竟令人愉快。
