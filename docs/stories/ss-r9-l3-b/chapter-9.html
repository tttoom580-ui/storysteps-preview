消失的汽笛

The Missing Horn

把海風留在最後一格 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next reel opened with a cabin window and the horn of a ferry crossing the river. The sound was powerful enough to make the speakers roar, yet the captain's log in the database showed that no ferry had traveled that route on the date printed on the tin. Nia felt faint. A wrong date could weaken the whole project. Jo checked the sound track and noticed that the horn entered suddenly, without the usual echo from the water. “Could it be a separate recording?” she asked. Eli said it was possible, but the evidence was not complete. Nia put the reel aside before frustration made her careless with it.

下一卷底片以船艙窗景與渡輪汽笛聲開場，聲音強到讓喇叭轟鳴；但資料庫裡的船長日誌顯示，鐵盒所標的日期根本沒有渡輪走那條航線。夏黎感到頭暈。日期錯誤可能削弱整個計畫。小喬檢查聲軌，發現汽笛聲突然插入，缺少水面應有的回音。「會不會是另一段錄音？」她問。以安說可能，但證據不完整。夏黎先把片卷放到一旁，免得挫折讓自己處理不慎。

A young worker named Leon came to help identify the ship. His grandfather had kept a weekly journal about the river, and Leon still owned several pages. The notes described a small boat that began to sink after water struck its side, sending a splash over the passengers. No one had been hurt, but the incident had caused a great fright. Nia compared the entries with the film and found a matching flag above the cabin. She asked Leon why the log book did not mention the boat. “Because it was not a ferry,” he said. “It was a private work boat, and the harbor office did not count those trips in the passenger record.”

年輕工人里昂來幫忙辨認船隻。他祖父曾每週寫河道日誌，里昂仍保留幾頁。筆記記載一艘小船被浪打中側面後開始下沉，水花濺過乘客，幸好無人受傷，卻造成極大驚嚇。夏黎比對影片，找到與筆記相同的船艙旗幟。她問里昂為什麼航行日誌沒列這艘船。「因為它不是渡輪，」他說，「它是私人工作船，港務單位不把那種航次算進載客紀錄。」

Leon showed them a photograph of his grandfather holding a horn near the workshop. The instrument had been borrowed for a theater show years later. A small note on the back read, “For the lover of loud music.” That playful line made Jo laugh. The question of the horn, however, was serious: somebody might have added the sound to make the work boat seem like a passenger ferry. Nia considered what such a change could achieve. It might create fear, or it might help people understand where a boat stood. Without knowing the editor's purpose, she could not call it a weapon of deception. She marked the sound as “unverified.”

里昂拿出祖父手持汽笛、站在工坊附近的照片。多年後，這個器具曾借給劇團表演。照片背面寫著「送給熱愛大聲音樂的人」，讓小喬笑了。然而汽笛的問題很嚴肅：有人可能後來加入聲音，使工作船看似載客渡輪。夏黎思考改動的目的，也許是製造恐懼，也許是幫觀眾理解船的位置。尚未得知剪輯者目的前，她不能把它當作欺騙的武器。她將聲音標記為「未經查證」。

Leon's grandfather had been a skillful mechanic. Shortly before the flood, he had tested the boat's horn with friends and filled the dock with laughter. Leon showed Nia a dated note about the test. That ordinary moment gave them another reason to question the later soundtrack. The horn might have been recorded during a harmless repair, then added to a different scene.

里昂的祖父是熟練技師。洪水前不久，他曾與朋友測試船笛，笑聲充滿碼頭。里昂拿出一則有日期的測試便條給夏黎看。這個普通時刻又提供一個質疑後來聲軌的理由：汽笛可能是在無害的維修中錄下，再被加到不同場景。

That afternoon the professor brought a lesson on how records could bore an impatient audience and still matter. A list of river depths included a short period when the tide had risen unusually fast. Nia found the same date in Leon's pages. The river had been dangerous, but the situation was not proof that anyone had planned to harm the town. In the film, a sailor cut his finger on a rough rope and began to bleed. Nia paused on the frame, then advanced carefully. She was looking for the gate tower behind him, not the injury. Keeping her attention on the relevant detail was harder than she expected.

當天下午，教授帶來一堂小課，談那些容易讓沒耐心的觀眾感到無聊、卻仍很重要的紀錄。河道水深表顯示某一小段時間潮水異常迅速上升。夏黎在里昂祖父的筆記裡找到同一天。河道確實危險，但這不證明有人預謀傷害城鎮。影片中，一名水手被粗繩割破手指出血。夏黎暫停影格，又小心往後播放。她真正要找的是水手後面的閘塔，不是傷口。把注意力維持在相關細節上，比她想像得難。

Afterward, Eli suggested using the independent audio and picture tracks in a public demonstration. Visitors could hear the raw sound, see the silent scene, and understand why the combined version was uncertain. Nia liked the idea but worried it might seem like a technical trick. Eli said that democracy needed more than a choice between believing a film and rejecting it. People should see how evidence was made. Nia added a question card so viewers could record doubts without writing their names. The card design was plain, with no bright arrows telling them what to think. It was the opposite of the dramatic posters she had once imagined making.

之後以安建議，在公開展示時把原始聲音與畫面分開播放。觀眾能先聽原聲、再看無聲片段，明白合成版本為何不確定。夏黎喜歡這個主意，又怕它看起來像技術把戲。以安說，民主不該只讓人選擇相信或不相信一部電影，也該讓人看見證據如何形成。夏黎加上一張提問卡，讓觀眾不必署名就能留下疑問。卡片設計很簡單，沒有鮮豔箭頭指示大家該怎麼想，與她以前想做的戲劇性海報完全相反。

When Leon left, he said his grandfather used to wander along the shore every Sunday, listening for the sounds of different engines. “He could tune a radio by ear,” Leon said, “but he never guessed when he could check.” Nia repeated that sentence in her notebook. She had spent the day worried that one missing fact would ruin everything, yet every correction had made the research stronger. Eli offered to walk with her to the market. She accepted, then admitted she had enjoyed the way he challenged her ideas. He looked surprised. “I was afraid you thought I was being difficult,” he said. “Sometimes you are,” she answered, smiling, “and sometimes that is helpful.”

里昂離開前說，祖父每個星期日都會沿岸散步，聽不同引擎的聲音。「他能靠耳朵替收音機調頻，」里昂說，「但能查證時，他從不亂猜。」夏黎把這句話抄進筆記。她一整天擔心一項缺失就會毀掉計畫，結果每次更正都讓研究更強。以安提議陪她去市場，她答應了，也承認自己喜歡他挑戰她想法的方式。以安看起來有點驚訝。「我還怕你覺得我難相處，」他說。「有時確實，」她笑答，「但有時那也很有幫助。」
