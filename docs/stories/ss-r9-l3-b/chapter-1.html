市集裡的鐵盒

The Tin at the Fair

把海風留在最後一格 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The harbor fair began with the smell of berry cakes and salt water. Nia Lin, twenty-nine, had come to record the last day of an old cinema, not to enjoy the circus beside the pier. A jazz band played outside a wooden hut, where former sailors sold photographs of ships that no longer existed. Nia had spent six months repairing films for private clients. She wanted one project that belonged to the people who appeared on the screen. When a woman with silver hair called her name, she thought it was another request to clean a family wedding video. Instead, the woman set a rusted film tin on the table and asked, “Can a picture tell a story that a town has refused to hear?”

港口市集一開始，空氣裡就飄著莓果蛋糕與海水的氣味。二十九歲的林夏黎來此是為了記錄老戲院營業的最後一天，而不是看碼頭旁的馬戲團。爵士樂團在一間木棚外演奏，幾名退休水手則販售那些早已不存在的船隻照片。夏黎花了六個月替私人客戶修復底片，卻一直想做一件真正屬於畫面中居民的作品。一位銀髮婦人叫住她時，她以為對方只是想修復家庭婚禮影片。沒想到婦人放下一只生鏽的底片鐵盒，問道：「照片能不能說出一座城不肯聽的故事？」

The woman introduced herself as Alma, a retired photographer. Behind her, a magician in a fancy coat gave children paper wings and called them fairy gifts. A small electric heater warmed his hands, although the afternoon was not cold. Alma bought a pint of tea and waited until the applause faded. She explained that the film showed Harbor Day in 1988, the week before a terrible flood. People remembered the festival but spoke little about the water. The city had built a new sea wall and made a political promise that the past was finished. Nia did not believe that a single film could solve an old argument. Still, she noticed that Alma held the tin as carefully as if it were a living thing.

婦人自我介紹叫艾瑪，是一名退休攝影師。她身後，一位穿華麗外套的魔術師送給孩子紙翅膀，稱它們為仙子禮物。下午並不冷，他卻開著小型電暖器取暖。艾瑪買了一品脫茶，等掌聲停下才解釋，底片記錄的是一九八八年的海港日，距離那場可怕洪水只有一週。人們記得慶典，卻很少談論洪水。市府蓋了新的海堤，還以政治承諾宣稱往事已經結束。夏黎不相信一卷底片就能解決舊爭議，但她注意到艾瑪捧著鐵盒的姿態，彷彿那是有生命的東西。

Alma had another reason for choosing Nia. The deed of the old cinema said its equipment would pass to a public trust when the building closed. Yet a property company wanted to buy the projectors and send the film collection away. “I do not want to rob anyone of a fair price,” Alma said. “I want people to know what they are selling.” Inside the tin was a short strip wrapped in paper. Its edge carried various dates written in different hands. Nia resisted the urge to spin the fragile roll open. One careless turn could damage an image forever. She promised to look only after checking whether Alma had the right to let her examine it.

艾瑪找夏黎還有另一個理由。老戲院的產權契據載明，戲院關閉時，設備應交由公共信託保管；然而某家地產公司想買走放映機，連同收藏的底片一起運走。「我不想搶走任何人的合理價款，」艾瑪說，「我只希望大家知道自己在賣什麼。」鐵盒裡有一小卷以紙包住的底片，邊緣寫著不同人留下的各種日期。夏黎忍住立刻轉開片卷的衝動。一次粗心的旋轉就可能永遠毀掉影像。她答應先確認艾瑪是否有權提供底片檢查，才會動手。

A jet crossed the sky as Eli Chen approached from the cinema's back door. At thirty-two, he repaired projection machines and taught evening classes in basic mechanics. He pointed to a loose screw on the projector stand and fixed it before touching the film. Then he showed Nia the small signal lamp that warned operators when the motor grew too hot. “Old machines need patience,” he said. “So do people who keep secrets.” Nia had almost decided to quit film restoration last winter, when a wealthy client demanded that she erase a person from a family recording. She had refused, but she still remembered how easily a screen could make someone disappear.

一架噴射機飛過天空時，三十二歲的陳以安從戲院後門走來。他平日修理放映機，也在夜間課程教授基礎機械。他先指出放映架上一顆鬆動的螺絲，鎖好之後才碰底片。接著，他向夏黎展示提醒操作員馬達過熱的小訊號燈。「老機器需要耐心，」他說，「保守祕密的人也是。」去年冬天，一名富有的客戶曾要求夏黎從家庭錄影中抹去某個人，讓她差點辭去影像修復工作。她雖然拒絕，卻一直記得螢幕能多輕易地讓人消失。

Nia asked Eli to help check the reel, but not to become its owner. She wrote their duties on a receipt: Alma supplied the material, Nia would record every change, and Eli would test the equipment. It was not a miracle that the brittle film still held an image; good storage and good luck had simply met. A child nearby tried to tame a paper kite in the sea wind. Nia watched him stop pulling when the string grew tight. She understood the lesson. A film could not be forced to reveal more than it contained. At sunset, Alma agreed to sign a temporary examination form. No public screening was permitted yet. That limit made Nia trust the old photographer more, not less.

夏黎請以安協助檢查片卷，但不讓他因此取得底片所有權。她把分工寫在收據上：艾瑪提供材料，夏黎記錄每次修改，以安測試設備。脆弱的底片仍保有影像並非奇蹟，只是良好保存與運氣剛好相遇。附近有個孩子試著馴服海風中的紙風箏，線一拉緊，他便不再硬扯。夏黎明白了其中的道理：不能強迫底片說出它沒有記錄的事。日落時，艾瑪同意簽下暫時檢查表，卻不允許公開放映。這項界線反而讓夏黎更加信任她。

They carried the tin into the silent cinema. The last audience had gone home, leaving cups beneath the seats and a pale rectangle of light on the wall. Eli offered to keep the reel in his workshop overnight, where the air was dry. Nia declined; the borrowed film would stay in a locked public cabinet with two names on the entry sheet. They could return together in the morning. Alma smiled at this small act of care, then told them something that changed the evening. The final part of the reel was missing from every copy she knew. Someone had cut it away after the flood. Nia looked at the dark screen. Now she had a question worth following, but she still had no answer.

三人把鐵盒帶進安靜的戲院。最後一批觀眾已經回家，座位下留著紙杯，牆上只剩一道淡淡的長方形光影。以安提議把底片放在自己乾燥的工作室過夜，夏黎卻婉拒。借來的影像應存入公共上鎖櫃，出入登記表必須留下兩個名字，明早兩人再一起來。艾瑪因這個細小而謹慎的舉動露出笑容，隨後說出改變整個夜晚的一件事：她見過的每一份拷貝，都少了片卷最後一段；洪水之後有人把它剪走了。夏黎望向漆黑的銀幕。她終於找到值得追問的問題，卻還沒有答案。
