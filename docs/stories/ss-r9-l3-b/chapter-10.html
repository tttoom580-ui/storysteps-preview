婚禮與潮汐

The Wedding and the Tide

把海風留在最後一格 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A wedding film arrived from a family who had heard about the research. The bride wore a simple silk dress and a pearl near her collar. Her brother had kept the camera steady during a strong tide, while an angel made of paper hung above the door. Nia thought the wedding might help determine the date of Alma's film because the same market flag appeared in both. The bride, now sixty, agreed to a private comparison but asked them not to show her children dancing. She was still proud of the celebration, yet the children had grown into adults with their own preferences. Nia copied only the approved frames and placed a clear note beside them.

一戶聽聞研究的家庭送來婚禮影片。新娘穿著簡單的絲質禮服，領口別了一顆珍珠。當天潮水很強，她的哥哥仍穩穩持著相機，門上方掛著紙做的天使。夏黎認為婚禮可能幫忙確定艾瑪影片的日期，因為兩卷底片都有同一面市場旗幟。如今六十歲的新娘同意私人比對，卻要求不要放出孩子們跳舞的畫面。她仍為婚禮驕傲，但孩子們已成年，有自己的偏好。夏黎只複製獲准使用的影格，並在旁邊放上清楚註記。

The wedding took place in a fashionable hall above a bakery. A stove warmed food in the kitchen, and the guests shared slices of melon and a fruit punch. A bold uncle stood to make a speech about marriage, but the old microphone failed. The bride's sister waved a handkerchief, and everyone laughed. Eli was impressed by the little details the family had kept. In one corner of the frame, a window showed the harbor tower and a clock. The clock did not tell them everything, but it gave them a useful structure for comparing the two reels. Nia carefully wrote the date from the wedding invitation rather than relying on the printed box label.

婚禮辦在麵包店樓上一間時髦禮堂。廚房的爐子正保溫食物，賓客分享甜瓜切片與水果賓治。一位大膽的叔叔站起來談婚姻，舊麥克風卻故障了。新娘的妹妹揮著手帕，大家都笑出聲。以安對一家人保存的細節印象深刻。影格一角的窗戶映出港口閘塔和時鐘。時鐘無法回答一切，但能提供比較兩卷底片的時間架構。夏黎謹慎抄下喜帖上的日期，而不是依賴盒子印的標籤。

A thin flame from a candle moved in the direction of the sea wind. The professor compared that movement with old weather records, then reminded Nia that indoor air could change the flame too. The important clue was not the candle but the height of the water beneath the window. The tide chart recorded the same level during a short part of the afternoon. Nia began to feel optimistic, yet she did not call the date certain until a second record matched it. The family had a newspaper from the morning of the wedding. Its front page showed a bridge under repair, and the same bridge appeared behind the market stalls in Alma's film.

蠟燭細小的火焰朝海風吹來的方向偏移。教授拿當年的天氣紀錄比對，又提醒夏黎，室內氣流也可能改變火焰方向。真正重要的線索不是蠟燭，而是窗下水位高度。潮汐表顯示下午一小段時間的水位正好符合。夏黎開始感到樂觀，卻等第二項紀錄符合後才願確定日期。家人留著婚禮當天早晨的報紙，頭版照片拍到維修中的橋；同一座橋也出現在艾瑪影片的市場攤位後面。

Somehow, the wedding frames could impress people more deeply than a grand speech. Nia tried not to stare at the bride's private family scenes; she looked only at the authorized window view. She understood that the date mattered precisely because she had kept her attention on a small, permitted detail rather than the most colorful part of the recording.

不知怎麼地，婚禮影格比宏大的演講更能打動人。夏黎努力不盯著新娘私人的家庭片段，而只看獲准使用的窗景。她理解日期之所以重要，正是因為自己把注意力放在獲准的小細節，而非錄影中最熱鬧的部分。

Nia finally had a reliable date for the festival scenes: June 10, not June 12 as the later label claimed. That discovery brought relief but also a moral problem. If the gate test occurred on June 12, the festival pictures could not directly show it. Someone had combined two different days in the same reel. Was it a mistake, a way to tell a story, or an attempt to mislead viewers? Nia asked Eli to stop the projector. “We must separate the days before we make any accusation,” she said. He agreed and drew a line through their earlier timeline, leaving the original notes visible underneath. They had lost a theory, but gained a clearer question.

夏黎終於取得節慶畫面的可靠日期：六月十日，不是後來標籤寫的六月十二日。這項發現令人鬆口氣，卻帶來道德難題。如果閘門測試發生在六月十二日，節慶畫面不可能直接拍到它。有人把不同日子的影像合在同一卷裡。這是錯誤、敘事手法，還是刻意誤導？夏黎請以安關掉放映機。「在指控任何人之前，必須先把兩天分開，」她說。以安同意，在時間線上劃線修正，但保留下面原先的筆記。他們失去一個假設，卻得到更清楚的問題。

That night Nia and Eli sat on the cinema steps, watching boats move across the dark water. He asked whether she wanted to go to dinner with him sometime when they were not working. The question surprised her more than any hidden frame. She stared at the lights and thought about how much she valued their friendship. “Yes,” she said at last, “but I want to keep the project decisions separate.” Eli nodded. “I would not ask for anything else.” She felt a gentle warmth, but she knew one agreeable evening would not decide her future. She still had a film to finish and a town to serve. That was part of the person he had invited.

那天夜裡，夏黎與以安坐在戲院台階上，看小船越過漆黑水面。他問她，改天沒有工作時願不願意一起吃晚餐。這問題比任何隱藏的影格都讓她驚訝。她凝視燈光，想到自己多麼珍惜這段友誼。「好，」她終於說，「但計畫決策還是要分開。」以安點頭：「我也不會要求別的。」她心裡泛起溫柔暖意，卻知道一個愉快夜晚不能決定未來。她仍有一部電影要完成，也有一座城要負責；這同樣是他所邀請的那個人不可分割的一部分。

Before leaving, Nia returned to the workbench to check the boxes one last time. The newly dated festival footage went on a separate shelf from the gate-test material. She added a card explaining why its date had changed and who had supplied the wedding evidence. The work was slow and not particularly exciting, but it protected the people whose memories she had borrowed. At the door, Eli asked whether he could carry the heavier case. She handed it to him and kept the key. They walked into the night laughing about the wedding uncle's broken microphone. For once, Nia did not feel the need to turn every moment into material for a film.

離開前，夏黎回到工作檯最後檢查一次盒子。重新確認日期的節慶片段被放在與閘門測試資料不同的架子上。她加了一張卡片，說明日期為何更正，以及婚禮證據由誰提供。這工作緩慢且不特別刺激，卻保護了借出記憶的人。到了門口，以安問能否幫她提較重的盒子。她把盒子交給他，鑰匙則自己留著。兩人走進夜色，一路笑談婚禮叔叔壞掉的麥克風。這一次，夏黎不覺得每個瞬間都必須變成電影素材。
