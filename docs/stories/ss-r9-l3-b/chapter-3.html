竹架上的畫面

A Frame Made of Bamboo

把海風留在最後一格 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first clear image appeared against a background of bright market flags. Workers carried a bundle of bamboo poles past the camera, then stopped to build a temporary screen. Nia had expected a dull official ceremony. Instead, the film showed people in ordinary clothes preparing dinner beside the harbor. A bird dealer sold cages of pigeons, and a child dropped a chip of wood into a puddle to watch it float. Eli adjusted the projector slowly so the light would not deepen a scratch. They watched without sound and wrote down what they could actually see. “We must not use our guesses as subtitles,” Nia said. The film offered evidence of a day, but not yet of the disaster that came afterward.

第一個清楚畫面出現在一片鮮豔市場旗幟前。工人抱著一捆竹竿走過鏡頭，隨後停下來架起臨時銀幕。夏黎原以為底片只會拍到無趣的官方典禮，結果畫面裡是身穿日常衣服、在港邊準備晚餐的人們。一名鳥販賣裝著鴿子的籠子，一個孩子則把小木片丟進水窪，看它如何漂浮。以安緩慢調整放映機，以免光學裝置與片道加重刮痕。兩人看著無聲影像，只記下真正看見的事。「不能把猜測直接當字幕，」夏黎說。這卷片證明某一天發生了什麼，卻還沒說明後來的災難。

Alma came in to chat during the afternoon session. She recognized a downtown café and the owner, who always gave a doughnut to children helping at the stalls. The owner had died years earlier, but his daughter ran a small restaurant. Nia asked Alma to call before they visited. A civil conversation mattered more than a quick interview, especially when families carried memories of poverty and loss. They agreed to dine there after closing time and show only still pictures that did not expose private moments. Nia wanted the research to be democratic in practice, not just in a speech: people should be able to correct names, refuse a recording, and decide whether their personal stories belonged in the final film.

下午檢查時，艾瑪進來聊天。她認出市中心一間咖啡館，也認出總會送甜甜圈給幫忙擺攤孩子的老闆。老闆多年前已過世，女兒仍經營一家小餐館。夏黎請艾瑪先打電話，再安排拜訪。對背負貧困與失落記憶的家庭而言，文明且有禮的談話比快速採訪重要。三人同意在餐館打烊後用餐，只展示不涉及私人時刻的靜態畫面。夏黎希望研究真正民主，而不只是口頭宣稱：居民能更正姓名、拒絕錄音，也能決定自身故事是否出現在最後的電影裡。

The professor brought a map showing the old river bridge. In the film, a mighty wooden crane stood where a public path should have been. That discovery made Nia leap from her chair, but the professor asked her to look again. The crane might have been temporary festival equipment. A poll conducted shortly after the flood recorded angry residents, yet it asked only whether people wanted a new sea wall. There was no question about the old river gate. The professor explained that for the sake of clear research, a missing question was not proof of a hidden answer. Nia wrote this in her notebook. It was a warning to herself as much as to the city.

教授帶來一張舊河橋地圖。影片裡，一架巨大的木製起重架立在理應是公共道路的地方。夏黎驚得從椅子上跳起來，但教授請她再看清楚：那架吊具也可能只是慶典的臨時設備。洪水後不久的一次民意調查記錄了憤怒的居民，卻只問他們是否支持新海堤，完全沒問舊河閘的事。教授提醒，為了研究清楚，缺少一個問題不等於已經證明有人藏起答案。夏黎把這句話寫進筆記。這既是在提醒市府，也是在提醒自己。

At the restaurant, the owner's daughter, Sera, placed a bowl of soup between them. She examined one frame for a long time. “My father wore that blue shirt,” she said, “but he would not have stood beside the crane unless someone asked him to help.” She showed them a shop diary describing a delivery of water and food to workers repairing the old floodgate. The ink had spread, and one page had been torn out. Nia did not react with an accusation. She asked whether Sera would allow a photograph of the page and whether she wanted her name printed. Sera agreed to a copy for research, but not to a public broadcast until the family had discussed it.

到了餐館，老闆的女兒瑟拉把一碗湯放到眾人中間，久久看著其中一格畫面。「我父親穿的就是那件藍襯衫，」她說，「但除非有人請他幫忙，否則他不會站在起重架旁。」她拿出店裡的日誌，上面記著送水與食物給修理舊防洪閘的工人。墨水已經暈開，其中一頁還被撕掉了。夏黎沒有立刻指控任何人，只問瑟拉是否允許拍下日誌，以及願不願意公開姓名。瑟拉同意先讓研究團隊留副本，但要等家人商量過後才考慮公開放映。

Eli studied the film again after dinner. A small white mark near the crane did not move between frames. It could be dust, a damaged spot, or a label painted on a machine. Nia wanted to clean it away, but he asked her to preserve the original strip first. “We can make a study copy and change that,” he said. “The source must remain untouched.” His simple method prevented them from destroying something they might later need. Meanwhile, Nia noticed a change in herself. She had started the week wanting a beautiful film for a public festival. Now she wanted a fair record, even if the result was less dramatic. That desire felt quieter, but much stronger.

晚餐後，以安再次檢視底片。起重架旁有一塊小白點，連續幾格都沒有移動。它可能是灰塵、損壞痕跡，或是畫在機器上的標籤。夏黎想把它清掉，以安卻提醒她先保留原片。「可以製作研究副本，在副本上調整，」他說，「來源必須保持原狀。」這個簡單方法讓他們沒有毀掉未來可能需要的證據。與此同時，夏黎發現自己也在改變。這週開始時，她只想為公共影展做一部漂亮的電影；如今，她更希望留下公平的紀錄，即使成品沒有那麼戲劇化。這份願望更安靜，卻也更堅定。

Before leaving, Sera brought an old receipt from a delivery truck. The printed description named a building material but did not identify its substance clearly. On the back, her father had written a date and the words “river side, after the meeting.” Nia photographed both sides with permission. The professor promised to compare the date with city records rather than guess what the load contained. As they stepped outside, a pigeon landed on the café sign and shook rain from its wings. Eli joked that the bird had offered the most honest answer of the evening: it knew only where it had been. Nia laughed, then set a new goal for tomorrow. They would locate the missing meeting, not invent one.

離開前，瑟拉又拿出一張舊貨車送貨收據。印刷說明寫了某種建材，卻沒有清楚標示其物質成分。背面是父親手寫的日期，以及「會議後，河邊」幾個字。夏黎取得允許後拍下正反面。教授答應先比對市府紀錄，不會猜測貨車裝了什麼。眾人走出餐館時，一隻鴿子落在招牌上，抖掉翅膀上的雨水。以安笑說，這隻鳥給了今晚最誠實的回答：牠只知道自己去過哪裡。夏黎笑了，接著訂下明天的目標。他們要找到那場遺失的會議紀錄，而不是自己編出一場會議。
