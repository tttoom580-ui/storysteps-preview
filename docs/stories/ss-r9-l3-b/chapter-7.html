A Jar of Lilies

把海風留在最後一格 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Mina welcomed them into a modest brick house with a red lily growing beside the door. She was sixty-six, and a white vase stood on her kitchen shelf next to a jar filled with old coins. She had kept a dime from the year of the flood, she explained, because its date reminded her of the first time she helped in an emergency. “I cannot promise you a terrific story,” she told Nia. “I can tell you what I saw.” Nia felt awkward in the small room, surrounded by photographs of people she did not know. She made no attempt to record until Mina had read the consent sheet and placed it, unsigned, beside her tea.

米娜在一棟樸素紅磚屋迎接兩人，門旁種著一株紅色百合。她六十六歲，廚房架子上有一只白花瓶，旁邊玻璃罐裝著舊硬幣。她保存了一枚洪水當年的一角硬幣，因為日期讓她想起第一次參與緊急救援。「我不能保證給你精彩故事，」她對夏黎說，「只能告訴你我親眼見過的。」夏黎置身狹小的屋子，四周都是不認識的人物照片，難免感到尷尬。米娜讀完同意書，把尚未簽名的紙放在茶杯旁之前，夏黎完全沒有打算錄音。

Mina remembered an awful noise from the old gate building, followed by a warning that workers should leave the lower floor. She had been helping a neighbor invent a simple signal using a bell and a string. When the flood began, the bell was drowned by a deeper sound that made the cupboard doors shake. One man had tried to steady a frightened child while another carried boxes up the steps. “People sometimes tell this as if the tower exploded,” Mina said. “It did not. The doors were still there afterward.” Nia wrote down the correction. An exciting error could travel farther than a quiet fact, but that did not make it worth repeating.

米娜記得舊閘門建築傳出可怕的巨響，隨後有人警告工人離開低樓層。當時她正幫鄰居發明一個用鐘與細繩組成的簡易訊號。洪水開始時，鐘聲被更深沉的聲響蓋過，震得櫥櫃門不停搖晃。一名男子試著穩住受驚的孩子，另一人則將箱子搬上台階。「有人講得好像整座塔爆炸了，」米娜說，「其實沒有，後來那些門仍在。」夏黎記下這項更正。刺激的錯誤可能傳得比平淡的事實更遠，卻不因此值得重複。

After a while, Mina showed them a paper calendar with wage figures written along one edge. Her aunt had kept track of unpaid rescue work because families needed the money. A public appeal after the flood had promised fair payment, but the list of recipients was incomplete. Mina still had the original pay envelope. She had never thought of it as evidence. Nia asked permission to photograph the envelope, including the date, while hiding the home address. “Some people want to throw old papers into the junk,” Mina said. “I kept them because the people named there were real.” The remark made Nia think about how easily an archive could leave working lives out of a heroic story.

過了一會兒，米娜拿出一張紙月曆，邊緣寫著工資數字。她姑姑曾記下未付的救援工資，因為許多家庭需要那筆錢。洪水後的公開呼籲承諾公平支付，受款人名單卻不完整。米娜至今仍保存當年的薪資信封，從未把它當成證據。夏黎請求允許拍下信封和日期，同時遮去住家地址。「有人想把舊紙當垃圾扔掉，」米娜說，「我留下來，因為上面的人都真實存在。」這番話讓夏黎想到，檔案多麼容易把勞動生活排除在英雄故事之外。

Mina remembered that a newspaper had once used the headline “Did the tower explode?” She said the word explode had frightened children who had been safe at home. She kept the clipping to remind herself that exciting language could cause real harm. Nia asked for permission to quote that headline only as an example of an unverified claim, and Mina agreed.

米娜記得報紙曾以「閘塔是否爆炸？」作標題。她說「爆炸」這個詞嚇壞了許多原本平安待在家裡的孩子。她保存剪報，是要提醒自己刺激語言真的可能造成傷害。夏黎問能否只把標題當成未經查證說法的例子引用，米娜同意了。

Mina wanted the film to remind the town of the families, not only the machinery. She described an old market where vendors sold spicy soup and sweets with a surprising flavor. One cook had carried a pot to the rescue workers every afternoon. He had burned the flesh of his hand while trying to lift a hot lid, but recovered and returned the next day. Nia listened without turning the incident into a dramatic scene. She knew that the appeal of a story could tempt an editor to make a small injury look much worse. Mina smiled when Nia asked for the cook's name instead of more details about the burn.

米娜希望電影提醒全城記得那些家庭，而不只是機械設備。她描述舊市場裡販賣香辣湯和口味令人驚喜甜點的攤販。一位廚師每天下午都替救援工人送去一鍋熱食；某次抬起鍋蓋時燙傷手上的皮肉，休養後隔天仍回來。夏黎沒有把這件事變成聳動場景。她知道故事的吸引力會誘惑剪輯者，把小傷寫得嚴重得多。當夏黎詢問廚師姓名，而不是追問燙傷細節時，米娜笑了。

At last Mina signed permission for a private research recording. She did not yet approve public use. Before they left, she pointed to a loose wire behind the cupboard and asked Eli whether it was dangerous. He fixed the connection safely after turning off the power. “You repair things without making a show of it,” Mina said. “That is a useful skill.” Nia glanced at Eli and felt her face grow warm. She would have to remind herself sometime that gratitude was not the same as a promise of love. For now, she simply thanked him for helping and asked whether he could check the signal timing that afternoon.

最後米娜簽下私人研究錄音的同意書，仍未允許公開使用。離開前，她指著櫥櫃後鬆動的電線問以安是否危險。以安先關掉電源，再安全修好接點。「你修東西從來不大張旗鼓，」米娜說，「這很有用。」夏黎看了以安一眼，感到臉有些熱。她得找個時間提醒自己：感激並不等於愛情承諾。目前她只是謝謝以安，並問他下午能否幫忙核對警報間隔。

On the return ferry, Nia reviewed the notes. Mina had supplied a direct account of the sounds, a wage envelope, and names they could check, but no explanation of who ordered the gate test. That was still a gap. She folded the unsigned public-use section of the form and placed it separately from the research permission. Each document had a different purpose, and confusing them would harm the trust they were trying to build. Eli offered her a cup of water and asked about the lilies near Mina's door. Nia told him that her grandmother once grew the same flowers. She had not spoken about her family so freely in months.

回程渡輪上，夏黎重新讀筆記。米娜提供親耳聽見聲響的證言、薪資信封，以及可核對的姓名，卻沒有說明是誰下令測試閘門。這仍是一道缺口。夏黎把尚未簽署的公開使用條款折起，與研究許可分開放置。每份文件都有不同用途，若混淆就會破壞團隊正在建立的信任。以安遞給她水，問起米娜門邊的百合。夏黎說祖母從前也種同樣的花。她已經幾個月沒如此自然地聊過家人。
