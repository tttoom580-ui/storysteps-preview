A Budget and a Map

把海風留在最後一格 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The cinema board met beside a bowling hall on Monday morning. For a decade, its members had handled repairs with a small public budget and many unpaid hours. Nia arrived with photographs of Alma's tin, not the reel itself. She had learned that archives could become a beast when a collector wanted every object and forgot the people connected to it. A board member named Victor wore a bright necktie and spoke about the value of selling the projectors. He called Nia's plan to restore a single film emotional and expensive. She answered with an expressive drawing of the damaged strip, then set a careful estimate beside it. “We are asking for time to learn what this is,” she said, “not a blank check.”

星期一早晨，戲院董事會在保齡球館旁開會。十年來，成員們靠著一小筆公共預算和許多無償工時維持戲院。夏黎只帶了艾瑪鐵盒的照片，並沒有把片卷帶來。她知道，當收藏者想獨占所有物件卻忘記其背後的人，檔案館也可能變成一頭吞噬一切的野獸。戴著鮮豔領帶的董事維克多強調出售放映機的價值，批評夏黎修復單卷底片的計畫感情用事又昂貴。夏黎拿出一張表現清楚的底片損傷圖，旁邊附上審慎估算。「我們要的是弄清楚它的時間，」她說，「不是任意支用的空白支票。」

Victor asked whether anyone had been drunk when the film was shot and whether the old camera could be trusted. His questions were meant to frighten the younger volunteers, but Nia treated them as useful doubts. She explained that a picture needed other evidence: geography, weather reports, and a witness able to recognize the place. An elderly professor from the adult college offered to check maps without charging for his time. The meeting became less tense when he admitted that even experts could make mistakes. A voter from the neighborhood asked why the city had never held a public discussion about the cinema. Victor replied that the sale was legal. “Legal is not the same as understood,” the woman said.

維克多問，當年拍攝影片的人是否喝醉，以及老相機是否可信。他本想藉這些問題嚇退年輕志工，夏黎卻把質疑當成有用的提醒。她解釋，一張影像還需要其他證據：地理資料、天氣報告，以及認得拍攝地點的證人。成人學院的一位退休教授願意免費核對地圖。當他坦承專家也會犯錯時，會議氣氛才緩和一些。一名住在附近、具有投票權的居民問，為何市府從未公開討論戲院去留。維克多說出售合法。「合法不代表大家都理解了，」那名婦人回答。

The proposed repair kit cost less than the price of one new cinema seat. Eli had included cotton gloves, a low-power lamp, and soft strips of lace to cushion the tools, but not a machine that could pull damaged film too fast. A passenger on the ferry had once found a broken reel and tried to smooth it with an iron; the heat had destroyed the pictures. Nia shared that example so nobody would mistake speed for care. A member asked if the school could permit students to help during the next semester. The professor agreed to supervise research, provided students could refuse to join and receive credit for their actual work.

建議購買的修復工具組，比一張新戲院椅子的價格還低。以安列入棉手套、低功率照明與墊住工具的柔軟蕾絲布，卻沒有列入可能太快拉扯破損底片的機器。過去曾有渡輪乘客撿到壞片卷，用熨斗壓平，結果高溫把畫面全毀。夏黎舉這個例子，提醒大家別把速度當成謹慎。一位董事問學校能否允許下學期的學生協助。教授答應督導研究，條件是學生有權拒絕，參與者也必須因實際貢獻獲得署名。

Nia did not hide the risk. Restoring the strip might reveal nothing important, and the cinema could still close. She told the board that she would regret a careless public show more than a quiet failure. Victor claimed that only greedy outsiders wanted access to old city property. Alma, who had remained silent, stood and presented the permit for her original camera work. She had not sold her negatives to the property company, and she had never given permission to erase the end of a film. Her permit was visible under a clear plastic cover. The room fell still. The board voted to delay the sale of the film collection for thirty days while the ownership questions were checked.

夏黎沒有隱瞞風險。修復後可能什麼重要內容都看不到，戲院也可能照樣關閉。她告訴董事會，比起安靜的失敗，她更會後悔草率公開放映。維克多聲稱只有貪心的外來者才想接觸城市舊資產。一直安靜坐著的艾瑪站起來，拿出當年拍攝工作的許可證。她從未把底片賣給地產公司，也從未允許任何人刪掉片尾。許可證就放在透明塑膠套裡，清晰可見。會議室頓時安靜。董事會表決決定，先暫緩出售底片收藏三十天，調查權屬問題。

Outside, Eli and Nia walked past the bowling lanes as balls struck their targets. He wanted to celebrate, but Nia felt only the weight of a new responsibility. Thirty days sounded generous until she counted the hours needed to clean the film, compare the maps, and speak to people who remembered the flood. Eli asked whether she wanted him at the next meeting. “Yes, if you come as the person who knows the machines,” she answered. “Not as the person who speaks for me.” He accepted the difference without complaint. They bought two sandwiches and wrote a task list on the back of the board's notice. The first task was simple: find out when and where the last frames had been photographed.

走出會議室後，兩人經過保齡球道，聽見球撞倒球瓶的聲音。以安想慶祝，夏黎卻只感到新的責任。三十天聽起來很寬裕，但她算起清潔底片、比對地圖、訪問記得洪水的人所需的時間，便知道並不多。以安問她下次開會要不要自己陪同。「要，但請你以懂機器的技師身分來，」夏黎回答，「不要代替我說話。」以安沒有抱怨，接受了這個區別。兩人買了三明治，在公告背面寫下工作清單。第一件事很單純：查明最後幾格畫面到底在何時、何地拍攝。

That evening Nia called her mother, who worked at a small library inland. Her mother remembered the flood only through newspaper photographs and said that stories sometimes grew sharper over the years, not more accurate. Nia looked at the board's signed decision and understood that a short delay was not a victory over anyone. It was an opening through which careful questions could enter. She placed the original reel in its cabinet, set the key in an envelope, and left a second record for the next person on duty. If the truth eventually proved uncomfortable for her own side, she would need the same honest process she was demanding from Victor. That thought kept her awake longer than the noise of the harbor.

那天傍晚，夏黎打電話給在內陸小圖書館工作的母親。母親只從報紙照片認識那場洪水，也提醒她：故事可能隨著年代變得更鮮明，卻不一定更準確。夏黎望著董事會簽過名的決議，明白短暫延後並不是對誰的勝利，而是一道讓審慎提問進入的門。她把原片放回櫃子，將鑰匙裝進信封，再替下一位值勤者留下紀錄。若真相最後讓自己的陣營也感到難堪，她仍得遵守要求維克多遵守的誠實程序。這個念頭使她比港口的噪音更難入眠。
