船長留下的剪接

The Captain's Edit

把海風留在最後一格 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The retired captain lived beyond a narrow canyon on the road north of town. He was elderly but still capable of climbing the steps to his front porch without assistance. Nia had arranged the visit through his daughter, who was also his client in a small family boat business. On the table lay a monthly sailing calendar, a flashlight, and a toy sword that belonged to a visiting grandchild. The captain smiled at their careful questions. He remembered transporting workers to the river gate and showing them the hatch beneath the deck. “If you want information about the gate,” he said, “start with the times we actually sailed. The sea did not care what the city printed on a schedule.”

退休船長住在城北一條狹窄峽谷外的道路盡頭。他年事已高，仍有能力不靠攙扶走上前廊階梯。夏黎透過他的女兒安排拜訪，女兒同時是他在家庭小船生意裡的客戶。桌上放著每月航行日曆、手電筒，以及孫兒留下的玩具劍。船長對他們謹慎的提問露出笑容。他記得曾載工人去河閘，也帶他們看過甲板下的艙口。「如果想知道閘門的事，」他說，「先從船真正出航的時間開始。海不會在意市府行程表印了什麼。」

The captain placed his log beside the film stills. One entry described bubbles rising near the gate after a test, followed by a flash from a warning lamp. He could not say whether the bubbles came from water pressure or a damaged pipe. He had also kept a receipt for fuel and a note about the position of another boat. Nia recorded both details without assuming the captain had seen more than he claimed. The old man stepped aside to fetch a cup of tea, leaving his daughter to answer a question about ownership. The family held the logs as private property but would lend certified copies for the project. Nia thanked them for setting the terms so clearly.

船長把航行日誌擺在影片截圖旁。一條紀錄寫著測試後閘門附近冒起氣泡，接著警示燈閃了一下。他無法確定氣泡來自水壓還是破損管線。另外，他還保存燃料收據和另一艘船位置的便條。夏黎沒有假設船長看見比他宣稱更多的事，只記下細節。老人走到旁邊倒茶時，由女兒回答資料所有權的問題。家人把日誌視為私人財產，卻願提供認證副本給研究計畫。夏黎謝謝他們把條件說得如此清楚。

At lunch, the daughter served bread with a strong spice mixture from her garden. Nia asked the captain whether he had ever seen the edited festival film. He nodded. Years ago, the city had hired an editor to represent the flood story in a short visitor video. The captain remembered feeling hopeful when he first heard about it, then disappointed when the final version showed the wrong boat beside the gate. “A picture can tell the truth about a place and still give the wrong impression about a moment,” he said. The words helped Nia understand that correcting the public record might require more than replacing a missing scene. It might require rebuilding the order of scenes entirely.

午餐時，女兒端來麵包和自家花園製成的濃香料醬。夏黎問船長是否看過剪輯後的節慶影片。船長點頭。多年前市府曾雇一位剪輯師，替遊客短片呈現洪水故事。他起初充滿希望，看到最終版本把錯誤船隻放在閘門旁時卻失望了。「畫面可以真實呈現一個地方，卻仍讓人誤解某個時刻，」他說。這讓夏黎理解，修正公共紀錄也許不只是補回遺失片段，而是必須重新建立場景的正確順序。

The captain showed Nia a photograph of a single bubble beneath the gate. A handwritten note said that electricity had failed in a nearby pump room that day. The photograph did not prove the two events were connected. Nia made separate cards for the bubble, the power failure, and the date, so later viewers could test the relationship for themselves.

船長給夏黎看一張照片，閘門下方有一個氣泡。手寫便條說，當天附近抽水機房曾停電。照片不能證明兩件事有關。夏黎替氣泡、電力故障與日期各做一張卡，讓後來的人能自行查驗其關係。

The captain's daughter brought out a letter from the original editor. It asked the family to approve a more dramatic arrangement, but the letter had never been answered. Nia felt a sudden sadness. She could not know whether silence meant refusal, forgetfulness, or a letter that had arrived too late. She asked permission to copy it and promised to show the uncertainty in her film. The captain looked down at the paper. “I wanted to weep when I watched that old visitor video,” he said, “not because the picture was ugly, but because it made my crew seem careless.” Nia decided that any new edit would have to protect the difference between an honest mistake and unsupported blame.

船長的女兒又拿出原剪輯師寫來的信，信中要求家人同意將畫面編排得更戲劇化，但家人從未回信。夏黎感到一陣難過。她無法知道沉默代表拒絕、忘記，還是信來得太晚。她請求複印，並答應在電影裡顯示這項不確定。船長低頭望著信。「我當年看遊客影片時幾乎想哭，」他說，「不是因為畫面難看，而是它讓我的船員看起來像一群粗心的人。」夏黎決定，任何新剪輯都必須守住誠實錯誤與缺乏根據指責之間的區別。

On the drive back, Jo sent a message about a new situation. A property agent had asked the cinema board to hand over all copies of the film for a private sale. Nia pulled over and read the message again. The board could not give away material that the family still owned, but it might have the power to sell the projectors and building. She called the clerk and requested written confirmation of the current agreement. The agent's demand was urgent, yet speed would not improve her answer. She set the phone aside and told Eli that they needed a plan for keeping copies safe while avoiding an argument they could not win by shouting.

開車回程時，小喬傳來新的狀況：一名地產仲介要求戲院董事會交出所有底片副本，以供私人出售。夏黎靠邊停車，再讀了一遍訊息。董事會無權送走仍由家人持有的材料，卻可能有權出售放映機和建築。她打電話請職員以書面確認現有協議。仲介的要求雖然急迫，但速度不會讓答案變得更好。她放下手機，告訴以安必須想辦法保護副本，也不要陷入單靠喊叫無法贏得的爭論。

That evening, the professor helped them draft a list of each reel and its owner. No item could be moved without a signed entry. Nia also began to edit a short explanation of what they had learned, intended for a private meeting with the families. She wanted people to understand why the next steps mattered before strangers started speaking for them. Eli made a cup of tea and sat nearby, checking the old projector manual. They worked in comfortable silence for a long time. When Nia finally looked up, she realized that being with him did not make her feel less independent. It made the work seem possible without having to pretend she could carry every burden alone.

那晚，教授幫他們起草清單，列出每卷底片和各自所有人。任何材料移動都必須簽名。夏黎也開始剪一段簡短說明，準備在家屬私人會議中解釋研究進展。她希望大家在陌生人代替他們發言之前，先明白下一步為何重要。以安泡了杯茶，坐在旁邊核對老放映機說明書。兩人很久都沒有說話，卻並不尷尬。夏黎抬頭時忽然發現，和以安在一起並沒有讓她更不獨立；反而讓她不必假裝所有負擔都能自己扛，工作也因此變得可能。
