The Alley Negative

把海風留在最後一格 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An old address led Nia to a narrow alley behind a closed bakery. A cherry tree grew beside its wall, and someone had tied a knitted ribbon to the lowest branch. She had come to investigate the missing meeting, not the tree, but the place helped her understand the film. The bakery had once stood beside the river path. From its back door, workers could see the floodgate tower. Sera remembered her father making tea there for tired repair crews. Nia felt dizzy when she compared the modern street with the old map; an entire lane had vanished beneath a parking building. She wrote the change down instead of trusting her memory of the walk.

一個舊地址帶夏黎走進歇業麵包店後方的狹窄巷子。牆邊長著一棵櫻桃樹，有人把編織絲帶繫在最低的樹枝上。她此行是為了調查遺失的會議紀錄，而不是看樹，但這個地方幫助她理解影像。麵包店以前就在河岸步道旁，工人從後門就能看到防洪閘塔。瑟拉記得父親會在那裡替疲憊的維修工人泡茶。夏黎對照現今街道和舊圖時一陣暈眩：整條小路已經被停車大樓覆蓋。她把差異記下來，不打算只憑行走的記憶推斷。

A retired mechanic named Tom met them with a cardboard box. He had kept a negative from the bakery's window camera, but a cable had damaged one edge during a storm. Tom said a crash of metal gates had woken him that night. He would not call it murder, although a neighbor had later died in the flood. “Those are different claims,” he said firmly. “One is a sound I heard. The other would need proof.” Nia admired his bravery in making that difference. She had heard people use terrible words whenever they wanted attention. Here was a man who refused to exaggerate even when his memories still hurt.

退休技師湯姆帶著一個紙盒來見他們。他保留了一張從麵包店窗邊攝影機取下的負片，但暴風雨中一條電纜曾刮壞底片邊緣。湯姆說，當晚金屬閘門碰撞的巨響把他吵醒。他不願將此稱作謀殺，雖然一名鄰居後來確實在洪水中喪生。「那是兩種不同的主張，」他堅定地說，「我能證明自己聽見聲音，另一件事則需要證據。」夏黎敬佩他分清差別的勇氣。她聽過太多人為了引人注意，隨口使用可怕的字眼；眼前這人即使記憶仍然疼痛，也不願誇大。

They carried the box to Tom's kitchen, where a saucer of biscuits stood beside a small lamp. His sister had come for a nap after the morning market and woke when she heard them talking. She pointed to a label on the paper sleeve: “Gate test, June 12.” Tom had thought the writing was his father's, but it was clearly a different hand. The date was solid enough to check against official records. Whether the photograph showed a legal test was another question. A glossy advertisement on the kitchen wall called the new apartments “sexy urban living.” Nia found the phrase strange beside Tom's plain memories of a street that no longer existed.

他們把紙盒帶進湯姆的廚房，桌上的茶碟裝著餅乾，旁邊亮著小燈。湯姆的妹妹逛完早市後來小睡，聽見談話便醒過來。她指著紙套上的標籤：「六月十二日，閘門測試。」湯姆原以為那是父親的字，但筆跡明顯不同。日期本身足夠明確，可以拿去查官方紀錄；照片是否顯示合法測試，仍是另一個問題。廚房牆上的亮面廣告把新公寓稱作「性感都會生活」。夏黎覺得這句話與湯姆樸素描述那條消失街道的記憶擺在一起，格外奇怪。

Nia asked Sera what the word alley meant on the oldest town plan. Its legal definition included a path to the river, although the modern city map showed only a service road. Each spring, local women still knit colored bands for the cherry tree in memory of lost neighbors. The ribbon was not proof of the gate test, but it showed that the place had never lost its meaning to the families.

夏黎問瑟拉，最舊城鎮圖上的「巷子」究竟指什麼。其法律定義包括一條通往河邊的小徑，但現代地圖只畫出服務道路。每年春天，附近婦女仍會編織彩帶繫在櫻桃樹上，紀念失去的鄰居。絲帶不能證明閘門測試，卻顯示這個地方從未失去對家屬的意義。

On the way back, they passed the old tower. Its locked door carried a warning that anyone entering without food or water could starve if trapped during a storm. The sign seemed excessive, yet the building had no working lift and almost no light. Nia felt thankful that Tom had stayed outside rather than trying to prove his story by breaking in. Eli photographed the rusted lock without touching it. A municipal van stopped nearby, and the driver asked whether they were planning to climb the fence. Nia showed their written permission to inspect public maps and explained that they were only comparing exterior views. The driver nodded and moved on.

回程時，他們經過舊閘塔。上鎖的門上貼著警告：暴風雨時若有人受困其中，又沒有食物飲水，甚至可能挨餓至危及生命。警告看似誇張，但那棟樓沒有可用的升降設備，照明也幾乎全壞。夏黎很感謝湯姆沒有為了證明故事就闖進建築。以安只拍下生鏽的鎖，沒有碰觸。一輛市政貨車在旁停下，駕駛問他們是不是打算翻越圍欄。夏黎出示查閱公共地圖的書面許可，說明兩人只是比對建物外觀。駕駛點頭後離開。

Back in the cinema, Nia compared Tom's negative with Alma's film. The same tower window appeared in both. Its shape gave the team a fixed point, and the distance between two marks suggested that the camera had stood much closer to the river than they first believed. Eli warned that an old lens could bend straight lines, so the estimate needed a second method. Nia agreed. She could feel the warmth of the projector lamp on her hands as she marked the two images. The meeting was still missing, but the physical place of the mystery was becoming clearer. That was enough progress for one day, and she allowed herself to feel it.

回到戲院，夏黎把湯姆的負片與艾瑪的底片並排比較。同一扇閘塔窗戶出現在兩張影像裡，形狀提供固定參照點；兩個標記間的距離也顯示，攝影機可能比他們原先以為的更靠近河道。以安提醒，舊鏡頭可能把直線拍彎，因此估算還需要另一種方法。夏黎同意。她一邊標註影像，一邊感受放映燈落在手上的溫暖。那場會議依舊找不到，但謎團發生的實際位置越來越清楚。這已是一天裡足夠的進展，她允許自己為此感到欣慰。

Before closing, Nia wrote Tom a short note explaining exactly how his negative would be used. The photograph belonged to him; their copy would be marked “research only” until he decided otherwise. She had once believed that a good investigation was simply the fastest route to a surprising fact. Now she saw how many small decisions stood between finding evidence and using it well. Eli asked if she wanted company on the walk home. She said yes, but only after finishing the entry sheet. He waited beside the door and listened to the rain without rushing her. When she finally joined him, the alley looked ordinary again, and that was strangely comforting.

閉館前，夏黎寫了一張短箋給湯姆，清楚說明負片會如何使用。照片仍屬於湯姆，團隊的副本在他另行決定前只能標示「研究用途」。她以前以為好的調查只是最快找出驚人事實的道路，如今才看見，從找到證據到妥善使用之間，還有多少細小決定。以安問她回家路上要不要有人陪。她說好，但得先填完入庫紀錄。他在門邊等著，聽雨聲而不催促。等夏黎終於走到他身旁時，那條巷子看起來又普通了，反而令人安心。
