沒有水的噴泉

The False Fountain

轉學考的最後一題是生存 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Rain, dust, and distant voices made the city's public fountain square feel smaller than it was. Gu Wei arrived with a plan, then saw a detail that the plan had not accounted for. A map promised drinking water at a fountain whose pipes had been disconnected years earlier. The difference between guessing and checking suddenly seemed enormous. A person who sounded brave could still be mistaken; a person who admitted uncertainty could still save the group. Gu Wei chose the second kind of courage. The disputed notice used the word accidental, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for barrier, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. Gu Wei asked whether the team could blink under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. Someone had marked the warning brutal; Xia Li checked whether that description was accurate.

雨聲、灰塵與遠處的人聲，讓城市公共噴泉廣場顯得格外狹窄。顧唯帶著計畫抵達，卻看見計畫沒有考慮到的細節。地圖標示噴泉可取飲水，但管線多年前就已拆除。猜測和查證的差別突然變得很大。聽起來勇敢的人也可能犯錯，敢承認不確定的人反而可能救下整個團隊。顧唯決定選擇後者。有爭議的通知使用「意外的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「柵欄」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。顧唯詢問小隊能否依安全規則「眨眼」，答案仍不明朗。有人把警告標成「粗獷而堅固的」，夏黎則查證這種描述是否準確。

Xia Li brought the next piece of information on a sheet that had been folded several times. Xia Li tested the remaining tap and found warning signs of unsafe water. The team compared its date with what they had witnessed for themselves. One line was persuasive; another contradicted the first. Gu Wei asked for the original source instead of selecting whichever version made their work easier. Even a genuine clue could be dangerous when removed from its context. One witness promised to choke if conditions improved, but Gu Wei would not treat hope as evidence. The working file contained a separate entry for composition, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. Gu Wei asked whether the team could evaluate under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. A witness mentioned gratitude during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

夏黎把下一份資料帶來，紙張已被折過好幾次。夏黎檢查剩餘水龍頭，發現水質不安全的警告跡象。小隊把上面的日期與親眼所見逐項比較。一行文字很有說服力，下一行卻與它矛盾。顧唯沒有挑比較方便的版本，而是要求查看原始來源。即使是真的線索，一旦脫離脈絡也可能造成危險。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「阻斷／堵住」，顧唯卻不能把希望當成證據。工作檔案為「結構；組成」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。顧唯詢問小隊能否依安全規則「評估」，答案仍不明朗。見證人在訪談中提到「感激」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

An argument broke out before anyone could announce a solution. Two applicants accused Gu Wei of wasting time when he refused to let anyone drink. Gu Wei did not win it with a long speech. Instead, the team drew two columns: what the record established and what remained uncertain. Proctor Shen challenged their interpretation, which was uncomfortable but useful. A plan that collapsed under one honest question was not ready to protect anyone outside the room. The evidence list included grave, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for intelligence, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. The report called the arrangement mild, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. Someone had marked the warning mountainous; Xia Li checked whether that description was accurate.

尚未有人宣布解法，爭論便已爆發。兩名考生責怪顧唯耽誤時間，因為他不讓大家喝水。顧唯沒有用長篇演說壓倒對方，而是在紙上分成兩欄：紀錄已證實的事，以及仍然不確定的事。沈監考質疑他們的解讀，雖然令人不快，卻也有用。禁不起一次誠實提問的計畫，還不能拿去保護門外的人。證物清單上有「嚴重的」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「情報／智能」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「溫和的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。有人把警告標成「山區的」，夏黎則查證這種描述是否準確。

The sealed city looked like a game from a high tower. From the street, it smelled of wet walls, food cooking behind windows, and people who had never volunteered to become obstacles in a student's test. Gu Wei began to understand that a city could not be reduced to a puzzle board. Xia Li asked him to keep a record of the residents' own requests rather than deciding what they needed from the outside.

從高塔看，封閉城市像一場遊戲；走在街上，卻有潮濕牆壁的氣味、窗後的飯香，還有從未同意成為考試障礙的居民。顧唯逐漸理解，一座城市不能縮成解謎棋盤。夏黎要他記下居民自己提出的需求，而不是由外來考生替他們決定。

Nothing in the trial worked exactly as predicted. The group searched a municipal maintenance record and located a verified refill station. Gu Wei insisted on keeping the unsuccessful measurements instead of replacing them with cleaner numbers. That choice annoyed Proctor Shen, who wanted a quick announcement. But the failure established a boundary they had only guessed at before. A useful boundary was better than a promise built on wishful thinking. The evidence list included neglect, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. A written instruction told the staff to offend before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. Xia Li found a reference to philosopher in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned reform during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

這次測試沒有一項完全照預測發展。小隊查找市政維護紀錄，找到已核實的補水站。顧唯堅持留下不成功的測量值，不用好看的數字替換。沈監考急著發公告，因此相當不滿；然而失敗讓他們找到了先前只能猜測的限制。清楚的界線，總比建立在一廂情願上的承諾可靠。證物清單上有「疏忽」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「冒犯」，卻沒有人記下結果。夏黎在舊筆記裡找到關於「哲學家」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「改革」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

When evening arrived, the immediate danger had changed shape rather than vanished. The official exam map still directed late arrivals toward the broken fountain. Gu Wei gathered the others and repeated only the facts they could support. Some people would need more time. Others had already decided to leave. Neither choice made anyone less worthy of protection. The next step belonged to those who would live with its consequences, not merely to the person speaking loudest. The evidence list included revenge, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for tendency, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. Xia Li found a reference to twig in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned urged during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

傍晚到來時，眼前的危險只是改變形態，並未完全消失。官方考試地圖卻仍把遲到者引向壞掉的噴泉。顧唯召集同伴，只重述有證據支持的事。有人還需要時間，也有人已決定離開；這兩種選擇都不會使任何人失去受保護的資格。下一步應由承擔後果的人決定，而不是交給聲音最大的人。證物清單上有「報復」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「傾向」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。夏黎在舊筆記裡找到關於「樹枝」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「催促」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。
