失分者的避難所

A Shelter Built by Losers

The day began inside the old municipal library. Gu Wei had come expecting an ordinary task, but the room already contained the sort of silence that follows bad news. Students who had lost points while helping others began turning the library into a shared shelter. Nobody could afford to confuse a confident explanation with a reliable observation. They had made that mistake before, and the cost was still visible in the people waiting outside. Gu Wei opened the notebook and decided to write down what could actually be tested. A witness mentioned acceptance during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The disputed notice used the word ambitious, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for atmosphere, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. The report called the arrangement authentic, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof.

這一天從舊市立圖書館開始。顧唯原以為只是處理例行工作，房間卻靜得像剛傳出壞消息。曾因幫人而被扣分的考生，開始把圖書館整理成共同避難所。他們不能把說得肯定的解釋誤當成可靠觀察。大家以前犯過這種錯，代價仍寫在門外那些等待者的表情上。顧唯打開筆記本，決定只記下真正能夠驗證的事情。見證人在訪談中提到「接受；同意」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。有爭議的通知使用「有企圖心的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「氣氛」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「真實可靠的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。

A new message reached Xia Li while the group was still checking its first result. Xia Li found building plans proving that a side room could be heated without overloading the wiring. Gu Wei refused to celebrate too early. They had learned that two records could repeat the same lie when both came from a single office. Before treating the discovery as independent evidence, the group searched for a witness who had nothing to gain by agreeing with them. The old manual told its readers to classify, and Xia Li wanted to know who had written that rule. One witness promised to differ if conditions improved, but Gu Wei would not treat hope as evidence. The working file contained a separate entry for dignity, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. Gu Wei asked whether the team could embarrass under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear.

小隊還在核對第一項結果時，夏黎就收到新的消息。夏黎找到建築圖，證明側室能供暖且不會使電線超載。顧唯不肯太早慶祝。他們已學到，兩份紀錄如果來自同一辦公室，也可能只是重複同一個謊言。要把發現視為獨立證據前，大家先去尋找一個不會因附和他們而得利的見證人。舊手冊要求讀者「分類」，夏黎想知道這條規則由誰制定。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「有所不同」，顧唯卻不能把希望當成證據。工作檔案為「尊嚴」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。顧唯詢問小隊能否依安全規則「使人難堪」，答案仍不明朗。

The easiest choice would have made Gu Wei popular for a single evening. Proctor Shen threatened to close the shelter as unauthorized cooperation. Xia Li reminded the group that their decision would still matter tomorrow, after the applause and anger faded. Nobody could offer a guarantee that every person would approve. They could, however, make the reasons public and leave a safe way for a witness to disagree. A witness mentioned facility during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The disputed notice used the word genuine, but its author had not signed the page. An inspector described the situation as offensive; Gu Wei wanted measurements instead of adjectives. Xia Li found a reference to option in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

最省事的選擇，或許能讓顧唯當晚受到歡呼。沈監考威脅關閉避難所，理由是未授權合作。夏黎提醒大家，掌聲與怒氣消失後，決定明天仍然會造成影響。沒有人能保證所有人都滿意，但至少可以公開理由，並留下見證人安全表達反對的管道。見證人在訪談中提到「設施」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。有爭議的通知使用「真實的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。檢查員把情況形容為「惡意的」，顧唯卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。夏黎在舊筆記裡找到關於「選項」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

In the world outside the gates, an examination normally asked students to solve problems printed on paper. Here the paper itself could give unsafe instructions. Gu Wei thought about the people who had trusted a map simply because it bore the academy's stamp. Xia Li said that questioning a rule did not mean refusing to learn. Sometimes it was the most important evidence that learning had begun. They carried that thought into the next task.

校門外的考試通常要學生解答紙上的問題，在這裡，紙張本身卻可能給出不安全的命令。顧唯想起那些只因地圖印著校章便相信它的人。夏黎說質疑規則不等於拒絕學習，有時反而是學習真正開始的證據。他們帶著這想法迎向下一項任務。

They agreed to test a limited part of the plan before touching the main system. Gu Wei submitted a public safety request bearing the signatures of students and local residents. Gu Wei assigned roles according to practical skill rather than confidence. Xia Li recorded each step, including the moments when someone had to stop. If the trial failed, the notes would explain why. If it worked, the notes would help the next group repeat it without depending on a hero who happened to be present. A witness mentioned perfume during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included promotion, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for publisher, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. Xia Li found a reference to refugee in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

他們同意先做小範圍測試，不直接碰主要系統。顧唯提交學生與當地居民共同簽署的安全申請。顧唯依照實際專長分派工作，不看誰說話最有自信。夏黎記錄每個步驟，連必須停止的時刻也不漏掉。如果測試失敗，筆記能說明原因；如果成功，下一批人也能按紀錄重做，不必依賴恰好在場的英雄。見證人在訪談中提到「香水」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「行銷／推廣」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「出版社」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。夏黎在舊筆記裡找到關於「難民」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

The result was smaller than the promise they had hoped to make, but it was real. The shelter stayed open, and its register recorded more lives protected than any scoreboard. Xia Li closed the notebook only after marking the unresolved questions. Gu Wei was tired of victories that depended on hiding their cost. This one could be examined in daylight. And the detail they had not solved was already pointing toward the next part of the investigation. A witness mentioned rescue during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included resignation, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for stockings, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. Gu Wei asked whether the team could transform under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear.

結果比原先希望宣布的成果小，卻是真的。避難所保留下來，登記簿保護的人數超過任何排行榜。夏黎直到標出所有未解問題，才闔上筆記本。顧唯早已厭倦靠隱瞞代價換來的勝利；這次的結果可以在日光下接受查證。那個尚未解開的細節，也已指向調查的下一步。見證人在訪談中提到「救援」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「辭職；辭職信」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「絲襪；長襪」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。顧唯詢問小隊能否依安全規則「轉變」，答案仍不明朗。
