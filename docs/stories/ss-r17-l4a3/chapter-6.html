Rain, dust, and distant voices made the closed city's morning market feel smaller than it was. Gu Wei arrived with a plan, then saw a detail that the plan had not accounted for. On the second day, food sellers accepted exam points instead of money and charged more for drinking water. The difference between guessing and checking suddenly seemed enormous. A person who sounded brave could still be mistaken; a person who admitted uncertainty could still save the group. Gu Wei chose the second kind of courage. A witness mentioned anniversary during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. One witness promised to assure if conditions improved, but Gu Wei would not treat hope as evidence. The working file contained a separate entry for campaign, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. The report called the arrangement coarse, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof.

雨聲、灰塵與遠處的人聲，讓封閉城市早市顯得格外狹窄。顧唯帶著計畫抵達，卻看見計畫沒有考慮到的細節。第二天，市場攤商只收考試分數，而且飲水更貴。猜測和查證的差別突然變得很大。聽起來勇敢的人也可能犯錯，敢承認不確定的人反而可能救下整個團隊。顧唯決定選擇後者。見證人在訪談中提到「周年紀念日」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「保證」，顧唯卻不能把希望當成證據。工作檔案為「活動」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「粗糙的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。

Xia Li brought the next piece of information on a sheet that had been folded several times. Xia Li noticed the price board had been revised after poor students arrived. The team compared its date with what they had witnessed for themselves. One line was persuasive; another contradicted the first. Gu Wei asked for the original source instead of selecting whichever version made their work easier. Even a genuine clue could be dangerous when removed from its context. The old manual told its readers to complicate, and Xia Li wanted to know who had written that rule. The evidence list included conductor, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for cord, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. The report called the arrangement courageous, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof.

夏黎把下一份資料帶來，紙張已被折過好幾次。夏黎發現貧困考生抵達後，價目表遭到更改。小隊把上面的日期與親眼所見逐項比較。一行文字很有說服力，下一行卻與它矛盾。顧唯沒有挑比較方便的版本，而是要求查看原始來源。即使是真的線索，一旦脫離脈絡也可能造成危險。舊手冊要求讀者「使複雜化」，夏黎想知道這條規則由誰制定。證物清單上有「列車長／導體」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「纜繩」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「勇敢的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。

An argument broke out before anyone could announce a solution. Proctor Shen claimed scarcity was fair because everyone had entered with the same number of points. Gu Wei did not win it with a long speech. Instead, the team drew two columns: what the record established and what remained uncertain. Proctor Shen challenged their interpretation, which was uncomfortable but useful. A plan that collapsed under one honest question was not ready to protect anyone outside the room. The old manual told its readers to devise, and Xia Li wanted to know who had written that rule. The disputed notice used the word diligent, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for dynasty, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. Xia Li found a reference to evaluation in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

尚未有人宣布解法，爭論便已爆發。沈監考聲稱人人起始分數一樣，稀缺就很公平。顧唯沒有用長篇演說壓倒對方，而是在紙上分成兩欄：紀錄已證實的事，以及仍然不確定的事。沈監考質疑他們的解讀，雖然令人不快，卻也有用。禁不起一次誠實提問的計畫，還不能拿去保護門外的人。舊手冊要求讀者「想出」，夏黎想知道這條規則由誰制定。有爭議的通知使用「勤勉的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「世代；王朝」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。夏黎在舊筆記裡找到關於「評估」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

The sealed city looked like a game from a high tower. From the street, it smelled of wet walls, food cooking behind windows, and people who had never volunteered to become obstacles in a student's test. Gu Wei began to understand that a city could not be reduced to a puzzle board. Xia Li asked him to keep a record of the residents' own requests rather than deciding what they needed from the outside.

從高塔看，封閉城市像一場遊戲；走在街上，卻有潮濕牆壁的氣味、窗後的飯香，還有從未同意成為考試障礙的居民。顧唯逐漸理解，一座城市不能縮成解謎棋盤。夏黎要他記下居民自己提出的需求，而不是由外來考生替他們決定。

Nothing in the trial worked exactly as predicted. Gu Wei organized a voluntary food-sharing record and challenged the changed prices. Gu Wei insisted on keeping the unsuccessful measurements instead of replacing them with cleaner numbers. That choice annoyed Proctor Shen, who wanted a quick announcement. But the failure established a boundary they had only guessed at before. A useful boundary was better than a promise built on wishful thinking. A witness mentioned foam during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included frequency, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for imitation, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. Xia Li found a reference to labor in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

這次測試沒有一項完全照預測發展。顧唯建立自願食物分享紀錄，質疑被修改的價格。顧唯堅持留下不成功的測量值，不用好看的數字替換。沈監考急著發公告，因此相當不滿；然而失敗讓他們找到了先前只能猜測的限制。清楚的界線，總比建立在一廂情願上的承諾可靠。見證人在訪談中提到「泡沫」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「頻率」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「模仿」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。夏黎在舊筆記裡找到關於「體力勞動」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

When evening arrived, the immediate danger had changed shape rather than vanished. The market manager admitted the prices were controlled from outside the sealed city. Gu Wei gathered the others and repeated only the facts they could support. Some people would need more time. Others had already decided to leave. Neither choice made anyone less worthy of protection. The next step belonged to those who would live with its consequences, not merely to the person speaking loudest. A witness mentioned mineral during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The disputed notice used the word needy, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for psychology, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. Gu Wei asked whether the team could reflect under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear.

傍晚到來時，眼前的危險只是改變形態，並未完全消失。市場管理員承認價格由封閉城市外部控制。顧唯召集同伴，只重述有證據支持的事。有人還需要時間，也有人已決定離開；這兩種選擇都不會使任何人失去受保護的資格。下一步應由承擔後果的人決定，而不是交給聲音最大的人。見證人在訪談中提到「礦物粉末中的礦物」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。有爭議的通知使用「需要幫助的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「心理學；心理」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。顧唯詢問小隊能否依安全規則「反射；反思」，答案仍不明朗。
