The day began inside a deserted cinema complex. Gu Wei had come expecting an ordinary task, but the room already contained the sort of silence that follows bad news. One student vanished after entering a cinema advertising a shortcut to the examination center. Nobody could afford to confuse a confident explanation with a reliable observation. They had made that mistake before, and the cost was still visible in the people waiting outside. Gu Wei opened the notebook and decided to write down what could actually be tested. The evidence list included accuracy, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. A written instruction told the staff to adapt before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. Gu Wei asked whether the team could analyze under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. A witness mentioned competitor during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

這一天從廢棄電影院開始。顧唯原以為只是處理例行工作，房間卻靜得像剛傳出壞消息。一名學生進入宣傳捷徑的電影院後消失。他們不能把說得肯定的解釋誤當成可靠觀察。大家以前犯過這種錯，代價仍寫在門外那些等待者的表情上。顧唯打開筆記本，決定只記下真正能夠驗證的事情。證物清單上有「準確度」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「調整」，卻沒有人記下結果。顧唯詢問小隊能否依安全規則「分析」，答案仍不明朗。見證人在訪談中提到「競爭者」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

A new message reached Xia Li while the group was still checking its first result. Xia Li discovered a service elevator that moved only when the film projector was running. Gu Wei refused to celebrate too early. They had learned that two records could repeat the same lie when both came from a single office. Before treating the discovery as independent evidence, the group searched for a witness who had nothing to gain by agreeing with them. The evidence list included diploma, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for frost, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. Xia Li found a reference to helicopter in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned herd during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

小隊還在核對第一項結果時，夏黎就收到新的消息。夏黎發現一座只有放映機啟動時才運行的貨梯。顧唯不肯太早慶祝。他們已學到，兩份紀錄如果來自同一辦公室，也可能只是重複同一個謊言。要把發現視為獨立證據前，大家先去尋找一個不會因附和他們而得利的見證人。證物清單上有「文憑」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「霜凍」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。夏黎在舊筆記裡找到關於「直升機」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「羊群」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

The easiest choice would have made Gu Wei popular for a single evening. Proctor Shen refused to report the disappearance because the student was officially marked as withdrawn. Xia Li reminded the group that their decision would still matter tomorrow, after the applause and anger faded. Nobody could offer a guarantee that every person would approve. They could, however, make the reasons public and leave a safe way for a witness to disagree. One witness promised to injure if conditions improved, but Gu Wei would not treat hope as evidence. An inspector described the situation as intense; Gu Wei wanted measurements instead of adjectives. Xia Li found a reference to mule in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. Someone had marked the warning mutual; Xia Li checked whether that description was accurate.

最省事的選擇，或許能讓顧唯當晚受到歡呼。沈監考拒絕通報失蹤，因為系統已把學生標成退考。夏黎提醒大家，掌聲與怒氣消失後，決定明天仍然會造成影響。沒有人能保證所有人都滿意，但至少可以公開理由，並留下見證人安全表達反對的管道。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「使受傷」，顧唯卻不能把希望當成證據。檢查員把情況形容為「激烈的」，顧唯卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。夏黎在舊筆記裡找到關於「騾子」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。有人把警告標成「共同的」，夏黎則查證這種描述是否準確。

In the world outside the gates, an examination normally asked students to solve problems printed on paper. Here the paper itself could give unsafe instructions. Gu Wei thought about the people who had trusted a map simply because it bore the academy's stamp. Xia Li said that questioning a rule did not mean refusing to learn. Sometimes it was the most important evidence that learning had begun. They carried that thought into the next task.

校門外的考試通常要學生解答紙上的問題，在這裡，紙張本身卻可能給出不安全的命令。顧唯想起那些只因地圖印著校章便相信它的人。夏黎說質疑規則不等於拒絕學習，有時反而是學習真正開始的證據。他們帶著這想法迎向下一項任務。

They agreed to test a limited part of the plan before touching the main system. Gu Wei contacted emergency staff through the separate battery-powered platform clock system. Gu Wei assigned roles according to practical skill rather than confidence. Xia Li recorded each step, including the moments when someone had to stop. If the trial failed, the notes would explain why. If it worked, the notes would help the next group repeat it without depending on a hero who happened to be present. One witness promised to oppose if conditions improved, but Gu Wei would not treat hope as evidence. The working file contained a separate entry for percent, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. Xia Li found a reference to plumber in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned reduction during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

他們同意先做小範圍測試，不直接碰主要系統。顧唯透過月台獨立電池系統聯絡緊急工作人員。顧唯依照實際專長分派工作，不看誰說話最有自信。夏黎記錄每個步驟，連必須停止的時刻也不漏掉。如果測試失敗，筆記能說明原因；如果成功，下一批人也能按紀錄重做，不必依賴恰好在場的英雄。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「反對」，顧唯卻不能把希望當成證據。工作檔案為「百分比」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。夏黎在舊筆記裡找到關於「水管工」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「減少」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

The result was smaller than the promise they had hoped to make, but it was real. The missing student answered from a locked screening room, alive but frightened. Xia Li closed the notebook only after marking the unresolved questions. Gu Wei was tired of victories that depended on hiding their cost. This one could be examined in daylight. And the detail they had not solved was already pointing toward the next part of the investigation. The disputed notice used the word remarkable, but its author had not signed the page. An inspector described the situation as slight; Gu Wei wanted measurements instead of adjectives. Xia Li found a reference to surroundings in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned welfare during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

結果比原先希望宣布的成果小，卻是真的。消失的考生從上鎖放映室裡回應，活著卻十分驚恐。夏黎直到標出所有未解問題，才闔上筆記本。顧唯早已厭倦靠隱瞞代價換來的勝利；這次的結果可以在日光下接受查證。那個尚未解開的細節，也已指向調查的下一步。有爭議的通知使用「不尋常的；顯著的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。檢查員把情況形容為「微小的」，顧唯卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。夏黎在舊筆記裡找到關於「周遭環境」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「福利；福祉」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。
