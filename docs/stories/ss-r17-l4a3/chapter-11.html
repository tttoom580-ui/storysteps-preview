穿學生外套的老師

The Teacher in a Student's Coat

轉學考的最後一題是生存 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

At a transit inspection checkpoint, the first sign of trouble was not a scream or an alarm. It was a small disagreement about something everyone believed they understood. One supposed applicant knew the exact location of every emergency door and avoided answering age questions. Gu Wei listened to the surrounding voices before making a judgment. Rumors traveled faster than careful questions, especially when the public wanted a simple villain. The team needed a record that would remain useful even if their favorite explanation proved false. One witness promised to accuse if conditions improved, but Gu Wei would not treat hope as evidence. The working file contained a separate entry for assurance, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. Gu Wei asked whether the team could boast under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. A witness mentioned commerce during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

在運輸稽查站，麻煩不是從尖叫或警報開始，而是從一件大家自認了解的小事產生分歧。一名自稱考生的人熟知所有緊急門位置，卻避談年齡。顧唯先聽周遭人的說法，沒有急著下判斷。群眾想找到簡單的壞人時，謠言總比仔細提問傳得快。即使最喜歡的推測最後被推翻，小隊也需要留下仍然有用的紀錄。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「指控」，顧唯卻不能把希望當成證據。工作檔案為「保證」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。顧唯詢問小隊能否依安全規則「吹噓」，答案仍不明朗。見證人在訪談中提到「商業」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

The oldest document on the table looked less important than the newest screen. That appearance was misleading. Xia Li found a staff identification badge stitched inside his borrowed coat. Xia Li read the handwritten notes aloud while Gu Wei checked the sequence of events. Their different skills mattered more than either person's rank. When they found a contradiction, they preserved both versions. Destroying an inconvenient record would only make the next investigation harder. The disputed notice used the word consistent, but its author had not signed the page. A written instruction told the staff to contribute before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. Xia Li found a reference to convention in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. The old manual told its readers to expose, and Xia Li wanted to know who had written that rule.

桌上最舊的文件看起來不如最新螢幕重要，但那只是表面。夏黎在他借來的外套內找到工作識別證。夏黎念出手寫備註，顧唯逐項核對事件順序。此時兩人的不同專長，比誰的職位更高還重要。找到矛盾後，他們把兩個版本都保存下來。毀掉一份不方便的資料，只會讓下一次調查更加困難。有爭議的通知使用「前後一致的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「貢獻」，卻沒有人記下結果。夏黎在舊筆記裡找到關於「大會；集會」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。舊手冊要求讀者「揭露」，夏黎想知道這條規則由誰制定。

By afternoon, the discovery had reached the people who could lose the most. Proctor Shen ordered the group to treat him as a fellow competitor, despite the concealed authority. Proctor Shen offered a quick settlement, but it required the group to stop asking questions. Gu Wei considered the frightened faces around the room. It would be easy to sound heroic and demand that everyone take the same risk. Instead, each person was allowed to describe what they were prepared to do and what they would refuse. The evidence list included hurricane, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for hydrogen, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. Xia Li found a reference to Ignorance in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. Someone had marked the warning informative; Xia Li checked whether that description was accurate.

到了下午，這項發現已傳到最可能蒙受損失的人耳中。沈監考命令大家把他當普通競爭者，即使身分權限被隱瞞。沈監考提出快速和解，條件卻是所有人不准再追問。顧唯看著房內不安的臉孔。此時說些英雄話、要求大家冒一樣的風險很容易；他們卻讓每個人自己說明願意做什麼，以及拒絕承擔什麼。證物清單上有「颶風」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「氫」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。夏黎在舊筆記裡找到關於「無知」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。有人把警告標成「提供資訊的」，夏黎則查證這種描述是否準確。

A scoreboard could measure speed, distance, and supplies, but it could not measure the relief on a frightened person's face. Gu Wei found himself paying attention to ordinary details: a cup passed to someone thirsty, a door kept open, a mistake admitted before anyone was hurt. Xia Li insisted that these actions should not become a new competition for moral points. They mattered because people mattered, even when no examiner was watching.

排行榜能測速度、距離與物資，卻測不出害怕的人終於安心時的神情。顧唯開始注意平凡小事：把水遞給口渴的人、替人留門、在有人受傷前承認錯誤。夏黎堅持這些行動不該變成新的道德比分競賽。它們有意義，是因為人本身有意義，即使沒有監考者看著。

The plan looked simple on paper and complicated the moment people began moving. Gu Wei asked the disguised teacher to testify about the scoring rules in writing. A delay forced Gu Wei to choose between keeping the schedule and checking the safety condition again. The group checked again. They had lost time, but a false success would have cost them much more. Xia Li marked the revised sequence where everyone could see it. The disputed notice used the word loyal, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for manufacture, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. Xia Li found a reference to misfortune in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned recovery during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

計畫寫在紙上很簡單，人一動起來就變得複雜。顧唯請偽裝的老師書面作證，說明評分規則。一次延誤讓顧唯必須在守住時間表與重新檢查安全條件之間選擇。小隊決定再查一次。雖然損失時間，虛假的成功卻可能造成更大代價。夏黎把修改後的順序標在所有人都看得見的位置。有爭議的通知使用「忠於」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「製造」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。夏黎在舊筆記裡找到關於「災禍；不幸」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「復原」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

The streets outside a transit inspection checkpoint were quieter, but quiet was not proof of safety. The teacher admitted an earlier class had refused the same dangerous task. Gu Wei checked that everyone knew how to ask for help before the group separated. They had gained one answer and inherited two new responsibilities. Tomorrow's work would require patience from people who had every reason to be angry. For the first time that day, the team had a direction worth following. The evidence list included relaxation, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for resistance, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. Xia Li found a reference to skyscraper in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. Someone had marked the warning troublesome; Xia Li checked whether that description was accurate.

運輸稽查站外的街道變安靜了，但安靜不代表安全。老師承認前一屆也拒絕過同樣危險的考題。顧唯先確認大家知道如何求助，才讓小隊分開。他們得到一個答案，也多了兩份責任。明天的工作需要那些本來就有理由憤怒的人保持耐心。至少在這一天結束時，小隊終於有了一個值得追查的方向。證物清單上有「休息時間」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「阻力」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。夏黎在舊筆記裡找到關於「摩天大樓」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。有人把警告標成「棘手的」，夏黎則查證這種描述是否準確。
