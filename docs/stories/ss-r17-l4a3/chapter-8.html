The Clinic Without Medicine

At the examination city's neighborhood clinic, the first sign of trouble was not a scream or an alarm. It was a small disagreement about something everyone believed they understood. A glowing health symbol led the applicants to a clinic with empty treatment cabinets. Gu Wei listened to the surrounding voices before making a judgment. Rumors traveled faster than careful questions, especially when the public wanted a simple villain. The team needed a record that would remain useful even if their favorite explanation proved false. The working file contained a separate entry for ancestors, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. Xia Li found a reference to Contrary in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned craft during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included crush, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

在考試城市社區診所，麻煩不是從尖叫或警報開始，而是從一件大家自認了解的小事產生分歧。發亮的醫療標誌把考生引到診所，藥櫃卻完全空了。顧唯先聽周遭人的說法，沒有急著下判斷。群眾想找到簡單的壞人時，謠言總比仔細提問傳得快。即使最喜歡的推測最後被推翻，小隊也需要留下仍然有用的紀錄。工作檔案為「祖先」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。夏黎在舊筆記裡找到關於「正好相反」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「手工技藝」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「擠壓；造成壓傷」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。

The oldest document on the table looked less important than the newest screen. That appearance was misleading. Xia Li checked the signed delivery record and found supplies had been moved to a secure warehouse. Xia Li read the handwritten notes aloud while Gu Wei checked the sequence of events. Their different skills mattered more than either person's rank. When they found a contradiction, they preserved both versions. Destroying an inconvenient record would only make the next investigation harder. An inspector described the situation as defensible; Gu Wei wanted measurements instead of adjectives. The report called the arrangement distinguished, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. The old manual told its readers to equip, and Xia Li wanted to know who had written that rule. The evidence list included guilt, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

桌上最舊的文件看起來不如最新螢幕重要，但那只是表面。夏黎查閱簽收單，發現物資已移往安全倉庫。夏黎念出手寫備註，顧唯逐項核對事件順序。此時兩人的不同專長，比誰的職位更高還重要。找到矛盾後，他們把兩個版本都保存下來。毀掉一份不方便的資料，只會讓下一次調查更加困難。檢查員把情況形容為「有充分理由的」，顧唯卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。報告說這項安排「傑出的；精湛的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。舊手冊要求讀者「器材／設備」，夏黎想知道這條規則由誰制定。證物清單上有「愧疚」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。

By afternoon, the discovery had reached the people who could lose the most. Proctor Shen claimed uncertainty was part of the challenge, but the clinic's nurse rejected that rule. Proctor Shen offered a quick settlement, but it required the group to stop asking questions. Gu Wei considered the frightened faces around the room. It would be easy to sound heroic and demand that everyone take the same risk. Instead, each person was allowed to describe what they were prepared to do and what they would refuse. The working file contained a separate entry for handwriting, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. Xia Li found a reference to hose in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. Someone had marked the warning identical; Xia Li checked whether that description was accurate. One witness promised to involve if conditions improved, but Gu Wei would not treat hope as evidence.

到了下午，這項發現已傳到最可能蒙受損失的人耳中。沈監考說不確定性是考試的一部分，診所護士卻拒絕這種說法。沈監考提出快速和解，條件卻是所有人不准再追問。顧唯看著房內不安的臉孔。此時說些英雄話、要求大家冒一樣的風險很容易；他們卻讓每個人自己說明願意做什麼，以及拒絕承擔什麼。工作檔案為「字跡」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。夏黎在舊筆記裡找到關於「水管」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。有人把警告標成「完全相同的」，夏黎則查證這種描述是否準確。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「涉及；參與」，顧唯卻不能把希望當成證據。

A scoreboard could measure speed, distance, and supplies, but it could not measure the relief on a frightened person's face. Gu Wei found himself paying attention to ordinary details: a cup passed to someone thirsty, a door kept open, a mistake admitted before anyone was hurt. Xia Li insisted that these actions should not become a new competition for moral points. They mattered because people mattered, even when no examiner was watching.

排行榜能測速度、距離與物資，卻測不出害怕的人終於安心時的神情。顧唯開始注意平凡小事：把水遞給口渴的人、替人留門、在有人受傷前承認錯誤。夏黎堅持這些行動不該變成新的道德比分競賽。它們有意義，是因為人本身有意義，即使沒有監考者看著。

The plan looked simple on paper and complicated the moment people began moving. Gu Wei requested supervised access to the real medical supplies for anyone in need. A delay forced Gu Wei to choose between keeping the schedule and checking the safety condition again. The group checked again. They had lost time, but a false success would have cost them much more. Xia Li marked the revised sequence where everyone could see it. A written instruction told the staff to launch before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. The report called the arrangement lousy, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. The old manual told its readers to misunderstand, and Xia Li wanted to know who had written that rule. The evidence list included pregnancy, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

計畫寫在紙上很簡單，人一動起來就變得複雜。顧唯要求在監督下，讓有需要的人取得真正醫療物資。一次延誤讓顧唯必須在守住時間表與重新檢查安全條件之間選擇。小隊決定再查一次。雖然損失時間，虛假的成功卻可能造成更大代價。夏黎把修改後的順序標在所有人都看得見的位置。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「推出；啟動」，卻沒有人記下結果。報告說這項安排「品質差的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。舊手冊要求讀者「誤會」，夏黎想知道這條規則由誰制定。證物清單上有「懷孕」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。

The streets outside the examination city's neighborhood clinic were quieter, but quiet was not proof of safety. The nurse disclosed that actual city residents still depended on the clinic. Gu Wei checked that everyone knew how to ask for help before the group separated. They had gained one answer and inherited two new responsibilities. Tomorrow's work would require patience from people who had every reason to be angry. For the first time that day, the team had a direction worth following. The working file contained a separate entry for riddle, and Gu Wei asked who had verified it. Xia Li found a reference to stroke in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned suggestion during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included technician, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

考試城市社區診所外的街道變安靜了，但安靜不代表安全。護士透露真實居民仍仰賴這間診所。顧唯先確認大家知道如何求助，才讓小隊分開。他們得到一個答案，也多了兩份責任。明天的工作需要那些本來就有理由憤怒的人保持耐心。至少在這一天結束時，小隊終於有了一個值得追查的方向。工作檔案為「謎題」設了獨立項目，顧唯詢問是誰查證過。夏黎在舊筆記裡找到關於「一筆／中風」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「建議」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「技術員」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。
