不會消失的門怎麼畫

How to Draw a Door Without Losing It

1998，被刪除的女主角拒絕退場！ · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A stranger arrived carrying a battered suitcase and a smile so formal it looked rehearsed. He introduced himself as a retired diplomat and claimed to represent a foreign distributor. Chikage asked for an appointment letter. Instead, he offered a colorful diagram showing how the studio could become a tourist attraction. "Magnificent international opportunities," he declared. Yui nearly applauded. Chikage noticed that his draft agreement omitted the cost of restoring the leaking roof. "An essential detail," she said. The man began an elaborate greeting about cultural friendship, but she insisted on speaking to the real rights holder before proceeding. Akari whispered from under the light table, "He has the voice of a villain in episode four."

一名陌生男子提著破舊行李箱走進工房，臉上的笑容正式得像排練過。他自稱退休外交官，代表海外發行商而來。千景請他出示委任信，對方卻拿出一張彩色示意圖，展示如何把工房打造成觀光景點。「前景壯麗的國際機會！」他宣布。小結差點鼓掌。千景注意到草擬合約完全沒提漏水屋頂的修繕費。「這是必要細節。」她說。男人開始冗長地寒暄文化友誼，千景卻堅持先與真正的權利人通話。燈里在燈箱下低語：「他的聲音好像第四集的反派。」

To test whether the distributor understood the work, Chikage showed him the animation manual. It described how every experimental scene must be paired with a safety check. The man barely looked. "We can replace the drawings with computer images. It will be cheaper." He reached to loosen the glass clips without permission. Chikage stopped his hand. "The paint may contain material sensitive to heat and moisture. Even an ordinary germ on a finger can stain a surface." Yui brought clean gloves. A visiting mechanic from the next workshop examined the light table's faulty switch and suggested replacing it before any more demonstrations. "If the lamp shorts, we flee rather than defend the projector," he told Akari solemnly.

為了測試發行商懂不懂這份工作，千景給他看動畫操作手冊。裡面說明每個實驗性場景都必須搭配安全檢查。男子幾乎沒看。「用電腦影像取代這些畫就好，更便宜。」他未經許可便伸手想鬆開玻璃夾。千景擋住了他。「顏料可能對熱與水分很敏感；連手指上的普通細菌都可能留下汙跡。」小結拿來乾淨手套。隔壁工房的機械技師檢查燈箱故障開關，建議再示範前先更換。「如果燈短路，我們是要逃跑，不是保衛放映機。」他鄭重對燈里說。

In the afternoon, the group visited a hill overlooking the magnificent harbor. The proposed film was supposed to explain how a weather satellite followed storms beyond the horizon. Chikage carried a portable camera; Yui photographed the landscape while the mechanic drew a simple circuit diagram. They wanted to strengthen the scene with real information, not decorate it with magical nonsense. The distributor followed, complaining that factual research was boring. At the top, they found the old public recreation ground locked behind a gate. A handwritten sign said a neighborhood committee had rented it for a sports day. Chikage decided not to film there until someone could accompany them with permission.

下午，大家爬上俯瞰壯麗港灣的小丘。預定的新影片要解釋氣象衛星如何追蹤地平線以外的風暴。千景帶了可攜式攝影機，小結替景色拍照，機械技師畫了簡單的電路圖。他們想用真正資訊強化場景，而不是灑滿沒有根據的魔法胡話。發行商跟在後面，一路抱怨研究事實很無聊。山頂的舊公共休憩場卻鎖著門，手寫牌子說鄰里委員會借去辦運動日。千景決定等到有人能正式陪同、取得許可後，再到那裡拍攝。

That evening, the distributor's real employer called. It was not an international company at all, but a small commercial agency trying to obtain the studio's archives without paying the artists. Chikage hung up and found Yui sitting on the floor in embarrassed silence. "I thought he could save us," the apprentice said. "You asked questions and learned the answer. That is more useful than pretending you never hesitated." Akari raised a paper umbrella with great ceremony. "The heroic defense of the light table has succeeded!" Chikage laughed despite herself. Their victory was tiny: a rejected contract and a repaired switch. Yet for the first time since coming home, she felt the studio might have room for an honest future.

當晚，發行商真正的雇主打來電話。那根本不是國際公司，而是一家小商業代理商，企圖在不支付畫師費用的情況下取得工房檔案。千景掛上電話，發現小結尷尬地坐在地板上。「我以為他能救我們。」學徒說。「妳提出問題，也知道了答案。這比假裝自己從沒猶豫過更有用。」燈里隆重地舉起紙傘。「保衛燈箱的英雄行動成功！」千景忍不住笑了。他們的勝利很小：拒絕一份合約，修好一個開關。然而，返鄉以來第一次，千景覺得這間工房也許還能有一個誠實的未來。

The mechanic's outstanding work deserved more than a handshake. Chikage wrote his hours into the production budget and asked him to send a formal invoice. She had noticed Yui's hesitation every time money came up; the apprentice still feared that naming a fee would seem ungrateful. 'A useful skill has a price,' Chikage told her. 'That does not make kindness disappear.' Akari nodded gravely, then asked what a pilot should charge for one heroic umbrella performance. 'First,' Chikage replied, 'we need to determine whether the umbrella is even safe under a hot lamp.'

機械技師出色的工作值得的不只是一個握手。千景把他的工時寫進預算，請他寄正式請款單。她注意到小結每次談錢都會猶豫，因為學徒仍怕提出報酬顯得不知感恩。「有用的技術有價格。」千景對她說。「這不會讓善意消失。」燈里鄭重點頭，接著問飛行員表演一次英雄打傘秀該收多少錢。「首先，」千景回答，「得確定那把傘在熱燈下究竟安不安全。」

Before sleeping, Chikage filed the day's notes with the earlier research. She could draw an open door in an animation, but she could not force every person to walk through it. That distinction mattered. The apprentice was not required to trust every visitor; Akari was not required to perform for a buyer; the former artists were not required to forgive a debt merely because the studio had changed managers. Chikage wrote all three principles on a card beside the new switch. Outside, the horizon disappeared behind rain. Inside, an unfinished drawing of a door remained on the light table, waiting for an artist willing to decide what was on the other side.

睡前，千景把今天的筆記和先前的研究資料歸檔。她可以在動畫裡畫出一扇敞開的門，卻不能強迫每個人走進去。這個差別很重要。學徒不必相信每一名訪客；燈里不必為買家表演；從前的畫師也不必因為工房換了經理，就原諒欠款。千景把這三條原則寫在卡片上，放在新開關旁。窗外，地平線消失在雨幕裡。室內，燈箱上的門仍然只畫了一半，等待願意決定另一側風景的畫師。
