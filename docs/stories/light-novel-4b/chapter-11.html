拒絕黃金結局的少女

The Girl Who Refused the Golden Ending

The arts council accepted Chikage's preliminary proposal, but requested proof that the last reel existed. Sora found an old telegraph slip in the studio's dusty storage room. It carried a code for a package sent to a private warehouse beside the solar power demonstration site. "A modern treasure map," Akari announced, "except it's written by people who never learned to draw." Chikage tried to explain the consequences of guessing an address from a code. They would verify the sender first. Meanwhile a rumor began to circulate that the canceled film had predicted an invasion from the sea. Chikage's expression turned grim. "Stories can travel faster than evidence," she said. "Unfortunately, they can wreck reputations just as quickly."

藝術委員會接受千景的初步提案，卻要求證明最後一捲底片確實存在。蒼在滿是灰塵的倉庫找到一張舊電報單，上面有個代碼，指向太陽能示範場附近的私人倉庫。「現代藏寶圖！」燈里宣布。「只是畫圖的人顯然沒學過畫畫。」千景解釋，不能光靠一個代碼就猜收件地址，必須先核對寄件人。與此同時，鎮上流傳起被取消的動畫曾預言海上入侵的謠言。千景臉色沉下來。「故事比證據傳得快，」她說，「很遺憾，摧毀聲譽也一樣快。」

The warehouse owner was a graceful woman who had once competed in a marathon. She said the package had been stored during an era when film reels were moved in wooden boxes. A rebel assistant director had negotiated the storage arrangement after quarreling with the sponsor. The woman showed Chikage a receipt, but refused access without the owner's permission. Akari wanted to use her paper parachute as a daring disguise. "We don't break into a building," Chikage replied. "Not even if its lock is shaped like a villain's nose." She thanked the owner and left a written request. The visit provided one useful indication: the package had not been destroyed when the show was canceled.

倉庫主人是一名舉止優雅、年輕時跑過馬拉松的女士。她說那批貨是在膠片還用木箱運送的年代寄存的。一名被稱為叛逆者的副導演，當年和贊助商吵翻後，談妥了寄存安排。女士出示收據，卻在未得到所有人許可前拒絕開倉。燈里想用紙降落傘做大膽偽裝。「我們不會闖空門。」千景回答。「就算那把鎖長得像反派鼻子也不行。」她謝過主人，留下書面申請。這趟至少得到一項有用跡象：節目取消時，那件包裹並沒有被銷毀。

At the public library, a volunteer was preparing a children's exhibition about the history of communication. Its exhibits included a telegraph key, a telephone, and a simple electric circuit. Akari's scene could expand the display with a hand-painted explanation, but Chikage asked who would maintain the equipment. The library director smiled. "You keep discovering that every invention has a caretaker." Chikage admitted it was becoming a habit. She also asked permission to correct a display that called one business family the dominant founder of the harbor. Old records showed many smaller groups had contributed, and persuasive labels could conceal as much as they explained.

在公共圖書館，一名志工正在準備兒童通訊史展覽，展品包括電報鍵、電話和簡單電路。燈里的場景可以擴充展示，加入手繪解說，但千景先問誰負責維護設備。館長笑了。「妳老是會發現，每項發明背後都有照顧的人。」千景承認這快變成自己的習慣。她也請求修正一面把某個企業家族稱作港口主導創辦者的看板。舊資料顯示許多小團體也曾貢獻，漂亮的標籤可能隱藏和解釋一樣多的事。

That night, Akari told Chikage what she remembered of the disputed ending. The sponsor's version had made her accept a golden reward and leave the town behind. The original, she thought, had required persuasion rather than conquest: the heroine would listen to people who wanted different futures. Chikage could not confirm a memory drawn on damaged film, yet she wrote it beside the verified cue sheet, clearly marked as unconfirmed. "Would you rather have the famous ending?" she asked. Akari shook her head. "I would rather have the one where the people I met can still decide." A pale blue line moved across her painted sleeve, then stopped.

那天晚上，燈里告訴千景自己記得的爭議結局。贊助商版本讓她接受黃金獎賞，然後拋下小鎮離開。原版似乎要求的不是征服，而是說服：女主角必須聽見想走向不同未來的人。千景無法證實損壞膠片上的記憶，卻仍把它寫在已驗證配樂提示旁，清楚標記「尚未證實」。「妳會比較想要成名的結局嗎？」她問。燈里搖頭。「我比較想要大家都能自己決定的結局。」一道淡藍色線條掠過她畫好的袖口，接著停住。

The false invasion rumor continued to breed whenever a new shopkeeper repeated it. One newspaper columnist tried to explain the dispute as a lesson in capitalism, arguing that whichever buyer had the most money deserved the story. Chikage invited him to examine the contracts rather than debate slogans. Akari tried to tickle the shadow of the printed newspaper with her paper wing, but the painted boundary kept her from touching it. 'Even my jokes have limits,' she complained. 'That may be the wisest thing you've said,' Chikage replied. The columnist eventually printed a correction, though not an apology.

虛假的入侵謠言只要被新店主轉述，就繼續滋生。一名報紙專欄作家試圖把爭議解釋成資本主義的教材，主張誰最有錢，誰就該擁有故事。千景請他先看合約，不要只辯論口號。燈里想用紙翅膀搔癢報紙印出的影子，卻被畫面邊界阻止。「連我的笑話都有上限。」她抱怨。「這可能是妳說過最有智慧的話。」千景回答。專欄作家最後刊出更正，雖然沒有道歉。

Before closing, Chikage phoned the warehouse again. The owner had found a letter addressed to the studio, unopened for years. It was from the assistant director who had opposed the sponsor, and it specified a procedure for releasing the box to the original workers' representatives. Chikage felt an eventual breakthrough taking shape, but she did not celebrate. Eleven people were owed wages; several had moved away; one had died. Finding legitimate representatives would take patience. She placed the letter beside the names and the code slip. Akari stood quietly in her rectangle of light. For the first time, even she seemed to understand that an ending could not be won by running faster.

關門前，千景再次打給倉庫。主人找到一封寄給工房、封存多年的信。寫信的是反對贊助商的副導演，信裡訂出了由原工作者代表領取箱子的程序。千景感覺突破逐漸成形，卻沒有慶祝。十一個人還有欠薪未領，其中幾位早已搬走，一位已經過世。找出合法代表需要耐心。她把信放在姓名表和代碼單旁。燈里安靜站在自己的光之長方形裡。這一次，連她似乎都明白，結局不是跑得快就能贏來的。
