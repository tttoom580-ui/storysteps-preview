分鏡稿裡的化石

A Fossil in the Storyboard

1998，被刪除的女主角拒絕退場！ · 第 2 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Yui arrived before sunrise with a school science textbook, two bent rulers, and a strangely cheerful greeting. "Today's lesson is how to rescue a cartoon without rescuing the entire company," she announced. Chikage had requested a productive morning of sorting, not a lecture. Yet the old shelves held a forgotten educational episode about a coastal fort, fossil shells, and an ancient voyage. Its landscape paintings were delicate enough to survive thirty years if handled properly. A caption explained an atom beside a drawing of a seashell; a second card described atomic energy with the careful warning that the two subjects were not identical. Chikage liked the research more than the script's clumsy jokes.

小結天還沒亮就來了，帶著一本自然科課本、兩把彎掉的尺，以及莫名開朗的問候。「今天教大家如何拯救一部卡通，而不必順便拯救整間公司！」她宣布。千景原本要求的是有效率地整理資料，可不是來聽講座。然而，架上竟藏著一集被遺忘的科教動畫，內容談海岸要塞、貝殼化石和古代航程。那些細緻的風景背景，只要妥善保管，甚至能再保存三十年。一張卡片在貝殼旁介紹原子，另一張提到原子能，並小心提醒兩者不能混為一談。千景喜歡這些研究資料，卻不太欣賞劇本裡笨拙的笑話。

Akari watched from the light box as Yui laid a painted shoreline beneath her. "I remember this voyage," she murmured. "I was supposed to carry a messenger's letter to the fort." Chikage asked what happened next. "The director stopped drawing." That was not a useful memory, but it was honest. Inside the storyboard folder, Chikage found a note from a researcher studying immigration through the harbor. The planned episode had mixed personal stories with a documentary landscape, and the combination had offended a sponsor who wanted a purely cheerful advertisement. "How far did their influence extend?" Yui asked. Chikage found three pages of revisions and a line reading, "Retain historical details; revise only the commercial reference."

燈里坐在燈箱裡，看小結把海岸背景墊到腳下。「我記得這趟航程。」她低聲說。「我本來要替信差把信送到要塞。」千景問後來發生什麼事。「導演不畫了。」這答案不算有用，但至少誠實。千景從分鏡稿夾裡找到一名研究港口移民史學者的便條。原本的影片把個人故事與紀錄式風景結合，卻惹惱了只想要歡樂廣告的贊助商。「他們的影響到底有多大？」小結問。千景翻出三頁修改稿，讀到一行字：「保留歷史細節；只修改商業置入。」

The studio's old production runner, Sora, knocked at noon and offered to help move boxes. He had grown up beside the abandoned flour mill and still remembered its workers. His gaze caught a photograph of the harbor before the breakwater was built. "My grandmother came here by ship," he said. "The film was going to include her story." A guarantee of accuracy had been promised in the contract, but the signed research approval was missing. Chikage refused to use the photograph until they could verify consent. Sora thought her caution excessive. "The woman is my grandmother!" "And the audience is not your family," Chikage said. "Intimate details need permission even when the film has a happy ending."

中午，工房以前的跑腿員蒼來敲門，主動幫忙搬箱子。他在廢棄麵粉廠附近長大，至今記得那些工人。他的目光落在一張防波堤建造前的港口照片上。「我祖母就是搭船來這裡的。」他說。「影片本來要放她的故事。」合約曾保證史料準確，但研究同意書的簽名頁卻不見了。千景堅持先核實同意，再使用那張照片。蒼認為她太小心。「那是我祖母耶！」「觀眾又不是你的家人。」千景說。「即使結局很快樂，私密細節也要經過同意。」

A sudden murmur rose from the light table. Akari's painted horizon had begun to fade where the lamp burned hottest. Chikage cut the power immediately. She measured the heat with an old thermometer and placed a protective sheet over the bulb. Akari became visible again only after the lamp cooled. They learned the extent of the danger: keeping the character illuminated too long could damage the original colors. "So even a miracle has a maintenance schedule," Yui said. Their psychological relief did not last. In a drawer beneath the table, Chikage found a payroll envelope marked LAST VOYAGE. Inside was a list of eleven artists who had worked without receiving the final month's wages.

燈箱上忽然傳來一陣低語。燈里腳下畫好的地平線，靠近燈泡最熱的地方開始褪色。千景立刻關掉電源，用舊溫度計測量熱度，又在燈泡上方裝上保護片。燈冷卻以後，燈里才再次現身。她們知道了危險的程度：讓角色長時間曝在強光下，可能損壞原始顏色。「原來奇蹟也有保養行程表。」小結說。這份心理上的安心沒有維持太久。千景從燈箱下的抽屜找到一只寫著「最後航程」的薪資袋，裡面列了十一位尚未領到最後一個月薪水的畫師。

The lesson materials included a map showing the coastal immigration route through three generations. A corner note asked the painter to avoid turning the families into anonymous scenery. Chikage copied that instruction into the new production notebook, where Yui could find it. They were not creating a museum, but even a lighthearted cartoon had the power to retain or erase a person's place in history. Akari watched them file the photograph in a protective envelope. 'If you can rescue a name from a forgotten picture,' she said, 'perhaps you can rescue an ending too.'

教材裡還有一張地圖，描繪三代人沿海岸移居的路線。角落的註記要求畫師不要把這些家庭畫成沒有姓名的風景。千景把指示抄進新的製作筆記，讓小結也看得見。他們不是在經營博物館，但即使輕鬆的卡通也有能力保存或抹除一個人在歷史中的位置。燈里看著她們將照片放入保護信封。「如果妳能把一個名字從被遺忘的照片裡救回來，」她說，「說不定也能救回一個結局。」

Chikage put the envelope beside the episode's missing final page. She could not guarantee that every claim was correct, but each now had a source. The animation might have been canceled for money, not merely for weak ratings. Her mother had retained the paperwork and hidden it from the buyers' summary. Chikage felt both angry and strangely grateful. A producer might revise a scene to fix a joke; she could not revise the past by wishing it away. Akari stared at the sea painting without speaking. "Is it possible," the girl finally asked, "that the people who drew me weren't paid?" Chikage answered, "Yes. And that is where our story has to begin."

千景把薪資袋放在那集遺失的最後一頁旁。她還不能保證每項說法都正確，但現在至少各有資料來源。動畫被取消，也許不只是收視不好，而是資金出了問題。母親保存了這些文件，卻沒有列在交給買家的摘要裡。千景又生氣又奇妙地感激。製作人可以修改場景，讓笑話更好笑，卻無法光靠希望就改寫過去。燈里盯著畫中的海面，一句話也不說。最後她問：「畫出我的那些人……有可能沒拿到薪水嗎？」千景答：「有。而且我們的故事必須從這裡開始。」
