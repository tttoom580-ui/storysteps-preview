停不下來的渡輪篇

The Emergency Ferry Episode

1998，被刪除的女主角拒絕退場！ · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first paid commission arrived in a thin envelope: a local school's request for a safety animation about the ferry crossing. The assignment looked simple until Chikage read the conditions. It needed a lifeguard, a realistic timetable, and a clear priority for passengers who required help. The principal wanted an incredible rescue with heroic music; the ferry captain preferred instructions that people could actually follow. Chikage chose the captain. "A student cannot eat inspiration for breakfast," she told Yui, "and an exciting scene cannot defend a dangerous plan." She set a maximum running time of four minutes and asked Sora to gather photographs of the wharf with the captain's permission.

第一份付費委託裝在薄薄的信封裡：當地學校要一支介紹渡輪安全的動畫。任務看起來很簡單，直到千景讀完條件。影片必須有救生員、真實班表，還要明確說明哪些乘客需要優先協助。校長想看不可思議的英雄救援，船長卻希望畫出大家真的能照做的指示。千景選了船長的意見。「學生不能把靈感當早餐吃，」她對小結說，「刺激的畫面也不能替危險計畫辯護。」她把片長上限定為四分鐘，並請蒼在徵得船長許可後拍攝碼頭照片。

At the pier, a lifeguard explained why a passenger should not sprint toward the edge. Chikage carefully recorded the pace of the safety demonstration. Yui photographed signs while Sora counted the percentage of passengers using the narrow ramp. Their work was interrupted by a boy shouting that a woman had left a bag on the ferry. The captain kept everyone behind the barrier and retrieved it himself. "Context matters," he said. "If a crowd rushes for one bag, ten people may get hurt." He pointed at a simple route drawn on the wall. It was less luxurious than the principal's proposed adventure, but a sound lesson for intermediate students.

在碼頭，救生員說明為何乘客不該朝邊緣衝刺。千景仔細記錄安全示範的速度與步調。小結替標誌拍照，蒼則計算使用狹窄坡道的乘客百分比。他們工作到一半，一個男孩大叫有人把袋子忘在渡輪上。船長讓所有人待在欄杆後面，自己走去取回。「情境很重要。」他說。「若人群為了一個袋子衝出去，可能有十個人受傷。」他指向牆上簡單的疏散路線。那不如校長提議的冒險那麼豪華，卻是適合中級學生的紮實課程。

Back at the studio, Akari insisted that she should be the heroine of the training film. Chikage handed her a raisin, which the little pilot could not lift beyond the painted sky. "A practical problem," Akari admitted. "But I can still demonstrate bad choices." She performed an exaggerated fall toward the edge of a drawn boat, then froze when Chikage removed the background. The apprentice burst into laughter. Akari called it an insult and demanded a better costume. "We need to concentrate on the learning goal," Chikage said. She had spent the morning doing housework at the studio because the cleaner's wages were overdue. Comedy was welcome, but no unpaid work would be hidden behind it.

回到工房，燈里堅持要擔任這支安全影片的女主角。千景遞給她一顆葡萄乾；小飛行員卻沒辦法把它舉出畫好的天空範圍。「這是實際問題。」燈里承認。「但我還是能示範錯誤行為。」她誇張地朝畫中的船邊跌去，千景一抽走背景，她就停格了。小結忍不住大笑。燈里說這根本是侮辱，要求換一套更帥的衣服。「我們得專注在教學目標上。」千景說。她早上自己打掃工房，是因為清潔員的工資還沒付清。搞笑當然可以，但不能拿歡樂掩蓋無薪勞動。

During lunch, Chikage reviewed the school's suggested nutrition scene. It incorrectly claimed that a single rice ball contained a precise number of calories. She refused to print a calorie figure without checking the portion and ingredients. "You would rather offend a school than guess?" Yui asked. "Yes, and I'd rather proceed slowly than teach a confident mistake." The school teacher phoned to ask whether they could add a dramatic rescue. Chikage offered an alternative: show how an ordinary passenger calls the lifeguard and lets trained people respond. The teacher paused. Then she said, "That might actually help children." It was the first compliment all week that Chikage believed.

午餐時，千景檢查校方建議的營養橋段，發現裡面聲稱一顆飯糰含有固定、精確的卡路里數字。她拒絕在未核對份量和食材前印上數字。「妳寧可得罪學校，也不願猜一個？」小結問。「對。我也寧願慢慢進行，不想教學生一個自信滿滿的錯誤。」老師打電話來，希望加上一段刺激救援。千景提出替代方案：畫出普通乘客如何呼叫救生員，並讓受訓的人處理。老師停頓了一下，接著說：「那可能真的能幫到孩子。」這是千景整週以來第一句相信的讚美。

The school's photography club delivered contact prints from its own camera. Chikage thanked the students and obtained written permission before drawing from their images. She also compared the photographs with the captain's sketch: one sign had been moved since the previous year. The correction meant repainting two backgrounds, a whole afternoon of work. Yui stared at the growing task list. 'Being accurate is expensive,' she complained. 'So is being wrong,' Chikage answered. She put the corrected sign into the film and promised the students they could see the final print before their teachers used it.

學校攝影社把自行拍攝的照片接觸印樣送來。千景向學生道謝，並在取材描繪前先取得書面許可。她拿照片與船長的草圖比較，發現一面告示牌在去年之後移了位置。這個修正代表得重畫兩張背景，足足多花一整個下午。小結望著變長的工作清單抱怨：「準確真貴。」「錯誤也很貴。」千景回答。她把正確位置的告示牌畫進影片，也答應學生在老師拿去上課前可以先看成品。

The trial screening took place with twelve students and the real ferry crew. Several children wanted more explosions, but they could explain the emergency priority in their own words. The captain signed the acceptance form and paid the first installment in cash. Akari bowed from a narrow strip of painted deck. Chikage logged the payment, reserved the tax amount, and gave Yui a proper receipt for her time. In the drawer beneath the light table, eleven unpaid names still waited. One modest commission could not repay them. It could, however, keep the lamp on while Chikage found out whose promise had been broken, and why the canceled heroine had never been allowed to reach shore.

試映會來了十二名學生和真正的渡輪船員。幾個孩子想看到更多爆炸，但他們也能用自己的話說出緊急情況下的優先順序。船長簽下驗收單，當場付了第一筆款。燈里站在窄窄的畫中甲板上鞠躬。千景把收入入帳、先留下稅款，還給小結一張正式工時收據。燈箱下的抽屜裡，十一個尚未領薪的名字依然等著。小小一筆委託不可能立刻還清所有欠款，卻能讓燈繼續亮著，讓千景查出究竟是誰違背承諾，以及那位被取消的女主角為什麼永遠到不了彼岸。
