The Tape That Lied With a Smile

Sora returned with a cassette that the former sponsor had once supplied to a television station. Its label described a happy ending and included an attachment sheet listing sound effects. Yet the narrator spoke with an accent unlike the voice actress Chikage remembered. An earphone slipped from Yui's clumsy fingers as she listened. "The content seems ordinary," she said, frowning, "but the pauses are strange." Chikage compared the tape with the composer's original cues. A brief interruption came exactly where the heroine should have made her choice. Someone had replaced a question with a cheerful instruction. The change might mislead children, even if it had never reached television.

蒼帶回一卷舊卡帶，是前贊助商曾經交給電視臺的版本。標籤稱它是快樂結局，附件單則列出使用的音效。然而旁白帶著千景不熟悉的口音，和她記得的配音員不同。小結笨手笨腳地聽錄音，耳機滑落。「內容看起來普通，」她皺眉說，「但停頓很奇怪。」千景拿它與作曲家的原始提示比對，發現恰好在女主角應該作出選擇的地方，有一段短暫中斷。有人把一個問題改成歡快的命令。即使這版本從未播出，也可能誤導孩子。

Akari leaned against a painted wall and peeped at the playback machine. "That's not my voice," she protested. "It makes me sound obedient and dull." Chikage gave her a modest smile. "Your original dialogue may have been changed without your permission, but that doesn't mean we can claim the tape is illegal without more evidence." The apprentice wanted to issue a public accusation at once. Chikage instructed her to keep the tape clean and record the source. She had learned in business that a persuasive story could cause economic harm when it ran ahead of facts. Their reputation depended on not repeating the same mistake.

燈里靠著畫好的牆，偷偷瞄著播放機。「那不是我的聲音！」她抗議。「聽起來又聽話又無聊。」千景含蓄地笑了一下。「妳原本的台詞可能未經允許就被改了，但這不表示我們能在缺乏更多證據前宣稱錄音違法。」小結想立即公開指控。千景指示她保持卡帶清潔，記下來源。她早就從工作中學到，有說服力的故事一旦跑在事實前面，也可能造成經濟損失。工房的聲譽得靠不重蹈覆轍來維持。

The television station's archive clerk offered a refund for the copying fee when she discovered the supplied label was wrong. She also found a broadcast schedule mentioning a canceled science episode about nuclear power. The episode had been part of the same educational series, but the script was stored under a different name. Yui looked as if she wanted to run to the station immediately. Chikage told her to wait. They had an afternoon appointment with a former employee whose testimony mattered more than a title. The employee, now living in his homeland village, had asked for privacy. Chikage would not turn his hesitation into public entertainment.

電視臺檔案員發現提供的標籤有誤，主動退還影印費。她還找到一張節目表，上面提到一集被取消的核能科教動畫。那原屬同一系列，劇本卻以不同名稱保存。小結看起來很想立刻衝去電視臺，千景卻叫她等一等。她們下午約了前員工，對方的證詞比標題更重要。那人現在住在故鄉村落，事先要求保密。千景不會把他的猶豫拿去當成公眾娛樂。

The former employee was a modest sound engineer who spoke slowly and never exaggerated. He confirmed that the sponsor had ordered a new ending after the director's departure. The explanation was blunt: executives wanted no dialogue suggesting that a girl might refuse a profitable journey. He could identify the person who gave the order, but only if an independent review protected his name. Chikage agreed. At the end of the visit he gave Akari a small wink, as if he somehow knew she could hear from inside the portfolio. "Tell the pilot the other shore was never supposed to be a prison," he said.

前員工是一名謙遜的錄音技師，說話很慢，從不誇大。他證實導演離開後，贊助商曾下令製作新結局。原因直接得令人難受：主管不想要任何暗示女孩可以拒絕有利可圖旅程的台詞。他能辨認下令的人，條件是獨立審查必須保護姓名。千景答應了。探訪結束時，他朝燈里藏身的畫稿袋眨了眨眼，彷彿知道她能聽見。「告訴那位飛行員，彼岸從來不該是一座監獄。」他說。

At the village station, the sound engineer offered them tea and a crunchy rice cracker before speaking. He insisted on a modesty that made his skill almost invisible. Under the circumstance of a canceled broadcast, he had kept a duplicate tape only because the station's policy required it. Chikage admired the discipline behind that ordinary act. She asked whether the edited ending could have been produced by a mistake. 'Possible in theory,' he said, 'but the new line was recorded by someone hired for the purpose.' He showed them the dated session sheet without disclosing the performer's address. That was enough to justify the next step.

在村落車站，錄音技師先請她們喝茶、吃酥脆米果，才開始談事。他一向謙遜，幾乎讓人看不出有多專業。節目遭取消的情況下，他仍保存一份備份錄音，只因電視臺規定必須如此。千景欣賞這個平凡行動背後的自律。她問改寫的結局會不會只是誤操作。「理論上可能。」他說。「但那句新台詞是特地找人錄的。」他出示有日期的錄音紀錄表，卻沒公開配音者的住址。這已足夠支持下一步調查。

On the train home, Yui asked whether their discovery counted as revolutionary. Chikage shook her head. "It is a correction. A revolution is what people do after they understand what needs changing." She opened the folder and laid the invoices side by side, careful not to expose the delicate sheets to the window. Akari remained silent. Outside, the rural stations passed in a rhythm that reminded Chikage of moving animation frames. The mystery had become more personal, but the next task was stubbornly practical: find out who still held the last reel, and why a sponsor had been willing to rewrite a heroine's goodbye.

回程火車上，小結問這發現算不算革命性的事件。千景搖頭。「這只是更正。真正的改變，是人在理解需要改變什麼之後做出的行動。」她打開資料夾，把發票並排，注意別讓細緻的紙張曝在車窗旁。燈里始終沒有說話。窗外鄉間車站有節奏地掠過，讓千景想到逐格移動的動畫。謎團變得更加私人，但下一個任務仍很實際：查出誰還握著最後一捲底片，以及贊助商為何願意改寫女主角的道別。
