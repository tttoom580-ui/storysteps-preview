水族館才不是童話宮殿

The Aquarium Is Not a Fairy Palace

1998，被刪除的女主角拒絕退場！ · 第 7 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The local aquarium offered the studio a short educational film, provided it did not invent facts about marine animals. Chikage welcomed the income but worried about the chemicals left in the paint room. Yui suggested an aquarium story about a fish that wanted to learn music. Akari immediately volunteered to play the fish. "You are a pilot," Chikage reminded her. "I am an outstandingly flexible performer," Akari declared. The aquarium educator showed them a shallow basin used to study water quality and explained the characteristic movements of different species. The lesson needed chemistry, not a magical fish princess. Chikage liked that challenge. A production company could be imaginative without being careless.

當地水族館委託工房製作一支短科教動畫，條件是不准胡編海洋動物的事實。千景歡迎這筆收入，卻仍擔心顏料室留下的化學問題。小結想做一條渴望學音樂的魚的故事。燈里立刻自告奮勇演那條魚。「妳是飛行員。」千景提醒。「我是極度有彈性的優秀表演者！」燈里宣布。水族館教育員帶她們參觀研究水質用的淺水盆，解釋不同物種的特徵性動作。這一課需要化學，不需要魔法魚公主。千景很喜歡這個挑戰：有想像力，不代表可以不用負責。

An accountant from the cooperative bank came to discuss the studio's payments. He was not impressed by Akari's dramatic poses. "The prime question is whether you can pay wages every month," he said. Chikage showed a systematic budget for the film, including paint, camera rental, transport, and the teacher's review fee. Yui's contribution was recorded by name. "This is relevant evidence of improvement," the accountant admitted, "but the older debt remains." Chikage nodded. The problem did not disappear because new work was fun. She was fortunate to have clients, but she refused to defeat a debt by pretending the people who held it were imaginary.

合作金庫的一名會計師來討論工房的付款狀況。他完全不被燈里戲劇化的姿勢打動。「首要問題是妳能否每個月支付工資。」他說。千景拿出水族館影片的系統性預算，包括顏料、攝影機租用、交通及教師審稿費。小結的貢獻也列有姓名。「這是改善狀況的相關證據。」會計師承認。「但舊債還在。」千景點頭。新工作再有趣，問題也不會自動消失。有客戶願意上門確實幸運，但她不願把債權人當成想像人物，藉此假裝打敗了欠款。

At the aquarium, Akari became fascinated by the rhythmic motion of a sea creature behind a glass wall. "The water has its own rhythm," she said from a painted travel cel. An elderly guide explained how shade and light changed the animals' behavior. Sora recorded her words with permission and asked about a large expansion planned for the building. "Bigger tanks aren't automatically better," the guide replied. "You must consider the animals' needs." Chikage felt admiration for the clarity of that answer. It was the same principle behind their studio's new safety rules. A place could strive for growth without treating every living thing as an obstacle.

到了水族館，燈里被玻璃牆後某種海洋生物有節奏的動作吸引。「水也有自己的節奏。」她坐在畫好的旅行場景裡說。年長導覽員解釋，陰影與光線會如何改變動物的行為。蒼徵得許可後錄下說明，也問起館方計畫擴建的事。「水槽大不一定更好。」導覽員回答。「還要考慮動物真正需要什麼。」千景欣賞這回答的清晰。那正與工房的新安全規則相同：一個地方可以努力成長，卻不該把所有活物都當成障礙。

The next morning brought an annoyance: someone had sent the aquarium a rumor that the studio used illegal dyes. Chikage did not dismiss the concern. She invited the teacher who had used the microscope to explain what could and could not be concluded from the old sample. The teacher refused to certify the room as safe until an independent test was complete. Chikage accepted the delay. Yui looked disappointed, then offered to paint the new film in a rented clean room. The aquarium agreed to the change. "Good decisions are not always elegant," Chikage said, "but they give people a reason to trust the work."

隔天早上又來了件令人煩惱的事：有人向水族館散播流言，說工房用了違法染料。千景沒有把擔憂一口否決。她邀請使用過顯微鏡的老師說明，舊樣本究竟能證明什麼、不能證明什麼。老師表示在獨立檢測完成前，不能認證那間房安全。千景接受延期。小結有些失望，卻提議改到租來的潔淨工作室上色。水族館同意更改。「好的決定不一定優雅，」千景說，「卻能讓別人有理由相信作品。」

The academic adviser from the coastal college offered to check the narration, provided the studio displayed the source notes. His strict tone initially annoyed Yui, but he explained that children's curiosity should motivate better research, not faster guessing. At dawn, Chikage had to waken Akari only briefly to confirm the timing of a painted fish's jump. The little pilot yawned magnificently and demanded a formal fish costume. Chikage refused to make the joke the center of the film, though she allowed it in the closing seconds. That compromise delighted Yui, who had feared the adviser would remove every spark of comedy.

海岸學院的一位學術顧問願意核對旁白，條件是工房必須列出資料來源。他語氣嚴格，起初讓小結很煩躁，卻也解釋：孩子的好奇應該激勵更好的研究，而不是更快的猜測。黎明時，千景只短暫喚醒燈里，確認畫中魚跳躍的時間點。小飛行員打了個誇張呵欠，要求一套正式魚裝。千景拒絕讓笑話變成全片中心，卻准許它出現在最後幾秒。小結十分高興，原本還怕顧問會把所有幽默都刪掉。

When the finished aquarium film was delivered, children laughed at a fictional fish whose tail kept landing off the beat. The factual narration remained fluent, the movement accurate, and the real educator appeared in the credits. The director paid on time. Back at the studio, Akari asked why the guide had refused the larger tank. Chikage answered that protection sometimes meant knowing when not to expand. Akari stared at her painted window. "Does that mean I should stop wishing for a bigger world?" Chikage considered the question. "No. It means the world you get should be big enough for everyone who has to live there."

水族館影片交片時，孩子們被一條尾巴總是跟不上拍子的虛構小魚逗得大笑。事實旁白依然流暢，動作也準確，真正的教育員還列在片尾名單。館長準時付款。回到工房後，燈里問導覽員為什麼不贊成大水槽。千景說，保護有時就是知道何時不該擴張。燈里望著畫好的窗戶。「這是不是表示我不該再希望世界變大？」千景想了想。「不是。我的意思是，得到的世界至少要大到足以容納每個必須住在裡面的人。」
