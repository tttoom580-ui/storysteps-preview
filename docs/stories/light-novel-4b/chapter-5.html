The Contract Nobody Can Sing

1998，被刪除的女主角拒絕退場！ · 第 5 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Five days before the library screening, a record company threatened to seize the film's music. Its lawyer said the original orchestra recording belonged to the sponsor, even though the composer had never received his agreed fee. Chikage felt anxiety rising like a kettle on the stove. She adjusted the budget and called the composer directly. He lived beside a river where schoolchildren practiced in a canoe, and he remembered the canceled series with painful clarity. "Don't abandon the music," he said. "But don't pretend the company owns it either." Chikage offered a paid license for a short excerpt and promised to enclose his name in the printed credits.

距離圖書館放映只剩五天，一家唱片公司威脅要扣押影片配樂。律師說原始管弦樂團錄音屬於贊助商，儘管作曲家從未拿到約定報酬。千景的焦慮像爐上的水壺一樣升起。她調整預算，直接打給作曲家。他住在河邊，小學生常在那裡練獨木舟，對被取消的系列仍記憶鮮明。「別放棄音樂。」他說。「但也別假裝公司擁有它。」千景提出付費取得一小段使用授權，並承諾在印製的演職員名單中附上他的名字。

Back at the studio, Akari announced that she had written a replacement anthem. It consisted of one syllable sung twenty times with tremendous confidence. Yui tried not to laugh. "Your performance has an impact," she told the girl. "We just don't know what kind." Akari objected, calling Yui an enemy of imaginative art. Chikage made them listen to the demonstration on a portable cassette player. The sound was distinct from the professional recording, and not in a favorable way. Still, the apprentice suggested they could use Akari's tiny song as a comic closing cue if the composer approved. Akari brightened. Even a failed idol needed an audience that knew how to say no.

回到工房，燈里宣布自己寫了一首替代主題曲。整首歌只有同一個音節，被她充滿自信地唱了二十遍。小結努力忍住笑。「妳的表演確實有影響力。」她對少女說。「只是我們還不知道是哪一種。」燈里提出抗議，說小結是想像力藝術的敵人。千景讓她們用可攜式卡帶播放機聽示範。那和專業錄音有明顯差別，而且並非好的差別。小結仍提議，只要作曲家同意，或許能把燈里的小歌放在片尾當搞笑提示。燈里眼睛亮了。就連失敗的偶像，也需要懂得說不的觀眾。

The mayor's office sent another device: a magnifier for enlarging tiny drawings during the public presentation. Chikage inspected it before use; the glass made one corner of the cel look warped. She found that the light table and magnifier needed a different distance between them. That meant another adjustment, another hour of testing, and no shortcut. A protest flyer arrived from former workers accusing the studio of using unpaid labor. Chikage called a meeting in the old recreation room rather than trying to silence them. "Their objection is not an attack on us," she told Yui. "It is the bill we inherited."

鎮公所又送來一件裝置：放大畫面的鏡片，好讓公開簡報時觀眾看清小小角色。千景使用前先測試，發現玻璃讓賽璐珞片一角看起來扭曲。燈箱和放大鏡必須保持不同距離，代表還要調整、還要花一個小時測試，沒有捷徑。以前的員工送來抗議傳單，指控工房利用無薪勞動。千景沒有試圖讓他們閉嘴，而是約在舊休憩室開會。「他們的反對不是在攻擊我們。」她對小結說。「那是我們繼承下來的帳。」

At the meeting, an elderly animator told an idiom about a borrowed umbrella: the one who holds it may remain dry, but the person who paid for it should not stand in the rain. Chikage smiled at the odd image and then showed a written repayment plan. The numbers were small, but the schedule was real. She proposed an allowance for current trainees, paid first from new commissions, and a separate claims process for historical wages. A former manager tried to flatter her courage. "I didn't come for applause," she answered. "I came so the people who worked can see the ledger." The workers agreed to appoint two representatives to check the records.

會議上，一名年長動畫師講了個關於借傘的俗諺：拿傘的人可以不淋雨，但付錢買傘的人不該站在雨裡。千景對這奇妙比喻微笑，接著拿出書面的還款計畫。數字不大，時程卻是真的。她提議先從新委託案的收入支付現任學徒津貼，歷史欠薪另設申請程序。前經理想奉承她的勇氣。「我不是來討掌聲的。」她回答。「我是要讓真正做事的人看見帳本。」員工們同意選出兩名代表核對紀錄。

The library director said that a town's prosperity could not be judged only by the number of shops on its main street. Children needed safe places to ask questions; older artists needed to know their names had not vanished. Chikage wrote the sentence on the back of the receipt and left it beside the light table. She had expected the screening to buy them a few days of time. Instead it had brought an audience, a small amount of income, and a threat from someone still protecting the last reel. None of those things was enough to explain why Akari was afraid.

圖書館主任說，一座小鎮的繁榮不能只看商店街上有多少店。孩子需要能安心提問的地方，年長畫師也需要知道自己的名字沒有消失。千景把這句話寫在收據背面，放到燈箱旁。她本以為這次放映只能替工房買到幾天時間，沒想到卻帶來觀眾、一小筆收入，還有一封出自某個仍想守住最後一捲底片之人的威脅。這一切依然不足以解釋燈里究竟在害怕什麼。

On screening night, the library hall filled with families. Akari's little parachute drifted across the painted harbor, and the audience laughed at her silly song. Afterward a child asked why the credits included so many people. "Because animation is made by many hands," Chikage said. The composer received his fee, the apprentices their allowances, and the old workers a copy of the repayment schedule. Yet just as the hall emptied, a stranger slipped an envelope beneath Chikage's coat. It contained a photograph of the canceled show's master negative and a note: STOP LOOKING FOR THE LAST REEL. For the first time, Akari's painted smile vanished without the lamp being switched off.

放映當晚，圖書館大廳擠滿家庭。燈里的小降落傘飄過畫中的港灣，她滑稽的片尾歌逗得觀眾大笑。結束後有個孩子問，為什麼片尾名單那麼多人。「因為動畫是很多隻手一起做的。」千景說。作曲家領到使用費，學徒領到津貼，老員工則拿到還款時程的副本。然而，觀眾散去時，一個陌生人悄悄把信封塞到千景外套下。裡面是一張取消動畫的原始底片照片，還有一句話：「別再找最後一捲。」第一次，燈箱明明沒有關，燈里畫好的笑容卻消失了。
