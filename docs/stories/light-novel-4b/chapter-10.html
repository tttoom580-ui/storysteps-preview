道歉不是大結局

An Apology Is Not an Ending

1998，被刪除的女主角拒絕退場！ · 第 10 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Fumie came to the studio carrying an old bandage box and the kind of stern expression that could stop a noisy classroom. She had heard about the missing reel. "I owe people an apology," she said, "but I won't accept blame for decisions I could not make." Chikage asked her to explain the final meeting with the sponsor. Fumie described a critical dispute over a scene in which Akari refused to leave a ruined village. The sponsor called the setting unsuitable for prosperous families. The director wanted to preserve the settlers' story near an ancient tomb; the executives preferred a spectacular explosion. Fumie had voted against the change, then lost her job as producer.

房江提著一盒舊繃帶來到工房，神情嚴厲得足以讓吵鬧教室瞬間安靜。她聽說了失蹤底片。「我欠某些人一個道歉。」她說。「但我不會替自己無權決定的事承擔全部責任。」千景請她解釋當年最後一次與贊助商開會的情況。房江說，雙方為了一幕燈里拒絕離開受損村落的場景激烈爭執。贊助商認為那不適合富裕家庭觀看。導演想保留古墓旁定居者的故事，主管們卻偏好一場壯觀爆炸。房江投票反對修改，最後失去了製作人的職位。

Yui found a handwritten resolution from that meeting and read it aloud. It forbade the use of the director's original ending, but the resolution lacked the required second signature. "Suspicious," Chikage said. She decided to illustrate the sequence of approvals rather than accuse anyone immediately. The former sponsor's successor, now a surgeon at the city hospital, had no connection to the animation business except that he inherited some papers. He agreed to search his father's files. Akari watched from the light table, her tiny boots resting on a peaceful village road. "I didn't want to abandon anyone," she whispered. "That was the whole point of the story."

小結找到那場會議的手寫決議，當眾念出。上面禁止使用導演原本的結局，但缺少規定要有的第二個簽名。「可疑。」千景說。她決定先畫出核准程序的時間線，不急著指控任何人。前贊助商的繼承人現在是市立醫院的外科醫師，除了繼承部分文件，與動畫生意沒有關係。他同意搜尋父親留下的檔案。燈里站在燈箱上，靴子踩著畫好的寧靜村道。「我本來就不想拋下任何人。」她低聲說。「那才是整個故事的重點。」

That afternoon, the hospital invited the studio to make a children's film explaining why certain procedures require special care. Chikage was tempted to bargain for a high fee, then asked what the hospital actually needed. The surgeon wanted an accurate, friendly explanation, not a medical promise the film could not fulfill. Yui suggested a scene in which a family congratulated someone after a successful recovery, but the surgeon reminded them that every patient's situation differed. They wrote a simple script that offered no guarantee of results. Chikage found it easier to respect a client's limits when those limits were clearly stated, not hidden inside a contract.

當天下午，醫院邀請工房製作兒童短片，說明為何某些醫療程序需要特別謹慎。千景原本想討價還價爭取高額費用，最後改問醫院真正需要什麼。外科醫師想要準確又友善的解說，不是動畫無法兌現的醫療承諾。小結提議畫家人慶祝病人順利康復的場景，醫師提醒她們每位病人的情況都不同。她們寫出不保證特定結果的簡單劇本。千景發現，只要委託人的限制說得清楚，而不是藏在合約裡，尊重起來就容易得多。

A fax arrived from the sponsor's former office. It contained a document with the title "Final Website Rights" and a date in early 1998. Chikage knew that small companies were beginning to publish material on websites, but the original film had been made years earlier. Why did someone suddenly need internet rights to a canceled show? The attachment listed a person who had never worked at the studio. Chikage added the mystery to her evidence board. A child visiting the workshop asked whether Akari was a real heroine. "She is real to the people who drew her," Chikage said. It was the most honest answer she could give without explaining the light table.

前贊助商辦公室傳來一張傳真，文件標題是「最終網站權利」，日期是一九九八年初。千景知道小公司開始把資料放上網站，但原始動畫早在多年前就已製作，為什麼有人突然需要一部取消作品的網路權利？附件列出一個從未在工房工作過的人。千景把這個謎團加到證據板上。一名參觀工房的小孩問燈里是不是真的女主角。「對畫出她的人而言，她是真的。」千景回答。在不解釋燈箱祕密的情況下，這是她能給的最誠實答案。

A physician visiting the studio approved the hospital film's calm tone. He pointed out that even a pregnant patient or a small child might watch the animation, so a mere entertaining picture could not replace clear guidance. The old script had described the rise of a civilization around the ancient tomb, but it should not imply medical practices survived unchanged for centuries. Chikage instructed Yui to install a new reference board beside the camera, then did forbid anyone from using unverified health claims. Akari described their rules as a tiny revolution in the kingdom of shortcuts. Chikage corrected her: this was ordinary care, and ordinary care was difficult enough.

一名來工房的醫師認可醫院影片平穩的語氣。他提醒大家，孕婦或小孩都可能觀看，因此一張僅供娛樂的圖畫不能取代清楚的指引。舊劇本描寫古墓旁文明的興起，但不該暗示數百年來的醫療方式都未改變。千景請小結在攝影機旁安裝新的參考板，並禁止大家採用未經核實的健康宣稱。燈里把這些規定稱作捷徑王國裡的小革命。千景糾正她：這只是普通的照顧，而普通的照顧本來就夠困難了。

Before leaving, Fumie asked for a private conversation. She confessed that she had kept one copy of the unpaid payroll because she feared the papers would be destroyed. "I thought hiding them would protect the artists," she said. "I see now that hiding also delayed their claims." Chikage could not grant forgiveness on behalf of eleven people. She offered to help her mother speak to them directly and make the records available. Akari listened without her usual jokes. An apology was not a magical resolution, Chikage thought. It was the first frame of a harder scene, one in which the people who were hurt were finally allowed to choose what happened next.

離開前，房江要求私下談談。她坦承自己保存了一份欠薪名冊，因為怕文件會被毀掉。「我以為藏起來就能保護畫師。」她說。「現在才看見，隱藏也耽誤了他們申請補償。」千景不能代替十一個人原諒母親。她提議陪母親親自向他們說明，並公開可查核的資料。燈里罕見地沒有開玩笑。千景心想，道歉不是魔法般的最終決議，而是更困難一幕的第一格；在那個場景裡，真正受傷的人終於能自己決定接下來的事。
