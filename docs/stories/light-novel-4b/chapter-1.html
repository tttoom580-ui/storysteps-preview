沒畫完的少女

The Girl in the Unfinished Frame

In April 1998, Morino Chikage returned to the small animation studio behind Tsukimi Station with two suitcases and a terrible prediction: it would be sold by Friday. She was thirty-five, and the studio's founder was her mother. A rain-soaked notice announced a property presentation for potential buyers. Chikage carried a cushion from her childhood cottage and set it on the cracked office chair. Her mother had always called animation a spiritual calling; Chikage considered the unpaid overtime a tremendous violation of common sense. Still, the smell of paper and paint made her chest ache. "I came to close the place properly," she told the empty room. "Don't flatter yourself."

一九九八年四月，三十五歲的森野千景拖著兩個行李箱，回到月見車站後方的小動畫工房。她的預測十分糟糕：星期五以前，工房就會被賣掉。創辦工房的是她母親。門上被雨淋濕的公告寫著要為潛在買家舉行資產說明會。千景把從童年小屋帶來的坐墊放上裂開的辦公椅。母親總說動畫是一種精神使命；千景則認為不支薪加班是嚴重違反常識的事。然而，紙與顏料的氣味仍讓她胸口微微發疼。「我是來好好關門的。」她對空房間說。「別自作多情。」

A fierce little voice answered, "Then why are you sitting on my light?" Chikage nearly fell off the chair. A girl no taller than a pencil stood inside the bright rectangle of an old light table. She wore a blue pilot's jacket and held a paper parachute. Her name, painted beneath the transparent sheet, was Akari. Chikage remembered the rejected heroine from a canceled children's show. "Are you a genius trick by some visual artist?" she asked. Akari crossed her arms. "I possess a name, not a trick. And you're blocking my sky." Chikage's laugh came out as a nervous giggle. She switched the lamp off. The girl instantly became a motionless drawing.

一個兇巴巴的小聲音回答：「那妳幹嘛坐在我的光上？」千景差點從椅子上跌下來。一名只有鉛筆那麼高的少女站在老舊燈箱的明亮長方形裡，穿著藍色飛行外套，手裡拿著紙降落傘。透明賽璐珞片下方寫著她的名字：燈里。千景認得這位遭到取消的兒童動畫女主角。「妳是哪位視覺藝術天才設計的惡作劇嗎？」千景問。燈里雙手抱胸。「我有名字，不是惡作劇。而且妳擋住我的天空了。」千景緊張地咯咯笑了兩聲。她關掉燈，少女立即恢復成一張不動的畫。

Chikage switched it on again, carefully keeping her hands outside the painted border. Akari reappeared with a sigh. She could move only where an actual painted background lay beneath the transparent cel. When Chikage slid a blank sheet under her feet, Akari froze; when she restored the cloudy sky, the girl moved again. "It's a defense against falling off the story," Akari explained, sounding pessimistic. Chikage tested the rule twice, never touching the character's painted surface. She wrote the results on a reference card. In a world full of diverse television miracles, she could at least witness one in a controlled way. The tiny pilot demanded to know whether her canceled show had ever reached its ending.

千景重新打開燈，刻意讓手避開畫面邊界。燈里嘆了口氣，又能活動了。她只能在賽璐珞片下方有實際繪製背景的地方移動。千景換上一張空白紙，燈里立刻僵住；換回雲朵天空，少女才恢復動作。「這是防止我掉出故事的防線。」燈里悲觀地說。千景小心測試兩次，始終不碰角色的顏料表面，然後把結果寫在參考卡上。電視裡或許充滿千奇百怪的奇蹟，但她至少可以用有控制的方式見證眼前這一件。小小飛行員追問，她那部被取消的動畫到底有沒有迎來結局。

"No," Chikage admitted. "The sponsor withdrew money after the first six episodes were painted." Akari turned toward an imaginary audience as if preparing for a workout rather than a confession. "Then we finish it!" "With what money?" Chikage asked. The answer came from the doorway, where a young apprentice stood holding a box of registration forms. Yui had been hired last month, apparently without anyone telling Chikage. "The town library wants a short film about the diversity of local stories," she said. "It's small, but it's paid." Akari waved both arms in fierce celebration. Chikage looked from the talking cel to the dusty accounting books. One impossible girl had already made her closing schedule completely useless.

「沒有。」千景承認。「贊助商在前六集畫完後撤回資金。」燈里轉向想像中的觀眾，架勢活像準備晨跑而不是聽壞消息。「那就把它完成！」千景問：「錢從哪裡來？」答案從門口傳來。一名年輕學徒抱著一箱報名表站在那裡。小結是上個月新聘的員工，顯然沒人告訴千景。「鎮立圖書館想委託一支介紹地方故事的短片。」她說。「預算不多，但有薪水。」燈里揮舞雙臂，激動得不得了。千景看看會說話的賽璐珞少女，再看看滿是灰塵的帳本。一個不可能存在的角色，已經把她的關門行程搞得一團糟。

Before the buyers' appointment, Yui found an exercise chart left by a stunt animator. It advised a gentle workout before a long day at the drawing desk. Chikage pinned it above the kettle, where everyone would see it. She also checked the label on the parachute cel: the prop had been designed by a junior painter, not the famous director who received the original praise. That small correction did not solve the studio's debt. It did, however, establish a rule Chikage intended to keep: no miracle would be permitted to steal the ordinary work behind it.

買家來訪前，小結找到一張以前特技動畫師留下的運動表，建議長時間伏案畫圖前先做溫和運動。千景把它釘在熱水壺上方，讓每個人都看得到。她還查了降落傘賽璐珞片的標籤：這件道具其實由初級畫師設計，而不是當年受到讚美的名導演。這項小小更正當然無法解決債務，卻確立了千景打算遵守的規則：任何奇蹟都不能偷走背後平凡勞動者的功勞。

That evening, Chikage called her mother at the retirement apartment. Fumie listened without interrupting, then asked whether the light table still had its original heat shield. "A lamp is not a toy," she said. "Neither is a person." Chikage hesitated to convey what she had seen. Instead she asked who owned the rejected pilot drawings. Her mother answered that the studio possessed the originals but not every musical recording. "Do not promise a finished film before checking the contracts." Akari, listening from the painted sky, held a finger to her lips. Chikage realized that keeping a magical secret and keeping honest accounts might become the same difficult job. On Friday, buyers would arrive; on Thursday, she would need a plan.

那天晚上，千景打電話到母親居住的退休公寓。房江安靜聽完，第一句卻是問燈箱原本的隔熱板還在不在。「燈不是玩具。」她說。「人也不是。」千景猶豫著要不要傳達自己看見的奇蹟，最後改問那些廢棄試播片究竟歸誰所有。母親回答，工房擁有原始畫稿，卻不擁有全部配樂錄音。「查清楚合約前，不要承諾完成影片。」燈里坐在畫好的天空裡，對千景豎起一根手指示意保密。千景突然發現，守住奇幻祕密與守住誠實帳目，也許會變成同一份困難的工作。星期五買家就會上門；星期四之前，她得有個辦法。
