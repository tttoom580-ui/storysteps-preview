The Composer's Missing Cue

1998，被刪除的女主角拒絕退場！ · 第 8 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The composer arrived on a rainy Friday with a heavy suitcase and an exhausted smile. He was a learned musician who took research seriously. He knew the studio was trying to recover the missing final reel, and he brought his own handwritten cue sheets. They contained music notes beside scenes about a fertile valley, immigration, and the first settlers of the harbor. "I wrote the music for the heroine's departure," he said. "But the director asked me to remove it." Chikage noticed that one cue was crossed out with a different pen. A systematic review could distinguish an ordinary correction from an unexplained alteration, but it could not identify the person who held the pen.

作曲家在一個下雨的星期五提著沉重行李箱抵達，臉上掛著疲憊的微笑。他是位學養深厚、重視研究的音樂人。他知道工房在找最後一捲底片，便帶來自己的手寫配樂提示單。那些紙上有音符，也記錄著肥沃山谷、移民及港口早期定居者的場景。「我替女主角的離別寫過曲子。」他說。「但導演叫我刪掉。」千景注意到其中一個提示被不同顏色的筆劃掉。系統性的核對可以分辨普通修改和無法解釋的變動，卻不能直接指出握筆的人是誰。

Yui had acquired a used computer running basic editing software, though most original film still needed a physical camera. She entered the cue times and produced a comparison chart. The estimated length of the missing scene was ninety seconds. Akari reacted with tremendous enthusiasm. "Ninety seconds! That's an entire final battle!" "Or a quiet conversation," Chikage replied. The original script described a bridegroom waiting for a ferry and a girl choosing a different voyage. It was not a romance built on jealousy; the scene was about separating duty from personal choice. The composer said the sponsor had wanted a simpler story in which the heroine obeyed.

小結買到一部二手電腦，能運行基本剪輯軟體，雖然大多數原始膠片仍得用真正的攝影機處理。她輸入配樂時間，做出比較表。失蹤場景估計長九十秒。燈里熱情得不得了。「九十秒！那夠打一整場最終決戰了！」「也可能只是一段安靜對話。」千景回答。原始劇本寫著一名新郎在渡輪旁等待，一個女孩則決定走向不同航程。那不是以嫉妒為核心的戀愛故事，而是區分責任與個人選擇。作曲家說，贊助商更想要女主角乖乖服從的簡單劇情。

A bulletin in the hallway announced that the local arts council would consider small grants for archival projects. Chikage asked whether a canceled animation could constitute an archive rather than a commercial product. The clerk explained that the proposal had to estimate costs, identify rights holders, and show what the public could actually learn. "A beautiful picture alone isn't an argument," he said. The council also required attention to gender in historical credit lists, since many women had been entered merely as assistants. Chikage felt a gulf opening between the old production culture and the kind of studio she wanted to build. She began a list of names from the surviving payroll.

走廊公告欄貼著地方藝術委員會將審查小額典藏補助的消息。千景詢問，一部被取消的動畫能不能構成典藏計畫，而不只是商業產品。承辦員解釋，提案必須估算成本、指出權利人，還要說明大眾究竟能學到什麼。「好看的畫面本身不算論證。」他說。委員會也要求留意舊演職員表中的性別問題，因為許多女性當年只被登記為助手。千景感覺舊製作文化與自己希望打造的工房之間，有道很深的鴻溝。她開始根據保存下來的薪資資料列出所有姓名。

That afternoon, Yui discovered that the missing cue had been entered under an abstract title, The Other Shore. Chikage listened to the demo tape through headphones. The music grew in intensity but never reached a final note. "It needs the image," the composer said. Akari stood inside a carefully prepared painted frame and tried to remember the last scene. Her answer came in fragments: a lamp, an open gate, a choice not to invade another person's life. Nothing resembled a battle. Chikage was almost certain the sponsor had rewritten the ending, but she told the group not to treat instinct as proof. She needed an original document or a witness who knew.

下午，小結發現失蹤配樂用了一個抽象標題：《彼岸》。千景戴耳機聽試錄卡帶。音樂強度逐漸增加，卻始終沒有真正的最後一個音。「它需要畫面。」作曲家說。燈里站在準備好的畫格裡，努力回想最後一幕。她只能想起零碎片段：一盞燈、一扇敞開的門、一個不願侵入別人生活的選擇。沒有任何部分像戰鬥。千景幾乎能確定贊助商改寫了結局，但她要大家別把直覺當成證據。她需要原始文件，或真正知情的證人。

In the old episode, a civilian nurse had waited outside the harbor gate while soldiers argued over the route. The sponsor's revised storyboard allowed the soldiers to dominate the scene, leaving the nurse without a line. Chikage compared both versions and noted the change in the grant application. Akari watched her old self disappear from one frame after another. 'Did I fail because I was too quiet?' she asked. 'No,' Chikage replied. 'Someone made a decision about which voices were worth recording.' The composer added that the original score had included a pause for the nurse's response. The silence had been composed deliberately.

舊集數裡，一名平民護士在港口大門外等待，士兵則為路線爭吵。贊助商修改的分鏡卻讓士兵主導整幕，護士連一句台詞都沒有。千景比較兩個版本，在補助申請中標出變更。燈里看著舊版的自己一格格消失。「是不是因為我太安靜才失敗？」她問。「不是。」千景回答。「有人決定哪些聲音值得錄下來。」作曲家補充，原始配樂本來留了一段給護士回答的停頓。那段安靜是刻意寫進音樂的。

Before leaving, the composer placed a folded sheet in Chikage's hand. It was a copy of the final music invoice, unpaid and signed by the sponsor's former director. "I was angry enough to burn it," he admitted. "Then I thought someone might need it one day." Chikage promised that the council application would include the debt rather than hide it. Akari asked to hear the unfinished music once more. The composer agreed, but only on a low volume to protect the old cassette. For ninety seconds the room was silent except for the piano. When it stopped, Akari said, "I think that was the sound of my real goodbye."

離開前，作曲家把一張折好的紙交到千景手裡。那是最後一筆配樂款的發票副本，仍未付款，卻有贊助商前董事的簽名。「我氣到差點把它燒掉。」他承認。「後來想，也許總有一天會有人需要。」千景答應補助申請會把欠款列出，不會藏起來。燈里請他再放一次沒有結尾的音樂。作曲家答應了，卻把音量調低，以保護老卡帶。九十秒裡，房間只剩鋼琴聲。音樂停下時，燈里說：「我想，那可能才是我真正道別的聲音。」
