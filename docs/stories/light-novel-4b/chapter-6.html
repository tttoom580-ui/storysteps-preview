不肯保持藍色的顏料

The Dye That Refused to Stay Blue

1998，被刪除的女主角拒絕退場！ · 第 6 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first person to recognize the photograph of the missing reel was a former coloring assistant named Hara. She arrived wearing a conservative linen suit and carrying a sample book of blue dye. "I worked here before the studio could afford a consultant," she said. "The company asked us to isolate faulty paint, then sent the same goods back to our tables." Chikage spread the old invoices on a clean cloth. The figures suggested that a supplier had charged a luxury price for cheap pigment. Hara's habitual grace disappeared as she read the names. "I was ignorant of the invoices," she whispered. "But I wasn't ignorant of what the fumes did to my hands."

第一個認出失蹤底片照片的人，是以前的上色助手原小姐。她穿著保守的亞麻套裝，帶來一本藍色染料樣本。「我在工房還請不起顧問時就在這裡工作。」她說。「公司叫我們隔離有問題的顏料，卻又把同一批貨送回工作桌。」千景在乾淨布上攤開舊發票，數字顯示供應商可能把廉價顏料賣成奢侈品價格。原小姐一向優雅的神情在讀到名字時消失。「我不知道那些發票的事。」她低聲說。「但我知道那些氣味對我的手造成什麼影響。」

Yui borrowed a microscope from the town high school to examine the damaged cel under a teacher's supervision. The picture revealed tiny cracks in the blue paint, not evidence of supernatural rage. Akari watched from the safe edge of the light box, unusually timid. "I don't like how that color looks," she said. "It reminds me of a storm that never ends." Chikage turned off the lamp before the girl could tremble any longer. The teacher explained that the microscope could show damage but could not identify the exact chemical without proper testing. "No guessing," Chikage repeated. "We've already seen what happens when an answer arrives before the question."

小結在老師監督下，向鎮立高中借來顯微鏡檢查受損畫片。鏡頭下看到的是藍色顏料的細裂紋，不是什麼超自然暴怒的證據。燈里待在燈箱安全的邊緣觀看，難得顯得膽怯。「我不喜歡那個顏色。」她說。「它讓我想到永遠不會停的風暴。」千景在她繼續發抖前關掉燈。老師解釋，顯微鏡能看見損傷，卻無法不經正式檢驗就判斷確切化學成分。「不要猜。」千景重申。「我們已經看過答案比問題先到會造成什麼事了。」

A distributor phoned to ask whether the studio would invest in a modern coloring machine. He promised that consumers wanted brighter colors and faster production. Chikage asked about safety training, repair costs, and the occupation of the workers whose jobs might disappear. He called her difficult. "A machine is not a moral victory just because it is new," she replied. Sora prepared an orientation sheet for anyone entering the paint room. It listed gloves, ventilation, and the right to stop work. Hara volunteered to review it, then insisted that her contribution be paid. Chikage agreed. From the old filing cabinet, a second envelope slipped out and landed at her feet.

發行商打電話來問工房要不要投資新式上色機，還保證消費者喜歡更鮮豔的顏色和更快的製程。千景問起安全訓練、維修成本，以及可能失去工作的員工職業安排。對方說她難搞。「機器不會只因為新，就自動成為道德上的勝利。」她回答。蒼替進入顏料室的人準備職前說明，列出手套、通風和停止工作的權利。原小姐願意協助審查，但堅持自己的貢獻必須付費。千景同意。就在這時，舊檔案櫃裡又滑出第二只信封，落到她腳邊。

Inside was a personal letter written by Fumie to the sponsor in 1993. It described the workers' exposure and asked permission to suspend production. Someone had marked it "Not approved" without a signature. Hara read it and grew quiet. "Your mother tried," she said. "Trying did not protect us, but it matters that the record survived." Chikage decided not to make her mother either a hero or a villain before hearing her version. She enclosed copies with a request for records from the supplier. The original went into an archival sleeve. Akari returned with a small salute. "For once," she said, "I'd rather have a safe workroom than a luxurious palace."

裡面是一九九三年房江寫給贊助商的私人信件，描述員工接觸顏料的情況，並要求暫停製作。有人在上面註記「不批准」，卻沒有簽名。原小姐讀完安靜下來。「妳母親試過了。」她說。「努力本身沒有保護我們，但紀錄保存下來這件事很重要。」千景決定在聽母親說明前，不急著把她塑造成英雄或壞人。她附上副本，向供應商索取資料；原件則收入檔案保護套。燈里敬了個小禮。「這次，」她說，「我寧可有安全的工作室，也不要豪華皇宮。」

Hara remembered the studio's first stereo recording booth, a narrow cabinet beside the paint room. It had once been marketed as a sign of prestige, even while painters were asked to bring their own gloves. Chikage inspected it with Sora and found an old ventilation drawing folded behind the speakers. The drawing showed a fan that was supposed to have been installed years before the questionable dye arrived. There was no completion signature. The discovery did not identify the supplier's motives, but it changed the questions they would ask. Chikage scheduled an independent inspection and told everyone the paint room would remain closed until it passed.

原小姐記得工房第一間立體聲錄音室，就在顏料室旁的一座狹窄櫥櫃裡。以前公司把它宣傳成光榮象徵，同時卻要求畫師自備手套。千景和蒼去檢查，發現喇叭後面折著一張舊通風圖。圖上畫著一臺理應在有問題的染料進貨前多年就已安裝的風扇，卻沒有驗收簽名。這項發現不能證明供應商的動機，卻改變了她們該提出的問題。千景安排獨立檢查，並宣布顏料室通過驗收前不得恢復使用。

Near midnight, Chikage called the supplier again and heard a trembling voice at the other end. A clerk said he had found the same dye number on two invoices, one marked school materials and one marked commercial animation. The products had been shipped through different companies. He could not prove fraud, and she would not ask him to break the law to help. She requested only copies he was authorized to share. When the call ended, she wrote three columns in her notebook: verified facts, unanswered questions, and people needing protection. The last column was the longest, but it made the first two worth finishing.

接近午夜時，千景再打給供應商，聽見電話那頭有人聲音發抖。一名職員說，他在兩張發票上發現相同染料編號，一張標為學校教材，一張標為商業動畫，貨卻經由不同公司運送。他無法證明詐欺，千景也不會要求他為了幫忙而違法。她只請他提供有權分享的副本。掛上電話後，她在筆記本畫了三欄：已確認事實、尚待回答的問題、需要保護的人。最後一欄最長，卻也讓前兩欄更值得寫完。
