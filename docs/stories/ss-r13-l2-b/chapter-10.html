開門測試

The Open Door Test

留給明天的燈光 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The student office made a classroom available for a longer public rehearsal. Each guest received one biscuit and a small map. Yu-an did not ignore the length of the line outside; she asked Bo to count people by meter along the path so that the doorway stayed clear. The room was allowed to hold a fixed number per session. Some guests were on vacation and had brought friends. Others came alone. Yu-an welcomed each person without promising a seat that did not exist.

學務處提供一間教室讓團隊辦較長的公開排練。每位來客都拿到一片餅乾與一張小地圖。余安沒有忽視門外長長的隊伍；她請博沿路每隔一公尺計算人數，確保門口暢通。每場次容納人數有固定上限。有些客人正在放假，帶著朋友同來；也有人獨自前來。余安歡迎每個人，卻不承諾不存在的座位。

The new musical scene used a fox puppet, a goose puppet, and a snail made from folded paper. The actors needed glue to repair one loose wing, but Wen checked that it had dried before the show. His style was gradual: test one change, watch what happens, and only then make another. "A fox runs fast and a snail moves slowly," he told a child. "Neither one has to become the other to belong in the story." The child laughed and asked to keep the map.

新增的音樂場景有狐狸布偶、鵝布偶，還有摺紙做的蝸牛。演員需要用膠水修好一片鬆掉的翅膀，文先確認乾透才讓它上場。他做事講究循序漸進：一次只測一項變動，觀察結果後再改下一項。他告訴孩子：「狐狸跑得快，蝸牛走得慢；牠們都不需要變成對方，才能屬於故事。」孩子笑了，還問能不能留下地圖。

The next morning, the company received a note from a visitor who had watched the test with her father. Her father used a cane and had been grateful for the wide path. The visitor wrote that courage was not only jumping into danger. Sometimes it meant asking the person beside you what would help. Yu-an read the note to the crew, then asked for one change to the door plan. They placed a chair near the entrance for people who needed to sit while waiting. Wen checked that the chair did not block the route. The group had learned to turn praise into an action instead of hanging it on a wall.

隔天早上，劇團收到一名觀眾的紙條。她曾和使用手杖的父親一起看測試，父親很感謝寬敞動線。觀眾寫道，勇氣不只是跳進危險；有時是問身旁的人需要什麼。余安把紙條讀給全團，接著提出門口計畫的一項改動：讓需要等候時坐下的人有一張椅子。文確認椅子不堵路。劇團已學會把稱讚變成行動，而不只是貼在牆上。

After the first act, the audience began to gather in the hall. One boy said the show deserved a prize already. Yu-an thanked him but explained that the proper test was whether strangers could understand the story and use the paths safely. She passed out a simple card with one question about each scene. The answers were not all correct. Wen saw this as good news: now they knew which parts to make clearer. He wrote one small unit of change beside each question rather than trying to rebuild everything.

第一幕後，觀眾聚到走廊。一名男孩說演出已經值得拿獎。余安謝謝他，卻解釋真正的測試是陌生人能否理解故事，也能安全行走。她發出簡單問卷，每幕只有一題。答案並非全對。文認為這是好消息：他們現在知道哪些部分需要更清楚。他在每個問題旁標上一個小小的改動單位，而不是企圖全部重做。

A cook from the dining hall offered baked yam for the volunteers. They sat in a circle and compared notes. Bo wanted to add six more jokes; the actors wanted a longer final song. Yu-an asked them to choose a change they could really test next week. Their answer was a shorter pause before the final line. It was a small decision, but the team made it together. When everyone called out congratulations to the actors, Yu-an added her thanks to the people who had managed the doors and chairs.

餐廳廚師送來烤地瓜給志工。大家圍坐一圈比對筆記。博想再加六個笑話，演員想把結尾歌曲拉長。余安請他們選一項下週真的能測試的改動。大家最後決定縮短最後一句臺詞前的停頓。這是小小的決定，卻由全隊共同完成。眾人向演員道賀時，余安也向負責門口和座位的人道謝。

At the final check, Yu-an invited a worker who had never seen the play to read the public information. He found the rehearsal time, the entrance, and the way to ask for help. He also noticed that the word "free" might make guests think no registration was needed. Yu-an changed the sentence to say that the event cost nothing but seats were limited. She thanked him for catching the confusion. A clear notice was part of a good performance: it began to care for people before they entered the room. Wen looked at the new sign and said, "That is an opening scene without any actors, and it works."

最後檢查時，余安邀請一名沒看過戲的工作人員閱讀公開資訊。他找到了排練時間、入口和求助方式，也發現「免費」一詞可能讓人以為不用事先登記。余安改成活動不收費，但座位有限，並感謝他的提醒。清楚公告本就是好演出的一部分；它在觀眾進門前就開始照顧人。文看著新告示說：「這是一場沒有演員的開場戲，而且很成功。」

Wen waited until the others had gone before telling Yu-an that her leadership had changed. "You used to ask how quickly we could put the show back in the old room," he said. "Today you asked whether everyone could take part." She thought for a while and admitted that he was right. Then he asked about their promised museum visit. "After we finish this week's report," she replied. "And please remind me to take a real day off." He smiled and promised not to let the work swallow their whole lives.

等其他人離開，文才告訴余安，她的帶領方式變了。「你以前只問多久能把戲搬回舊劇場，」他說，「今天卻在問每個人能否參與。」余安想了一會兒，承認他說得對。接著他問起約好的美術館之行。「等我們寫完這週報告，」她回答，「也請提醒我要真正放一天假。」文笑著答應，不讓工作吞掉兩人的全部生活。
