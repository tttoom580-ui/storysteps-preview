最初的五分鐘

The First Five Minutes

留給明天的燈光 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The cast was ready for a five-minute test, but Yu-an had lost her appetite. A report of electrical damage in another building had spread through campus. People feared their event would close as well. Wen explained the difference between their safe gym and the closed Lantern Room. He did not praise the team for having brilliant ideas; he showed the official room permit and the method they had used to count the chairs. Clear facts gave their guests a reason to trust them.

演員準備好做五分鐘測試，余安卻緊張得沒有食慾。校園裡傳開另一棟建築電路損壞的報告，大家害怕活動也會被取消。文說明安全的體育館與封閉的小劇場有何不同。他沒有只稱讚團隊點子精彩，而是拿出正式場地許可和計算椅子數量的方法。清楚的事實，讓來客有理由信任他們。

Their small play began with a girl carrying a bowl of noodles to an empty house. A rooster called in the distance, then thunder rolled across the sound system. The girl's journey was about a broken connection between two families. Yu-an had feared that the audience would not understand. Instead, they watched closely. When the actor spoke too fast, Wen lowered the music, and the words became clear. Nobody needed a snowy mountain or a mass of expensive lights to believe in the scene.

小戲從一名女孩端著麵條走向空屋開始。遠處響起公雞叫聲，接著音響傳出雷鳴。女孩的旅程關乎兩個家庭斷掉的聯繫。余安原先擔心觀眾看不懂，沒想到大家看得專注。演員說話太快時，文降低背景音樂，話語就清楚了。不必有雪山，也不必堆滿昂貴燈光，觀眾同樣相信那個場景。

A child in the front row asked if the girl in the play would ever return home. The question surprised Yu-an, because it showed the child had followed more than the sound effects. She asked the actor to answer as her character, without giving away the ending. "I do not know yet," the actor said. "But I know the people who want me to come home." The child thought about that answer for a long time. Wen wrote the question in his notes. He wanted the next scene to show the girl's choice more clearly, without making the story longer. One honest line from a child had given the team a useful direction.

前排一名孩子問，戲中的女孩最後會不會回家。余安很意外，因為這代表孩子聽懂的不只是音效。她請演員以角色身分回答，卻不要劇透。「我還不知道，」演員說，「但我知道有人希望我回家。」孩子想了好久。文把問題記進筆記，希望下一幕能更清楚呈現女孩的選擇，又不必把故事拉長。一個孩子誠實的問題，就替團隊指明了有用方向。

Afterward, one visitor said the story touched a domestic conflict in her own life. Another felt the dialogue needed more attention. A child asked why the actor's hands were blue. The answer was harmless stage paint tested on the skin first. Yu-an thanked every person for their words. She invited questions whenever guests needed help. She wanted the opinions to spread through the group without becoming personal blame. Wen wrote down each comment and marked the ones they could test at the next rehearsal.

演出後，一名觀眾說故事讓她想起自己的家庭衝突；另一人認為對話還需要更仔細處理。孩子問演員的手為何是藍色的，答案是已先在皮膚上測試過的安全舞臺顏料。余安感謝每個人，也邀請來客需要協助時隨時發問。她希望意見在團隊裡流通，而不變成人身責備。文記下每條回饋，標出下一次排練能實際測試的項目。

In the hallway, a wool scarf lay on a bench beside a forgotten bag of snacks. Yu-an took it to the office instead of leaving it where anyone could claim it. Wen came back with warm water, and they sat together without rushing. "I thought our support would disappear if we left the old room," she said. "Now I see we can discover other ways to work." He answered that his own plan had changed too. He had joined to repair a few speakers; he now cared about the people listening.

走廊長椅上留著一條羊毛圍巾和一袋點心。余安把它們送到辦公室，沒有放在原地任人領走。文帶著溫水回來，兩人不慌不忙地坐了一會兒。「我原以為離開舊劇場，就會失去大家的支持，」她說，「現在才發現可以有其他做事方式。」文回答自己的計畫也變了：他原本只是來修幾個喇叭，如今卻在乎那些正在聽故事的人。

Later, the actors stood in a circle and each said which moment had felt true. One liked the family argument, because the characters had listened instead of shouting. Another liked the little pause before the girl's answer. A third thought the thunder was too loud. Yu-an wrote every idea beside the scene it belonged to. She did not need to use every idea, but she wanted to remember who had offered it. At the end of the meeting, she asked each person to name one thing they would change and one thing they would protect. The answers were different, and that was exactly what made the company useful.

稍後，演員圍成一圈，逐一說出哪個時刻最真實。有人喜歡家庭爭執，因為角色願意聽而不只吼叫；有人喜歡女孩回答前的小停頓；第三人覺得雷聲太大。余安把每個意見記在所屬場景旁。她不必採用所有想法，卻想記得提出者。會議最後，她請每人說出一項想改的事情和一項想保留的事情。答案各不相同，而那恰好是劇團可貴之處。

Yu-an sent a short report to the department that night. It included the test time, audience comments, a drawing of the chair plan, and one picture of the empty gym after cleanup. She gave the cast full credit and asked for permission to invite more guests next week. There was no promise that the Lantern Room would reopen. Still, five minutes had turned an uncertain idea into a real show. The team celebrated with hot tea, and Yu-an allowed herself one quiet moment of pride before planning tomorrow.

那天晚上，余安把簡短報告寄給系上，附上測試時間、觀眾意見、座位配置圖，和打掃後空體育館的一張照片。她把功勞歸給全體演員，並申請下週邀請更多來客。舊劇場仍沒有重開的承諾，但五分鐘已經把不確定的點子變成真正的演出。團隊用熱茶慶祝；余安在規畫明天之前，也容許自己安靜地驕傲一會兒。
