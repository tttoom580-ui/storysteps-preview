杯子裡的聲音

The Sound in the Cup

留給明天的燈光 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Wen's advice was to test sound in the empty gym before another audience came. The actual room had a low ceiling and made every beat return twice. He set a china cup on a table and tapped its edge. Yu-an could hear one clear note, then a softer one. "Sound is like a listener," he said. "It answers the room it is in." She wrote the address of the new rehearsal space on a board and asked the team to check that every visitor could find it.

文建議在下次觀眾來之前，先測試空體育館的聲音。實際的空間天花板不高，每一下節拍都會反彈兩次。他把一只瓷杯放在桌上，輕敲杯緣。余安先聽到清楚的一聲，接著是較小的回音。「聲音像聽眾，」他說，「會回應所在的房間。」她把新排練空間的地址寫在白板上，請大家確認每位來客都找得到。

The costume designer arrived just before midnight with a necklace and an old camera lens. She wanted to use the necklace as a sign of freedom for a prisoner in the story. The necklace was valuable to her grandmother, so she did not want an actor to run with it. Yu-an helped her sort the objects and offered to make a safe copy instead. "None of us should risk someone else's memory for a scene," she said. Their friend Bo, usually naughty and full of jokes, agreed at once.

服裝設計師在午夜前帶來一條項鍊和一枚舊相機鏡頭。她想用項鍊象徵故事中囚犯的自由。但項鍊是祖母珍惜的物品，不願讓演員戴著奔跑。余安協助她分類道具，提議改製安全的複製品。「我們不該為了場景，讓別人的記憶冒險。」她說。平常愛惡作劇的博這次立刻同意。

The camera student asked Wen whether a show needed constant music to keep people interested. He answered by asking her to close her eyes and listen to the room for half a minute. They heard a distant door, a fan, and one person laughing at the other end of the hall. "There is already sound here," he said. "Our work is to choose when to add something and when to leave space." Yu-an joined them and tried the same exercise. She noticed how a quiet room could still feel full. Then she asked the actors to perform the first line without any music at all. The words carried more feeling than she had expected.

攝影學生問文，一齣戲是否需要不停播放音樂，才能維持觀眾興趣。文請她閉上眼睛，聽半分鐘。遠處的門聲、電風扇和走廊另一端的笑聲都傳來。「這裡本來就有聲音，」他說，「我們的工作是選擇何時加入，何時留白。」余安也一起試，發現安靜的房間仍能很飽滿。接著她請演員完全不放音樂說出第一句臺詞，那些文字傳出的情感比她想像中更多。

A visiting expert in sound suggested a different form for the first scene. Instead of a recorded voice, an actor could speak from the back row while the others turned toward her. That would help show the origin of the story. Yu-an worried the change would take away Wen's role, but he said he had no need to control every sound. "If a voice is clearer without me, that is good work," he replied. She found the answer both practical and unexpectedly pleasant.

一位來訪的聲學專家建議第一幕採用不同形式：不播錄音，而讓演員在後排說話，其他人轉身面向她。這樣更能表現故事的起源。余安擔心改動會削弱文的角色，但他說不必控制每一種聲音。「如果沒有我反而讓人聽得更清楚，那就是好工作。」他回答。余安覺得這句話既實際，又意外令人愉快。

After rehearsal, they shared toast from a late food stand. Wen asked how Yu-an had first become interested in theater. She described a school play where she had been too frightened to speak. Someone had offered her a place behind the curtain, and she had discovered she liked making other voices possible. "That sounds like the whole reason you are still here," Wen said. Yu-an looked at him across the table. He seemed to understand a part of her that she rarely had time to explain.

排練後，兩人在深夜小吃攤分享烤吐司。文問余安為何喜歡劇場。她說起自己在學校演出時，曾怕得不敢說話。有人讓她躲到布幕後方幫忙，她才發現自己喜歡讓其他人的聲音被聽見。「這聽起來就是你還留在這裡的原因。」文說。余安隔著桌子望著他，感覺他似乎理解了她很少有空解釋的一部分。

Yu-an went home through the main square because the side street was under repair. She took the longer route rather than walk beside the open work area. Wen kept talking about how the actors had handled the new scene. She found that she liked hearing him think out loud, not only hearing his finished answers. When he realized he had been speaking for several minutes, he stopped and asked about her day. She told him about the actor who had been afraid to disappoint the group. They discussed how a leader could make it easier for someone to say no. Their conversation lasted until the buses arrived, and neither seemed in a hurry to end it.

余安回家時穿過主廣場，因側街正在維修。她寧願繞遠路，也不走在開放的工區旁。文一路談演員如何處理新場景。余安發現自己不只喜歡聽他的結論，也喜歡聽他邊想邊說。文察覺自己講了好幾分鐘，就停下來問她今天如何。她說起那名怕讓大家失望的演員，兩人討論領導者怎樣讓人更容易說不。談話一直延續到公車進站，誰都不急著結束。

They were not alone in the hall; cleaners were preparing the rooms for the morning. Yu-an thanked them and moved her bag away from their path. She did not want her pleasant night to become someone else's extra work. On the walk home, Wen asked if she would like to visit an art museum together when the rehearsal week ended. She said she would, but only on a day when both could really rest. They agreed to choose the date later, without turning a simple invitation into a test.

大廳裡不只有他們；清潔人員正準備隔天早上的教室。余安向他們道謝，並把包包移開，不讓自己的好夜晚增加別人的工作。走回家時，文問排練週結束後，是否願意一起參觀美術館。余安說願意，但要挑兩人都能真正休息的一天。他們同意之後再定日期，不把簡單邀請弄成一場考試。
