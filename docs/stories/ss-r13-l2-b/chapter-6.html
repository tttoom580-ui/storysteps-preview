聽見雨的人

An Ear for Rain

留給明天的燈光 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The crowd arrived early on the eve of their first open rehearsal. A storm was moving toward campus, and the western windows rattled. Yu-an had to accept a hard fact: the building officer could close the gym if water entered the floor. She asked everyone to examine the exits before setting out chairs. A child in a bright raincoat waited on the sidewalk with her mother. They wanted to watch the show, but Yu-an explained that nobody would enter until the officer approved the room.

公開排練前夕，觀眾提早到來。暴風雨正朝校園靠近，西側窗戶不斷作響。余安必須接受一個難以改變的事實：如果雨水淹進地板，校舍管理員就能封閉體育館。她請大家在排椅子前先檢查出口。一名穿著鮮亮雨衣的孩子和母親在騎樓下等候。她們想看演出，但余安說明，管理員核可之前沒人能進去。

Wen brought a musician from the music school to repair a noisy speaker. The young woman listened carefully and said the problem was not the sound box but the cable. "It is my favorite sort of problem," she joked. "A small cause makes a very big noise." She was sensitive to sudden sounds, so Yu-an promised to warn her before a test. The musician also offered a short piece of poetry for the opening scene. Wen asked the author before using it, then wrote her name on the program.

文請來音樂學院的一位音樂家幫忙修理雜音喇叭。她仔細聽過後指出，問題不在音箱，而在線材。「這是我最喜歡的問題，」她笑說，「小原因卻造成大聲響。」她對突然的聲音很敏感，余安便答應測試前先提醒她。這名音樂家也提供了一小段詩作當開場。文先取得作者同意，再把她的名字寫進節目單。

Before the officer arrived, Yu-an asked Bo to move the costumes away from the doors. He began to carry everything in one trip, but she reminded him that a wet floor made heavy boxes harder to control. They worked in pairs, each person taking one light load. A visitor watched and asked why the actors were helping with such ordinary tasks. "Because an actor is also a person who uses the room," Yu-an replied. "If we do not help keep it safe, nobody will enjoy what happens inside." The visitor offered to hold the door, and Yu-an accepted after showing her where to stand. The little work did not stop the storm, but it made the waiting less frightening.

管理員抵達前，余安請博把戲服移離門口。博想一次搬完，她提醒濕地板會讓重箱更難控制。大家兩人一組，每人只拿一件輕物。一名來客看著問，為何演員也要做這麼普通的事。「因為演員也是使用這個房間的人，」余安說，「不一起維護安全，裡面的表演就沒人能享受。」來客願意幫忙扶門，余安示範站位後接受。這些小工作不能阻止暴風雨，卻讓等待沒那麼可怕。

Their official plan allowed the audience to leave and return without losing a seat. Yu-an thought of a war story she had once seen, where the hero refused to leave a dangerous place. She did not want that kind of hero for her own show. "Courage can mean knowing when to stop," she said. An actor suggested a giraffe puppet for the last scene, to make the ending less heavy. The idea was silly, but it brought laughter from people who had been looking at the dark sky.

正式的觀眾安排允許人們離開後再回到座位，不會因此失去位置。余安想起以前看過一則戰爭故事，英雄拒絕離開危險地點。她不希望自己的戲也有這樣的英雄。「勇敢也可能是知道什麼時候該停下來。」她說。一名演員提議最後一幕加上長頸鹿布偶，讓結尾別那麼沉重。點子有點傻，卻讓原本緊盯烏雲的人笑了出來。

The rain became strong, and the officer asked them to wait. Rather than argue over a short term delay, Yu-an led everyone to the covered hall. She told the visitors how the show was made and let them ask questions about each character. Wen stood beside her, keeping his equipment covered. When someone said the theater had no soul without its old walls, he pointed to the actors telling stories under the roof. "Listen," he said. "The soul is here, even when the stage is not."

雨變大了，管理員請所有人等候。余安沒有為了短暫延期爭辯，而是帶大家到有遮雨棚的大廳，說明戲如何製作，並讓來客發問。文站在她身旁，替器材蓋上保護布。有人說失去舊牆的劇場沒有靈魂，文指著屋頂下正在說故事的演員：「聽，靈魂就在這裡。就算舞臺不在，也沒有離開。」

When the rain slowed, a little boy asked to hear the song they had planned. Wen did not turn on the equipment. Instead, he invited the musician to hum only if she wanted to. She smiled and sang a few quiet notes without words. A second child joined, then a parent. Yu-an could see that the music had no need for a big room to make people feel close. Yet she also understood that this warm moment was not permission to begin a show in an unsafe space. She thanked the group and helped people find the dry exit. The difference between a kind moment and an official performance mattered, especially when children were present.

雨勢轉小時，一名男孩想聽原訂的歌曲。文沒有打開器材，只問音樂家願不願意哼幾句。她笑著輕聲唱了幾個沒有歌詞的音，一名孩子跟著唱，接著一位家長也加入。余安發現，音樂不需要大房間，也能讓人靠近。但她也明白，這溫暖片刻不等於可以在危險空間正式開演。她向大家道謝，協助人們找到乾燥出口。尤其有孩子在場時，友善時刻與正式演出的界線仍很重要。

The officer finally ruled that the gym would stay closed for the evening. Yu-an announced the decision herself and gave everyone a new time. Some people looked disappointed, yet nobody was put in danger. When the visitors left, Wen told her that he had liked the way she spoke to the child in the raincoat. "You explained the truth without making her feel small," he said. Yu-an blushed and changed the subject to wet shoes. Later, she found she was smiling as she folded the unused programs.

管理員最後判定體育館當晚不能使用。余安親自宣布決定，提供下一次時間。有些人失望，卻沒有任何人因此冒險。來客離開時，文告訴余安，他很欣賞她向穿雨衣的孩子解釋的方式。「你說了真話，也沒有讓她覺得自己不重要。」余安臉紅了，趕緊把話題轉到濕鞋子。後來整理沒用上的節目單時，她發現自己還在微笑。
