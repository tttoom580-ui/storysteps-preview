屋頂上方的便條

A Note Above the Roof

留給明天的燈光 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The inspection report arrived with an exact description of the Lantern Room's damage. Water had entered under the roof and reached a protective box near the main lights. The college would delay any decision about using the room until repair work was complete. Yu-an felt upset but read every page. "There is no evil person hiding in this paper," she told the actors. "There is only a problem that must be fixed." She selected the sections that guests needed to know and prepared a clear public reply.

檢查報告送來，精確描述燈籠小劇場的損壞：雨水從屋頂下滲入，流到主燈附近的保護箱。學院會延後決定是否重用劇場，直到修繕完成。余安很難過，卻把每頁讀完。「這份紙裡沒有藏著邪惡的人，」她告訴演員，「只有必須解決的問題。」她選出來客需要知道的部分，準備清楚的公開回覆。

At rehearsal, the sound system made a sudden click, and a young actor twisted his ankle while turning. The movement stopped at once. Nobody tried to make him swing his foot again for the sake of the scene. Wen called the health office, and Yu-an took down what had happened. Outside, a dog began to bark at a passing bicycle. The ordinary sound made the room feel strangely calm after the surprise. The actor could walk, but they still asked him to rest and seek medical advice.

排練時，音響突然發出喀的一聲，一名年輕演員轉身時扭傷腳踝。大家立即停下，沒有人為了場景要求他再擺動腳。文打電話給保健室，余安記錄經過。外頭一隻狗對經過的腳踏車吠叫。驚嚇之後，這平凡聲音反而讓房間感到奇妙的安靜。演員仍能走路，但大家還是請他休息並就醫諮詢。

In the rehearsal kitchen, an actor held a spoon and practiced offering soup to the lost traveler. The first version was quick and funny, but Yu-an felt that the traveler had no choice. She asked the actor to select a different action. He held out the spoon, waited, and let the traveler decide whether to take it. The change lasted only a few seconds. Yet the scene was now about kindness that could be accepted or refused, not kindness pushed upon another person. Wen noticed the difference too. He said a small action could show a rule better than a long speech. The actors kept the revised moment in the script.

排練用的小廚房裡，一名演員拿著湯匙練習把湯端給迷路的旅人。第一版快速又好笑，但余安覺得旅人沒有選擇。她請演員挑另一個動作。他伸出湯匙、等候，讓旅人決定接不接。這項改動只有幾秒，場景卻變成可以接受或拒絕的善意，而不是把善意強加在別人身上。文也注意到差別，說小動作有時比長演說更能表現規則。演員把修改後的時刻留在劇本裡。

Their cultural festival was only two weeks away. Yu-an had hoped to travel to the next town for a larger rehearsal, but that plan now seemed too risky. She bought guava from a street seller and sat with Wen under a tree. He described a novel he had read about a hero who believed he could solve every problem alone. "I used to enjoy that kind of hero," he said. "Now I think asking for help makes a person more interesting." Yu-an smiled. She had been thinking the same thing.

文化藝術節只剩兩週。余安原本想去鄰鎮做更大規模的排練，如今卻覺得風險太高。她向路邊攤買了芭樂，與文坐在樹下。文談起讀過的一本小說，主角相信所有問題都能獨自解決。「我以前喜歡這種英雄，」他說，「現在覺得會求助的人更有意思。」余安笑了，因為她剛好也在想同一件事。

A youth theater group offered to lend them soft floor mats. The group asked only that the students clean the mats and return them on time. Yu-an thanked them and wrote the promise into the equipment list. The new mats helped the injured actor watch rehearsal from a comfortable chair while another student learned his steps. He did not lose his place in the company. "A role can wait for a person," Yu-an said, "even when the festival cannot."

一個青年劇團願意借出柔軟地墊，只要求學生清潔並準時歸還。余安向他們道謝，把承諾寫進器材清單。新地墊讓受傷演員能坐在舒適椅子上看排練，另一名學生暫時學他的走位。他沒有失去團員身分。余安說：「即使藝術節不能等，角色也可以等一個人。」

Yu-an also called the festival office to explain that the roof problem had changed their transport needs. The first staff member could not answer and passed her to the building team. She waited without blaming the person on the phone. The building team asked for a written plan showing where the mats and carts would go. She sent a simple drawing that afternoon. When the approval came, it covered only the new location, not the closed theater. Yu-an read the limits aloud at rehearsal. She wanted nobody to hear the word "approved" and imagine that every door was open. A clear limit could make the next step safer for everyone.

余安也打電話給藝術節辦公室，說明屋頂問題改變了運輸需求。第一名工作人員無法作答，便轉給校舍團隊。她耐心等待，沒有責怪電話另一頭的人。校舍團隊要求書面計畫，說明地墊與推車會放哪裡。她當天下午寄出簡圖。核准下來時，只涵蓋新場地，不包含封閉的舊劇場。余安在排練時讀出限制，避免有人聽到「已核准」就以為每扇門都能開。清楚的限制，能讓下一步對大家更安全。

That evening, Wen stood with Yu-an in front of the closed theater. Rain had finally stopped. They could hear water falling from a pipe far above them. He did not tell her that everything would be fine; he said they had enough information to plan the next safe step. Yu-an took a picture of the notice, then put her phone away. For the first time she did not feel the need to touch the locked door. Tomorrow's work was waiting elsewhere.

當晚，文與余安站在封閉的劇場前。雨終於停了，能聽見高處水管滴水。他沒有說一切都會沒事，只說他們已掌握足夠資訊，可以安排下一個安全步驟。余安拍下公告，收起手機。這是她第一次不再想伸手碰那扇上鎖的門。明天真正的工作，在別的地方等著。
