每個人的座位

Chairs for Everyone

留給明天的燈光 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The theater department held a meeting about where people could sit. Yu-an had invited a student who used a wheelchair and another who disliked strong lights. The first wanted to attend the show with friends instead of sitting alone near a door. The second asked for a quiet place. In the eastern gymnasium, the group found a wide area with a firm floor. A worker from the sports department explained the standard fire exits and the places where they could not put a chair.

戲劇系開會討論觀眾座位。余安邀請一名使用輪椅的學生和另一名不喜歡強光的學生參與。前者希望和朋友坐在一起，而不是單獨坐在門邊；後者需要一處安靜角落。他們在東側體育館找到一大片地面堅實的空間。體育部門的工作人員說明標準消防出口，以及不能擺椅子的區域。

Wen brought a small cage used to carry cables, a comb for a costume, and a pan from the kitchen scene. In that scene, an actor would pretend to fry an egg without real heat. None of these belonged in an exit lane. He set them aside as an example of how minor objects could block an important path. "A safe room needs more than good purpose," he said. "It needs people to move through it." Yu-an asked the group to practice a full entrance and exit in slow motion. The test showed that two rows of chairs were too close.

文帶來裝線材的小籠架、一把戲服用梳子和廚房場景的平底鍋。那場戲裡，演員會假裝用平底鍋煎蛋，但不真的加熱。這些東西都不該放在逃生通道上。他把它們放在一旁，示範小物品也可能堵住重要路線。「安全的房間不只要有好目的，還要能讓人順利通行。」他說。余安請大家用慢動作練習完整進場與離場，結果發現兩排椅子太近。

The student who needed a quiet place made a short list of sounds that bothered her most: sudden whistles, metal chairs scraping the floor, and applause close to her ear. She did not ask anyone to stop enjoying the play. She wanted to know what would happen, and to have a place she could leave without drawing attention. Yu-an put a note on the public map saying that visitors could step out and return. Wen tested soft pads under the chairs. The simple pads reduced the noise enough that people could speak at ordinary volume. The student thanked them, then asked a hard question about whether the final show would still keep these choices open.

需要安靜空間的學生列出最困擾她的聲音：突然的哨音、金屬椅在地板拖動、耳邊太近的掌聲。她不是要大家停止享受演出，而是希望知道接下來會發生什麼，也能不引人注目地暫時離開。余安在公開地圖上註明來客可中途出去再回來。文測試椅腳下的軟墊，簡單的墊片就把噪音降到人們能用一般音量說話。學生向他們道謝，接著問了個難題：正式演出時，這些選擇還會保留嗎？

A volunteer suggested using an umbrella with a paper wolf on it for the forest scene. Another asked whether their costume could have a large bird wing. Yu-an wanted to encourage both ideas but required the objects to stay away from people during the test. One girl asked about a private medicine bag she carried. "You may keep your drug with you," Yu-an said, "and you do not need to explain your health to the audience." The girl's shoulders relaxed. Safety was also about what no one had to share.

一名志工建議森林場景用印著紙狼的雨傘，另一人則問戲服能否裝一隻大鳥翅膀。余安鼓勵兩個想法，但要求測試時道具不能碰到觀眾。一名女孩問自己攜帶的私人藥袋怎麼辦。余安說：「藥物可以留在你身邊，你不必向觀眾解釋健康狀況。」女孩放鬆了肩膀。安全也包括哪些事不必公開。

They made a simple map of the region around the gym. The nearest entrance had a gentle slope, but the side door had a high step. The team added clear signs and asked the building office to check both. Wen noticed how Yu-an listened to each person's need before changing her plan. When she worried that the changes made the story less exciting, he said, "The audience is not a problem to solve. They are the reason for the work." She put that sentence at the top of her page.

他們畫出體育館周圍區域的簡圖。最近的入口有平緩斜坡，側門卻有一階高臺。團隊加上清楚指示，並請校舍辦公室檢查兩處。文注意到余安總先聽完每人的需求，才修改自己的計畫。當她擔心這些變動讓戲不夠刺激時，文說：「觀眾不是等著我們解決的問題；他們是我們工作的理由。」她把這句話寫在頁首。

Yu-an answered with a promise she could check. They would print the same seating map on the festival program, and every volunteer at the door would receive a short training session. She asked another student to review the signs from the height of a wheelchair seat. One arrow had been placed too high to read easily. It was moved that afternoon. "The point is not to feel good about ourselves," she told the team. "The point is to let someone arrive, understand the room, enjoy the story, and leave safely." When they tested the path again, the new visitor reached the quiet seat without asking for help.

余安作出一個能被檢查的承諾：藝術節節目單會印上相同座位圖，每位門口志工都會接受短訓。她還請另一名學生從輪椅座位的高度查看指示牌，發現一個箭頭掛得太高，不容易讀，當天下午就移低。「目的不是讓我們自我感覺良好，」她告訴團隊，「而是讓人能抵達、理解空間、欣賞故事，再安全離開。」再次測試時，新來客不用求助就找到了安靜座位。

That evening, an actor tried the new route with his eyes closed, guided by a partner. He stopped before a small table that had been left near the wall. Nobody laughed at the mistake. They moved the table and tried again. Yu-an took notes while Wen counted the time. The new path was a little longer but much easier to understand. They had not built a stage, yet they had built something the old theater had never offered: a place planned with every guest in mind.

當晚，一名演員在夥伴引導下閉著眼走新路線。他在牆邊一張被遺忘的小桌前停住。沒有人嘲笑這個失誤；大家移開桌子，再試一次。余安做筆記，文計時。新路線稍長，卻更容易理解。他們還沒有搭起舞臺，卻做出了舊劇場從未提供的東西：一個從一開始就把每位來客放在心上的空間。

What did the wheelchair user ask for?

  2. To perform on the stage.
  3. To stop all the lights.
