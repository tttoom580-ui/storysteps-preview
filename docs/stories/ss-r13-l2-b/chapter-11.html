完整書目 / 留給明天的燈光

作者的名字

The Writer's Name

留給明天的燈光 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The script had been based on a short tale written by a former student. Its author was now a skilled teacher in another city, but no one had asked her for permission. Yu-an noticed the problem while measuring the stage with a tape marked in centimeters. "We cannot include a story just because somebody left a copy here," she said. An actor raised an eyebrow and asked whether permission would cost a fee. Yu-an said they should ask the author instead of making up an answer.

劇本改編自一名畢業生寫的短故事。作者如今在另一座城市教書，是位有經驗的老師，但竟沒有人徵求她的同意。余安用公分刻度的捲尺測量舞臺時發現這件事。「不能因為有人留下影本，就直接把故事拿來演。」她說。一名演員挑起眉毛，問授權是否要付費。余安說，應該去問作者，而不是猜答案。

The group did not agree about the best next step. Wen suggested sending a letter with the full script and a clear description of the show. Bo wanted to guard the plan as a secret until the festival. "The author is not our enemy," Wen replied. "Nor is a question a rude act." Yu-an wrote a short message explaining their aim. She also offered to remove the tale if the writer did not wish to share it. This was a harder choice than simply waiting for someone to notice.

團隊對下一步看法不同。文建議寄出完整劇本與清楚的演出說明。博卻想把計畫保密到藝術節。「作者不是敵人，提問也不是無禮。」文說。余安寫了一封簡短訊息，說明目的，也表示如果作者不願分享，他們會撤下故事。這比抱著僥倖心態等人發現更難，卻是她願意做的選擇。

While waiting for the author's answer, the team practiced a short scene without the borrowed words. It was only a test, not a plan to steal the story in another form. Yu-an measured the walking path again. Moving one chair by a centimeter seemed unimportant, but moving several chairs together made the entrance clearer. Wen offered a fresh sound cue for the scene, and the actor playing the guide found a new way to speak. They learned that a missing part did not mean everyone had to stop working. The team could use the time to strengthen the sections that were truly theirs. Yu-an wrote a reminder: if permission did not come, they would make an original ending.

等作者回覆時，團隊先練習不使用借來文字的短場景。那只是測試，不是想用另一種形式偷用故事。余安重新測量走道，單把椅子移一公分似乎沒差，幾張椅子一起移卻讓入口更清楚。文替場景提出新音效，飾演嚮導的演員也找到新的說話方式。他們明白，少了一部分不等於所有工作都得停下。團隊能趁機加強真正屬於自己的片段。余安記下提醒：如果授權沒來，就創作自己的結尾。

The author replied the next morning. She was pleased that students still remembered her forest puzzle, but asked them to change one scene. In her first version, a priest forced two people to hold a wedding before the village would help them. She no longer liked that idea. "It gives the characters no choice," she wrote. "Please let them make their own relationship." Yu-an read the letter aloud. The group agreed to replace the scene with a free promise of friendship.

隔天早上，作者回信。她很高興學生仍記得她的森林謎題，但要求改掉一幕。原版中，一名神父逼兩個人在村人願意相助前先辦婚禮。她如今不喜歡這個安排。「角色沒有選擇權，」她寫道，「請讓他們決定自己的關係。」余安把信讀給大家聽。團隊同意改成角色自願許下友誼承諾的場景。

They celebrated by meeting at a small restaurant. A tired waiter served soup, and outside a crow called from a narrow tree. Yu-an noticed that Wen looked pale. He had worked late on the sound files and skipped lunch again. She asked him to go home rather than stay for a second rehearsal. He wanted to argue, but saw that she meant it. "Then you must rest too," he said. "A good team does not need two tired leaders pretending they are fine."

團隊到小餐館慶祝。疲倦的服務生端來湯，門外狹窄樹枝上有烏鴉叫著。余安發現文臉色蒼白。他熬夜整理聲音檔，又沒吃午餐。她請他回家，不要留下來參加第二場排練。他原本想爭辯，卻看出她是認真的。「那你也要休息，」他說，「好團隊不需要兩個裝作沒事的疲倦領導者。」

Bo still wanted a faster answer and complained that the author should be grateful for the attention. Yu-an let him disagree without treating him as disloyal. She asked him to imagine finding his own film used by strangers who never mentioned his name. Bo went quiet. "I would want to be asked," he admitted. Yu-an nodded. She did not make him apologize in front of the group. Instead, she asked him to prepare a credit page that could include every writer, actor, and designer. It gave his energy a useful direction. By the end of the day, Bo had made a clear page with space for new names and permission dates.

博仍希望更快拿到答案，抱怨作者應感謝有人注意她。余安讓他表達不同意見，沒有把他當成不忠心的人。她請博想像自己拍的影片被陌生人使用，卻沒人提到他的名字。博安靜下來，承認：「我也希望有人先問。」余安點頭，沒有逼他在全團面前道歉，而是請他準備署名頁，能寫入作者、演員與設計師。這讓他的精力有了用處。一天結束時，博做出清楚的頁面，也留好新名字與授權日期的空間。

After the letter was signed, the team put the author's name on the program and saved the permission with the script. Yu-an felt relief, not victory. The play now belonged to everyone doing the work, but they were also responsible for the words they borrowed. Wen walked beside her toward the station and said he admired how she could change her mind in public. She answered that changing a plan was easier when someone stood next to her without making fun of the first mistake.

作者簽回同意書後，團隊把她的名字加進節目單，並將授權與劇本一起保存。余安感到安心，卻沒有勝利的感覺。這齣戲由參與者共同完成，他們也必須對借來的文字負責。文陪她走往車站，說自己很欣賞她能當眾改變想法。余安回答，當身旁有人不嘲笑原本的錯誤時，改變計畫就容易多了。
