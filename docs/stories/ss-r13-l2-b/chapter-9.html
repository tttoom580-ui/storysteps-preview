檔案裡的一頁

The Page in the Records

留給明天的燈光 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A daily article in the campus paper said the Lantern Room would be turned into a storage area. Yu-an felt fear, but she asked for the source before reacting. The article mentioned a historical plan from five years ago, not the latest decision. With Wen as her partner, she visited the records office. A worker showed them the legal section about public use of the building. There was no final answer yet; the college had to review several possibilities before choosing one.

校園報紙的一篇每日報導說，燈籠小劇場將被改成倉庫。余安很害怕，卻先問消息來自哪裡。文章提到的是五年前的歷史計畫，而非最新決定。她與夥伴文前往檔案室，工作人員指出建築公共使用的法律條款。目前還沒有最後答案；學院必須先審查幾種可能性才能決定。

In the records office, Yu-an opened a chapter of a book about campus art. A painting on the cover showed the theater when it was new. A paper star still seemed to shine above the old door. A student had once drawn the star with a crayon and given it to the first shopkeeper who sold tickets for student shows. Yu-an copied the story with permission. She did not want to paste it into her program without checking who had written it.

在檔案室，余安翻開一本校園藝術史，其中一章談到燈籠小劇場。封面畫著劇場新落成時的模樣，一顆紙星星似乎仍在舊門上方發光。當年有學生用蠟筆畫了那顆星，送給第一位替學生演出售票的店主。余安獲准抄下故事，卻不想在未確認作者之前就把它貼進節目單。

The editor asked Yu-an for a quote about the old room. "I want to keep it," she answered, "unless an expert shows us there is no safe way to repair it. Then we must find another way to remember it." The editor looked surprised. She had expected a simple fight between students and the college. Yu-an explained that people could disagree about a plan without pretending facts were different. She also said the college should share the costs and decisions openly. The editor recorded the answer and asked if she could return after the next rehearsal. Yu-an agreed because a full story needed time, not just a sharp headline.

編輯請余安說一句關於舊劇場的話。「我想保留它，」她回答，「除非專家證明已沒有安全的修復方法。那時我們就得用別的方式記住它。」編輯很意外，原本預期會是學生與校方的單純對抗。余安解釋，人們可以對方案有不同看法，卻不必假裝事實不同。她也主張校方應公開費用與決策。編輯錄下回答，問下次排練後能否再採訪。余安同意，因為完整故事需要時間，而不只是尖銳標題。

Outside, a small puppy followed them past the workshop. Wen crouched to tie his shoe and saw a loose nail beside the doorway. He moved people away and reported it to the office rather than pulling it out himself. "We cannot fix every system with our hands," he said. "Sometimes we succeed by telling the right person what is wrong." Yu-an thought of the old theater and wondered how many people had noticed small problems but failed to share them before they grew worse.

出門後，一隻小狗跟著他們經過工坊。文蹲下綁鞋帶，看見門邊一根鬆動的鐵釘。他請人繞開並通報辦公室，沒有自己硬拔。「不是每個系統都能用雙手直接修好，」他說，「有時候，告訴對的人哪裡出錯，就是成功。」余安想到舊劇場，不知道過去有多少人注意到小問題，卻沒有在它們惡化前說出來。

A student editor joined them in the afternoon. She offered to print an addition to the first article: the closure was temporary, and no storage plan had yet been approved. Yu-an thanked her for checking the facts. They agreed that the correction should be as easy to find as the first report. Then she asked whether the editor would visit rehearsal and write about the actors, not just the empty building. "A room without people is only half a story," she said. The editor promised to come.

下午，一名學生編輯加入討論。她提出補刊更正：目前只是暫時封閉，沒有任何倉庫計畫獲准。余安感謝她核對事實。兩人同意，更正必須和原報導同樣容易被找到。接著余安邀請編輯來排練現場，寫寫演員，不要只寫空蕩蕩的建築。「沒有人的房間，只是半個故事。」她說。編輯答應了。

Back in the studio, Wen helped the cast turn an old picture into a soft background for the traveling scene. They used a copy with permission and kept the original safe in the records office. A volunteer wanted to place the original onstage for one moment, arguing that it would make the scene more real. Yu-an said no. If the picture were damaged, a single beautiful moment could destroy a memory that belonged to many people. They found a way to suggest its colors with cloth instead. The actors tried the change and agreed that the audience still understood where the story took place, even without touching a fragile object.

回到工作室，文協助演員把舊照片做成旅途場景的柔和背景。他們取得允許後使用副本，原件仍安全放在檔案室。一名志工想讓原件短暫上臺，認為這會更真實。余安拒絕了。如果照片受損，一個美麗瞬間就可能毀掉屬於許多人的記憶。大家改用布料呈現照片色彩。演員試過後同意，觀眾即使沒看見易損的原物，也能理解故事發生在哪裡。

At home, Yu-an placed her notes next to the first page of the script. She wanted to defend the Lantern Room, but she also wanted the college to be able to tell the truth about the cost of repairs. A dramatic claim might bring attention today and damage trust tomorrow. Wen sent a short message: "You asked a hard question without making an enemy. I am proud of that." She read it twice before returning to the next scene, where a character had to choose whether to speak or stay silent.

回家後，余安把筆記放在劇本第一頁旁。她想守護燈籠小劇場，也希望學院能誠實說明修繕費用。戲劇性的指控也許今天引來注意，明天卻會破壞信任。文傳來一則簡訊：「你問了很難的問題，卻沒有急著把人當敵人。我很佩服。」她讀了兩次，才回到下一幕：劇中人物正必須決定要開口，還是保持沉默。
