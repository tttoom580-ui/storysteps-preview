裝著故事的推車

A Cart for the Story

留給明天的燈光 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next challenge was a cart. It had to carry an average load of costumes, signs, and small speakers from one building to another. Bo pushed it too fast, and a wooden stick fell from the shelf. Yu-an gave a gentle command to stop. She had learned during childhood that a cart could seem easy to control until the road turned. Wen checked the wheels and explained the means of keeping weight low. "We can move forward," he said, "but not by pretending that heavy things will disappear."

下一個難題是一臺推車。它必須在建築物之間運送平均重量的戲服、指示牌和小喇叭。博推得太快，一根木棍從架上落下。余安溫和但明確地叫他停下。她小時候就知道，推車看似容易控制，轉彎時卻可能出事。文檢查輪子，說明讓重物放低的方法。「我們可以向前走，」他說，「但不能假裝重物會憑空消失。」

The cart had been used for a barbecue event, and someone had left chopsticks and plates inside it. Yu-an returned them to the kitchen. An actor asked whether the play's servant could carry a stone to show how hard his life was. "A real stone is too much," she said. "Use foam and let your face tell the rest." The actor had believed that greater weight meant deeper meaning. He tested the light stone and found he could move more freely while the audience still understood the scene.

這臺推車之前用於烤肉活動，裡頭還留著筷子和盤子。余安把餐具送回廚房。一名演員問，劇中的僕人能否搬大石頭，象徵生活艱難。「真石頭太重，」她說，「用泡棉，再用表情補足。」演員原以為重量愈大，意義愈深；試了輕石頭後，發現自己能更自由地移動，觀眾依然看懂。

The new route showed them a hard fact about reality. The building had steps that no clever story could remove, and the cart could not pass through a door that was too narrow. Yu-an could not solve those problems by telling the actors to try harder. She called the campus access office and asked about another entrance. The worker suggested a different corridor and offered to check it with them. Bo pushed the cart slowly along the new route while Wen watched the wheels. They reached the far room without hitting a wall. It was less exciting than a quick rescue in a play, but it was a solution that ordinary people could use again.

新路線讓他們面對一個現實：建築物有階梯，聰明的故事也無法把它變不見；太窄的門，推車再努力也過不去。余安不能只叫演員更用力。她打電話給校園無障礙辦公室，詢問另一個入口。工作人員建議不同的走廊，並願意一起檢查。博緩慢地推車，文觀察輪子，最後順利抵達遠處房間，沒有撞上牆。這比戲裡的快速救援不刺激，卻是普通人下次仍能使用的解法。

Wen found an old note on the side of the cart. Its source was the student workshop, which had closed several years earlier. "Whatever we do, we should ask before changing the frame," he said. Yu-an called the workshop office, where an older employee explained who could approve repairs. The cart belonged to the college, not to a person whom they could thank with a gift. They filled out the short form and received permission to replace a damaged wheel.

文在推車側面發現一張舊紙條，顯示它原來自幾年前關閉的學生工坊。「無論我們做什麼，都該先問能不能改車架。」他說。余安打電話到工坊辦公室，一名資深員工說明誰能批准維修。推車是學院的，不是哪個人可以收禮答謝的私人物品。他們填好簡表，取得更換壞輪子的許可。

That night the actors tried moving the entire play through three rooms. No one was supposed to be carried by the cart. Yu-an explained that this was not a matter of fate but of choice and planning. Halfway through, the wheels caught on a door frame. Wen did not push harder; he measured the space and asked Bo to find a wider route. The longer trip took five extra minutes. In the real world, five minutes were cheaper than a broken instrument or an injured actor.

當晚，演員試著讓整齣戲穿過三間教室。沒有人可以搭上推車。余安說，這不是命運，而是選擇與計畫的問題。走到一半，輪子卡在門框。文沒有硬推，而是測量空間，請博找更寬的路。新路線多花五分鐘；在現實世界裡，五分鐘遠比弄壞器材或讓演員受傷便宜。

While the crew practiced, Yu-an watched a young volunteer carry a box of soft props with both arms. He walked carefully, checked the path ahead, and asked another person to open the door. When he reached the next room, he smiled as if he had completed an important mission. Yu-an smiled back. She had once thought good stage work meant doing something impressive where everyone could see it. Now she knew a safe delivery could be an achievement too. Wen made a note to put the cart instructions beside the storage door. No one should have to learn the same lesson by dropping something heavy on a foot.

工作人員練習時，余安看著一名年輕志工雙手抱著柔軟道具箱。他小心走路、先看前方，並請另一人開門。安全抵達下一間房時，他笑得像完成重大任務，余安也回以微笑。她以前以為好的舞臺工作是做出讓所有人看見的精彩效果，現在知道安全送達同樣是成就。文記下要把推車說明放在儲藏門旁，不該讓每個人都靠把重物砸到腳，才學會相同教訓。

By the time they finished, the cast could open a scene anywhere there was enough safe floor. Yu-an looked at their little cart and realized it had changed the story's shape. There was no single front row now. People could follow from room to room, or stay seated and watch the parts made for them. Wen asked which version she liked best. "I do not know yet," she answered. "I want to ask the people who will see it." He smiled at the answer and helped her lock the wheels.

試驗結束時，只要有足夠安全的地板，演員就能在任何地方展開一幕戲。余安望著小推車，發現它改變了作品的形狀。現在不再只有唯一的第一排；人們可以跟著走，也能坐在原地看專為他們安排的段落。文問她喜歡哪一版。「我還不知道，」她說，「我想問真正來看的觀眾。」文對這答案微笑，幫她鎖上輪子。
