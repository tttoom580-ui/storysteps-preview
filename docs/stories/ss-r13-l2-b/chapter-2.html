沒有布幕的房間

The Room with No Curtain

留給明天的燈光 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next day, the building officer confirmed that the Lantern Room had to stay closed until an electrical expert finished the inspection. Yu-an nearly asked him to cancel the notice, but she knew that would be selfish. She moved the first rehearsal into a bright classroom with no curtain. Its beauty was different from the theater's dark walls. Wen brought a portable speaker and made a trial display of sound levels, while the actors set chairs in a chain around the empty center.

隔天，校舍管理員確認燈籠小劇場必須維持封閉，直到電氣專家完成檢查。余安差點要求取消公告，但知道那樣太自私。她把第一次排練搬進一間沒有布幕的明亮教室。它的美與劇場的深色牆面很不同。文帶來攜帶式喇叭，試著展示音量數據；演員則把椅子連成一圈，留出中央空間。

Their current plan was a play about a lost map. A boy playing the guide wanted to hunt for a huge treasure under the stage, but there was no stage. Wen suggested that the audience could become part of the map. Two students argued over club membership: should visitors be allowed to help if they had not paid a fee? "A guest can respond to a question without buying a place in our club," Yu-an said. She refused to let a small rule cause a larger loss of trust.

他們目前排練的是一齣尋找失落地圖的戲。飾演嚮導的男生想在舞臺底下尋找巨大寶藏，但新教室根本沒有舞臺。文提議讓觀眾也成為地圖的一部分。兩位社員為會員資格爭論：沒有繳費的訪客能否參與？余安說：「客人不必買下社團身分，也能回答問題。」她不願讓小規矩造成更大的信任損失。

In the first test, Wen asked the actors to walk through the story without any music. Yu-an stood behind the chairs with a notebook. She noticed a small girl who watched the actors instead of the empty center. When the guide raised his hand, the girl leaned forward; when the action stopped, she sat back. Yu-an realized that the audience did not need an expensive curtain to know where to look. The actors were learning to make a clear invitation with their bodies. She asked them to keep that movement and remove two unnecessary lines. The change was small but made the next scene easier to follow from the last row.

第一次測試中，文請演員不放音樂，把故事走過一遍。余安站在椅子後方做筆記，注意到一名小女孩看的是演員，而不是中間的空地。嚮導舉手時，女孩向前傾；動作停下時，她坐回原位。余安明白，觀眾不必靠昂貴布幕，也知道該看哪裡。演員正學會用身體清楚地邀請觀眾。她請他們保留動作、刪掉兩句不必要的臺詞。改動雖小，卻讓最後一排的人更容易跟上下一幕。

The worst problem came from a message on the student forum. It claimed the show was over and the cast had broken apart. Yu-an wanted to answer at once, but Wen advised a short, clear update instead. She wrote the time and place of the next rehearsal. The speaker played a soft bell, and a new volunteer arrived carrying a pear in his lunch bag. He said he had joined because the message sounded honest rather than super exciting. Yu-an thought that honesty might help their growth more than loud promises.

最糟糕的問題來自學生論壇的一則訊息：有人聲稱演出已經取消、演員也各自散去。余安想立刻反駁，但文建議只發布簡短清楚的更新。她寫明下一次排練的時間與地點。喇叭響起柔和鐘聲，一名新志工帶著午餐袋裡的梨子走進來。他說自己加入，是因為公告聽起來誠實，而不是誇大刺激。余安想，也許誠實比響亮承諾更能帶來成長。

During a break, Wen showed Yu-an how he played chess with his grandfather. "The best move is sometimes to leave a space open," he said. She looked at the chairs and understood. She had tried to guide every person's step, leaving none for the actors to choose. Now she asked them to invent their own paths. A quiet student crossed the floor while the others stepped aside. The scene suddenly worked. Wen did not claim credit, and Yu-an did not hide her surprise.

休息時，文向余安說起他與祖父下西洋棋的方式。「最好的那一步，有時是先留下一個空位。」他說。余安看著椅子，明白自己一直想指揮每個人的腳步，沒有給演員自行選擇的空間。於是她請大家設計各自的路線。一名安靜的學生穿過地板，其他人自然讓開，場景突然流暢起來。文沒有搶功，余安也沒有掩飾驚訝。

Before leaving, Yu-an found an unsigned paper under a chair. It said, "I am tired of people choosing for me." She did not know whether an actor or a guest had written it, and she would not turn it into public news. Instead, she asked the group a question about self: what part of the show did each person want to choose for themselves? One actor wanted to design a costume, another wanted to speak only in a small scene, and a third wanted to leave early on Tuesdays. Yu-an wrote down their answers. A team could grow without asking everyone to become the same person or to give up a private life.

離開前，余安在椅子下找到一張沒有署名的紙，上頭寫著：「我已經厭倦別人替我選擇。」她不知道是演員還是客人寫的，也不會把它變成公開新聞。她改問全團一個關於自我的問題：在這齣戲裡，每個人想親自決定哪件事？一名演員想設計戲服，另一名只想演小場景，第三名星期二需要早走。余安逐一記下。團隊可以成長，卻不必要求所有人變成一樣，也不必放棄自己的生活。

The rehearsal ended with a small public test. Six students watched and each named one thing they understood. No one called it perfect, yet everyone could see a story beginning. Yu-an kept the feedback instead of only praising herself. She told Wen that the change had saved a whole week. He offered half his pear, and she accepted it as they put the chairs away. Outside the closed theater, the notice remained. Inside the borrowed classroom, the company had found its first new way of moving.

排練最後，他們邀請六名學生觀看小型測試，請每人說出自己理解的一件事。沒有人說它完美，但每個人都看見故事的開端。余安把意見記下，而非只顧著稱讚自己。她告訴文，這項改變省下一整週。收椅子時，文把梨子分她一半，她也欣然接受。關閉的劇場外，公告仍然貼著；借來的教室裡，劇團卻找到了移動的第一種新方法。
