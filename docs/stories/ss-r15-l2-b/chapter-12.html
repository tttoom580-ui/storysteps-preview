書架上方的水

Water Above the Shelves

借一本到明天 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A drop of water fell from the student center roof onto a donated novel. Hsin moved the books away and closed the affected corner immediately. It was a small leak, but the team had promised not to hide risks, however inconvenient the truth might be.

學生中心屋頂落下一滴水，打在捐贈小說上。欣立刻移開書籍，封閉受影響的角落。漏水不大，但團隊答應過不隱瞞風險，即使真相帶來不便。

Hsin almost turned her ankle while moving away from the wet corner. A dog began to bark outside as the workers checked the damaged roof. Kai heard a click from the light switch and asked everyone not to touch it. The cultural club had reserved the room for a reading on local history. The repair delay meant the next exchange would have fewer shelves.

欣離開濕角落時，差點扭傷腳踝。 工人檢查受損屋頂時，外面一隻狗開始吠叫。 凱聽見電燈開關發出喀噠聲，請大家不要碰。 文化社原本預訂那間房間舉辦地方史讀書會。 修理延誤表示下一場換書可用的書架會變少。

Kai tested the dry route to another room but would not touch the damaged ceiling. The building worker said the repair could not begin until the following week. Hsin checked the event plan and cut the next gathering down to a smaller size.

凱測試通往另一間教室的乾燥路線，卻沒有碰受損天花板。校舍工人說修理得等下週才能開始。欣檢查活動方案，把下一場規模縮小。

Bo wrote a clear description of the wet area for the building office. A visitor called the leak an evil plan, but Hsin asked for evidence. Kai recorded the exact location of the drip on the approved floor map. A guava tree outside the hall kept its fruit through the rain. The damaged novel was dried by trained staff instead of eager students.

博向校舍辦公室寫出濕區的清楚描述。 訪客稱漏水是邪惡陰謀，欣卻要求證據。 凱在核准平面圖上記錄水滴的精確位置。 大廳外一棵芭樂樹在雨中仍掛著果實。 受損小說由受訓人員處理乾燥，不讓熱心學生自行動手。

Some students became angry about another delay. Hsin showed them the inspection request and explained what was known and unknown. She avoided blaming the worker, who had not caused the leak. Kai stood beside her and answered technical questions without promising a date.

有些學生對再度延期生氣。欣出示檢查申請，解釋已知與未知的情況。她沒有責怪並未造成漏水的工人。凱站在旁邊回答技術問題，但不保證完工日期。

When the water was first noticed, one volunteer tried to save the wet novel by placing it near a strong heater. Hsin asked him to stop until trained staff could advise them. Quick heat might damage old paper further, and nobody had checked the room for electrical risk. The volunteer apologized and helped carry dry boxes instead. Kai wrote a short notice explaining why the closed corner was not simply an inconvenience. It was an area where people needed to wait for a proper inspection.

剛發現漏水時，一名志工想把濕小說放在強力暖氣旁搶救。欣請他先停手，等受訓人員提供建議。高溫可能進一步損壞舊紙，電氣風險也尚未檢查。志工道歉後改搬乾書箱。凱寫了短公告，說明封閉角落不只是帶來不便，而是必須等待正式檢查的區域。

A protective sheet covered the books while they were moved to the safe room. The building officer promised to reply after the inspection was complete. Nobody was allowed under the wet roof until the area had been checked. Hsin had to select fewer titles for the smaller temporary reading area. A clean spoon left on a table was returned to the student kitchen.

書籍移往安全教室時，表面蓋著保護布。 校舍管理員答應檢查完成後回覆。 濕屋頂區域檢查前，任何人都不得站在下方。 欣必須為較小的臨時閱讀區挑選較少書名。 桌上遺留的一支乾淨湯匙被送回學生廚房。

They selected books that could be safely carried by hand and made a small reading corner in a nearby hall. The change cost them visitors, but no one had to walk under the wet roof. The group wrote down that loss rather than pretending nothing had happened.

他們挑選能安全手搬的書，在附近大廳設小型閱讀角。改變讓訪客變少，但沒有人需要走過濕屋頂下方。團隊如實記下這次損失，不假裝一切無事。

A broken swing outside the hall was fenced off for safety. Kai did not want people to travel across campus for a canceled event. Upon seeing the water mark, Hsin stopped the setup and called for help. Some readers were upset, but most understood why safety came first. The youth club offered its dry meeting room for the smaller event.

大廳外壞掉的鞦韆為了安全而圍起來。 凱不希望人們穿越校園後才發現活動取消。 欣一看見水痕，就停止布置並求助。 有些讀者感到不快，但多數人理解安全優先的理由。 青年社團借出乾燥會議室，讓縮小後的活動使用。

That evening Hsin rewrote the event notice three times. The first version apologized too much and made it sound as though she had personally caused the leak. The second was so technical that a new visitor could not understand it. Kai read the third version and pointed to the sentence that mattered: 'The room is closed; here is the safe place to go instead.' Hsin kept it. She was learning that a clear answer could be both honest and gentle, without making promises she could not keep.

那晚欣把活動公告改寫三次。第一版道歉過多，彷彿漏水是她親手造成；第二版太過技術化，新訪客根本看不懂。凱讀完第三版，指出最重要的一句：「這間房暫停使用；請改到此處的安全場地。」欣留下這句。她學會了清楚答覆可以誠實又溫柔，不必做無法兌現的承諾。

Kai asked Hsin if she was tired of finding new rooms. She admitted she was. "But I am not tired of this work," she added. He offered to carry the last dry box, and she let him; accepting help was becoming easier.

凱問欣是否厭倦不斷找新場地。她承認有點累。「但我沒有厭倦這件事。」她補充。凱提議搬最後一箱乾書，她答應了；接受幫助開始變得容易。
