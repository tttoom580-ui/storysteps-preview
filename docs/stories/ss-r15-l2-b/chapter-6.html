開門前的雨

Rain Before Opening

借一本到明天 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Heavy rain arrived before the new books did. The delivery truck stopped at the far gate because a road had flooded. Hsin refused to send volunteers into deep water. She moved the day's exchange into the student center, which had a covered entrance and an approved route.

新書還沒到，大雨先來了。送貨卡車因道路積水停在遠方校門。欣不准志工涉水，她把當天的換書活動移到學生中心，因為那裡有遮雨入口和核可的路線。

Hsin said the team could accept a delay if it kept everyone out of danger. The student-center building had a dry entrance and rooms approved for public use. The crowd was smaller because the rain had stopped several buses. On the eve of the first big delivery, they checked the weather report twice. Kai asked the driver to examine the route before moving any heavy boxes.

欣說，只要能讓大家遠離危險，團隊就可以接受延期。 學生中心大樓有乾燥入口，也有核准供大眾使用的教室。 因為大雨讓幾班公車停駛，人群變少了。 第一次大規模配送前夕，他們查了兩次天氣報告。 凱請司機先檢查路線，再搬動重箱。

Kai and Bo checked the list of boxes. Two were missing, but the driver had recorded their numbers, so the team could find them after the storm. Hsin reminded everyone that a late book was less serious than an injured person.

凱和博對照書箱清單。兩箱尚未送達，但司機記錄了編號，風雨過後仍能找到。欣提醒大家，一本遲到的書，遠不及一個人受傷嚴重。

A book about gardening became one child's favorite thing in the temporary room. A picture of a giraffe helped the volunteers find the animal-book box. A musician from the next room offered to play quietly while people waited. They followed the official storm notice rather than a rumor in a group chat. The student read poetry aloud when the power briefly went out.

一本園藝書成了某個孩子在臨時教室裡最喜愛的東西。 長頸鹿圖案幫志工認出動物書箱。 隔壁教室的音樂家提議在大家等待時輕聲演奏。 他們遵照官方暴雨公告，而不是群組中的傳聞。 短暫停電時，學生朗讀詩歌。

A music student offered to read poems while people waited. Her quiet voice made the temporary room feel welcoming. Hsin arranged dry mats by the door and asked visitors to keep the walkway clear. A small plan worked better than a brave but unsafe rush.

音樂系學生提議在等待時朗讀詩。她柔和的聲音讓臨時場地變得溫暖。欣在門口鋪上乾墊，請訪客保持走道暢通。小小而安全的計畫，勝過勇敢卻危險的冒進。

While the rain continued, a mother explained that she had come because the free shelf was the only place nearby with books for her son's reading level. Hsin offered to help her find a suitable title among the boxes already indoors. The boy found a story about two friends building a bridge and sat down at once. Kai watched him read the first page aloud. It was a small reason to keep the temporary room open, but not a reason to ignore the storm outside.

雨勢持續時，一位母親說自己來這裡，是因為免費書架是附近唯一有適合兒子閱讀程度書籍的地方。欣幫她從已搬入室內的書箱挑選。男孩找到兩個朋友蓋橋的故事，立刻坐下朗讀第一頁。凱看著他讀，覺得這是維持臨時場地的小小理由，但仍不能因此忽視外面的暴風雨。

Bo lent a raincoat to a visitor whose umbrella had broken. Kai promised to repair the cart wheel only when the floor was dry. Hsin was sensitive to visitors who felt embarrassed about asking for help. The sidewalk near the gate was under water, so they chose another path. A story about a brave soul helped the waiting children stay calm.

博把雨衣借給雨傘壞掉的訪客。 凱答應等地面乾燥後才修理推車輪子。 欣能體會那些不好意思求助的訪客的感受。 校門附近的人行道淹水，於是大家另選路線。 一個勇敢靈魂的故事讓等待的孩子保持平靜。

When the driver called with an update, Kai wrote the expected time beside the missing boxes. He had learned to share uncertain information clearly instead of promising what he could not control. Hsin noticed that he now asked before moving her signs.

司機來電更新進度時，凱在失蹤書箱旁寫下預計時間。他已學會清楚說明不確定的資訊，而不是承諾自己無法控制的事。欣注意到他如今移動標示前會先詢問。

The storm moved across the coast faster than the first forecast had suggested. Bo wrote the new delivery term clearly on the shared notice. The driver would move toward the north gate when the road reopened. A history book described how families survived war by sharing reading rooms. The western campus bus stop remained closed until the water went down.

暴風雨沿海岸移動得比第一份預報還快。 博在共同公告上清楚寫下新的配送期限。 道路重開時，司機將朝北門前進。 一本歷史書描述家庭如何在戰爭中靠共享閱讀室度日。 校園西側公車站直到水退後才重新開放。

At the end of the day, the delivery driver sent a photograph of the two boxes left in his truck. Their numbers matched Bo's list. Hsin was relieved that they were not lost, but she did not ask the driver to return during dangerous weather. Kai added a new line to the delivery form: the safest place to wait if a road closed. Bo agreed to ask future drivers for that information before they began a journey. They had learned something useful without anyone needing to take a risk.

當天結束時，司機傳來卡車內兩箱書的照片，編號與博的清單吻合。欣很安心書沒丟，卻沒有要求司機在危險天候折返。凱在配送表新增一欄：道路封閉時可安全等待的地點。博同意未來在旅程開始前先向司機詢問。這次大家學到有用的事，不需要任何人冒險。

The rain eased after dark. The books could wait until morning, and the readers went home safely. Kai offered to walk with Hsin to the bus stop. She accepted, not because she needed protection, but because she wanted to keep talking about the strange day.

入夜後雨勢轉小。書可以等到早上，讀者則平安回家。凱提議陪欣走到公車站。她接受了，不是因為需要保護，而是想繼續聊這奇特的一天。
