第一座開放書架

The First Open Shelf

借一本到明天 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Saturday brought the first courtyard exchange. Hsin placed a clear sign at the entrance: nobody had to donate a book to take one. Some students came with full bags; others came with empty hands. Both groups were welcome. The rule was tested sooner than anyone expected.

星期六，第一次中庭換書活動開始。欣在入口放上清楚告示：不必先捐書也能領一本。有人帶著滿袋書來，有人兩手空空，兩種人都受歡迎。這條規則比想像中更快受到考驗。

Hsin had little appetite before the first open event because she was nervous. Kai asked everyone to pay attention to the safe weight of each shelf. A brilliant idea from a first-year student made the signs much clearer. A conflict began when visitors argued about whether taking books without giving was fair. Hsin explained the connection between free access and the annex's history.

首次開放活動前，欣緊張得沒什麼胃口。 凱請大家注意每座書架的安全承重。 一名大一學生的絕妙點子讓標示清楚多了。 訪客爭論不捐書卻拿書是否公平，衝突於是開始。 欣解釋免費使用與舊館歷史之間的連結。

A student complained that someone had taken three books without giving any back. Bo checked the agreed limit and explained that everyone could take up to three. Hsin did not shame the visitor or change the rule in secret. She asked the team to count what remained at closing time.

一名學生抱怨有人沒有捐書卻拿走三本。博查核原先同意的上限，說明每人都可帶走最多三本。欣沒有羞辱訪客，也沒有暗中改規則，而是請團隊打烊時清點剩餘數量。

Bo was glad to discover that donated books still remained after the busy morning. A domestic cooking book found a new home with a young parent. Kai checked every electrical outlet before asking permission to use one. The mass of one heavy box was too great for the small cart. Their method was to count books again at closing time.

博高興地發現，忙碌一上午後仍有捐贈書留下。 一本家常料理書被一名年輕家長帶回家。 凱在申請使用插座前，先檢查每個電源插座。 某個重箱的質量太大，小推車無法承受。 他們的方法是打烊時再清點一次書量。

The books did not vanish. In fact, enough remained for the next meeting, and several visitors offered to help sort them. Kai's movable shelves held their weight, although one wheel needed repair. The first public test was imperfect and useful.

書並沒有被拿光。事實上，還有足夠的書留給下一次活動，幾位訪客也主動幫忙整理。凱的活動書架承受住重量，雖然有個輪子需要修理。第一次公開測試不完美，卻有用。

By noon the line had reached the courtyard gate, and one volunteer asked whether they should stop allowing new visitors inside. Hsin counted the people in the room and checked the maximum number approved by the university. They were still within it, so she arranged a clear waiting area instead. Kai brought two more chairs from the safe store room. The visitors waited longer than expected but could see where the line began and ended. Small details changed a confusing crowd into something everyone could understand.

中午時排隊已延伸到中庭門口，一名志工問是否該停止放人進場。欣數了室內人數，核對校方核准的上限，發現尚未超過，便改設清楚的等候區。凱從安全倉庫搬來兩張椅子。訪客比預期等得久，卻看得出隊伍的起點與終點。小細節讓混亂的人群變得容易理解。

A recipe book about noodle dishes became the first item borrowed that morning. The officer gave praise for honest records but not permission to reopen the old hall. A picture of a rooster made two children burst into laughter. Hsin protected the skin of old book covers with clean, dry hands. A snowy mountain story remained popular even in the warm courtyard.

一本麵食食譜成了當天早上第一本被借走的書。 管理員稱讚紀錄誠實，卻沒有准許重開舊館。 一張公雞圖片逗得兩個孩子大笑。 欣用乾淨乾燥的手保護舊書封面的表面。 即使在溫暖中庭，雪山故事仍很受歡迎。

The university officer visited and praised the clear records, but refused to promise a permanent room. The building still needed an independent review. Hsin thanked the officer for an honest answer. She would rather earn a limited chance than build hope on a false promise.

校方管理員來訪，稱讚紀錄清楚，卻拒絕承諾永久空間，因為館舍仍需獨立檢查。欣感謝對方坦白回答。她寧願爭取有限的機會，也不想把希望建立在假承諾上。

The visitors helped spread the news without sharing anyone's private contact list. Small donations could support new labels but did not buy special borrowing rights. A distant sound of thunder made the volunteers check the weather plan. Whenever a reader looked lost, a helper offered the printed guide. A soft wool scarf left on a chair was taken to the lost-property desk.

訪客幫忙傳播消息，沒有分享任何人的私人聯絡名單。 小額捐款能支持新標籤，卻不能換得特殊借書權。 遠方雷聲響起，志工立刻查閱天候應變計畫。 每當讀者看起來迷惘，幫手就遞上印刷指南。 一條遺忘在椅上的柔軟羊毛圍巾被送到失物招領處。

Near closing, a girl stood beside the free shelf for a long time without choosing anything. Hsin asked if she wanted help, and the girl said she had never taken a book without paying before. Hsin showed her the written rules and said she could take one without explaining her life. The girl chose a story about a family on the sea. Kai saw her leave with the book held carefully against her chest. He understood why Hsin had refused to charge even a small entrance fee.

快打烊時，一名女孩在免費書架前站了很久，什麼都沒選。欣問是否要幫忙，女孩說自己從未不付錢就拿走一本書。欣給她看書面規則，說她能取一本，不必解釋自己的生活。女孩選了海上家庭的故事，小心把書抱在胸前離開。凱看見後，明白欣為何連少量入場費都不願收。

At sunset, Kai showed Hsin a photograph of the crowded tables. He asked whether she was happy. "Yes," she said, "but I am already thinking about next week." He laughed. For once, he did not tell her to rest; he simply offered to help make a better plan.

日落時，凱拿出熱鬧桌面的照片，問欣開不開心。「開心，」她說，「可是我已經在想下週了。」他笑了。這次他沒有叫她休息，只說願意幫忙把計畫做得更好。

  2. Everyone had to pay a small fee.
  3. Visitors could take up to three without donating.

  2. Nobody had visited the event.
  3. The books were too heavy to count.

答案：A。舊館仍需獨立安全評估，因此管理員沒有承諾永久開放。
