A Count That Failed

借一本到明天 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Their first test of the book-counting system failed in front of the volunteers. A box held forty books, yet the screen showed fifty-two. Bo looked worried because the university wanted a reliable number before lending them the courtyard. Hsin asked everyone to stop guessing and count the books by hand.

第一次測試書籍統計系統，就在志工面前出錯。一箱明明只有四十本書，螢幕卻顯示五十二本。博很擔心，因為校方要求可靠數字才肯借出中庭。欣請大家先停止猜測，親手重新點書。

Their first attempt to count the books took longer than anyone expected. A damaged label was the cause of the extra number on the screen. Bo brought cereal to the workshop because nobody had stopped for breakfast. Hsin said they would not cheat by changing the paper count to match the computer. An old classmate of Kai's offered to inspect the scanner after class.

第一次嘗試點書，比所有人預期都花時間。 破損的標籤是螢幕數字多出來的原因。 博帶麥片到工作室，因為大家都還沒吃早餐。 欣說，他們不會為了配合電腦而作弊修改紙本數字。 凱的一位老同學提議下課後檢查掃描器。

Kai discovered that one torn label was being scanned twice. He admitted that he had been too eager to show a working tool. Hsin did not laugh at him. She asked him to make the error easy for another volunteer to find, even without understanding the code.

凱發現一張破損標籤被重複掃描。他承認自己太急著展示好用的工具。欣沒有嘲笑，而是請他讓不懂程式的志工也能發現同樣的錯誤。

The morning was cloudy, and the dim light made small labels hard to read. They agreed to continue testing even after the first corrected count appeared. A silver earring found beneath a box went straight to the public lost-property desk. The side entrance was wide enough for carts but had no clear direction sign. Before the final test, Kai asked a volunteer who had never used the scanner to try.

早上多雲，昏暗光線讓小標籤很難辨認。 即使第一份更正數字出現，他們仍同意繼續測試。 箱子底下發現的銀耳環立刻送到公開失物招領處。 側門入口足夠推車通過，卻沒有清楚的方向標示。 最後測試前，凱請一名從未使用掃描器的志工操作。

The group replaced color-only signals with words and clear marks. They tested the system again with books from three different boxes, and the new count matched the paper list. The result was small, but everyone could check it.

團隊把只靠顏色的提示改成文字與清楚標記。他們用三箱不同的書重測，新的數字終於和紙本清單相符。這是小小的成果，但人人都能查核。

Kai tried to explain the scanner in a way a new reader might understand. He placed three books on a table and scanned the first one twice. The number on the screen rose twice, which made Bo laugh in surprise. Then they asked the newest volunteer to repeat the test. She caught the problem before anybody gave her a hint. Kai added a warning on the screen and a second check at the end. The system was slower now, but people could see what it was doing.

凱試著用新讀者能理解的方式解釋掃描器。他把三本書放在桌上，把第一本掃了兩次。螢幕數字也增加兩次，讓博驚訝地笑了。接著他們請最新加入的志工重做測試。沒有人提醒她，她就找出了問題。凱在畫面加上警告，並在結尾多一道查核。系統如今慢了些，但大家看得懂它在做什麼。

Nobody tried to force Bo to approve a count he had not checked. A general rule required two people to sign each large delivery list. They used the public government accessibility guide when making labels easier to read. The club welcomed liberal discussion, but decisions still needed evidence. Kai found the broken link between the scanner and the book-counting page.

沒有人強迫博核准尚未查驗的數字。 一般規則要求每張大型交貨清單有兩人簽名。 他們參考政府公開的無障礙指引，讓標籤更易閱讀。 社團歡迎開放自由的討論，但決策仍需要證據。 凱找出掃描器與書籍統計頁面之間失效的連結。

A classmate accused Kai of hiding the mistake. Hsin showed the notebook, where his first failed test and his correction stood side by side. "A mistake written down is something we can learn from," she said. The classmate apologized.

一名同學指控凱隱瞞錯誤。欣打開筆記本，讓大家看見他第一次失敗的紀錄與修正方式並列在一起。「把錯誤記下來，我們才能從中學習。」她說。那位同學道了歉。

Hsin gave a nod when the new numbers finally matched the paper notes. Only trained helpers could operate the scanner during public opening hours. A runner from the sports club delivered another box of donated books. A scooter blocked the ramp, so Bo asked its owner to move it. The office promised a limited supply of blank labels for the next test.

當新數字終於和紙本紀錄一致，欣點了點頭。 只有受訓幫手能在公開時段操作掃描器。 田徑社的一名跑者送來另一箱捐贈書。 一輛機車擋住坡道，博請車主移走。 辦公室承諾提供數量有限的空白標籤供下次測試。

Later, Hsin found Kai alone beside the paper list. He said he was afraid people would remember only his failed demonstration. She told him she still remembered the first time her poster had confused visitors, and how embarrassed she had felt. She had fixed it after listening to their questions. "You don't owe anyone a perfect first try," she said. "You owe them an honest second try." Kai wrote those words on the back of his tool box and asked her not to tease him about it.

後來欣在紙本清單旁找到獨自一人的凱。他說怕大家只記得自己失敗的展示。欣說她仍記得第一張海報讓訪客迷路時的羞愧，後來她聽取問題，才修好了設計。「你不欠任何人完美的第一次，」她說，「你欠的是誠實的第二次。」凱把這句話寫在工具箱背面，請她不要取笑。

Outside the workshop, Kai offered Hsin a hot drink. She told him she did not need thanks for defending a fair record. He answered that he was grateful she cared more about truth than about winning their old argument. They walked back without hurrying.

工作室外，凱遞給欣一杯熱飲。她說，替公平紀錄說話不需要道謝。他答道，自己很感謝她重視真相，勝過贏得兩人過去的爭論。他們不急不慢地走回去。
