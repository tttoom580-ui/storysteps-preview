不屬於我們的書

The Book That Was Not Ours

借一本到明天 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A rare book arrived without a donation form. Its cover carried the seal of a nearby school. Bo thought it might be an exciting gift, but Hsin asked him to set it aside. The exchange could not give away something that might belong to another library.

一本珍貴的書沒有附捐贈表就送來了，封面還有附近學校的印章。博以為是令人興奮的禮物，欣卻請他先放一旁。換書活動不能把可能屬於其他圖書館的物品送出去。

The actual owner of the marked book was the nearby school library. Kai wrote the school's public address on a return slip, not a student's name. Hsin asked Bo for advice about storing the book until its owner replied. Her heart began to beat faster when she saw the school's seal. An old china plate with a library picture was part of another donated collection.

印章書的真正所有者是附近的學校圖書館。 凱在歸還單上寫學校公開地址，而不是某位學生姓名。 欣請博建議如何暫存這本書，直到主人回覆。 看見學校印章時，她的心跳開始加速。 另一批捐贈品裡有一個畫著圖書館的舊瓷盤。

Kai searched the public library catalog and found the same title. He did not use the old borrowing card inside the cover to contact a private reader. Instead, he wrote to the school office through its public address. The difference mattered to Hsin.

凱查詢公開圖書館目錄，找到相同書名。他沒有依封面內的舊借閱卡私自聯繫讀者，而是寄信到學校公開的辦公地址。這個差別對欣很重要。

The archive expert confirmed that a public catalog number could identify the book. Hsin filled out the return form before anyone carried the book away. Kai checked the library seal through a magnifying lens under good light. The listener at the school office took their message and promised a reply. They did not call anyone at midnight about a problem that could wait.

檔案專家確認，公開館藏編號可以辨識這本書。 任何人帶走書之前，欣先填好歸還表單。 凱在明亮處用放大鏡檢查圖書館印章。 學校辦公室接電話的人記下訊息，答應回覆。 他們沒有為了可以等待的問題，在午夜打電話給任何人。

While they waited, a younger volunteer asked why one book caused so much work. Hsin explained that a free exchange depended on trust. If the team ignored ownership once, even careful donors might stop bringing books.

等待期間，年輕志工問為何一本書要花這麼多工夫。欣解釋免費交換靠的是信任；若團隊有一次忽視所有權，再謹慎的捐贈者也可能不願送書了。

The teacher who had packed the wrong box arrived looking worried. She apologized to the students and explained that her school had moved several rooms at once. Books with old labels had been placed beside items meant for donation, and she had misunderstood the signs. Hsin told her that the team had also made labeling mistakes. What mattered now was returning the book and changing the process so the same error was less likely to happen again. The teacher promised to add a second check at her school.

把書裝錯箱的老師憂心地來了。她向學生道歉，解釋學校同時搬遷好幾間教室，貼舊標籤的書放在待捐物品旁，她看錯了標示。欣說團隊也犯過標籤錯誤。現在重要的是歸還書，並改進流程，減少再次出錯的機率。老師答應學校會增加第二次檢查。

A naughty joke about a secret thief made Bo laugh, but Hsin corrected the rumor. A necklace found in the same box went to the proper lost-property desk. None of the volunteers was allowed to keep the unclaimed book. The stamp on the cover helped them learn the book's origin. The school librarian sounded pleasant and relieved when Kai called.

關於神祕小偷的調皮玩笑逗笑博，欣卻澄清了傳聞。 同箱發現的一條項鍊送到正式失物招領處。 沒有任何志工可以私自留下這本尚未領回的書。 封面印章幫助他們查明書籍來源。 凱打電話時，學校圖書館員的聲音友善又安心。

The school replied that the book was still in its collection. A teacher had left it in the wrong box during a move. Kai returned it with a receipt, and the teacher thanked the team. There was no dramatic thief, only a mistake corrected in time.

學校回覆那本書仍屬館藏。搬遷時一名老師把它放錯箱。凱帶著收據歸還，老師向團隊致謝。沒有戲劇性的竊賊，只有及時改正的錯誤。

A novel about a prisoner escaping a tower sat beside the mystery books. Bo's role was to record the date and witness the return. The volunteers began to sort books with missing ownership records into a separate box. Hsin offered the teacher warm toast after the careful return was complete. The whole team learned that a mistake did not always mean bad intentions.

一本寫囚犯逃出高塔的小說放在推理書旁。 博的職責是記下日期並見證歸還。 志工開始把缺少所有權紀錄的書分到另一箱。 歸還程序完成後，欣請那位老師吃熱吐司。 整個團隊明白，一個錯誤不一定代表惡意。

On the way out, Kai noticed that Bo had written 'stolen' on the first draft of the incident log. He asked Bo to change it to 'ownership unclear' because the team had never seen evidence of theft. Bo nodded and corrected the form. Hsin was quietly pleased. Their project was beginning to protect people from careless words as well as from lost books. She asked Kai to keep the first and corrected versions together, so the team could explain why the wording had changed.

離開前，凱發現博在事故紀錄初稿寫了「被偷」，便請他改成「所有權不明」，因為團隊從未看見偷竊證據。博點頭改正表單，欣暗自高興。計畫開始不只保護書，也保護人不受草率用語傷害。她請凱保留初稿與修正版，讓團隊能說明改字原因。

On the walk back, Kai asked Hsin whether she had ever been tempted to keep an unusual book. She admitted she had. "That's why we need rules even when nobody is watching," she said. He nodded and carried the empty box without another joke.

返程路上，凱問欣是否曾想留下罕見的書。她承認有過。「所以即使沒人看著，我們也需要規則。」她說。凱點頭，沒再開玩笑，抱著空箱子走回去。
