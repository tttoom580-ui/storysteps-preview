作者的允許

The Author's Permission

借一本到明天 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A visiting author offered to read from her new book at the exchange, but one volunteer had already copied several pages for a wall display. Hsin took the pages down before the event opened. The author had offered her time, not unlimited permission to print her work.

一位來訪作家答應在換書活動朗讀新作，卻有志工已把好幾頁文字影印成展牆。欣在開門前把影印頁取下。作者答應付出時間，並不代表允許任意複製作品。

The visiting author asked the team to print only the passage she had approved. Kai measured the poster border in centimeter units before cutting paper. A crow landed outside the reading hall as the event began. A small disagreement over copies ended after the author explained her rights. The author's eyebrow rose when she saw how many pages had been copied.

來訪作家請團隊只印出她同意使用的段落。 凱剪紙前，用公分為單位量了海報邊框。 活動開始時，一隻烏鴉落在閱覽廳外。 作者解釋權利後，關於影印份數的小分歧結束了。 作者看見影印頁數時，眉毛挑了起來。

Kai helped the team write a request explaining the display, the number of copies, and where they would appear. The author approved one short passage with credit, but refused the longer version. Hsin thanked her for making the limit clear.

凱幫團隊寫出使用申請，說明展覽目的、份數與展示位置。作家同意註明來源後使用一小段，拒絕較長的版本。欣感謝她明確說明限制。

There was no entry fee for the reading, and nobody had to buy a book. A picture of a forest appeared on the first approved poster. Bo served as the guard of the approved files, not as the author's manager. The written permission would include the exact number of copies allowed. The narrow desk made it difficult to display both posters side by side.

朗讀會不收門票，也沒有人必須買書。 第一張核准的海報上有一幅森林圖。 博負責保管核准檔案，不是作家的經紀人。 書面許可會包含允許影印的精確份數。 桌子太窄，難以把兩張海報並排展示。

At the reading, a shy student asked whether someone without a rich family could become a writer. The author answered that practice mattered more than a grand room, though every writer needed time and support. Hsin thought of the old annex and its quiet chairs.

朗讀會上，一名害羞學生問沒有富裕家庭背景的人能否成為作家。作家說練習比豪華房間更重要，但寫作者都需要時間與支持。欣想起舊館安靜的椅子。

The author told Hsin that her first book had been written at a shared table, not in a beautiful private office. She remembered people who had offered quiet space and a glass of water without asking what she was working on. Hsin wanted the exchange to become that kind of place for others. Yet she also understood that kindness did not cancel the need for permission. She asked the author to check the draft poster once more, and carefully recorded which version the author had approved.

作家告訴欣，自己的第一本書是在共用桌上寫成的，不是在漂亮的私人辦公室。她記得曾有人借她安靜的空間與一杯水，卻不追問她在寫什麼。欣希望換書活動也能成為那樣的地方，同時明白善意不能取代授權。她請作家再次檢查海報草稿，並仔細記下獲准的版本。

The author did not approve the long passage, nor did she owe an explanation. Hsin looked pale when she realized the unauthorized copies had already been made. A book about a village priest was among the older donated novels. The writer answered a puzzle question about how she planned her story. A healthy relationship between authors and readers required respect on both sides.

作者沒有同意使用長段落，也無義務解釋原因。 欣發現未授權的影本已印好時，臉色發白。 一本描寫鄉村神父的書在較舊的捐贈小說中。 作家回答讀者關於她如何構思故事的謎題。 作者與讀者之間健康的關係，需要雙方尊重。

Bo wrote a proper record of the permission, including its end date. Kai added the approved passage and removed all earlier copies. When Hsin thanked him, he said that building a shelf for a story should not mean taking the story away from its creator.

博留下正式授權紀錄，包含使用期限。凱加入獲准段落，收回所有先前影本。欣道謝時，他說替故事搭書架，不該變成從創作者手中拿走故事。

Kai apologized for a rude comment made by a volunteer before the reading. The skilled writer turned a simple question into a thoughtful conversation. The small stage was only a low platform approved by the center staff. A waiter from the nearby restaurant brought water for the invited guest. The last story mentioned a wedding but was really about choosing a home.

朗讀會前，凱為志工的一句無禮評論道歉。 這位技巧純熟的作家把簡單提問變成深刻的對話。 小舞臺只是學生中心員工核准使用的低矮平台。 附近餐廳的一名服務生替受邀來賓送水。 最後一篇故事提到婚禮，真正主題卻是選擇歸屬。

After the reading, Kai waited until the author had finished answering questions before asking for the signature on their record. He had once believed that a friendly spoken yes would always be enough. Now he knew people could remember conversations differently. The paper did not make their relationship cold; it helped both sides feel safe. Hsin gave him the last clean pen and thanked him for remembering the detail. He asked her to leave one blank page in the notebook for whatever they learned next.

朗讀會後，凱等作家回答完問題才請她簽署紀錄。他曾以為友善口頭答應就夠了，如今知道談話可能被不同人記成不同內容。紙本不會讓關係冰冷，反而使雙方安心。欣遞給他最後一支乾淨筆，感謝他記住細節。凱請她在筆記本留一頁空白，記下未來會學到的事。

After the event, the author gave Hsin a signed copy for the public shelf. Hsin wanted to keep it, but placed it where any reader could borrow it. Kai saw the choice and quietly wrote a note of admiration in their shared notebook.

活動結束後，作家送欣一本簽名書供公共書架使用。欣想留下，卻仍放在人人可借的位置。凱看見了，悄悄在共用筆記本寫下佩服的話。
