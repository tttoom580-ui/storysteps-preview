A Hundred Readers

借一本到明天 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first month ended with one hundred registered readers and enough books for another two exchanges. Bo checked the numbers twice. Hsin prepared a short public report showing the limits of the count: a visit was not proof that every visitor found the right book.

第一個月結束時，登記讀者達一百人，剩餘書量也足夠再辦兩場。博把數字核對了兩次。欣準備簡短公開報告，說明統計的限制：有人來過，不代表每個人都找到合適的書。

A small room became available for one more month of safe book exchange. Bo handed out biscuits while volunteers prepared the public meeting. A visitor offered congratulations, but Hsin reminded everyone the approval was temporary. Kai praised Hsin's courage in saying that the old annex was still unsafe. A children's book about a fox drew a long line at the story table.

一間小教室獲准再供安全換書使用一個月。 志工準備公開會議時，博分送餅乾。 一名訪客表達祝賀，欣卻提醒大家核准只是暫時的。 凱讚賞欣有勇氣承認舊館仍不安全。 一本講狐狸的童書讓故事桌前大排長龍。

The student council invited the team to make a musical presentation at its meeting. Hsin declined the performance idea but offered a clear talk with pictures. Kai supported her, although he would have liked to see her on a brighter stage.

學生會邀請團隊在會議上做一場音樂式簡報。欣婉拒表演形式，改提出有圖片的清楚報告。凱支持她，雖然他其實很想看見她站在更亮的舞臺上。

The volunteers would gather in the hall before the council meeting. Kai used a little glue to fix a torn sign, not the pages of an old book. A book with a drawing of a goose helped a child learn new sounds. They expected gradual growth rather than one dramatic day of success. The team could not ignore the cost of storing old paper safely.

志工們將在學生會議前聚集於大廳。 凱用少許膠水修補破標示，而不是拿來黏舊書書頁。 一本畫著鵝的書幫助孩子學習新的聲音。 他們期待逐步成長，而不是某一天戲劇性的大成功。 團隊不能忽略安全保存舊紙本的成本。

A visiting lecturer asked why the annex itself should be saved if books could move elsewhere. Hsin answered that the first request was for a continuing public service, not an unsafe building. The old room was important, but people were more important.

來訪講師問，既然書能搬去別處，為何還要保住舊館？欣回答，他們首先要求的是持續的公共服務，而非不安全的建物。舊房間很重要，人卻更重要。

The council asked whether the hundred visits included people who returned more than once. Bo explained that the total was a count of separate registrations, while the number of visits was recorded elsewhere. He offered both figures and described their different meanings. Hsin was proud of him. Months earlier, she might have been tempted to use the larger figure because it sounded impressive. Now she knew a smaller, accurate number would help people make a better decision.

學生會問一百名登記讀者是否包含重複來訪者。博解釋登記總數是不同讀者的人數，來訪次數另有紀錄。他提供兩項數字，說明意義不同。欣為他驕傲。幾個月前，她或許會想使用聽起來較厲害的大數字，如今卻知道準確的小數字更能幫助決策。

Kai checked the length of the temporary reading tables. The exit needed another meter of clear space for safe movement. A musical performance was suggested, but Hsin chose a clear report instead. Bo wrote the cost per book without claiming that every book was worth the same. The proper response was to show costs and limits as well as successes.

凱檢查臨時閱讀桌的長度。 出口還需要多一公尺淨空，才能安全通行。 有人建議音樂演出，欣卻選擇清楚的報告。 博寫下每本書的平均成本，沒有宣稱每本價值相同。 適當的回應是同時顯示成本、限制與成果。

The council approved another month of courtyard use and asked for a realistic plan for long-term storage. Kai was pleased, but Hsin reminded the team that a short approval was not a permanent victory. They celebrated with food rather than promises.

學生會核准中庭再使用一個月，並要求提出可行的長期倉儲方案。凱很開心，欣卻提醒大家短期核准並非永久勝利。他們用食物慶祝，而不是用承諾慶祝。

A snail drawn on the sign reminded everyone that steady progress could be slow. Kai changed the style of the chart so visitors could read it more easily. Each unit of shelving needed its own weight limit on the plan. A student on vacation asked whether the exchange would still operate next month. A cookbook with yam recipes was borrowed before the meeting began.

標示上的蝸牛圖案提醒大家，穩定進步也可能很慢。 凱改變圖表樣式，讓訪客更容易閱讀。 計畫裡每個書架單元都需要標示承重上限。 一名放假的學生問下個月換書活動是否仍會辦。 一本山藥食譜在會議開始前就被借走。

During the break, a council member walked with Kai through the courtyard. She asked how much longer the cart wheels would last and who would fix them after Kai graduated. He showed her a maintenance form with dates, spare parts, and space for another person's signature. Hsin watched from the doorway. She realized the strongest answer to 'Who will do this?' was no longer always a name she already knew. It was a method that another person could learn.

休息時，一位學生會成員與凱走過中庭，問推車輪子還能用多久，凱畢業後誰來修。他拿出維修表，上面有日期、備品及其他人簽名的位置。欣從門邊看著，明白「誰來做」最有力的回答，不一定是她熟悉的人名，也可以是別人學得會的方法。

After everyone left, Kai found Hsin looking at the empty chairs. "You counted people," he said, "but I think you also helped them feel seen." Hsin told him that he had helped too. His face turned red, and neither of them tried to hide a smile.

人們散去後，凱看見欣望著空椅子。「妳統計的是人數，」他說，「但我想妳也讓大家感覺自己被看見。」欣告訴他他也有功勞。凱紅了臉，兩人都沒掩飾笑意。
