Names on the Cards

借一本到明天 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the second day, old borrowing cards appeared inside donated books. Some held names, phone numbers, and notes from students who had graduated years ago. Hsin stopped the volunteers before anyone photographed them. Keeping an interesting story was not worth exposing a stranger's private life.

第二天，捐贈書裡出現舊借閱卡，有些寫著多年以前畢業生的姓名、電話和留言。欣在志工拍照前阻止了大家。保存一個有趣故事，不值得公開陌生人的隱私。

The beauty of the old borrowing cards lay in their history, not in private names. Hsin would cancel the photo session until the sensitive cards were removed. Kai found a chain on a storage door and asked who held its key. A chess club donated boards and books, but requested a separate table for games. The current list showed titles and numbers without the donors' personal details.

舊借閱卡的美感在其歷史，而不是私人姓名。 欣決定先取消拍攝，等敏感卡片移走再說。 凱在倉庫門上發現一條鏈子，詢問鑰匙由誰保管。 西洋棋社捐了棋盤和書，卻希望另設一張遊戲桌。 目前的清單只顯示書名與編號，沒有捐贈者的私人資料。

Kai wanted to build a quick online list. Hsin replied that a list could show a title without showing an old owner's identity. They tested a new label using made-up numbers. At first Kai thought she was making simple work difficult; then he saw a real address on a card.

凱想迅速建立線上目錄。欣說，目錄可以顯示書名，不必顯示舊主人的身分。他們用虛構編號測試新標籤。起初凱覺得她把簡單的事弄難了，直到他看見卡片上的真實地址。

The public display used new labels instead of old cards with addresses. Bo said the project's growth would mean little if readers lost trust. Hsin wrote a short guide for volunteers who found letters inside books. They did not hunt for the old owners on social media. One volunteer feared the loss of interesting stories would make the display boring.

公開展區使用新標籤，而不是寫著地址的舊卡。 博說如果讀者失去信任，計畫規模成長就沒意義。 欣寫了一份短指南，給在書裡發現信件的志工。 他們沒有在社群媒體上追查舊書主人。 一名志工擔心少了有趣故事，展覽會變無聊。

A quiet volunteer named Bo offered to hold a locked box for personal papers. Only the university archive officer would decide what could be kept. Hsin wrote that rule in large letters and placed the box out of public reach.

安靜的志工博提議用上鎖的盒子存放私人文件，由校方檔案管理員決定哪些可保留。欣用大字寫下這條規則，並把盒子放在訪客碰不到的地方。

Before lunch, the volunteers tried a simple exercise. Each person received a pretend borrowing card with a false name and a number. They had to decide which details were needed to find the right book. Most chose the number and title; none needed a phone number. Hsin pointed to the result. "We can design for a purpose instead of collecting every fact we see," she said. Kai crossed out three empty boxes on his form. He was surprised at how much clearer the page became.

午餐前，志工做了簡單練習。每人拿到一張有假姓名與編號的模擬借閱卡，要決定找書究竟需要哪些資訊。大多數人選了編號與書名，沒人需要電話。欣指著結果說：「我們能為明確用途設計，不必收集看到的所有資訊。」凱劃掉表單上三個空欄，驚訝頁面因此清楚許多。

Membership in the club did not give anyone permission to read private documents. The archive officer left a message asking the team to lock the box. A small pear sticker helped them recognize a box of children's stories. Kai promised to respond to every complaint before Friday evening. Hsin said her sense of self did not depend on how many books she saved.

社團會員身分不代表有權閱讀私人文件。 檔案管理員留訊息，請團隊把盒子鎖好。 一張小梨子貼紙幫他們認出裝兒童故事的書箱。 凱答應週五晚上前回覆每一件申訴。 欣說，她的自我價值不取決於自己救下多少本書。

When a disappointed visitor complained that the book cards were part of the fun, Kai defended Hsin's decision. He explained that they could celebrate the books without treating their past readers as exhibits. Hsin had not expected him to speak first.

有位失望的訪客抱怨借閱卡才有趣，凱卻主動支持欣。他解釋大家可以慶祝書本，不必把過去的讀者當成展品。欣沒想到他會先開口。

She thought it selfish to use another person's letter simply to attract visitors. A guest speaker described how public archives protect living people's information. The new label was not super fancy, but every visitor could understand it. They began a one-week trial with made-up borrower numbers. Even the worst-looking card might hold a private detail, so they checked them all.

她認為只為吸引訪客而利用他人信件是自私的。 一位受邀講者介紹公共檔案館如何保護在世者的資訊。 新標籤並不特別花俏，但每位訪客都看得懂。 他們開始為期一週的試辦，使用虛構借閱編號。 即使外觀最破舊的卡片也可能含私人資訊，所以每張都要檢查。

At closing time, two sisters came looking for a book their grandfather had once loved. Hsin could not promise to search private records for their family. Instead, she helped them use the public title list and showed them a similar edition. The sisters thanked her, even though they did not find the exact copy. Kai had watched the conversation from the door. He realized a kind answer did not have to be an answer that gave people everything they asked for.

打烊時，兩姊妹來找祖父曾喜歡的一本書。欣不能答應替她們查私人紀錄，於是教她們使用公開書名清單，還找出相似版本。兩姊妹雖然沒有找到那一本，仍向她道謝。凱從門邊看著，終於明白善意的答覆不一定是把對方要求的東西全給出去。

They finished the day with fewer photographs but more trust. Kai left a small drawing of a closed box beside Hsin's rules. Beneath it he wrote, "Not every story belongs to us." She kept that page.

當天留下的照片變少了，信任卻增加了。凱在欣的規則旁畫了一個關著的小盒子，底下寫著：「不是每個故事都屬於我們。」欣留下了那一頁。
