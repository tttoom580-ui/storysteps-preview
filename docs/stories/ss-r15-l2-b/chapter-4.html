東坡來的訪客

The Visitors from East Hill

借一本到明天 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A group from East Hill asked whether the exchange could bring books to their neighborhood. The old library there had reduced its hours. Hsin invited the visitors to explain what they needed rather than choosing books for them. A parent said large-print books mattered more than a bright poster.

東坡的居民詢問換書活動能否把書送到社區，因為那裡的舊圖書館縮短了開放時間。欣請訪客親自說明需求，而不是替他們選書。一名家長說，大字版書籍比漂亮海報更重要。

Families from East Hill planned to attend the listening meeting in the hall. A child asked whether a picture book about a bird in a cage could be borrowed. Hsin used a comb to fix her hair before speaking with the visiting parents. The education department sent a staff member to explain local library services. Someone asked for a book explaining why a drug must be used only as directed.

東坡家庭計畫參加大廳裡的需求傾聽會。 孩子問一部關於籠中鳥的繪本能否借閱。 欣在與來訪家長交談前，用梳子整理頭髮。 教育系派一名工作人員來說明當地圖書服務。 有人想找一本說明藥物為何必須遵照指示使用的書。

Kai measured the path and discovered that his first cart could not move safely along the narrow ramp. He was embarrassed but willing to change the wheels. Hsin asked a wheelchair user to review the new route without expecting that person to fix the whole design.

凱測量路線後發現，第一版推車無法安全通過狹窄坡道。他感到尷尬，但願意換輪子。欣請一位輪椅使用者檢視新路線，卻不把整個設計責任丟給對方。

The eastern neighborhood had lost two public evening study rooms. Hsin tried to encourage visitors to describe needs in their own words. The group stayed firm about keeping the main walkway open. A parent brought rice to fry for the community meal after the meeting. The gym was offered as a backup venue, but it lacked quiet reading corners.

東部社區已失去兩間公共夜間自習室。 欣鼓勵訪客用自己的話描述需求。 團隊堅持主要走道必須暢通。 一名家長帶米飯來，準備會後炒飯給大家吃。 體育館被提為備用場地，但缺少安靜閱讀角。

The department gave them permission to use the ground-floor hall for a public meeting. Their purpose was not to collect praise; it was to find out which books people could actually use. The replies were more varied than the team had imagined.

系上准許他們使用一樓大廳召開公開會議。他們的目的不是收集稱讚，而是了解人們真正用得上的書。回覆比團隊想像得更多元。

The visitors did not agree with one another about everything. A student wanted exam books; a grandmother hoped for picture books she could read with her grandson; a parent asked for quiet seats. Hsin placed three separate cards on the wall instead of forcing them into one answer. She asked each person whether the words matched their request. Kai listened with his arms crossed, then quietly offered to design shelves that could be moved when needs changed. The meeting had become a conversation rather than a vote about who mattered most.

訪客對需求並非意見一致。學生想要考試用書，祖母盼望能與孫子一起讀繪本，家長則需要安靜座位。欣把三張不同的需求卡貼上牆，不強迫大家只選一個答案。她逐一詢問文字是否符合原意。凱抱著手臂傾聽，最後輕聲提議設計可移動書架，讓需求改變時也能調整。會議成了對話，而不是決定誰最重要的投票。

A minor change to wheel size made Kai's cart easier to control. A slow motion test showed that the cart turned too sharply near the ramp. Bo placed a pan of snacks on a side table far from the books. The team refused to write visitors' private health stories on public request cards. The purpose of the meeting was to listen, not to choose for others.

輪子尺寸的小幅調整讓凱的推車更易操控。 慢動作測試顯示，推車在坡道附近轉彎過急。 博把裝點心的平底鍋放在遠離書本的側桌。 團隊拒絕把訪客的私人健康故事寫在公開需求卡上。 會議的目的是傾聽，不是代替別人選擇。

When one volunteer wanted to promise weekly deliveries at once, Hsin asked for a trial first. A useful service needed a realistic schedule and someone responsible for each trip. Kai agreed and added a repair day to the plan.

一名志工想立即承諾每週送書，欣要求先試辦。好的服務需要可行時程，也要有人對每趟運送負責。凱同意，並在計畫中加上維修日。

Families from the region wanted books in several different languages. Hsin proposed a basic standard for clear letters and safe paths. Rain began suddenly, so a volunteer handed an umbrella to an older guest. A child loved a drawing of a bird with one broken wing. The animal shelf held a surprising book about the return of the wolf.

該地區家庭希望取得幾種不同語言的書。 欣提議訂下字體清楚、動線安全的基本標準。 雨忽然下起來，志工把雨傘借給年長訪客。 一名孩子喜歡一幅單側翅膀受傷的鳥兒圖畫。 動物書架上有一本談論狼群回歸的意外好書。

After the group left, the hall looked large and empty. Kai pointed to the place where a parent had stopped to rest. He suggested leaving a chair there during future events. Hsin wrote the idea down and asked whether the chair would block the safe route. They measured together before deciding. That small act felt different from their earlier arguments. They had stopped trying to prove who was more thoughtful, and begun checking how thoughtfulness could work in real space.

訪客離開後，大廳顯得空曠。凱指著剛才一位家長休息的位置，提議未來活動放張椅子。欣記下主意，詢問是否會擋住安全路線。他們一起測量後才決定。這件小事與過去爭論不同。兩人不再急著證明誰更體貼，而開始檢查體貼如何在真實空間中落實。

After the meeting, a child handed Hsin a drawing of a cart full of stories. Kai held the door while the visitors left. "Perhaps the annex is bigger than its walls," he said. Hsin looked at the drawing and knew exactly what he meant.

會後，一名孩子送給欣一張裝滿故事的推車畫。訪客離開時，凱扶住門。「也許舊館比它的牆更大。」他說。欣看著那幅畫，完全明白他的意思。
