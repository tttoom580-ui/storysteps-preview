東邊的門

The East Door

總裁，假婚約星期五到期 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On Thursday Tessa walked to the east door of the hall. A green carpet covered the old floor, and a block of wood stood before the stairs. She saw a bath bowl in a nearby service room and a medium-sized box under a chair. Both objects were in the wrong place. “We must not let guests cross this line,” she told Julian. Just then someone gave a quick knock at the door. Mrs. Cole, the house manager, arrived with a cute dog and said she had a surprise: an old floor plan that showed another way into the hall.

星期四，泰莎走向大鐘廳東邊的門。舊地板上鋪著綠地毯，樓梯前放了一塊木塊。她在附近服務室看見洗澡用的盆子，椅子下還有一個中等大小的箱子。兩樣物品都放錯位置。「我們不能讓客人跨過這條線。」她對朱利安說。這時門響起短短的敲門聲。館務經理柯爾太太帶著一隻可愛小狗來了，說有驚喜：一張顯示另一個入口的舊平面圖。

The map showed a choice between the main door and a quiet side hall. Tessa looked at Julian. “The side hall may help people who do not want to use the stair,” she said. Julian wanted to open it that afternoon, but Mrs. Cole shook her head. The back door had not been checked for years. A taxicab waited outside with workers who could study the wall and floor. Julian asked them to pass through the service entrance instead of the guest door. “No one gets a special pass because I am the boss,” he said.

圖上有兩個選擇：主要入口，以及安靜的側廊。「這條側廊也許能幫助不方便走樓梯的人。」泰莎說。朱利安想當天下午就開放，柯爾太太卻搖頭：後門多年沒檢查了。外面有輛計程車，載來能檢查牆與地板的工人。朱利安請他們走服務入口，不要穿越客人出入的門。「就算我是老闆，也沒有特殊通行權。」他說。

Calvin Mo, Julian's cousin, appeared with a smile that did not reach his eyes. “A quiet hall is not good business,” he said. “A grand wedding room will bring rich families.” Tessa kept her head still and answered, “A beautiful party is not a reason to forget safety.” Calvin laughed. “And who asked the clock woman?” Julian did not move between them. He simply said, “She is our repair expert. We asked her.” Tessa liked that he defended her work, not a false story about their love. Calvin left, saying the family board would soon make a choice.

朱利安的堂兄凱文出現了，笑容卻沒到眼底。「安靜的大廳做不了好生意，豪華婚宴室才會吸引有錢家族。」他說。泰莎抬起頭回答：「漂亮的宴會不能成為忽視安全的理由。」凱文笑了：「誰問妳這修鐘女人？」朱利安沒有站到兩人中間，只說：「她是我們聘請的修復專家。我們有問她。」泰莎喜歡他維護她的工作身分，而不是假戀情。凱文離開前說董事會很快要做決定。

At the end of the day the workers found that the side hall was clear, but a loose stone near the door still needed repair. Tessa wrote a note on the plan: NOT OPEN YET. It was not an exciting surprise. It was the right answer. Julian walked with her as far as the outside steps and offered to call a taxi. Tessa chose to walk. “I live near the river,” she explained. “I like to look at the shops on my way.” He did not follow. A quiet goodbye felt better than a long talk he had not been invited to have.

一天結束時，工人發現側廊本身已清理乾淨，但門邊有塊鬆動的石頭，仍須修補。泰莎在圖上註記：「尚未開放。」這不是令人興奮的驚喜，卻是正確答案。朱利安陪她走到外頭階梯，提出幫忙叫計程車。泰莎選擇步行：「我住在河邊，喜歡回家路上看看商店。」他沒有跟著走。平靜的道別，比她沒有邀請的漫長談話更舒服。

Mrs. Cole held the old plan against the wall and asked the workers to check each door. One man measured the steps twice; another wrote down the height of the floor. Tessa asked them to speak in words ordinary visitors could understand. “If this door is open too soon, a person may fall,” the worker said. “That is much clearer,” she replied. Julian decided to put the finding in the next public report, including the part that needed repair. He did not want to hide a safety problem behind a beautiful picture. Later the little dog sat quietly by Mrs. Cole's feet while everyone packed the tools. Tessa looked at the work they had done. The hall was still closed, but it was one step closer to being honestly ready.

柯爾太太把舊平面圖靠在牆上，請工人逐門檢查。一人量兩次台階，另一人記錄地板高度。泰莎請大家用一般訪客聽得懂的話說明。「這扇門若太早開，人可能跌倒。」工人說。「這就清楚多了。」她回答。朱利安決定把發現寫進下次公開報告，連需要修的部分也列明，不願用美麗照片掩蓋安全問題。稍後小狗安靜坐在柯爾太太腳邊，大家收拾工具。泰莎看著工作成果：大廳雖仍關閉，但距離真正能開放又近了一步。

Later Mrs. Cole called to report that the old plan had been placed in a safe file. “You can look at it tomorrow,” she told Tessa. “We can also ask if there is another copy.” Tessa thanked her. Julian, meanwhile, stood alone at the red-carpet door and listened to the empty hall. He had always thought a boss should make a choice quickly. Now he understood why people sometimes needed to stop and look twice. If he wanted the public to trust him, he would have to show that safety and honesty were not just words for a speech.

稍後柯爾太太來電說，舊平面圖已放進安全檔案。「妳明天能來看，也可以查是否有另一份。」她說。泰莎道謝。此時朱利安獨自站在紅地毯門前，聽著空蕩大廳的聲音。以前他總覺得老闆必須很快做選擇，如今明白有時人需要停下，再看一次。若想讓大眾信任自己，安全與誠實就不能只是演講時說說而已。
