沒有劇本的晚餐

Dinner Without a Script

總裁，假婚約星期五到期 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Julian invited Tessa to an evening dinner with his family. She was free to say no, but she wanted to watch how they spoke about the hall. The dining room was dark except for a lamp above a rose in a glass. A waiter passed a menu with potato soup and sweet bread. Tessa touched a loose button on her coat and smiled at the simple food. “I usually eat at home,” she said. Julian sat to her left. “Then stay only as long as you like.” Across the table Calvin watched them with the look of a student waiting for a difficult test.

朱利安邀請泰莎參加家族晚餐。她可以拒絕，但想親眼看看家人怎麼談大鐘廳。餐廳有點暗，只有燈照著玻璃杯裡的一朵玫瑰。服務生送上菜單，有馬鈴薯湯和甜麵包。泰莎摸摸外套鬆動的鈕扣，對簡單食物微笑。「我通常在家吃飯。」她說。朱利安坐她左邊：「妳想待多久就多久。」桌對面的凱文盯著他們，像等著難題考試的學生。

Calvin began with an interesting story about the buyer, then turned it into trouble. “Our guests worry that a public hall will bring noise,” he said. “We have to protect our name.” Tessa asked whether they had measured the sound or counted the guests. Calvin said she was making dinner boring. Julian answered, “It is not boring to ask for evidence.” His aunt rose from the table and told him he had changed. “Perhaps,” he said, “but I still have a company to run.” Tessa felt the room grow colder. She kept eating slowly and let the men answer their own business questions.

凱文先說起買方一段有趣的故事，接著把話題帶入爭執。「客人擔心公共大廳會太吵，」他說，「我們必須保護品牌。」泰莎問他們有沒有測過聲音或統計人數。凱文說她讓晚餐變無聊。朱利安回答：「要求證據不是無聊。」姑媽從桌旁起身，說他變了。「也許吧，」他說，「但我還是得經營公司。」泰莎感覺房間更冷，只慢慢吃東西，讓男人自己回答商業問題。

A waiter dropped a spoon. The sharp sound made everyone turn. Tessa noticed the waiter was shaking. She helped move a chair so he could pick it up safely. He whispered thanks. Calvin called her a sweet little guest. “I am a clock repairer, and I know how it feels when people only watch your mistakes,” Tessa said. Julian did not answer for her. Later, when they stepped into the hall, he said, “You made that worker feel seen.” She replied, “He was already there. You just have to look.” Julian smiled at the plain truth.

服務生掉了一支湯匙，清脆聲響讓大家回頭。泰莎發現他正在發抖，便把椅子移開，讓他安全拾起湯匙。他低聲道謝。凱文稱她是甜美的小客人。「我是修鐘師，也知道別人只盯著你犯錯是什麼感覺。」泰莎說。朱利安沒有代替她回答。稍後走進大廳，他說：「妳讓那位員工感到被看見。」她回答：「他本來就在那裡，你只要願意看。」朱利安為這句直白真話笑了。

At the door Tessa's coat button fell to the floor. Julian bent to pick it up but stopped when she reached first. “May I?” he asked. “Yes, please,” she said, and took the button from him. Their hands touched for a moment. This time there was no camera. Neither person moved away quickly. Tessa felt warm, then remembered their agreement. “It is still a work contract,” she said quietly. “I know,” Julian replied. “I will not ask the contract to be something it is not.” His voice was soft enough that only she heard it.

到了門口，泰莎外套的鈕扣掉到地上。朱利安彎腰撿，見她也伸手便先停住。「可以嗎？」他問。「好，謝謝。」她從他手中接過鈕扣。兩人的手碰了一下，這次沒有相機。誰都沒有急著躲開。泰莎感覺心裡暖暖的，隨即想起協議。「這仍是工作合約。」她輕聲說。「我知道，」朱利安答，「我不會要求合約變成它本來不是的東西。」他的聲音只有她聽得到。

As the evening ended, Tessa walked around the empty dining room to find the little button she had dropped. She had already sewn it back on, but she wanted to check whether another one might fall. The waiter returned with a box of spare thread. “We keep this for guests who need a quick fix,” he said. Tessa thanked him and asked whether the workers could speak at the public meeting. “Of course,” Julian answered from the door. “They know this place better than I do.” Tessa saw his smile and felt a warm pull in her chest. She did not need to hide that feeling from herself. She only needed to keep it separate from the work until both of them were ready to talk openly.

夜晚將盡時，泰莎繞著空餐廳走，確認先前掉鈕扣的位置。鈕扣已縫回去，但她想檢查其他鈕扣會不會也鬆了。服務生帶來一盒備用線。「我們留這個給需要緊急修補的客人。」他說。泰莎道謝，問員工能不能在公開會議發言。「當然，」朱利安在門邊回答，「他們比我更懂這地方。」泰莎看見他的微笑，心裡有股溫暖牽引。她不用對自己隱瞞感受，只要在雙方準備好坦白談之前，仍把感情與工作分清楚。

Back in her apartment Tessa watched the city lights turn on and thought about the dinner. She did not worry that Julian had failed to be perfect. She worried that she might begin to like him before the work was done. She took a needle, fixed the button, and then read the public meeting plan for Monday. Every speaker would get the same short time, including the buyer. Tessa liked that rule. A fair meeting would make room for voices that the rich family had ignored. She went to bed with the small silver clock part beside her notes and decided to keep her eyes open.

回到公寓，泰莎看著城市燈光亮起，回想晚餐。她不擔心朱利安不完美；她擔心自己會在工作結束前開始喜歡他。她拿針縫好鈕扣，再讀星期一的公開會議安排。每位發言人都有相同的短時間，包括買方。她喜歡這項規則。公平會議才能讓長期被豪門忽略的聲音進來。她把小銀色鐘零件放在筆記旁，帶著保持清醒的決心入睡。
