月光下的收購案

The Moonlight Offer

總裁，假婚約星期五到期 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Calvin met Julian at the bank beside his family's old farm. He brought a letter from a buyer who wanted to turn the hall into a private movie room. The buyer promised to sell expensive tickets and bring in many rich guests. “We can feed the company with that money,” Calvin said. Julian looked through the paper and noticed that the price did not include the work needed to keep the old clock safe. Outside, the moon rose above the yellow field. A teenager rode past on a bicycle with a long string tied to a paper bird. Julian wondered where the real cost would fall.

凱文約朱利安在家族舊農場旁的銀行見面，帶來買方的一封信。買方想把大鐘廳變成私人電影室，賣昂貴門票，吸引有錢客人。「這筆錢可以養活公司。」凱文說。朱利安翻看文件，發現報價沒包含確保古鐘安全的費用。屋外月亮升上黃色田野，一個少年騎腳踏車經過，紙鳥用長線綁在車後。朱利安想，真正的成本最後會落在誰身上？

Tessa visited the farm later to speak with an old worker who had once helped clean the hall. The worker moved slowly and kept rubbing one toe through a worn shoe. He remembered a strange day when the public clock stopped after someone used too much oil. “The wheel was not lost then,” he said. “It went straight to the repair shop.” He showed her a small box with an old address. Tessa did not think every old story was true. But she copied the address because it gave them a place to check. She thanked him for his time and made sure he had a seat.

稍後泰莎到農場拜訪一位曾協助打掃大鐘廳的老員工。老人走得很慢，鞋裡的一根腳趾一直摩擦著破舊鞋面。他記得某天有人加太多油，公共大鐘便停了。「當年齒輪不是弄丟，」他說，「而是直接送去修理店。」他拿出一個寫著舊地址的小盒子。泰莎沒有把每個老故事都當真，但她抄下地址，因為那是能查證的線索。她謝謝老人，也確認他有椅子可坐。

At dinner Julian asked Tessa what she thought of the offer. She answered, “You should not ask the person who repairs the clock to decide your company policy. But I can tell you which parts of the report are wrong.” She took out a sheet and drew a line through the buyer's false repair number. Julian had planned to make a fast answer that night. Instead he told the board he needed more time. Calvin sent a message: “Your new love is making you weak.” Julian read it and put the phone away. He had begun to think that careful work was a kind of strength.

晚餐時，朱利安問泰莎怎麼看收購案。「你不該叫修鐘的人替公司決定政策，」她回答，「但我可以告訴你報告哪裡錯。」她拿出紙，劃掉買方不實的維修數字。朱利安原本打算當晚快速答覆，如今通知董事會需要更多時間。凱文傳訊說：「你的新戀人讓你軟弱。」朱利安看過後收起手機。他逐漸明白，謹慎做事也是一種力量。

The next morning a local paper made the private movie plan its main topic. Some people wanted the new room because they hoped it would bring jobs. Others worried that children would never again hear the old clock. Tessa asked the hotel to hold a public meeting where people could ask questions. “A good plan must go through more than one door,” she said. Julian agreed on one condition: the meeting would happen in a safe room, not under the clock. Mrs. Cole reserved the small dining hall. When Calvin heard the news, he called it a waste of time.

隔天早晨，地方報紙把私人電影室計畫當成頭條。有些人希望新場地帶來工作，另一些人擔心孩子再也聽不到老鐘報時。泰莎請飯店舉辦公開說明會，讓大家提問。「好計畫要經得起不同人的檢視。」她說。朱利安同意，但要求會議在安全房間舉行，不在大鐘底下。柯爾太太訂下小餐廳。凱文聽見後說那是浪費時間。

Before the public meeting, the family accountant asked Julian to look at a slow rise in the hall's repair costs. The number was not a secret, but it had been spread across several small bills. Julian put the bills together on one table and asked for a clear total. He wondered how many times the company had made a choice without seeing the whole price. Tessa did not help him decide what to spend. Instead she checked that her own repair bill had the right dates and parts. She knew a good relationship could not begin with hidden money. At the door Julian thanked her for keeping their jobs separate. She answered, “It is easier to like someone when we both know where the work ends.” They walked out into the cool evening.

公開會議前，家族會計請朱利安注意大廳維修費緩慢上升。數字不是祕密，只是散在好幾張小帳單裡。朱利安把帳單攤在同一桌，要求算出清楚總額。他想公司過去究竟有多少次沒看全價格就決定。泰莎不替他決定怎麼花錢，只核對自己的維修帳單日期與零件。她知道好關係不能從藏錢開始。門口朱利安謝謝她把工作界線守清楚。她回答：「兩個人都知道工作何時結束，才更容易真心喜歡對方。」他們一起走進涼爽夜色。

That evening Tessa went home and hung the farm address beside her desk. It was only a clue, not a promise. Julian called and asked if she could join him at the repair shop the next day. She checked her own work schedule before answering yes. “We are still not a couple,” she reminded him. “I know,” he said. “I asked because you know clocks.” She smiled after ending the call. Outside her window the city lights made the clouds look pale yellow. For the first time, she felt curious about the man behind the dark suit, rather than the rich name printed on every hotel door.

那晚泰莎回家，把農場地址貼在書桌旁。那只是一條線索，不是保證。朱利安打來問她明天能否一起去修理店。她先看過自己的工作行程，才答應。「我們仍不是情侶，」她提醒。「我知道，」他說，「我邀請妳是因為妳懂鐘。」掛掉電話後她笑了。窗外城燈把雲照成淡黃色。第一次，她好奇的是深色西裝裡的那個人，而不只是印在每間飯店門上的富豪姓氏。
