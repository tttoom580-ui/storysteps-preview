給每個人的票

A Ticket for Everyone

總裁，假婚約星期五到期 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The public meeting began at four o’clock in the afternoon. At one corner of the room, an actor and an actress helped visitors find seats. A small band played soft music, and a woman with glasses handed out a package of papers. Julian wanted to celebrate the old hall, but he knew the meeting was not a party. Some people were tired after work; others had walked from the supermarket. Tessa checked the key facts on her last page. The media had come in large numbers. She hoped they would report the question of public use instead of asking which dress she wore.

公開會議在下午四點開始。房間一角，一男一女兩位演員幫訪客找座位。小樂團演奏柔和音樂，戴眼鏡的女士分發資料包。朱利安想慶祝古老大廳，但知道會議不是派對。有人下班後很疲倦，有人從超市走來。泰莎再核對最後一頁的關鍵事實。媒體來了很多人，她希望記者報導公共使用問題，不是問她穿什麼衣服。

The first speaker said his father had used the hall when the family was poor. Another woman said a new wedding room might bring more work. Julian listened to both without making a face. Calvin took the microphone and said the clock might die if the hall remained open. Tessa raised her hand. “A clock is not alive,” she said. “Its parts can stop working. That is why we repair and test them.” Some visitors laughed, but she did not make fun of Calvin. “We can disagree and still keep our facts clear,” she added. The room grew calm enough for more questions.

第一位發言者說家境貧窮時，父親曾使用大廳。另一位女士說新婚宴室可能帶來更多工作。朱利安聽兩邊說，沒有露出不耐表情。凱文拿起麥克風，說若開放大廳，鐘可能會「死掉」。泰莎舉手：「鐘不是活物。零件會停止運作，這也是為什麼要維修和測試。」有些人笑了，她卻沒嘲弄凱文。「意見不同，事實仍要清楚。」她補充。房間平靜下來，大家得以繼續提問。

A worker asked whether a free public day could still need a ticket to count visitors. “Yes,” Tessa said, “if the ticket is free and the reason is clear.” A girl asked whether children could play music again. Julian answered that the repairs must finish first. He did not promise more than he knew. “My grandmother would have liked this question,” he said. After the last speaker, the room held a short silence. Then people began talking to one another, not only to the CEO. Tessa saw a little more hope in that than in the beautiful lights above them.

一位員工問免費開放日可否仍發票以統計人數。「可以，」泰莎說，「只要票免費、用途清楚。」一個女孩問孩子能否再來演奏。朱利安回答必須先完成修繕，沒有承諾超出已知的事。「祖母一定會喜歡這個問題。」他說。最後一位講者說完，房內短暫安靜，接著大家互相交談，不只對總裁說話。泰莎覺得這比頭頂漂亮的燈光更有希望。

After the meeting the hotel gave every visitor a card with two ways to send a comment. They could write one now or send it later. Julian's aunt said the family looked weak for asking the public. “Maybe,” Julian replied. “But I want a decision that lasts.” Tessa was tired, but she stayed to help count the cards. Several people wrote that the hall should have both music events and a safe room for celebrations. “That sounds like a useful question for the building team,” Tessa said. She did not claim it was the final answer.

會後飯店給每位訪客一張卡片，可現場填寫，也能之後寄送意見。朱利安的姑媽說家族向大眾發問顯得軟弱。「也許，」朱利安答，「但我想要能持續的決定。」泰莎雖然疲倦，仍留下協助計數。好幾人寫，希望大廳同時能有音樂活動與安全的慶祝空間。「這是值得建築小組研究的問題。」泰莎說，沒有假裝那已是最後答案。

The actor who had helped with seats came back after the visitors left. He told Tessa that some older guests could not hear the last question because the music had been too loud near the door. Tessa thanked him for the note. Julian asked the band to stop playing while people spoke at future meetings. “A meeting is not a show,” he said. “We need to hear one another.” The actor agreed and offered to help test the sound before the next public event. It was a small change, but it answered a real problem. Tessa wrote it down beside the comments about seats and doors. She liked that the public meeting had given them work they could do, not only opinions to count. Julian liked it for the same reason.

協助帶位的男演員在訪客離開後回來，告訴泰莎有些年長客人聽不見最後一題，因為門口音樂太大聲。泰莎謝謝他提出意見。朱利安請樂團往後有人發言時先停止演奏。「會議不是表演，」他說，「我們得聽見彼此。」演員同意，表示下次活動前可以協助測音。這是小修改，卻回答了真問題。泰莎把它寫在座位與門的意見旁。她喜歡公開會議不只留下可計數的意見，也留下可執行的工作。朱利安同樣喜歡。

In the empty room Julian thanked Tessa for helping. “You stood by me,” he said. “I stood by the facts,” she corrected with a gentle smile. He accepted the difference. They went outside, where the air was cool and the city looked quiet. Julian asked if she wanted to see a small play at the weekend. He added, “As two people, not for a photograph.” Tessa looked at him carefully. “After the repair test,” she said. “And only if I can still say no on the day.” “Of course,” Julian answered. For the first time his smile stayed after she walked away.

房間空了，朱利安謝謝泰莎幫忙。「妳站在我這邊。」他說。「我站在事實這邊。」她溫柔微笑糾正。他接受了這差別。兩人走到外面，空氣清涼，城市安靜。朱利安問週末要不要看一場小戲，補充：「以兩個人的身分，不為了照片。」泰莎仔細看他：「先完成維修測試，而且當天我仍可以說不。」「當然。」朱利安回答。她離開後，他第一次笑了很久。
