有出口的契約

A Contract With Open Doors

總裁，假婚約星期五到期 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next morning anybody in the city seemed to have seen the picture. Tessa was late to her small apartment because a reporter waited outside the building. She asked the reporter to move away and called Julian on the phone. “I do not want your money to buy my quiet,” she said. Julian answered, “Then tell me what you need.” Tessa sat on her old sofa with a pen. She could hear children playing near a church across the road. She made a clear list: correct the false story, protect her private address, and pay the full repair bill on time.

第二天早上，城裡彷彿每個人都看過那張照片。因為一位記者守在公寓外，泰莎回到住處時已經晚了。她請記者離開，打電話給朱利安。「我不要你用錢買我的安靜。」她說。朱利安回答：「那請告訴我妳需要什麼。」泰莎坐在舊沙發上，拿起筆。街對面教堂附近傳來孩子玩耍的聲音。她清楚列出三件事：更正假新聞、保護私人住址、準時付清修鐘費。

Julian came for lunch at a small cafe beside the pond, not in a black car with ten helpers. He was serious about a problem Tessa had not heard. His family wanted to sell the Grand Clock Hall, the public room inside the hotel. A third party had offered to buy the space and close it. His grandfather had once said that the hall must remain open to the country, but no one could find the paper. The picture of Julian and Tessa had made people think he was planning a wedding there. “That is a strange guess,” Tessa said. “It is also useful to my uncle,” Julian admitted.

朱利安到池塘旁的小咖啡館一起吃午餐，沒有帶黑色轎車與十名隨從。他認真說起一件泰莎不知道的事：家人想賣掉飯店內對民眾開放的大鐘廳。第三方出價收購，打算關閉空間。祖父曾說那裡必須繼續向大家開放，現在卻沒人找得到相關文件。朱利安與泰莎的照片讓外界誤以為兩人準備在大廳結婚。「這猜測真奇怪。」泰莎說。「對我叔叔倒很有用。」朱利安坦白。

“My uncle says the false story proves we should turn the room into a private wedding place,” Julian explained. Tessa put down her tea. “You cannot use me as an answer to that question.” He agreed. Then she suggested a third choice: they would attend three planned public events together while the hotel made its correction, and she would explain the clock project as an independent expert. Their written agreement would last twenty-one days. There would be no private demands, no surprise touch, and no promise of marriage. Either person could end the public appearances at any time. Julian wrote every word down.

「叔叔說，這則假消息證明大廳應改成私人婚禮場地。」朱利安解釋。泰莎放下茶杯：「你不能拿我當這個問題的答案。」他同意了。接著泰莎提出第三個選擇：飯店公開澄清期間，兩人一起參加三場事先安排的活動，由她以獨立專家的身分解說修鐘計畫。書面協議期限二十一天，不得有私人要求、突然碰觸或結婚承諾。任何一方都能隨時停止公開露面。朱利安把每個字寫下來。

A reporter at the next table raised a phone. Tessa did not forget her rules. She said, “We are not engaged. I repair clocks. You may report that fact.” Julian repeated her words. The reporter looked unhappy; an ordinary repair job was less exciting than a secret wedding. At the cafe door, Julian offered to walk Tessa home. She said no because the reporter might follow. He stepped aside without asking a third time. “Your choice,” he said. Tessa knew that a polite answer once did not prove a good character. But it was a better beginning than a guess made from a photograph.

隔壁桌的記者舉起手機。泰莎沒有忘記自己的規則。「我們沒有訂婚。我修理鐘錶，你可以報導這個事實。」她說。朱利安重複了她的話。記者顯得失望，普通維修案不如祕密婚禮刺激。離開咖啡館時，朱利安提出送泰莎回家。她拒絕了，因為記者可能跟蹤。他沒有再問第三遍，只讓開路。「妳決定。」他說。泰莎知道一次禮貌回答不能證明一個人值得信任，但總比憑照片猜測好。

Before sleep Tessa put the signed page in a small folder marked HERE. She tore a clean piece of paper from her notebook and wrote three questions for the lawyer. Could a reporter show her home address? What would happen if a public event was cancelled? Who would answer if a hotel worker broke the rule? She sent the questions in the morning rather than guessing at night. Julian answered with the lawyer's contact details and said she could choose another adviser if she wished. His answer did not make the questions disappear, but it showed that the paper was more than a nice story. The agreement had clear limits and a way for each person to be heard. Tessa placed it beside her work schedule, not beside her private letters.

睡前，泰莎把簽好的紙放進標著「放這裡」的小資料夾。她從筆記本撕下一小張乾淨紙，寫下三個問題要問律師：記者能否公開她家地址？公開活動取消會怎麼處理？飯店員工違反規則由誰回應？她決定早上寄出，不在夜裡胡亂猜測。朱利安提供律師聯絡方式，並說她想找另一位顧問也可以。這回答沒有讓問題消失，卻說明協議不只是好聽故事；它有清楚界線，也讓雙方都能發聲。泰莎把它放在工作日程旁，而非私人信件旁。

In the evening Tessa read their short agreement on the sofa again. It had a payment date, the name of a lawyer who could answer questions, and a clear way to stop. It did not give Julian the right to enter her apartment or ask about her life. She signed the page and sent it by email. Julian returned a copy with his own name. He looked at the old family photograph on his desk: his grandfather stood beside the Grand Clock Hall with a little child. Behind them was a sign that read EVERYONE IS WELCOME. He wondered why no one had told him that the sign had been taken down.

傍晚，泰莎又在沙發上讀了一遍簡短的協議。上面有付款日期、能回答疑問的律師姓名，以及清楚的終止方式。協議沒有允許朱利安進她家，也沒有要求她交代私生活。她簽名後寄出電子郵件。朱利安也簽名回傳。他看著桌上的家族舊照片：祖父與一個孩子站在大鐘廳旁，身後的牌子寫著「歡迎所有人」。他想知道，為何從沒有人告訴自己那塊牌子已被拿走。
