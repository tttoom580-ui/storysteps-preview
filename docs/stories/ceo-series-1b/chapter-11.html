The White Wall

總裁，假婚約星期五到期 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The next morning Tessa brought a basket of old pictures to the public library. A thin white belt held the pages together. She wanted to check whether the room had been used for more than music. A librarian showed her a picture of children flying a kite beside the hotel. The note under it said that a hospital had once used the hall for a health talk. “Dear me,” the librarian said. “People often forget these little events.” Tessa did not hate the private wedding idea. She only wanted the board to see the useful history before making a choice.

隔天早上，泰莎帶著一籃舊照片到公共圖書館，細細的白色帶子把紙頁綁在一起。她想查大鐘廳是否除了音樂還有其他用途。館員拿出孩子在飯店旁放風箏的照片。下方註記：醫院曾在大廳辦健康講座。「哎呀，」館員說，「人們常忘記這些小活動。」泰莎並不討厭私人婚禮的想法，只希望董事會決定前能先看到這段有用的歷史。

A young player from the old music class came to help. He was now a teacher and still had a picture of himself beside the clock. His mother had sent him to the class when he was small. “I was often sick and could not run around outside,” he said. “Those lessons gave me a place to sit.” He made a joke about how badly he had played the drum. Tessa laughed and asked if she could use his words in her report. “Yes,” he said, “but not the part about my terrible drum.” She marked the lines he approved.

一位舊音樂班學員來幫忙，如今他已是老師，還留著自己在鐘旁的照片。小時候他常生病，母親送他去上課。「我不常能在外面跑，」他說，「那些課讓我有地方坐。」他開玩笑說自己的鼓打得多糟。泰莎笑了，問能否引用他的話。「可以，」他說，「但別寫我鼓打得糟的部分。」她把他同意的句子標記起來。

At the hotel Julian checked the first public meeting plan. He asked for water, seats, and a clear picture of the two possible futures for the hall. The staff had made a long speech for him, but he cut it down. “If we cannot explain an idea in plain words, we should study it again,” he said. Calvin wanted to send private invitations only. Julian refused. “The hall has belonged to the city in people's minds for years. We must listen to the people who used it.” He felt a little afraid of the crowd, but his hands remained steady.

飯店裡，朱利安檢查公開會議計畫。他要求備妥飲水、座位，以及兩種大廳未來方案的清楚圖片。員工替他寫了長篇演講，他刪短了。「如果我們不能用簡單話說明，就該再研究。」凱文想只寄私人邀請函，朱利安拒絕：「多年來大家心中都覺得大廳屬於城市，我們得聽使用過它的人說話。」他有點害怕群眾，雙手卻很穩。

Tessa arrived with the library records and saw that someone had left a sharp metal hook near the meeting seats. She asked the staff to move it. “If people come to ask questions, we should not make them worry about the floor,” she said. Julian asked whether she needed help carrying her basket. This time she said yes because the pages were heavy. They walked side by side through the hall. “You do not have to do everything alone,” he told her. “Neither do you,” she answered. It was the first time he had considered that she might be watching out for him too.

泰莎帶著圖書館資料到飯店，發現有人把尖銳金屬掛鉤放在會議座位旁，便請員工移走。「大家是來問問題，不該擔心地板有危險。」她說。朱利安問要不要幫她提籃子，這次她答應了，因為紙張很重。兩人並肩走過大廳。「妳不必每件事都一個人做。」他說。「你也一樣。」她回答。他第一次想到，她也可能正在照顧他。

A child came to the library desk and asked what color the old clock had been. Tessa showed the cleaned photograph: the clock was brown, while the star above it was red. “Will it look the same again?” the child asked. Tessa said the broken parts could be repaired, but old things did not always become exactly new. “That is all right,” the child replied. “My grandmother's table has marks too. I like it.” The librarian wrote the question in the visitor book. Julian later read it and smiled. The room's future was no longer a question asked only by the family board. It belonged to people of different ages who cared about simple things, like a clock that told the right time and a place to sit.

一個孩子到圖書館櫃檯，問老鐘原本是什麼顏色。泰莎拿出整理過的照片：鐘是棕色，上方星星是紅色。「它會變回一模一樣嗎？」孩子問。泰莎說壞掉的部分能修，但老東西不一定能變成全新。「沒關係，」孩子答，「奶奶的桌子也有痕跡，我喜歡。」館員把問題寫進訪客簿。朱利安後來讀到，露出笑容。房間的未來不再只是家族董事會的問題，也屬於不同年齡、在乎簡單事物的人：一座準確報時的鐘，以及能坐下的地方。

At the end of the day they tested the microphone and the large screen. The picture of the old music class looked clear, but the faces were covered because the people had not all agreed to be shown. Calvin called that silly. Julian said it was a simple rule, and he meant to keep it. Tessa put the approved names on one page and the private names on another. When she looked up, Julian was holding the door for an older visitor. There was no news camera to record him. She smiled to herself and went back to work.

一天最後，他們測試麥克風與大螢幕。舊音樂班照片很清楚，但未取得所有人同意，因此人臉被遮住。凱文說這很傻。朱利安表示那是簡單規則，他會遵守。泰莎把已同意公開的人名寫在一頁，私人姓名放在另一頁。她抬起頭，看見朱利安正替一位年長訪客扶門，旁邊沒有新聞攝影機。她自己微笑了一下，繼續工作。
