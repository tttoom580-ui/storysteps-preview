鐘上方的紅星

The Red Star Above the Clock

總裁，假婚約星期五到期 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Tessa Lin, a twenty-eight-year-old clock repairer, stood in the middle of a hotel hall in North Gate City. A red star hung above a very old clock. A small bug walked across its glass. Tessa did not use her camera yet; first she wanted to learn one simple fact. Why had the clock stopped at nine? A friendly woman from the hotel offered orange juice and asked if Tessa could finish before the evening picnic for rich guests. Tessa said the future of an old clock did not matter as much as the safety of the people under it. Then she opened her tool box.

二十八歲的鐘錶修復師林泰莎站在北門市一間飯店大廳中央。一顆紅星掛在古老大鐘上方，小蟲爬過鐘的玻璃。泰莎沒有立刻使用相機；她想先弄清一個簡單的事實：鐘為何停在九點？飯店一位友善的女士端來柳橙汁，問她能不能在富有賓客的晚間野餐會前完工。泰莎說，舊鐘的未來固然重要，但站在鐘下的人更需要安全。接著她打開工具箱。

Julian Mo, the thirty-four-year-old CEO of Lumen Hotels, came through the other door without a group behind him. He wore a dark suit and had the kind of quiet face that made workers move out of his way. He noticed that Tessa had put a paper sign around the clock: DO NOT STAND HERE. “Will you reach the problem today?” he asked. “This is not a race,” Tessa replied. “Would you tell a lie to make an old pet feel better?” Julian looked at the sign, then at the people walking by. “No,” he said. “Neither will I,” said Tessa.

三十四歲的路明飯店集團總裁莫朱利安獨自從另一扇門進來。他穿著深色西裝，安靜的表情卻讓員工不自覺替他讓路。他注意到泰莎在鐘旁圍起紙牌：「請勿站在此處。」「今天能找出問題嗎？」他問。「這不是比賽。」泰莎回答，「你會為了讓一隻老寵物開心而說謊嗎？」朱利安看看告示，又看看走動的客人。「不會。」他說。「我也不會。」泰莎回答。

At the event, a guest stepped backward to photograph the star. His foot came close to Tessa's open tool case. Julian reached out to stop the guest, and Tessa caught the case before it fell. A camera flashed just as Julian's hand touched hers. The other guests laughed and called them a beautiful pair. Tessa pulled her hand away. “Please do not make a story from one picture,” she said. Julian gave the camera owner a direct look. “She is here for work. Ask before you take another photo.” The guests were surprised that their cold boss had spoken so plainly.

活動開始後，一位客人為了拍紅星倒退走，腳差點碰上泰莎打開的工具箱。朱利安伸手攔住客人，泰莎則在箱子倒下前接住它。就在朱利安的手碰到她的手時，相機閃了一下。其他賓客笑著說兩人真是天生一對。泰莎抽回手。「請不要只看一張照片就編故事。」她說。朱利安直視拍照者：「她是來工作的。下次拍照前請先問她。」賓客很意外冷面總裁會說得如此直接。

Before Tessa could leave, a woman in a white coat showed her a note on a phone. “A reporter has put the picture online,” the woman whispered. “It says our CEO has a secret love.” Julian did not ask Tessa to smile for more pictures. He asked what she wanted. “Correct the story,” she answered. “And do not let a romance report hide this unsafe clock.” He told his staff to write those two points down. For the first time that day, Tessa thought he might listen to someone who was not rich. She still did not trust him, but she accepted his business card.

泰莎還沒離開，穿白外套的女士便把手機上一則消息拿給她看。「有記者把照片放上網了，」女士低聲說，「寫總裁有祕密戀人。」朱利安沒有要求泰莎再對鏡頭微笑，而是問她想怎麼處理。「澄清新聞，還有，別讓戀愛消息掩蓋這座鐘不安全的事。」他請員工把兩點記下。泰莎第一次覺得，這個男人也許願意聽不是富豪的人說話。她還不信任他，但收下了名片。

The following morning Tessa would have to tell the hotel which part of the clock could be moved and which part had to stay. She made a small drawing on paper before going home. A guard stood near the entrance and asked if she had found the missing wheel. “Not yet,” she answered. “Please notice the line on the floor and keep people outside it.” The guard thanked her and put another sign beside the door. Julian watched the simple exchange from a distance. He had wanted to hire the best repairer so that a problem would disappear without his help. Now he saw that good work also meant explaining danger to the people who walked past it every day. He wrote himself a reminder to ask the guards what they needed.

隔天早上，泰莎得告訴飯店哪些鐘零件可以移動、哪些必須保持原位。回家前她先在紙上畫了小圖。入口的警衛問是否找到缺少的齒輪。「還沒，」她回答，「請注意地板上的線，讓大家待在線外。」警衛道謝，在門旁再放一張告示。朱利安遠遠看著這段簡單對話。他原本只想聘最好的修復師，好讓問題在自己不用幫忙時就消失。如今才知道好工作也包含向每天經過的人解釋危險。他替自己記下提醒：要問警衛需要什麼協助。

Late that night Tessa studied the clock's old wheel. Someone had taken away a small part, and it was not broken by age. A date was written behind the red star, but the letters were hard to see. She took a clear picture of the date, put the part safely in a box, and wrote what she knew. She did not guess who had moved the wheel. In another room Julian read the same false love story for the third time. He could make a phone call and silence one reporter, but he could not change what people thought. He needed facts, and perhaps he needed Tessa's help.

深夜，泰莎研究大鐘的舊齒輪。有人拿走一個小零件，這不是自然老化造成的。紅星後面寫著日期，但字母很難看清。她拍下清楚的照片，把零件安全放進盒子，並記下已知事實。她沒有猜是誰動了齒輪。另一個房間裡，朱利安第三次看著不實的戀愛報導。他打通電話或許能讓一個記者閉嘴，卻無法改變每個人的想法。他需要事實，也可能需要泰莎幫忙。
