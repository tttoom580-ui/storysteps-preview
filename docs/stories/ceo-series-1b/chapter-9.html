銀戒指

The Silver Ring

總裁，假婚約星期五到期 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A famous singer named Lila came to the hotel to talk about the clock. She was brave enough to tell a room full of rich people that her father had been a farmer, not a wealthy man. As a lonely teenager she had come to the public music class for free. She kept a silver ring from her mother in a small box, and she always wore it when she sang. “I have nothing to sell you today,” she told Julian. “I came because this room helped one ordinary person become a singer.” Tessa asked whose photograph hung behind the singer. “My first teacher,” Lila answered.

知名歌手莉拉來到飯店談古鐘。她勇敢地告訴滿屋富人，自己的父親曾是農夫，不是有錢人。她青少年時很孤單，曾免費參加大廳音樂課。母親留給她一枚銀戒指，她總在唱歌時戴著。「我今天沒有東西要賣給你，」她告訴朱利安，「我來，是因為這個房間曾幫一個普通人成為歌手。」泰莎問歌手身後的照片是誰。「我的第一位老師。」莉拉回答。

Lila showed them a photograph of a warm room with a small toilet sign on the wall. There was no new paint, but there were books, milk, bread, and smiling children. A foreigner who had just moved to the city stood near the window. Lila explained that the teacher had helped everyone feel welcome, even children who could not speak the language well. “If you lose this room, please do not lose the idea behind it,” she said. Julian felt sad. His hotels had beautiful rooms, but he could not remember when he had last seen one used so freely.

莉拉拿出一張溫暖房間的照片，牆上有小小的廁所指示牌。沒有新油漆，卻有書、牛奶、麵包和笑著的孩子。一位剛搬到城裡的外國人站在窗旁。莉拉說，老師讓每個人都感到受歡迎，包括不太會說當地語言的孩子。「如果失去這房間，至少別失去背後的心意。」她說。朱利安感到難過：自己的飯店有美麗房間，卻想不起何時見過有人如此自在使用。

Calvin entered and offered Lila money to sing at a private opening night. She refused. “A person does not have to take every job,” she said. Calvin turned to Julian. “Your girlfriend has made the singer angry.” Tessa spoke before Julian could answer. “Please stop calling me his girlfriend. We have said the same fact several times.” Lila stood beside her. “Then let us keep the report on the room, not on a woman.” A hotel worker rang a small bell for the next meeting. Calvin left without smiling. Tessa did not feel brave, but she was glad she had spoken.

凱文進來，提出付錢請莉拉在私人開幕夜唱歌。她拒絕了。「人不必接下每一份工作。」她說。凱文轉向朱利安：「你女朋友把歌手惹生氣了。」泰莎搶先回答：「請不要再叫我他的女朋友，我們已多次說明同一事實。」莉拉站到她身旁：「那就讓報告討論房間，不是討論某個女人。」飯店員工敲小鈴提醒開會。凱文沒笑就走了。泰莎不覺得自己勇敢，但很高興開了口。

Later Lila offered to sing at a public event without asking a high price. Julian thanked her but said the building needed a safety check first. “You are doing well,” Lila told him. “My mother taught me that music is not worth a broken wall.” Tessa laughed. She liked that Julian had remembered the safety rule when a famous person offered help. The singer gave them a copy of the old class photograph, with permission to use only the room image. The names of the children were left out. It was another small piece of the past, and this time they knew where it came from.

稍後莉拉表示願意在公共活動獻唱，不收高額費用。朱利安道謝，但說建築必須先通過安全檢查。「做得好，」莉拉說，「媽媽教我，音樂不值得拿一堵破牆去冒險。」泰莎笑了，她喜歡朱利安即使有名人幫忙，也記得安全規則。莉拉交給他們一張班級舊照副本，只同意用房間的部分，不公開孩子姓名。這是又一小塊過去的拼圖，而他們這次知道來源。

Lila did not bring expensive meat or flowers to the hotel. She brought a small lunch box and said she could bear the cost of her own trip. “My first teacher taught me not to forget the people who opened a door,” she explained. The singer helped Tessa put the old photograph in a safe cover. Julian carried a chair for her, but she waved him away when she could manage it herself. That made him smile. After Lila left, Tessa asked Julian what he had learned from the visit. “That a public place can change one life,” he said. “And that a famous person does not need me to decide for her.” Tessa nodded. Both answers sounded much more honest than the news story that had brought them together.

莉拉沒有帶昂貴肉食或鮮花到飯店，而是帶著小飯盒，說自己負擔得起旅費。「第一位老師教我不要忘記曾替我開門的人。」她解釋。歌手幫泰莎把舊照片放進保護套。朱利安想替她搬椅子，她自己能處理便揮手拒絕，這讓他笑了。莉拉離開後，泰莎問朱利安學到什麼。「公共空間能改變一個人的一生，」他說，「還有，名人也不需要我替她決定。」泰莎點頭。兩個答案都比讓他們相遇的新聞故事誠實許多。

When the visit ended, Julian walked Tessa to the taxi stand. “I was afraid you would think all rich families are like Calvin,” he said. “I think people make choices,” she replied. “You can choose what kind of person you want to be.” He wanted to hold her hand, but he did not move. Tessa saw him pause and smiled softly. For a short moment the noise of the street seemed far away. Then her taxi arrived, and she went home to finish the repair notes. The silver ring, the song, and the old wall stayed in her mind long after the lights went out.

拜訪結束後，朱利安陪泰莎走到計程車站。「我怕妳以為所有富裕家庭都像凱文。」他說。「我認為人會做選擇，」她回答，「你可以決定想成為怎樣的人。」他想握她的手，卻沒動。泰莎看見他的遲疑，柔和地笑了。片刻間街道的聲音彷彿變得遙遠。計程車到了，她回家繼續修理紀錄。銀戒指、歌聲與老牆的影子，在熄燈後仍留在她心裡。
