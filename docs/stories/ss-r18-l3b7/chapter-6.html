The trade guild refuses Celia membership because she lacks a royal sponsor. A narrow strip of daylight crossed the table in Rosebridge. Beside it lay the merchant ledger, covered with marks that only made sense to someone who had been there. Rowan asked Celia to start from the beginning, not from the conclusion everybody wanted. The first account was untidy and sometimes embarrassing. That was a reason to ask careful questions, not a reason to throw it away. Outside, footsteps hurried past the door. The instruction used the word anyhow, and Celia asked the team to agree on its meaning. If they chose to assume, someone would have to explain the result to the people outside. A witness mentioned bacteria in a hurried account; Celia wrote it down without guessing its importance.

貿易公會因賽莉亞沒有王室贊助人，拒絕讓她入會。 一道細窄日光照在玫瑰橋城的桌面上，旁邊攤著商會帳簿，上面滿是只有親臨現場的人才看得懂的記號。洛恩請賽莉亞從事情的開頭講起，而不是從大家想聽的結論開始。最初的說法雜亂，甚至令人難堪，但那是需要仔細提問的理由，不是丟棄它的理由。門外腳步聲匆匆經過。 指示用了「總之」這個詞，賽莉亞請團隊先釐清它的意思。如果他們選擇「假設」，就必須有人向外頭的人解釋結果。一名證人在匆忙的敘述中提到「細菌」；賽莉亞先記下來，沒有猜測它有多重要。

A messenger came from Prince Adrian with an offer that sounded generous until Celia read the last line. The offer protected the person making it, not the people who would carry out the work. Rowan reached for the merchant ledger and wrote down the missing condition. They were not refusing cooperation. They were refusing to confuse cooperation with obedience. That distinction changed the way the next conversation began. Celia found bump on the list of details that the first report had left unexplained. A witness mentioned creator in a hurried account; Celia wrote it down without guessing its importance. Someone asked whether excellence was connected to the trouble, and the room fell quiet for a moment.

艾德里安王子派來使者提出一項看似大方的條件，直到賽莉亞讀到最後一行才發現問題。條件保護的是提出者，不是實際工作的人。洛恩拿起商會帳簿記下缺少的前提。他們並非拒絕合作，而是拒絕把合作和服從混為一談。這個區別改變了下一場談話的方式。 賽莉亞在第一份報告沒有說明的細節清單上，找到「碰撞」。一名證人在匆忙的敘述中提到「創建者」；賽莉亞先記下來，沒有猜測它有多重要。有人問「卓越」是否與麻煩有關，房間一時間安靜下來。

In a quieter corner, Celia and Rowan tried to understand each other without an audience. One favored action because people needed help now; the other feared that a rushed action could shut the safest route. Both concerns were real. Celia admitted that the merchant ledger did not answer every question. Rowan admitted that waiting forever was not a plan either. They decided to separate the urgent work from the larger decision. Celia set a charm on the counter and asked Rowan to test the clasp before she named a price. She remembered how easily a beautiful object had deceived her in her first life. At first the change seemed inner; a second observation made the team less certain. Someone asked whether missile was connected to the trouble, and the room fell quiet for a moment. Rowan wanted to multiply, but agreed to wait for one more safe test.

到了安靜角落，賽莉亞與洛恩試著在沒有觀眾的情況下理解彼此。一人主張立刻行動，因為有人正在求助；另一人擔心匆忙行動會堵住最安全的路。兩種擔憂都真實。賽莉亞承認商會帳簿無法回答一切，洛恩也承認無限等待不是計畫。兩人決定把緊急工作和較大的決策分開。賽莉亞把護符放上櫃臺，請洛恩在標價前先測試扣環。她記得前世自己多麼容易被漂亮物品欺騙。 起初這項變化看來「內部的」，第二次觀察卻讓團隊不再那麼確定。有人問「飛彈」是否與麻煩有關，房間一時間安靜下來。洛恩想要「使倍增」，但同意先等一次安全測試的結果。

The test seemed almost foolish at first. They used plain tools, asked a witness to describe what could be seen, and repeated the same action twice. Celia kept the merchant ledger open so that Rowan could challenge each step. On the second attempt, a detail changed. That difference did not solve the whole mystery, but it ruled out the easiest explanation. For the first time that day, they had something firmer than a rumor. Celia checked the price and the maker’s name before signing a single order. Someone asked whether necessity was connected to the trouble, and the room fell quiet for a moment. Rowan circled the entry for politician and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. The margin of the merchant ledger contained a reference to resource, which Rowan marked for a later check.

起初，測試看起來幾乎有點傻。他們只使用簡單工具，請證人描述眼前的情形，並把相同動作重做兩次。賽莉亞攤開商會帳簿，讓洛恩可以質疑每一步。第二次嘗試時，一個細節改變了。那不能解開整個謎團，卻排除了最簡單的解釋。他們終於握有比流言更可靠的東西。賽莉亞簽任何訂單前，都先核對價格與製作者姓名。 有人問「必要」是否與麻煩有關，房間一時間安靜下來。洛恩圈起「政治人物」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。商會帳簿的頁邊提到了「資源」，洛恩做上記號，準備日後查證。

Rowan read the last line aloud. Celia checked the date and kept a copy for the morning team. Rowan wrote stale beside the early proposal, then added a question mark because opinions differed. The margin of the merchant ledger contained a reference to stomach, which Rowan marked for a later check.

洛恩朗讀最後一行。賽莉亞核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 洛恩在初步提案旁寫下「不新鮮的」，又加了問號，因為意見並不一致。商會帳簿的頁邊提到了「肚子」，洛恩做上記號，準備日後查證。

Someone suggested that Celia should keep the discovery secret until the whole argument was over. Rowan objected: a secret about danger could also harm people who never had a chance to ask questions. They separated private names from public facts and prepared a short notice. The notice was less dramatic than the rumor, but it explained what could be used now and what still needed an independent check. Rowan sanded a rough edge on the new shelf, then stepped aside while Celia arranged the goods. His hands were skilled, but the final decision about her shop was hers. Rowan circled the entry for summary and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. Rowan wanted to suspect, but agreed to wait for one more safe test.

有人建議賽莉亞等爭議結束再公開發現。洛恩反對：危險的祕密也可能傷害那些根本沒有機會提問的人。他們把私人姓名與公共事實分開，準備簡短公告。公告不如流言驚人，卻清楚說明現在能使用什麼、哪些仍需獨立檢查。洛恩磨平新貨架的粗糙邊緣，便退到一旁讓賽莉亞整理商品。他手藝熟練，但商店的最後決策權仍屬於她。 洛恩圈起「摘要」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。洛恩想要「嫌疑人」，但同意先等一次安全測試的結果。

Rowan read the last line aloud. Celia checked the date and kept a copy for the morning team. Rowan circled the entry for technique and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. Rowan wrote teenage beside the early proposal, then added a question mark because opinions differed.

洛恩朗讀最後一行。賽莉亞核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 洛恩圈起「技巧」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。洛恩在初步提案旁寫下「青少年的」，又加了問號，因為意見並不一致。

Celia left the merchant ledger open so Rowan could add the next finding. Neither could see the whole road yet. Rowan wrote tropical beside the early proposal, then added a question mark because opinions differed. The margin of the merchant ledger contained a reference to wax, which Rowan marked for a later check.

賽莉亞讓商會帳簿保持打開，好讓洛恩補上新的發現。兩人都還看不見整段路。 洛恩在初步提案旁寫下「熱帶的」，又加了問號，因為意見並不一致。商會帳簿的頁邊提到了「蠟」，洛恩做上記號，準備日後查證。
