Rowan shows Celia a safe route around the royal toll bridge, opening business for small villages. At Rosebridge, a small crowd had already gathered before anyone knew what to call the trouble. Celia was holding the merchant ledger when Rowan arrived with a question that did not have an easy answer. They could have waited for an official order. Instead, they stood where the problem could be seen and listened to the person who had first noticed it. The air felt unusually still, as though the whole street were waiting to hear who would speak first. The instruction used the word aside, and Celia asked the team to agree on its meaning. Celia found bubble on the list of details that the first report had left unexplained. A witness mentioned canyon in a hurried account; Celia wrote it down without guessing its importance.

洛恩帶賽莉亞走一條避開王室收費橋的安全路線，幫小村開拓生意。 在玫瑰橋城，大家還不知道該如何稱呼這場麻煩，一小群人便已聚集。賽莉亞拿著商會帳簿時，洛恩帶著一個不容易回答的問題趕到。他們原本可以等待官方命令，卻選擇站在看得見問題的地方，先聽最早發現異狀的人說話。空氣異常安靜，彷彿整條街都在等待第一個開口的人。 指示用了「在一旁」這個詞，賽莉亞請團隊先釐清它的意思。賽莉亞在第一份報告沒有說明的細節清單上，找到「氣泡」。一名證人在匆忙的敘述中提到「峽谷」；賽莉亞先記下來，沒有猜測它有多重要。

What worried Rowan was not the loudest objection. It was the quiet question from someone who would have to live with the result. Celia repeated the question so that everyone could hear it, then left a little space for an answer. Prince Adrian had promised speed. Speed could be useful, but only if the group understood what it might lose. The merchant ledger could guide them; it could not make that choice for them. Nobody wanted to describe the situation as capable without explaining the reason in plain words. A witness mentioned captain in a hurried account; Celia wrote it down without guessing its importance. Someone asked whether client was connected to the trouble, and the room fell quiet for a moment.

讓洛恩擔心的不是最大聲的反對，而是某個必須承受結果的人提出的小問題。賽莉亞把問題重複一次，讓眾人聽清，也留出回答的時間。艾德里安王子承諾快速解決。速度確實有用，但前提是大家知道可能失去什麼。商會帳簿可以指引方向，無法替他們作決定。 沒有人願意在沒有清楚解釋理由時，就把情況稱為「有能力的」。一名證人在匆忙的敘述中提到「隊長」；賽莉亞先記下來，沒有猜測它有多重要。有人問「客戶」是否與麻煩有關，房間一時間安靜下來。

For a moment Rowan seemed ready to walk away. Celia closed the merchant ledger and asked what had gone wrong. The answer was ordinary: too many people had been talking about a decision without speaking to those who would bear its cost. Nothing dramatic happened as they listened. Yet by the time the conversation ended, they had changed their next step. It was a change neither could have achieved by giving another order. The amber coin rang against the merchant ledger as a customer paid for repairs. Celia counted the cost of labor before calculating any profit. The plan mentioned a need to edit; neither of them accepted that instruction without a reason. At first the change seemed elderly; a second observation made the team less certain. Rowan circled the entry for electricity and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident.

有一瞬間，洛恩幾乎要轉身離開。賽莉亞闔上商會帳簿，問到底哪裡出了問題。答案很普通：太多人在討論決策，卻沒有詢問將承受代價的人。聆聽時沒有戲劇性事件發生，但談話結束後，下一步已經改變。這不是多下一道命令就能達成的結果。顧客支付維修費時，琥珀硬幣在商會帳簿邊叮噹作響。賽莉亞先算工資成本，再計算利潤。 計畫提到需要「編輯、從紀錄中刪除」；兩人都不願毫無理由便接受這項指示。起初這項變化看來「年長的」，第二次觀察卻讓團隊不再那麼確定。洛恩圈起「電力」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。

Outside, a few spectators demanded something impressive. Celia ignored the request for a grand display and asked Rowan to check the simple measuring tools. They had seen enough in Rosebridge to know that bright light and confident speeches could hide a broken detail. When the figures finally agreed, the result was modest. It was also something another team could verify without believing either of them on faith. Celia checked the price and the maker’s name before signing a single order. Someone asked whether flash was connected to the trouble, and the room fell quiet for a moment. Rowan circled the entry for hatch and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. Rowan wrote hopeful beside the early proposal, then added a question mark because opinions differed.

外頭的旁觀者要求看些驚人的事。賽莉亞沒有表演，而是請洛恩檢查簡單的測量工具。他們在玫瑰橋城已見過太多耀眼光芒與自信演說掩蓋故障細節的例子。數據終於一致時，成果其實不大，卻能讓另一支隊伍不必盲信任何人就自行驗證。賽莉亞簽任何訂單前，都先核對價格與製作者姓名。 有人問「閃光」是否與麻煩有關，房間一時間安靜下來。洛恩圈起「艙口」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。洛恩在初步提案旁寫下「有希望的」，又加了問號，因為意見並不一致。

Rowan read the last line aloud. Celia checked the date and kept a copy for the morning team. Rowan circled the entry for information and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. Rowan wrote monthly beside the early proposal, then added a question mark because opinions differed.

洛恩朗讀最後一行。賽莉亞核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 洛恩圈起「資訊」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。洛恩在初步提案旁寫下「每月的」，又加了問號，因為意見並不一致。

The pressure did not disappear with the evidence. Prince Adrian still controlled a room, a schedule, or an important signature. Celia could see why some neighbors wanted to surrender the argument and go back to normal life. Rowan suggested a narrower demand: protect the people at immediate risk, then review the larger dispute in the open. It was not a perfect victory, but it kept a bad decision from becoming permanent. Iris came by the shop without a royal carriage. Celia made room for her sister behind the counter and asked what she wanted to do with her own afternoon. Rowan circled the entry for property and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. Rowan wanted to represent, but agreed to wait for one more safe test.

證據沒有讓壓力消失。艾德里安王子依然控制著房間、時程或重要簽名。賽莉亞理解有些鄰居想放棄爭論、回歸正常生活。洛恩提出較有限的要求：先保護眼前有危險的人，再公開檢視更大的爭議。這不是完美勝利，卻能避免錯誤決策永久化。艾莉絲沒有搭王室馬車就來到店裡。賽莉亞讓妹妹站到櫃臺後方，問她想如何度過自己的下午。 洛恩圈起「財產／產權標記」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。洛恩想要「代表」，但同意先等一次安全測試的結果。

Rowan read the last line aloud. Celia checked the date and kept a copy for the morning team. Rowan circled the entry for situation and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. The margin of the merchant ledger contained a reference to spice, which Rowan marked for a later check.

洛恩朗讀最後一行。賽莉亞核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 洛恩圈起「情況」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。商會帳簿的頁邊提到了「香料」，洛恩做上記號，準備日後查證。

Celia left the merchant ledger open so Rowan could add the next finding. Neither could see the whole road yet. Rowan circled the entry for sword and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. Rowan wanted to weep, but agreed to wait for one more safe test.

賽莉亞讓商會帳簿保持打開，好讓洛恩補上新的發現。兩人都還看不見整段路。 洛恩圈起「長劍」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。洛恩想要「哭泣」，但同意先等一次安全測試的結果。
