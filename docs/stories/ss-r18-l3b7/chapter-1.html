The Day I Returned

重生後，我把王子讓給妹妹 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Celia wakes on her engagement day with memories of a ruined future and decides to give up Prince Adrian. By sunrise, the rumor had traveled across Rosebridge faster than any honest explanation. Celia found Rowan beside the merchant ledger, making a list of the things they had actually seen. There were fewer items on that list than on the rumor sheet. The difference mattered. A frightened person could mistake a guess for a promise, and a promise could send somebody toward a danger they had never agreed to face. A cup of berry tea warmed Celia while the rain tapped against the window. A faded circus poster hung beside the door, a reminder of the hall’s earlier life. The deed in the locked drawer named the true owner of the building.

賽莉亞在訂婚當天醒來，記得毀滅的未來，決定放棄艾德里安王子。 日出時，流言已在玫瑰橋城傳得比任何誠實解釋都快。賽莉亞看見洛恩守在商會帳簿旁，列出兩人真正親眼見到的事。那張清單比流言單上的項目少得多，而差別至關重要。害怕的人可能把猜測誤認成承諾，承諾又可能把某人帶進他從未同意面對的危險。 雨點敲窗時，一杯莓果茶讓賽莉亞暖和起來。門邊掛著褪色的馬戲團海報，提醒眾人這座廳堂過往的用途。鎖著的抽屜裡有一份產權契據，寫著建築物的真正所有人。

The problem grew more complicated when Prince Adrian asked for a quick decision. Celia understood the attraction of a simple answer, but the royal trade control had already made simple answers expensive for other people. Rowan traced a line in the merchant ledger and showed exactly where the available information ended. Neither of them wanted to make a public promise across that blank space. They agreed to measure one small fact before taking the next step. A tiny fairy charm hung from the lamp, casting moving shapes on the wall. The fancy invitation promised a perfect evening but said nothing about the people doing the work. The old heater clicked twice before it began warming the room.

艾德里安王子要求迅速決定後，問題變得更複雜。賽莉亞理解簡單答案的吸引力，但王室貿易控制早已讓別人替這些簡單答案付出高昂代價。洛恩沿著商會帳簿畫出一道線，指出現有資訊到哪裡為止。兩人都不願跨過空白處作公開承諾，因此同意先測量一件小事再前進。 一枚小小的仙子護符掛在燈上，在牆面投下移動的影子。華麗邀請函承諾完美夜晚，卻沒提及真正做事的人。老暖爐喀喀響了兩聲，才開始讓房間變暖。

The first disagreement was small, but it revealed the difference between their habits. Celia tended to notice patterns; Rowan noticed who was missing from the room. Together they looked at the merchant ledger and named one question each. There was no need for either person to win the argument. A good plan would leave room for both questions and for the people whose answers might be different from theirs. Rowan sanded a rough edge on the new shelf, then stepped aside while Celia arranged the goods. His hands were skilled, but the final decision about her shop was hers. A small hut behind the garden offered shelter from the sudden rain. Soft jazz played from a radio in the corridor, nearly covering the distant bell. A model jet on the shelf belonged to a visitor who had once dreamed of flying.

第一次分歧不大，卻顯露兩人的習慣差異。賽莉亞擅長注意規律，洛恩則察覺誰沒有出現在房裡。他們一起查看商會帳簿，各自提出一個問題。爭論不必分出輸贏；好計畫應容納兩個問題，也容納答案可能不同的其他人。洛恩磨平新貨架的粗糙邊緣，便退到一旁讓賽莉亞整理商品。他手藝熟練，但商店的最後決策權仍屬於她。 花園後方的小屋讓大家得以躲避突來的大雨。走廊的收音機播放輕柔爵士樂，幾乎蓋過遠方的鐘聲。架上的噴射機模型屬於一位曾夢想飛行的訪客。

No one cheered when their first attempt failed. Celia marked the failure in the merchant ledger, and Rowan asked whether the result could be repeated safely. They chose a smaller version of the task, with a clear signal to stop if anything went wrong. The second attempt produced a different sound and a different answer. They still needed another witness, but now they knew what question that witness should be asked. Rowan offered practical help rather than a promise to rescue her business. A street magician made a coin disappear, but Celia was more interested in the missing receipt. Rowan called the rescue a miracle; Celia thanked the people who had made it possible. The café sold a pint of warm soup to anyone caught in the storm.

第一次嘗試失敗時，沒有人歡呼。賽莉亞把失敗記在商會帳簿裡，洛恩詢問是否能安全重做。他們選擇規模更小的測試，並約定出錯時立即停止的訊號。第二次嘗試出現不同的聲音與答案。雖然還需要另一名證人，但他們終於知道該問對方什麼。洛恩提供實際協助，而非承諾拯救她的事業。 街頭魔術師讓硬幣消失，賽莉亞卻更關心不見的收據。洛恩稱救援是奇蹟；賽莉亞則感謝讓它成真的人。咖啡館向被暴風雨困住的人供應一品脫熱湯。

A practical question finally interrupted the argument: who would do the work after the visitors went home? Celia had no magical answer. Rowan read the names in the merchant ledger, then asked the people involved whether they actually wanted those duties. Several agreed. One refused, and nobody punished that choice. The group adjusted the schedule rather than pretending consent had already been given. That small correction prevented a larger mistake. The argument became political when the council tried to control who could speak. Celia wanted to quit the unfair game, not abandon the people affected by it.

一個實際問題終於打斷爭執：訪客回家後，誰負責工作？賽莉亞沒有魔法般的答案。洛恩讀出商會帳簿上的名字，再問相關人員是否真的願意承擔職責。幾人同意，一人拒絕，沒有人因此受罰。小組調整時程，而非假裝已取得同意。這項小小修正避免了更大的錯誤。 議會試圖控制發言權時，爭論變成政治問題。賽莉亞想退出不公平的遊戲，卻不願拋下受影響的人。

A new message arrived before the meeting could end. It mentioned the royal trade control, then claimed that only Prince Adrian had the authority to decide what happened next. Celia read it twice. The words sounded certain, but there was no date beside the claim and no name of the person who had checked it. Rowan suggested saving a copy and asking for the original. The question was not whether to trust a powerful person. It was what anyone could verify. The amber coin rang against the merchant ledger as a customer paid for repairs. Celia counted the cost of labor before calculating any profit. A witness warned that someone planned to rob the archive, so Rowan asked for better locks. A loose screw rolled out from beneath the broken machine and stopped beside Celia.

會議尚未結束，一則新訊息就送到了。訊息提及王室貿易控制，並宣稱只有艾德里安王子有權決定下一步。賽莉亞讀了兩遍。文字很肯定，卻沒有日期，也沒有查核者姓名。洛恩建議保留副本並索取原件。問題不是該不該相信有權勢的人，而是任何人能核實什麼。顧客支付維修費時，琥珀硬幣在商會帳簿邊叮噹作響。賽莉亞先算工資成本，再計算利潤。 證人警告有人打算搶劫檔案室，洛恩因此要求加強門鎖。一顆鬆脫的螺絲從壞機器下滾出，停在賽莉亞腳邊。

Rowan read the last line aloud. Celia checked the date and kept a copy for the morning team. The two friends agreed that one clear signal would mean the test had to stop. A small wheel began to spin when Rowan turned the handle.

洛恩朗讀最後一行。賽莉亞核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 兩名朋友約定，一個明確訊號代表測試必須停止。洛恩轉動把手後，小輪子開始旋轉。

Celia left the merchant ledger open so Rowan could add the next finding. Neither could see the whole road yet. Celia knew that care meant more than trying to tame someone else’s fear. Various visitors offered different accounts, and none was erased just for being unusual.

賽莉亞讓商會帳簿保持打開，好讓洛恩補上新的發現。兩人都還看不見整段路。 賽莉亞明白關懷不是試圖馴服別人的恐懼。各式各樣的訪客提出不同說法，沒有人因說法不尋常就被抹除。
