第一批小觀眾

The First Small Audience

把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 15 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The test performance was held in the school gym with a small audience. The crew arrived early in a mobile equipment van and checked the stage edges. Cora wore a simple jewel sewn into her costume, while another dancer carried a light carriage frame as a prop. A teacher reminded everyone to pause if the floor became wet. Rain had left moisture near the back entrance, but the approved route remained dry. Lian reviewed the final checklist and reached a temporary conclusion: the gym could support a limited show, while the old hall still required repair. She shared the conclusion with the audience before the demonstration began, so nobody confused the test with the final festival. A global arts newsletter had asked to see the result, but Lian wanted local visitors to test it first.

試演在學校體育館舉行，觀眾人數不多。工作團隊一早開著裝有行動設備的廂型車抵達，檢查舞台邊緣。可拉的服裝縫著一顆簡單寶石，另一位舞者搬著輕巧馬車框架當道具。老師提醒大家，地板變濕就要暫停。雨水在後門留下潮氣，但核准動線仍乾燥。黎安重新看過清單，得到暫時結論：體育館可以容納有限演出，舊舞廳仍需維修。示範開始前，她先把結論告訴觀眾，避免有人把試演當成正式節慶。 一本全球藝術電子報想看成果，但黎安希望先讓在地訪客測試。

The opening dance used a slow musical scale, not a loud recording. Ron had mostly chosen simple lights because they were easier to control. A child in the audience ate a pancake while his grandmother explained why the first movements resembled a river. Lian watched from the side and took a deep breath as the dancers found their rhythm. When Cora entered, she was surrounded by performers moving in different directions, not displayed alone at the center. Their arms reached toward one another and then drew back. A gentle tug on a ribbon marked the change. The audience grew quiet, following the shapes without needing an explanation.

開場舞使用緩慢的音階，而不是吵雜錄音。羅恩大多選簡單燈光，因為比較容易控制。觀眾席上有個孩子吃著鬆餅，奶奶向他解釋為何第一段動作像河流。黎安站在側邊，看著舞者找到節奏，深深吸了一口氣。可拉進場時，其他表演者朝不同方向移動，並沒有把她單獨陳列在中心。他們的手臂彼此伸向對方，再慢慢收回。輕輕拉動緞帶表示轉換。觀眾安靜下來，不用解說也能跟上形狀。

During the second section, a microphone made a sharp buzz. Ron recognized the sound as a loose connector and stopped the music. One performer grinned and waited at her place, while the audience remained seated. The backup cable worked, but the interruption took almost five minutes. Lian recorded the incident in the report instead of deleting it from the video. A safe show did not mean nothing ever failed; it meant the crew could notice a problem, stop without confusion, and restart only when conditions allowed. After the music returned, the dancers finished the section with more confidence than before. Their patience had become visible. A volunteer offered a pain pill, but the performer chose to consult the nurse before taking anything.

第二段開始時，麥克風發出尖銳嗡嗡聲。羅恩認出是接頭鬆動，立刻停止音樂。一位舞者笑著留在位置上等待，觀眾也都坐著。備用線可以使用，但中斷將近五分鐘。黎安在報告中保留事件，沒有把它從影片中刪掉。安全演出不代表從不故障，而是工作團隊能發現問題、清楚停止，並在條件允許時才重啟。音樂恢復後，舞者完成第二段，比之前更有信心。耐心成了觀眾看得見的東西。 一名志工遞來止痛藥片，但表演者決定先詢問護理師，再考慮是否服用。

At intermission, volunteers served tasty fruit, tea, and a small bowl of chicken breast salad for anyone who wanted a meal. Lian thanked the kitchen staff and checked that no food blocked the exit. The audience had received cards asking what worked, what did not, and whether they wished to remain for a short discussion. Some people praised the beauty of the movement; others noted that a sign near the restroom was difficult to read. Cora recognized the complaint immediately. The arrow had been placed too high for some visitors. Ron lowered it and photographed the change. The point of the test was not applause. It was information that improved the next event. It was easy to recognize a useful report when people could surround a problem with evidence rather than blame.

中場休息時，志工供應美味水果、茶和雞胸肉沙拉，有需要的人可以吃點東西。黎安向廚房團隊道謝，也檢查食物沒有堵住出口。觀眾拿到回饋卡，詢問哪些可行、哪些不行，以及是否願意留下簡短討論。有些人稱讚舞蹈美麗，另一些人指出廁所旁的指示牌不容易看清。可拉立刻認出問題：箭頭貼得太高，部分訪客看不方便。羅恩把它移低，拍照記錄更動。試演的重點不是掌聲，而是能改善下次活動的資訊。 當人們能用證據圍繞問題，而不是互相指責，就容易辨認什麼才是有用的報告。

Afterward a local school director asked Lian whether she would lead an educational workshop next term. The offer was tempting, but she did not accept before knowing the schedule and pay. Ron helped pack the gear while Cora reviewed the feedback forms with other dancers. A small disagreement arose over the final bow: some performers wanted it short, others wanted a longer ending. Lian asked them to test both instead of voting immediately. They tried two versions on the empty floor and chose the one that allowed everyone to leave without crossing paths. The artistic choice and the practical choice turned out to support each other.

演出後，一位地方學校主任問黎安下學期願不願帶教育工作坊。提議很吸引人，但她沒有在知道時程與酬勞前立刻答應。羅恩幫忙收設備，可拉則和其他舞者一起看回饋表。最後謝幕方式引發小分歧：有人希望短一點，有人想把結尾拉長。黎安沒有立刻投票，而是請大家兩種都試。舞者在空地板上測試後，選了讓所有人不用交叉動線就能離場的版本。藝術選擇與務實選擇竟然彼此支持。

Outside the gym, a light wind shook the trees. Lian sat on the steps with Ron after the last box was loaded. He said he had enjoyed seeing a plan become something people could actually experience. She told him she was proud of the dancers, not because they had looked flawless, but because they had trusted one another through an interruption. He asked if she wanted to take another walk by the river on Sunday. She said yes. There was no sudden declaration, only two adults making room for something they both wanted. Tomorrow the committee would read the test report, and the project's future would be decided by more than one good afternoon.

體育館外，微風吹動樹梢。最後一箱上車後，黎安和羅恩坐在台階上。他說很高興看見一份計畫變成別人能實際體驗的事。黎安告訴他，她為舞者感到驕傲，不是因為大家毫無瑕疵，而是因為中斷時仍能互相信任。他問她星期日願不願再沿河散步。她答應了。沒有突然的告白，只有兩個成年人為彼此都想要的關係留出空間。明天委員會將閱讀試演報告，計畫前途仍得靠超過一個美好下午的證據決定。
