河流改了方向

The River Changes Course

把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 16 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

The trust sent a letter to confirm that the school gym could host the first public festival, provided the group completed its final tests. Lian thought the decision was fairly reasonable, although she was fond of the old hall. A serious flood warning had also arrived after days of rain. The river had left a narrow gap between the footbridge and the rising water, making the low road unsafe. Ron, whose younger sister was a recent engineering graduate, asked her to help read the official river report. She gave him a brief grin when she saw how carefully he had marked the uncertain figures. The report did not promise disaster, but it showed why the group needed a reliable backup transport route. The mystery of the rising river was not supernatural; public readings showed the increasing water level clearly.

藝術信託來信確認，學校體育館可以承辦第一場公開節慶，條件是團隊完成最後測試。黎安覺得決定相當合理，雖然她仍喜愛舊舞廳。連日降雨也帶來嚴重洪水警報。河水升高，步橋與水面之間只剩狹窄空隙，低窪道路變得不安全。羅恩請剛從工程系畢業的妹妹協助閱讀官方水文報告。她看見哥哥仔細標記不確定數字時，露出短短一笑。報告沒有保證會發生災難，但說明為何團隊需要可靠的備用交通路線。 上升河水的神祕之處並非超自然現象，公開讀數已清楚顯示水位增加。

At the emergency meeting, Cora proposed moving all rehearsals to the higher school campus until the warning ended. Mrs. Wu wanted to honor the volunteers who had cleaned the old hall, but agreed that gratitude did not make the road safe. Lian studied the neat evacuation map and observed that one entrance still depended on a lift that might stop during a blackout. The technical officer added a surface-level route through the garden. A child nearby repeated the officer's words like a parrot until his mother asked him to listen instead. It took patience to explain the change without frightening people, and even more patience to answer the same practical question again and again. Lian asked the observers to observe how long each person needed to pass through the garden entrance.

緊急會議上，可拉建議警報解除前，把所有排練移到高處的學校校區。吳女士想表揚清理舊舞廳的志工，但同意感謝不會讓危險道路變安全。黎安研究整齊的疏散圖，注意到其中一個入口仍依賴停電時可能停止的電梯。技術人員於是加上一條穿過花園的地面路線。旁邊一個孩子像鸚鵡般重複技術人員的話，媽媽請他先聽清楚。要解釋更動而不嚇壞大家，需要耐心；要一再回答相同的實務問題，更需要耐心。 黎安請觀察員注意每個人穿過花園入口需要多少時間。

The rehearsal team had spent weeks preparing for an evening performance, yet the storm made their preparation look incomplete. Ron explained that safety planning could not rely on perfect weather. They moved the music stands, chairs, and costumes to the upper floor of the school, keeping paths clear for people who needed more space. Cora reminded everyone that the change was significant for dancers who used accessible transport. Lian called each performer directly instead of assuming a group message would reach them. Several could attend the new location; two needed rides; one chose not to rehearse that night. All three decisions were respected.

團隊為晚間表演準備數週，暴雨卻讓先前準備顯得不完整。羅恩解釋，安全規劃不能依賴永遠的好天氣。他們把譜架、椅子與戲服移到學校上層樓，並保持需要較大空間的人能順利通行。可拉提醒，改動對依賴無障礙交通的舞者有重大影響。黎安逐一打電話，不假設一則群組訊息就能通知到所有人。幾位能到新場地、兩位需要接送、還有一位決定當晚不練。三種選擇都受到尊重。

During a history lesson held in the neighboring classroom, students were studying the word slave and the lives of people who had been denied freedom. Lian stopped to listen when the teacher explained why the term should not be used carelessly as a joke about ordinary work. She thought about the way her group described volunteer labor. Their project depended on freely chosen participation, and no one should be pressured to stay because they feared disappointing a friend. The lesson did not belong to the dance program, but it sharpened her understanding of words and responsibilities. She returned to the gym and checked that the work schedule still included clear rest times.

隔壁教室正在上歷史課，學生研究「奴隸」一詞，以及那些被剝奪自由的人所遭遇的生活。黎安停下來聽老師說明，為什麼不該輕率拿這個詞開玩笑，形容一般工作。她想到團隊如何談志工勞動。計畫依賴自由選擇的參與，沒有人該因為怕朋友失望而被迫留下。這堂課不是舞蹈節目的一部分，卻讓她對文字與責任更敏感。她回到體育館，確認排班表仍有明確休息時間。

The following morning Ron climbed a short trail toward the town's hilltop summit to check the higher road with an authorized safety officer. They did not cross the flooded bridge. From above, the water's swift movement was plain. Ron sent photographs with location and time information, not simply a dramatic image. Back at the school, a nurse advised volunteers to bring water and a simple snack with vitamin-rich fruit for long shifts. Lian appreciated the concern. She had once worked through injury because a director treated a successful performance as more important than the people making it. This time she wanted the order reversed.

隔天早晨，羅恩和獲授權的安全人員沿短步道走向鎮上的山頂，檢查高處道路，沒有穿越淹水橋梁。從上方看，水流迅速的情況十分清楚。羅恩寄回照片，附上地點和時間，而不只是一張戲劇化的影像。回到學校後，護理師提醒長班志工帶水與含維生素的水果點心。黎安感謝提醒。她曾在受傷時繼續工作，因為導演把演出成功看得比做出作品的人還重要。這一次，她想把順序反過來。

In the evening Lian received a message from the arts trust: the school gym reservation would remain in place despite the weather delay. She thanked the secretary, then sent an updated schedule to everyone involved. Ron appeared with two warm drinks and asked whether she had eaten. She laughed, realizing he had remembered the lunch she had missed. Their connection no longer felt like a problem to solve, but it still required care. They agreed not to use their private conversations to settle committee disputes. With the rain beating gently against the windows, they sat together and listened to the quiet between work and tomorrow.

晚上，藝術信託傳來訊息：即使天氣造成延誤，學校體育館的預約仍有效。黎安向秘書道謝，寄出更新時程給所有人。羅恩帶著兩杯熱飲出現，問她吃過飯沒有。她笑了，發現他記得自己漏吃午餐。他們的關係不再像一道待解的難題，卻仍需要照顧。兩人同意不透過私人對話決定委員會爭議。雨輕敲窗戶時，他們一起坐著，聽著工作與明天之間短暫的安靜。
