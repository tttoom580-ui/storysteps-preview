公平的試演

A Fair Audition

把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 13 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the first audition, Lian's assistant greeted each performer and explained the timetable. Some wore attractive costumes, while others came in ordinary clothes. One applicant could barely hear the music from the far side of the room, so Ron adjusted the speaker before continuing. Outside, a worker moved a barrel of rainwater away from the entrance, and the air carried a bitter smell from the old drain. Cora asked that the judges begin only after everyone understood the movements and had a chance to ask questions. An audition should make a comparison of artistic choices, not secretly test whether applicants knew unwritten rules. Lian agreed and read the same instructions aloud to every group.

第一次試演時，黎安的助理迎接每位表演者，解釋時程。有些人穿吸引目光的服裝，也有人穿平常衣服。一名報名者在房間另一端幾乎聽不見音樂，羅恩先調整喇叭才繼續。屋外工人把一個雨水桶移離入口，舊排水道飄出苦澀氣味。可拉要求評審等所有人理解動作、也有機會提問後才開始。試演應比較藝術選擇，而不是暗中測誰知道沒寫出來的規矩。黎安同意了，對每組都朗讀同樣的指示。

The first group had rehearsed together for years and called themselves the Lantern Gang, although their work was cheerful rather than threatening. Their leader tried to grasp every movement at once, then laughed when the timing fell apart. Another performer rolled across the marble-patterned practice floor and stopped to remove a mosquito from her sleeve. A third performed with a small puppet tied to one wrist, making the wooden figure copy her steps. Lian found all three approaches interesting, but she did not announce a winner. Each group had different strengths, and the performance would work better if dancers helped shape roles around those strengths rather than compete for one central spot. Through the window they could see a crane lifting materials at a nearby construction site; its movement had to remain outside the rehearsal zone.

第一組已一起練舞多年，自稱「燈籠幫」，其實他們的作品開朗而不具威脅。領舞者試著一次掌握所有動作，節拍亂掉時自己也笑了。另一位舞者滑過大理石花紋的練習地板，停下來拍走袖上的蚊子。第三人手腕綁著小木偶，讓木偶模仿舞步。黎安覺得三種方式都很有趣，卻沒有立刻選出勝者。每組都有不同長處；與其爭同一個中心位置，不如依那些長處共同塑造角色，作品會更好。 透過窗戶，他們看見附近工地的起重機吊起材料；它的移動區域必須與排練區分開。

During lunch, volunteers served pea soup beside a table displaying costume samples. A tailor offered to adjust clothing for a modest rate and asked that changes be made by agreement with the person wearing each outfit. One applicant wanted a long trumpet solo. Ron said the musician would need an appropriate microphone and rehearsal time, while Lian wondered whether the solo served the story. They rated each proposal against published criteria: clarity, teamwork, safety, and how well it fit the shared theme. The numbers were not perfect, but they made the process more satisfactory than a secret decision by one director. Participants could also ask for an explanation afterward.

午餐時，志工在展示服裝樣品的桌旁供應豌豆湯。一名裁縫願意以合理價格改衣，並要求每次更動都與穿著者商量。一位報名者想有長長的小號獨奏。羅恩說需要適當麥克風與練習時間，黎安則思考獨奏是否符合故事。大家依公布的標準評分：清楚度、合作、安全，以及是否符合共同主題。數字並不完美，但比導演一個人私下決定更令人滿意。參與者之後也能詢問評分理由。

A stubborn dancer refused to change a movement that would have blocked an exit. Lian explained the underlying safety reason and offered time to create another path. He argued at first, then asked to try the new route with Cora. His dance did not lose its meaning; it varied in shape while keeping the emotional rhythm. That change impressed the group more than his first performance. Lian realized that strong art was not always the art that refused to move. Sometimes it became stronger by noticing the people sharing its space. She wrote his revised idea into the draft with his name beside it, as well as Cora's contribution to the path. The distance between partners could vary, as long as everyone understood the next meeting point.

一位固執的舞者拒絕修改會堵住出口的動作。黎安解釋背後的安全原因，並給他時間設計另一條路。他起初爭辯，後來主動請可拉一起試新動線。舞蹈沒有失去原意，只是形狀有所變化，情緒節奏仍在。這次改動比他的第一版更打動大家。黎安發現，強大的藝術不一定是不肯改的藝術；有時，正因注意到共享空間的人，作品才更有力量。她把修改後的想法寫進草稿，註明他的名字，也註明可拉對動線的貢獻。 搭檔之間的距離可以變化，只要每個人都知道下一個集合位置。

At the end of the audition Lian published a short decision note with no private medical information. Everyone could see the criteria and ask for a review of their own result. Cora accepted a leading role in one section but declined another that left her alone on stage as a symbol of courage. “I want to be part of the dance,” she said, “not a lesson about somebody else's kindness.” Lian thanked her for stating the difference so clearly. The team had found a cast, but more importantly, they had learned how to let people refuse a role without being treated as ungrateful.

試演結束時，黎安公布簡短的決策紀錄，不包含私人醫療資訊。每個人都能看見評分標準，也可申請檢視自己的結果。可拉接受其中一段的主角位置，卻拒絕另一段：那段把她獨自留在台上，當成勇氣的象徵。「我想成為舞蹈的一部分，」她說，「不是教大家稱讚別人善良的一堂課。」黎安感謝她清楚指出差別。團隊找到了演員，更重要的是學會讓人拒絕角色，而不把拒絕視為忘恩負義。

That evening Ron asked Lian how she felt about the day's choices. She was tired but pleased that the room had held disagreements without breaking apart. He told her he liked the way she made time for people to think. She laughed and said she had learned that patience from him and Cora. Neither tried to turn the compliment into a promise about romance. They had a date planned for the weekend, and both wanted to discover what they enjoyed together outside work. Lian returned home carrying the audition notes, aware that the next challenge would be harder: the trust had to choose between the hall and the school gym.

晚上，羅恩問黎安對今日的選擇有什麼感覺。她很累，卻高興大家能有分歧而不散掉。他說喜歡她願意給人思考時間。她笑著說，這份耐心也是從他和可拉身上學到的。兩人沒有把稱讚變成浪漫承諾。他們週末約好見面，也都想知道在工作之外彼此喜歡什麼。黎安帶著試演筆記回家，知道下一個挑戰更難：信託得在舊舞廳與學校體育館之間作選擇。
