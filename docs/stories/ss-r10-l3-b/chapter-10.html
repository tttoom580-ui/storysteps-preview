雨中的提案

A Proposal in the Rain

把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

The town's spring wedding market opened beside the hall, where a bride in a pearl necklace posed for photographs. An organizer asked Lian to present a bold dance at the opening and promised fashionable costumes. Lian replied that her group had not yet determined whether the floor was safe. A volunteer dressed as an angel handed out programs, and children carried paper lanterns shaped like tiny flames. The decorations looked lovely, but they reminded Lian of how easily appearance could impress people before the important questions were answered. She agreed to give a short talk about the new project instead of presenting an unfinished performance. Lian needed to determine the actual limits before a bright costume could persuade anyone otherwise.

鎮上的春季婚禮市集在舞廳旁開幕，一位戴珍珠項鍊的新娘正拍攝照片。主辦者請黎安在開幕表演大膽的舞蹈，並承諾提供時髦服裝。黎安回答，團隊還沒確認地板安全。一名打扮成天使的志工發節目單，孩子們提著火焰形狀的紙燈籠。裝飾很美，卻提醒黎安，外表多容易在重要問題解答前就打動人心。她同意簡短介紹新計畫，而不是表演尚未完成的作品。 黎安必須先確定實際限制，不能讓亮眼戲服改變人們的判斷。

The morning's rain turned cold, and Ron offered Lian a clean handkerchief when she sneezed. She stared at the empty stage and wondered whether the public would consider their refusal a failure. Ron reminded her that safety was a moral responsibility, not a prize for perfect planning. In the food tent, a cook heated soup on a small stove while volunteers served melon and fruit punch. Lian drank some punch and felt relief after a tense week. She tried to remain optimistic, but the cost sheet in her bag was still heavy with problems. Ron did not ask her to pretend otherwise. He said they could make the talk clear, honest, and short. A painted flame on the food tent looked harmless, and a silk ribbon at the entrance moved gently in the wind.

早晨的雨帶著寒意，黎安打噴嚏時，羅恩遞給她乾淨手帕。她盯著空舞台，擔心居民會把拒絕表演當成失敗。羅恩提醒，安全是道德責任，不是完美規劃才得到的獎賞。餐飲帳篷裡，廚師在小爐上熱湯，志工供應甜瓜和水果潘趣飲料。黎安喝了一杯，緊繃一週後終於稍感放鬆。她想保持樂觀，但包裡那份成本表仍沉重。羅恩沒有要她假裝不是如此，只說他們可以把介紹講得清楚、誠實、簡短。 餐飲帳篷上畫著無害的火焰圖案，入口的絲緞帶隨風輕擺。

At the public forum Lian brought three drawings: the current structure, the essential repairs, and an alternative program if the building failed inspection. She pointed out that the river's tide affected the low road during storms and that transport planning mattered as much as stage design. A council member asked whether they could open early and fix the remaining details afterward. Cora answered that people who needed a safe route could not wait until after an accident. The room was quiet. Then a young worker asked whether there would be late classes for night-shift staff. Lian showed the draft timetable, with a blank space reserved for exactly that suggestion. Somehow she managed not to stare at the empty chairs, but to look directly at the people asking questions.

公開說明會上，黎安帶來三張圖：建築現況、必要修繕，以及建物不通過檢查時的替代方案。她指出暴風雨時河流水位會影響低窪道路，因此交通規劃和舞台設計同樣重要。一位議員問能否先開放，再慢慢補完細節。可拉回答，需要安全路線的人不能等事故發生後才得到改善。房間安靜下來。接著一位年輕工人問是否有夜班族能參加的晚間課程。黎安展示草擬時程，裡面正好有為這類建議留下的空位。 不知怎麼地，她終於沒有一直盯著空椅子，而能直視發問的人。

The company that wanted to rent the hall sent a representative. She explained that private events could pay for repairs more quickly and asked for a fair comparison. Lian agreed. The town should compare real costs and services rather than decide from fear or nostalgia. Ron distributed the updated inspection list and marked which items had independent evidence. The representative looked surprised that the club had included the commercial offer among its options. “I thought you wanted us to disappear,” she said. Lian shook her head. “I want a choice residents can understand. If your plan is better on some points, we should say so.” The conversation changed from an argument into a discussion about conditions.

想租下舞廳的公司派代表到場。她解釋私人活動能更快支付修繕費，並要求公平比較。黎安同意了。小鎮應該比較真實成本與服務，而不是根據恐懼或懷舊作決定。羅恩分發更新後的檢查清單，標記哪些項目有獨立證據。代表發現社團把商業方案也放進比較，十分驚訝。「我以為妳只想讓我們消失。」她說。黎安搖頭。「我希望居民能理解自己的選擇。若妳的方案有更好的部分，我們也該承認。」對話從爭吵轉為條件討論。

After the meeting, Ron and Lian walked along the river under one umbrella. He told her about a small dance class he had taught after college, when he first discovered how rhythm could help strangers work together. She confessed that she had once believed a good choreographer needed to control every movement. Cora had helped her abandon that idea. Ron said he enjoyed working with her, but asked whether she wanted the two of them to keep their friendship separate from the grant decisions. “Yes,” Lian said, “and I would like that coffee we talked about.” They agreed on Saturday afternoon at a cafe without a committee agenda.

會後，羅恩和黎安共撐一把傘沿河散步。他說起大學畢業後教的第一堂小型舞蹈課，那時才發現節奏能幫助陌生人合作。黎安承認自己曾認為好編舞者必須控制每個動作，而可拉幫她放下這個想法。羅恩說很喜歡和她工作，但也問她是否希望將私人友誼與補助決策分開。「是，」黎安回答，「而且我想去喝我們說好的咖啡。」兩人約好週六下午在沒有委員會議程的咖啡館見面。

That evening Lian wrote a short public note about the forum. It listed agreements, unresolved questions, and the date of the next inspection. She included the commercial company's argument as fairly as her own, because a decision made in public should leave a record others could question. Mrs. Wu called to say several residents had offered help after hearing the presentation. Lian thanked her but asked that every offer be matched to a real task. Before sleep she opened the drawing of the floor once more. The routes were still incomplete. For the first time, she could look at the blank spaces without feeling that they proved she had failed.

當晚，黎安寫了一份公開會議簡報，列出共識、未決問題和下次檢查日期。她盡可能公平記錄商業公司的主張，也記錄自己的，因為公開做出的決定，應留下可供他人提問的資料。吳女士來電說，聽完說明後有幾位居民主動提出幫忙。黎安道謝，也請她把每個善意提議對應到實際任務。睡前，她再看一次地板設計圖。動線仍不完整，但她第一次能看著空白處，而不覺得那證明自己失敗了。
