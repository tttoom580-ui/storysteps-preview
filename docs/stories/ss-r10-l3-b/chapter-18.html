把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 18 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

A cultural ambassador from the neighboring city attended the public hearing. She had come to discuss an exchange of dance workshops, not to decide Cedar Hill's budget. The meeting was held in a bare room with chairs placed in a wide circle. Lian welcomed questions from people who lived in the old workers' colony near the railway. A woman from the drugstore asked why the hall repair remained on the agenda when the school gym was already available. Ron explained that the gym solved the first festival's space problem but did not address the long-term future of the public hall. Both questions mattered, and each required a different decision.

鄰市的文化大使參加公開聽證，想討論舞蹈工作坊交流，並不是來決定杉丘鎮的預算。會議在一間空蕩房間舉行，椅子圍成寬大的圓圈。黎安歡迎住在鐵道旁舊工人聚落的居民提問。一位藥局婦人問，既然學校體育館能用，為何舊舞廳維修仍在議程上。羅恩說，體育館解決第一場節慶的空間問題，卻沒有處理公共舞廳的長遠未來。兩個問題都重要，也需要不同決策。

Cora presented an experiment comparing the old entrance with the garden route. She had folded the charts so each visitor could look at the same information. Her findings were direct: the gym route worked during a controlled test, but more signs were needed during rain. A man in the audience posted a hateful comment online, calling the access plan a waste of effort. The chairperson asked people to respond to arguments rather than insult one another. Lian did not turn the comment into a horror story about the whole town. Most residents had asked practical questions, and those deserved careful answers. After the meeting the moderator recorded the abusive message separately. A simple way to fold the printed chart made it easier for people on the opposite side of the circle to read.

可拉展示一項實驗，比較舊入口與花園路線。她把圖表摺好，讓每位訪客都能看相同資訊。結果直接：體育館路線在受控測試中可用，但下雨時需要更多標示。一位聽眾在網路上留下充滿仇意的留言，說無障礙規劃浪費力氣。主席請大家回應論點，不要互相侮辱。黎安沒有把一則留言變成全鎮恐怖故事。大多數居民提出的是實際問題，值得仔細回答。會後，主持人把攻擊性留言另行紀錄。 把印好的圖表簡單摺起來，能讓圓圈對面的人也更容易閱讀。

At the lunch break, volunteers served mushroom soup with bread. The garden had become sticky after the rain, and people were asked to remain indoors until the path could be checked. A survivor of the old river flood offered to tell the audience how difficult it had been to find transport during the emergency. Lian thanked her but asked whether she wished to speak publicly or provide a written note. The woman chose a private interview with the transport officer. Her memory would inform the plan without becoming a performance against her wishes. Cora called this an important part of safety: people should control how their own experiences were used. A volunteer gave the wet dog outside a gentle pat, then washed her hands before returning to the food table.

午休時，志工供應蘑菇湯與麵包。花園下雨後變得黏滑，路線檢查前，大家被請留在室內。一名舊河洪水的倖存者願意說明當年緊急情況下找交通工具有多困難。黎安感謝她，問她想公開發言還是提供書面資料。婦人選擇私下與交通人員訪談。她的回憶會協助計畫，卻不必在違背意願的情況下變成表演。可拉說，這也是安全的重要部分：人應能掌握自己的經驗如何被使用。 一名志工輕輕拍了拍外面淋濕的狗，洗手後才回到餐飲桌。

The afternoon session covered transport, rehearsal hours, and long-term management. Ron showed that the school would provide the gym for a limited period, not forever. Mrs. Wu proposed a community board to supervise future hall repairs, with seats for instructors, performers, and residents. Some speakers favored more tourism, while others wanted inexpensive daily classes. Lian did not ask the hearing to settle everything at once. She suggested separate votes on the first festival and the building renovation. This would allow people to support one proposal without being forced to approve the other. The chairperson agreed to place both decisions on the official record. The board planned a routine review each month and would shorten any meeting that no longer had useful business.

下午場討論交通、排練時數與長期管理。羅恩指出，學校只提供有限期間的體育館，不是永久使用。吳女士建議成立社區董事會監督未來舞廳修繕，讓教師、表演者和居民都有席位。有些人偏好更多觀光活動，也有人想要便宜的日常課程。黎安沒有要求聽證一次解決所有問題，而是建議首場節慶與建物整修分開表決。如此一來，居民可以支持其中一案，不必被迫接受另一案。主席同意把兩項決定都列入正式紀錄。 董事會規劃每月例行檢討，沒有實際議題的會議則會縮短。

After the hearing the visiting ambassador asked whether Lian would consider a workshop exchange next year. Lian said she was interested, but could not promise on behalf of the new group. The ambassador respected that boundary. Outside, a caretaker pulled weeds from the garden path while school workers checked a small statue near the gate. Lian watched the ordinary maintenance and realized how much of the day's work was invisible when a performance looked smooth. Nothing in the hearing had created a magical solution. Instead, people had named specific tasks and accepted that they would need records, money, and time to complete them. The gardener asked the team to leave every weed pulled from the path in a marked waste bin.

聽證後，來訪的大使問黎安是否願意明年交流工作坊。黎安說自己有興趣，但無法代表新團隊承諾。對方尊重這個界線。屋外，管理人從花園步道拔除雜草，學校工人檢查校門旁的小雕像。黎安看著這些平凡維護，想到表演順利時，有多少日常工作是看不見的。聽證沒有生出神奇解方，而是讓人們列出明確任務，承認需要紀錄、金錢和時間才能完成。 園丁請團隊把步道上拔出的每株雜草放入標示好的廢棄物桶。

Ron walked with Lian to the bus stop and told her he had been offered a short lighting contract in another city. It would begin after the festival and last three months. He did not want to hide the offer or make her decide for him. Lian asked about the work and said she hoped he would consider it seriously. She was also waiting to hear about a choreography course in the capital. For a moment their future felt less simple than the warm walks they had shared. Then Ron said they could talk about it when both knew more. She agreed. Affection did not require one of them to stop moving.

羅恩陪黎安走到公車站，告訴她自己得到一份外地燈光工作邀約。工作在節慶後開始，為期三個月。他不想隱瞞，也不想要她替自己決定。黎安詢問工作內容，說希望他認真考慮。她也在等待首都一門編舞課程的消息。那一刻，未來似乎不像兩人共享的散步那麼簡單。羅恩接著說，等彼此知道更多再談。她同意。喜歡一個人，不代表其中一方得停止前進。
