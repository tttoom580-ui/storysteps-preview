留著的那扇門

A Door Left Open

把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 24 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

On the day Ron left for his contract, an airline delay changed his travel plan, so he took the train instead. Lian met him at the station to check the changed booking, where a cheerful volunteer helped visitors find the correct platform. The morning was chilly, and Ron carried the old helmet he used when cycling to work. A newspaper headline claimed the police would arrest people who entered the closed post office, but the town had issued no such order. The actual notice warned only that the unsafe section remained closed. Lian called the office to correct the misleading story. She had learned to look for the document behind the headline before reacting to its tone. The notice also warned visitors not to enter the vacant rooms behind the sealed staircase.

羅恩出發做外地合約那天，航空公司延誤改變了行程，他最後改搭火車。黎安先到車站陪他確認改票，一位開朗志工正在協助旅客找月台。早晨有寒意，羅恩提著平日騎車上班用的舊安全帽。一則報紙標題聲稱警方會逮捕進入封閉郵局的人，但鎮上根本沒發布這種命令。真正的公告只警告危險區域仍關閉。黎安打電話要求更正誤導的報導。她已學會在被標題的語氣影響前，先找出背後文件。 公告也警告訪客不要進入封閉樓梯後方的空房間。

At the station Ron asked whether she would dare to run the next workshop without him. Lian laughed at the question. Of course she would; the team had learned to share technical responsibilities. He admired her attitude and said his contract would not be a reason to control her plans. Across the square a worker at the river dock was wearing protective clothing because a cleaning product could be harmful without correct handling. A faded navy poster from an old exhibition hung behind him. Lian noticed these ordinary signs of care and work. The town was full of people managing responsibilities that others rarely saw.

在車站，羅恩問她敢不敢在沒有他的情況下帶下一場工作坊。黎安笑了。當然敢，團隊已學會分攤技術責任。他欣賞她的態度，也說自己的合約不會成為控制她計畫的理由。廣場另一側，河港工人穿著防護衣，因為清潔用品若操作不當可能有害。工人身後掛著一張海軍舊展覽的褪色海報。黎安注意到這些日常的照顧與工作。鎮上有許多人管理著不容易被看見的責任。

The arts center wanted to promote the festival's success with a bright poster showing every dancer. Cora asked for a review of the images first. One picture placed a performer in a costume that looked like jungle leaves, but the performer disliked that photograph and chose another. The designer replaced the file without complaint. Lian explained to the board that a dancer was not a loser for refusing publicity. Talent did not require accepting every opportunity. The final poster credited each choreographer and technician who wished to be named. It also listed the public discussion where residents could learn about the next trial classes.

藝術中心想用色彩鮮明的海報宣傳節慶成功，照片裡有每位舞者。可拉要求先讓本人審核。有張照片裡，一名舞者穿著像叢林樹葉的戲服，但本人不喜歡，便另選一張。設計師沒有抱怨，直接更換。黎安向董事會說明，舞者拒絕宣傳並不代表是輸家。有才能不代表必須接受每個機會。最後海報列出願意具名的編舞者與技術人員，也公布居民能了解下期試辦課程的討論會。

At noon Lian helped clean the gym. A volunteer handed her a rag to wipe the mirrors, and she sighed when she noticed a long scratch on one panel. The school manager inspected it and confirmed it had been recorded before the festival. Lian was relieved they had photographs to compare. She did not want the school to suffer damage because of their event, nor did she want the team blamed for marks already present. They checked every returned vehicle and equipment case against the inventory. The process was slow, but it allowed the group to leave the gym in good order and preserve the school's trust. She did not sigh at the long inspection list; by now she knew why each item mattered. She would warn the board promptly if the next report contained any new hazard.

中午，黎安幫忙清理體育館。志工給她一塊抹布擦鏡子，她發現其中一片有長長刮痕，不禁嘆氣。學校管理員檢查後確認，刮痕在節慶前就已記錄。黎安慶幸有照片可比對。她不希望活動傷害學校財物，也不願團隊背負原本就存在的損傷。大家依清單核對每輛歸還的車和器材箱。過程緩慢，卻讓團隊能把體育館整理妥當地交回，維持學校的信任。 她不再對冗長的檢查清單嘆氣；如今她知道每項為何重要。若下次報告出現新危險，她會立刻警告董事會。

In the late afternoon Lian returned to the old hall with Mrs. Wu. The unsafe doorway remained closed, but a new engineering report was ready for public review. The trust would decide on repairs in a separate meeting, not hide them inside the festival budget. Mrs. Wu said she had once believed the building needed a single hero to save it. Now she knew a strong community could be made of ordinary people who checked one another's work. Lian touched the doorframe and promised nothing she could not deliver. She would teach where it was safe, and keep helping the public board ask the right questions.

傍晚，黎安與吳女士回到舊舞廳。危險門口仍封閉，但新的工程報告已準備公開審閱。信託會在另一場會議決定修繕，不會把費用藏進節慶預算。吳女士說，她以前相信建築需要一位英雄來拯救，現在知道穩固社區可以由許多普通人組成，彼此核對工作。黎安輕觸門框，沒有承諾自己無法做到的事。她會在安全的地方教學，也會繼續協助公共董事會提出正確問題。

At the train platform Ron's departure announcement finally sounded. He and Lian hugged, then stepped apart so neither would miss the train or feel trapped by the goodbye. “Tell me about the first class,” he said. “Tell me how the new stage works,” she replied. They smiled. A relationship could be warm without closing every door in advance. When the train left, Lian walked back through the square, passing people carrying groceries and students going home. She felt a brief ache, but also a steady sense of possibility. The town remained hers to work in, and the future remained hers to choose.

火車月台終於響起羅恩的離站廣播。兩人擁抱，隨後退開，既不耽誤列車，也不讓告別變成束縛。「跟我說第一堂課怎樣。」他說。「那你也告訴我新舞台的狀況。」她回答。兩人微笑。關係可以溫暖，卻不必事先關上所有門。列車離開後，黎安穿過廣場走回去，身邊是提著雜貨的人和放學的學生。她短暫心酸，卻也有穩定的可能感。小鎮仍是她能工作的地方，未來也仍由她自己選擇。
