我們共同的地板

The Floor We Share

把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 25 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

Three months later, the town held a market celebrating local agriculture and new community classes. A farmer pushed a cart filled with vegetables and explained how each crop depended on healthy soil. A cleaner swept the school entrance while visitors arrived for the first open dance workshop. Lian stood beside a heap of folded mats, checking the list of registered students. She wore comfortable shoes with a soft heel, not a glamorous costume. The group had earned enough to offer a small number of free places. Nobody pretended the money would last forever, but the new board had published a plan for future fees, repairs, and decisions. This was a start that ordinary residents could understand.

三個月後，鎮上舉辦慶祝地方農業與新社區課程的市集。農夫推著裝滿蔬菜的手推車，說明每種作物都依賴健康土壤。清潔人員掃著學校入口，訪客陸續來參加第一次公開舞蹈工作坊。黎安站在一堆摺好的軟墊旁，核對報名名單。她穿著軟跟的舒適鞋子，不是華麗戲服。團隊已賺到足夠資源，能提供少量免費名額。沒有人假裝資金永遠夠用，但新董事會已公布未來的收費、修繕與決策計畫。這是一個普通居民也能理解的起點。

Gossip about the old hall had not disappeared. Some people still said the original closure had been a plot against the arts, while others claimed the dancers had ruined a profitable business. Lian asked the board to respond with documents, not insults. The inspection showed that the unsafe area had existed for years; the festival had neither caused it nor repaired it. People innocent of any wrongdoing should not be blamed merely because they worked near the building. The trust's public lobby displayed the latest report, including its limits. Anyone could ask questions about location, cost, and responsibility. The truth was less exciting than gossip, but much more useful.

關於舊舞廳的八卦還沒有消失。有人說當初關閉是反藝術陰謀，也有人指控舞者破壞一門有利可圖的生意。黎安請董事會用文件回應，不用辱罵。檢查報告顯示危險區域已存在多年；節慶既不是原因，也沒有修好它。沒有犯錯的人不該只因在建物附近工作就被責怪。信託的公開大廳展示最新報告，也註明限制。任何人都可以詢問地點、成本與責任。真相沒有八卦刺激，卻有用得多。

The neighborhood workshop began with an educational game about space. Dancers moved inside a rectangle marked by bright tape, then learned how to choose different routes without blocking one another. Cora led the first section, and Lian assisted without taking over. Students had plenty of questions. One asked why there was no raised stage. Cora answered that a flat floor allowed more kinds of movement and made the space efficient for their purpose. A second student brought a raw idea for a dance about growing vegetables. Lian encouraged her to test it with a partner before deciding on the final form. Good teaching made room for ideas that were not yet complete.

社區工作坊用一個關於空間的教育遊戲開始。舞者在亮色膠帶標出的長方形裡移動，學習選擇不同路線而不阻礙彼此。可拉帶第一段，黎安從旁協助，沒有接管。學員有很多問題。一人問為何沒有升高舞台。可拉回答，平地能容納更多種類的動作，也更符合這門課的需求。另一位學生帶來一個未加工的想法，想做關於蔬菜生長的舞。黎安鼓勵她先和夥伴測試，再決定最後形狀。好的教學會替尚未完成的想法留出空間。

At the break, volunteers served fruit sliced into small pieces and drinks through reusable cups. A child tried to suck the last drop of juice through a paper straw and made everyone laugh. Ron, back from his contract, had arrived quietly at the school gate. He greeted Cora first and asked what equipment needed checking rather than announcing himself as the hero of the show. Lian met him near the water table. They smiled, happy to see one another after weeks of calls and visits. The time apart had not made their choices disappear. It had given each of them new work to share, when they wished to share it. The kitchen worker offered to slice another pear, but the children said the prepared fruit was enough.

休息時，志工提供切成小片的水果，以及可重複使用的杯子。一個孩子用紙吸管吸最後一滴果汁，把大家逗笑。羅恩結束合約後悄悄來到校門。他先向可拉打招呼，問哪些器材需要檢查，而不是把自己宣布成節目的英雄。黎安在飲水桌旁見到他。經過數週電話和短暫相聚，兩人很高興再相見。分開的時間沒有抹去他們各自的選擇，反而讓彼此有更多新工作，可以在願意時分享。 廚房工作者想再切一顆梨子，孩子們說已準備的水果就夠了。

In the afternoon the new board reviewed plans for the old hall. The revised design would shrink the private event area but preserve a shared teaching floor. An unused tunnel beneath the former post office would remain closed until a separate engineering study was complete. Mrs. Wu did not promise an opening date. Instead, she described the next inspection, the money still needed, and the people responsible for each step. The room listened carefully. Lian saw that the board had learned to tend a public project as patiently as farmers tend their fields. It did not need a miracle. It needed repeated choices, made openly and corrected when necessary.

下午，新董事會審閱舊舞廳的計畫。修改後的設計會縮小私人活動區，保留共用教學地板。舊郵局下方一條未使用的隧道，將在獨立工程研究完成前持續關閉。吳女士沒有承諾開放日期，而是說明下次檢查、仍需的經費，以及每一步由誰負責。眾人仔細聽著。黎安看見董事會學會像農夫照顧田地那樣，耐心照料公共計畫。它不需要奇蹟，需要的是一再做出公開選擇，並在必要時修正。

At sunset the dancers gathered for one final practice on the safe school floor. Lian stepped into the circle beside Cora and Ron. There was no special place for a director, an adviser, or a technician. Each person moved differently, but everyone could hear the same music and see the same open exits. When the last note ended, they remained in place for a moment, breathing together. Lian looked at the people around her and understood what the project had saved. It was not only an old dream of hers, and not yet the building she loved. It was the right to create together without surrendering safety, respect, or the freedom to choose another path. She raised her hand, and the dancers bowed to one another.

日落時，舞者在安全的學校地板上聚集，進行最後一次練習。黎安走進圓圈，站在可拉與羅恩旁邊。導演、顧問或技師都沒有特殊位置。每個人動作不同，卻都能聽見同一段音樂，也都看得見暢通出口。最後一個音符停下，大家暫時不動，一起呼吸。黎安看著周圍的人，終於明白這項計畫救回了什麼。那不只是她童年的夢，也還不是她深愛的那棟建築；而是大家可以共同創作，同時不必交出安全、尊重與選擇其他道路的自由。她舉起手，舞者彼此鞠躬。
