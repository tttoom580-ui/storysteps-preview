The Marked Alley

把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Tuesday morning the delivery van stopped in the narrow alley behind the hall. A worker stepped out carrying a roll of cable and almost slipped on a crushed cherry. Lian caught the box he dropped, but the sudden movement made her dizzy. Ron asked her to sit before doing anything else. A loud crash came from the loading door: an old shelf had fallen because its base was no longer solid. Mrs. Wu praised Lian's bravery, but Lian shook her head. Being brave did not mean ignoring a warning. They stopped the delivery, blocked the alley, and called a qualified inspector. No rehearsal would begin while the rear exit remained unsafe.

星期二早晨，送貨廂型車停在舞廳後方的狹窄巷子。一名工人抱著一捲電纜下車，差點踩到壓爛的櫻桃滑倒。黎安接住他掉落的箱子，突然的動作卻使她頭暈。羅恩先請她坐下。裝卸門傳來巨大碰撞聲：一座舊架子倒了，因為底座已經不牢固。吳女士稱讚黎安勇敢，但她搖頭。勇敢不等於無視警訊。他們停止送貨、封鎖巷道，並聯絡合格檢查員。後門出口安全以前，不會有任何排練。

The inspector asked for a clear definition of the work they planned to do. Would they merely clean the floor, or change a public exit? The distinction mattered because different permits applied. He agreed to investigate the fallen shelf, the door frame, and the wiring before anyone gave approval. Beside the damaged wall stood a woman knitting a scarf for her grandson. She had seen the rear door blocked by boxes on several previous market days. Lian asked her to write what she remembered and place a date on the note. The woman wanted to label the incident a murder of common sense, but Lian suggested less dramatic words that could support a useful report. The woman said she had learned to knit in this very building, before the floor became dangerous.

檢查員要求他們明確定義預定工程。只是清理地板，還是改動公共出口？兩者不同，適用的許可也不同。他同意先調查倒塌架子、門框與電線，再談核准。破牆旁，一名婦人正在替孫子編織圍巾。她以前幾次市集日都看過後門被箱子堵住。黎安請她寫下記憶並註明日期。婦人想把事件稱為「常識被謀殺」，黎安則建議使用不那麼戲劇化、卻能幫助正式報告的說法。 婦人說，她就是在這棟建築學會編織的，當時地板還沒有危險。

Across the alley a poster showed a model in a sexy costume advertising a late-night dance contest. Cora said the picture did not represent the classes they were trying to build. “Some adults enjoy that style and should be free to choose it,” she explained, “but our poster should show our actual participants.” A volunteer, who had taken a short nap after a night shift, arrived carrying tea on a saucer. He was thankful nobody had been hurt. The little group stood beside the fallen shelf as a breeze came through the alley. For all the trouble, there was warmth in the way people asked if others were safe before discussing who was to blame.

巷子對面，一張海報用穿性感服裝的模特兒宣傳深夜舞蹈比賽。可拉說，這不代表他們正在建立的課程。「有些成年人喜歡那種風格，應該可以自由選，」她解釋，「但我們的海報應該呈現真正的參與者。」一名剛上完夜班、短暫打過盹的志工端著放在茶碟上的茶走來。他很慶幸沒有人受傷。眾人站在倒掉的架子旁，巷子裡吹來微風。即使麻煩不斷，大家先問彼此是否安全，再討論責任，仍帶著一種溫暖。

From the old clock tower, the hour bell sounded over the roofs. The inspector said he had seen small organizations starve because they kept taking on costs without a plan. Lian thanked him for the warning. She asked him to separate essential safety repairs from decoration so the budget would not hide either. Ron prepared a photograph record and marked every loose board with tape. The team also moved the unused materials to a covered storage room rather than leaving them in the alley. A closed exit might look like a small problem on an ordinary day, but during a crowded event it could decide whether people got home safely.

舊鐘塔的整點鐘聲越過屋頂。檢查員說，他見過一些小組織因不斷承擔沒有規劃的成本而逐漸撐不下去。黎安感謝提醒，請他把必要安全工程與裝飾工程分開列出，避免預算混淆。羅恩建立照片紀錄，用膠帶標示每塊鬆動木板。團隊也把不用的材料移進有遮蔽的儲藏室，不再留在巷子裡。平日看似不起眼的封閉出口，遇上人潮時卻可能決定大家能否安全回家。

After the inspector left, Cora rolled to the north door and tried the temporary route herself. A turning space that looked wide in the drawing was too narrow when another person stood nearby. Ron moved two chairs and measured again. Lian wrote down the failure instead of choosing a better-looking photograph. She had once worked for companies that displayed only successful rehearsals. Their new group would publish mistakes too, as long as private information remained protected. A reliable plan could change when facts changed. She wrote that sentence in large letters at the top of the safety board.

檢查員離開後，可拉自己從北門試走臨時路線。圖上看來寬敞的轉彎空間，只要旁邊站個人就太窄。羅恩移走兩張椅子，再次丈量。黎安記錄失敗，而不是挑一張好看的照片。她以前替只展示成功排練的公司工作，如今這個小團隊會公布錯誤，只要能保護個人隱私即可。可靠的計畫，應該會隨事實改變。她把這句話大字寫在安全板最上方。

At closing time Lian sent the inspector's preliminary observations to the arts trust and council. She made no promise that the hall would pass its final inspection. Instead, she requested time to complete a detailed repair plan. Mrs. Wu locked the back door and carried the key to a named custodian. The alley was empty again, and the fallen shelf had been removed. Lian looked at the clean passage and felt relief, but she understood that a single tidy evening could not prove the building safe. Tomorrow would bring another test, and she was willing to let it tell her something she did not expect.

打烊時，黎安把檢查員的初步觀察寄給藝術信託與議會。她沒有保證舞廳一定通過最後檢查，而是要求時間完成詳細修繕計畫。吳女士鎖好後門，把鑰匙交給指定管理人。巷子再次空了，倒下的架子也被移走。黎安望著乾淨的通道，感到安心，卻知道一個晚上整齊，不代表建物已經安全。明天還有新的測試，她願意讓測試告訴自己沒預料到的事。
