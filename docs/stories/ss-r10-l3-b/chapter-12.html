The Second Door

把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Cora moved aside to let an elderly client enter the council office. The woman had lived near the river for forty years and remembered a storm that trapped passengers behind the old market. She brought a map of the canyon road above town, where a school gym stood on higher ground. “You are capable of holding a smaller event there,” she told Lian, “if the hall does not pass inspection.” A retired ferry captain offered to help check transport arrangements, but insisted that people should not rely on his memory alone. Lian thanked them both. An alternative venue would not be a defeat. It would be another way to keep the promise to the dancers.

可拉往旁邊移動，讓一位年長的委託人進入議會辦公室。婦人在河邊住了四十年，記得曾有暴風雨把乘客困在舊市場後方。她帶來山城上方峽谷道路的地圖，標出地勢較高的學校體育館。「如果舞廳沒通過檢查，妳有能力在那裡辦一場較小的活動。」她對黎安說。一位退休渡輪船長願意幫忙檢查交通安排，但堅持不能只依賴他的記憶。黎安向兩人道謝。備用場地不是失敗，而是另一種遵守對舞者承諾的方法。

Ron offered to edit the technical information for the grant application, while Lian kept control of the artistic plan. A flash of light from an electrician's camera showed the open hatch above the equipment room. Inside was a damaged cable, not a secret passage. The electrician turned off the electricity before examining it. Lian read the new report and felt hopeful for the first time in days. It explained what could be repaired, how long it might take, and which tests were required. The monthly property fee for the hall was still due, however, and the trust could not pay for unlimited delays. They had to decide which route was realistic. A bubble formed in the water near the drain during a test, so Ron asked the plumber to check for trapped air.

羅恩主動替補助申請修改技術資料，藝術部分則仍由黎安決定。電工相機的一道閃光照出設備間上方打開的檢修口。裡面是一條受損電纜，不是祕密通道。電工關閉電源後才查看。黎安讀完新報告，幾天來第一次感到希望。報告解釋哪些能修、需要多久，以及必須完成哪些測試。然而舞廳每月的房產費仍然要付，信託無法承受無限期拖延。他們必須決定哪條路線真正可行。 測試時，排水口旁的水裡冒出一個氣泡，羅恩便請水管工檢查是否有積存空氣。

At a planning meeting Lian asked Cora to represent the performers' access requirements in her own words. The group discussed the situation if rain blocked the low road. Ron listed necessary information: transport capacity, travel time, emergency contacts, and the person authorized to close the event. Someone suggested adding a sword dance from an old festival. Lian said props could be considered only after the safety plan was settled. In the kitchen, a volunteer added spice to soup and handed out bread. The small meal gave everyone a chance to talk without turning every question into a formal argument. Even practical meetings needed room for ordinary conversation.

規劃會上，黎安請可拉用自己的話代表表演者提出無障礙需求。大家討論如果下雨封住低窪道路該怎麼辦。羅恩列出必要資訊：運輸容量、交通時間、緊急聯絡方式，以及誰有權取消活動。有人提議加入舊節慶的劍舞，黎安說，道具必須等安全計畫確定才考慮。廚房裡，志工替湯加香料，分發麵包。簡單的一餐讓人們有機會自在聊天，不必把每個問題都變成正式辯論。即使務實的會議，也需要普通的對話空間。

During the visit to the school gym, Cora pointed to a narrow doorway beneath the spectator seats. It could not serve as the main entrance for her chair, but a wider side door was close by. Lian checked whether the route would work when a large audience arrived, not just during an empty tour. The gym manager agreed to hold one possible date until the trust made a decision. No one had signed a final contract yet. Ron took photographs of exits and power points, while Lian sketched where a small group could perform without raised platforms. The alternative looked less beautiful than the old hall but more dependable.

參觀學校體育館時，可拉指出觀眾席下的一扇窄門。她的輪椅無法把它當主要入口，但附近有一扇較寬的側門。黎安檢查人潮到場時是否仍能通行，而不是只看空場的樣子。體育館管理員同意暫時保留一個日期，等信託決定。此時還沒有人簽下最終合約。羅恩拍下出口與電源位置，黎安則畫出一小群人不必升高舞台也能演出的站位。替代場地沒有舊舞廳漂亮，卻似乎更可靠。

In the evening Lian called the elderly woman to describe the visit. The woman wept softly because the school gym had once served as a shelter during the flood. “I am glad it can still help people,” she said. Lian understood that buildings carried more than one history, and no single story belonged to everyone. She wrote two program drafts: one for the hall, one for the gym. Both preserved the same artistic theme: people finding their own way through a shared space. The dancers would be asked to review both, and no one would have to accept a dangerous route for the sake of a memory. The woman said it was all right to weep for people she had lost while still planning a safer future.

晚上，黎安打給那位年長婦人，說明參訪結果。婦人輕輕落淚，因為那間體育館在洪水期間曾是避難所。「很高興它還能幫助人。」她說。黎安明白，建物承載不只一種歷史，也沒有任何一個故事屬於所有人。她寫出兩份節目草案：一份在舞廳，一份在體育館。兩份保留同一藝術主題：人們在共同空間裡找到自己的路。她將請舞者審閱兩種方案，也不會要求誰為了回憶而接受危險動線。 婦人說，為逝去的人落淚並不妨礙自己同時規劃更安全的未來。

Ron came to return a borrowed level and found Lian studying two maps on the floor. He asked what she would prefer if no money or deadlines existed. She answered honestly: the old hall, repaired and open to everyone. Then she folded that map and placed the gym plan above it. “But the people must come first.” Ron sat beside her, careful not to disturb the drawings. The choice was not between courage and fear. It was between a wish and the facts available today. He said he would help make either option work, within the limits of his technical role. Lian felt grateful for a promise that did not pretend to control the future.

羅恩來歸還借用的水平儀，發現黎安坐在地上研究兩張地圖。他問她如果沒有經費和期限限制，最想選哪個場地。她誠實回答：修好並對所有人開放的舊舞廳。隨後她折起那張圖，把體育館方案放到上面。「但人必須優先。」羅恩坐在她旁邊，小心不碰亂圖紙。這不是勇氣與恐懼之間的選擇，而是願望與今日可得事實之間的選擇。他說會在自己的技術職責範圍內協助任何一種方案。黎安感激這份不假裝能控制未來的承諾。
