贊助者的條件

The Sponsor's Conditions

把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 14 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The arts trust was preparing an announcement when a large tourism company offered to fund the whole festival. Its representative arrived in an indoor market decorated with images of a fantastic sea creature. She proposed bringing visitors by coach and charging a high fare for special seats. Lian asked whether that plan would enable residents with low incomes to attend. The representative replied that tourism had to pay for itself. Cora sat apart from the group at first, reviewing the contract. A clause gave the sponsor the right to change the order of dances. Lian did not want to fight over every detail, but this condition would remove artistic decisions from the performers. The trust planned to announce its decision after reviewing the revised contract, not before.

藝術信託正準備公告時，一家大型觀光公司提出資助整個節慶。代表在室內市場與奇幻海洋生物圖像之間出現，建議以遊覽車送來旅客，並對特別座位收取高票價。黎安問這樣能否讓收入不高的居民參加。代表回答觀光得自己賺回成本。可拉起初坐在旁邊仔細閱讀合約。其中一條授權贊助者更動舞蹈順序。黎安不想為每個細節爭吵，但這條件會把藝術決策權從表演者手中拿走。 信託計畫等修正合約審閱完成後才公告決定，不會提前。

At brunch the next morning, Ron showed Lian the company's event plan. It included an elevated stage reached by a narrow ladder, with no accessible route for performers. The representative had nicknamed the design “the crown,” but Cora called it a barrier. A worker peeled an orange while they studied the drawings, and its fresh smell helped clear the heavy air. “I pity anyone who believes a photograph proves access,” Cora said. Lian proposed a flat stage and several possible entrances, but the company wanted a dramatic raised platform. The disagreement was no longer a question of taste. Their signed principles required safe access and shared artistic authority. After Ron helped the worker peel a second orange, Cora checked a slippery patch near the door and asked the cleaner to dry it.

隔天早午餐時，羅恩拿出公司的活動設計。裡面有一座升高舞台，只能從狹窄梯子上去，沒有給表演者的無障礙路線。代表替設計取了「王冠」的暱稱，可拉卻說那是一道障礙。一名工人邊剝柳橙邊看圖，清新的果香稍微減輕沉重氣氛。「我真替相信照片就能證明無障礙的人感到可惜。」可拉說。黎安提出平面舞台與多個入口，公司卻堅持高台才有戲劇效果。分歧已不只是品味問題。他們簽訂的原則要求安全通行與共同藝術決策。 羅恩幫工人再剝一顆柳橙後，可拉檢查門邊一塊濕滑區域，請清潔人員擦乾。

At the town library an old newspaper showed that a similar event had once been canceled after a robbery near its ticket office. The company representative said safety problems belonged to the past, but Lian asked for current plans rather than old promises. Someone raised a suspicion that the sponsor might favor only certain neighborhoods. Lian said the group should examine the stated terms instead of guessing at motives. They added a requirement that selection rules must not exclude people because of racial background, income, or disability. The clause was clear enough for the company's lawyers to review. A fair contract could name unacceptable conditions without accusing anyone of a crime. The company wanted to export several decorations after the event, so the contract needed to say who owned them. A costumed fighter from the local theater also offered props, but no one accepted them without inspection.

鎮圖書館裡，一份舊報紙記載類似活動曾因售票處附近發生搶劫而取消。公司代表說安全問題早已過去，但黎安要求看當前計畫，而不是舊承諾。有人懷疑贊助商只想照顧特定社區。黎安說應該檢查明文條件，而不是猜測動機。他們加入規定，選人標準不能因種族背景、收入或身心障礙排除任何人。條款清楚到足以交由公司法務檢查。公平合約可以指出不可接受的條件，而不必指控誰犯罪。 公司想在活動後將部分裝飾出口，因此合約必須寫明所有權。地方劇團一位穿戰士戲服的演員也願提供道具，但未檢查前沒有人收下。

An official from the city planning office visited the hall and said the indoor festival permit could not be issued until the emergency exits passed inspection. The trust asked whether a temporary approval would help. The official explained that a temporary document could cover only limited activities, not crowded performances. Lian accepted that answer and asked for it in writing. If a private company promised to obtain permission by friendship rather than proper inspection, she would reject that promise. The decision reduced their options, but it protected the dancers. The arts trust began reviewing the school gym as the more realistic venue for the first public show. The official explained that breaking a building rule was not automatically a reason to send someone to jail, but the approval system existed to prevent harm.

市府規劃單位人員來到舞廳，表示室內節慶許可必須等逃生出口通過檢查才能核發。信託問臨時核准能否幫忙。官員解釋臨時文件只涵蓋有限活動，不能用於擁擠表演。黎安接受答案，請對方提供書面說明。如果私人公司承諾靠交情而非正式檢查取得許可，她會拒絕。決定讓選擇變少，卻保護了舞者。藝術信託開始重新評估學校體育館，認為那是首場公開演出比較實際的場地。 官員說明違反建物規定不代表會自動入獄，但核准制度正是為了防止傷害。

Ron worried that the company might withdraw all support. Lian said he was right to worry, but that fear was not a reason to give away Cora's rights. At the next meeting they presented two versions of the budget: one using the company's conditions and one using smaller donations with a simpler program. The second needed more work and fewer seats, but kept the published access policy. The company representative listened and asked whether she could revise the proposal. Lian agreed to review new terms, not to promise acceptance. Negotiation could be respectful without being an endless series of concessions. The representative's nickname for the proposal remained on her slide, although the new agreement would use formal names and define the shared urban space carefully.

羅恩擔心公司會撤回全部支持。黎安說他的擔心有道理，但恐懼不能成為放棄可拉權利的理由。下次會議上，他們提出兩種預算：一種接受公司條件，另一種靠較小捐款和較簡單節目。第二種更費工、座位更少，卻保留已公布的無障礙政策。公司代表聽完，問能否修改提案。黎安同意審閱新條款，卻不保證接受。談判可以尊重彼此，也不代表必須無止盡讓步。 代表對提案的暱稱仍留在簡報上，但新協議會用正式名稱，並仔細定義這片共用都市空間。

After everyone left, Cora thanked Lian for refusing the raised stage, then corrected her gently. “Next time, let me explain the design problem myself before you speak for me.” Lian felt embarrassed, but she knew Cora was right. Supporting a colleague did not mean replacing her voice. She apologized and agreed to change the meeting procedure. Cora smiled. “Good. We still have a show to build.” Lian walked into the quiet square understanding that leadership was not one long speech. Sometimes it was the act of making space, moving aside, and listening before deciding what to say.

人散後，可拉感謝黎安拒絕高台，接著溫和地指出：「下次，讓我自己先解釋設計問題，妳再發言。」黎安有些尷尬，卻知道可拉說得對。支持同事不代表取代她的聲音。她道歉，答應修改會議程序。可拉笑了。「很好。我們還有一場演出要做。」黎安走進安靜廣場，明白領導不是一場說不完的演說。有時，領導是留出位置、稍微退開，先聽再決定要說什麼。
