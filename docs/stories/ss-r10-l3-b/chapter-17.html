穿過花園的路

A Route Through the Garden

把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 17 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

By Wednesday everyone was aware that the low road remained unsafe. The old bridge had been badly damaged, and workers had to bury a broken pipe before reopening the pavement. Ron placed orange traffic cones along the safe garden route while Cora checked where a wheelchair might turn. A painted dolphin on the school wall helped visitors recognize the correct entrance. Lian bumped her elbow on a low railing and asked the maintenance team to mark it more clearly. The expectation that a public route would work for everyone needed to be tested with actual people, not just approved on a drawing. Ron inspected the corner again and added a simple protective cover. One cone had fallen into the road, so the team retrieved it only after a traffic officer stopped vehicles.

星期三，所有人都知道低窪道路仍不安全。舊橋損壞嚴重，工人得先把破裂水管埋好才能重新鋪路。羅恩沿安全的花園路線擺上橘色交通錐，可拉則檢查輪椅轉彎處。學校牆上的海豚彩繪幫助訪客認出正確入口。黎安的手肘撞到低矮欄杆，請維護團隊標示得更清楚。公共路線能讓所有人使用的期待，必須透過真實使用者測試，不能只在圖上核准。羅恩再次檢查轉角，裝上簡單保護套。 有一個交通錐倒進馬路，團隊等交通人員先攔停車輛後才取回。

The town's industrial district had offered a warehouse for costume storage, but it was farther away and lacked a safe evening bus service. Lian declined the offer for now. On the trip back, a dog with thick fur followed the group to the school gate and wagged its tail. Cora laughed and called it a cheerful escort. Their journey through the garden had been slower than the original route, but it was calm and lively, with room for people to stop. The team measured how often volunteers would need to guide arrivals and whether a clear sign could replace one of those posts. Practical questions mattered because an opening-night crowd would be less patient than a rehearsal team.

鎮上工業區提供一間倉庫收戲服，但距離較遠，也沒有安全的夜間公車。黎安暫時婉拒。回程時，一隻厚毛狗跟著大家到校門口，開心搖尾巴。可拉笑說牠是熱情的護送員。穿過花園的旅程雖比原路慢，卻平靜而有活力，也有讓人停下的空間。團隊計算需要多常安排志工引導來客，以及清楚標示能否取代某些固定站點。這些實務問題很重要，因為開幕夜的觀眾不會像排練團隊那麼有耐心。

At lunch the mayor visited the school. She asked whether the festival could become a model for other communities, but Lian warned that a successful demonstration did not prove every building could use the same design. A producer from a regional arts program offered to document the process. Cora asked for a clear agreement about filming, and the producer accepted. Outside, an old fruit tree had begun to rot at its base. The school gardener planned to inspect it before the event; no one would sit beneath it until the danger was assessed. Lian added the tree to the site log. Even a beautiful garden had limits that an organizer should not overlook. A volunteer served pasta with tomato sauce, and the mayor asked whether meals were labeled for allergies.

午餐時，鎮長來到學校。她問節慶能否成為其他社區的範例，黎安提醒，一次成功示範不代表所有建物都能照搬設計。地方藝術節目製作人提議記錄過程。可拉要求明確拍攝協議，對方接受。屋外一棵老果樹的樹根開始腐爛，學校園丁計畫在活動前檢查；危險評估前，沒有人會坐在樹下。黎安把這棵樹加入現場紀錄。再漂亮的花園也有界線，主辦者不能忽略。 志工供應番茄醬義大利麵，鎮長詢問餐點是否標示過敏原。

A discussion about the program caused unexpected discomfort. One volunteer wanted to include a dramatic scene involving suicide to make the story more emotional. Cora asked whether such a scene belonged in a joyful community dance without proper care or context. The group agreed it did not serve their theme and chose a different way to show loss. Another volunteer proposed a whip as a prop for a historical folk dance. Lian rejected it because the piece was not prepared to address that object's history or use it safely. They kept the rhythm of the dance with a cloth ribbon instead. Artistic freedom included the responsibility to choose what not to use.

討論節目時，意外出現一段令人不舒服的爭論。有志工想加入涉及自殺的戲劇性場面，讓故事更有情緒。可拉問，若沒有適當脈絡與照顧，這樣的場面是否適合歡樂的社區舞作。大家同意它不符合主題，改用其他方式表達失落。另一名志工想在歷史民俗舞裡使用鞭子。黎安拒絕，因為作品沒有準備好處理這個物件的歷史，也無法確保安全。大家改用布緞帶保留節奏。藝術自由也包含知道什麼不該使用的責任。

In the afternoon a nurse checked the first-aid station and asked volunteers to practice how they would request emergency help. Ron made the instructions frequent and short rather than giving one long lecture. Lian noticed that the group's confidence had grown since the first emergency drill, but she did not confuse confidence with readiness. She asked someone unfamiliar with the plan to find the nearest exit and water station. The visitor found both, though the sign for the quiet room was hidden by a plant. They moved the plant immediately. The change was small, yet it showed why an outside perspective was useful before welcoming a crowd. An unused tent from the canceled market was kept folded in storage rather than blocking the garden path.

下午，護理師檢查急救站，請志工練習如何請求緊急協助。羅恩把說明分成經常重複的短指示，而不是一次長篇演講。黎安發現團隊自第一次演練起已更有信心，但她沒有把自信當成準備就緒。她請一位不熟悉計畫的訪客找出最近出口與飲水站。對方都找到了，只是安靜室標示被盆栽遮住。大家立刻移開植物。改動很小，卻顯示迎接人潮之前，外部視角有多重要。 取消市集後剩下的帳篷摺好收在倉庫，沒有擋住花園路線。

On her way home, Lian walked beside Ron as the evening wind rose. He asked whether she ever missed traveling with a professional company. She said she missed the stages and skilled dancers, but not the feeling that every disagreement might cost her job. Here she could build a different kind of work, even if the pay was uncertain. Ron told her he hoped she would continue choreographing after the festival, wherever that led. Lian appreciated the openness of the wish. He was not asking her to stay in town for him. Their relationship was beginning to make room for two careers, not just one shared schedule.

回家路上，晚風漸起，黎安和羅恩並肩走著。他問她是否想念專業舞團的巡演。她說想念舞台與優秀舞者，卻不想念每次不同意都可能失去工作的感覺。這裡能建立不同形式的工作，即使收入還不穩定。羅恩說，希望她在節慶結束後繼續編舞，不管那會帶她去哪裡。黎安喜歡這份開放的祝福。他沒有要求她為自己留在鎮上。他們的關係開始為兩份事業留下空間，而不只是一張共同排班表。
