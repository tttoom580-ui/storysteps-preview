好主意的代價

The Price of a Good Idea

把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the next planning meeting, Ron said, “Anyhow, we must decide what we can finish in six weeks.” Lian had assumed that enthusiasm would be their greatest resource, but volunteers had jobs and families. Cora, now a paid artistic adviser, explained that the creator of a dance should not be expected to teach it for free simply because the project had a good purpose. Excellence required rehearsal time, rest, and honest payment. Lian looked at the inner columns of the budget and realized she had listed equipment in detail while giving labor only one line. She drew a line through that mistake. Paying people fairly was not a reward to consider at the end. It was a basic cost from the beginning. She would no longer assume that unpaid hours appeared by magic; every person's effort belonged in the calculation.

下次規劃會上，羅恩說：「無論如何，我們得決定六週內能做完什麼。」黎安原本以為熱情就是最大的資源，但志工有工作，也有家庭。如今成為有薪藝術顧問的可拉解釋，不能因為計畫立意良善，就期待舞蹈創作者免費教學。優秀的成果需要排練時間、休息與誠實付費。黎安看著預算內側的欄位，才發現自己把器材列得很細，卻只用一行寫人事。她劃掉那個錯誤。合理給薪不是最後才考慮的獎賞，而是從一開始就存在的基本成本。 她不再假設無薪工時會憑空出現；每個人的努力都該算進去。

The rehearsal hall had developed a strange smell after the storm. Ron warned that standing water might allow bacteria to grow, so the cleaners tested and dried the affected corner. When a worker pushed a trolley across the hall, its wheel struck a bump and several boxes fell. Cora stopped the group before anyone rushed to pick them up. One box held a stage prop shaped like a missile, left from a student comedy about space travel. Nobody had checked the old prop list. The incident was harmless, but it showed how easily small problems could multiply. They put the props into clear groups and moved only those approved for the next rehearsal.

暴雨之後，排練廳出現一股奇怪氣味。羅恩提醒，積水可能使細菌滋生，因此清潔人員檢測並烘乾受影響的角落。一名工人推著手推車經過，輪子撞上凸起處，幾個箱子掉下來。可拉先叫大家停住，不要急著撿。其中一箱裝著一支飛彈形狀的舞台道具，是以前學生太空喜劇留下的。沒有人核對舊道具清單。這次沒有危險，卻顯示小問題多容易成倍增加。他們把道具分門別類，只搬動下次排練核准使用的物品。

A local politician visited with a photographer and asked Lian to pose beside the newly cleaned floor. She replied that it was not ready for public use. “A picture won't change the inspection result,” she said. He seemed disappointed, but he listened when Cora explained the necessity of a clear entrance and an accessible toilet. Ron showed the week's summary, with costs supported by invoices. The politician could offer advice about public grants, not technical approval. After he left, a volunteer joked that the town would suspect them of being difficult. Lian answered that questions were welcome; pretending to have passed an inspection would be much worse.

一位地方政治人物帶著攝影師來訪，想請黎安站在剛清理的地板旁合照。她回答，這裡尚未開放公眾使用。「照片不會改變檢查結果。」她說。對方似乎失望，但可拉說明清楚入口與無障礙廁所的必要性時，他仍仔細聽著。羅恩展示本週摘要，費用都附有收據。政治人物能提供公共補助的建議，卻不能取代技術核准。人走後，有志工開玩笑說全鎮會懷疑他們難相處。黎安回答，提問很歡迎；假裝通過檢查才是更糟的事。

During a training session, Ron taught a simple technique for checking the floor before anyone crossed it. A teenage assistant from the nearby school helped demonstrate, with an adult teacher supervising. The room was warm and damp after a week of almost tropical weather. Lian used a strip of colored wax to mark the safe testing line. Several dancers complained that their stomach hurt because they had worked through lunch, so she ended the lesson early and ordered sandwiches. The decision cost an hour, but they returned more focused. She wrote a note to herself: people cannot think clearly when a project treats their bodies as machines that never need care. After lunch, someone found stale bread in an unopened box, and the kitchen team disposed of it rather than serving it.

訓練課上，羅恩教大家一種簡單技巧，在穿過地板前先做檢查。附近學校的一名青少年助理在成年教師監督下示範。連日近乎熱帶的悶熱天氣，讓室內溫暖潮濕。黎安用一條彩色蠟筆線標出安全測試區。有幾位舞者因忙得沒吃午餐而胃痛，她便提早結束課程，叫來三明治。這個決定花掉一小時，但大家回來時更能專心。她在筆記上寫著：若一項計畫把人的身體當成永不需要照顧的機器，就無法期待大家清楚思考。 午餐後，有人在未開封的箱子裡找到不新鮮的麵包，廚房團隊選擇丟棄，不拿來供餐。

At home Lian opened an old notebook from her first professional tour. Its pages were full of drawings but almost no names. She remembered how hard it had been to tell an experienced director that she could not perform a dangerous lift. This time she wanted every performer to be able to say no without losing a place in the group. She drafted a rehearsal rule: safety concerns could pause work immediately, and any change would be discussed with the people affected. Cora approved the principle but suggested clearer language about who made the final decision. Lian accepted the correction. Good intentions were not a substitute for clear authority.

回家後，黎安翻開第一次專業巡演的舊筆記。頁面畫滿草圖，卻幾乎沒有姓名。她想起當年要向資深導演說自己無法完成危險托舉有多困難。這一次，她希望每位表演者都能說不，而不會因此失去參與資格。她草擬一條排練規定：提出安全疑慮可以立即暫停工作，任何更動都要和受影響的人討論。可拉贊同原則，但建議把最後決策權寫得更清楚。黎安接受修正。善意不能代替明確權責。

On Friday Ron came by with the updated cost sheet. The figures were higher than they had hoped. Lian felt the familiar temptation to cut the paid rehearsal hours, but she put down her pen. They could reduce decoration, shorten the program, or hold fewer performances. They could not build a fair project by asking the people with the least power to carry the heaviest burden. Ron asked whether she wanted to walk home together. She said yes, and they talked about street lamps and childhood dances rather than money. For a short distance the problem remained unsolved, yet the company made the walk feel lighter.

星期五，羅恩帶來更新後的成本表，數字比預期高。黎安又想刪掉有薪排練時數，但她放下筆。他們可以減少裝飾、縮短節目，或減少演出場次，不能靠要求最沒有權力的人承擔最重的負擔來建造公平的計畫。羅恩問她要不要一起走回家。她答應了，兩人一路談街燈和童年的舞步，暫時不談錢。問題並未在那短短一段路上得到解決，但有人同行，讓腳步輕了些。
