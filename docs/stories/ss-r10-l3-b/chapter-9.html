消失的一週

The Missing Week

把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The repair record for the previous summer was missing from the hall's database. Ron suspected an ordinary filing error, but the arts trust wanted certainty before renewing the permit. The inspection team traveled to a small cabin used for storing records. The route passed a field where a farm animal had cut its leg; a worker called for help before the wound could bleed badly. Lian did not stop to photograph the scene. She was there to find evidence about the hall, not to turn a stranger's trouble into content. At the cabin the caretaker warned them that the records might bore anyone hoping for a mystery. “Mostly invoices and schedules,” she said.

舞廳去夏的維修紀錄從資料庫裡不見了。羅恩猜是普通歸檔錯誤，但藝術信託希望在更新許可前確定。調查小隊前往一間存放紀錄的小屋，途中經過農地，一隻家畜割傷腿，工人立刻求助，避免傷口嚴重流血。黎安沒有停下拍照。她是來找舞廳證據，不是把陌生人的困境變成內容。到了小屋，管理人提醒他們，若期待驚奇謎團，這些檔案恐怕會讓人無聊。「大部分是發票和班表。」她說。

The box contained records from several local elections, including debates about democracy and public buildings. Lian carefully separated those from the hall's files. On a dusty shelf Ron found a receipt with the repair company's name but no completion signature. A sudden sound from a truck horn outside caused the caretaker to faint for a moment; Cora called for medical help while Ron cleared space around her. The fright passed when the caretaker recovered, but nobody treated it as a joke. Laughter returned only after she asked them to bring her a chair and made a joke herself. They postponed the search until she was comfortable continuing.

箱子裡有幾次地方選舉的紀錄，包含民主制度與公共建物辯論。黎安小心把它們與舞廳文件分開。羅恩在滿是灰塵的架子上找到一張維修公司收據，上面有公司名稱，卻沒有完工簽名。外頭貨車突然按喇叭，管理人一時昏厥。可拉呼叫醫療協助，羅恩則清出她周圍的空間。管理人恢復後，驚嚇逐漸過去，但沒有人拿這件事開玩笑。直到她自己請大家搬椅子，還先說笑，笑聲才回來。他們等她舒適些才繼續查找。

One file described a missing stage panel that had been replaced without a final test. Another included a recording of a skillful singer practicing a tune for the old spring parade. Her voice was strong enough to roar through the tiny speaker, yet the note beside it said she had refused public use. Ron turned the device off immediately. The caretaker explained that the singer's lover had once managed the hall, but the relationship was not relevant to the repair report. Lian agreed to leave the recording sealed. They were searching for an inspection date, not private history. Shortly before sunset, Ron finally found a delivery signature on the back of a separate invoice.

一份檔案記載曾更換一塊失蹤的舞台板材，卻沒有做最後測試。另一份有技藝純熟的歌者替舊春季遊行練習曲調的錄音。她的歌聲幾乎能從小喇叭裡咆哮出來，但旁邊註明她拒絕公開使用。羅恩立刻關掉播放器。管理人說，歌者的戀人曾管理舞廳，不過這段關係與維修報告無關。黎安同意封存錄音。他們要找的是檢查日期，不是私人往事。日落前不久，羅恩終於在另一張發票背面找到收貨簽名。

Outside, rain began to splash into a shallow ditch. Cora asked Lian to help lift the box above the water so the paper would not sink into mud. They watched a lost dog wander between the trees until its owner called it back. Ron said the wet road made driving dangerous, and suggested waiting for the town shuttle. Lian opened her weekly planner and entered a new task: request the repair company's original work order. She also noted that no person should use the old stage panel until the missing test was completed. A record might be absent because someone had made a mistake, not necessarily because anyone intended harm. Evidence had to come before an accusation.

屋外開始下雨，水花濺進淺溝。可拉請黎安幫忙把箱子抬高，免得紙張沉入泥水。她們看到一隻迷路的狗在樹間徘徊，直到主人把牠叫回。羅恩說濕滑道路不適合開車，建議等鎮上的接駁車。黎安打開每週手帳，新增任務：索取維修公司的原始工作單。她也記下，在缺少的測試完成前，任何人都不得使用那塊舊舞台板材。紀錄消失可能只是犯錯，不一定有人蓄意傷害。指控之前必須先有證據。

Before the team left the cabin, the caretaker showed Lian the shelf where the original repair manuals had been stored. The shelf itself carried a date in faded ink, but the manuals had been moved after a roof leak. Lian photographed the empty space and wrote down the caretaker's explanation, including what she could not remember. She promised to request a copy from the repair company instead of guessing. Cora said that careful gaps in a record were better than invented answers. The group then sealed the box they had examined, signed a transfer note, and returned it to its proper keeper. A missing document was a reason to ask more questions, not a reason to name a villain.

團隊離開小屋前，管理人帶黎安看原始維修手冊以前放置的架子。架上用褪色墨水寫著日期，但屋頂漏水後，手冊已搬走。黎安拍下空位，記錄管理人的解釋，也記下她不記得的部分。她承諾向維修公司索取副本，而不是猜測。可拉說，紀錄裡誠實的空白，勝過編造的答案。大家隨後封好檢查過的箱子、簽下移交紀錄，交還指定保管人。文件遺失應該帶來更多提問，而不是立刻找一個反派。

A wooden stage sword lay in the same storage box as the costume papers. Lian placed the prop weapon in a separate container with a clear description, then sealed it. It was harmless during ordinary storage but still required a rehearsal rule. At the shuttle stop, Ron asked what she would do if the missing report showed the hall could not reopen. Lian watched the rain move across the field. “Then we will find another space,” she answered. “The people are the project, not these walls.” Ron did not offer an easy reassurance. He simply moved closer so they could share the shelter without blocking Cora's path.

同一只道具箱裡還有一把木製舞台劍。黎安把這件表演武器放進獨立容器，寫清楚說明後封好。它在普通存放時沒有危險，排練時仍需要規則。在接駁車站，羅恩問她：如果缺少的報告證明舞廳不能重新開放，怎麼辦？黎安望著雨穿過田野。「那就找另一個空間，」她回答，「真正的計畫是這些人，不是牆壁。」羅恩沒有給輕易的安慰，只是靠近一些，讓兩人能共享遮雨處，也不妨礙可拉通行。
