地板上的痕跡

A Trace on the Floor

把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 23 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

The morning after the show, an arts adviser visited to review the event with the team. A newspaper reporter had tried to bait Lian into criticizing the commercial promoter, but she declined. The discussion began with a loud bang from a delivery truck closing its door, then settled into quiet work. The adviser asked for a constant record of changes, not a collection of success stories. Lian showed photographs of the grassy garden path and explained which signs had moved since the pilot test. The crowd's applause had brought glory to the dancers for a night, yet the long-term question remained: could the community support regular classes without depending on one extraordinary festival? She did not hesitate to show the record of an awkward correction because that was part of the real project.

演出隔天早晨，一名藝術顧問來檢視活動。有記者想引誘黎安批評商業活動商，她沒有上當。討論開始時，貨車關門發出一聲巨響，隨後大家安靜工作。顧問要求持續的更動紀錄，而不是蒐集成功故事。黎安展示草地花園路線的照片，解釋哪些標示在試演後移動過。掌聲讓舞者光榮了一晚，長期問題卻仍在：社區能否不依賴一場特別節慶，就持續提供日常課程？ 她毫不猶豫展示一項尷尬修正的紀錄，因為那本來就是計畫的一部分。

Cora had felt pain in her shoulder after the performance and chose to rest before rehearsing again. Lian did not ask her to heal on a timetable set by the grant. Ron called to say an imported light controller could be returned to the supplier now that the event was over. The delivery driver arrived in a small jeep, accompanied by his daughter, who carried a toy koala. She placed the toy on a clean napkin while her father completed the paperwork. Lian smiled at the tiny scene, then checked that the borrowed equipment left in good condition. The project was not finished until its debts, property, and responsibilities were properly closed. Ron could hear the soft hum of the equipment and checked the import paperwork for the borrowed controller.

可拉演出後肩膀疼痛，決定先休息再排練。黎安沒有要求她依補助的時程恢復。羅恩來電說，一台進口燈光控制器可在活動結束後歸還供應商。送貨員開著小吉普車來，女兒抱著玩具無尾熊。她把玩具放在乾淨餐巾上，等爸爸完成文件。黎安對這幅小景微笑，接著確認借來的設備完好歸還。計畫不是謝幕就結束，債務、財產與責任都結清才算完成。 羅恩聽得見設備低低的嗡鳴，並核對借用控制器的進口文件。

That afternoon Lian walked through her hometown with the adviser and Ron. They passed the pub where she had first watched adults dance as a child, then turned toward the river. A ray of sunlight fell onto a faded painted line on the pavement. Ron could trace the old route to the post office, but one corner had been blocked by construction. The adviser asked whether they intended to reopen that path. Lian hesitated, then answered that they would wait for a new inspection. The question was scary because she loved the place, but a sensible decision did not grow less sensible just because it disappointed her.

下午，黎安陪顧問與羅恩走過故鄉街道。他們經過她童年第一次看大人跳舞的酒館，轉向河岸。一道陽光落在路面褪色的彩線上。羅恩能沿線追到舊郵局，但一處轉角已因施工封閉。顧問問他們是否打算重新開放。黎安遲疑後回答，要等新的檢查。這問題令人害怕，因為她深愛那個地方；但一項合理決定不會只因讓自己失望，就變得不合理。

The team spent the afternoon reading feedback from the festival. Some visitors wanted louder music, while others appreciated the quiet room. Several night-shift workers asked for classes at different hours. Lian did not promise to meet every request. She proposed three trial sessions with published times and a clear registration process. Cora suggested that one session be led by different instructors so the project would not depend on Lian alone. Ron added a maintenance schedule for the equipment. They had learned to separate a good idea from the resources required to carry it out. That distinction would help the new board decide what the community could sustain.

團隊下午閱讀節慶回饋。有些訪客想要更大音量，另一些人很欣賞安靜室。幾位夜班工人要求不同時段課程。黎安沒有承諾滿足所有要求，而是提出三場試辦課，公開時間並建立清楚報名流程。可拉建議其中一場由不同教師帶領，讓計畫不只依賴黎安。羅恩補上設備保養時程。大家學會把好主意與執行所需資源分開。這種區分將幫助新董事會決定社區能長久維持什麼。

In the early evening Lian and Ron sat beside the river. She told him she had been accepted into the choreography course in the capital. The class would meet two weekends each month, leaving time for her local work if the new board wanted her to continue. Ron congratulated her. He would leave for his lighting contract the following week. They discussed how often they hoped to speak, whether travel felt manageable, and what each needed when work became busy. Nothing was certain, but neither treated distance as proof the relationship had failed. Lian loved that they could talk about change without turning it into a test of loyalty.

傍晚，黎安與羅恩坐在河邊。她告訴他，自己獲錄取首都的編舞課程，每月上課兩個週末；若新董事會希望她繼續在地工作，時間仍可安排。羅恩恭喜她。他下週就要去外地做燈光合約。兩人談希望多常聯絡、往返是否可負擔，以及工作忙碌時各自需要什麼。未來並不確定，但誰都沒有把距離當作關係失敗的證明。黎安喜歡他們能談改變，而不把改變變成忠誠測驗。

Before they separated, Ron asked whether she would like to have dinner with him on his final night in town. Lian said yes, then pointed to the sketchbook in her lap. She was drawing a new piece about routes that cross and separate without disappearing. Ron watched the pencil move and said he hoped to see it one day. “When it is ready,” Lian replied. She did not feel pressure to show every unfinished thought. He understood. The first dance they had made together was no longer a single event, but a set of choices they could carry into other places.

分開前，羅恩問她離鎮前最後一晚願不願一起吃飯。黎安答應，接著指向膝上的速寫本。她正在畫一支新舞，關於道路交會、分開，卻不會因此消失。羅恩看著鉛筆移動，說希望有天能看到它。「等完成了再說。」黎安回答。她不再覺得有壓力要展示每個未完成的想法。他懂。兩人共同創作的第一支舞，不再只是一場活動，而是一套能帶去其他地方的選擇。
