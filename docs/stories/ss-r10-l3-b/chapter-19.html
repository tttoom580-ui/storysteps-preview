為演出投票

A Vote for the Show

把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 19 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

The trust met again the following week. Afterward, the audience would receive a public summary rather than a private announcement to selected friends. The chairperson approved the school gym as the festival location, subject to final safety checks. Outside the council hall a farmer led a young bull away from the parade route. A clown from the market waved at children, but the meeting inside remained serious. Lian had brought the original rehearsal plan and a new version showing every changed route. A photographer asked to stand near the door, and the chairperson confirmed where cameras were permitted. The day's performance was not on a stage. It was the public act of making a decision that people could examine. The final officer would still need to approve the technical conditions before opening night.

隔週，信託再次開會，之後會向所有觀眾公開摘要，而不是只私下通知幾位朋友。主席核准學校體育館作為節慶地點，條件是完成最後安全檢查。議會廳外，農夫牽著一頭年輕公牛離開遊行路線，市集小丑向孩子揮手，屋內會議卻十分嚴肅。黎安帶來原始排練計畫與標示所有路線更動的新版本。攝影師詢問能站在哪裡，主席確認允許拍攝的區域。今天的表演不在舞台上，而是讓眾人能檢視公共決策的過程。 開幕夜之前，最後一位主管人員仍須核准技術條件。

The first vote concerned the event deposit and required volunteer training. Ron explained that countable costs and uncertain estimates should appear in separate columns of the ledger. A missing item could make a cheap plan look better than it was. Cora showed feedback from the pilot audience and described the strong reaction to the revised entrance signs. The forms were truthful about failures as well as successes. Mrs. Wu asked for approval to continue the old hall's repairs through a separate fund. The trustees agreed to consider that request later, after a new inspection and public consultation. They would not use the festival's grant to cover unrelated building work. A sound test was designed to detect weak connections rather than to hide problems behind loud music.

第一項表決涉及活動訂金與必要志工訓練。羅恩解釋，可以逐項計算的成本與仍不確定的估價，應分開列在帳冊的不同欄位。漏掉一項，就會讓便宜方案看起來比實際更好。可拉展示試演觀眾的回饋，說明入口標示修改後得到的強烈反應。表格誠實記下成功與失敗。吳女士請求以獨立基金繼續修繕舊舞廳。董事們同意之後另行討論，待新檢查與公開諮詢完成再決定。他們不會挪用節慶補助支付無關的建物工程。 聲音測試是為了偵測薄弱接頭，而不是用大音量掩蓋問題。

The festival permit covered a modest parade from the school garden into the gym. A photographer would document it only from approved positions, and performers could opt out of individual images. Ron reviewed the stage plan with the crew while Lian checked every costume stitch on a sample outfit. Outside, volunteers stacked sacks of wheat for a harvest display, making sure the pile did not block public movement. Cora pointed out the different zone for quiet seating and asked that it be marked in large print. Her practical questions had become a normal part of the production, rather than a special favor. That change pleased her more than praise. A hollow prop column was placed well away from the passage, and a visitor who had lost her purse found it at the reception desk.

節慶許可涵蓋一段從學校花園進入體育館的小型遊行。攝影師只能從核准位置拍攝，表演者也可拒絕個人照片。羅恩與工作團隊檢查舞台圖，黎安則查看樣衣的每一道縫線。屋外，志工把小麥袋堆成收穫展區，確保不妨礙通行。可拉指出安靜座位的不同區域，要求使用大字標示。她的實務問題已成為製作流程的日常部分，而非特殊恩惠。這份改變比稱讚更讓她高興。 一根中空道具柱放在遠離通道的地方，一位遺失錢包的訪客則在接待處找回它。

When the vote ended, Lian expected to feel nothing but joy. Instead, she was exhausted. So much of the plan remained to be done. Ron brought her a bottle of water and said she had been holding her breath through the meeting. She laughed and admitted it was true. The trust secretary handed them the written conditions and asked for signatures. Lian read them slowly before signing for the artistic team; Ron signed separately for technical responsibilities. The arrangement made their roles visible to anyone who later questioned a decision. They had won permission to continue, not a promise that everything would now be easy.

表決結束時，黎安原以為自己只會高興，沒想到感覺非常疲憊。計畫還有許多事沒完成。羅恩拿水給她，說她整場會議都像憋著氣。她笑著承認是真的。信託秘書交出書面條件，請他們簽名。黎安仔細閱讀後，以藝術團隊代表身分簽署；羅恩則另外簽下技術責任。這樣的安排讓日後任何人質疑決策時，都能看見清楚分工。他們贏得的是繼續前進的許可，不是一切從此容易的保證。

Before leaving the council room, the commercial company representative approached Lian. Her business had not won the exclusive rental agreement, but she said the public comparison had helped her understand the town's goals. She offered to sponsor one small technical workshop without choosing the performers or controlling photographs. Lian thanked her and said the board would review the offer under its published rules. The representative accepted. Their earlier disagreements had not made either person disappear. The exchange gave Lian a new sense of what compromise could mean: not giving away an important right, but finding a smaller area where the two plans genuinely overlapped.

離開議會廳前，商業公司代表走近黎安。她的公司沒有取得獨家租約，但她說公開比較讓自己更了解小鎮目標。她提議贊助一場小型技術工作坊，不選演出者，也不控制照片。黎安道謝，說董事會會依公布規則審查。代表接受了。早先的分歧沒有讓任何人消失。這段對話讓黎安重新理解妥協：不是交出重要權利，而是在兩份方案真正重疊的較小範圍內合作。

That night Lian gathered the cast in the school gym to share the decision. She did not open with the word victory. Instead, she explained exactly what was approved and what might still delay the event. Cora spoke about the access conditions, and Ron described the final safety checks. The dancers applauded, then volunteered for the tasks they understood. After everyone left, Lian remained with the empty stage. The route through the garden was marked on the floor like a promise. She knew it could still change, but that no longer frightened her. She had learned to build plans that could withstand a necessary correction. The vote seemed to awaken new interest in classes that included people of different ages and abilities.

當晚，黎安召集舞者到學校體育館，分享決定。她沒有先喊勝利，而是清楚說明哪些已核准、哪些仍可能延誤。可拉講無障礙條件，羅恩說明最後安全測試。舞者鼓掌，接著依自己的能力認領工作。人散後，黎安獨自留在空舞台。花園通道像承諾般標在地上。她知道它仍可能變更，但已不再害怕。她學會建立可以承受必要修正的計畫。 這次表決似乎喚起人們對不同年齡和能力者共同參與課程的新興趣。
