A Post Without Permission

把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

After the weekend market, Lian noticed unfamiliar baggage stacked beside the dressing room. A street actor had left a buffalo mask and a toy bullet from a comedy performance. Ron moved them out of the exit path and filed a note. The electronic controller for event check-in sat near the boxes. A new volunteer wanted to throw the old props away, but Cora suggested asking the owner first. Downstairs a rice cooker was preparing crispy rice for the workers. An elderly citizen tried to grab a plate before the food had cooled; Lian gently asked him to wait. Small scenes like these reminded her that managing a public event required more than writing an attractive program.

週末市集過後，黎安發現更衣室旁堆著陌生的行李。一名街頭演員留下水牛面具，以及喜劇演出用的玩具子彈。羅恩把它們移離出口，並留下紀錄。活動報到的電子控制器放在箱子旁。一名新志工想把舊道具全扔掉，可拉建議先問物主。樓下，電鍋正替工人煮著酥脆米飯。一名年長居民急著抓盤子，食物卻還很燙；黎安溫柔請他稍等。這些小事提醒她，管理公共活動不只需要寫出好看的節目表。

Lately the community had been discussing the hall on the town web page. Someone posted a photograph of Cora rehearsing without asking her permission. The caption praised the club for helping a “brave girl,” although Cora was an adult artist who did not need rescue. The picture also showed litter on the floor and a loose cable, making the room look more dangerous than it was during the controlled test. Lian wrote a request for the page administrator to remove it while they checked what had happened. She did not yell at the unknown photographer. She told the team that the process mattered: people needed a way to report a problem and receive an answer.

最近，社區一直在鎮上的網頁討論舞廳。有人未經可拉同意就貼出她排練的照片，還誇獎社團幫助一位「勇敢女孩」，但可拉是成年藝術家，不需要別人拯救。照片也拍到地上的垃圾與鬆動電線，讓受控測試中的舞廳看起來比實際情況更危險。黎安寫信要求網頁管理員先撤照，再釐清事情。她沒有對不知名的拍攝者吼叫，而是告訴團隊程序很重要：人們需要能通報問題，也能得到回覆。

At a meeting the next day, a talkative sponsor offered to pay for publicity. He was a wealthy shop owner with a large following, but he insisted on choosing the photographs himself. Cora asked for a written agreement saying performers could approve images of their own bodies. The sponsor looked offended. Lian explained that consent was not a gift the club could sell. The group also added a rule against sexual jokes and unwanted touching during rehearsal. Such behavior could make people feel unsafe even when the stage itself passed inspection. The sponsor left without signing, and the room became quiet. Losing the offer hurt the budget, but accepting it would have hurt trust.

隔天的會議上，一名健談的贊助商提議負擔宣傳費。他是追蹤者很多的富裕店主，卻堅持自行選照片。可拉要求書面協議，確認表演者能審核涉及自己身體的影像。贊助商不高興。黎安說明，同意權不是社團可以轉賣的禮物。團隊也加入規定，禁止排練時的性暗示玩笑與未經同意的碰觸。即使舞台通過檢查，這些行為仍會讓人不安全。贊助商沒有簽字就離開，房間安靜下來。失去贊助讓預算難受，但接受條件會傷害信任。

Ron found a way to replace part of the promised money by borrowing lamps from a school, with a proper agreement about damage and return dates. He said the group owed the school careful use, not endless thanks. Lian asked the dancers to review a simple picture permission form written in everyday language. Every participant could choose public photographs, limited internal records, or no photographs at all. A shadow crossed the wall as afternoon clouds gathered, and Cora asked whether the forms could also cover live streaming. Lian added the question to the next agenda. No one had thought of that detail until the people being filmed raised it. The club would owe the school the full cost of replacing any lamp it damaged through careless use.

羅恩找到方法，依正式協議向學校借燈具，部分補足失去的贊助，並清楚列出損壞責任與歸還日期。他說團隊欠學校的是小心使用，而不是無止盡道謝。黎安請舞者審閱一份用日常語言寫的拍攝同意書。參與者可選擇公開照片、有限的內部紀錄，或完全不被拍攝。午後烏雲聚集，牆上掠過陰影，可拉問表格是否也涵蓋直播。黎安把問題加進下次議程。在真正被拍攝的人提出前，沒有人想到這個細節。 如果社團因粗心而損壞燈具，就必須負擔向學校賠償的全部費用。

That evening the unknown photographer sent an apology to Cora and explained that he had mistaken the rehearsal for a public demonstration. She accepted the apology but did not agree to restore the image online. Lian respected her decision. Ron told Lian he admired how she had handled the sponsor, although he worried about their remaining costs. She admitted the same worry. They walked along the market road without pretending the loss was easy. At the bridge he asked whether she would like coffee on Saturday, outside any planning meeting. She smiled and accepted, adding that neither of them should talk the other into something merely because they worked together.

當晚，拍照者向可拉道歉，說自己誤以為那是公開示範。可拉接受道歉，卻不同意照片重新上網。黎安尊重她的決定。羅恩告訴黎安，他欣賞她處理贊助商的方式，卻擔心剩下的經費。她承認自己也擔心。兩人沿市場道路散步，沒有假裝損失容易承受。走到橋上，他問她星期六願不願在工作會議之外單獨喝咖啡。她笑著答應，並補上一句：兩人都不該因為一起工作，就勉強對方答應任何事。

Before bed Lian reviewed the consent form one last time. It did not solve every problem; there would still be difficult choices about groups and backgrounds in photographs. Yet it gave participants a starting point for those conversations. She placed the rejected sponsor proposal in the archive with the date and reason clearly written. Future leaders could learn from it without needing to discover the same problem the hard way. Tomorrow they would return to rehearsals, and Cora would help choose camera positions. Lian realized that respect could change the artwork itself, because people moved differently when they knew their choices mattered.

睡前，黎安最後一次檢查同意書。它不能解決所有問題，團體照片裡誰在背景仍可能需要艱難選擇，但至少提供討論的起點。她把遭拒絕的贊助提案存檔，註明日期與原因。未來的管理者可以從中學習，不必再用同樣痛苦的方式重蹈覆轍。明天他們要回到排練，可拉會一起決定拍攝位置。黎安發現尊重也會改變作品本身，因為當人們知道自己的選擇有重量，身體移動的方式也會不一樣。
