舞步之間的空間

The Space Between Steps

把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 22 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first section began like a quiet adventure. Dancers moved beneath a line of hanging lamps, then formed a wide circle without blocking either exit. A soft recording of a cricket filled the room, followed by the distant sound of a donkey from the hillside. A child in the audience made a small fist of excitement, but his father reminded him to keep his hands close to himself. Behind the curtain, Ron checked the approved straps that bind the lighting stands to their safety points. He had labeled each connection so another technician could understand the setup. The music was simple, but the movement made ordinary sounds feel like part of a shared landscape.

第一段像安靜的冒險般開始。舞者走到一排吊燈下方，再圍成寬大的圓圈，沒有擋住任何出口。室內響起蟋蟀的輕柔錄音，接著是山坡遠處驢子的叫聲。一個孩子興奮地握緊小拳頭，父親提醒他把手留在自己身邊。布幕後，羅恩檢查把燈架綁在安全固定點的核准固定帶。他清楚標記每個接頭，讓其他技師也看得懂。音樂很簡單，動作卻讓平凡聲響成為共同風景的一部分。

In the second section, Cora moved through an open lane while other dancers turned in pairs. A forgotten coat hanger near the door caught Ron's eye, and he quietly asked a volunteer to remove it before anyone walked there. The movement on stage continued, because the route remained safe. The scene's words came from an old tale in which a lord asks people to cross a river. Lian had rewritten the story so no ruler could demand that someone take a dangerous path. An oral narrator described choices, not commands. Cora's presence changed the scene's meaning: people did not need permission from a powerful figure to choose a safe route. Meanwhile, a teacher stood near the side door to check whether the audience route stayed clear.

第二段裡，可拉沿開放動線前進，其他舞者兩兩旋轉。門邊一個被遺忘的衣架引起羅恩注意，他安靜請志工移走，免得有人經過時絆倒。舞台動作繼續，因為通道本身仍安全。這段台詞改編自一則古老故事：領主要求人們渡河。黎安重新寫過故事，讓任何統治者都不能命令人走危險路線。口述旁白描述選擇，而非命令。可拉的存在改變了這段意義：人們不必等待掌權者批准，才能選安全的路。 同時，一位老師守在側門附近，查看觀眾動線是否保持暢通。

During a short interval the school health team shared educational material about organ donation, including a diagram of the kidney. Lian had asked that the table remain outside the performance area and that information be offered without pressure. Nearby, a volunteer reserved seats for visitors who needed more space, while another checked that a shallow step had a bright edge marker. A young performer teased her friend about forgetting a turn, then stopped when she saw the friend looked upset. She apologized and offered to practice together later. The ordinary exchange reminded Lian that respect was made of small corrections, not only rules printed on a wall. An organizer entered the seat reservations on a tablet, but kept a paper copy as proof of the agreed capacity.

短暫休息時，學校健康團隊提供器官捐贈的教育資料，其中有腎臟示意圖。黎安要求桌位設在表演區外，資訊應不帶壓力地提供。附近一位志工為需要更多空間的訪客保留座位，另一位確認淺階梯邊緣有鮮明標記。一位年輕舞者拿朋友忘記轉身開玩笑，看到對方難過便停下，道歉並提議之後一起練習。這段平凡互動提醒黎安，尊重由一次次小小修正組成，不只靠牆上的規則。 主辦人用平板電腦記錄座位預約，也保留紙本當作約定容量的證據。

The last section required dancers to toss soft pieces of cloth into the air and catch them again. It looked easy, but the timing was tricky when people moved at different speeds. Cora suggested a reasonable change: each person could choose the height of the toss, while the group would still meet on the same final count. Lian accepted it. The result was more varied and more beautiful than a line of identical movements. The audience watched the colored cloth rise and fall like leaves. Ron later told Lian that he could see the work of many people in the scene, not just a director's single idea. They would reserve the front path for anyone who needed extra space, and nobody should tease those who used it.

最後一段要舞者把柔軟布片拋向空中，再接住。動作看似容易，但每個人速度不同，節拍就很難抓。可拉提出合理修改：每人自行選擇拋起的高度，大家仍在同一個最後拍點相會。黎安接受了。成果比整齊劃一的動作更多變，也更美。觀眾看著彩色布片像葉子般升降。羅恩後來說，他在這段看見很多人的努力，而不只是導演一個人的想法。 他們會保留前方通道給需要更多空間的人，任何人都不該取笑使用者。

When the dancers bowed, applause filled the gym. Lian felt grateful, but she did not ask anyone to declare the event perfect. The audience would receive feedback cards and an invitation to a later discussion. One spectator asked whether the program could be repeated next year. Lian answered that the group would review costs and safety results before committing to a schedule. A good experience did not remove the need for another inspection. Cora accepted congratulations for her choreography and introduced the dancers who had helped shape it. She was proud that the audience recognized her as an artist whose choices mattered.

舞者鞠躬時，掌聲充滿體育館。黎安感激，卻沒有請任何人宣稱活動完美。觀眾會收到回饋卡，以及稍後參與討論的邀請。一名觀眾問明年能否再辦。黎安回答，團隊必須先檢查成本與安全結果，再承諾時間表。一次美好經驗不能省去下一次檢查。可拉接受大家對編舞的祝賀，也介紹共同塑造作品的舞者。她很驕傲，因為觀眾看見她是一位選擇有重量的藝術家。

After the audience left, Ron and Lian folded chairs together. She thanked him for staying calm during the earlier power problem. He said his calm had come from the team's preparation, not from being unusually brave. Lian admitted she had enjoyed watching the show without needing to direct every second of it. For a long moment they stood in the quiet hall, listening to the faint sound of people talking outside. Ron reached for her hand, then waited until she reached back. She did. The contact was gentle, an answer freely given. Neither needed to speak over the silence to make it important.

觀眾離開後，羅恩和黎安一起摺椅子。她感謝他先前電力出問題時保持冷靜。他說冷靜來自團隊準備，而非自己特別勇敢。黎安承認，她很享受不用控制每一秒就能觀看演出。兩人在安靜的廳裡站了一會，聽外面人們遠遠的談話聲。羅恩伸出手，等她也伸過來。她握住了。那接觸很輕柔，是自願給出的回答。兩人都不需要打破沉默，才能讓這一刻重要。
