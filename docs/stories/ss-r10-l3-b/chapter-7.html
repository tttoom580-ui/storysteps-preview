把地板留給每一種舞步 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

The appeal for volunteers brought an awful amount of paperwork. People offered skills, clothes, and old props, but the group needed to know what could be used safely. Lian felt awkward when she had to refuse an expensive-looking donation that lacked a maker's label. Ron carried a brick to hold the side door while the cleaner moved a cupboard away from the exit. A visitor placed a dime in a jar marked “support the dancers.” Lian thanked him, then explained that donations would go through the trust's official account. A jar of coins could inspire kindness, but it could not replace a clear record of wages or expenses.

招募志工的呼籲帶來大量繁瑣文件。人們捐出技能、衣物和舊道具，但團隊得先知道哪些能安全使用。黎安不得不婉拒一件看來昂貴、卻沒有製造商標籤的物品，心裡有些尷尬。羅恩用一塊磚頭暫時固定側門，好讓清潔人員移開堵在出口旁的櫥櫃。一名訪客往寫著「支持舞者」的罐子投了一枚一角硬幣。黎安向他道謝，也說明捐款必須進入信託正式帳戶。零錢罐能表達善意，卻不能取代清楚的薪資與支出紀錄。

During the first emergency drill, a volunteer announced that the stage lights might explode if anyone touched the old switch. Ron stopped the rumor and showed the manufacturer's warning: overheating was a risk, but the word explode had not appeared in the report. The group needed accurate language. After lunch, an artist offered to invent a new flavor of fruit drink for opening night. Cora said refreshments could wait; they had not yet finished the exit test. A boy helping at the market cut the flesh of an orange into slices and offered them to the cleaners. The ordinary kindness eased the tension without making the problem disappear.

第一次緊急演練時，有志工宣稱碰到舊開關，舞台燈就可能爆炸。羅恩阻止謠言，展示製造商警告：過熱確實有風險，但報告沒有使用「爆炸」一詞。團隊需要精準用語。午餐後，一位藝術家提議為開幕夜發明新口味果汁。可拉說飲料可以等，出口測試還沒完成。市場裡幫忙的一個男孩把柳橙果肉切片，分給清潔人員。平凡的善意緩和緊張，卻沒有讓問題憑空消失。

At the property store, Lian found a cracked jar, a box of junk cables, a drawing of a lily, and three clean vases. She kept the drawing because it belonged to a dance made by the first teacher; the other items needed individual checks. Ron reminded her that a donated object was not automatically useful. “Sometime this week we may have to say no, even when a person wants to help,” he said. Cora suggested letting volunteers choose tasks with steady hours rather than calling them whenever a crisis appeared. The plan was terrific in principle, but it depended on someone keeping a schedule. Lian agreed to do that administrative work instead of leaving it invisible. Ron asked Lian to remind him to return a borrowed vase after the exhibition, and she added the task to her list.

在道具倉庫裡，黎安找到破裂的玻璃罐、一箱雜亂電纜、一張百合花的畫和三只乾淨花瓶。她留下畫，因為那是第一任老師創作舞蹈時的道具；其他物品需要逐件檢查。羅恩提醒，受贈物不一定就有用。「本週某個時候，即使別人想幫忙，我們可能也得說不。」他說。可拉建議安排穩定時段讓志工自行選擇工作，而不是只在危機發生時才叫人來。構想很棒，但需要有人維持排班表。黎安答應自己處理那份容易被忽略的行政工作。 羅恩請黎安提醒他在展覽後歸還借來的花瓶，她把事情加進清單。

The question of wages caused a difficult meeting. Two volunteers believed everyone should work for free until the hall was saved. A professional instructor asked whether they would also expect an electrician to repair the roof without payment. The room fell silent. Lian proposed a division: volunteers could choose limited support tasks, while specialist work and teaching would be paid at published rates. Nobody would be punished for declining either kind of role. Cora asked that access planning count as specialist work, not a favor. The others agreed. The policy made the project more expensive, but it also made their promises easier to trust. They published a minimum hourly wage for paid specialist tasks, so no one would need to guess about compensation.

薪資問題引發一場難談的會議。兩名志工認為舞廳救回來以前，每個人都應該無償工作。一位專業教師問，他們是否也期待電工免費修屋頂。房間突然安靜。黎安提出區分：志工可自行選擇有限的支援工作，專業工程與教學則依公開費率支付。不管拒絕哪一類角色，都不該受罰。可拉要求把無障礙規劃列為專業工作，而不是人情幫忙。大家同意了。政策讓計畫更昂貴，也讓承諾更值得信賴。 他們公布專業有薪工作的最低時薪，讓任何人都不必猜測報酬。

As dusk fell, Ron tested the sound system at low volume. A light melody filled the hall. Lian demonstrated a step that could be danced standing or seated, then asked Cora whether the movement made sense to her. Cora changed the timing and showed a stronger version. The other dancers tried both, choosing their preferred way. They were no longer making one original dance with a second version added for someone else. They were building one work from several kinds of movement. Lian watched the group and began to understand how much authorship could be shared without losing the shape of an artistic idea.

黃昏時，羅恩用低音量測試音響，柔和的旋律填滿舞廳。黎安示範一組可以站著或坐著完成的步子，再問可拉這個動作對她是否合理。可拉改了節奏，做出更有力量的版本。其他舞者兩種都試過，自行選擇喜歡的方式。他們不再是先創作一支舞，再為某個人加上另一版，而是從多種動作一起建立同一部作品。黎安看著大家，開始了解作者身分可以共享到什麼程度，而不會失去創作想法的形狀。

On the way home Lian found a folded receipt beneath her coat. It showed the first small payment to Cora for her design work, properly signed and recorded. She remembered the dime in the jar and smiled at the distance between a kind gesture and a responsible system. Ron asked whether she had considered naming the new show. “Not yet,” she said. “I want the people who will dance in it to help.” He admired the answer and did not press. The hall was still uncertain, but the group was learning how to make decisions together. That was a kind of performance they could practice every day.

回家路上，黎安從外套下找到一張摺好的收據，上面記錄著支付給可拉的第一筆設計酬勞，簽名與帳目都完整。她想起零錢罐裡的一角硬幣，笑著想起善意手勢與負責任制度之間的距離。羅恩問她想過節目名稱沒有。「還沒，」她說，「我想讓真正會跳舞的人一起決定。」他欣賞這個回答，沒有繼續催問。舞廳的命運仍不確定，但團隊正在學習共同決策。那也是一種每天都能練習的表演。
