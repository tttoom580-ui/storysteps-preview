The friends see fresh yellow paint beside a wooden castle in the drama room. The three young detectives had one rule before they began: a person was not a clue just because somebody whispered their name. Maya liked drawing arrows on a map, but an arrow meant nothing unless the place on either end could be checked. Jun carried a notebook, and their third friend looked for changes in ordinary objects. They were not trying to make the case look like a movie. They wanted the trophy found and the people at school treated fairly. That seemed a harder kind of courage than making a wild accusation. "We can arrive when the others are ready," Maya suggested. Before leaving, Maya checked the open list again. Maya remembered a drawing of boat seen earlier that week. Someone had asked Jun a question about box. Somebody had packed coffee for a short break later.

三人在戲劇教室木城堡旁看見未乾的黃漆。 三名小偵探開始前就訂下一條規則：有人低聲說出某個名字，不代表那個人就是線索。瑪雅喜歡在地圖上畫箭頭，但若兩端地點無法核對，箭頭就沒有意義。阿俊帶著筆記本，第三位朋友負責觀察平常物品的變化。他們不是想把案件演得像電影，而是希望找回獎盃，也讓校園裡的人受到公平對待。這比亂指責別人更需要勇氣。 「等其他人準備好，我們就能抵達。」瑪雅提議。離開之前，瑪雅又核對公開清單。瑪雅想起本週稍早看到的船圖畫。有人曾問阿俊關於箱子的問題。有人為稍後的休息準備了咖啡。

A drama student explained that the stage crew painted there all week. The information was small enough to overlook. Maya nearly moved on, but Jun wanted to know what it meant for the people involved. They talked through two possibilities, then checked which parts could actually be supported. In the school, other things were happening at the same time; it would have been easy to mix them together. 'Let's keep these separate,' Maya said. One careful question led to another, and the situation became clearer without anyone needing to raise a voice or make a dramatic accusation. The open book contained a short story about drive. Everyone agreed except one friend who needed more time. Finally, the last note was put into its proper place. A free chair was left for the next person. They talked about the house near the entrance and how to keep it safe.

戲劇社學生說舞台組整週都在此處上漆。 這項資訊很小，幾乎會被忽略。瑪雅正想往下走，阿俊卻想知道它對相關的人代表什麼。他們討論兩種可能，再確認哪些有證據支持。學校同時還發生其他事，很容易混淆。「先把它們分開。」瑪雅說。仔細的一問接著一問，不必大聲說話或戲劇性指控，情況也漸漸清楚。 翻開的書有一則談到開車的小故事。除了還需要時間的一位朋友，大家都同意。最後，最後一張紙也放回正確位置。留下一張空椅子給下一個人。他們討論入口附近的房屋，以及如何保持安全。

Maya checked whether the powder from the case looked the same in daylight. The next step sounded small when Maya said it aloud. That was a good sign. A clear action was easier to try than a large promise that no one understood. Jun repeated the plan to make sure both had heard it in the same way. When one part sounded unfair, they changed the wording. The plan left space for a person to ask a question or take a break. Maya noticed a quiet sense of relief. The problem remained real, but it no longer needed to be faced through shouting, rushing, or guessing about another person's feelings. A drawing of a lion hung on the classroom notice board. "Can you see the small mark on this page?" Maya asked. A small poster on the wall included the word shape. The open book contained a short story about sign. "Calling someone stupid will not solve our problem," Maya said.

瑪雅在日光下比對兩處粉末是否相同。 瑪雅把下一步說出口時，聽起來只是一件小事。這反而是好現象。明確行動比大家聽不懂的大承諾更容易嘗試。阿俊重複計畫，確認兩人理解相同。有一部分聽來不公平，他們便改了說法。計畫也讓人能提問和休息。瑪雅感到些許輕鬆。問題依然存在，卻不必靠吵鬧、急躁或猜測別人的心思來面對。 教室公告欄上掛著一張獅子的圖畫。「你看得見這頁的小記號嗎？」瑪雅問。牆上的小海報寫著形狀這個字。翻開的書有一則談到標誌的小故事。「說別人笨也不會解決問題。」瑪雅說。

The colors were different, so they cross out their first dramatic theory. By the time the hall grew quiet, the friends had written more questions than answers. Maya once would have been disappointed by such a page. Now the empty spaces felt like places where a later fact could fit. Jun reminded everyone not to take a private object home or search a cupboard without permission. Their third friend checked the school map one last time. The trophy was still the reason for the work, but their way of doing the work had become important too. They would not trade fairness for a faster ending. "That would be a terrible way to treat a friend," Jun said. The open book contained a short story about top. Maya remembered a drawing of uncle seen earlier that week. The class reading card used wave in an example sentence. A small poster on the wall included the word weekend.

顏色並不相同，他們劃掉第一個戲劇性的猜測。 大廳安靜下來時，朋友們寫下的問題仍比答案多。以前瑪雅會失望，現在空白反而像是留給新事實的位置。阿俊提醒大家不要擅自把私人東西帶回家，也不要未經允許搜查櫃子。第三位朋友最後查看校園地圖。獎盃仍是調查的原因，但調查方法也變得重要。他們不會為了早點結案而犧牲公平。 「那樣對待朋友太糟糕了。」阿俊說。翻開的書有一則談到頂端的小故事。瑪雅想起本週稍早看到的舅舅圖畫。課堂閱讀卡用揮手造了一個例句。牆上的小海報寫著週末這個字。

Before leaving, Maya gave the day one last careful look. It had contained a surprise, a question, a decision, and a result. Those parts did not always arrive in the order people wanted. Jun asked whether they were ready for tomorrow. 'Ready to listen,' Maya answered, 'even if tomorrow changes the plan.' The reply was small, but it was true. They put away the things they had used, thanked the people nearby, and made sure nobody was left expecting a promise that had never been agreed to.

離開前，瑪雅最後仔細回顧這一天：有驚喜、問題、決定，也有結果。事情不一定照人希望的順序發生。阿俊問明天準備好了嗎？「準備好傾聽了，」瑪雅回答，「即使明天又要改計畫。」這句話很簡單，卻是真的。他們收拾用品、謝謝附近的人，也確認沒有讓誰期待一個從未答應的承諾。

On the way home, Maya kept thinking about one small moment from the day. It was not necessarily the loudest moment. Sometimes the most important change happened when somebody listened, stopped, or asked one more question. Jun waved goodbye at the corner. Maya waved back and felt ready to begin again when the next problem came. The story had moved forward, not because every difficulty was gone, but because they now knew something they had not known in the morning.

回家路上，瑪雅一直想著今天的一個小片刻。那不一定是最熱鬧的一刻。有時真正重要的改變，就發生在有人願意聽、停下來，或多問一句時。阿俊在轉角揮手再見，瑪雅也揮手回應，覺得下次遇到問題時還能重新開始。故事之所以前進，不是因為困難全消失了，而是因為他們比早上多懂了一件事。
