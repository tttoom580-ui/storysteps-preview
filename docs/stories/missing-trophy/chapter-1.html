The Empty Glass Case

小鎮偵探與消失的冠軍獎盃 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Maya arrived at school and found the championship trophy missing from its locked display case. Sunlight reached the floor of the school corridor in long shapes. It showed dust, scratches, and the marks left by many ordinary shoes. Maya had to remember that not every mark was new and not every new mark belonged to the case. Jun asked a question, and the third friend offered a different answer. They wrote down both without deciding which sounded more exciting. The trophy was important, but no object was worth hurting a person with a careless rumor. That thought guided Maya when another student walked past and stopped to look at their notes. The small clock read 9 a.m. when Maya checked it. A bell rang once in the hall, marking the next part of the day. Maya remembered the breakfast that had helped the morning begin. A friend promised to bring a small cake for the break. Maya thought of the soft couch at home and wished for a rest.

瑪雅到校後發現冠軍獎盃從上鎖展示櫃裡消失了。 陽光在學校走廊地板上拉出長長的形狀，照見灰塵、刮痕和許多普通鞋印。瑪雅提醒自己，不是每個記號都是新的，也不是每個新記號都和案件有關。阿俊提出問題，第三位朋友又給出不同答案。他們都記下來，沒有選比較刺激的那個。獎盃很重要，卻沒有任何物品值得用不小心的流言傷害人。另一位學生經過、看向筆記時，瑪雅仍記著這件事。 瑪雅看鐘時，時刻是上午九點。大廳響起一聲鈴，提醒大家進入下一個時段。瑪雅想起幫自己開始一天的早餐。一位朋友答應休息時帶來小蛋糕。瑪雅想起家裡柔軟的沙發，真想休息一下。

A small white square of paper lay where the trophy's base used to stand. The information was small enough to overlook. Maya nearly moved on, but Jun wanted to know what it meant for the people involved. They talked through two possibilities, then checked which parts could actually be supported. In the school, other things were happening at the same time; it would have been easy to mix them together. 'Let's keep these separate,' Maya said. One careful question led to another, and the situation became clearer without anyone needing to raise a voice or make a dramatic accusation. A clean cover lay folded beside the shared equipment. Maya arrived early and waited beside the entrance. A short exercise helped everyone warm up and focus. A foreign visitor asked for a simple map. Fresh fruit waited in a bag for the afternoon break.

原本放獎盃底座的位置只剩一張白色小紙。 這項資訊很小，幾乎會被忽略。瑪雅正想往下走，阿俊卻想知道它對相關的人代表什麼。他們討論兩種可能，再確認哪些有證據支持。學校同時還發生其他事，很容易混淆。「先把它們分開。」瑪雅說。仔細的一問接著一問，不必大聲說話或戲劇性指控，情況也漸漸清楚。 共用器材旁整齊摺著一塊乾淨的罩布。瑪雅提早抵達，在入口旁等候。短短的運動讓大家暖身，也比較能集中精神。一位外國訪客詢問簡單地圖。袋子裡放著下午休息時要吃的新鮮水果。

She asked her friends Jun and Tao to help note facts without accusing classmates. The next step sounded small when Maya said it aloud. That was a good sign. A clear action was easier to try than a large promise that no one understood. Jun repeated the plan to make sure both had heard it in the same way. When one part sounded unfair, they changed the wording. The plan left space for a person to ask a question or take a break. Maya noticed a quiet sense of relief. The problem remained real, but it no longer needed to be faced through shouting, rushing, or guessing about another person's feelings. "He has a part in this too," Maya told Jun. The heavy bag was moved slowly to avoid an accident. Maya thought about a hobby that had taught patience. A cartoon monkey smiled from a child’s paper sticker. A teammate had packed salad in a lunch box.

她請阿俊和小桃幫忙記錄事實，不隨便指控同學。 瑪雅把下一步說出口時，聽起來只是一件小事。這反而是好現象。明確行動比大家聽不懂的大承諾更容易嘗試。阿俊重複計畫，確認兩人理解相同。有一部分聽來不公平，他們便改了說法。計畫也讓人能提問和休息。瑪雅感到些許輕鬆。問題依然存在，卻不必靠吵鬧、急躁或猜測別人的心思來面對。 「他也參與了這件事。」瑪雅告訴阿俊。沉重的袋子被緩緩搬開，避免意外。瑪雅想起一項曾教會自己耐心的興趣。孩子的紙貼紙上有一隻微笑的卡通猴子。一名隊友把沙拉裝進午餐盒。

Their first list held only three facts: a locked case, a missing cup, and a paper square. One of the children pointed to the trophy's empty place and asked what it would feel like when the cup came back. Maya did not have a ready answer. Finding an object and understanding why it disappeared were different tasks. Jun spoke of the people whose names were written on the old base, people who had once trained and played together. They deserved care in the way the story was told. Maya closed the notebook gently. When the answer arrived, it would need to be a true one, even if it was quieter than everyone expected. "Shall we ask before we decide?" Maya suggested. The sun came through the high window and warmed the floor. "Could you teach me that step?" Maya asked Jun. A child’s drawing showed a white tooth and a smiling face. Maya used a pen to write the result in the shared notebook.

第一張清單只有三項事實：上鎖的櫃子、不見的獎盃、一張白紙。 其中一個孩子指著獎盃原本的空位，問它回來時會有什麼感覺。瑪雅沒有現成答案。找回物品和理解失蹤原因是兩件事。阿俊談起刻在老底座上的隊員名字，那些人曾一起練習與比賽。說出這段故事時也要照顧他們的心血。瑪雅輕輕合上筆記本。真正的答案到來時，必須是真實的，即使它比大家想像的平淡。 「我們先問清楚再決定，好嗎？」瑪雅提議。陽光穿過高窗，溫暖了地板。「你能教我那個步驟嗎？」瑪雅問阿俊。孩子的畫上有一顆白牙和一張笑臉。瑪雅用筆把結果寫進共用筆記本。

Before leaving, Maya gave the day one last careful look. It had contained a surprise, a question, a decision, and a result. Those parts did not always arrive in the order people wanted. Jun asked whether they were ready for tomorrow. 'Ready to listen,' Maya answered, 'even if tomorrow changes the plan.' The reply was small, but it was true. They put away the things they had used, thanked the people nearby, and made sure nobody was left expecting a promise that had never been agreed to.

離開前，瑪雅最後仔細回顧這一天：有驚喜、問題、決定，也有結果。事情不一定照人希望的順序發生。阿俊問明天準備好了嗎？「準備好傾聽了，」瑪雅回答，「即使明天又要改計畫。」這句話很簡單，卻是真的。他們收拾用品、謝謝附近的人，也確認沒有讓誰期待一個從未答應的承諾。

On the way home, Maya kept thinking about one small moment from the day. It was not necessarily the loudest moment. Sometimes the most important change happened when somebody listened, stopped, or asked one more question. Jun waved goodbye at the corner. Maya waved back and felt ready to begin again when the next problem came. The story had moved forward, not because every difficulty was gone, but because they now knew something they had not known in the morning.

回家路上，瑪雅一直想著今天的一個小片刻。那不一定是最熱鬧的一刻。有時真正重要的改變，就發生在有人願意聽、停下來，或多問一句時。阿俊在轉角揮手再見，瑪雅也揮手回應，覺得下次遇到問題時還能重新開始。故事之所以前進，不是因為困難全消失了，而是因為他們比早上多懂了一件事。
