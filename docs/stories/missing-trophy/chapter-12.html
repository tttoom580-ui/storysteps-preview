Rain closed the sports field and forces the school fair indoors. The three young detectives had one rule before they began: a person was not a clue just because somebody whispered their name. Maya liked drawing arrows on a map, but an arrow meant nothing unless the place on either end could be checked. Jun carried a notebook, and their third friend looked for changes in ordinary objects. They were not trying to make the case look like a movie. They wanted the trophy found and the people at school treated fairly. That seemed a harder kind of courage than making a wild accusation. A small poster on the wall included the word answer. Maya remembered a drawing of bicycle seen earlier that week. The class reading card used body in an example sentence. Maya remembered a drawing of bow seen earlier that week. Someone had asked Jun a question about jeans.

下雨讓操場關閉，園遊會只好搬進室內。 三名小偵探開始前就訂下一條規則：有人低聲說出某個名字，不代表那個人就是線索。瑪雅喜歡在地圖上畫箭頭，但若兩端地點無法核對，箭頭就沒有意義。阿俊帶著筆記本，第三位朋友負責觀察平常物品的變化。他們不是想把案件演得像電影，而是希望找回獎盃，也讓校園裡的人受到公平對待。這比亂指責別人更需要勇氣。 牆上的小海報寫著答案這個字。瑪雅想起本週稍早看到的自行車圖畫。課堂閱讀卡用身體造了一個例句。瑪雅想起本週稍早看到的鞠躬圖畫。有人曾問阿俊關於牛仔褲的問題。

A wet shoe mark appeared near the side entrance to the repair corridor. For several seconds, nobody spoke. Maya was thinking about the new fact and the way it touched earlier choices. Jun drew a short line under the important part of the notes. They agreed that a record was useful only if it was clear enough to correct. If a number, name, or time was uncertain, they marked it as uncertain. That extra care took a little longer, but it protected people who were not in the room to explain themselves. At last, Maya could see a practical question worth asking next. A notice near the entrance mentioned language. The open book contained a short story about mine. A piece of paper lay beside the book. Maya said the word own in a careful answer. Maya remembered a drawing of pick seen earlier that week.

維修走廊側門附近出現一個濕鞋印。 好幾秒沒有人開口。瑪雅思考新事實與先前選擇的關係。阿俊在筆記重要處畫了短線。他們同意紀錄要清楚，才方便日後更正。數字、姓名或時間若不確定，就標明不確定。這樣雖然多花時間，卻能保護不在場、無法替自己說話的人。最後瑪雅終於想到下一個值得問的實際問題。 入口附近的告示提到語言。翻開的書有一則談到我的的小故事。書旁放著一張紙。瑪雅仔細回答時說出了「自己的」。瑪雅想起本週稍早看到的挑選圖畫。

Jun measures the mark with the caretaker present and drew its shape. Making a choice was harder than making a wish. Maya could imagine a quick solution, but a real plan needed to fit the time, the place, and the people taking part. Jun listened while Maya explained the steps aloud. They stopped at one unclear part, changed it, and started again. 'If somebody says no, we need another way,' Maya said. Jun agreed that helping was not the same as deciding for others. Together they wrote a short plan anyone involved could question, improve, or leave if it was not right for them. Maya remembered a drawing of planet seen earlier that week. Somebody had packed salt for a short break later. "What would you like to say?" Jun asked. A small poster on the wall included the word second. Maya felt unsure, so Jun suggested a small test.

阿俊在校工陪同下量尺寸、畫下形狀。 做選擇比許願難。瑪雅可以想像快速解法，但真正的計畫必須配合時間、地點與參與者。阿俊聽瑪雅逐步說明，在不清楚的地方停下修改，再重新開始。「如果有人說不，我們要有其他辦法。」瑪雅說。阿俊同意，幫忙不等於替別人做決定。他們一起寫下簡明計畫，讓相關的人都能提問、改善或選擇退出。 瑪雅想起本週稍早看到的星球圖畫。有人為稍後的休息準備了鹽。「你想說什麼呢？」阿俊問。牆上的小海報寫著第二個這個字。瑪雅不太確定，所以阿俊提議小規模測試。

The mark was too common to name a person, but it showed which door was used. By the time the hall grew quiet, the friends had written more questions than answers. Maya once would have been disappointed by such a page. Now the empty spaces felt like places where a later fact could fit. Jun reminded everyone not to take a private object home or search a cupboard without permission. Their third friend checked the school map one last time. The trophy was still the reason for the work, but their way of doing the work had become important too. They would not trade fairness for a faster ending. Maya said the word some in a careful answer. The next meeting would begin soon, so they checked the time. The open book contained a short story about spring. A small poster on the wall included the word wind. The class reading card used wish in an example sentence.

鞋印太普通，無法判定是誰，卻指出有人用過哪道門。 大廳安靜下來時，朋友們寫下的問題仍比答案多。以前瑪雅會失望，現在空白反而像是留給新事實的位置。阿俊提醒大家不要擅自把私人東西帶回家，也不要未經允許搜查櫃子。第三位朋友最後查看校園地圖。獎盃仍是調查的原因，但調查方法也變得重要。他們不會為了早點結案而犧牲公平。 瑪雅仔細回答時說出了「一些」。下一場會議很快要開始，他們先看時間。翻開的書有一則談到春天的小故事。牆上的小海報寫著風這個字。課堂閱讀卡用希望造了一個例句。

After the busy part ended, Maya and Jun found a quiet minute. There were still things they wanted to say, yet neither felt a need to win the conversation. Maya asked what had been most difficult. Jun answered honestly and then asked the same question back. They were surprised to hear two different answers. That did not make either one wrong. The talk helped them see that working together did not mean thinking exactly alike. It meant taking those differences seriously enough to make a plan both could understand.

忙碌告一段落後，瑪雅和阿俊有了片刻安靜。他們還有話想說，卻不必在對話中爭贏。瑪雅問最困難的是哪部分。阿俊誠實回答，也反問同一個問題。兩人的答案竟然不同，卻不代表誰錯了。這場談話讓他們理解，合作不必想法完全一致，而是認真看待差異，做出雙方都能理解的計畫。

When Maya turned the last page of the notes, one line stood out: people deserve the chance to explain their own choices. It was easy to forget that in a busy day, especially when everyone wanted a quick ending. Jun had reminded Maya more than once to keep an open mind. The lesson was not that people should wait forever. It was that acting with care usually made the next action safer and fairer. Maya closed the book and made a quiet plan to remember that tomorrow.

瑪雅翻到筆記最後一頁，看見一行話特別醒目：每個人都應有機會解釋自己的選擇。忙碌時很容易忘記，尤其人人都想快點結束。阿俊不只一次提醒瑪雅保持開放心態。這不是要大家永遠等待，而是說仔細行動通常會讓下一步更安全、更公平。瑪雅合上本子，默默決定明天也要記住。
