A tiny silver screw turned up beside the display case's back wall. The school hall held many objects that looked important because someone had placed them behind glass. Maya had always liked the championship cup, but had never noticed how easily people could speak for it without knowing its history. Now every small mark mattered: an empty space, a line in a record, and an ordinary time written on a sheet. Jun asked if they were allowed to look at the next place. Maya checked with the responsible adult before stepping closer. The friends were not police, and they knew that curiosity did not give them permission to cross every line. Although the group was tired, they finished the final check. Somebody had packed bean for a short break later. A notice near the entrance mentioned deal. A notice near the entrance mentioned dig. During the break, Jun checked the note.

展示櫃後牆旁找到一顆小銀色螺絲。 學校大廳裡放著許多隔著玻璃、看來很重要的物品。瑪雅一直喜歡冠軍獎盃，卻從未發現人們很容易在不了解歷史時替它說故事。如今每個小記號都重要：空位、紀錄上的一行字和紙上的平常時間。阿俊問能不能再靠近下一個地方。瑪雅先向負責成人確認才走近。朋友們不是警察，也知道好奇心不代表能越過所有界線。 雖然大家累了，仍完成最後一次核對。有人為稍後的休息準備了豆子。入口附近的告示提到處理。入口附近的告示提到挖。休息時，阿俊查看紙條。

It matched the size of the loose nameplate in yesterday's photograph. At first, the discovery seemed to point in only one direction. Then Jun asked a quiet question that made Maya look twice. A useful observation should not depend on everybody liking the same answer. Maya wrote down what was certain and left a blank space for what they did not know. It felt a little strange to leave a page unfinished. Still, that blank space was honest. It meant they could learn something new tomorrow without having to pretend yesterday's guess had always been right. A drawing of a frog hung on the classroom notice board. Maya remembered a drawing of height seen earlier that week. A notice near the entrance mentioned hen. Maya said the word least in a careful answer. The long line moved slowly, and nobody was pushed.

它的大小和昨日照片中鬆動的名牌一致。 一開始，這項發現似乎只有一個解釋。接著阿俊輕聲問了一句，讓瑪雅重新思考。有用的觀察不需要所有人喜歡同一個答案。瑪雅寫下已確定的事，也為未知留下空白。讓頁面沒寫滿有點奇怪，但這份空白很誠實。它表示明天可以學到新東西，不必假裝昨天的猜測永遠正確。 教室公告欄上掛著一張青蛙的圖畫。瑪雅想起本週稍早看到的高度圖畫。入口附近的告示提到母雞。瑪雅仔細回答時說出了「至少」。長長的隊伍緩慢前進，沒有人被推擠。

Jun sketches the screw and let the teacher keep it in a safe envelope. Some people wanted to act immediately. Maya understood their worry, yet the choice would affect more than one person. Jun helped turn scattered suggestions into a plan with a beginning and an end. They wrote what each person could do, and what no one should be pushed to do. That last part mattered. 'A fair answer cannot depend on someone being too afraid to refuse,' Maya said. When they finally moved, it was with a plan that could be checked instead of a guess that only sounded confident. The open book contained a short story about minute. Ms. Chen signed the note after checking the facts. Maya remembered a drawing of neck seen earlier that week. The class reading card used point in an example sentence. "Put the paper beside the notebook," Maya said.

阿俊畫下螺絲，交由老師放入安全的信封。 有些人希望立刻行動。瑪雅理解焦急，但這個選擇會影響不只一人。阿俊把零散建議整理成有開頭和結尾的計畫。他們寫下每人能做什麼，也寫下哪些事不能強迫別人做。後面這一點尤其重要。「公平的答案不能靠別人不敢拒絕。」瑪雅說。最後他們按可以檢查的計畫行動，而不是跟著聽起來很有信心的猜測。 翻開的書有一則談到分鐘的小故事。陳老師核對事實後簽了字。瑪雅想起本週稍早看到的脖子圖畫。課堂閱讀卡用指向造了一個例句。「把紙放在筆記本旁邊。」瑪雅說。

They begin to suspect someone moved the trophy to deal with a broken piece. The clue chart had changed again. A line that once seemed promising now had a question mark beside it; another line had a clear time and a person willing to confirm it. Maya did not feel angry about the change. The chart was doing its job. Jun moved the newest note into place while the third friend asked what could be checked next. They were learning that a mystery became less frightening as its facts became clearer. A dramatic guess might make people listen for a moment, but a careful record would still matter after the excitement was gone. A notice near the entrance mentioned race. The safe path was marked clearly for every visitor. A notice near the entrance mentioned snow. "I am sorry about that mistake," Maya said. The open book contained a short story about win.

大家開始懷疑有人為了處理破損部件而移動獎盃。 線索表又改變了。原本看似有希望的線旁多了問號，另一條線則有清楚時間和願意確認的人。瑪雅沒有因此生氣，因為線索表正在發揮作用。阿俊把新紙條放好，第三位朋友詢問下一步能查什麼。他們學會謎團會隨事實清楚而沒那麼可怕。誇張猜測也許能暫時吸引人，仔細紀錄卻能在熱鬧過後繼續有用。 入口附近的告示提到賽跑。安全路線清楚標示給每位訪客。入口附近的告示提到雪。「那個錯誤真的很抱歉。」瑪雅說。翻開的書有一則談到贏得的小故事。

The notebook was becoming thicker with small observations. Maya had once imagined that a good story needed one dramatic moment to make everything clear. Real days did not behave that way. One small choice led to another, and people sometimes changed their minds for good reasons. Jun helped put today's paper in the right place. They could return to it later and see exactly why they had acted as they did. That mattered more than making the day sound exciting to someone who had not been there.

筆記本裡的小觀察越來越厚。瑪雅以前以為好故事需要一個戲劇性瞬間讓一切清楚，真實生活卻不是這樣。一個小選擇連著下一個選擇，人也可能因合理理由改變心意。阿俊幫忙把今天的紙收在正確位置。日後再翻開，就能知道當初為什麼那樣行動。這比向沒到場的人說得刺激更重要。

As the lights outside grew softer, Maya remembered the feeling at the start of the chapter's trouble. It had seemed larger before anyone could name it. Now it had a shape, a few facts, and a next step. That did not make it harmless, but it made it possible to face. Jun had helped without taking the whole choice away. Maya hoped to remember that balance. It was a kind of strength that did not make a loud sound, and it might be needed again very soon.

窗外的光漸漸柔和，瑪雅想起麻煩剛開始時的感受。那時在還沒說清楚前，事情看起來特別巨大。如今它有了形狀、幾項事實和下一步。問題不一定變得無害，卻能面對了。阿俊提供幫助，卻沒有奪走瑪雅的選擇權。瑪雅想記住這種平衡。這份力量不需要大聲，也許很快又會派上用場。
