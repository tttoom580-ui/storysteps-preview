The Last Photograph

小鎮偵探與消失的冠軍獎盃 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Jun found a photograph taken during yesterday's school tour. The school hall held many objects that looked important because someone had placed them behind glass. Maya had always liked the championship cup, but had never noticed how easily people could speak for it without knowing its history. Now every small mark mattered: an empty space, a line in a record, and an ordinary time written on a sheet. Jun asked if they were allowed to look at the next place. Maya checked with the responsible adult before stepping closer. The friends were not police, and they knew that curiosity did not give them permission to cross every line. "We can add when the others are ready," Maya suggested. Jun also wanted to hear another point of view. "Please bring the notebook tomorrow," Jun said. The plan looked simple, but one part needed checking. Maya remembered a drawing of cousin seen earlier that week.

阿俊找到昨天校園導覽拍下的照片。 學校大廳裡放著許多隔著玻璃、看來很重要的物品。瑪雅一直喜歡冠軍獎盃，卻從未發現人們很容易在不了解歷史時替它說故事。如今每個小記號都重要：空位、紀錄上的一行字和紙上的平常時間。阿俊問能不能再靠近下一個地方。瑪雅先向負責成人確認才走近。朋友們不是警察，也知道好奇心不代表能越過所有界線。 「等其他人準備好，我們就能增加、加入。」瑪雅提議。阿俊也想聽聽其他人的看法。「明天請把筆記本帶來。」阿俊說。計畫看來簡單，但有一部分需要查證。瑪雅想起本週稍早看到的表親、表哥圖畫。

The trophy was visible in the photo, but its bottom plate hangs loose. For several seconds, nobody spoke. Maya was thinking about the new fact and the way it touched earlier choices. Jun drew a short line under the important part of the notes. They agreed that a record was useful only if it was clear enough to correct. If a number, name, or time was uncertain, they marked it as uncertain. That extra care took a little longer, but it protected people who were not in the room to explain themselves. At last, Maya could see a practical question worth asking next. A small poster on the wall included the word family. "What does famous mean in this note?" Maya asked. The open book contained a short story about fill. The class reading card used guy in an example sentence. A helpful classmate offered to carry the notes.

照片裡獎盃還在，底部金屬牌卻鬆動了。 好幾秒沒有人開口。瑪雅思考新事實與先前選擇的關係。阿俊在筆記重要處畫了短線。他們同意紀錄要清楚，才方便日後更正。數字、姓名或時間若不確定，就標明不確定。這樣雖然多花時間，卻能保護不在場、無法替自己說話的人。最後瑪雅終於想到下一個值得問的實際問題。 牆上的小海報寫著家人這個字。「這張筆記裡的「有名的」是什麼意思？」瑪雅問。翻開的書有一則談到裝滿、填入的小故事。課堂閱讀卡用男子造了一個例句。一位熱心同學主動幫忙搬筆記。

Maya asked the teacher for permission to compare the photo with the display record. Making a choice was harder than making a wish. Maya could imagine a quick solution, but a real plan needed to fit the time, the place, and the people taking part. Jun listened while Maya explained the steps aloud. They stopped at one unclear part, changed it, and started again. 'If somebody says no, we need another way,' Maya said. Jun agreed that helping was not the same as deciding for others. Together they wrote a short plan anyone involved could question, improve, or leave if it was not right for them. Maya remembered a drawing of lip seen earlier that week. The class reading card used power in an example sentence. They talked about the river near the entrance and how to keep it safe. A small poster on the wall included the word science. A drawing of a sheep hung on the classroom notice board.

瑪雅向老師徵得同意，比對照片與展示紀錄。 做選擇比許願難。瑪雅可以想像快速解法，但真正的計畫必須配合時間、地點與參與者。阿俊聽瑪雅逐步說明，在不清楚的地方停下修改，再重新開始。「如果有人說不，我們要有其他辦法。」瑪雅說。阿俊同意，幫忙不等於替別人做決定。他們一起寫下簡明計畫，讓相關的人都能提問、改善或選擇退出。 瑪雅想起本週稍早看到的嘴唇圖畫。課堂閱讀卡用權力造了一個例句。他們討論入口附近的河流，以及如何保持安全。牆上的小海報寫著科學這個字。教室公告欄上掛著一張綿羊的圖畫。

They learn the trophy was still present at four o'clock yesterday afternoon. The clue chart had changed again. A line that once seemed promising now had a question mark beside it; another line had a clear time and a person willing to confirm it. Maya did not feel angry about the change. The chart was doing its job. Jun moved the newest note into place while the third friend asked what could be checked next. They were learning that a mystery became less frightening as its facts became clearer. A dramatic guess might make people listen for a moment, but a careful record would still matter after the excitement was gone. Maya remembered a drawing of south seen earlier that week. "What does sunny mean in this note?" Maya asked. "They may need more time," Jun answered. Someone had asked Jun a question about tool. A notice near the entrance mentioned video.

他們確認昨天下午四點獎盃仍在原位。 線索表又改變了。原本看似有希望的線旁多了問號，另一條線則有清楚時間和願意確認的人。瑪雅沒有因此生氣，因為線索表正在發揮作用。阿俊把新紙條放好，第三位朋友詢問下一步能查什麼。他們學會謎團會隨事實清楚而沒那麼可怕。誇張猜測也許能暫時吸引人，仔細紀錄卻能在熱鬧過後繼續有用。 瑪雅想起本週稍早看到的南方圖畫。「這張筆記裡的「晴朗的」是什麼意思？」瑪雅問。「他們可能需要更多時間。」阿俊回答。有人曾問阿俊關於工具的問題。入口附近的告示提到影片。

After the busy part ended, Maya and Jun found a quiet minute. There were still things they wanted to say, yet neither felt a need to win the conversation. Maya asked what had been most difficult. Jun answered honestly and then asked the same question back. They were surprised to hear two different answers. That did not make either one wrong. The talk helped them see that working together did not mean thinking exactly alike. It meant taking those differences seriously enough to make a plan both could understand.

忙碌告一段落後，瑪雅和阿俊有了片刻安靜。他們還有話想說，卻不必在對話中爭贏。瑪雅問最困難的是哪部分。阿俊誠實回答，也反問同一個問題。兩人的答案竟然不同，卻不代表誰錯了。這場談話讓他們理解，合作不必想法完全一致，而是認真看待差異，做出雙方都能理解的計畫。

When Maya turned the last page of the notes, one line stood out: people deserve the chance to explain their own choices. It was easy to forget that in a busy day, especially when everyone wanted a quick ending. Jun had reminded Maya more than once to keep an open mind. The lesson was not that people should wait forever. It was that acting with care usually made the next action safer and fairer. Maya closed the book and made a quiet plan to remember that tomorrow.

瑪雅翻到筆記最後一頁，看見一行話特別醒目：每個人都應有機會解釋自己的選擇。忙碌時很容易忘記，尤其人人都想快點結束。阿俊不只一次提醒瑪雅保持開放心態。這不是要大家永遠等待，而是說仔細行動通常會讓下一步更安全、更公平。瑪雅合上本子，默默決定明天也要記住。
