考試週的猩猩告白

Chimpanzee of Love

戀愛嫌疑犯通緝中 · 第 15 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Exam week approached like a slow-moving train carrying textbooks. I had a mountain of assignments, and Eli had signed up for a scholarship interview with an affiliate research institute. He aspired to study historical education after graduation, but the application required a presentation, an essay, and an interview with three examiners. I volunteered to help. June offered a free coupon for coffee; Clara offered to perform a motivational chant. Eli chose the coffee. I suggested yoga to help him relax, and he asked whether breathing exercises counted as revision. He really did need a holiday.

考試週像一列載滿課本的慢車逐漸逼近。我有一大堆作業，以禮則報名了某個附屬研究機構的獎學金面試。他立志畢業後研究歷史教育，但申請需要簡報、論文，以及三位考官的面試。我自願幫忙。小君提供免費咖啡折價券，克拉拉提議唱激勵口號。以禮選了咖啡。我建議做瑜珈放鬆，他卻問呼吸練習能不能算複習。他真的很需要放假。

We practiced in the library, beside a glass cabinet containing a ceramic vase. On the wall hung a poster about vocational education and lifelong learning. Eli carefully explained why museums should welcome people with different interests and backgrounds. I was proud of him, although I kept inventing ridiculous interview questions. “What would you do if a chimpanzee stole your presentation?” I asked. “Recover the file from the cloud,” he answered. “What if the cloud also disappeared?” “Then I would admit defeat.” His calm was impressive, but it clearly rested on the belief that monkeys obeyed computer security protocols.

我們在圖書館練習，旁邊玻璃櫃裡擺著一個陶瓷花瓶。牆上貼著職業教育與終身學習海報。以禮仔細解釋博物館為何應歡迎興趣和背景不同的人。我為他驕傲，卻還是不斷發明荒唐面試題。「如果黑猩猩偷走你的簡報怎麼辦？」我問。「從雲端還原檔案，」他說。「如果雲端也消失？」「那我會承認失敗。」他的冷靜令人佩服，但顯然建立在猴子會遵守資訊安全規範的信念上。

One afternoon our study group reached the brink of collapse. June's online newspaper subscription had expired, Clara accidentally spilled tea over her notes, and my favorite pen disappeared. The campus security club happened to be rehearsing a skit featuring a stern sergeant, so his dramatic shout of “ATTENTION!” sent us diving under the table. “This is a library,” the librarian said, in a voice far more frightening than his. Eli apologized immediately. Clara claimed she had reacted with instinctive self-defense. I suggested the rest of us needed to defend ourselves against her performances.

某天下午，我們的讀書小組到了崩潰邊緣。小君的線上報紙訂閱到期了，克拉拉不小心把茶灑在筆記上，而我最喜歡的筆也不見了。校園安全社剛好在彩排一齣有嚴厲中士的短劇，他一聲「注意！」讓我們全部鑽進桌下。「這裡是圖書館，」圖書館員說，聲音比中士還可怕。以禮立刻道歉。克拉拉說那是本能性的自衛反應。我認為我們其他人更需要防衛她的演出。

The night before the interview, Eli admitted he was afraid of being rejected. He had worked for years to make himself dependable, and now one committee might say no. “The world won't perish if they reject you,” I said. “You will still be you.” I worried that sounded too casual, so I added, “Besides, I would choose you even without a scholarship.” He looked at me as though I'd offered him something bigger than a diploma. “That was unexpectedly good advice,” he said. “Please don't reproduce it on a poster.” I promised only that I would not sell it commercially.

面試前一晚，以禮承認自己害怕被拒絕。他努力多年讓自己可靠，如今一個委員會可能說不。「就算他們拒絕你，世界也不會毀滅，」我說，「你還是你。」我擔心說得太輕鬆，又補充：「而且就算沒有獎學金，我還是會選你。」他看著我，彷彿我給他的比文憑更珍貴。「這是出乎意料的好建議，」他說，「拜託別印成海報。」我只保證不會拿去商業販售。

On interview day I waited outside while he presented his project. Two sparrows hopped along the windowsill, completely indifferent to my anxiety. Inside, the committee discussed whether to ratify a proposed community partnership as part of next year's plan. Eli was careful not to promise anything the institute couldn't deliver. Afterward he emerged looking pale but relieved. “I didn't try to sound clever,” he said. “I just answered honestly.” June ran toward him with a celebratory sign reading NO MORE RECKLESS STUDYING. Clara had decorated it with glitter, which made the security guard sneeze.

面試當天，我在外面等他完成簡報。兩隻麻雀在窗台跳來跳去，完全不在乎我的焦慮。會議室裡，委員會討論是否批准明年的社區合作計畫。以禮謹慎地不承諾機構做不到的事。出來時他臉色蒼白，卻鬆了口氣。「我沒有刻意裝聰明，」他說，「只是誠實回答。」小君拿著慶祝牌子跑來，上面寫：「停止魯莽讀書。」克拉拉撒了亮粉，害保全打噴嚏。

Three days thereafter, Eli received good news: he had advanced to the final selection round. We celebrated with a small picnic instead of a grand banquet. He unfolded a note I'd hidden in his bag, which read, “Your best qualification is being kind.” “You know,” he said, “I'm keeping this one.” I laughed because he already saved practically everything I wrote. When he leaned in to kiss me, June shouted that public displays of affection required advance notice. Clara chanted our names like a sports crowd. Eli kissed me anyway. Perhaps the most remarkable result of exam week was that my cautious boyfriend had learned a little recklessness.

三天後，以禮收到好消息：他進入最後甄選。我們用簡單野餐慶祝，而不是辦盛大宴會。他打開我偷偷放進包裡的紙條，上頭寫著：「你最好的資格就是善良。」「妳知道嗎，」他說，「這張我要留著。」我笑了，因為他幾乎把我寫的東西全收起來。他俯身要親我時，小君大喊公開親密行為必須提前通報，克拉拉則像球迷一樣喊我們名字。以禮還是吻了我。或許考試週最值得紀念的成果，是我謹慎的男朋友學會了一點點冒險。

On the walk home, I teased Eli that the scholarship application had become his favorite distraction from sleep. He admitted I was right. We agreed to spend the next morning without phones or revision notes. Then June sent us a picture of herself studying beneath a sign reading 'LEARN OR PERISH.' Neither of us replied.

回家路上，我取笑以禮，說獎學金申請已經變成他逃避睡覺最愛的分心事物。他承認我說得對。我們約好隔天早上不碰手機和複習筆記。接著小君傳來一張她在「不讀書就完蛋」標語下念書的照片。我們都沒回。
