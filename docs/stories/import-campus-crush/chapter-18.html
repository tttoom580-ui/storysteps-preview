古堡舞台的大逃亡

Escape from the Cardboard Castle

戀愛嫌疑犯通緝中 · 第 18 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Clara's drama club invited Eli and me to help restore a cardboard castle for its spring production. The play featured an outlaw, a captive prince, and a mischievous court musician. Clara insisted that I play the musician because I looked “naturally difficult to control.” I preferred to consider that a compliment. Eli worked on the restoration committee, which had rules about paint and electrical equipment. He showed me the club statute stating that all volunteers had to complete basic safety training. I read it with a sigh. In my life, romance and paperwork had developed a remarkably strong partnership.

克拉拉的話劇社邀請我和以禮協助修復春季演出的紙板城堡。戲裡有不法之徒、被俘虜的王子，以及淘氣的宮廷樂師。克拉拉堅持讓我演樂師，說我「天生看起來就很難控制」。我寧願把它當稱讚。以禮參與修復委員會，負責油漆和電器規範。他拿給我看社團章程，上面規定志工都得完成基本安全訓練。我嘆了口氣讀完。對我的人生而言，浪漫和文書工作已建立強大的合作關係。

During rehearsal, a language coach asked us to practice a troublesome consonant sound. She would dictate a line, and we had to repeat it without rushing. Clara delivered her speech beautifully. I made the outlaw sound like someone ordering noodles. “Perhaps we should use a shorter excerpt,” the coach suggested. Eli offered to help, and we practiced together for awhile. His gentle voice made the difficult sentence easier. “You need to pause before the final word,” he explained. “Otherwise your prince sounds like a sandwich.” I decided a sandwich prince was much more entertaining than a traditional one.

彩排時，語言指導老師請我們練習一個難發的子音。她口述一句台詞，我們得不急不徐地重複。克拉拉說得很完美，我卻把不法之徒演得像正在點麵。老師建議換短一點的節錄。以禮主動幫忙，我們一起練了一會兒。他溫柔的聲音讓難句變得容易。「最後一個字前要停頓，」他說，「不然妳的王子聽起來像三明治。」我覺得三明治王子反而比傳統王子有趣。

The set designer had built a miniature house for a fantasy dwarf, and a vast cape for the villain. The cape was so long that three actors stepped on it at once. Clara insisted on buying replacement fabric at wholesale prices, although we only needed two meters. Eli persuaded her that more material was not automatically better. Then a student with a camera tried to zoom in on the castle gate while walking backward. He struck a pillar made of foam. The gate fell, and everybody shouted, “Intruder!” It took us several minutes to explain that he belonged to the media team.

場景設計師替奇幻矮人造了迷你房子，還替反派做了一件巨大的斗篷。斗篷長到三名演員同時踩上去。克拉拉堅持用批發價買替換布料，雖然我們只需要兩公尺。以禮說服她，不是買越多就越好。接著，一名攝影學生邊倒退邊想把鏡頭拉近城堡大門，結果撞上一根泡棉柱子。大門倒下，所有人大喊：「有闖入者！」我們花了好幾分鐘才解釋他是影音組的人。

On opening night, a fan created a harmless tornado of loose programs near the entrance. The performance remained operative, but the cast grew nervous. Clara announced that we would survive this ordeal through theatrical courage. I accidentally stepped on my cape and felt the fabric pinch my shoulder. Eli whispered from backstage that I could remove it if it became unsafe. His advice helped me remember that no performance was worth an injury. I shortened the cape, made my entrance, and delivered the consonant-heavy line correctly. The audience applauded. I bowed with such pride that my paper crown fell off.

首演當晚，一台電風扇在入口吹出一陣像小龍捲風的節目單旋風。演出仍可正常運作，但演員們緊張起來。克拉拉宣布我們要憑戲劇勇氣度過這場磨難。我不小心踩上斗篷，布料夾住肩膀。以禮從後台低聲提醒，若不安全就可以脫掉。我因此想起，沒有任何表演值得受傷。我縮短斗篷，順利進場，正確唸出那句難發的台詞。觀眾鼓掌。我驕傲地鞠躬，紙皇冠卻掉了。

After the curtain call, Eli showed me a photograph he'd taken just before my entrance. I looked terrified but determined. “You were brave,” he said. “I was a walking costume accident,” I replied. “Both can be true.” He explained that theater had helped broaden his idea of historical storytelling. A good performance didn't need perfect objects; it needed people willing to care. Clara approached and informed us that her next play would include a royal wedding. June offered to charge admission to ours. I told them there was no wedding to organize, and Eli almost dropped the prop sword he'd been holding.

謝幕後，以禮拿給我看一張進場前的照片。我看起來害怕卻堅定。「妳很勇敢，」他說。「我是會走路的戲服事故，」我回答。「兩件事可以同時成立。」他說劇場拓寬了他對歷史敘事的理解。好表演不一定要物件完美，而是需要願意在乎的人。克拉拉走來說，她下一齣戲會有皇家婚禮，小君表示願意替我們的婚禮收門票。我告訴她們根本沒有婚禮要辦，以禮差點掉了手上的道具劍。

We left through the garden, where someone had hung little lights among the trees. Eli took my hand and said he hadn't meant to look shocked at the wedding joke. “I was just imagining how much planning June would demand,” he explained. I laughed. “You'd make a spreadsheet, wouldn't you?” “Several,” he admitted. The word “several” sounded suspiciously affectionate. We walked toward the dormitory under the lights. I wasn't ready to plan years ahead, but I was beginning to understand why the idea made him smile. For now, our immediate future contained hot noodles, tired feet, and a walk home together.

我們從花園離開，樹上掛著小燈。以禮握住我的手，說他不是故意對婚禮笑話露出震驚表情。「我只是想像小君會要求多少規劃，」他解釋。我笑了。「你一定會做試算表吧？」「好幾張，」他承認。「好幾張」聽起來竟然有點深情。我們在燈下走回宿舍。我還沒準備規劃好幾年以後，但開始懂他為何會因那個念頭微笑。現在，我們眼前的未來只有熱騰騰的麵、疲憊的腳，和一起回家的路。

A little music was meant to precede the final scene, but the sound engineer played the wrong track. Instead of a royal fanfare, everyone heard a cheerful advertisement for noodle soup. I nearly collapsed laughing. Eli later said the mistake made the performance more memorable, and even Clara admitted it had been a gift from the theater gods.

終場戲原本應先播放一段配樂，音控卻播錯音軌。觀眾聽到的不是皇家號角，而是歡樂的麵湯廣告。我差點笑倒。以禮後來說那個錯誤讓演出更難忘，連克拉拉都承認這是戲劇之神送的禮物。
