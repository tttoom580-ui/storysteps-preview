紙箱船上的救援之吻

The Cardboard Boat Rescue

戀愛嫌疑犯通緝中 · 第 16 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The engineering club announced a race in which teams had to build boats from cardboard and cross the swimming pool. I thought it sounded delightfully foolish. Eli thought it sounded like a controlled study of failure. We joined anyway, because the prize would fund new stationery for the children's reading program. Our team received three cartons of materials, a roll of tape, and a handwritten notice decorated with beautiful calligraphy. June immediately claimed the notice was a lucky charm. “Please don't become superstitious about paper,” Eli said. “You literally work in an archive,” she replied.

工程社宣布舉辦紙箱船比賽，隊伍必須用硬紙板造船並橫渡游泳池。我覺得這主意愉快又荒謬，以禮則認為這是受控的失敗研究。我們還是參加了，因為獎金將替兒童閱讀計畫購買新文具。隊伍拿到三個材料紙箱、一卷膠帶，以及一張寫著漂亮書法的通知。小君立刻宣稱它是幸運符。「拜託別迷信紙張，」以禮說。「你明明就在檔案室工作，」小君反駁。

The food department offered to cater the event with snacks, including walnut cookies. A volunteer nutrition student explained why dietary cholesterol was more complicated than a single scary headline. Meanwhile Clara rehearsed a victory speech so loudly that it caused a disturbance in the adjacent lecture hall. The professor came out to complain. June grew indignant and declared that science was being silenced. Eli gently mediated the disagreement, promising we would relocate the dramatic speeches. We returned to our boat, where a strip of tape had already begun peeling off. I wasn't sure whether this counted as an engineering warning or a prophecy.

餐飲系答應替活動承辦點心，包括核桃餅乾。一名營養系志工解釋，飲食中的膽固醇議題比單一聳動標題複雜。另一方面，克拉拉排練勝利演說太大聲，引起隔壁教室的騷動。教授出來抱怨，小君憤慨地宣布科學正在遭到壓制。以禮溫和地調解，承諾把戲劇演說移到別處。我們回到船旁，發現一條膠帶已經翹起。我不確定這是工程警訊還是預言。

At the starting line, we were instructed to paddle slowly and pass the baton in a poolside relay. Eli checked that the water had been properly purified and the lifeguards were present. The race began with a splash. Our boat traveled three meters before tilting downward. June screamed that betrayal had struck again. Clara waved her lucky paper. I grabbed the edge of the boat and shouted, “Stay balanced!” Eli shifted his weight and somehow brought us upright. For one miraculous second I thought we might win. Then my shoe fell into the water and floated away like a tiny abandoned island.

起跑線上，工作人員指示我們緩慢划槳，並在池邊接力傳棒。以禮確認池水已妥善淨化且救生員到位。比賽隨著水花開始。我們的船前進三公尺就向下傾斜。小君尖叫，說背叛再次降臨。克拉拉揮舞她的幸運紙張。我抓緊船沿喊：「保持平衡！」以禮挪動重心，居然讓船恢復正立。有那麼奇蹟般的一秒，我以為我們可能會贏。接著我的鞋掉進水裡，像被遺棄的小島般漂走。

The lifeguard retrieved my shoe, but our cardboard vessel was finished. Other students applauded our persistent attempt. We climbed out safely, wet and laughing. “I suppose we lost,” Eli said. “Technically,” I replied, “we won a scientific demonstration of sinking.” The audience seemed to agree. A volunteer porter helped move the damaged boat away from the pool, and the organizer thanked us for not taking risks once it began falling apart. I felt proud of Eli for knowing when to stop; he said he felt proud of me for knowing how to laugh. June described the whole scene as an aquatic catastrophe of historic importance.

救生員取回我的鞋，但紙箱船已經報廢。其他學生為我們堅持到底的嘗試鼓掌。我們安全爬出泳池，濕透又笑個不停。「看來輸了，」以禮說。「技術上，」我回答，「我們贏得了沉船科學示範。」觀眾似乎同意。一位搬運志工幫忙把損壞的船移走，主辦人感謝我們發現船要散架時沒有逞強。我為以禮懂得何時停止而驕傲，他則說自己為我懂得笑而驕傲。小君把整件事形容成歷史性的水上災難。

Later, I found Eli looking at the photograph of our boat in the engineering hall. “Do you marvel at its elegance?” I asked. “I marvel that it traveled three meters.” He handed me a small paper card printed with the race results. At the bottom he'd written, “Favorite teammate: Summer.” I was suddenly shy. “That's not an official category,” I protested. “It should be.” Outside, June was offering to build a second boat shaped like a giant cucumber. We ignored her and walked away together, leaving the promise of another impossible project happily behind us.

後來，我發現以禮在工程館看我們船的照片。「你在驚嘆它的優雅嗎？」我問。「我驚嘆它居然走了三公尺。」他遞給我一張印著比賽結果的小卡，底下寫著：「最喜歡的隊友：小夏。」我突然害羞起來。「這不是官方獎項，」我抗議。「應該是。」外頭小君正提議造第二艘巨型小黃瓜船。我們選擇忽略她，牽手走開，愉快地把另一個不可能的計畫留在身後。

The following weekend, the engineering club invited us to watch the next race from the safety of the stands. The winning boat moved slowly but steadily across the pool. A small child beside us said it looked like a floating biscuit tin. Eli laughed, and I realized that our failed boat had taught us more than a perfect design might have. We had practiced choosing safety over pride and patience over embarrassment.

隔週工程社邀我們到安全的觀眾席觀看下一場比賽。冠軍船緩慢卻穩定地橫渡泳池。旁邊小朋友說它像漂浮的餅乾盒。以禮笑了。我忽然覺得我們那艘失敗的船，教給我們的比完美設計更多。我們練習了把安全放在面子之前，把耐心放在丟臉之前。

Before going to sleep, I found a photograph of our first handmade boat taped to my desk. It looked terrible, a soggy box with too many strips of tape. Yet it reminded me of the way Eli had reached out when I almost slipped, and how easily we'd laughed together afterward. I sent him the picture with one question: “Ready for the next adventure?” He answered, “As long as we bring dry socks.”

睡前，我在書桌上找到第一艘手作船的照片。它看起來糟透了，就是一個貼了太多膠帶的濕紙箱。但它讓我想起差點滑倒時，以禮如何伸手扶我，以及後來我們笑得多自在。我把照片傳給他，只問一句：「準備好下一次冒險了嗎？」他回答：「只要我們帶乾襪子就好。」
