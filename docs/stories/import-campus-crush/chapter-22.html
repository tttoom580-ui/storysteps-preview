Hail at the Health Fair

戀愛嫌疑犯通緝中 · 第 22 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the morning of the campus health fair, Eli and I helped carry boxes up the stairs. “Please ascend carefully,” he said, so formally that June accused him of training for life as a palace guard. Our university anthem played over the speakers while volunteers set up healthful snacks and exercise booths. A visiting pharmacist offered a careful presentation about medication safety, including why illegal opioids such as heroin could be life-threatening. I appreciated that the fair provided reliable information without sensational details. Clara, meanwhile, wanted to decorate the stage as a polar research station. Nobody understood why, including Clara herself.

校園健康市集早晨，我和以禮一起把紙箱搬上樓梯。「請小心往上走，」他說得太正式，小君指控他正在練習當皇宮衛兵。學校校歌從音響播放，志工布置有益健康的點心和運動攤位。來訪藥師仔細介紹用藥安全，包括海洛因等非法類鴉片物質為何可能致命。我很高興活動提供可靠資訊，而不是聳動細節。另一方面，克拉拉想把舞台布置成極地研究站。沒人知道為什麼，連她自己都不知道。

The student council had arranged an audit of the event's expenses. June complained that the financial report contained a trillion unnecessary boxes, which was exaggeration even by her standards. Eli checked the records for errors, and I helped correct a small defect in the printed budget. Afterward we met an exchange student whose family resided on the mainland before moving overseas. She spoke about rebuilding friendships in a new city. Her words made me think of Eli's coming semester away. He noticed my expression but didn't press me to speak. Sometimes intimacy meant allowing a worry to arrive in its own time.

學生會安排稽核活動支出。小君抱怨財務報表有一兆個沒必要的格子，誇張程度連她自己都少見。以禮檢查紀錄，我協助修正印刷預算上的一處小缺陷。後來我們遇見一名交換生，她的家庭出國前住在大陸本土。她談搬到新城市後重新建立友情的經歷，讓我想起以禮即將離開的一學期。他注意到我的神情，卻沒有催我開口。有時親密感就是讓擔憂按照自己的速度出現。

A sudden storm brought hail against the tent roofs. Volunteers moved everyone indoors, behind a thick hedge that separated the garden from the building. One student began to grumble that the event was ruined. June called the weather “a barbarian invasion,” then apologized to the organizer for making a bad joke. People mingled in the hallway, sharing umbrellas and chairs. Eli stood upright beside the door, counting visitors to ensure nobody remained outside. I helped distribute towels. The rain was loud, but there was something comforting about watching a crowd take care of one another without being asked.

突然的暴風雨帶來冰雹，打在帳篷頂上。志工帶所有人進室內，經過分隔花園和建築的厚樹籬。一位同學開始抱怨活動毀了。小君稱天氣是「野蠻人入侵」，接著向主辦人為這個不好笑的笑話道歉。人們在走廊交際，分享雨傘和椅子。以禮挺直站在門旁清點人數，確保沒有人留在外面。我幫忙發毛巾。雨聲很大，但看著大家無須吩咐就互相照顧，讓人覺得安心。

When the storm eased, Eli told me that his stepmother had called to congratulate him on the placement. He spoke warmly of her; she had helped raise him after his parents separated, and she never tried to replace his mother. “She asked whether I'd met someone special,” he said. “What did you say?” I asked. “I said yes, and that she's surprisingly resistant to sensible plans.” I pretended to be offended. “I am perfectly sensible.” He raised an eyebrow. “You once hosted a mushroom history presentation to save a love letter.” I had no defense. Some facts should have been classified forever.

暴風雨稍停時，以禮說繼母打電話祝賀他的錄取。他提到繼母時很溫柔：父母分開後，她協助照顧他，也從不試圖取代他的媽媽。「她問我是不是遇到特別的人，」他說。「你怎麼回答？」我問。「我說有，而且她對合理計畫的抵抗力令人驚訝。」我假裝生氣。「我很合理好嗎。」他挑眉。「妳曾為了救一封情書，主持蘑菇歷史簡報。」我完全無法辯護。有些事實應該永久列為機密。

At the end of the fair, the organizer invited everyone back to the courtyard. Eli and I hung a poster explaining how to seek health information from qualified professionals. June took a picture of us and promised not to write any insulting caption this time. “You're growing,” I told her. “Don't tell anyone,” she said. Eli held my hand as we walked past the wet hedge. I realized I wasn't only afraid of missing him during his placement. I was afraid of how the relationship might change. But that day had reminded me that care could remain real through storms, distance, and all the ordinary disruptions that no schedule could prevent.

健康市集結束時，主辦人邀大家回中庭。我和以禮掛好海報，介紹如何向合格專業人員尋找健康資訊。小君替我們拍照，保證這次不寫冒犯性圖說。「妳成長了，」我告訴她。「不要說出去，」她回答。我和以禮牽手經過濕透的樹籬。我發現自己不只怕實習期間想念他，也怕感情跟著改變。但今天提醒我，關懷能穿過暴風雨、距離，以及所有行程表無法預防的日常意外。

After the fair, we attended a smaller discussion about coping with uncertainty. The speaker emphasized that no one needed to feel ashamed for seeking professional advice, and that friends could offer companionship without pretending to be experts. I wrote the idea in my notebook. Eli read it over my shoulder and said, 'That sounds like what we've been learning all year.' He was right. We didn't have to know every answer in order to care for each other.

市集之後，我們參加一場較小的討論會，主題是如何面對不確定。講者強調尋求專業意見並不丟臉，而朋友也能在不假裝專家的情況下陪伴。我把這個想法寫進筆記本。以禮從肩後看了一眼，說：「這不就是我們這一年一直在學的事嗎？」他說得對。我們不必知道所有答案，也能關心彼此。

Eli suggested that we write down three things we were grateful for before the semester ended. Mine were simple: his steady attention, our friends' impossible energy, and the fact that we could finally speak honestly about fear. He smiled and said he'd written almost the same list.

以禮提議在學期結束前，各寫下三件感謝的事。我寫的很簡單：他穩定的關心、朋友們驚人的活力，以及我們終於能坦白談恐懼。他笑著說，他寫的幾乎是同一份清單。
