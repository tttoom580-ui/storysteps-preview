宿舍水災與告白交響曲

The Flooded Dorm Symphony

戀愛嫌疑犯通緝中 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first rule of dating Eli was never to touch his carefully labeled folders. The second was to expect trouble whenever June said, “I have a simple plan.” Her plan this week was to clean our old dormitory with bleach before the residence inspection. She had prepared a comprehensive checklist, which made her seem credible for approximately four minutes. Then she poured two buckets into the same sink and left the tap running. Water began to overflow. From my bedroom came the sound of June announcing, “Good news! The floor is getting washed automatically!” I called Eli before the water reached my shoes.

和以禮交往的第一條守則，是不要碰他貼好標籤的資料夾。第二條則是，只要小君說「我有一個簡單計畫」，就要準備迎接災難。這星期她的計畫，是宿舍檢查前用漂白劑清潔老舊房間。她還做了一張全面的檢查清單，讓她看起來可靠了大約四分鐘。接著，她把兩桶水倒進同一個水槽，還忘了關水龍頭。水開始溢出。房間裡傳來小君的聲音：「好消息！地板正在自動清洗！」我在水淹到鞋子前打電話給以禮。

Our dormitory superintendent arrived wearing rubber boots and the expression of a man who had seen civilizations collapse. The landlady of the nearby student apartments also came because water was running toward her front steps. “I manage students, not fish,” she declared. Eli asked everyone to move the electrical equipment first. We carried my wooden dresser into the hallway. On top sat an antique thermometer and a photograph of my grandfather, who had taught me to love old stories. He had died last year, and I still sometimes mourned him in quiet moments. Eli placed the photograph carefully out of danger.

宿舍總管理員穿著雨靴趕來，臉上是一副見證過文明崩潰的表情。附近學生公寓的女房東也來了，因為水正流向她的門階。「我管理的是學生，不是魚，」她說。以禮請大家先搬走電器。我們把木製梳妝台搬到走廊，上面擺著一支古董溫度計，還有祖父的照片。他教我喜歡老故事，去年卻離開了人世。我有時仍會在安靜時思念他。以禮小心地把照片放到安全地方。

A ridiculous argument erupted over responsibility. The superintendent blamed a broken pipe; June insisted on defending her cleaning method; our floor representative proposed a new emergency hierarchy involving plastic buckets. I tried to refute the claim that the building was haunted, but Clara arrived in dramatic costume and declared that “the watery spirits demand a sacrifice.” Eli said only, “Let's turn off the main valve.” He found it in two minutes. His quiet dedication was more useful than all the speeches combined. June, standing in a puddle, announced that she would nominate him for president of Indoor Oceans.

一場荒謬的責任爭論爆發了。管理員怪罪破水管，小君堅持捍衛她的清潔方法，樓層代表則提議成立一個以塑膠水桶為核心的緊急階層制度。我試圖駁斥大樓鬧鬼的說法，克拉拉卻穿著戲服現身，宣布「水之精靈需要祭品」。以禮只說：「先關總水閥吧。」兩分鐘後，他就找到了。他安靜的奉獻比所有演講加起來還有用。站在水窪裡的小君宣布，她要提名他當室內海洋總統。

While we waited for the floor to dry, Eli brought tea from the café. Outside our window, lush green trees bent in the wind, and a seagull had somehow landed on the sports field far from the coast. I told him about my grandfather's collection of opera records. Eli asked which one I remembered best. “A terrible recording of a symphony,” I said. “He used to conduct the kitchen spoons.” Eli took two pencils and performed a silent concert over our wet towels. I laughed until my stomach hurt. For a few seconds the dorm felt less like a disaster and more like home.

等地板乾時，以禮從咖啡店帶來熱茶。窗外青翠繁茂的樹木隨風彎曲，一隻不知怎麼飛來的海鷗居然停在離海很遠的運動場上。我告訴他祖父收藏的歌劇唱片。「妳最記得哪一首？」他問。「一張錄得很糟的交響曲，」我說，「他以前總拿廚房湯匙指揮。」以禮拿起兩枝鉛筆，在濕毛巾上方演出無聲音樂會。我笑到肚子痛。有那麼幾秒鐘，宿舍不再像災區，而像真正的家。

Later, June overheard me telling Eli that I was grateful for him. “Aha,” she cried from behind the door, “emotional progress!” I felt instant turmoil. Eli had never said he loved me, and I had not wanted to pressure him. He noticed my face. “I know we're new at this,” he said, “but I like the familiarity we're building. No dramatic announcement required.” He spoke about how affection needed space to nurture itself. That was exactly what I wanted to hear. “Besides,” he added, “I'd prefer not to declare love while wearing emergency rubber gloves.”

後來，小君無意間聽見我對以禮說很感謝他。「啊哈！」她從門後叫道，「感情有進展！」我心裡立刻亂成一團。以禮還沒說過愛我，我也不想給他壓力。他注意到我的表情。「我知道我們都還在學，」他說，「但我喜歡我們慢慢建立的熟悉感。不用戲劇性的宣告。」他說，感情需要空間，才能好好培養。那正是我想聽的話。「何況，」他補充，「我寧可不要戴著緊急橡膠手套告白。」

That evening the superintendent approved our repairs, and June served a surprisingly wholesome dinner: vegetables, rice, and soup with nothing dangerous in it. The landlady sent dry blankets. Clara left behind a note reading, “LET LOVE FLOW, BUT TURN OFF THE FAUCET.” Eli walked me back from the laundry room and squeezed my hand. I had begun the day with a flooded room and an almost ruined photograph. I ended it knowing that whenever my life became a mess, he would not judge me from the doorway. He would roll up his sleeves and stand beside me.

那天晚上，宿舍管理員確認修繕完成，小君竟然準備了一頓出乎意料健康的晚餐：蔬菜、飯，以及沒有任何危險成分的湯。女房東送來乾毯子。克拉拉留下一張紙條：「讓愛流動，但記得關水龍頭。」以禮陪我從洗衣間回來，握緊我的手。今天開始時，我房間淹水，祖父的照片也差點受損。結束時我卻明白，每當生活亂成一團，他不會站在門口批評。他會捲起袖子，站到我身旁。

Before sleeping, I overheard June telling Clara on the phone that Eli had repaired more than our sink. She said he had made me less frightened of being cared for. I pretended not to hear, but I kept smiling into my pillow. For once, my roommate's commentary was annoyingly accurate.

睡前，我無意間聽見小君和克拉拉講電話，說以禮修好的不只是水槽，他也讓我沒那麼害怕被人照顧。我假裝沒聽到，卻一直對著枕頭笑。這一次，室友的評論令人討厭地精準。
