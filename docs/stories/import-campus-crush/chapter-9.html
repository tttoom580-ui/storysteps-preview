雨中遠足的防水戀愛

Waterproof Love

戀愛嫌疑犯通緝中 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

I organized a weekend photography trek for the campus arts club. After months of Eli's perfect plans, I wanted to prove that spontaneity was not merely a fashionable word for disaster. We would walk through a maple forest, visit an old mountain lodge, and photograph the jasmine growing near the stream. “Have you checked the weather?” Eli asked. “The forecast is a suggestion,” I replied. His expression suggested that our relationship's continuity was now being tested. June packed six chocolate bars and declared herself the official expedition mistress, meaning the person in charge, not whatever Clara was about to imply.

這次我替校園藝術社安排週末攝影遠足。經歷好幾個月以禮的完美計畫後，我想證明即興不只是「災難」的時尚說法。我們要穿過楓樹林，參觀山上的舊小屋，再拍溪邊盛開的茉莉花。「妳查天氣了嗎？」以禮問。「預報只是一種建議，」我回答。他的表情說明，這段戀情的連續性正在受考驗。小君帶了六條巧克力，宣布自己是遠征隊的正式女主人——也就是負責人，至於克拉拉正想暗示什麼，最好別問。

The first hour was pleasant. We stopped for pictures and tried to socialize with a group of hikers. One student, a history major, identified himself as a socialist and joined an animated debate about who should pay to maintain public trails. Eli listened; I tried to keep the argument from alienating anyone who simply wanted a peaceful walk. The trail spanned two small valleys, and our guide explained that the cumulative distance was longer than the map made it seem. I chose not to mention that I'd read the map upside down. Love could survive many truths, but I preferred to reveal them one at a time.

第一個小時很愉快。我們停下拍照，也試著和其他健行者社交。一名歷史系學生說自己是社會主義者，接著和大家熱烈討論公共步道應由誰出錢維護。以禮認真聽，我則努力避免辯論疏遠那些單純想安靜散步的人。山徑跨越兩座小山谷，嚮導說累積路程比地圖看起來還長。我決定暫時不提自己把地圖拿反了。愛情能承受很多真相，但我希望一次揭露一點。

At noon a cloudburst struck. Eli opened a waterproof bag and passed out rain covers. His readiness was impressive, although June called it “romantically offensive.” My notebook got a dark ink blot, and the paper photograph I'd brought as a gift nearly dissolved. Eli made a decisive move: he sheltered the photograph inside his jacket. “It's dispensable,” I said, embarrassed. “It's just a silly picture of us beside the cucumber booth.” “Not to me,” he answered. It was such a simple sentence that the whole forest suddenly seemed quieter, even under the rain.

中午突然下起暴雨。以禮打開防水包，分發雨罩。他的準備令人佩服，雖然小君稱它為「對浪漫的冒犯」。我的筆記本沾上墨水污點，原本要當禮物的紙照片也差點泡爛。以禮果斷地把照片藏進外套。「那張可以不要，」我不好意思地說，「只是我們站在小黃瓜攤旁邊的傻照片。」「對我來說不能不要，」他回答。這句話太簡單了，卻讓整座森林彷彿突然安靜下來，即使雨還在下。

We reached the lodge with wet hair and shoes full of water. Its owner served soup so spicy that one spoonful of chili made Clara dance in circles. June described the storm as proof of her vigorous leadership, despite having spent most of it behind Eli's umbrella. The lodge kept a little reading room where local artists had written about their persistence through years of bad weather and difficult work. One poet described finding a muse in ordinary kindness. I read the sentence twice. The person who had saved a cheap photograph seemed more inspiring to me than any perfectly posed hero.

我們到達小屋時，頭髮濕透，鞋子裡全是水。屋主端出的湯辣得驚人，一匙辣椒就讓克拉拉原地轉圈。小君把暴雨稱為她精力充沛領導能力的證明，儘管她大部分時間都躲在以禮的傘後。小屋有個小閱覽室，裡頭收錄本地藝術家寫下的故事：他們如何靠堅持度過多年的壞天氣和艱難工作。一位詩人說，平凡的善意也能成為靈感女神。我把那句話讀了兩遍。對我來說，救下一張便宜照片的人，比任何擺好姿勢的英雄更有啟發性。

By evening the rain had stopped, but the guide said the trail back remained slippery. We called for a van rather than pretending we could conquer the mountain. “Your plan was nearly perfect,” Eli told me. “Nearly?” “You forgot to schedule a dry pair of socks.” I produced two from my bag. His eyebrows rose. “I thought you were mobilizing chaos.” “I can plan too,” I said. “Occasionally.” June demanded documentary evidence. I took a picture of Eli wearing my bright yellow spare socks. He wore them without complaint, which was either devotion or an extraordinary lack of vanity.

傍晚雨停了，但嚮導說回程路面依然濕滑。我們叫了廂型車，不再假裝能征服整座山。「妳的計畫幾乎完美，」以禮說。「幾乎？」「妳忘了安排乾襪子。」我從包包拿出兩雙。他挑起眉。「我以為妳正在動員混亂。」「我也會計畫好嗎，」我說，「偶爾。」小君要求拍照存證。我替穿著亮黃色備用襪的以禮拍了一張。他毫無抱怨地穿上了，不知道是深情，還是虛榮心低得驚人。

On the ride home, Eli fell asleep against my shoulder. I carefully moved the ruined notebook so it would not stain him again. It occurred to me that we had spent a day being cold, tired, and occasionally ridiculous, yet I felt no urge to be elsewhere. The lodge keeper had given us a tiny jar of jasmine tea. I held it like a treasure. When Eli woke, he asked what I was thinking. “That I don't need every day to be impressive,” I said. “I just want a lot of ordinary days with you.” He smiled, and for once neither of us made a joke.

回程車上，以禮靠著我的肩睡著。我小心移開泡爛的筆記本，免得再次弄髒他。我忽然發現，我們一整天又冷又累，還時不時鬧笑話，我卻完全不想待在其他地方。小屋主人送了我們一小罐茉莉花茶，我像捧寶物般拿著。以禮醒來後問我在想什麼。「我不需要每天都很厲害，」我說，「我只想和你一起度過很多普通的日子。」他笑了，這次我們誰都沒有開玩笑。
