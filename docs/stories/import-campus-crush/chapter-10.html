餐廳法庭與香草戰爭

The Cafeteria Trial

戀愛嫌疑犯通緝中 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

The university cafeteria launched a new dessert called Galactic Vanilla. Clara claimed the purple cream was radioactive. It was actually colored with a harmless food dye, but that explanation did not prevent June from proposing a mock trial. She would prosecute the cafeteria for crimes against dessert. I would represent the defense. Eli refused to serve as judge until June promised that no real employee would be embarrassed. “This is a performance,” she said. “Of course it is,” Eli replied, “which means you must be careful.” June announced that he was ruining her career as a theatrical criminal lawyer.

學生餐廳推出新甜點「銀河香草」。克拉拉聲稱紫色奶油具有放射性。其實那只是無害的食用色素，但這個解釋並未阻止小君提議舉辦模擬法庭。她要起訴餐廳犯下的甜點罪，我負責辯護。以禮原本拒絕當法官，直到小君承諾不會讓真正的員工難堪。「這只是表演，」她說。「正因如此才要小心，」以禮回答。小君宣布他正摧毀她身為戲劇犯罪律師的生涯。

Our preparations soon depleted the club's supply of paper. We asked the administration to subsidize the event, but the finance office requested a proper budget. June called the request a cynical attempt to silence creative freedom. Eli patiently wrote down every expense. The most expensive item was a borrowed stage spotlight, followed by six synthetic wigs. I proposed a concession: use fewer wigs, keep the lights, and spend the savings on actual food for visitors. June sighed as though democracy had personally disappointed her, then signed the revised budget.

我們準備活動，很快就耗盡社團的紙張。向行政部門申請補助時，財務室要求正式預算。小君說這是憤世嫉俗地壓制創作自由。以禮耐心列出每筆開支。最貴的是借來的舞台聚光燈，其次是六頂合成纖維假髮。我提出讓步：減少假髮、保留燈光，並把省下的錢用來提供真的食物。小君嘆氣，彷彿民主親自辜負了她，但最後仍簽下修正版預算。

On performance day the fragrance of warm vanilla filled the hall. A student technician arrived with a screwdriver to repair a loose sign. He had barely started when Clara tried to intrude on the technical work, declaring that the sign needed “greater dramatic splendor.” The technician politely asked her to stand clear. Meanwhile I accidentally wrote “one serving of justice” on the menu instead of “one serving of cake.” June insisted it was perfect. Eli read the phrase twice, making the facial expression he wore when deciding whether a historical document was a joke.

演出當天，溫熱香草的香氣充滿大廳。一位學生技術員拿著螺絲起子來修鬆掉的招牌。他才剛開始，克拉拉就想闖進技術工作區，宣稱招牌需要「更華麗的舞台氣勢」。技術員禮貌請她退後。我則不小心把菜單上的「一份蛋糕」寫成「一份正義」。小君堅稱非常完美。以禮看了兩遍，露出他判斷歷史文件是不是玩笑時的表情。

June called a witness who testified that the dessert had tasted “like a meteorite wearing perfume.” I offered the defense's literal interpretation: since nobody had tasted a meteorite, the comparison lacked evidence. A boy dressed as a horse-racing jockey objected from the audience, claiming his uncle knew about unusual flavors. June delivered a rapid retort, and the crowd cheered. Then our prosecutor began to stutter because Clara unexpectedly switched on a dramatic thunder soundtrack. Eli banged a spoon against his glass. “Order,” he said, and everyone immediately settled down. I loved that his calm voice could control even this carnival.

小君傳喚證人。對方作證說甜點吃起來「像噴了香水的隕石」。我提出字面上的反駁：既然沒人吃過隕石，這項比較沒有證據。一名穿著賽馬騎師服裝的男生在觀眾席抗議，說叔叔懂奇怪的味道。小君立刻反唇相譏，觀眾歡呼。接著克拉拉突然播放雷聲配樂，害我們的檢察官開始結巴。以禮拿湯匙敲玻璃杯。「肅靜，」他說，所有人居然立刻安靜。我喜歡他冷靜的聲音竟能控制這場嘉年華。

When the trial ended, Eli delivered a ruling: the dessert was not dangerous, the publicity had been excessive, and students should be allowed to express honest opinions without mocking staff. Nobody had defaulted on a contract, and nobody was going to prison. The audience applauded. A cafeteria worker thanked him for making the event friendly instead of cruel. June's morale improved as soon as she received a complimentary slice of cake. She declared the ruling a glorious compromise. “The legal system survives another day,” she said, with cream on her nose.

模擬審判結束後，以禮宣判：甜點並不危險，宣傳確實誇張，但學生應能誠實表達看法，而不嘲笑員工。沒有人違約，也沒有人要入獄。觀眾鼓掌。餐廳員工謝謝他讓活動友善而不刻薄。小君拿到免費蛋糕後，士氣立刻恢復，宣布判決是光榮的折衷。「法律制度又存活了一天，」她說，鼻子上還沾著奶油。

That evening Eli admitted that his stepfather used to cook with him on weekends. “He taught me that imperfect food tastes better when people laugh together,” he said. I was surprised by how warmly he spoke. “Then let's have a spontaneous dinner,” I suggested. No reservations, no spreadsheets, just the night market. He agreed, although he quietly checked whether the stalls were open. At the entrance he kissed my cheek and asked if that violated any rules of our mock court. “Absolutely,” I said. “The penalty is that you have to do it again.” For once he complied without consulting the handbook.

那晚以禮提到，繼父以前常在週末跟他一起下廚。「他教我，不完美的食物只要和人一起笑著吃，也會更好吃，」他說。他語氣裡的溫柔讓我意外。「那我們來個即興晚餐吧，」我提議。不訂位、不做試算表，只去夜市。他答應了，雖然還是偷偷查了攤販是否營業。入口處，他親了我的臉頰，問這是否違反模擬法庭的規定。「絕對違反，」我說，「罰則是再做一次。」這次他沒有查手冊，就照辦了。

When we got to the night market, Eli ordered one savory pancake and one sweet one so we could share. No one brought a gavel or a legal charge. June sent one final message asking whether the vanilla dessert had been convicted. I muted my phone and chose the sweet pancake, followed by Eli's hand.

到了夜市，以禮點一份鹹煎餅和一份甜煎餅，讓我們分著吃。沒有人帶法槌或起訴書。小君最後傳訊息問香草甜點是否被判有罪。我把手機靜音，選了甜煎餅，也牽起以禮的手。
