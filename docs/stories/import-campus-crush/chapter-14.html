健康講座與失蹤的船長

The Captain Who Missed the Seminar

戀愛嫌疑犯通緝中 · 第 14 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The university health department invited us to a wellness symposium aboard a retired training ship moored at the wharf. Its museum guide liked to dress as an admiral, complete with a spectacular hat. June called him Captain Romance, which he accepted with alarming pride. We were supposed to help visitors find the correct lecture rooms. Instead, Clara led an entire group astray through a storage corridor and ended up beneath a sign reading ENGINE ROOM. “We are doomed,” she announced. Eli calmly consulted the floor plan. “Not doomed,” he corrected. “Just one deck below where we should be.”

大學健康部門邀請我們到停泊碼頭的退役訓練船上，參加身心健康研討會。博物館導覽員喜歡扮成海軍上將，還戴著誇張的大帽子。小君叫他「浪漫船長」，他竟然非常得意。我們原本負責引導訪客到正確講座室，克拉拉卻領著整團人誤入儲藏通道，最後站到寫著「機艙」的牌子底下。「我們完蛋了，」她宣布。以禮冷靜打開樓層圖。「不是完蛋，」他糾正，「只是比正確樓層低了一層。」

The first lecture explained the need to differentiate medical facts from online rumors. A clinician spoke about diabetes prevention and managing risk without shaming patients. Another speaker introduced counseling and psychotherapy as resources people could seek when useful. I noticed how carefully the presenters avoided language that might inflict unnecessary fear. Eli asked whether it was feasible to provide shorter, accessible summaries for students. The organizer thanked him and said it was an imperative part of future outreach. June, whose attention had wandered toward the biscuit tray, suddenly praised Eli for being “professionally decent.”

第一場講座說明如何區分醫療事實和網路謠言。醫師談糖尿病預防與風險管理，強調不應羞辱患者。另一位講者介紹諮商和心理治療，說明需要時可以尋求協助。我注意到講者們努力避免使用會造成不必要恐懼的語言。以禮詢問是否可行，替學生提供更簡短易懂的摘要。主辦人道謝，說這是未來推廣工作必須重視的事。注意力早飄向餅乾桌的小君，突然稱讚以禮「專業上的善良」。

At lunch, a nutrition educator explained that excess sugar was best considered within overall eating habits, not as a reason to feel guilty about every dessert. June announced that she had just been spiritually rescued. We met volunteers from a student brotherhood group that organized peer support without excluding anyone. One student asked whether certain uniforms looked too feminine for the ship's theme. The guide replied that practical clothing mattered more than stereotypes. I liked that answer. So did Eli, who wore a small pink ribbon on his lapel because I'd given it to him that morning.

午餐時，營養教育者說明，攝取過量糖分應放在整體飲食習慣中看待，而不是每吃一次甜點就自責。小君宣布她的靈魂獲得拯救。我們遇見由學生組成、開放所有人參與的同儕支持團體。有位學生問制服是不是太女性化，不符合船舶主題。導覽員回答，實用衣著比刻板印象重要。我喜歡這答案，以禮也是。他外套上別著一條粉紅緞帶，是我早上送他的。

During the afternoon tour, we had to cross an underpass beneath the harbor road. Clara wandered toward the wrong pier again, and I had to inquire at the information desk. The guide asked us to stay within a marked radius around the group leader for safety. June argued that no one should reign over her movements, then immediately lost sight of the ship. When we found her, she was admiring a souvenir hat. Eli simply guided us back. “You have a serious shortcoming,” June told Clara. Clara replied, “Only one?” Their laughter echoed down the walkway.

下午導覽時，我們得穿過港口道路下方的地下通道。克拉拉又走向錯誤碼頭，我只好去服務台詢問。導覽員為安全起見，請大家待在領隊周圍標示的半徑範圍內。小君抗議沒有人能統治她的行動，接著就立刻看不見船了。我們找到她時，她正在欣賞紀念帽。以禮只是帶大家走回來。「妳有一個嚴重缺點，」小君對克拉拉說。克拉拉回答：「只有一個嗎？」她們的笑聲沿著步道傳開。

Later, Eli and I sat on the deck overlooking the harbor. I confessed that I sometimes worried our different personalities would cause trouble. “You're careful,” I said, “and I'm always discovering new ways to embarrass us.” He gently tucked a loose strand of hair behind my ear. “That's not the same as being wrong for each other.” A boat horn sounded below. He explained that learning about health had reminded him that care wasn't just fixing problems. Sometimes it meant listening before offering a solution. I realized he was doing exactly that now, and my nervousness slowly eased.

後來我和以禮坐在甲板，望向港口。我坦白有時會擔心我們個性太不一樣。「你很小心，」我說，「我卻一直發現新的丟臉方式。」他輕輕把一縷散髮塞到我耳後。「那不等於我們不適合彼此。」下方傳來船鳴。他說這次健康講座提醒他，照顧一個人不只是替對方解決問題，有時是先聽再說。我發現他現在正這麼做，心裡的緊張便慢慢退去。

As sunset colored the water, June appeared wearing the admiral's spare hat. “All hands on deck,” she cried. “Captain June has defeated every woe!” The guide chased her, laughing, because she had forgotten to return it. Eli squeezed my hand. We were two different people, standing on a very old ship that had survived worse weather than our occasional misunderstandings. Maybe that was enough for now: not a promise that nothing difficult would happen, but confidence that we would turn toward each other when it did. Then June tripped over a rope, and I decided to postpone the philosophy until after we rescued her.

夕陽染紅水面時，小君戴著上將的備用帽子出現。「全員到甲板，」她大喊，「小君船長已征服一切悲傷！」導覽員笑著追她，因為她忘了歸還帽子。以禮握緊我的手。我們是兩個不同的人，站在一艘曾經經歷比我們誤會更嚴重風浪的老船上。眼下這樣或許就夠了：不是保證永遠不會有困難，而是相信遇到困難會轉向彼此。然後小君被繩子絆倒，我決定哲學話題還是等救完她再繼續。

The captain sent our class a thank-you note, complete with a photograph of the borrowed hat. Clara framed it and declared our field trip a triumph. I put the note beside my lecture materials. It reminded me that asking for help is not a weakness, and that a good relationship can make room for different kinds of courage.

船長寄來感謝卡，還附上借用帽子的照片。克拉拉把它裝框，宣布校外教學大獲全勝。我把卡片放在講義旁。它提醒我，求助不是弱點，而好的關係能容納不同形式的勇氣。
