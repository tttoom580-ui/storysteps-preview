Wanted: One Campus Crush

戀愛嫌疑犯通緝中 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

Professor Chen called us “a promising comparative experiment.” I called us an insurance claim waiting to happen. At twenty-one, I preferred studying romance from a safe distance, but for our university's Culture Night, she had assigned me to narrate an exhibition called Love Through the Ages. My partner was Eli Wu, the quiet student who managed the history department's archive. Eli could sort centuries-old letters in minutes. I could lose my phone while holding it. Professor Chen looked from him to me and smiled. Apparently, that was exactly the problem she wanted us to solve.

陳教授稱我們為「很有前景的比較實驗」；我則認為，我們根本是一場等待發生的保險事故。二十一歲的我一直認為，戀愛這門學問最好保持安全距離來研究。可惜，教授指定我在大學文化之夜，擔任「穿越時代的愛情」展覽旁白。我的搭檔是吳以禮——歷史系檔案室的安靜男生。以禮能在幾分鐘內整理好好幾個世紀前的情書，我卻能在手機拿在手上時把它弄丟。陳教授看看他，再看看我，露出微笑。顯然，這正是她希望我們解決的問題。

Eli arrived with an orderly schedule, color-coded labels, and a suggestion that we abbreviate “Love Through the Ages” to LTA. “That sounds like a transportation authority,” I said. “Love is a journey,” he replied without blinking. One of my eyelids began to twitch. Our budget was finite, and the fashion club accidentally delivered a costume box to our table. Inside were a plastic diamond, a gangster hat, and a vintage bra labeled “1950s fashion display.” Eli stared at the bra as if it were an unexploded bomb.

以禮帶著井然有序的時間表、按顏色分類的標籤，還建議把「穿越時代的愛情」縮寫成 LTA。「聽起來像大眾運輸局，」我說。「愛情也是一段旅程，」他面不改色地回答。我的一邊眼皮開始跳。我們的預算有限，偏偏服裝社還把一箱戲服誤送到我們攤位。箱裡有塑膠鑽石、假黑幫帽，以及一件貼著「1950 年代服飾展示用」標籤的復古胸罩。以禮盯著胸罩，彷彿那是一顆未爆彈。

“Hide it,” I whispered. Eli grabbed the nearest folder and pressed everything against his bosom, taking the unfortunate garment along with it. At precisely that moment, Professor Chen appeared. “Excellent!” she declared. “You two have found your historical material!” Eli went red. I considered changing universities. Then a white envelope slipped from his folder. On its front was written, For the person I can't stop thinking about. Eli barely looked at me outside class, except when I might drop something. Still, for one ridiculous second, I hoped.

「快藏起來，」我小聲說。以禮抓起最近的資料夾，把所有東西連同那件尷尬的衣物一起緊緊抱在胸前。就在這個精準得令人崩潰的時刻，陳教授現身了。「太好了！」她宣布，「你們已經找到歷史素材了！」以禮臉紅了，我則開始考慮轉學。接著，一封白色信封從他的資料夾滑了出來。封面寫著：「給那個讓我無法停止想念的人。」除了我快把東西摔下來的時候，以禮平常幾乎不看我。可是有那麼荒謬的一秒，我竟然希望那封信屬於我。

Culture Night began at the busy junction between the library and the student center. I was supposed to anchor the opening ceremony, but somebody had placed a theatrical warrant on my desk: “AUTHORIZED SEARCH FOR A STOLEN HEART.” Beneath it lay a photograph of Clara, the drama club's leading actress. Of course. Eli liked Clara. He helped her rehearse a play about a lovesick criminal. The letter made sense, and my confidence went for a walk without me.

文化之夜在圖書館和學生活動中心交會的熱鬧路口揭幕。我原本要主持開幕式，卻發現桌上有人放了一張戲劇道具搜查令，上頭寫著：「授權搜查失竊之心。」搜查令底下還壓著話劇社女主角克拉拉的照片。原來如此。以禮喜歡克拉拉。他最近一直在陪她排練一齣關於癡情罪犯的戲。那封情書突然變得合情合理，而我的自信也不告而別，獨自散步去了。

At the charity booth, my roommate June, who worked part-time as a bank teller, offered to bribe me with a chocolate muffin. “Ask him,” she said. “Do you like Clara?” “That's an interrogation, not flirting.” “Fine. Ask whether the emotional evidence is applicable to your situation.” June studied law and was unbearable. Then smoke rose from the cooking society's stall. Someone tried to broil mushrooms beneath an extra-virgin olive oil banner. They resembled spacecraft wreckage. Eli ran straight toward the smoke. Naturally, I ran after him.

在慈善攤位，兼職當銀行櫃員的室友小君想用一個巧克力馬芬收買我。「去問他，」她說，「你是不是喜歡克拉拉？」「那叫審問，不叫調情。」「好吧，那就問他，那些感情證據是否適用於妳的情況。」小君讀法律，現在已經變得令人難以忍受。接著，烹飪社的攤位就冒出了煙。有人想在寫著「特級初榨橄欖油」的布條下炙烤蘑菇，結果蘑菇看起來像墜毀太空船的殘骸。以禮直奔濃煙而去。我當然跟在後頭。

We managed to save the decorations, though I twisted my ankle and began to limp. Eli insisted on walking me to the first-aid station. “You don't have to,” I said. “You have a whole festival to manage.” “The festival can survive five minutes without me.” “What about Clara?” He stopped. “What about her?” I shrugged, wearing a paper towel as a temporary shoe liner. “Your mysterious letter. The photograph. Your heroic rescue technique. It all points to Clara.” Eli looked so surprised that I wanted to disappear into the nearest recycling bin.

我們成功救下佈置品，但我扭傷腳踝，開始一跛一跛地走。以禮堅持陪我去急救站。「不用啦，」我說，「整個活動還等著你管理。」「活動少了我五分鐘也不會垮。」「那克拉拉呢？」他停下腳步。「她怎麼了？」我用一種腳上還墊著紙巾充當臨時鞋墊的人所能維持的最高尊嚴聳聳肩。「你的神祕情書、那張照片、你英勇的救援姿態。所有線索都指向克拉拉。」以禮震驚得讓我想直接躲進最近的資源回收桶。

“Clara plays the criminal,” he explained. “The photograph goes beside the fake warrant. And the letter—” He hesitated. For once, the world's most organized man couldn't finish a sentence. “The letter is private,” I said quickly. “You don't have to explain.” Eli nodded, but he looked disappointed. That hurt more than my ankle. Nearby, a trumpet cheerfully assaulted a romantic ballad. I had planned to confess my feelings with confidence and a graceful flourish. Instead, I had accused an innocent man of being in love with someone else.

「克拉拉演的是那個罪犯，」他解釋，「照片本來就要放在假搜查令旁邊。至於那封信——」他猶豫了。世界上最會整理東西的男人，竟然第一次接不完一句話。「情書是私人的，」我連忙說，「你不用解釋。」以禮點點頭，神情卻有些失落。這比腳踝還痛。我們沉默地站著，旁邊音樂社正在用過度歡快的小號演奏浪漫情歌。我原本打算充滿自信、瀟灑俐落地告白。結果我卻把一個無辜男生誤認成暗戀別人的人。

Then the projector behind us flashed the words For the person I can't stop thinking about. The media team had scanned every loose page on our desk, including Eli's envelope. “No,” he whispered. “Absolutely not.” I didn't think. I grabbed the microphone and announced, “Technical difficulty! Please enjoy our emergency presentation on the romantic history of mushrooms!” The audience laughed. Someone applauded. Eli switched off the projector before the letter itself appeared. When he came back, he was laughing so hard that his glasses slipped down his nose. “You saved me,” he said. “You saved the decorations,” I replied. “We're even.”

就在這時，我們身後的投影幕突然亮出一句：「給那個讓我無法停止想念的人。」顯然，影音組把我們桌上所有散落的紙張都掃描進簡報了，連以禮的信封都沒放過。「不會吧，」他低聲說，「絕對不行。」我根本來不及思考，直接搶過麥克風宣布：「技術問題！現在請欣賞我們臨時加播的『蘑菇浪漫史』！」觀眾笑成一團，甚至有人鼓掌。以禮趕在信件內容出現前關掉投影機。他回來時笑得眼鏡都滑到鼻尖。「妳救了我，」他說。「你救了那些佈置，」我回答，「我們扯平了。」

Later, we escaped to the quiet garden behind the library. Eli took out the same envelope and handed it to me. “If you're willing to read it,” he said. Inside, in his careful handwriting, was this: I like the girl who can turn a disaster into a joke. She talks too fast, forgets where she puts her phone, and makes every room feel less frightening. I keep inventing reasons to stand beside her. This is one reason I hope she'll notice. At the bottom he had written my name. I read it twice. Once wasn't enough.

後來，我們逃到圖書館後方安靜的花園。以禮拿出那封信，交給我。「如果妳願意看的話，」他說。裡面只有一頁紙，字跡一如既往地工整：「我喜歡那個能把災難變成笑話的女孩。她說話很快，總忘記手機放在哪裡，卻能讓任何房間都變得不那麼可怕。我一直在發明各種理由，好站在她身旁。希望這封信也能成為她注意我的一個理由。」最後一行寫著我的名字。我看了兩遍。看一次根本不夠。

“So,” I said, “this is not about Clara.” “Not even slightly.” “And you didn't just hand me a historical document by mistake?” “No.” “And if I ask you on a date, will you say yes?” Eli's ears turned pink. “That's the answer I've been preparing for three weeks.” I kissed his cheek before my courage could expire. From inside the student center came a crash, followed by June shouting, “WHO PUT A BRA ON THE CHARITY MASCOT?” Eli and I looked at each other. Our first date could wait until we rescued the university again.

「所以，」我說，「這不是寫給克拉拉的？」「完全不是。」「你也不是不小心把一份歷史文件交給我？」「不是。」「那如果我約你出去，你會答應嗎？」以禮的耳朵變粉紅色了。「這個答案，我已經準備三個星期了。」趁勇氣還沒耗盡，我親了一下他的臉頰。學生活動中心裡突然傳來一聲巨響，接著是小君的咆哮：「到底是誰把胸罩套在慈善吉祥物頭上？」我和以禮對看了一眼。看來，我們的第一次約會得等到最後一次拯救大學之後了。
