The Space Octopus Problem

戀愛嫌疑犯通緝中 · 第 21 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The week after Eli's scholarship offer, our class visited an aviation museum. Its main hall contained an enormous model spacecraft hanging above an exhibition on aviation history. Clara called it an imposing symbol of ambition; June called it an excellent place to hide from adulthood. I had read the museum booklet beforehand, yet I still managed to enter through the wrong door. Our organizer found me standing beneath a display about prehistoric birds, wondering whether they counted as early aircraft. Eli rescued me with a smile, but neither of us mentioned the city where he might soon move.

以禮收到獎學金錄取通知後一週，我們班參觀航空博物館。大廳中央懸著巨大太空船模型，下方是航空史展。克拉拉稱它為象徵雄心的宏偉裝置，小君則稱之為逃避長大的絕佳場所。我事先讀過博物館小冊子，卻還是走錯入口。活動籌辦人找到我時，我正站在史前鳥類展下，思考牠們算不算早期飛行器。以禮笑著救我出來，但我們誰都沒提他可能即將前往的那座城市。

The next exhibition compared imaginary imperial starships with real engineering constraints. A professor explained how cellular structures inspired lightweight design and why the duration of a mission mattered. June became obsessed with a cartoon octopus astronaut and bought three stickers. At lunch a volunteer described an old proverb about journeys teaching people who they really were. “What if the journey is just to another city?” I asked. He said even a short trip could change someone's perspective. Eli touched my hand beneath the table. It was comforting, and also a reminder that we had not decided what our own journey would look like.

下一個展區比較虛構帝國星艦和真實工程限制。教授說明細胞結構如何啟發輕量化設計，以及任務持續時間為何重要。小君徹底迷上一隻卡通太空章魚，買了三張貼紙。午餐時，志工引用一則旅行讓人認識自己的諺語。「如果旅程只是去另一個城市呢？」我問。他說就算短途旅行也可能改變人的觀點。以禮在桌下碰了碰我的手。這令人安心，也提醒我們尚未決定自己的旅程將長什麼樣子。

The museum offered a workshop on respectful teamwork. Its leader explained the difference between persistent questions and harassment, and how authority must not be used to oppress quieter members of a group. That made me think of how often Eli allowed louder people to interrupt him. Later, when June demanded that he decide his future immediately, I asked her to stop. “We don't oblige him to answer on our schedule,” I said. June looked startled, then apologized. She could joke about almost anything, but she was a good friend when it mattered. For once, she remained silent for nearly a whole minute.

博物館提供尊重合作的工作坊。帶領者解釋持續提問與騷擾的差別，以及掌權者不該壓迫團體中較安靜的成員。我想到以禮常讓聲音較大的人插話。後來小君要求他立刻決定未來，我請她停下。「我們沒有義務要求他按照我們的時間表回答，」我說。小君吃了一驚，隨後道歉。她幾乎什麼都能拿來開玩笑，但真正重要時，她仍是好朋友。這次她竟然安靜了快整整一分鐘。

In the museum café, a family exhibition discussed the history of food transport, from poultry farming to refrigerated shipping. Clara discovered a model chicken wearing goggles and insisted on posing beside it. The curator explained that one early flight had ended in peril because the pilot ignored weather warnings. Eli listened closely. “Planning isn't wrong,” he said quietly. “But neither is wanting something that scares you.” I looked at him. “You mean the scholarship?” He nodded. He admitted he wanted to go, but feared making me feel abandoned. I told him I feared that too, and felt strangely relieved to say it aloud.

博物館咖啡廳旁的家庭展介紹食物運輸史，從家禽飼養到冷藏船運。克拉拉發現一隻戴護目鏡的模型雞，堅持合照。策展人說明早期某次飛行因飛行員忽略天氣警報而陷入危險。以禮認真聽著。「計畫沒有錯，」他輕聲說，「但想要一件令人害怕的事也沒有錯。」我看向他。「你是說獎學金？」他點頭。他承認想去，卻怕讓我覺得被拋下。我說我也害怕，卻意外地因為說出口而鬆了口氣。

We sat together beneath the model spaceship while children ran past with souvenir stickers. Eli explained that the program would last one semester, not forever. He could return on weekends when possible, and we could visit each other without promising impossible schedules. “I don't want you to give up something meaningful because of me,” I said. “And I don't want us to pretend distance is easy,” he replied. We were still uncertain, but the conversation no longer felt like a secret. He smiled, showing the tiny wrinkle that appeared beside his eye whenever he laughed. I decided I liked that detail more than any model in the museum.

我們坐在太空船模型下，孩子拿著紀念貼紙跑來跑去。以禮說明計畫只持續一學期，不是永遠。他有機會就能週末回來，我們也能互相探訪，但不必承諾做不到的行程。「我不希望你為我放棄重要的機會，」我說。「我也不希望我們假裝遠距離很容易，」他回答。我們仍不確定，但談話不再像祕密。他笑了，眼角出現每次大笑都會有的小皺紋。我決定自己比起博物館任何模型，都更喜歡這個細節。

As we left, June presented the three astronaut-octopus stickers. One went on my notebook, one on Eli's folder, and one on her own phone. “Team Space Romance,” she announced. Clara wanted to write a musical about us; we vetoed that idea at once. Eli walked me toward the bus and said he would accept the placement, provided we kept talking honestly about how we felt. I told him yes. It wasn't a fairy-tale ending, but it felt like a beginning we had chosen together. June pointed at the sticker and shouted that the octopus had eight arms, so long-distance hugging should be easy.

離開時，小君拿出三張太空章魚貼紙。一張貼在我的筆記本、一張貼以禮的資料夾，最後一張貼在她手機上。「太空戀愛小隊，」她宣布。克拉拉想寫一齣我們的音樂劇，我們立刻否決。以禮陪我走向公車站，說他會接受實習，但希望我們能一直誠實談感受。我說好。這不是童話式結局，卻像是我們一起選擇的開始。小君指著貼紙大喊，章魚有八隻手，所以遠距離擁抱應該很簡單。

Near the exit, a children's storytelling corner shared folklore about birds carrying messages across mountains and seas. The guide explained that these tales weren't engineering manuals, but they expressed hopes people had held long before airplanes existed. I listened beside Eli, imagining all the letters we'd send while living in different cities. Suddenly the distance felt less empty.

出口附近的兒童說故事角落，分享鳥兒替人跨越高山大海傳信的民間傳說。導覽員解釋那些故事不是工程手冊，卻承載人們在飛機出現很久以前的希望。我站在以禮旁邊聽，想像未來住在不同城市時我們會互傳多少信。距離突然沒那麼空洞了。
