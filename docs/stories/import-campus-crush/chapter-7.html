愛情農場與啄木鳥

Love, Mud, and a Woodpecker

戀愛嫌疑犯通緝中 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The campus sustainability club invited volunteers to its experimental farm, which occupied five acres beyond the northern intersection. Eli said fresh air would improve our concentration. June said a farm was a terrible place for a date because manure was not romantic. Clara suggested wearing a provisional wedding veil for photographs, which made Eli nearly drop his water bottle. We arrived to find a sign reading: NO BOXING, NO FEEDING MACHINERY, NO RACING THE GOATS. “Does this farm have unusual visitors?” I asked. “Last semester's drama club,” said the supervisor, pointing directly at Clara.

校園永續社邀請志工前往實驗農場。農場占地五英畝，就在北邊十字路口之後。以禮說新鮮空氣能提升專注力，小君說農場很不適合約會，因為肥料不浪漫。克拉拉提議戴著臨時婚紗頭紗拍照，害以禮差點掉水瓶。我們抵達時，看見告示：「禁止拳擊、禁止餵食機器、禁止和山羊賽跑。」「這裡的訪客都很奇特嗎？」我問。「上學期的戲劇社，」管理員直接指向克拉拉。

The guide explained that the farm studied soil fertility and ways to nourish plants without wasting water. A group of exchange students described family farming traditions, including one student whose grandfather had been an emigrant. Another student mentioned that he was gay and hoped to create an inclusive agricultural education program with his partner someday. “People often equate farming with one narrow kind of life,” he said. “But everyone eats.” Eli nodded, and I realized that his best quality wasn't being correct. It was being willing to listen when somebody else's experience was different from his own.

導覽員說農場研究土壤肥力，以及不浪費水又能滋養植物的方法。幾名交換生分享家族務農傳統，其中一位學生的祖父曾是移居海外的人。另一位同學提到自己是同性戀，希望有一天能與伴侶成立包容性的農業教育計畫。「大家常把農業等同於某一種狹窄的生活型態，」他說，「但每個人都要吃飯。」以禮點頭。我忽然發現，他最好的優點不是總是正確，而是願意傾聽與自己經驗不同的人。

Our next assignment was repairing a small fence. June called Eli a confirmed bachelor of tools because he arranged nails by length. “He is literally dating Summer,” Clara pointed out. “Details,” said June. While we worked, a woodpecker hammered at a nearby tree. Its rhythm made Clara announce that the forest had hired a drummer. I tried to imitate the sound and slipped in the mud. Eli caught my elbow before I fell. For one second, his confidence seemed to falter, but he held on. “Please don't forsake gravity entirely,” he whispered. I replied that gravity had already abandoned me.

接下來，我們負責修理一段小柵欄。小君見以禮按長度排列釘子，稱他為「工具界的標準單身漢」。「他明明就在和小夏交往，」克拉拉提醒。「細節而已，」小君說。我們工作時，一隻啄木鳥在附近樹上敲個不停。那節奏讓克拉拉宣布森林聘請了鼓手。我試圖模仿聲音，卻踩進泥巴差點滑倒。以禮抓住我的手肘，他的鎮定似乎有一秒動搖，但還是穩穩扶住我。「拜託不要完全拋棄重力，」他輕聲說。我說重力早就拋棄我了。

At lunch, the club announced a prohibition on wasting food. June interpreted it as a challenge and constructed a sandwich taller than her head. The group voted unanimously that she should reduce its height before eating. Then the supervisor mentioned a recent landslide that had damaged an access road during heavy rain. Nobody had been hurt, but the story cast a small gloom over the table. We helped pack emergency supplies for nearby residents, realizing that volunteering meant taking ordinary risks seriously, not inventing dramatic ones. June quietly made three smaller sandwiches for the workers.

午餐時，社團宣布禁止浪費食物。小君把它當成挑戰，堆出比頭還高的三明治。大家一致同意，她應先降低高度再吃。接著管理員提到最近一場大雨引發山崩，損壞了通行道路。幸好無人受傷，但這故事讓餐桌上的氣氛有點陰鬱。我們一起替附近居民打包應急物資，也明白志工服務是認真面對真實風險，而不是憑空製造戲劇。小君默默替工人做了三個較小的三明治。

In the afternoon, Clara confessed she was worried about a disciplinary warning after last week's fake mustache incident. She had attached it to the statue without permission, although nothing was damaged. Eli helped her write an apology that accepted responsibility without unnecessary drama. “There's no shame in admitting a mistake,” he said. I gave Clara an encouraging smile. My own biggest mistake that day had been wearing white shoes, and my punishment was visible to everyone. June claimed the mud had improved their artistic character. I informed her that vanity would not be enough to save my laundry.

下午，克拉拉坦白，她擔心上星期假鬍子事件會遭到紀律警告。她未經許可把鬍子貼到雕像上，雖然並未造成損傷。以禮協助她寫道歉信，承認責任又不過度戲劇化。「承認犯錯沒什麼好丟臉，」他說。我對克拉拉鼓勵地笑笑。我自己那天最大的錯誤是穿白鞋，懲罰則明顯得人人都看得見。小君宣稱泥巴增加了鞋子的藝術性。我告訴她，虛榮無法挽救我的洗衣工作。

As we left, Eli reached into his bag and produced a small packet of seeds. “We could grow these on the dorm windowsill,” he suggested. “Something orthodox and sensible?” I asked. “Actually, they're experimental purple tomatoes.” I stared at him. My cautious boyfriend had chosen a strange vegetable on purpose. He smiled, almost shyly. “You make me curious about surprising things.” Behind us the woodpecker resumed its loud performance. June shouted that this was definitely a wedding march. This time neither Eli nor I denied it; we simply walked toward the bus holding hands.

離開時，以禮從包裡拿出一小包種子。「我們可以種在宿舍窗台，」他提議。「很傳統正統、很合理的植物嗎？」我問。「其實是實驗品種紫色番茄。」我盯著他。這個謹慎的男朋友居然故意選了奇怪的蔬菜。他有點害羞地笑了。「是妳讓我開始好奇意外的事。」身後啄木鳥又敲了起來。小君大喊這絕對是婚禮進行曲。這回我和以禮都沒否認，只是牽著手朝公車走去。

On the bus, the group took a final vote on whether June should be allowed to design next month's event. The answer was unanimous: yes, but only if Eli could inspect the safety plan. June called this unfair government interference. Clara called it an excellent sequel. I quietly suspected the next disaster already had a date.

公車上，大家對小君能否規劃下個月活動進行最後投票。結果一致同意：可以，但前提是以禮必須先看過安全計畫。小君稱此為不公平的政府干涉，克拉拉則稱它為精彩續集。我暗自懷疑，下場災難已經排好日期。
