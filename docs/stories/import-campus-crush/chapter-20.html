The Butterfly Maze

戀愛嫌疑犯通緝中 · 第 20 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The university garden opened an educational maze about insects and conservation. Visitors were asked to walk clockwise in accordance with the safety arrows, but June had brought a compass and insisted that north was more romantic. “North is not a feeling,” Eli reminded her. “That sounds dubious,” she replied. The maze entrance featured a giant paper caterpillar and a row of butterfly ornaments. I had prepared a short talk about how our interests changed between adolescence and adulthood. Clara wanted to add a dramatic metamorphosis scene. I suggested she first master the difference between a stage butterfly and a real one.

大學花園開放了一座昆蟲保育教育迷宮。依照安全箭頭，訪客得順時針走；小君卻帶著指南針，堅稱北方比較浪漫。「北方不是感覺，」以禮提醒。「這說法值得懷疑，」她回答。迷宮入口有巨大紙毛毛蟲和一排蝴蝶裝飾品。我準備了一段介紹，談我們的興趣如何從青春期到成人時期改變。克拉拉想加一段戲劇性的變身表演。我建議她先分清楚舞台蝴蝶和真的蝴蝶。

My speech was intended to enhance public interest in small animals, not to celebrate my personal disasters. Unfortunately, June had created a slideshow of my worst photographs. One showed me falling over a garden hose. Another captured Eli holding the suspicious mushroom from our first festival. I tried to contradict June's claim that we were the campus's most accident-prone couple. She had twenty-six examples, including one in which I accidentally grazed a table with my elbow and sent five pencils flying. “Some evidence,” Eli said gently, “is difficult to dismiss.” I told him that love should make him less honest, not more.

我的演講原本是要提升大眾對小動物的興趣，不是慶祝我的個人災難。可惜小君做了一份簡報，全是我最糟的照片。有一張是我絆到花園水管，另一張則是以禮拿著我們第一次校慶那顆可疑蘑菇。我試著反駁小君說我們是校園最容易出事故的一對。她卻拿出二十六個例子，包括我手肘擦過桌面，讓五枝鉛筆飛走的瞬間。「有些證據，」以禮溫柔地說，「的確很難否認。」我告訴他，愛應該讓他少一點誠實，不是更多。

In the central garden, a student dressed as a solitary butterfly began to cry because her costume wing had torn. Clara called for emergency repairs, and Eli summoned the design club. I used a staple remover to take out a sharp fastener while the costume supervisor watched, then let the specialist fix the material safely. A younger child asked whether the butterfly was dying. “No,” I said, “just having a difficult fashion day.” The child nodded gravely. I had a hunch that Clara would quote that sentence for years. The performer returned to the stage and received a cheerful salute from the audience.

花園中央，一名扮成孤單蝴蝶的學生哭了，因為她的翅膀戲服破掉。克拉拉呼叫緊急修復，以禮召集設計社。我在服裝主管看著的情況下，用除釘器取下尖銳釘書針，再讓專業的人安全修補。小朋友問蝴蝶是不是快死了。「沒有，」我說，「只是今天服裝出問題。」孩子鄭重點頭。我有預感克拉拉未來會引用這句話很多年。表演者回到舞台，觀眾高興地向她致意。

Later, the education booth invited students to compare science facts with popular myths. One display explained that butterfly migration did not automatically represent human nationalism, even though artists sometimes used such images symbolically. A volunteer described how scientific intellect worked best when joined with curiosity and humility. June claimed she was exempt from humility because she had already completed a whole semester of legal writing. Nobody accepted that rule. Eli and I filled out the activity sheet together. He noticed my answers had improved since last year, and I told him that learning felt easier when mistakes weren't treated as disasters.

後來教育攤位邀請學生比較科學事實與常見迷思。一項展示說明，蝴蝶遷徙不會自然等同人類民族主義，儘管藝術家有時會拿它作象徵。志工說，科學智慧若與好奇心和謙遜結合，效果最好。小君聲稱她修了一整學期法律寫作，已獲豁免謙卑義務。沒人接受。我和以禮一起完成活動單。他發現我的答案比去年進步，我說有人不把錯誤當災難，學習就容易多了。

Near closing time, Eli received an email about his scholarship application. He was offered a placement in another city for the coming semester. The message made him go very still. I could tell at once that something important had happened. “Is it good news?” I asked. “Yes,” he said, but his smile was uncertain. We stepped away from the crowded garden. He showed me the offer, and I felt pride mixed with the sudden fear of distance. We had spent months learning how to stand beside each other. What would happen if the next stage of his life took him away?

接近閉園時，以禮收到獎學金申請的郵件。對方提供他下學期到另一座城市實習的機會。他突然安靜下來，我立刻知道發生了重要的事。「是好消息嗎？」我問。「是，」他說，笑容卻不確定。我們離開擁擠的花園。他把錄取通知給我看，我感到驕傲，也突然害怕距離。我們花了好幾個月學會站在彼此身旁。如果人生下一階段把他帶到遠方，會怎麼樣？

Eli apologized for not knowing what to say. I looked down at the paper caterpillar, now being carried away by volunteers. Nothing in the garden stayed exactly as it had been in the morning. Even butterflies changed because change was part of living. “You should take the opportunity seriously,” I said. “So should we,” he replied. We promised to talk without rushing. On the way home, I walked so quietly that June later said she thought I was practicing tiptoeing through a library. Perhaps I was. I was learning how to carry happiness and uncertainty at the same time.

以禮為不知道怎麼說而道歉。我低頭看志工正搬走紙毛毛蟲。花園裡沒有任何東西能永遠停留在早晨的模樣。連蝴蝶也會改變，因為改變是生活的一部分。「你應該認真考慮這個機會，」我說。「我們的事也應該認真考慮，」他回答。我們約好不急著下結論。回家路上，我安靜得小君後來說以為我在圖書館練習踮腳走路。也許吧。我正在學著同時捧著幸福和不確定。

Before we left, I noticed that my sloppy handwriting had made the activity sheet difficult to read. Eli offered me a spare pen but didn't take over the task. I rewrote my answers myself. It was a small thing, yet I liked that he trusted me to improve without needing to rescue me every time.

離開前，我發現自己潦草的字跡把活動單弄得很難看懂。以禮借我一枝備用筆，卻沒有接手。我自己重新寫了一遍。這是小事，但我喜歡他相信我能改進，而不是每次都必須被拯救。
