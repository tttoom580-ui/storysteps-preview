海岸旅館的國王陛下

King of the Coral Hotel

戀愛嫌疑犯通緝中 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

For the semester break, our research group visited a coastal town to study local architecture. Eli promised that this was only partly a date. Our bus passed an inland village beside a brook before reaching a majestic hotel overlooking the sea. The building had once belonged to a trading family, and the owner proudly displayed portraits of a long-forgotten monarch. June declared that the portraits showed her future husband. Clara asked if the hotel offered royal costumes. I asked whether breakfast was included. Eli, naturally, checked the room contract and the terms of our group's lease.

學期假期時，研究小組到海濱小鎮考察地方建築。以禮保證，這趟旅行「只有部分是約會」。巴士穿過一個位於小溪旁的內陸村落，抵達一間俯瞰大海、氣勢雄偉的旅館。旅館以前屬於一個貿易家族，老闆自豪地展示一位被遺忘君主的肖像。小君宣布畫中人是她未來的丈夫。克拉拉問有沒有皇家戲服，我則問早餐包不包括。以禮當然是在檢查房間合約和團體租約條款。

At the museum next door, a conservation worker explained how pollution could deprive sea animals of healthy habitats. We saw photographs of coral colonies and the tiny offspring of creatures living among them. The guide showed us an old lamp whose expiration date had long passed; the museum kept it as an artifact, not for use. “Please don't test it,” she told June, who had already picked it up. A local legislator was visiting to discuss public support for conservation. We listened from the back, where I could admire Eli's serious face without disturbing anyone.

隔壁博物館裡，保育工作者解釋污染如何剝奪海洋動物健康的棲地。我們看見珊瑚群和住在其中的小生物後代照片。導覽員還拿出一盞早過有效期限的舊燈，說館方是將它當文物保存，不是拿來使用。「請不要試它，」她對已拿起燈的小君說。當地一位立法委員來參觀，討論公共保育資源。我們站在後面聽，而我可以安靜欣賞以禮認真的表情，不打擾別人。

Our main assignment was to rotate through three research stations. At the first, students measured a building's roof. At the second, they studied an emergency surgical kit used by rescue teams decades earlier. At the third, they practiced reading navigation charts. My eyesight was perfectly fine, but I mistook a drawing of a lighthouse for a giant birthday candle. Eli laughed so suddenly that he nearly dropped his pen. “Don't strangle yourself trying not to laugh,” I told him. He breathed out, still smiling. I liked that he was no longer afraid to look ridiculous in front of me.

我們主要的任務是輪流到三個研究站。第一站測量建築屋頂；第二站研究幾十年前救援隊用的緊急外科手術器材組；第三站練習閱讀航海圖。我的視力完全正常，卻把燈塔圖案看成巨型生日蠟燭。以禮突然笑出來，差點掉筆。「不要為了忍笑把自己勒死，」我對他說。他吐了口氣，依舊笑著。我喜歡他現在不再害怕在我面前顯得可笑。

Lunch was a hearty meal prepared by the hotel kitchen. Seagulls hovered above the nearby harbor, hoping for scraps. June insisted on feeding them until a sign explained that doing so would harm the birds. The owner thanked her for stopping and showed us where to put food waste. Eli had brought a small present for me: a bookmark printed with a tiny coral pattern. “I thought it might elevate your reading experience,” he said solemnly. “It certainly raises my standards for gifts,” I replied. He looked relieved, as though this particular experiment had finally succeeded.

午餐是旅館廚房準備的豐盛餐點。海鷗在附近港口盤旋，等著撿食物。小君本想餵牠們，直到看見告示說餵食會傷害鳥類。老闆感謝她停止，並帶我們看廚餘放置處。以禮拿出給我的小禮物：一張印著珊瑚圖案的書籤。「我想它也許能提升妳的閱讀體驗，」他一本正經地說。「它的確提高了我對禮物的標準，」我回答。他看起來鬆了口氣，彷彿這項實驗終於成功。

In the evening we walked to a lighthouse. The wind was strong, but the ocean gave us a steady sense of momentum, as though the whole world were breathing. Eli had planned a group photograph, yet I insisted on one picture with just the two of us. “My insistence is scientifically justified,” I said. “Evidence?” he asked. “I like you.” He stopped walking and looked at me with a seriousness that made the cold vanish. “I like you too,” he said. Then he kissed me, without waiting for a photographer, a schedule, or a royal announcement.

晚上我們走到燈塔。風很強，但大海的節奏帶來一股穩定前進的動力，彷彿世界正在呼吸。以禮安排了團體照，我卻堅持要一張只有我們兩人的照片。「我的堅持有科學根據，」我說。「證據呢？」他問。「我喜歡你。」他停下腳步，認真看著我，讓我忘了寒冷。「我也喜歡妳，」他說。接著，他吻了我，沒有等待攝影師、行程表或皇室公告。

Back at the hotel, Clara revealed she had filmed our silhouettes against the lighthouse beam. She promised to keep the video private. June called it a violation of her right to mock us publicly, but she agreed to silence in exchange for the last pudding. I kept the coral bookmark inside my notebook. The trip had begun with contracts, buildings, and the odd portrait of a king. It ended with something I could not measure or list: the warmth of Eli's hand, the smell of sea air, and the feeling that an ordinary holiday had become a memory I would carry for years.

回到旅館，克拉拉透露她拍下我們站在燈塔光束前的剪影，答應影片只留給我們。小君說這侵害了她公開吐槽的權利，但為了最後一個布丁，她同意閉嘴。我把珊瑚書籤夾進筆記本。旅行從合約、建築和奇怪國王肖像開始，結束時留下了無法測量或列清單的東西：以禮掌心的溫度、海風的味道，以及平凡假期變成珍貴回憶的感覺。

On the way back, our bus stopped at a petrol station for a scheduled break. Eli bought two cups of tea, and I showed him the photograph from the lighthouse. We both looked windswept and happy. He asked whether I'd like to visit the coast again. I said yes, but next time we should leave the portraits of ancient kings alone.

回程巴士依行程停在加油站休息。以禮買了兩杯茶，我拿燈塔前的合照給他看。我們被風吹得亂七八糟，卻很快樂。他問我還想不想再去海邊。我說想，但下次就別理那些古代國王肖像了。
