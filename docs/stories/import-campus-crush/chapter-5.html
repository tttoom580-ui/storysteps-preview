海邊的全民投票

The Seaside Referendum

戀愛嫌疑犯通緝中 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Our university's Marine Science Academy invited volunteers to help prepare a coastal conservation day. Eli signed us up before I could remind him that our last adventure had involved indoor flooding. “The sea is already wet,” he argued. “What could possibly go wrong?” I informed him that this sentence was now banned in our relationship. We traveled with twelve other students to a small town where a local fishery cooperative would host a community banquet. June came along as the legal team's representative, although her true interest seemed to be the promise of unlimited shrimp. Clara brought a microphone and five emergency costume changes.

大學海洋科學學院邀請志工協助籌備海岸保育日。以禮在我提醒他上次冒險造成室內水災之前，就幫我們報名了。「海本來就是濕的，」他辯稱，「還能出什麼事？」我正式宣布，這句話禁止出現在我們的戀愛關係中。我們和另外十二名學生一起到一座小鎮，當地漁業合作社將舉辦社區宴會。小君以法律團隊代表身分同行，雖然她真正的興趣似乎是無限量供應的蝦。克拉拉帶著麥克風和五套備用戲服。

At the harbor, a guide explained how the offshore wind affected fishing boats and why farms should keep excess fertilizer away from streams. Eli listened carefully through one ear of his headphones, recording notes for a student podcast. I pointed to a damaged wooden pier and asked whether it had suffered a fracture. “Wood cracks,” the guide said kindly. “Bones fracture.” Eli looked at me with the patience of a man correcting a footnote. I declared that my vocabulary was simply being adventurous. He offered to refine our questions together, which sounded better than being corrected in public.

到了港口，導覽員說明離岸風如何影響漁船，以及農田為何應避免多餘肥料流入溪水。以禮單邊戴著耳機，認真聽講並替學生播客錄筆記。我指著受損木碼頭問：「它是不是骨折了？」導覽員親切回答：「木頭會裂，骨頭才會骨折。」以禮以校正註腳的耐心看著我。我宣布自己的字彙只是比較勇於冒險。他提議一起改進我們的提問方式，聽起來比當眾被糾正好多了。

The town council was holding a nonbinding student referendum about whether the beach should allow more weekend vendors. June immediately formed a campaign. Her first accusation was that the opposition intended to replace every seashell with a parking ticket. Nobody had proposed such a thing. Eli asked us to distinguish facts from enthusiastic fiction. A prospective vendor thanked him and explained that her family rented a small stall near the harbor. She was also a tenant in the town, so she understood the tension between earning money and protecting quiet neighborhoods. We listened rather than making slogans.

鎮公所正舉行一場不具約束力的學生公投，討論週末是否開放更多海灘攤販。小君立刻組了競選團隊。她的第一項指控是反對派打算把所有貝殼換成停車罰單。其實根本沒人這麼提議。以禮提醒我們分清事實和過度熱情的想像。一位有意申請攤位的商販向他道謝，並解釋家人在港邊租了小攤。她自己也是鎮上的房客，明白賺錢和維護寧靜社區之間的拉扯。我們決定先聽，而不是喊口號。

During the cleanup, Eli showed me how volunteers could harness tidal information to collect trash safely. I was assigned to record bottled-water intake so no one became dehydrated. Unfortunately, Clara mistook my checklist for the guest list of the evening banquet and announced, “Everyone who drinks water gets a free lobster.” Twelve people tried to shuffle toward the food tent at once. The cook delivered a twenty-minute sermon on why lobsters could not appear by magic. June looked genuinely wounded. “It's unfair to raise hopes and then remove shellfish,” she said. I thought of telling her this was not a constitutional issue, but let it pass.

清理海岸時，以禮示範志工如何利用潮汐資訊安全撿拾垃圾。我負責記錄瓶裝水攝取量，避免有人脫水。不幸的是，克拉拉把我的清單誤認為晚宴來賓名單，宣布：「喝水的人都能免費領龍蝦。」十二個人同時拖著腳步往餐帳移動。廚師花二十分鐘長篇訓話，解釋龍蝦不會憑空變出來。小君看起來真的受傷了。「先給人希望又剝奪海鮮，太不公平，」她說。我原想提醒這不是憲法問題，最後還是算了。

At sunset we walked along a serene stretch of sand, apart from the crowd. “I'm sorry I keep turning dates into projects,” Eli said. I stopped beside him. “I don't resent your projects. I like how you care about things.” It was our first serious disagreement about nothing, and our first serious conversation about something important. His maiden attempt at a romantic surprise was a paper boat folded from the back of an unused event map. Inside, he had written, “Next time, you choose the place.” I laughed. “That's dangerous freedom.” “I trust you,” he said, as if it were the easiest fact in the world.

日落時，我們離開人群，沿著一段寧靜的沙灘散步。「抱歉，我總把約會變成專案，」以禮說。我在他身邊停下。「我沒有怨恨你的專案。我喜歡你這麼在乎事情。」我們第一次認真討論了一件看似沒什麼、卻很重要的事。他第一次試著製造浪漫驚喜：用沒用過的活動地圖背面摺了一艘紙船。裡面寫著：「下次由妳選地點。」我笑了。「那可是很危險的自由。」「我相信妳，」他說得像全世界最簡單的事實。

Back at the banquet, the volunteers chose a compromise proposal in the referendum. Both sides began to rejoice, proving that a student vote could still produce good humor. The cooperative served fish, vegetables, and precisely zero surprise lobsters. June put down her fork and announced that she had learned humility. Nobody believed her. I slipped Eli's paper boat into my pocket and leaned against his shoulder. He did not ask for a schedule. Instead, he asked what I wanted tomorrow. “Sleep,” I said. “And maybe pancakes.” He opened his mouth, probably to plan it, then kissed my forehead instead.

回到宴會現場，志工們在公投中選出折衷方案。雙方都歡欣鼓舞，證明學生投票有時也能帶來好心情。合作社端出魚、蔬菜，以及數量精確為零的驚喜龍蝦。小君放下叉子，宣布她學會了謙卑。沒人相信。我把以禮摺的紙船放進口袋，靠在他肩上。他沒有詢問行程，反而問我明天想做什麼。「睡覺，」我說，「也許再吃點鬆餅。」他張嘴似乎想安排時間表，卻改成輕輕吻了我的額頭。
