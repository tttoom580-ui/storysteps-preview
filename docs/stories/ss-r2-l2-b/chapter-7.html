項鍊與安靜的聲音

A Necklace and a Quiet Voice

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The following week Ethan invited Xia to visit his grandmother, who had once worked at the campus radio station. The old woman greeted Xia with warm toast and a pleasant smile. A blue china cup stood beside the window, and a silver necklace hung from a small hook on the wall. Ethan explained that his grandmother had given him the necklace to carry to a repair shop, but he had never found time. Xia asked about its origin. His grandmother said it had belonged to her sister, who had left the city long ago. The story made Ethan quiet. He seemed to carry more family memories than he ever showed at school.

隔週，以森邀夏去拜訪祖母。祖母過去曾在校園廣播站工作。老人家拿溫熱的吐司和親切的笑容迎接夏。窗旁放著一只藍色瓷杯，牆上的小掛勾吊著一條銀項鍊。以森解釋，祖母交代他拿項鍊去修，但他始終沒找到時間。夏問項鍊的來源。祖母說，它原本屬於她很久以前離開這座城市的妹妹。這個故事讓以森安靜下來。他似乎承載了比平日在學校表現出來更多的家庭記憶。

His grandmother showed them an old radio form with names written in careful lines. Each listener used to send advice about songs, local news, and student life. “A good host must address a question, not merely beat the other speaker,” she said. Ethan's ears turned red. Xia tried not to smile. The grandmother pointed at a black-and-white photograph. It showed students sorting letters after midnight. One boy had been rather naughty and had hidden the microphone behind a chair, but none of the others had been angry. “They learned by making mistakes,” she said. Xia thought of the camera falling at the shelter and felt strangely welcome.

祖母拿出舊電台表格，上面整齊寫著姓名。從前每位聽眾都會來信，對歌曲、地方新聞和學生生活提供建議。「好主持人要回應問題，而不只是擊敗另一個發言者，」她說。以森耳朵紅了，夏努力忍住笑。祖母指著一張黑白照片。照片裡，學生在午夜後整理信件。有個淘氣男孩把麥克風藏到椅子後面，但其他人都沒有生氣。「他們靠犯錯來學習，」祖母說。夏想起收容所差點摔落的攝影機，奇妙地感到自己被接納。

Later, they sat with the grandmother to listen to a short recording from the past. The image was rough because the old lens of a recording camera had been damaged, but the audio remained clear. A young voice explained that students wanted a room where ordinary people could speak. “The actual problem was never a lack of talent,” the voice said. “It was that many people believed their stories were not important.” Xia asked who had spoken. The grandmother answered, “It was me.” Ethan looked surprised. He had known her as someone kind and quiet, not as a young woman who had argued for the whole campus to hear.

後來，他們陪祖母聽一段舊錄音。當年錄影攝影機的鏡頭壞了，所以影像模糊，但聲音依然清楚。年輕的聲音說，學生想要一個讓普通人也能發言的房間。「真正的問題從來不是沒有才能，」那聲音說，「而是太多人覺得自己的故事不重要。」夏問當時是誰在說話。祖母回答：「就是我。」以森很驚訝。他只認識親切安靜的祖母，沒想到年輕的她曾爭取讓全校聽到自己的主張。

Ethan's grandmother also knew a retired sound expert who could clean old tapes without damaging them. She offered to ask him for advice. Xia said the recordings should be copied before anyone tried to repair them, so there would be another version if something went wrong. Ethan smiled at her caution. A month earlier, he would have been the one explaining that rule. Now Xia was reminding him. The grandmother watched them exchange ideas and looked pleased. She seemed to know something neither young adult had yet said aloud, but she was kind enough to keep it to herself.

以森的祖母還認識一位退休錄音專家，懂得在不傷害舊帶子的情況下清潔它們。她願意替他們請教。夏說在嘗試修復前應先複製錄音，免得出錯後沒有備份。以森因她的謹慎而微笑。一個月前，這種規則會是由他向她解釋；現在則是她提醒他。祖母看著兩人交換意見，神情欣慰。她似乎知道兩位年輕成年人尚未說出口的事，卻善良地留在心裡。

Ethan and Xia discussed the role of their new show on the walk home. He said a good debate needed a winner. She said a good interview could have two people who understood each other better at the end. “Are you saying I should stop debating?” he asked. “No,” Xia replied. “I am saying your skill can do more than win.” They stopped beneath a streetlight. Ethan admitted that he sometimes felt like a prisoner of people's hopes. His teachers wanted him to speak perfectly, and his family expected every plan to succeed. Xia did not give him easy comfort. She simply stood there and listened.

走回家的路上，以森與夏討論新節目的角色。他說好的辯論需要勝利者。她說好的訪談能讓兩個人在結束時更了解彼此。「妳是說我該停止辯論嗎？」他問。「不是，」夏回答，「我是說你的能力不只可以用來獲勝。」他們停在路燈下。以森坦承，自己有時像被別人的期待關住的囚犯。老師希望他發言完美，家人期待每個計畫都成功。夏沒有給他輕易的安慰，只是站在那裡聽著。

Before they parted, Xia said she could help sort the old tapes and return them to the studio, if his grandmother agreed. Ethan smiled. He said his grandmother would be delighted because she had looked after those recordings alone for years. The thought pleased Xia. She had been trying to save a room; now she was helping save the voices that had once filled it. “You have a new job,” Ethan said. “And you?” Xia asked. “I will finally repair that necklace.” It was not a grand promise, but it was honest, and she liked that more than another careful speech.

分開之前，夏說如果祖母同意，她可以幫忙整理舊錄音帶，再送回工作室。以森微笑，說祖母一定很高興，因為她多年來都獨自保管這些錄音。夏也感到開心。她原本只是想救一個房間；如今卻也在保存曾充滿其中的聲音。「妳有新工作了，」以森說。「那你呢？」夏問。「我終於要去修那條項鍊。」這不是宏大的承諾，卻很真誠。比起另一篇字字斟酌的演說，她更喜歡這樣的話。
