不再假裝

No More Pretending

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 24 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

On the morning of the ceremony, a failure in the bus system delayed Xia's arrival. She rushed into the old bakery downstairs from the hall, hoping to find breakfast. The baker had made salty bread and sweet buns in the shape of a camel and an ape. A balloon floated above the counter. Xia had no appetite, but she bought a bun and sat down. She remembered the day she first met Ethan, when she had called him her enemy because he seemed too handsome and confident to understand her. Now she knew how wrong that first idea had been. A person's appearance could hide fear, care, and the nerve it took to admit a mistake.

頒獎典禮當天早晨，公車系統故障，害夏遲到。她匆忙走進會堂樓下的老麵包店，想找早餐。麵包師做了鹹麵包，以及駱駝和猿猴造型的甜麵包。櫃台上方飄著一顆氣球。夏沒胃口，仍買了一個麵包坐下。她想起初遇以森那天，因為他看起來太英俊、太有自信，不可能理解自己，便稱他為敵人。現在她知道最初想法有多錯。一個人的外表可能藏著恐懼、關懷，以及承認錯誤需要的勇氣。

Ethan arrived a few minutes later. He had gone to the café to look for her after noticing her absence at rehearsal. When she explained the bus problem, he laughed and offered to walk with her. They passed the garden where their first film had been made. The gardener was watering new plants, and a group of students helped carry soil. Xia saw that the work had survived without a camera. Ethan said their contest had been a mixture of good plans, poor choices, and unexpected friends. “And one very difficult partner,” Xia added. “Former difficult partner,” he corrected. They both smiled.

幾分鐘後，以森也到了。他發現夏沒出現在彩排，便去咖啡館找人。夏解釋公車的事，他笑著說陪她一起走。他們經過最早拍片的花園，高先生正替新植物澆水，一群學生幫忙搬土。夏看見，即使沒有攝影機，工作仍在繼續。以森說，這場競賽混合了好計畫、差選擇與意外出現的朋友。「還有一個非常難搞的搭檔，」夏補充。「前難搞搭檔，」他糾正。兩人都笑了。

They stopped by the studio before the ceremony began. Ethan found the old camera label and remembered the time he had tried to fix it without reading the manual. Xia teased him for that failure. He said it had taught him how to behave with a partner: ask before changing a shared plan. This simple rule became the basis of their work. Xia looked at the room and felt a rush of memory. Her hands were shaking, but she did not need to hide her nerves. “The work may tire us, but we can share it,” he said. They had survived mistakes, bad weather, and public questions. Now they could face a result they did not control.

典禮開始前，他們先到工作室看看。以森找到舊攝影機標籤，想起自己曾沒讀說明就試著修它。夏拿那次失敗取笑他。他說那讓自己學會如何與搭檔相處：改變共同計畫前先詢問。這條簡單規則成了他們工作的基礎。夏望著房間，回憶一瞬間湧來。她的手在發抖，但不必藏起緊張。「工作可能讓我們疲累，但我們可以分擔，」他說。他們撐過錯誤、惡劣天氣和公開質問。現在能面對無法控制的結果了。

At the hall, the director announced that the team had completed every required stage. They would receive a prize for community reporting, while another team had won the overall contest. The decision hurt for a moment. Xia had hoped for the larger award, but she knew the judges had reasons she could read. More important, the university agreed to a two-year trial for a student-led studio, funded through an open account and checked each term. The group cheered. Their room would survive, though it would still require careful work. Xia felt that the result was complete in a way a simple first-place win could never have been.

到了會堂，主任宣布團隊完成所有必要階段。他們獲得社區報導獎，而總冠軍由另一隊拿下。夏短暫地感到難過。她本來希望拿更大的獎，但知道評審有可以查閱的理由。更重要的是，大學同意讓學生自主工作室試行兩年，使用公開帳戶，每學期檢查。大家歡呼。房間能存續，雖然還需要細心工作。夏感到這結果比單純奪冠更完整。

After the ceremony, Ethan asked Xia to walk with him to the empty garden path. He looked more nervous than he had during any debate. “I made a promise to tell you the truth,” he said. “So here it is. I like you. Not because we won a prize, and not because I need you to stay here. I like the person you choose to be.” Xia took time to answer. She thought about her work, her future, and the respect they had learned. “I like you too,” she said. “But I do not want us to become another project with a deadline.” Ethan nodded. “Then we can begin slowly.”

典禮後，以森邀夏到空蕩的花園小路散步。他看起來比參加任何辯論時都緊張。「我答應過會告訴妳真話，」他說。「所以我要說了。我喜歡妳。不是因為我們得獎，也不是因為需要妳留下。我喜歡妳選擇成為的那個人。」夏花了些時間回答。她想著自己的工作、未來，以及兩人學會的尊重。「我也喜歡你，」她說。「但我不希望我們變成另一個有截止日期的計畫。」以森點頭。「那就慢慢開始吧。」

They did not promise to be together forever. They promised to speak clearly, make space for each other's decisions, and share a meal that had nothing to do with filming. Xia reached for his hand, and he waited until she took it herself. The small scene would never appear on their channel. That was part of what made it special. They returned to the hall where Nora and Professor Lin were waiting. There were budgets to approve, new students to train, and many stories still to hear. But for one quiet minute, Xia let herself enjoy a beginning that belonged only to the two of them.

他們沒有承諾永遠在一起，而是答應清楚溝通、尊重彼此的決定，並一起吃一頓與拍攝無關的飯。夏伸手向他，以森等她自己握住。這個小小場景永遠不會出現在頻道上。正因如此，它特別珍貴。兩人回到會堂，諾拉與林教授正在等他們。還有預算要通過、新學生要培訓，以及許多故事等著被聽見。但在安靜的一分鐘裡，夏允許自己享受這個只屬於兩人的開始。
