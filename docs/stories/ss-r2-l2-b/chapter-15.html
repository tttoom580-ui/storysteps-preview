有兩道門的園遊會

The Fair with Two Doors

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 15 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The campus fair began in a narrow lane behind the library. Someone had built a cardboard castle for children, and colorful lights hung from its ceiling. Nearby, a food stand sold mango drinks, pork rice, and cold fruit from a refrigerator. Xia was helping manage the stage, while Ethan carried equipment between the two entrances. They were tired but excited. Altogether, more than thirty student groups were taking part. Nora asked them to share a workbook so each team could record its task and the money it received. The system was simple, and Xia liked it because every group had an equal place on the page.

校園園遊會在圖書館後方的窄巷展開。有人替孩子們搭了紙板城堡，天花板掛滿彩燈。附近餐攤販售芒果飲料、豬肉飯，以及從冰箱取出的冰涼水果。夏幫忙管理舞台，以森在兩個入口間搬運設備。兩人疲累卻興奮。總共有三十多個學生團體參與。諾拉請大家共用一本工作紀錄簿，讓各隊登記任務與收款。制度很簡單，夏喜歡，因為每個團體在頁面上都有平等的位置。

A woman named Mrs. Hu came to the public health booth. She had recovered from cancer and now helped other patients find practical support. She asked not to be treated as a sad symbol. Xia respected that request and focused the interview on transport, food, and the work of volunteers. At the end Mrs. Hu suggested a phrase for the video: “Care is a daily choice.” The crowd gave a warm cheer when she thanked the students. Ethan saw Xia smiling beside the camera, and for a moment he forgot the contest numbers. The fair felt full of people choosing to help one another without asking for a prize.

胡女士來到公共健康攤位。她曾罹患癌症，康復後協助其他病人尋找實際支持。她要求不要把自己當成悲傷的象徵。夏尊重這個要求，把訪談重點放在交通、食物與志工工作。結束時，胡女士建議影片加上一句話：「關懷是每天的選擇。」她向學生致謝時，人群熱烈歡呼。以森看著鏡頭旁微笑的夏，有一瞬間忘了比賽數字。園遊會充滿了不求獎賞、願意互助的人。

Then Nora made a discovery. A library worker had found a box in a room nearby, and its label matched the one from the missing donation records. The door required a password, so the worker asked the manager to open it rather than guessing. Inside were old letters, a list of equipment, and a note about a fair agreement made when the studio began. The papers said that any major change to the room needed a public student meeting. Xia saw the word fair underlined twice. “We have found it,” she whispered. Ethan reminded her that finding a document did not yet prove who had moved it or why.

接著諾拉有了新發現。一名圖書館員在附近房間找到一個箱子，標籤與失蹤的捐款紀錄相符。房門需要密碼，所以工作人員請經理開門，而不是胡亂猜測。裡面有舊信、設備清單，以及工作室成立時公平協議的筆記。文件說，任何重大改變都需要召開公開的學生會議。夏看見「公平」一詞被畫了兩道底線。「我們找到了，」她低聲說。以森提醒她，找到文件還不能證明是誰移動了它，以及原因是什麼。

At the next table, a student tried to cut a wooden sign with a saw. The sound made Xia jump. She took one breath and asked the library worker whether they could photograph the papers. Permission was given, provided that private donor addresses were covered. Ethan used a sheet of paper to hide those lines, and Xia photographed the rest. The records included a negative note written years earlier, warning that outside money could influence a room's purpose. The writer had not banned support; the note simply asked students to keep decisions open. Xia felt as if the past were speaking directly to the present.

隔壁桌，一名學生用鋸子切木牌，聲音嚇了夏一跳。她深吸一口氣，問圖書館員能不能拍文件。館員同意，但要求遮住私人捐款人地址。以森用紙蓋住那些行，夏才拍下其餘部分。紀錄裡有一張多年前的負面警告，提醒外部資金可能影響房間用途。寫信的人沒有禁止贊助，只是要求學生保持決策公開。夏覺得過去彷彿直接對現在說話。

Professor Lin suggested that the team ask a librarian to explain the old document at the public meeting. She did not want students to treat one sentence as a magic answer. “I suggest we ask a librarian to explain it,” she told them. The librarian agreed and pointed out that some pages were copies made many years after the first agreement. Each page would need a clear date and source. Xia felt briefly disappointed, then remembered how much trouble a careless picture had caused her. She asked Nora to add a column for uncertain details in the workbook. “We can be hopeful and careful at the same time,” she said. Ethan nodded with a proud smile.

林教授建議團隊邀圖書館員在公開會議解釋舊文件。她不希望學生把一句話當成萬能答案。「我建議請圖書館員解釋，」她告訴大家。館員同意，也指出有些頁面是在首份協議多年後才製作的影本。每頁都需要清楚的日期與來源。夏短暫失望，接著想起一張草率照片曾造成多少麻煩。她請諾拉在工作紀錄簿增加「尚不確定」欄位。「我們可以同時懷抱希望與保持謹慎，」她說。以森驕傲地微笑點頭。

They did not publish the letters that night. Instead, Xia asked Professor Lin to help arrange a public meeting with the university. Ethan prepared a careful summary showing which parts of the agreement they had checked and which questions remained. Nora put the box back in safe storage and recorded who held the key. The three friends watched the fair lights go out one by one. Xia felt joy at finding the missing records, but she also understood that evidence created responsibility. They could no longer pretend this was only a contest for two students. They now had to help the community decide what its studio should become.

那天晚上他們沒有公開信件。夏先請林教授協助安排與校方的公開會議。以森做了審慎摘要，列出已核對的協議內容與仍未解答的問題。諾拉把箱子放回安全儲藏處，並記錄鑰匙由誰保管。三位朋友看著園遊會燈光一盞盞熄滅。夏因找到紀錄而開心，也明白證據會帶來責任。他們不能再假裝這只是兩名學生的競賽。如今，他們得協助社群決定工作室的未來。
