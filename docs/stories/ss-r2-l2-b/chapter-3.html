不實指控

A False Accusation

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On a cloudy Monday, Xia found a paper near the studio entrance. It accused Ethan of trying to cheat in the contest by buying fake views. Her classmate Nora had seen the same note on three doors. “Someone wants to force you apart,” she said. Xia did not want to believe it, but she knew that a false charge could cause real damage. They had only one chance to continue in the competition. Ethan was outside on his scooter, bringing a supply of batteries and cereal bars for the crew. When he read the paper, his face became pale. He made no attempt to laugh. “Ask me anything,” he said. “I will show you the records.”

多雲的星期一，夏在工作室入口找到一張紙，上面指控以森買假觀看次數，企圖在比賽中作弊。同學諾拉也在另外三扇門看見相同紙條。「有人想逼你們拆夥，」她說。夏不願相信，但知道不實指控也可能造成真實傷害。他們只有這一次機會能繼續參賽。以森騎著機車在外面，替團隊送來電池和穀片棒。他讀完紙條，臉色發白，完全笑不出來。「妳想問什麼都可以，」他說。「我會給妳看紀錄。」

The university's general office kept a record of contest viewers, and the student government had agreed to make basic numbers public. Xia asked Professor Lin whether they could check the figures without seeing private names. The professor nodded. They found that most viewers had joined after Nora's club members shared the link. A sudden rise had come from a student running group; its runner had posted their short garden video. There was no proof of paid views. Ethan also showed Xia how the broadcasting system could operate on another account. He never asked her simply to trust him. That mattered more than his words.

大學總務處保存比賽觀眾紀錄，學生會也同意公開基本數字。夏問林教授能否在不查看私人姓名的情況下核對數據。教授點頭同意。他們發現，多數觀眾是在諾拉的社員分享連結後加入的。突然增加的一批觀看次數來自學生跑步團；一名跑者曾發布他們的花園短片。沒有證據顯示有人買觀看數。以森也向夏展示直播系統能如何使用另一個帳號運作。他從沒要求夏只憑信任相信他。這比他的話更重要。

The pair met with the contest director, Ms. Yu. She wore one silver earring and carried a folder marked FINAL. She said the rules allowed an investigation when complaints appeared. Ethan asked whether she would link the paper to any real evidence before making a decision. Ms. Yu said she could not tell them who wrote it. Xia answered that she did not need the writer's name. She wanted a fair check. Ms. Yu gave them two days and warned them not to publish private records. The meeting ended with a stiff nod. In the hall, Xia felt angry, not because the office had asked questions, but because a stranger had turned the contest into a personal attack.

兩人去見競賽主任余女士。她戴著一只銀耳環，手上拿著標有「最終版」的資料夾。她說，規則允許在收到投訴時展開調查。以森問她，做決定前是否會把紙條與真正證據連結。余女士說不能透露寫紙條的人。夏回答，她不需要那個人的名字，只想要公平查核。余女士給他們兩天，並警告不要公開私人紀錄。會議在僵硬的點頭後結束。走廊裡夏很生氣，不是因為辦公室提問，而是陌生人把比賽變成人身攻擊。

Professor Lin later introduced them to a teacher from the liberal arts department. The teacher explained that people could disagree about numbers without changing the numbers themselves. A public report, she said, should make room for questions and corrections. Xia wrote that sentence at the top of their new record book. Ethan suggested giving every source a date, so later viewers could see when each fact had been checked. Nora drew three columns for evidence, questions, and answers. Their work suddenly looked less like an exciting contest and more like a small newsroom. Xia found that she liked the change.

林教授後來介紹一位人文學院老師給他們認識。那位老師說，人們可以對數字的解釋有不同意見，卻不該改動數字本身。公開報導應該留出提問和更正的空間。夏把這句話寫在新紀錄簿上方。以森建議每個來源都標上日期，讓後來的觀眾看得出事實何時被核對。諾拉畫出證據、問題、回答三欄。他們的工作突然不像刺激的比賽，更像一間小新聞室。夏發現自己喜歡這個改變。

They prepared a short explanation instead of a fight. Xia made a chart showing where the public views had come from, and Ethan wrote a plain sentence about what they did not know. Their producer, Nora, suggested that they invite the running club to explain its part of the story. During the live talk, the student runner described sharing the garden clip after training. He had never been paid. Several viewers asked if the pair would name the person behind the note. Xia refused. “Our purpose is to check a claim, not to start another rumor,” she said. Ethan looked at her with quiet respect. He had expected her to shout, but she had chosen a stronger answer.

他們準備了一段簡短說明，而不是吵架。夏製作圖表，呈現公開觀看數的來源；以森寫了一句白話，說明他們還不知道的事。擔任製作人的諾拉建議邀跑步社說明事情經過。直播中，一名跑者說自己在訓練後分享花園影片，從未收過錢。幾位觀眾問他們會不會公布紙條作者的名字。夏拒絕了。「我們的目的是查證指控，不是製造另一個謠言，」她說。以森安靜而敬重地看著她。他本以為她會大吼，沒想到她選擇了更有力的回答。

The contest office later confirmed that the numbers were normal. Xia was relieved, yet she still felt the danger ahead. Someone had tried to hurt the team and had failed only because records were available. She suggested saving copies of each broadcast and keeping a record of every change to the video. Ethan agreed at once. As they closed the studio, he offered her the last cereal bar. “Peace?” he asked. “For tonight,” she said, taking it. Outside, the sky was clearing. Neither of them knew who had written the note, but now they knew how they would face the next one: together, and with evidence.

比賽辦公室後來確認數字正常。夏鬆了一口氣，卻仍感覺前方有危險。有人試著傷害團隊，而他們之所以能撐過來，只因為有紀錄可查。她提議保存每次直播的副本，也記下每一次影片修改。以森立刻同意。關工作室時，他把最後一根穀片棒遞給她。「和好？」他問。「今晚可以，」她接過來說。外頭的天空漸漸放晴。他們不知道是誰寫了紙條，但已知道下次該如何面對：並肩站著，拿出證據。
