糟透了的第一次搭檔

A Very Bad First Meeting

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Lin Xia saw her new partner before he saw her. Ethan Chen was sitting in the campus café, discussing the temperature of his coffee as if it were a national problem. A short beard made him look older than twenty-three. On his coat was a bright watermelon pin. Xia almost laughed. Then she remembered him rejecting her plan last month. She was twenty-two, in her final year, and the student radio room was about to close. She needed a new show to save it. She did not need his calm personality telling her why she would fail. "Wrong table?" he asked when she stopped. "I hope so," she said.

林夏先看見了她的新搭檔，對方還沒注意到她。陳以森坐在校園咖啡館裡，討論咖啡的溫度，認真得像在處理國家大事。短鬍子讓他看起來比二十三歲成熟，外套上卻別著一枚鮮豔的西瓜徽章。夏差點笑出來，隨即想起上個月他否決她提案的樣子。她二十二歲，即將畢業，學生廣播室卻要關了。她需要一個新節目挽救它，不需要他那冷靜的個性再告訴她一次，為什麼她會失敗。「找錯桌了？」她停下時，他問。「希望是，」她說。

Professor Lin, their teacher, arrived carrying a rubber bag full of camera cables. She explained that a live video contest would give its winner money to keep a student media space open for one year. The contestants would work in pairs chosen by the department. Xia and Ethan had been placed together. “That has to be a crime,” Xia said. Ethan replied, “Not a real crime, I hope.” The professor did not smile. She asked them to make a plain, useful program about everyday campus life. Their first subject was a gardener named Mr. Gao, whose small garden had suffered after someone used a strong chemical near the vegetable beds. A yellow leaf lay on the table as evidence.

林教授提著裝滿攝影線材的橡膠袋走來。她說一場直播競賽將提供獲勝者一筆經費，讓一處學生媒體空間能再開放一年。參賽者必須兩人一組，搭檔由系上指定。夏和以森正好被分在一起。「這根本是犯罪，」夏說。以森回答：「希望不是真的犯罪。」教授沒有笑。她要求兩人製作簡單而實用的校園日常節目。第一個題目是高先生的菜園；有人在菜圃旁使用強烈化學藥劑，害花園受損。一片黃色葉子就放在桌上當證據。

“Before you begin, divide the work,” Professor Lin said. “One person can ask questions, and the other can watch the sound and picture. Do not forget that the winner must report how the prize money is spent. Even a tiny purchase needs a record, and you must understand any tax on the payment.” Ethan offered to handle the numbers. Xia said she would not accept a partner who made every choice for her. To test the camera, they had to weigh a small box of equipment and move it to the garden. Ethan carried the heavy box without a complaint. Xia noticed that his hands were not as soft as she had expected.

「開始之前先分工，」林教授說。「一個人可以負責提問，另一個人注意聲音與畫面。別忘了，得獎者必須報告獎金用途。即使很小的支出也要留下紀錄，並理解付款涉及的稅。」以森主動負責數字。夏說，她不會接受一個替自己做所有決定的搭檔。為了測試攝影機，他們得先替一小箱設備秤重，再搬到花園。以森毫無怨言地抱起沉重箱子。夏注意到，他的手沒有她想像中那麼細嫩。

In the garden, Mr. Gao spoke about the plants rather than his own trouble. He said he often came before sunrise, sometimes still in pajamas under his old coat. Ethan asked whether the university could do him a favor by paying for new soil. Xia asked how the students could relate his problem to their own lives. Mr. Gao looked at them with kind eyes. “People like the flowers,” he said, “but they forget the person who keeps them alive.” For a moment Xia had the strange feeling that he was speaking about the radio room as well. Ethan became quiet, and she wondered whether he had understood the same thing.

在花園裡，高先生談的是植物，而不是自己的困境。他說自己常在日出前到這裡，有時舊外套裡還穿著睡衣。以森問學校能不能幫忙出錢換土。夏則問，怎麼讓學生理解這件事與自己生活的關係。高先生眼神溫和。「大家喜歡花，」他說，「卻忘了照顧花的人。」有一瞬間，夏奇妙地覺得他也在說廣播室。以森安靜下來，她想知道他是否也聽懂了。

At the garden gate, Mr. Gao stopped them. "Please show the plants too," he said. "Not just my worried face." Xia lowered the camera. She had been planning to use his face for the opening shot. "All right," she said, and wrote down his request. Ethan moved closer to photograph the yellow leaf, keeping the nearby homes out of the picture. "We should ask for the soil test," he said. Xia waited for a speech about her mistakes. None came. "I thought you were going to tell me how to do my job." He looked up. "You already heard him." For once, she had no quick reply.

到了花園門口，高先生叫住他們。「也請拍植物，」他說，「不要只拍我擔心的臉。」夏放下攝影機。她剛才確實想用他的表情當開場。「好，」她說，把要求記下來。以森靠近拍那片黃葉，避開附近住家的畫面。「我們應該問問土壤檢測結果，」他說。夏等著他指教自己哪裡做錯了，他卻沒再說下去。「我以為你又要教我怎麼工作。」「妳已經聽見他的話了。」他抬頭說。這一次，她竟想不出立刻回嘴的話。

Their first recording was only two minutes long. Xia's words were quick, while Ethan waited carefully between questions. They did not yet know how to work together. He wanted to check every fact; she wanted the audience to hear a real voice. But when a gust of wind knocked the camera to one side, both reached for it at the same time. They caught it before it fell. “You owe me a favor,” Ethan said. “You owe me half a camera,” Xia answered. Such a foolish exchange made them laugh. As they walked back to the café, Xia decided that she would give the partnership one honest week before calling it impossible.

第一段錄影只有兩分鐘。夏說話很快，以森則謹慎地在問題之間停頓。他們還不懂怎麼合作。他想核對每一個事實；她則希望觀眾聽見真實的聲音。可是當一陣風把攝影機吹歪時，兩人同時伸手，及時在它落地前抓住。「妳欠我一個人情，」以森說。「你欠我半台攝影機，」夏回答。這種傻氣的對話讓他們笑了。走回咖啡館的路上，夏決定先真誠地給這段合作一週機會，再下結論。
