店主的信

The Shopkeeper's Letter

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A week later Xia found an article in a local paper about the old student radio room. It described a chapter of the city's history when a group of small shops had helped students buy their first microphones. The article showed a painting of the old street and named a shopkeeper called Mrs. Fang. Xia and Ethan visited her store near campus. A puppy slept under the counter, and a child sat nearby with a crayon and a drawing book. Mrs. Fang smiled when they explained their project. “I remember the station,” she said. “It was a place where people spoke when the usual system did not listen.”

一週後，夏在地方報紙找到一篇學生廣播室的舊文章。文章記述城市歷史中的一段時期：一些小店曾集資幫學生買第一批麥克風。報上刊有舊街道的畫作，還提到名叫方太太的店主。夏與以森前往她在校園附近的店。櫃台下有隻熟睡的小狗，旁邊坐著一個拿蠟筆和畫冊的孩子。方太太聽完他們的企畫後笑了。「我記得那座電台，」她說。「當平常的制度不肯聽人說話，那裡就能讓人發聲。」

Mrs. Fang opened a drawer and took out a letter with a rusty nail holding several pages together. It said local businesses had donated money on one condition: students must always have a voice in decisions about the room. The promise was not a legal requirement, but it was an important part of the station's founding agreement. Xia felt hope shine through her fear. In addition, the letter referred to a list of donors kept in the library. That was the box they were trying to find. Ethan carefully took a photograph with permission. He also wrote down which section of the agreement they needed to check with the university.

方太太打開抽屜，拿出一封舊信，幾張紙被生鏽的釘子固定在一起。信裡說，當地商家捐款有一個條件：學生必須始終有權參與工作室的決定。這個承諾不等同法律條文，卻是電台成立協議的重要部分。夏在恐懼中看見希望。此外，信還提到一份存放於圖書館的捐款人名冊。那正是他們在找的箱子。以森在獲准後小心拍照，並記下協議中需要向校方查證的段落。

Their daily work suddenly felt more serious. Xia wanted to paste the whole letter on the show's page that night. Ethan suggested waiting until they could speak to the university archive office. “Unless we know whether the document is complete, we could make things worse,” he said. Xia almost argued, then remembered the false accusation against him. She did not want to treat another person with the same carelessness. She sent Mrs. Fang a copy of the photograph and asked whether the shopkeeper would be willing to speak on camera. Mrs. Fang agreed, provided that they also told the story of the other stores that had helped.

他們的日常工作突然變得更嚴肅。夏想當晚就把整封信貼到節目頁面。以森建議等到能向大學檔案室詢問後再發布。「除非我們知道文件是不是完整的，否則可能把事情弄得更糟，」他說。夏差點爭辯，卻想起他曾遭不實指控。她不想用同樣粗心的方式對待別人。她把照片副本寄給方太太，問店主願不願意上鏡。方太太答應，但要求他們也講述其他幫忙店家的故事。

Mrs. Fang later showed them a historical photograph of the street before the university had grown. In the picture, shop owners stood beside young students and held a sign welcoming anyone who wanted to learn. Xia thought about her new partner and how much their own ideas had changed. Ethan asked the shopkeeper whether he could copy the photograph for the archive. She agreed, but asked them to keep the names of the owners with it. “A picture without names can lose its meaning,” she said. Xia wrote that down, thinking of how easily the work of ordinary people could be forgotten.

方太太後來又給他們看一張歷史照片，拍的是大學擴建前的街道。照片裡，店主與年輕學生一起站在歡迎所有想學習者的標語旁。夏想到自己的新搭檔，以及兩人的觀念改變了多少。以森問店主可否複製照片放進檔案。她同意，但要求保留店主姓名。「沒有名字的照片，可能失去意義，」她說。夏把這句話記下，想到平凡人的努力多容易被忘記。

That evening they worked side by side in the studio. Xia wrote the questions, and Ethan checked the history against the newspaper. Their partnership had become more useful because each knew what the other could do. The shopkeeper's child had drawn a small sun for them, and Xia placed it beside the camera. She said it would remind them to let ordinary stories shine. Ethan laughed at her serious tone. “Is that our new sign?” he asked. “Maybe,” she said. “If we succeed, I want the room to belong to more people than just us.” Ethan stopped smiling and answered, “I want that too.”

當晚兩人在工作室並肩作業。夏撰寫訪談問題，以森拿報紙核對歷史。兩人知道彼此擅長什麼，合作因此更加有效。店主的孩子畫了一個小太陽送給他們，夏把它放在攝影機旁。她說，這能提醒他們讓平凡的故事發光。以森笑她語氣嚴肅。「這是我們的新標誌嗎？」他問。「也許吧，」她說，「如果成功，我希望工作室屬於更多人，而不只是我們。」以森收起笑容，回答：「我也這麼希望。」

Before leaving, Xia checked the recording log and saw one strange detail. The letter box had last been signed out by someone from the contest office, not the library. She felt a cold fear but did not call anyone a thief. She wrote down the date and asked Professor Lin for an appointment with the office. Ethan said they should bring Nora too, so another student could hear the answer. “You are becoming a careful reporter,” he said. Xia grinned. “And you are becoming less annoying.” Outside, the puppy from the shop appeared in a photograph on her phone, sleeping beside the little paper sun. The night felt a little brighter.

離開前，夏查看借閱紀錄，發現一個奇怪細節。最後一次借走信件箱的是競賽辦公室的人，不是圖書館。她心裡一冷，卻沒有稱任何人為小偷。她記下日期，請林教授安排辦公室會面。以森說應帶上諾拉，讓另一名學生一起聽回答。「妳越來越像細心的記者了，」他說。夏咧嘴一笑：「而你越來越不討人厭了。」外頭，手機照片裡的店主小狗睡在紙太陽旁。夜晚彷彿明亮了些。
