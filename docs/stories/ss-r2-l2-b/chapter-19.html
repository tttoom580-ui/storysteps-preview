離開的來賓

The Guest Who Walked Away

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 19 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A local delivery worker named Mr. Wu agreed to be the next guest. He had worked for a food company during a difficult year and wanted to describe how students could order meals without wasting food. The team planned a friendly discussion over pizza, but the company's new policy made the story harder. It required every employee to sign a form before speaking about work. Mr. Wu arrived alone, holding a paper bag and looking nervous. “I do not want trouble,” he said. Xia told him he could leave at any time. She had learned that a guest's right to stop was more important than a good show.

一名叫吳先生的地方外送員答應成為下一位來賓。他曾在艱難的一年為餐飲公司工作，想說明學生如何訂餐而不浪費食物。團隊本來打算邊吃披薩邊輕鬆討論，但公司的新政策讓事情複雜起來：員工談工作前都必須簽署表格。吳先生獨自到場，手提紙袋，看起來很緊張。「我不想惹麻煩，」他說。夏告訴他隨時可以離開。她已學會，來賓停止的權利比一集好節目更重要。

Before filming, they gave Mr. Wu a copy of their guest agreement to download and keep. Ethan explained its important parts, including how to ask for a correction. Nora placed a mat under the table so the chair would not slide. Mr. Wu laughed and admitted that he had a toothache, so he could not eat much pizza anyway. The joke helped him relax. Xia asked a few questions about his daily work and the effect of bad weather on deliveries. He answered carefully. But when she asked about the company's rules, he became silent and said he needed to stop.

拍攝前，他們讓吳先生下載並保存一份來賓同意書。以森解釋重要條款，包括如何要求更正。諾拉在桌下鋪墊子，讓椅子不會滑動。吳先生笑著承認自己牙痛，反正也吃不了多少披薩。玩笑讓他稍微放鬆。夏問了幾題日常工作與惡劣天氣如何影響外送。他謹慎回答，但她問到公司規定時，他沉默下來，說需要停止。

The team switched off every recording device. Mr. Wu said the company manager had warned employees that speaking to media could affect their jobs. He did not know whether that warning was legal or fair. Xia did not offer legal advice. She suggested he speak to a trusted worker representative if he wanted help. Ethan said they could protect the information he had already shared and not use any part he withdrew. Mr. Wu thanked them. His struggle was real, but it was not theirs to display without permission. After a long pause he chose to leave. Xia walked him to the door and did not ask him to return.

團隊關掉所有錄音設備。吳先生說公司經理警告員工，向媒體說話可能影響工作。他不知道這樣的警告是否合法或公平。夏沒有提供法律意見，而是建議他若需要幫助，可以詢問信任的勞工代表。以森說，他們會保護先前分享的資料，不使用他決定撤回的部分。吳先生向他們道謝。他的掙扎是真實的，卻不是他們能未經同意展示的故事。停頓許久後，他選擇離開。夏送他到門口，沒有要求他回來。

With no interview to air, the team made a short program about guest rights instead. They used examples from earlier episodes and asked viewers to send questions about safe reporting. A traditional radio host joined by phone and explained that an effective interview depended on trust. “You cannot protect a person by promising that nothing difficult will happen,” she said. “You can protect their choice about what they share.” Ethan added a clear list of steps the team used to check permission. Xia realized that losing a guest had not meant failure. Sometimes the necessary story was about the rules that allowed other stories to exist.

沒有訪談可播，團隊改做一集關於來賓權利的短節目，使用前幾集案例，請觀眾寄來安全報導相關問題。一位傳統電台主持人透過電話加入，解釋有效訪談依賴信任。「你不能用保證不發生困難來保護一個人，」她說，「但可以保護他決定分享什麼的權利。」以森補充團隊確認同意的步驟清單。夏明白，失去來賓不代表失敗。有時真正必要的故事，是那些讓其他故事得以存在的規則。

Mr. Wu later wrote a short note saying he was grateful the team had respected his difficulty. He explained that he needed time to speak with people he trusted before making any more public decisions. His note ended with a quiet prayer for the safety of workers during bad weather. Xia read it only to Nora and Ethan, because it was a private message. The three agreed not to turn his thanks into a new episode. They could learn from the meeting without using his fear as material. Xia kept the note in their protected records, where only the team could read it.

吳先生後來寄來一張短箋，感謝團隊尊重他的難處。他說在做出更多公開決定前，需要時間與信任的人討論。短箋最後寫了一句安靜的祈願，希望惡劣天氣中工作的員工平安。夏只讀給諾拉和以森聽，因為那是私人訊息。三人同意不把他的感謝變成新一集。他們可以從會面學習，卻不該把他的恐懼當材料。夏把短箋收在只有團隊能讀取的受保護紀錄裡。

That evening Ethan found Xia sitting alone on the studio floor. He said he had read the latest contest notice: their team had reached the final round, but the prize would be decided after a public presentation. Xia asked whether he still wanted to stay with her team. He said yes, unless she preferred a different arrangement. She looked at him for a long moment. “I want you here,” she said. “But I want us to tell each other about meetings that could affect our work.” Ethan agreed. He did not make a grand promise. He asked how they could record those contacts, and they began a shared log that same night.

當晚，以森發現夏獨自坐在工作室地板上。他說已看過最新競賽通知：他們進入決賽，但獎金要在公開發表後才決定。夏問他是否還想留在隊裡。他說想，除非她希望另作安排。她看著他好一會兒。「我希望你在這裡，」她說，「但我希望我們能告訴彼此所有可能影響工作的會面。」以森同意。他沒有做宏大的承諾，而是問怎麼記錄聯繫。兩人當晚就開始建立共用紀錄。
