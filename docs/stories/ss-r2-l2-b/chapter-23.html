必須回答的問題

The Questions We Must Answer

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 23 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The final hearing began in a formal meeting room with a long steel table. Xia placed a drawing of the proposed student studio on the screen. It showed three areas: recording, training, and public access. The design used a triangle to show how the three purposes supported one another. A committee member asked why the room needed so much width when students could record on phones. Xia explained that safe equipment, guest privacy, and lessons for beginners required space. She spoke in a calm manner, though her stomach was tight with stress. Ethan sat beside her, not to answer for her, but to help if she needed a fact checked.

最後聽證在一間有長鋼桌的正式會議室開始。夏在螢幕上展示學生工作室設計圖，分為錄製、訓練、公共使用三區。她用三角形表示三項用途如何互相支持。一名委員問，既然學生可以用手機錄製，房間為什麼需要那麼寬。夏解釋，安全設備、來賓隱私與初學者課程都需要空間。她語氣平靜，肚子卻因壓力緊繃。以森坐在旁邊，不是替她回答，而是在需要時協助核對事實。

A representative from the former sponsor offered a large new gift, but only if the school agreed to place its name on every program. Xia thanked him and said the public fund would accept donations without control over content. The representative asked who would be responsible if the room failed. Nora explained that an elected student team would report spending, while teachers would check safety. Ethan added that no donor or employer could tell a student what opinion to hold. The committee listened in silence. Xia realized she was no longer asking adults to save her room. She was presenting a system that people could test and change together.

前贊助商代表再提出一筆大額捐助，但條件是校方同意每集都放上公司名稱。夏向他致謝，表示公開基金可接受捐款，卻不讓捐助者控制內容。代表問若房間經營失敗，誰要負責。諾拉解釋，民選學生團隊會報告支出，教師則檢查安全。以森補充，任何捐款人或雇主都不能命令學生持有何種意見。委員們安靜聽著。夏發現，自己不再是請成年人拯救房間，而是在提出一套大家能一起檢驗與修改的制度。

During a break, a student with a ladybug pin came to speak to Xia. She said she had followed the show since the garden episode and had learned how to ask better questions. A volunteer handed Xia water because she looked thirsty. Her old friend Bo brought a sandwich, then joked that her hip might hurt from standing so long. Xia laughed. She was surprised to feel the weight of friendship all around her. No single person had built the project. Every guest, viewer, and volunteer had made it stronger. This was the story she wanted the committee to remember after the charts and numbers were forgotten.

休息時，一名別著瓢蟲胸針的學生走來找夏。她說自己從花園那集就追節目，也學會怎麼問更好的問題。一名志工遞水給夏，因為她看起來很渴。老朋友博帶來三明治，還開玩笑說她站太久，髖部大概會痠。夏笑了。她驚訝地感到友情的重量圍繞自己。這個計畫從來不是單一人建立的。每位來賓、觀眾和志工都讓它更強。她希望委員忘記圖表與數字後，仍能記住這個故事。

When the hearing continued, one member asked whether Xia would promise to remain at the university for five years. She refused to make a promise she could not control. “I may work in another city,” she said, “but the studio must survive without me.” Another member asked about training new hosts. Ethan offered a plan with classes open to any student, and Nora suggested a public review every term. The questions were difficult, yet Xia felt ready for them. She knew the difference between being a hero in a film and being a responsible person in real life. The first might save a day. The second built something that could last.

聽證繼續時，一名委員問夏能否承諾留在大學五年。她拒絕做自己無法控制的承諾。「我也許會去另一座城市工作，」她說，「但工作室必須在沒有我時仍能生存。」另一名委員問如何訓練新主持人。以森提出對所有學生開放的課程，諾拉則建議每學期公開檢討。問題很難，但夏已準備好。她知道電影英雄和現實中負責任的人有何不同：前者也許能拯救一天，後者才能建立長久的東西。

A committee member said the team could request help anytime during the trial period, but asked them to explain the limits. Xia said, “I offer a clear plan for training and guest rights. I realize that even a careful system will need review.” She described how new students would receive training and how guests could request changes. A diet club volunteer offered free snacks for long sessions, though the team would not make food a condition for joining. The room went silent when a student asked what would happen if Xia moved away. She realized that this was the question the whole plan had prepared her to answer. She would still care about the studio, but she would not be the only person responsible for its future.

一名委員說團隊試行期間可以隨時求助，但要求說明限制。夏說：「我提出清楚的訓練與來賓權利計畫。我明白即使謹慎的制度，也需要檢討。」她描述新學生會如何受訓、來賓怎麼要求更改。飲食社志工願意提供長時間活動的免費點心，但團隊不會把飲食當成加入條件。當一名學生問夏搬走後怎麼辦，房間忽然安靜。她領悟到，整個計畫一直在為回答這個問題做準備。她仍會關心工作室，卻不會是唯一對它的未來負責的人。

After the meeting, the committee said it would announce its decision at the contest ceremony. Xia and Ethan walked down the hall, both too tired to speak. Then Ethan stopped and asked whether they could talk after the ceremony, whenever she had time. His voice was steady, but she saw nervousness in his eyes. Xia said yes. She did not ask what he wanted to mention. She thought she knew, and the possibility made her smile all the way home. For the first time in weeks, the coming result did not feel like a judge deciding her whole future. It felt like one important door among several she could choose to open.

會後，委員表示會在競賽頒獎典禮公布決定。夏和以森走過走廊，累得一時說不出話。接著以森停下來，問典禮後她何時有空能不能談談。他聲音穩定，眼裡卻有緊張。夏說好，沒有問他想說什麼。她覺得自己知道，而那個可能性讓她一路微笑回家。幾週以來第一次，接下來的結果不再像一位裁判要決定她整個未來，而像眾多可選擇的大門之一。
