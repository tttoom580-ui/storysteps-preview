The Trial Broadcast

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first live trial took place beside the café window. Xia had made a simple display of photographs showing the garden's slow growth. Ethan had prepared a guide to the show's rules. Their professor warned that the university could cancel their place in the contest if they shared private information. A chain across the studio door kept curious students out, but it also made the small room feel like a place for secrets. Xia opened the window. “Let the people hear us,” she said. Ethan thought a good video should have beauty as well as clear facts. She told him that a clear fact could be beautiful too.

第一次直播試播在咖啡館窗邊進行。夏做了一面照片展示板，呈現花園緩慢的成長。以森則準備好節目規則指南。教授提醒他們，若洩漏私人資料，學校可能取消參賽資格。工作室門口有條鍊子擋住好奇的學生，但也讓小房間像藏著秘密的地方。夏打開窗戶。「讓大家聽得見我們，」她說。以森認為好影片除了事實清楚，也該有美感。她告訴他，清楚的事實本身也能很美。

Their current plan was to speak for twenty minutes and ask viewers to send one question. A student named Nora agreed to be their first guest. She was worried that the garden club might lose its membership because few people attended meetings now. “I do not want to hunt for someone to blame,” she said. “I want to find a way to bring people back.” Ethan placed a pear beside the microphone as a joke about healthy food, then moved it when Xia gave him a sharp look. The sound speaker made a loud noise. He fixed the cable quickly, but their first message from a viewer had already arrived: “Is this the worst show on campus?”

他們目前的計畫是播二十分鐘，並請觀眾送來一個問題。名叫諾拉的學生答應當第一位來賓。她擔心花園社因為出席率太低而失去社團資格。「我不想追著找誰該被責怪，」她說。「我想找到讓大家回來的方法。」以森在麥克風旁放了一顆梨子，開了個健康飲食的玩笑，夏瞪他一眼，他就把梨子移走。喇叭突然發出刺耳聲響。他很快修好線材，但第一則觀眾留言已經出現：「這是校園最爛的節目嗎？」

Xia wanted to respond with an angry answer, but Ethan raised one hand. “We can show our real self,” he whispered. “That is more useful than fighting a stranger.” They asked Nora what the club had already tried. She explained that they had played chess with new students, given away seeds, and held a super small plant sale. Nothing worked for long. The loss of the club room would also mean losing a place where students could rest without buying anything. Ethan's face changed. He knew what it meant to lose a quiet room. Xia could see that his interest was no longer just about winning the contest.

夏想生氣地回覆，以森卻舉起一隻手。「我們可以展現真正的自己，」他低聲說。「那比和陌生人吵架有用。」兩人問諾拉已經試過什麼辦法。她說，社團曾跟新生下西洋棋、送種子，也辦過一場超小型植物義賣。但都沒有長久奏效。失去社辦也代表學生少了一處不必消費就能休息的地方。以森的表情變了。他知道失去安靜房間是什麼感覺。夏看得出來，他現在感興趣的不只是贏得比賽。

A second viewer called their guest selfish for wanting a free room. Xia stopped the program for a second and asked Nora whether she wanted to answer. Nora said yes. “I care about this room because it helped me during a hard year,” she said. “I do not think that makes me selfish.” Ethan agreed, then asked what support the club really needed. Nora suggested a shared calendar, so students could choose work times. Xia put the idea on the screen. Their audience began to grow. Some viewers offered tools; others asked for a day when they could come and help. The room suddenly felt less lonely.

第二位觀眾說諾拉想要免費空間很自私。夏暫停節目一秒，問諾拉願不願意回答。諾拉說願意。「我在艱難的一年裡得到這間教室的幫助，」她說。「我不認為這樣叫自私。」以森表示同意，再問社團真正需要什麼支援。諾拉提議使用共用行事曆，讓社員選擇工作時間。夏把這個點子放到畫面上。觀眾人數開始增加。有些人願意提供工具，其他人詢問什麼時候可以到場幫忙。房間突然不再那麼孤單。

Before turning off the camera, Xia invited viewers to suggest one useful action for the garden club. A student proposed a weekly meeting at the same time, so members could plan around classes. Another offered a free poster design. Ethan wrote down the ideas and promised that the next episode would report which ones had worked. Nora said she liked this better than an exciting show that forgot its guests afterward. Xia agreed. The audience was not a crowd to be counted and left behind. People could become part of a story by helping it continue after the screen went dark.

關掉攝影機以前，夏邀觀眾為花園社提出一項實用行動。有人建議每週固定時間開會，讓社員能配合課表安排。另一人願意免費設計海報。以森記下點子，答應下集報告哪些方法奏效。諾拉說，她更喜歡這種節目，而不是熱鬧一時、之後就忘了來賓的秀。夏同意。觀眾不是算完數字就被丟下的人群。他們可以在螢幕熄滅後繼續幫忙，成為故事的一部分。

When the trial ended, the screen showed ninety-eight viewers. It was not a large number, but it was more than either host had expected. Professor Lin congratulated them on the clear discussion and the care they had shown their guest. She also warned them that the next broadcast would be harder. On the way out, Ethan admitted that he had once voted against the radio room because he thought money should go to a larger project. Xia did not forgive him at once. Still, she was pleased that he had told her without making an excuse. For the first time, she imagined they might become a real team.

試播結束時，畫面顯示有九十八位觀眾。這不是很大的數字，卻超過兩位主持人的預期。林教授恭喜他們討論清楚，也照顧到了來賓的感受，但提醒下一次直播會更難。離開時，以森坦承自己曾投票反對廣播室，因為當時認為經費應該投入更大的計畫。夏沒有立刻原諒他。然而，她很高興他說出真相，沒有找藉口。她第一次想像兩人或許真的能成為團隊。
