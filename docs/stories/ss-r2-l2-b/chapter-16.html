The Practice Alarm

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 16 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The university scheduled an earthquake practice on Wednesday, just as the team was preparing to share the old donation agreement. Xia was in a poor mood because the library manager had asked for another week to check the records. Ethan reminded her that an extra check could be valuable if it prevented an error. The practice alarm rang, and a bright arrow on the wall showed the safe exit. Students moved from the upper floor into the open yard. A volunteer wearing a large hippopotamus costume stood beside the gate to make the safety day less frightening. Xia laughed despite herself. It was difficult to stay angry at someone dressed as a smiling hippo.

星期三大學安排地震演練，正好碰上團隊準備公布舊捐款協議。夏心情不好，因為圖書館經理要求再給一週核對文件。以森提醒她，多一次檢查若能避免錯誤，就是有價值的。演練警報響起，牆上明亮的箭頭指向安全出口。學生從樓上移到空曠中庭。一名志工穿著巨大的河馬裝，站在大門旁，希望安全日不那麼令人害怕。夏忍不住笑了。要對一隻微笑河馬維持憤怒，實在很難。

After the alarm, the students joined a short hike around the campus hill to study safe meeting points. Their guide taught the basics of first aid and asked them to stretch before walking. Near the path, Ethan stopped to look at a rare little plant with a worm crawling across one leaf. Xia told him they had too much work for a nature lesson. He answered that noticing small things was part of their job. “Indeed,” said their guide, “a person who sees the path clearly is less likely to get hurt.” Xia accepted the gentle correction. She was learning not to treat every delay as an enemy.

警報結束後，學生沿校園小山進行短程健行，認識安全集合點。導覽員教基本急救，也請大家走路前伸展。小徑旁，以森停下來看一株稀有小植物，一條蟲正爬過葉面。夏說他們工作太多，沒時間上自然課。他回答，注意小事也是他們的工作。「的確如此，」導覽員說，「看清道路的人比較不容易受傷。」夏接受這個溫和提醒。她正在學習，不把每次延誤都當成敵人。

Back in the studio, the printer failed while Nora was making copies for the public meeting. The paper came out with missing lines, and the quality was too poor for anyone to read. Xia wanted to beg the department for a new machine, but Ethan suggested that they create a digital version first. They scanned each page slowly and compared the copy with the original. Professor Lin checked the important dates. A student from the drama club offered a royal blue folder to protect the paper from water. The small group worked quietly for two hours. Every person had a role, and nobody needed to be a hero.

回到工作室，諾拉為公開會議影印資料時，印表機壞了。紙張缺了好幾行，品質差到無法閱讀。夏想求系上換新機器，以森則建議先建立數位版本。他們慢慢掃描每一頁，再與原件比較。林教授核對重要日期。戲劇社學生提供深寶藍資料夾，保護文件不受潮。小團隊安靜工作了兩小時。每個人都有角色，不需要誰扮演英雄。

Ethan also suggested keeping a backup of the meeting papers outside the old building. A flood or fire could destroy both printed pages and a nearby computer. Professor Lin gave them permission to store a protected copy in the university archive. Xia wrote down the process and the names of the people allowed to open it. She no longer thought paperwork was the opposite of action. When documents mattered to a community, keeping them safe was a form of care. Nora sealed the originals in their folder and placed the copies beside the door, ready for the next day's questions.

以森還建議，把會議資料備份放在舊大樓之外。洪水或火災可能同時毀掉紙本與附近的電腦。林教授允許他們在大學檔案室保存受保護的副本。夏記下流程，以及獲准開啟檔案的人員姓名。她不再認為文書工作與行動相反。當文件對社群重要，妥善保存也是一種關懷。諾拉把原件封進資料夾，影本則放在門邊，準備迎接隔天的提問。

At sunset the manager called with news. The box had been moved because the library storehouse had suffered water damage, but the transfer had not been recorded properly. The manager apologized and promised to correct the log. Xia wanted to recover the missing details of who had ordered the move. Ethan said they could ask again at the meeting. He also reminded her that a mistake in record keeping did not by itself prove a bad purpose. The relation between the lost box and the sponsor was still unknown. Xia wrote that in large letters. She did not want her anger to grow bigger than the facts.

日落時，經理來電帶來消息。箱子是因為圖書館儲藏室曾受水害而移動，但移轉沒有妥善登記。經理道歉，答應更正紀錄。夏想繼續追查是誰下令搬走箱子。以森說可以在會議上再問。他也提醒，一次登記錯誤本身不能證明有惡意。遺失的箱子與贊助商之間是否有關聯，仍然未知。夏把這句話寫得很大。她不想讓憤怒超過事實。

Before going home, they tested their files on two different computers. Every page opened, and the important lines could be read. Xia looked at the completed folder and felt the first calm moment of the day. “We cannot make every problem disappear,” she said. “But we can make the next step clear.” Ethan agreed and offered to walk her to the bus stop. This time she did not pretend that the offer was unnecessary. They spoke about music and food rather than rules. Their work remained difficult, but she began to see a future in which they could share an evening without a camera or a contest between them.

回家前，他們用兩台不同電腦測試檔案。每頁都能開啟，重要文字也看得清楚。夏望著整理好的資料夾，第一次在今天感到平靜。「我們不能讓每個問題消失，」她說，「但可以讓下一步清楚。」以森同意，主動陪她走到公車站。這次她沒有假裝不需要陪伴。他們談音樂與食物，不談規則。工作依然困難，但她開始看見一種未來：沒有攝影機，也沒有競賽，他們仍能分享一個傍晚。
