海邊的選擇

The Choice at the Sea

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 20 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Ethan's father invited them to meet at a small restaurant near the ocean. He was a businessman whose company supplied video equipment to schools, and he had heard about the team's funding problem. He offered a major gift to the studio. Xia felt surprised, especially when he said the gift could be made in Ethan's name. She knew the money would fix several broken cameras and improve sound quality. But the idea also gave her pain. If their project depended on one family, would it still be independent? She asked for time to study the offer. Ethan's father looked at her with a curious expression but agreed.

以森的父親邀兩人到海邊的小餐廳見面。他是替學校供應攝影設備的商人，聽說團隊有經費困難，便提出捐一大筆錢給工作室。夏很驚訝，尤其聽見他說可以用以森的名字捐款。她知道那筆錢能修好幾台壞攝影機，也能提升音質，但心裡仍有疼痛般的不安。如果計畫依靠一個家族，還能獨立嗎？她要求時間研究提案。以森父親好奇地看著她，最後同意。

The restaurant served warm cocoa in cups with a small blue dot near the handle. Ethan watched the waves and said he had grown up hearing that one good decision could change a business. His father replied that a company could not stay alive by giving away everything. Xia agreed that money needed balance, but she asked whether the donation would come with any secret conditions. The older man said no, then admitted he hoped the gift would bring good attention to his company. He did not hide that wish. Xia appreciated the honesty but knew the team would need written terms if it accepted the money.

餐廳供應熱可可，杯柄旁有個小藍點。以森望著海浪，說自己從小聽父親講，一個好決定可能改變一家企業。父親回答，公司不能把一切都送出去還指望存活。夏同意金錢需要平衡，但問這筆捐款有沒有秘密條件。長者說沒有，接著承認希望捐款能讓公司獲得好形象。他沒有隱藏這個願望。夏欣賞他的誠實，卻知道如果接受，仍需要白紙黑字的條件。

Ethan's father spoke about the central office of his company, which served schools across the country. He said it had handled projects worth more than a billion in total over many years, but even the old organ used for music at a school could need costly repairs when a tiny part failed. Xia listened carefully. She wanted to refuse a private gift, not refuse all help from a person whose work she did not fully know. She suggested that his company could join the same public process as every other donor. The older man said he would read the conditions. Ethan looked grateful that she had given both honesty and respect a place at the table.

以森的父親談起公司中央辦公室，服務全國許多學校。他說多年來經手的計畫總額超過十億，但即使是學校演奏用的老管風琴，也可能因一個小零件故障而需要昂貴修理。夏仔細聽著。她想拒絕的是私人贈款，不是拒絕一個自己尚未完全理解其工作的人提供的所有協助。她建議他的公司和其他捐助者一樣參與公開流程。長者說會閱讀條件。以森感激她讓誠實與尊重都在桌上有了位置。

A child at the next table asked why the sea had so many waves. Her mother explained that the ocean was always moving, even when it looked calm. The child spoke with a funny expression and showed her tongue to the wind. Xia smiled. She began to wonder whether a public project had to keep moving in the same way, with different people helping at different times. Ethan's father asked about the birth of the radio room and seemed interested in the old donor agreement. He had not known its story. When Xia explained, he listened without interrupting. The conversation became less like a business meeting and more like a lesson about shared history.

隔壁桌有個孩子問海為什麼一直有浪。母親說，海洋即使看起來平靜，也始終在動。孩子做出滑稽表情，對著風吐舌頭。夏笑了，開始想一項公共計畫是否也該如此，不同時間有不同的人來幫忙。以森的父親問起廣播室的誕生，對舊捐款協議似乎很感興趣。他以前不知道這段歷史。夏解釋時，他沒有打斷。談話不再像商業會議，反而像是一起學習共享的歷史。

After lunch, Ethan walked with Xia along the shore. He admitted that he had once wanted to win the contest to prove himself to his family. Now he wanted to help the studio because it mattered to other people. Xia asked whether he would be angry if she refused the gift. He said no. “My father can make an offer,” he said, “but he cannot make your decision.” She liked that answer, yet she wanted Ethan to make choices for himself too. He said he would speak to his father about supporting a public fund instead of a private gift. That way, many people could contribute and no one donor would control the program.

午餐後，以森和夏沿海邊散步。他坦承曾想贏比賽來向家人證明自己，如今卻想幫工作室，因為它對其他人重要。夏問若拒絕捐款他會不會生氣。他說不會。「我父親可以提出邀請，」他說，「卻不能替妳做決定。」夏喜歡這答案，也希望以森能替自己做選擇。他說會和父親談，改成支持公開基金，而不是私人贈款。如此許多人都能捐助，也不會有單一捐款人控制節目。

The team returned to campus with no new money, but with a clearer plan. Nora made a chart showing how a public fund could work, and Professor Lin agreed to bring it to the final meeting. Xia felt sleepy after the long journey, yet her mind stayed active. She had started the contest afraid of losing a room. Now she was asking how to build something that could last without depending on her alone. Ethan stood beside the studio door and waited while she found her keys. “You know,” he said, “I think I would still want to make a show with you if there were no prize.” Xia looked at him, but neither spoke again just then.

團隊回到校園時沒有新經費，卻有了更清楚的計畫。諾拉製作公開基金運作圖表，林教授答應帶到決賽會議。夏長途奔波後很想睡，腦中卻仍不停思考。她開始比賽時只害怕失去房間，如今思考的是如何打造不只依靠自己的長久制度。以森站在工作室門旁，等她找鑰匙。「妳知道嗎，」他說，「就算沒有獎，我想我還是會想和妳一起做節目。」夏看著他，但兩人那一刻都沒再說話。
