雷雨夜

The Night of Thunder

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On Friday evening, the student food club asked Xia and Ethan to help with a meal drive. A snowy mountain trip had been canceled, so the club wanted to use its extra food for students who needed it. Their leader, May, had a brilliant plan: cook noodles in the dining hall, then send warm meals to nearby homes. The school allowed the work but warned them about electrical safety. Outside, thunder rolled across the sky. Xia was hungry and in a bad mood, but she knew the club needed attention more than she needed another perfect broadcast. Ethan checked the power cables while she talked to volunteers about the food list.

星期五傍晚，學生食物社請夏與以森協助餐食配送。原本的雪山旅行取消了，社團想把剩餘食材送給有需要的學生。負責人梅有個很棒的計畫：在餐廳煮麵，再把熱食送到附近住處。學校允許，但提醒他們注意用電安全。外頭雷聲滾過天空。夏肚子餓，心情也不好，但她知道社團比自己的一場完美直播更需要關注。以森檢查電線，她則訪問志工，確認食材清單。

Their first conflict began when Ethan asked to stop filming until the wires were dry. Xia wanted to keep the audience watching. “We will lose our connection,” she said. “We could lose a person if someone touches wet electricity,” he replied. She knew he was right, but the way he spoke made her feel like a careless child. May brought them two wool towels and asked them to work on a safer table. A rooster made of paper stood beside a donation box, and someone had drawn bright red skin on its face. The silly decoration made everyone smile for a moment. Xia put the camera down and helped move the boxes away from the rain.

第一場衝突發生在以森要求電線乾燥前停止錄影時。夏想讓觀眾繼續收看。「我們會失去連線，」她說。「如果有人碰到潮濕電線，我們可能會失去一個人，」他回答。她知道他是對的，但他的語氣讓她覺得自己像粗心的小孩。梅拿來兩條羊毛毛巾，請他們改到安全的桌邊工作。捐款箱旁擺著一隻紙公雞，臉上被塗了鮮紅色。滑稽的裝飾讓大家暫時笑了。夏放下攝影機，幫忙把箱子搬離雨水。

The power went out just as the first pot of noodles was ready. A mass of students had gathered at the door, and nobody knew whether they should wait or leave. Ethan found a safe battery lamp. Xia used her phone to spread a simple message: the meal drive would continue, but food would be served in smaller groups. Their method was not exciting, yet it worked. A volunteer discovered that the old kitchen had a gas stove and could still heat soup. Other students offered support by carrying water, checking names, and keeping the line clear. The club did not need one hero. It needed people who could listen.

第一鍋麵快煮好時，突然停電。一大群學生聚在門口，不知道該等還是離開。以森找到安全的電池燈。夏用手機傳送簡單消息：餐食配送會繼續，但改成分批領取。他們的方法不刺激，卻奏效。一名志工發現舊廚房有瓦斯爐，仍能加熱湯。其他學生幫忙提水、核對姓名、維持隊伍動線。社團不需要單一英雄，而需要願意傾聽的人。

During the break, a student said his appetite had disappeared after he heard the thunder. May gave him a small bowl of noodle soup and asked him to rest. She explained that the domestic kitchens in nearby apartments could help heat food if the dining hall remained closed. Xia called two volunteers to discover which kitchens were safe and willing to join. She reminded them not to enter private homes without permission. Later, the volunteers received praise from students who had been waiting for meals. Ethan listened to the thanks and said the best report should show these ordinary hands at work.

休息時，一名學生說聽見雷聲後就沒了胃口。梅給他一小碗麵湯，請他休息。她解釋，如果餐廳持續關閉，附近公寓的家庭廚房也許能幫忙加熱食物。夏打電話給兩位志工，請他們去查明哪些廚房安全且願意參與。她提醒志工，未經允許不可進入私人住家。後來，等餐的學生紛紛稱讚志工。以森聽著謝意，說最好的報導應該呈現這些平凡雙手的工作。

When the line grew shorter, Ethan apologized for his sharp words. “I was afraid,” he said. “I saw an electrical accident when I was younger. Whenever I hear that sound, I remember it.” Xia had not known this part of his life. She told him why losing the broadcast had frightened her too. Her family could not pay for another year of school without her scholarship, and she hoped the contest might lead to paid media work. The talk changed their conflict. It did not make either fear disappear, but both understood the reason behind it. Ethan promised to explain safety concerns without speaking down to her again.

隊伍漸漸縮短後，以森為尖銳的話道歉。「我害怕，」他說。「我小時候見過一次電氣事故。每次聽到那種聲音，我就會想起來。」夏原本不知道他生命中的這一段。她也告訴他，自己為何害怕直播中斷。沒有獎學金，她的家人無法負擔再一年的學費，而她希望比賽能帶來有薪媒體工作。這段談話改變了他們的衝突。恐懼沒有因此消失，但兩人都理解了背後的原因。以森答應以後解釋安全顧慮時，不再用高高在上的口氣。

By midnight the storm had passed, and the food club had delivered every meal. May gave them a small bowl of noodles and thanked them for their help. Xia tasted the warm soup and laughed because it was far too salty. Ethan laughed too, and for once neither tried to win an argument. Their final video showed the volunteers working together rather than a host giving orders. The comments praised the people who had carried food through the rain. Xia knew the show still had problems, but she felt the first real change between them. Trust was not something a camera could create. It was built through choices people made when nobody knew the result.

到午夜時風雨已過，食物社也送完每一份餐點。梅端來一小碗麵，謝謝他們幫忙。夏喝了口溫熱的湯，因為太鹹而笑出來。以森也笑了，這一次誰也沒想贏過對方。最後的影片呈現志工並肩工作，而不是主持人發號施令。留言稱讚那些冒雨搬餐的人。夏知道節目還有問題，卻感到兩人之間有了第一個真正的改變。信任不是攝影機能創造的，而是人們在不知道結果時，透過選擇一點一滴建立的。
