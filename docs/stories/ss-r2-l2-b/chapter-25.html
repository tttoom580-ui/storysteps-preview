永遠開著的門

The Door Stays Open

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 25 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Three months later, the student studio opened under its new rules. Its aim was simple: give ordinary people a place to speak and learn. At the entrance, a chart showed how donations were spent, and another showed the steps for reporting a problem. No company could control the stories. A student group had to obey the safety rules, but it could question them and suggest better ones. Xia had helped write the policy, yet she was not its master. The team had elected Nora as the first student leader. She welcomed guests with a curious smile and asked everyone to look around before the first public broadcast began.

三個月後，學生工作室依新規定開放。它的目標很簡單：讓平凡的人有地方說話、學習。入口有圖表列出捐款用途，另一張則說明如何提出問題。沒有公司能控制故事。學生團隊必須遵守安全規則，但也能質疑並提出更好的辦法。夏幫忙寫過政策，卻不是它的主人。團隊選出諾拉擔任第一任學生負責人。她帶著好奇的笑容迎接來賓，請大家在首場公開直播開始前四處看看。

The room's new appearance was simple, with bright windows and shelves for shared tools. A small sign said, “Every question is welcome,” plus a line explaining how to post a correction. One volunteer had written the sentence too rapidly and made it seem that complaints were forbidden. Xia helped her change the wording. It was a reminder that even friendly rules could be misunderstood. A student asked whether the new studio would always stay this way. Xia said she hoped it would improve. The room belonged to people who were still learning, and learning meant being willing to change. Even rapid change could be handled carefully when everyone was allowed to ask why.

房間的新外觀很簡潔，有明亮窗戶和共享工具架。一塊小標誌寫著「歡迎任何問題」，另外加了一行說明如何張貼更正。一名志工寫得太快，讓句子看起來像禁止申訴。夏幫忙改了措辭。這提醒她，即使友善規則也可能被誤解。一位學生問新工作室是否會永遠保持這個樣子。夏說她希望它繼續進步。房間屬於還在學習的人，而學習就意味著願意改變。即使是快速改變，只要每個人都能問原因，也能謹慎處理。

The first guest was Mrs. Fang, the shopkeeper who had shared the old letter. She brought her niece, a girl who liked classical music and wanted to learn audio editing. The child looked at the wall of photographs and asked why one picture showed a broken camera. Xia explained that the photo was from the day she and Ethan nearly dropped their equipment. “Why keep a picture of a mistake?” the girl asked. “Because we learned from it,” Xia answered. “And because the room should never pretend that only perfect people belong here.” Ethan, standing behind the sound desk, gave her a small smile.

第一位來賓是曾分享舊信的店主方太太。她帶著姪女，一位喜歡古典音樂、想學剪輯聲音的女孩。女孩望著照片牆，問為何有張照片裡的攝影機壞了。夏解釋，那是她和以森差點摔壞設備那天的照片。「為什麼留著失敗的照片？」女孩問。「因為我們從中學到東西，」夏回答。「也因為這個房間不該假裝只有完美的人才配進來。」站在聲音控制桌後的以森對她微微一笑。

A visitor asked what had happened to the contest prize. Nora explained that the community reporting award had paid for one microphone and a training class, while the public fund covered other needs. The larger contest prize had gone to another team, and that fact was written clearly on the wall. There had been some anger when the results first appeared, but the students had learned to accept a fair decision. Xia believed justice was not the same as getting every result she wanted. It meant that people could see the rules, ask questions, and have their voices heard. That was the lesson she wanted the new hosts to remember.

一位訪客問競賽獎金的去向。諾拉解釋，社區報導獎用來買一支麥克風、辦一堂培訓課，其他需求則由公開基金支應。總冠軍獎金由另一隊獲得，這件事也清楚寫在牆上。結果剛公布時有些人憤怒，但學生們學會接受公平的決定。夏相信，正義不是每個結果都符合自己心意，而是大家看得見規則、問得出問題，也能讓聲音被聽見。這是她希望新主持人記住的功課。

During the broadcast, a viewer posted a message asking whether Xia and Ethan were now a couple. Xia felt her face grow warm. Ethan did not answer for her. He looked at the camera and said, “Our personal lives are not part of today's program.” Then he asked the guest about her favorite old song. Xia was grateful. Their relationship did not have to become a prize for the audience. They still had separate plans: she had accepted a summer job at a media company near the border of the city, and he planned to study communication for another year. Their time together had to fit around real work, and both were willing to make that effort.

直播中，有觀眾留言問夏與以森是不是一對了。夏臉熱起來。以森沒有替她回答。他望著鏡頭說：「我們的私生活不是今天節目的內容。」接著問來賓最喜歡哪首老歌。夏很感激。兩人的關係不必成為取悅觀眾的獎賞。他們仍有各自的計畫：夏接受了城市邊緣一家媒體公司的暑期工作，以森則打算再讀一年傳播。他們得在真實工作之間安排相處時間，而兩人都願意付出努力。

After the last guest left, Xia helped a new volunteer sort paper, old cables, and garbage from the room. The young student had made a spelling mistake on a poster and looked worried. Xia gave her a pen. “A mistake can be fixed,” she said. “You do not need to hide it.” Outside, the evening rain came rapidly and tapped against the windows. Ethan brought two cups of tea. The room seemed warm and bright, even with the lights turned low. Xia looked at the open door and remembered the first day, when she had wanted only to win. Now she had helped build something other people could carry forward. Ethan stood beside her, and they walked home together, not as enemies, not as perfect heroes, but as two people still choosing one another.

最後一位來賓離開後，夏幫新志工整理紙張、舊線材與垃圾。年輕學生在海報上拼錯字，看起來很擔心。夏遞筆給她。「錯誤可以修正，」她說。「不需要藏起來。」外面傍晚的雨急促落下，敲著窗戶。以森端來兩杯茶。即使燈光調暗，房間仍顯得溫暖明亮。夏望著敞開的門，想起第一天自己只想獲勝。如今她協助建立了能讓其他人繼續承接的事物。以森站在她身旁，兩人一起走回家，不是敵人，也不是完美英雄，而是仍在選擇彼此的兩個人。
