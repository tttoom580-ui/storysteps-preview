狐狸小徑

The Fox Trail

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

During the short university vacation, the contest sent teams to make a field report at a nature park. Their task was to gather stories about local families and the land. The park offered a proper walking trail one kilometer in length, with signs at every hundred meters. Xia brought biscuits, and Ethan packed a ruler to measure a broken sign. At the entrance a fox appeared on a poster beside a goose. Nora laughed and said the animals looked like they were having a musical argument. Their guest guide explained that both animals lived near the wetlands, but visitors should never chase them to get a better picture.

大學短假期間，競賽要求各隊前往自然公園製作實地報導，蒐集當地家庭與土地的故事。公園有一條完善的步道，全長一公里，每隔一百公尺便有指示牌。夏帶了餅乾，以森準備尺子，打算量一塊損壞的標誌。入口海報上，狐狸旁畫著一隻鵝。諾拉笑說，動物們看起來像在進行音樂劇式的爭論。導覽員解釋兩種動物都住在濕地附近，但遊客絕不能為了拍到好照片而追趕牠們。

At the first rest point, a farmer offered a roasted yam and told the students how her family had cared for the land. Each year she kept a record of rain per month, then used those numbers to decide what to plant. One meter of water could change the future of the field, she said, but years of careful work mattered more than one lucky season. Xia liked the farmer's plain style. Ethan noticed a small snail crossing the path and guided everyone around it. “This is our slowest guest,” he joked. The group laughed, then waited until the animal reached the other side.

第一個休息點，一位農民拿出烤地瓜，並說起家人如何照顧土地。她每年記錄各月份的雨量，再依數據決定種什麼。她說，一公尺深的水可能改變田地的未來，但多年的細心耕作比單次好運重要。夏喜歡她樸實的說話方式。以森注意到一隻小蝸牛正在過路，便引導大家繞道。「這是我們最慢的來賓，」他開玩笑說。大家笑了，然後等蝸牛抵達另一側。

A sudden gust broke the corner of a paper map. Nora found glue in her bag and fixed it while Ethan checked the next unit of distance on the trail. Their progress was gradual, and Xia began to worry they would not finish filming before sunset. Ethan asked her to ignore the clock for ten minutes and listen to the farmer's daughter. The girl explained that visitors often loved the fox picture but knew nothing about the wetland workers. The story needed patience, not speed. Xia took a long breath and asked one more question. The answer gave them the best part of the whole program.

一陣強風撕破紙地圖的一角。諾拉在袋裡找到膠水黏好，以森則核對步道下一段的距離單位。他們進度緩慢，夏開始擔心日落前拍不完。以森請她先別理時鐘十分鐘，仔細聽農民女兒說話。女孩解釋，遊客常喜歡狐狸圖案，卻對濕地工作者一無所知。這個故事需要耐心，不需要速度。夏深吸一口氣，再問一個問題。答案成了整集最棒的部分。

At the park office, the teams learned that the contest sponsor would give a small bonus to reports that included local voices. The director sent a message of congratulations to several groups, including Xia's. Yet the note also asked whether they would change the film's ending to show the sponsor's product. Ethan thought they should at least ask what the payment meant. Xia worried that money could change the meaning of the story. They agreed not to accept or reject anything until they saw a written offer. That decision required more courage than either had expected, because the studio needed funds and the contest was becoming more costly.

在公園辦公室，各隊得知贊助商會給納入地方聲音的報導一筆小獎金。主任向幾組隊伍發出恭喜訊息，其中也包括夏的團隊。然而，訊息還詢問他們是否願意更改影片結尾，加入贊助商的產品。以森認為至少該先詢問付款條件。夏擔心金錢會改變故事的意義。他們同意在看到書面提案以前，不接受也不拒絕。這個決定比預想中更需要勇氣，因為工作室需要經費，而比賽也越來越花錢。

On the bus, Nora offered everyone a biscuit from the last bag. Ethan said the park guide had made her notes available to the students, so they could check the facts before editing. Xia was pleased. She suggested a short ending that invited viewers to learn about the wetland without visiting sensitive areas. The guide had also explained that some paths were closed during nesting season. Their report could help people understand that rule instead of treating it as an unfair limit. Xia looked at her tired friends and thanked them for taking the slower route through the story.

巴士上，諾拉從最後一袋餅乾裡分給每人一片。以森說公園導覽員已把筆記提供學生，讓他們在剪輯前核對事實。夏很高興，提議用一段短結尾邀觀眾認識濕地，但不要進入敏感區域。導覽員還解釋，某些步道在築巢季會關閉。他們的報導能幫人理解規定，而不是把它當成不公平的限制。夏看著疲憊的朋友，感謝他們願意用較慢的方式走進故事。

On the bus home, Xia fell asleep against the window. Ethan held the equipment box steady so it would not slide into her knees. When she woke, she saw him looking over the farmer's notes rather than his phone. “Did we get the story?” she asked. “Yes,” he said. “But I think the hardest part is still ahead.” Xia looked at the little fox on the cover of their notebook and smiled. They had walked a long road in one day, yet she no longer felt that their partnership was a contest between two enemies. She wanted to see what they could build if they both chose the same direction.

回程巴士上，夏靠窗睡著了。以森穩穩扶著設備箱，避免滑到她膝上。她醒來時，看見他在讀農民的筆記，而不是滑手機。「我們有拍到故事嗎？」她問。「有，」他說，「但我想最難的還在後面。」夏看向筆記本封面上的小狐狸，微笑了。一天之內他們走過很長的路，而她不再覺得這段搭檔關係是兩個敵人的競賽。她想知道，如果兩人都選擇同一個方向，最後能建造出什麼。
