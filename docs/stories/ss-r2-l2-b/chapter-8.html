不見的架子

The Missing Shelf

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Xia's next idea was a community barbecue beside the old library. Her childhood friend Bo offered to cook, and the library staff agreed to lend tables. The average student had never visited the library's small history room, so Xia hoped a friendly meal would bring people inside. Ethan carried a load of paper plates while Xia placed chopsticks beside each table. An older volunteer, Mr. Sun, spoke in a gentle voice about the room's meaning. “This was a place where people shared books when they had no money,” he said. Xia liked his story, but she noticed that one shelf in the corner had disappeared since her last visit.

夏的下一個點子是在舊圖書館旁辦社區烤肉。她的童年好友博願意負責烹飪，圖書館人員也同意借桌子。一般學生很少拜訪館內的小歷史室，所以夏希望一頓友善的餐食能吸引人走進來。以森抱著一大疊紙盤，夏則在每桌放上筷子。年長志工孫先生用溫和的聲音談這間房的意義。「以前人們沒錢買書，就在這裡分享，」他說。夏喜歡他的故事，但她注意到角落有一座書架不見了。

When she asked about it, the librarian looked worried. A box of letters had been on that shelf, including early records of donations to the student radio room. Nobody knew whom to ask about its removal. The librarian said a new manager had ordered staff to move old papers to a storehouse. “An order is not the same as a clear reason,” Xia said. Ethan asked to see the written command. The librarian showed them a paper signed only with an initial. It gave no room number. The two hosts realized they had found a small problem that might lead to a larger question about who controlled shared campus history.

夏詢問時，圖書館員露出擔心的神色。那座架子原本放著一箱信件，其中包括學生廣播室早年的捐款紀錄。沒有人知道該向誰詢問書架被移走的事。館員說新經理曾命令員工把舊紙張送進儲藏室。「命令不等於清楚的理由，」夏說。以森要求查看書面指示。館員拿出一張只簽了姓名首字母的紙，沒有標示房號。兩位主持人意識到，他們找到的小問題，可能牽涉更大的疑問：誰有權控制校園共同的歷史。

Bo called everyone outside before the food grew cold. He gave Mr. Sun a stick with bread on it to hold over the fire. Nearby, a child built a small tower from stone pieces and announced that fate had made him king. An older woman laughed and said she had once been a servant in a play, so she could teach him royal manners. For a moment the event felt like a storybook. But Xia could not stop thinking about the missing letters. Ethan noticed her face and asked whether she wanted to leave. “No,” she said. “People came for a meal. We should finish this first. Then we can search properly.”

博在食物冷掉前叫大家到外面。他給孫先生一根串著麵包的木棒，讓他放在火上烤。附近有個孩子用石頭堆小塔，宣布命運讓自己成為國王。一位婦人笑著說，她曾在戲劇裡演過僕人，可以教他王室禮儀。那一刻活動彷彿童話。但夏一直想著不見的信件。以森注意到她的表情，問她要不要先離開。「不，」她說，「大家是來吃飯的。先把活動做好，之後再認真查。」

After the last guest left, the librarian took them into the storehouse. They searched each shelf, moving old chairs and broken lamps. They found the missing wooden shelf leaning against a wall, but the letter box was gone. Ethan took photographs of its empty place while Xia made notes about what they could prove. They had a source for the moving order, but not yet a source for the missing box. “We cannot tell viewers that someone stole it,” Ethan said. “I know,” she replied. “Whatever happened, we need facts.” Their disagreement had become a way of keeping each other careful.

最後一位來賓離開後，館員帶兩人到儲藏室。他們逐一查看架子，搬開舊椅子與壞燈。失蹤的木書架靠在牆邊，但信件箱不見了。以森拍下空缺的位置，夏則記下哪些事情可以證明。他們有移動指示的來源，卻沒有箱子去向的來源。「我們不能告訴觀眾有人偷了它，」以森說。「我知道，」她回答，「無論發生什麼事，我們都需要事實。」兩人的分歧，現在成了幫助彼此保持謹慎的方法。

The librarian asked whether the team's story would make the old room disappear from public memory if it closed. Xia said that was exactly why they wanted to preserve the letters. Even if the room changed, people should be able to learn who had built it and why. Ethan offered to create a public index of documents, with personal addresses removed. Mr. Sun agreed to help because he remembered some of the early volunteers. The plan was small, but it gave the search a purpose beyond the contest. Xia felt less afraid once she saw that the work could continue in many hands.

圖書館員問，如果舊房間關閉，他們的故事會不會跟著從大眾記憶中消失。夏說，正因如此他們才想保住信件。即使房間改變，人們仍應能知道是誰建造它、為了什麼。以森提出製作公開文件索引，移除私人地址。孫先生因記得部分早期志工，答應幫忙。計畫很小，卻讓尋找行動有了競賽以外的目的。夏看見工作能由許多人接手，便不再那麼害怕。

Walking back, Ethan asked why the lost box mattered so much to her. Xia said the letters might explain how the studio had first been opened and who had promised to protect it. Without that history, students had little means to question decisions. “I used to think a camera could move any story forward,” she confessed. “Now I see that reality is slower.” Ethan answered that slow work could still have value. He did not take her hand, though their arms nearly touched on the path. Xia felt that something between them was changing, and she decided not to hurry it. They had a box to find and a promise to understand.

走回去時，以森問夏為什麼那個箱子對她那麼重要。夏說，信件可能解釋工作室最早如何成立、又是誰答應保護它。沒有這段歷史，學生就很難有方法質疑決策。「我以前以為攝影機能讓任何故事往前走，」她坦承。「現在才知道現實慢得多。」以森回答，緩慢的工作仍有價值。他沒有牽她的手，雖然兩人的手臂在小路上幾乎碰到。夏感覺彼此關係正在改變，決定不急著推進。他們還有箱子要找，也有承諾要弄明白。
