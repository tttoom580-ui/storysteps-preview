得獎前的風暴

The Storm Before the Prize

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 22 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A typhoon warning arrived on the morning of the final rehearsal. The university canceled outdoor events, and the hall roof began to leak. Water could damage cameras in an instant, so the team moved everything to a dry room. Nora slipped on the wet floor but caught herself before falling. Ethan put a warning sign by the door and asked everyone to walk slowly. Outside, wild wind shook the trees. Xia wondered whether the whole contest might be canceled. But the director sent a message: if the storm passed safely, the final presentation could take place two days later. The team had to solve the immediate safety problem first.

決賽彩排當天早晨，颱風警報來了。大學取消戶外活動，會堂屋頂開始漏水。水一瞬間就可能損壞攝影機，團隊趕忙把所有東西移到乾燥房間。諾拉在濕地板滑了一下，幸好沒跌倒。以森在門口放警告牌，請大家慢慢走。外頭狂風吹搖樹木。夏擔心整場競賽會取消。但主任傳來訊息：如果風暴安全過去，決賽可延到兩天後。團隊必須先解決眼前安全問題。

The university shelter opened in the central sports hall. Xia helped volunteers carry blankets, while Ethan worked with a fisherman from the nearby coast who knew how quickly weather could change. The fisherman brought a silver radio that still worked without the internet. He said his mate had given it to him after a difficult season, and he never traveled without it. A child asked whether he was a hunter of storms. He smiled. “No,” he said, “I listen to warnings so I can return home.” His simple opinion stayed with Xia. She had spent months chasing stories, but sometimes the best work was to listen before moving.

大學在中央體育館開設避難空間。夏幫志工搬毛毯，以森則和一名附近海岸的漁夫合作。那漁夫很清楚天氣能變得多快。他帶來一台不用網路也能運作的銀色收音機，說那是夥伴在艱難的一季後送給他的，自己出海從不離身。一個孩子問他是不是追獵風暴的人。他笑道：「不是，我聽警報，是為了能回家。」這個簡單看法留在夏心裡。她好幾個月都在追故事，有時最好的工作卻是先聽再動。

At lunch, the shelter team made a bill for food and medicine, so donors would know how their support was used. A local store sent cola and bread, while a small restaurant brought hot soup. The director announced a golden rule: no person should be filmed in the shelter without permission. Xia agreed at once. A volunteer asked whether they could use the studio camera to record the event for an emergency report. She saw the possibility of useful work, but also the risk to people who had lost their homes. She offered to film the supplies and the work tables instead. The director thanked her for making that choice.

午餐時，避難團隊製作食物與藥品帳單，讓捐助者知道資源用途。當地商店送來可樂與麵包，小餐館帶來熱湯。主任宣布一條黃金規則：未經同意，不得拍攝避難者。夏立刻同意。一名志工問能否用工作室攝影機記錄活動，製作緊急報導。她看見實用可能，也看到對失去住處者的風險。她提議改拍物資與工作桌。主任感謝她的選擇。

During the warning, the fisherman showed them where to keep the silver radio so it would be available anywhere in the shelter. The floor near the door was wet, and he warned them that people could slip while carrying heavy boxes. Ethan moved the boxes to a safer table. Xia put a simple sign on the floor, then asked volunteers to tell new arrivals about the danger. It was a small action, but it prevented two children from running through the wet area. Later, the fisherman said he liked seeing students use their cameras carefully. They had learned when filming should wait and helping should come first.

警報期間，漁夫教他們把銀色收音機放在哪裡，才能讓避難所任何角落的人都能取用。門邊地板很濕，他警告搬重箱的人可能滑倒。以森把箱子移到安全的桌上。夏在地面放上簡單標示，並請志工提醒新抵達的人注意危險。這是件小事，卻避免兩名孩子跑過濕滑區。後來漁夫說，他很高興看到學生謹慎使用攝影機。他們學會了何時應先停拍、先幫忙。

Later an employment office representative asked whether students could help after the storm. The university had a list of temporary jobs for clearing safe areas and delivering supplies. Ethan checked that the terms were clear before sharing them. Xia felt a little doubt about their ability to help so many people, but Nora reminded her that they were part of a larger team. A volunteer from the latter group of arrivals offered clean towels, and another brought phone chargers. Their small acts did not end the storm. They did, however, make the waiting safer and less lonely. Xia realized this mattered more than the number of people watching them.

後來，就業服務處代表問學生能否在風暴後幫忙。大學列出安全區域清理與配送物資的短期工作。以森確認條件清楚後才分享。夏有點懷疑自己能幫這麼多人，但諾拉提醒，她們只是大團隊的一部分。較晚到的志工中，有人帶來乾淨毛巾，其他人帶來手機充電器。這些小事不能阻止風暴，卻讓等待更安全、不再那麼孤單。夏明白，這比有多少人看他們更重要。

The next morning the wind finally slowed. There was damage on campus, but everyone in the shelter was safe. The university confirmed that the final contest would go ahead after the rooms were checked. Xia and Ethan returned to the studio to see whether their files had survived. Everything was dry because they had moved the boxes early. Ethan smiled and said, “We may have one more chance.” Xia answered, “We already had a useful day, even without a show.” He looked at her with the same quiet warmth she had come to trust. Whatever happened in the final, neither of them could return to being the people who had first argued in the café.

隔天早晨，風終於緩了。校園受損，但避難所裡的所有人都平安。大學確認房間檢查後，決賽會照常進行。夏和以森返回工作室，查看檔案是否倖存。因為他們提早移動箱子，一切都保持乾燥。以森微笑說：「我們也許還有一次機會。」夏回答：「即使沒播節目，我們昨天也做了有用的事。」他以她已逐漸信任的溫暖眼神望著她。不論決賽結果如何，兩人都不可能變回最初在咖啡館爭吵的自己。
