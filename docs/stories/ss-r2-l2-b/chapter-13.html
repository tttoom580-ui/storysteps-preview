A Brand and a Choice

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 13 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The university business fair filled the main hall with bright clothing, posters, and a model aircraft hanging from the ceiling. Their former sponsor had a large stand near the door. Its brand name was printed on purple paper stars, and workers offered a small prize to anyone who could guess the amount of metal in a model plane. Xia wanted to walk past, but Ethan said they should listen before judging. The company representative, a polite gentleman, offered them a new contract. It would pay more than the first one, but it included a strict rule: the company could reject any part of a video it considered harmful to its image.

大學商業展把大廳布置得五彩繽紛，滿是服裝、海報，還有吊在天花板上的飛機模型。之前的贊助商在門口附近設有大攤位。品牌名稱印在紫色紙星星上，工作人員讓人猜模型飛機用了多少金屬，猜中就有小獎。夏想直接走過，以森卻說應先聽完再判斷。公司代表是一位有禮貌的先生，提出新合約。金額比第一次高，卻有嚴格條款：公司可拒絕影片中任何被認為損害形象的部分。

Xia asked the representative whether the rule would apply to a story about a factory's effect on local water. He said it would depend on the degree of public concern and the facts shown. Ethan asked who would make that decision. The man answered, “Our leadership team.” Xia knew enough now to see the problem. The person paying for the video would also decide whether the report could criticize the payer. She said they had nothing against business or advertising, but they would not accept that kind of control. The man remained polite and wished them luck. It was a quieter meeting than she had expected, yet the choice still hurt because their equipment needed money.

夏問代表，如果影片討論工廠對地方水源的影響，這條款也適用嗎？他說要看大眾關切程度與呈現的事實。以森問由誰決定，代表回答：「我們的領導團隊。」夏現在已足以看出問題。付錢拍影片的人，竟也能決定報導可否批評付款者。她說自己不反對商業或廣告，但不接受這種控制。代表仍很有禮貌，祝他們順利。會面比她預想中平靜，拒絕卻仍令人難受，因為設備需要資金。

At another stand, drama students invited them to film a short scene about a prince and a princess who refused to marry simply because a story expected it. A spider made from wire hung above the stage, and actors wore purple clothes that matched the fair signs. The play was funny, but its message felt natural to Xia: a person could choose a different ending. She asked the student director why she had written it. The director said her parents wanted her to study business, while she hoped to work in theater. Ethan nodded. “You can respect a family and still make your own choice,” he said. Xia looked at him, wondering whom he was trying to convince.

另一攤的戲劇學生邀他們拍短劇，內容是一位王子與公主拒絕只因故事安排就結婚。舞台上吊著鐵絲蜘蛛，演員穿著與展場標誌同色的紫衣。劇很有趣，但主題讓夏感到自然：人可以選擇不同結局。她問學生導演為何這樣寫。導演說父母希望她念商科，但她想從事劇場工作。以森點頭。「你可以尊重家人，同時做自己的選擇，」他說。夏望向他，好奇他想說服的是誰。

They returned to the hall during a quiet break in the business fair. A tired actor was asleep on a chair, still wearing a paper crown. A worker asked whether the color of the crown should match the stage curtain. Xia said the exact match did not matter as much as the story behind the scene. Ethan helped fold the chairs while she thanked the drama students for letting them film. The actor woke and laughed at his own photograph. Xia liked how ordinary the stage looked without its lights. It reminded her that the people behind an exciting show needed time to rest too.

商業展中場稍微安靜時，他們再次回到大廳。一名疲累演員仍戴著紙皇冠，睡在椅子上。工作人員問皇冠顏色是否要和舞台布幕一致。夏說精確配色沒有場景背後的故事重要。她感謝戲劇學生允許拍攝時，以森幫忙收椅子。演員醒來，看著自己的照片笑出聲。夏喜歡沒有燈光時舞台的平凡模樣。這提醒她，精彩節目背後的人也需要休息。

In the evening, Ethan brought Xia to a small science show where students could bathe metal parts in a safe cleaning solution. The teacher explained that a piece weighing only one gram could still be important in a machine. Xia touched a smooth piece of steel and thought of all the tiny choices that kept their show alive. Ethan confessed that his family owned a business and had encouraged him to join it after graduation. He had never told Xia because he feared she would think he was defending his own future. “I don't want to be against you,” he said. “I want to understand what I can support without hiding anything.”

傍晚，以森帶夏去看小型科學展示。學生可以把金屬零件浸入安全的清潔液中。老師解釋，即使重量只有一公克，零件在機器裡仍可能非常重要。夏摸著平滑鋼片，想到那些讓節目存活下去的小小決定。以森坦承家裡經營公司，家人希望他畢業後加入。他一直沒告訴夏，因為怕她以為他在維護自己的前途。「我不想與妳站在對立面，」他說。「我想知道，自己能不隱瞞任何事情，支持什麼。」

Xia did not ask him to reject his family. She asked whether he would be honest if their interests ever crossed. Ethan answered yes, even if it cost him a chance he wanted. She could not know whether he would keep that promise, but she respected the seriousness of his answer. They returned to the stand and filmed the young actors explaining their choices. The treasure of that day was not the company's money or a perfect picture. It was the understanding that they could disagree with someone without making that person an enemy. For Xia, this was a larger lesson than any school contest had promised.

夏沒有要求他拒絕家人，而是問如果家族利益與他們衝突，他是否仍會誠實。以森說會，即使因此失去想要的機會。夏不知道他是否能守住承諾，卻尊重他認真回答的態度。他們回到攤位，拍攝年輕演員談自己的選擇。那天的寶藏不是公司的錢或完美畫面，而是理解：可以不同意一個人，卻不必把那個人變成敵人。對夏而言，這比學校競賽承諾的任何課程都更重要。
