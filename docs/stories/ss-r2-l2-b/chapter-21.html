決賽前的練習

Practice for the Final Day

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 21 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Two days before the final presentation, Nora filled the blackboard with tasks. Xia would give the introduction, Ethan would explain the budget, and Nora would handle audience questions. A student from the theater club lent them a microphone and a small musical instrument for sound testing. The room was cold, so someone brought a blanket for the guest chair. Their plans had become somewhat more serious than the silly first show at the café. Xia read her opening text aloud and found it dull. She tried again, speaking about a lone student who needed a place to be heard. This time the others looked up. She had found her real beginning.

決賽前兩天，諾拉把黑板寫滿工作事項。夏負責開場，以森解釋預算，諾拉處理觀眾提問。戲劇社學生借給他們麥克風和一件小樂器做音效測試。房間很冷，有人替來賓椅拿來毛毯。他們的計畫比咖啡館那次傻氣的初試嚴肅多了。夏念出開場文字，覺得很無聊。她再試一次，講起一個需要被聽見的孤獨學生。這次大家都抬起頭。她終於找到了真正的起點。

A magazine reporter came to review the team's work. She was not interested in a perfect story about two talented hosts. She asked what they had learned from mistakes. Ethan described how he had once trusted a rule without asking whom it served. Xia admitted that she had judged him before trying to know him. Neither blamed the other. The reporter smiled and asked what gave them pride. “Our guests could say no,” Xia answered. “And when someone said no, we listened.” Ethan added that their budget could be read by any student. The reporter wrote both answers down and thanked them for speaking without an excuse.

一名雜誌記者前來評論團隊的工作。她對兩位天才主持人的完美故事沒興趣，而問他們從錯誤中學到什麼。以森描述自己如何曾相信規則，卻沒問規則服務誰。夏承認自己曾在認識他之前就下判斷。兩人都沒有責怪對方。記者微笑，問什麼讓他們驕傲。「來賓可以說不，」夏回答，「而有人說不時，我們會聽。」以森補充，每位學生都能讀懂他們的預算。記者記下答案，謝謝他們沒有找藉口。

The theater student returned with a spare cable and asked whether the team wanted to lend the microphone to other clubs after the contest. Xia said the new studio plan included an equipment library, with clear borrowing times and simple safety lessons. Ethan added that a student should not have to know the right people to borrow a tool. Nora wrote the idea on the board and invited comments. A quiet first-year student said that this would help her art group record music. Xia thanked her for speaking. The room felt more useful each time someone imagined a different way to share it.

戲劇社學生帶著備用線材回來，問團隊賽後願不願意把麥克風借給其他社團。夏說新工作室計畫包含設備借用庫，有明確借用時間與簡單安全課程。以森補充，學生不應該非得認識特定的人，才能借工具。諾拉把想法寫在白板，邀大家提供意見。一名安靜的大一學生說，這能幫她的藝術社錄音。夏謝謝她願意發言。每當有人想出不同的共享方式，房間就變得更有用。

In the afternoon they visited a market to ask about small donations. A salesperson at a farm stall offered to give them soap for cleaning equipment. Beside the stall stood a friendly goat wearing a red ribbon. Xia laughed because the animal kept trying to eat the sign. Another shop offered a discount on bottled water. A café owner offered leftover bread, while a restaurant invited the students to share steak after the contest. They thanked everyone but explained that supplies and money would be recorded publicly. The owners liked that promise. No one needed to give a great deal; many small offers could make the project possible.

下午，他們到市場詢問小額捐助。一名農場攤販願意提供清潔設備用的肥皂。攤位旁有隻繫著紅緞帶的友善山羊。山羊一直想吃標示牌，讓夏忍不住笑。另一家店願意折價提供瓶裝水。咖啡館老闆答應給剩餘麵包，餐廳則邀學生賽後吃牛排。他們感謝每個人，也解釋物資和金錢都會公開登記。店主們喜歡這項承諾。沒有人需要提供龐大援助；許多小小的捐助也能讓計畫成真。

While they walked back, Ethan asked whether Xia would remain at the university after graduation. She said she might work in a distant city if she found a good job. She did not want to limit her future to one room, even one she loved. Ethan looked disappointed, then said he respected that. Xia asked about his plans. He hoped to study public communication rather than join his father's company immediately. He was still deciding. Their paths might separate after the contest. The thought made Xia's chest tight, but she liked that they could talk about it without trying to hold each other in place.

走回學校時，以森問夏畢業後是否會留下。她說如果找到好工作，也許會去遙遠城市。她不想把未來限制在一個房間，即使那是她所愛的。以森看起來失望，仍說尊重她。夏問他的打算。他希望先研究公共傳播，而不是立刻進父親公司，但還沒完全決定。兩人的路也許在賽後分開。想到這點，夏胸口發緊，但她喜歡他們能談論此事，而不企圖把彼此固定在原地。

Late that night Xia checked the printed plans one last time. Everything was ready except the question neither host had dared ask. She took out her phone and wrote, “After the final, can we have dinner just as ourselves?” Before sending it, she erased the text. It was not fear of rejection alone. She wanted to speak face to face, without a screen giving her courage. Ethan arrived with two warm drinks and said he had forgotten his notebook. They sat quietly for a while. For once, their silence was not awkward. It felt like a place where an honest answer might soon grow.

深夜，夏最後一次核對印好的計畫。萬事俱備，除了兩位主持人都不敢問的問題。她拿起手機打字：「決賽後，我們能不能以自己本來的身分去吃晚餐？」送出前，她刪掉了。不只是怕被拒絕，她想面對面開口，不讓螢幕代替勇氣。以森帶著兩杯熱飲回來，說忘了拿筆記本。兩人安靜坐了一會兒。這一次，沉默並不尷尬，反而像一處真誠答案即將生長的地方。
