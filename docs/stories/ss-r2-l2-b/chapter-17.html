The Open Ledger

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 17 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The first public meeting took place in the grand hall of the education building. Xia placed a wooden box beside the microphone and explained that it held copies of the old radio documents. The originals remained safe in the library. The university had given them one hour to present a project for student management of the studio. Their plan depended on three basic ideas: open spending records, a clear rule for guest rights, and a small group of students elected every year. Nora showed a sample budget on the screen. It separated money for repairs from money for programs, so the audience could see each division without needing special skill.

第一次公開會議在教育大樓的大廳舉行。夏把一個木盒放在麥克風旁，解釋裡面是舊廣播文件的影本，原件仍安全保管在圖書館。大學給他們一小時提出學生自主管理工作室的計畫。他們依靠三個基本理念：公開支出紀錄、明確的來賓權利，以及每年改選的小型學生團隊。諾拉在螢幕展示預算樣本，把修繕費和節目費分開，讓觀眾不用特殊技能也能看懂每筆分配。

A local teacher asked whether students knew enough to manage expensive equipment. Ethan answered that education did not stop when someone left a classroom. “We can learn,” he said, “and we can ask an expert when the work is beyond us.” A student from a nearby village asked why the university should give money to a room used mostly by campus people. Xia explained that the studio could open a public channel for village groups as well. “Suppose you have a story about your road or your school,” she said. “You should have a way to tell it.” Several people nodded, and the teacher asked for a trial period.

一位當地老師問學生是否懂得管理昂貴設備。以森回答，教育不會在離開教室後停止。「我們可以學，」他說，「超出能力時，也能請教專家。」附近村莊的學生問，為何大學要出錢支持主要供校內使用的房間。夏解釋，工作室也能為村莊團體開設公共頻道。「假設你想說一段關於道路或學校的故事，」她說，「你應該有方法說出來。」好幾個人點頭，老師提出先試行一段時間。

Then Ms. Yu gave the audience a shock. She announced that the contest rules did not require the winner to keep the original radio room open. The prize could support any approved student media space. Xia had believed the room itself was protected. She reached into her drawer of notes and found the sentence she had missed. Ethan asked Ms. Yu to explain the rule clearly, not to change it on the spot. The director agreed that the language had always been there. Some students were angry, but Xia did not accuse anyone of lying. Instead, she admitted that her own reading had been incomplete.

接著余主任讓觀眾大吃一驚。她宣布競賽規則不要求獲勝者保留原本的廣播室；獎金可以支持任何核准的學生媒體空間。夏原以為房間本身受到保護。她翻開抽屜裡的筆記，找出自己漏看的句子。以森請余主任清楚解釋規則，而不是臨時改變它。主任同意那句文字一直存在。有些學生很生氣，夏卻沒有指控誰說謊，而是承認自己的閱讀不完整。

The meeting moved from blame to a useful quiz. Professor Lin asked the audience what a fair media room needed to do. People suggested equal access, safe equipment, and a way to correct mistakes. A student joked that it should also offer free soda, which made everyone laugh. The serious answers were written on the board. Xia saw that people with different ideas were sometimes more alike than they expected. All wanted a place that could hear them. She felt a new kind of hope. Even if the contest prize went elsewhere, the public could still ask the university to protect the studio through an open policy.

會議從責怪轉向一場實用的小測驗。林教授問觀眾，公平的媒體空間需要做什麼。大家提出平等使用、安全設備，以及更正錯誤的方法。一名學生開玩笑說還該提供免費汽水，惹得眾人笑出來。嚴肅的答案被寫在白板。夏看見想法不同的人，有時比想像中更相似。他們都想要一處能聽見自己的地方。她得到新的希望。即使獎金流向別處，大眾仍能要求學校透過公開規定保護工作室。

After the public meeting, a local restaurant owner offered to host an evening discussion. He said he could serve tea or water, and would keep wine away from any event involving younger students. Xia thanked him and explained that the meeting needed to welcome people with different habits and budgets. The owner agreed to provide the room without requiring anyone to buy food. Ethan said that was the kind of support the studio hoped to encourage: practical help with clear conditions. A student from the nearby village volunteered to make signs. The project was beginning to belong to a wider community.

公開會議後，一位地方餐廳老闆願意提供場地辦晚間討論。他說可以供應茶或水，若活動有未成年學生，也會把葡萄酒移開。夏向他道謝，說會議需要歡迎不同習慣與經濟能力的人。老闆同意免費借房間，不要求任何人購買餐點。以森說，這正是工作室想鼓勵的支持方式：實際幫忙、條件明確。附近村莊的學生主動要做指示牌。這個計畫開始屬於更廣大的社群。

After the meeting, the student council offered a small team to help plan the trial period. Ethan and Xia accepted, but insisted that other students should lead parts of the work. The studio could not depend forever on two hosts. Walking back, Xia thanked Ethan for helping her correct the mistake about the prize. “You did the brave part,” he said. “You admitted it in front of everyone.” She smiled. It had been painful, but she felt stronger for doing it. Their relationship was changing in much the same way as the project: not through one dramatic promise, but through many chances to act with care.

會後，學生會提出組成小隊協助規劃試行期。以森和夏同意，但堅持讓其他學生也帶領部分工作。工作室不能永遠依賴兩位主持人。走回去時，夏謝謝以森幫她更正獎金的誤解。「勇敢的是妳，」他說，「妳在所有人面前承認了。」她微笑。那很痛，但她因而覺得更有力量。他們的關係與計畫以相似方式改變：不是靠一個戲劇性承諾，而是透過一次次小心行動的機會。
