被剪掉的聲音

A Cut in the Recording

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 18 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next morning, a recent video appeared online before Xia and Ethan had arrived at the studio. It claimed that the team wanted to close the old room and build a private club. The clip used a short statement Ethan had made at the public meeting, but someone had cut away his next sentence. The edited version made him sound as if he did not care about student voices. Xia felt the old anger return. She knew this was not his fault, yet she was hurt that he had not told her he had spoken to a company employee after the meeting. Ethan said the talk was about equipment prices, not a secret plan. She asked for time to check.

隔天早晨，夏和以森還沒到工作室，一支新影片已在網路上流傳。影片宣稱他們想關閉舊房間，建立私人俱樂部。片中使用以森在公開會議說過的一小段話，卻剪掉了下一句，讓他聽起來像不在乎學生聲音。夏心中的舊怒氣又浮上來。她知道剪輯不是他的錯，卻因他沒有告訴自己會後和公司員工談過話而受傷。以森說談的是設備價格，不是秘密計畫。夏要求給她時間查證。

Nora helped them search for the full recording. An artist from the drama club had filmed the meeting and saved the original sheet of camera notes. Another student had recorded audio from behind a snack bar. By comparing the files, they could show the missing sentence. In it, Ethan had said, “I do not want to build a private club; I want an independent student space.” The fault was in the edit, not his speech. But Xia still felt troubled by the private conversation. Ethan did not ask her to forget it. He said he should have told her before meeting the employee, and he accepted that trust could be damaged without any great crime.

諾拉協助尋找完整錄影。戲劇社一名藝術學生拍下會議，保存了原始鏡頭紀錄表。另一位學生則在點心吧後錄到聲音。比對檔案後，他們能呈現被剪掉的下一句：「我不想建立私人俱樂部；我想建立獨立的學生空間。」錯在剪輯，不在他的發言。但夏仍因私人會談而不安。以森沒有要求她忘掉。他說自己應該先告訴她與公司員工會面，也接受即使沒有重大過錯，信任仍可能受損。

A small fire drill interrupted their work. The alarm made everyone leave the building, and a worker checked that no candle had been left burning near the old curtains. Outside, someone pointed at a cockroach crawling along the wall, and the tired group laughed at the strange interruption. When they returned, Ethan asked whether Xia wanted him to step back from the next broadcast. She said yes. She would speak with Nora and explain the record. Ethan agreed without argument. He knew the mature choice was not always to stay in front of the camera. Sometimes the better choice was to give another person room to decide.

一場小型消防演練打斷工作。警報讓所有人離開大樓，工作人員檢查舊窗簾附近有沒有燃燒的蠟燭。外面有人指著牆上爬過的蟑螂，疲累的團隊因這奇怪插曲笑了。回來後，以森問夏是否希望下一場直播由他退到幕後。夏說是。她想和諾拉一起說明紀錄。以森沒有爭辯，接受安排。他知道成熟的選擇不總是站在鏡頭前。有時更好的選擇，是讓別人有決定的空間。

Nora remembered that the artist had recorded the arrival of every speaker at the meeting, which helped confirm the order of the speeches. She said no one should burn old paper records or make a careless claim about them. The team agreed to switch from fast replies to a scheduled daily update while the facts were being checked. Xia knew this might cost them attention online. Yet the quiet plan let everyone work without fear of missing a rumor. Ethan thanked Nora for her help and waited outside while Xia finished the report. He understood that trust would return through many small acts, not through one public defense.

諾拉想起戲劇社藝術學生也錄下每位講者抵達會場的情況，這有助確認發言順序。她說沒有人應該焚燒舊紙本紀錄，也不該草率對紀錄做出指控。團隊同意停止即時回擊，改成查證期間每天固定更新。夏知道這可能讓網路上的關注減少，但安靜的計畫讓大家不用害怕漏接謠言。以森謝謝諾拉，並在外面等待夏完成報告。他明白信任會透過許多小事恢復，而非一次公開辯護。

Xia and Nora opened the program with two versions of the clip. They explained exactly where the edit had moved backward in time and why the missing sentence changed the meaning. A student asked whether they would name the editor. Xia said they had not found proof of who made the cut. She did not turn the report into a hunt. She also said the group had a duty to keep its own contact records clear. Some viewers thanked her for the calm answer. Others wanted more drama. Xia would rather receive fewer views than accuse an unknown person. She felt sad, but certain about the choice.

夏與諾拉以兩個版本的影片開場，精確說明剪輯在哪裡往前移回時間，又為何少了那句話就改變意思。一名學生問會不會公布剪輯者名字。夏說他們沒有證據知道是誰，她不會把報導變成獵巫。她也說團隊有責任清楚保存自己的聯繫紀錄。有些觀眾感謝她冷靜的回答，其他人卻想要更多戲劇性。夏寧願少些觀看數，也不願指控未知的人。她感到難過，卻確信自己做了正確選擇。

That night Ethan sent her the full message from the company employee, including the price list and meeting time. He did not ask for an immediate answer. Xia read it after dinner, then went to visit his grandmother and return an old recording of folk songs. On the tape a singer spoke about a whale that crossed the sea alone but found its group again. Xia listened twice. She knew a relationship was not a picture that could be repaired with one cut. It needed patience and honest work. She sent Ethan a short message: “Thank you for the records. We will talk tomorrow.” It was not forgiveness yet, but it was a beginning.

當晚，以森把公司員工傳給他的完整訊息寄給夏，包括價目表與會議時間，沒有要求立刻回覆。夏晚餐後讀完，接著去拜訪他的祖母，歸還一段民謠舊錄音。錄音裡有人唱一隻鯨魚獨自越海，最後又找到夥伴。夏聽了兩次。她知道關係不是一張只靠剪一刀就能修好的照片，而需要耐心與誠實。她傳給以森一則短訊：「謝謝你給我紀錄。我們明天談。」這還不是原諒，但已是開始。
