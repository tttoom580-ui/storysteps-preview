青年中心的屋頂

The Roof Above the Youth Center

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Their next guest invited them to a youth center near the station. The center helped teenagers travel safely after school and offered a place for cultural events. Xia was twenty-two and Ethan was twenty-three, but both remembered how lonely school could feel at sixteen. The center director gave them a short description of its work, then warned that the roof was being repaired. A delay in the work had closed the upper floor. When they entered, a dog began to bark beside the door. The director explained that it belonged to a volunteer and was trained to stay away from children who feared dogs. Xia was glad to hear such care in an ordinary rule.

下一位來賓邀兩人前往車站附近的青年中心。中心幫助青少年放學後安全往來，也提供文化活動空間。夏二十二歲、以森二十三歲，但兩人都記得十六歲時學校可能有多孤單。中心主任拿出簡短的工作介紹，並提醒屋頂正在整修。工程延誤使樓上暫時關閉。他們走進去時，一隻狗在門邊吠叫。主任解釋，那是志工的狗，訓練過不接近怕狗的孩子。夏很高興在平常規定中聽見這樣的體貼。

A student showed them a small garden with a swing, a guava tree, and a table set for afternoon snacks. She had hurt her ankle while playing sports, so she moved carefully. Ethan carried a box of books for her. On the table lay a novel about a child who believed the whole town was evil. “I used to feel that way,” the student said, “but I learned that some people will reply if you ask for help.” Xia asked whether she could include that sentence in their video. The student said yes, but she did not want her face shown. Xia promised to select a picture of the garden instead.

一名學生帶他們參觀小花園，那裡有鞦韆、芭樂樹，以及放點心的桌子。女孩運動時扭傷腳踝，所以走得很小心。以森幫她搬一箱書。桌上有一本小說，主角相信整座小鎮都很邪惡。「我以前也這麼想，」女孩說，「但後來知道，只要開口求助，有些人會回應。」夏問能不能把這句話放進影片。女孩同意，但不希望露臉。夏答應改用花園畫面。

During the youth center visit, a quiet boy asked if he could try the camera. Xia showed him which button to press, and he heard a small click. He pointed it toward the empty garden rather than the other children. Ethan praised him for remembering the privacy rule. The boy then asked whether he could join the university's media club when he grew older. Xia said the club welcomed students who wanted to learn, not only those who were already good at filming. The boy smiled and took one careful picture of the swing. For Xia, that small moment made the long trip worthwhile.

參訪青年中心時，一個安靜男孩問能不能試用攝影機。夏教他按哪個按鈕，他聽見一聲輕喀噠。男孩把鏡頭朝向空花園，而不是其他孩子。以森稱讚他記得隱私規定。男孩問長大後能不能參加大學媒體社。夏說社團歡迎想學的人，不只歡迎本來就擅長拍片的人。男孩微笑，仔細地拍了一張鞦韆照片。對夏而言，這個小瞬間讓長途參訪很值得。

At lunch the center served soup with a wooden spoon beside every bowl. Xia clicked the camera on, then turned it off when the director asked for a quiet meal. The center had a protective rule: guests could film public rooms, but not children during private talks. Ethan wrote the exact rule in their notebook. He said it made his job easier because there would be no guessing about limits. Xia smiled at him. He had once seemed like someone who loved rules for their own sake. Now he was helping people use rules to protect choices. She felt warm toward him and tried not to show it too clearly.

午餐時，中心供應湯，每個碗旁都放著木湯匙。夏開啟攝影機，主任要求安靜用餐時，她又關掉了。中心有一條保護規定：來賓可以拍公共房間，但不得拍孩子的私人談話。以森把完整規定記在筆記本。他說有明確界線，工作便不用猜測。夏對他微笑。他曾像是為規則而熱愛規則的人；如今卻在幫大家用規則保護選擇。她對他心生暖意，努力不表現得太明顯。

Before leaving, the student with the injured ankle gave them a drawing of the center. Upon seeing it, Xia grew upset. The picture showed the roof full of dark holes, and children standing below with umbrellas. “Is it really that bad?” she asked. The director admitted that money for repairs was running low. He did not ask for a sad video. He asked them to show the cost clearly and explain how donations would be used. Ethan offered to help make a simple chart after their visit. Xia realized that the team's skills could serve people even when the camera was turned off.

離開前，扭傷腳踝的女孩送他們一幅中心的畫。夏看見畫後很難過。屋頂滿是黑洞，孩子們站在下面撐傘。「真的有這麼糟嗎？」她問。主任承認整修經費快不夠了。他沒有要求悲傷影片，而是希望他們清楚呈現費用，說明捐款用途。以森提出參訪後可幫忙製作簡單圖表。夏明白，團隊的能力在關掉攝影機時也能幫到人。

Back at school, they gave the director a copy of the finished film and waited for his approval. He asked them to remove one child's voice from the background. They made the change and received a kind reply. The episode earned fewer views than an animal story but brought enough volunteers to help clean the youth center on Saturday. Xia felt proud. Ethan suggested that their future show could sometimes travel without a live audience and take time to listen first. “That sounds almost like my idea,” she teased. He laughed. “Then I am learning from the best.” She turned away quickly so he would not see how much that pleased her.

回到學校後，他們把完成的影片交給主任，等候審核。主任請他們移除背景中一名孩子的聲音。兩人修改後，收到親切回覆。這集觀看數不如動物故事，卻吸引足夠志工在星期六幫忙打掃青年中心。夏很驕傲。以森建議，未來節目有時可以不直播，而是先去現場花時間傾聽。「這聽起來幾乎是我的點子，」夏取笑他。他笑了：「那我是在跟最厲害的人學習。」她趕快轉頭，免得他看見自己有多開心。
