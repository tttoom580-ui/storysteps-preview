面具後的辯論

The Debate Behind the Mask

死對頭成了我的雙人直播搭檔 · 第 14 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A student debate about media money drew a crowd larger than the contest room could hold. Xia and Ethan were invited to speak after a football match on the southern field. The organizers placed a large electric screen beside the stage, so people could see the speakers from far away. Xia prepared a short essay about their work. Ethan brought a list of questions about contracts and guest rights. He thought a serious debate should include every important detail; Xia thought people needed a clear story before they could follow the rules. Their difference was real, but this time it helped them produce a better presentation rather than another argument.

一場關於媒體經費的學生辯論吸引了大量觀眾，遠超競賽房間能容納的人數。夏和以森受邀在南邊球場的足球賽後發言。主辦方在舞台旁放了大型電子螢幕，讓遠處的人也看得清楚。夏準備了一篇工作經驗短文，以森則帶來合約與來賓權利問題清單。他認為嚴肅辯論應包含每個重要細節；夏覺得人們需要先聽懂故事，才跟得上規則。他們的差異是真實的，但這次反而讓發表更好，而不是引發另一場爭吵。

Before the debate began, Nora found a message claiming that Xia had accepted sponsor money in secret. Someone had posted a picture of her holding a shopping bag at the business fair. The picture made it seem that she was hiding a product behind a mask. Xia felt as if she had been given a wound she could not show. The bag had held a book and two sandwiches, nothing more. Ethan wanted to seek the original photograph and explain the truth. Xia stopped him. “We are due on stage in five minutes,” she said. “Let us answer with what we can prove, then investigate afterward.”

辯論開始前，諾拉發現一則訊息，指控夏暗中接受贊助款。有人貼出她在商業展拿購物袋的照片，讓她看起來像戴著面具隱藏產品。夏覺得自己受了一道看不見的傷。袋子裡只有一本書和兩份三明治。以森想找原始照片解釋真相，夏卻制止了他。「五分鐘後就要上台，」她說。「先用能證明的事回答，之後再調查。」

On stage, Xia told the audience how the team had chosen to keep control of its reporting. She did not repeat the false story. Ethan explained that a suitable agreement must name who could edit a video and what rights a guest kept. One student asked whether all sponsorship was wrong. Xia replied that support could be good when it did not buy silence. Another asked about their own ending if they lost. Ethan paused. “The room matters beyond our prize,” he said. “If we lose, we will still ask for fair rules.” It was the first time he had said that promise in front of so many people.

台上，夏向觀眾說明團隊如何決定保有報導控制權，沒有重複不實故事。以森解釋，適當的合約必須寫清楚誰能編輯，以及來賓保有哪些權利。一名學生問是不是所有贊助都錯。夏回答，只要不買走沉默，支持就可能是好的。另一人問，如果輸了，他們的結局會怎樣。以森停頓片刻。「這間房的價值超過我們的獎，」他說。「即使輸了，我們仍會要求公平規則。」這是他第一次在那麼多人面前說出承諾。

A teacher in the audience later showed them the original photograph. The image had been taken from a public school album, then cut to hide the table beside Xia. On that table lay a stamp from a book fair, showing where the bag had come from. It was simple evidence, not a secret tale. Nora made a careful note of the time and source. The team decided to publish both versions with an explanation of how a cut image could change meaning. Xia asked Ethan not to speak for her during the video. He agreed and stood behind the camera while she gave her own account.

之後一位觀眾老師拿出原始照片。那張圖取自學校公開相簿，後來被裁掉了夏旁邊的桌子。桌上有書展的印章，能看出袋子從哪裡來。這是簡單證據，不是祕密故事。諾拉仔細記下時間與來源。團隊決定同時公布兩個版本，解釋裁切如何改變意思。夏請以森不要在影片裡替她發言。他同意站在攝影機後，讓她自己說清楚。

The next morning, the team checked the time on each version of the photograph. The public album included a wider view taken moments later, showing Xia talking to the book seller. Nora saved both pictures and wrote a note explaining what could and could not be concluded. Ethan suggested that the team share the note with the school before posting it online. Xia agreed but insisted that her own words remain at the center of the answer. He accepted that without hesitation. The work took nearly an hour, yet no one complained. They knew that careful corrections were part of reporting, not an embarrassing side task.

隔天早晨，團隊核對每個版本照片的時間。公開相簿還有稍後拍攝的一張較廣角照片，可看到夏正在跟書商說話。諾拉保存兩張圖，寫下能與不能推論的內容。以森建議發布前先把說明交給學校。夏同意，但堅持回答的主體要是自己的話。他毫不猶豫接受。工作花了將近一小時，卻沒有人抱怨。他們知道，仔細更正是報導的一部分，不是丟臉的附帶任務。

After filming, the three friends sat under the empty stands. Xia felt the weight of the past weeks around her waist like a tight belt. She had wanted to win, then to protect the studio, and now to protect the people whose stories they told. Ethan passed her a bottle of water and asked whether she was all right. “Not yet,” she said, “but I will be.” He nodded and stayed nearby without trying to fix her feelings. That quiet conversation made her feel seen. The next day they would return to the missing records. Tonight, it was enough to know that a false image had not stolen her own voice.

拍完後，三位朋友坐在空看台下。夏感到過去幾週的重量像緊緊的腰帶纏在腰間。她起初想贏，後來想保護工作室，現在也想保護受訪者。以森遞水給她，問她是否還好。「還沒有，」她說，「但我會好的。」他點頭，安靜地待在附近，沒有急著修復她的情緒。這樣的談話讓她覺得自己被看見。明天他們會繼續尋找紀錄。今晚，只要知道一張不實影像沒有奪走她的聲音，就夠了。
