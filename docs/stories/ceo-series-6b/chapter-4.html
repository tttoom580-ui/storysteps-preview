The Dinner with Empty Chairs

裴總，這份婚約不在收購清單裡 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The charity dinner took place in a restored concert hall, where a runway had been laid between two rows of tables. Yining's models included a teacher who used a cane, a retired dancer, and a software engineer whose clothing needed room for a medical device. She insisted that each participant approve the photographs to be published. The event organizer called those requirements inconvenient; Yancheng responded by asking the organizer to put the complaint in writing.

慈善晚宴在整修過的音樂廳舉行，兩排餐桌中間鋪成伸展台。以寧的模特兒包括拄手杖的老師、退休舞者，以及衣服需要容納醫療裝置的軟體工程師。她堅持每位參與者都要核准公開照片。主辦人嫌麻煩；硯承請他把抱怨寫成正式文件。

The sponsor's addiction to publicity worried the event manager. Yining balanced an elegant aesthetic with practical accessibility. The planner could give only an approximate count of attendees. A museum donor funded archaeology lessons for the apprentices' families.

贊助商沉迷曝光的態度讓活動經理擔心。 以寧在優雅美學與實用無障礙之間取得平衡。 規劃者只能提供來賓人數的大約估計。 博物館捐款人資助學徒家庭的考古課程。

On the stage, one chair remained empty. It belonged to a former seamstress who had helped create the adjustable sleeves and then died before the collection was finished. Yining dedicated the first walk to her, not to the sponsor. A trustee from the foundation later asked whether the brand could provide training for apprentices whose schools had closed. She said yes, if the foundation paid the apprentices rather than treating their labor as a donation.

舞台上留了一張空椅，紀念曾協助設計可調袖口、卻在系列完成前離世的女裁縫。以寧把第一場走秀獻給她，而不是贊助商。基金會的一位受託人問，品牌能否培訓因學校關閉而失去課程的學徒。她答應了，條件是基金會必須支付學徒薪資，不能把他們的勞動當成捐贈。

Every volunteer wore a badge with their approved access zone. A controlled blaze on the projection screen simulated stage lighting. An elderly tailor called her devoted apprentice a disciple of fine craft. The fitting demonstration could enlighten buyers about hidden barriers.

每位志工佩戴標示授權區域的識別證。 投影幕上的模擬火光呈現舞台燈效。 老裁縫稱自己勤奮的學徒是工藝門徒。 試衣示範可以使買家了解看不見的障礙。

Yancheng's mother, Madame Pei, approached between courses. She praised the collection and then suggested that a public engagement might give the partnership a warmer image. Yining set down her glass. “No one is entitled to turn my private life into advertising,” she said. Madame Pei looked surprised, but Yancheng stated clearly that the idea was not part of their arrangement. The conversation ended without raised voices, though its chill lingered all evening.

裴夫人在兩道菜之間走近，先稱讚服裝，再提議公開訂婚或許能讓合作關係顯得更溫暖。以寧放下杯子：「沒有人有權把我的私生活變成廣告。」裴夫人很意外，硯承卻明確說這不在雙方協議內。談話沒有提高音量，但冷意持續了一整晚。

Yining asked the host not to make a fuss about her arrival. The seating plan carried an implicit assumption that everyone could climb stairs. Xinyi agreed to preside over the independent design-credit session. Corporate prestige could not excuse rude treatment of apprentices.

以寧請主持人別為她的到場大驚小怪。 座位圖暗含每個人都能爬樓梯的假設。 心儀同意主持獨立的設計署名會議。 企業聲望不能成為粗魯對待學徒的理由。

In the service corridor, Yining found the apprentice coordinator shaking after a sponsor had criticized her accent. The young woman wanted to quit immediately. Yining listened, then took her to the event manager to make a formal complaint. She did not insist that the coordinator remain at work. When Yancheng learned what had happened, he instructed his team to preserve the incident report rather than hide it behind the night's expensive photographs.

服務走廊裡，學徒協調員因贊助商嘲笑她的口音而發抖，想立刻辭職。以寧先聽她說完，再陪她向活動經理正式申訴，也沒有強迫她留下工作。硯承得知後，指示團隊保存事件報告，不得把它藏在華麗的晚宴照片背後。

One sponsor proposed to privatize the community training program. The warm stage lights seemed to radiate through the silk. The volunteer rotation gave everyone time for dinner. A backstage shaver used to remove lint had a damaged cord.

一名贊助商提議把社區訓練計畫私有化。 溫暖的舞台燈光彷彿透過絲綢向外散發。 志工輪班安排讓每個人都有時間吃晚餐。 後台去毛球的小型電動刮除器電線受損。

They left through separate doors to avoid another false headline. Before leaving, Yancheng asked whether Yining had eaten. She laughed at the ordinary question after so many tense conversations. He ordered no meal for her and made no decision on her behalf; he simply told her where the kitchen kept the extra bread. She took two rolls, thanked the cook, and walked home under the trees. Her name, not his, appeared on the fashion show's final credit card.

為了避免新的假新聞，他們從不同出口離開。出門前，硯承問以寧有沒有吃飯。經過一整晚緊張談話，這個普通問題讓她笑了。他沒有替她點餐、也沒有代她決定，只告訴她廚房還留著麵包。她拿了兩個餐包，向廚師道謝，沿著樹蔭走回家。秀場最後的署名卡，寫的是她自己的名字。

A stray cat slept beneath the service corridor's heater. A thin veil covered a historical headpiece in the display. A vineyard owner sponsored reusable garments for agricultural workers. The invitation included a clear warranty for every purchased garment.

一隻流浪貓睡在服務走廊的暖氣下。 展櫃裡一件歷史頭飾覆著薄紗。 一位葡萄園主人贊助農業工人的可重複使用服裝。 邀請資料清楚說明每件售出衣服的保固。

The following morning, Yining called the apprentice coordinator to ask whether she needed help documenting the complaint. The young woman said she had chosen to continue working but wanted the sponsor barred from direct supervision. The organizers agreed to review the request. For Yining, that was more important than any flattering photograph of the dinner.

隔天早上，以寧打電話問學徒協調員需不需要協助整理申訴。年輕女子說她決定繼續工作，但希望那名贊助商不得直接指揮自己。主辦方答應審查要求。對以寧而言，這比晚宴中任何漂亮合照都重要。
