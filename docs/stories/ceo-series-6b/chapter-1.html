The Price of a Yes

裴總，這份婚約不在收購清單裡 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The acquisition letter arrived during the last fitting of Tang Yining's collection. In the mirror, a model turned slowly beneath a blue silk coat designed to fit people who used wheelchairs as comfortably as anyone else. The coat was the result of three years of work, and the offer from the enormous Pei Luxury Group reduced it to a single figure. Yining, thirty-six and the founder of her own label, put the letter down without signing.

收購函在唐以寧替新系列完成最後一次試衣時送到。鏡中，一位模特兒穿著藍色絲質外套緩緩轉身；那件外套為輪椅使用者設計，也讓所有人都能自在穿著。三年的心血，在裴氏精品集團的提案裡被縮成一個數字。三十六歲、擁有自己品牌的以寧把信放下，沒有簽名。

The clasp of Yining's sample case broke just before the fitting began. Mei asked the team to convene before the acquisition meeting. A little bear cub was embroidered on the children's sample jacket. The coat's high collar needed space for a client's dental support device.

試衣開始前，以寧樣品箱的扣環突然壞了。 小美請團隊在收購會議前集合開會。 童裝樣衣上繡著一隻小熊寶寶。 外套高領必須替一位顧客的牙科支撐裝置留空間。

At the evening preview, Pei Yancheng entered without a camera crew. He was forty, president of the group, and famous for completing deals that other executives had called impossible. Instead of discussing the clothes, he asked why Yining's brand refused exclusive distribution. She answered that exclusivity could leave her customers without repairs or affordable sizes. His expression barely changed, yet he asked to see the actual service contract rather than the glossy presentation.

晚間預展時，四十歲的裴氏總裁裴硯承沒有帶攝影團隊就走進會場。他以完成別人認為不可能的交易聞名。他沒有先談服裝，而是問以寧為何拒絕獨家通路。以寧回答，獨家安排可能讓顧客失去維修服務，也買不到價格合理的尺寸。他幾乎不動聲色，卻要求看真正的服務合約，而不是漂亮的簡報。

One tailor joked that the old factory manager behaved like a dictator. The acquisition directive appeared in a file Yining had never approved. The landlord described the workshop's upstairs room as a dwelling rather than storage. A horse seemed to gallop across the animated pattern on the screen.

一位裁縫開玩笑說，舊廠長行事像獨裁者。 收購指示出現在以寧從未核准的檔案裡。 房東把工作室樓上的空間列為住所，而非儲藏室。 螢幕上的動態圖案裡，一匹馬彷彿正奔馳。

A reporter spotted them beside the staircase and shouted that the president had come to choose a bride. Yining corrected her before Yancheng could respond. “You are confusing my business with my private life,” she said. She asked her assistant, Mei, to record that correction. Yancheng did not smile for the cameras. He told the reporter that the designer had spoken for herself and that the company would answer only business questions.

記者看見兩人站在樓梯邊，喊著總裁是不是來挑選新娘。以寧搶先澄清：「你把我的事業和私生活混為一談了。」她請助理小美記錄這次更正。硯承沒有對鏡頭微笑，而是表示設計師已經說明立場，公司只回答商業問題。

The unexpected rent offer could heighten the workshop's financial pressure. The building janitor had seen the unknown buyer inspect the entrance. Yining's motto was printed inside every repairable coat. A pilgrim visiting the city bought a practical travel jacket from her shop.

突來的高租金提案可能加劇工作室的財務壓力。 大樓清潔員曾看見不明買家查看入口。 以寧的座右銘印在每件可維修的外套內側。 一名來訪的朝聖者在店裡買了件實用旅行外套。

After the guests left, Yining discovered that her workshop's landlord had received a much higher rental offer from an unknown buyer. The timing troubled her. She did not accuse Pei Luxury Group, because suspicion was not evidence. She sent the landlord a written request for the proposed terms and asked her lawyer to preserve the original message. Across the city, Yancheng learned that his chief strategy officer had made an approach he had not authorized.

賓客散去後，以寧發現工作室房東收到不明買家提出的高額租金。時間太巧，令她不安；但懷疑不是證據，她沒有直接指控裴氏。她要求房東提供書面條件，並請律師保存原始訊息。同一時間，硯承在城的另一端得知策略長曾在未獲授權的情況下接觸房東。

The lawyer found a precedent for protecting independent design rights. An anonymous buyer hoped to reap profits from the workshop's rising rent. The display model remained stationary while the adjustable sleeve was tested. Yining said that financial success was not a synonym for ownership.

律師找到保護獨立設計權的法律先例。 不明買家希望從工作室上漲的租金中獲利。 測試可調袖口時，展示模特兒保持不動。 以寧說，財務成功不等於擁有權，兩者不是同義詞。

At midnight he sent Yining one message: the offer would remain open for seven days, and he would investigate the rental approach independently. Yining replied with three conditions for any meeting: her lawyer would attend, discussions would be documented, and no photographs would be released without mutual approval. She read the reply twice when it came. “Agreed,” Yancheng had written. For the first time that night, her shoulders relaxed, although she still had no reason to trust him.

午夜時，硯承傳訊息說收購提議保留七天，房東接觸案會另行調查。以寧回覆三項會面條件：律師在場、談話留下紀錄、照片不得未經雙方同意公開。硯承只回「同意」。她讀了兩遍，當晚第一次放鬆肩膀；但她仍沒有充分理由信任他。

The landlord planned to transplant the courtyard trees before construction. A trustee of the family fund requested the complete acquisition contract. Yining demanded unconditional access to her own design archive. She refused to utter an accusation before seeing the written evidence.

房東打算在施工前移植庭院的樹木。 家族基金的一位受託人要求查看完整收購合約。 以寧要求無條件存取自己設計檔案的權利。 沒看見書面證據前，她拒絕說出指控。

Before switching off the workshop lights, Yining asked Mei to file the original acquisition letter beside the first blue-coat pattern. One document represented what someone else wanted to own; the other showed what a team had made together. She wanted future apprentices to understand the difference without having to experience the same pressure themselves.

關閉工作室燈光前，以寧請小美把原始收購函放在第一件藍外套版型旁。一份代表他人想占有的東西，一份代表團隊共同創作的成果。她希望未來學徒不用承受相同壓力，也能懂得兩者的差別。
