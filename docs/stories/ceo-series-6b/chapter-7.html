家宴的座位

The Family Table

裴總，這份婚約不在收購清單裡 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Madame Pei invited Yining to a family dinner and described it as an apology for the rumor. Yining accepted on the condition that she could bring Mei and leave at any time. Around the long table, relatives asked whether her modest label could survive without the group. She answered with figures from three independent shops. The conversation became uncomfortable when one cousin proposed putting all her future designs under the Pei family trust.

裴夫人邀以寧參加家宴，說是為謠言道歉。以寧同意，條件是可以帶小美、也能隨時離席。長桌旁的親戚問她小品牌沒有集團能否生存。她拿出三家獨立門市的數字回答。一名堂親提議把她未來所有設計納入裴家信託，氣氛頓時僵硬。

The archived wedding costume was too bulky for the narrow display case. Yining refused to deem a family dinner a corporate obligation. Xinyi compared the old trust's concentrated powers to a miniature dictatorship. The fabric seemed to glide over the table as the designer unfolded it.

檔案中的婚禮服裝太笨重，放不進狹窄展示櫃。 以寧拒絕把家宴視為公司義務。 心儀把舊信託集中的權力比作小型獨裁體制。 設計師攤開布料時，布彷彿在桌面滑行。

Yancheng's younger sister, Pei Xinyi, served as legal counsel for a charity outside the family company. She told Yining privately that the trust's old rules had once been used to silence junior shareholders. Xinyi had not proved a current violation and did not present gossip as evidence. She offered to introduce an independent lawyer who could review the trust documents. Yining accepted the contact but insisted that any engagement of the lawyer be paid by her own firm.

硯承的妹妹裴心儀在家族企業之外擔任慈善機構法律顧問。她私下告訴以寧，舊信託規則曾被拿來壓制小股東；但她沒有證實現在仍有違規，也不把傳聞當證據。她願介紹獨立律師審閱文件。以寧接受聯絡方式，卻堅持由自己的公司支付律師費。

A handicraft display honored the family's forgotten tailors. An interpreter helped the visiting tailor understand the trust meeting. A lesbian designer asked that her partner be welcomed without intrusive questions. Yining described financial independence as one form of liberation.

手工藝展示向被家族遺忘的裁縫致敬。 口譯員幫助來訪裁縫理解信託會議。 一位女同志設計師希望伴侶受到歡迎，不必回答冒犯的問題。 以寧把財務獨立視為一種解放。

At dessert Madame Pei produced a velvet box that made several relatives smile knowingly. Inside was not a ring but a historical brooch from the family's fashion archive. She invited Yining to wear it at the next gala. Yining admired the craftsmanship but asked who owned it, whether it had been insured, and whether wearing it would imply a public relationship. Madame Pei finally admitted that the press team had suggested the idea. Yining returned the box unopened.

甜點時裴夫人拿出絲絨盒，幾位親戚露出會心笑容。裡面不是戒指，而是家族時裝檔案裡的一枚古老胸針。她邀以寧在下次晚宴佩戴。以寧欣賞工藝，卻問清楚所有權、保險與佩戴是否暗示公開關係。裴夫人終於承認這是公關團隊建議。以寧把盒子原樣還回。

A lofty portrait of the company founder dominated the dining room. Xinyi helped Yining navigate the complicated trust documents. Mei warned that overwork would hurt the apprentices before the gala. Yining kept a smooth pebble from the coast in her pocket for calm.

創辦人高掛的肖像占據餐廳最醒目的位置。 心儀幫以寧看懂複雜的信託文件。 小美提醒晚宴前過勞會傷害學徒。 以寧口袋放著一顆海邊光滑石子，讓自己冷靜。

Yancheng walked her to the gate. He looked angry with his family, but Yining did not want to become the excuse for another private quarrel. “If you need to change how your company uses family influence,” she said, “do it because it is right, not because you want my approval.” He nodded. He then apologized for failing to foresee the dinner's pressure. She accepted the apology without pretending the evening had been pleasant.

硯承送她到大門，顯然對家人生氣。以寧卻不想成為另一場家庭爭吵的藉口。「如果你要改變公司如何運用家族影響力，應該因為那是對的，而不是想得到我的肯定。」他點頭，為未預料到晚餐壓力道歉。她接受道歉，沒有假裝這是一場愉快的聚會。

The proposed gift remained pending until ownership could be confirmed. An archive photo showed workers at an old cotton plantation. A historical photograph showed farmers using a plow before the cotton harvest. The contract preserved the original artist's right to a design royalty.

禮物在確認所有權前仍處於待決狀態。 一張檔案照片拍到舊棉花種植園的工人。 歷史照片顯示棉花收成前，農民用犁耕地。 合約保留原創者收取設計權利金的權利。

In the taxi, Mei asked whether Yining liked the president. Yining looked out at the city lights and answered that she was beginning to like the person he could become when he stopped treating every problem as something to acquire. That answer frightened her more than the tabloids. It meant the choice ahead might be real. She nevertheless asked Mei to schedule the independent trust review for the next morning; desire would not be allowed to rewrite the business contract.

計程車上，小美問以寧是不是喜歡總裁。她望著城市燈火，說自己開始喜歡那個不把所有問題都當成併購標的的他。這答案比八卦更令她不安，因為未來可能真的要作選擇。她仍請小美安排隔天的獨立信託審查；好感不能拿來改寫商業契約。

The uncomfortable dinner would spur Yining to seek independent legal advice. Yancheng proposed a stroll to the gate, not a private confrontation. A tan leather sample lay beside the unused brooch box. Yining refused to treat workers' design credits as a trifle.

這場不舒服的晚宴促使以寧尋求獨立法律意見。 硯承提議散步到大門，而不是私下對質。 未使用的胸針盒旁放著一塊棕褐色皮革樣本。 以寧拒絕把員工設計署名當成小事。

Back at the workshop, Mei asked whether the dinner had produced any useful leads. Yining showed her the independent lawyer's contact card and the dates of the old trust amendments. These were modest gains, but they were not decorative ones. The more she learned, the more carefully she separated what the family said about itself from what its records could establish.

回工作室後，小美問家宴有沒有得到線索。以寧拿出獨立律師名片和舊信託修訂日期。收穫不大，卻不是裝飾性的成果。她知道愈多，愈仔細區分家族怎麼談自己，以及紀錄真正能證明什麼。
