The Photograph After Midnight

裴總，這份婚約不在收購清單裡 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The scandal began with a photograph taken after a late audit meeting. Yancheng had held an umbrella above Yining while she searched for her house keys, and the angle made the gesture look like an embrace. By dawn the tabloids called them secretly engaged. A public relations director proposed pretending the rumor was true until the trial was over. Yining said no before Yancheng spoke; his silence lasted only long enough for her answer to become the official position.

風波起於深夜稽核會議後的一張照片。以寧找住家鑰匙時，硯承替她撐傘，拍攝角度卻像兩人相擁。天亮前，八卦媒體已稱他們祕密訂婚。公關主管建議乾脆假裝是真的，撐到試辦結束。以寧先說了「不」，硯承只沉默到足以讓她的答案成為正式立場。

Yining wanted an airtight privacy policy rather than vague assurances. A publicity campaign could not attain trust through false engagement claims. A congresswoman asked the industry to disclose its advertising practices. Yining's father packed homemade curry for her late dinner.

以寧要的是無漏洞的隱私政策，而非模糊保證。 宣傳活動不能靠虛構訂婚來取得信任。 一名女國會議員要求產業公開廣告手法。 以寧父親替她準備自製咖哩當晚餐。

The correction was simple: they were business partners, and neither person had authorized any announcement about romance. Still, the rumor reached Yining's elderly father, who worried that she was being forced into a wealthy family's plans. She visited him after work and showed him the contract clauses she had negotiated. He did not ask whether Yancheng was rich enough. He asked whether his daughter could leave the arrangement whenever she chose. She showed him the exit provision.

澄清內容很簡單：兩人是商業合作夥伴，沒有任何一方授權發布戀情消息。謠言仍傳到以寧年長的父親耳裡，讓他擔心女兒被有錢人家利用。她下班去看父親，拿出自己談成的條款。父親沒有問硯承夠不夠有錢，只問女兒能否隨時退出。她指給他看退出條款。

She did not despise reporters, only their unsupported assumptions. The rumor threatened to eclipse the apprentices' actual achievements. Mei checked that the event's decorative flowers were not presented as edible. Yining continued to hold her staff in high esteem during the scandal.

她不鄙視記者，只反對沒有根據的臆測。 謠言恐怕會掩蓋學徒真正的成就。 小美確認活動裝飾花不會被誤認為可以食用。 風波中，以寧仍十分尊重自己的員工。

Yancheng meanwhile confronted the publicity director. The director claimed that an invented romance would rescue the company's image. Yancheng told her she could recommend a strategy but could not take away someone's right to refuse it. He asked for a log of every employee who had accessed the photograph, then transferred the investigation to an outside firm. He did not order the image erased: it had already become evidence in a dispute over privacy.

硯承同時找公關主管談話。對方說虛構戀情能挽救集團形象。硯承表示她可以提出策略，卻不能剝奪別人拒絕的權利。他要求列出接觸照片的員工紀錄，再交外部公司調查；也沒有命令把照片刪掉，因為它已成為隱私爭議的證據。

An evergreen tree shaded the bench where Yining read the correction. A children's coat used a soft shark fin as a playful hood decoration. A flake of old paint fell from the workshop's window frame. The newspaper photograph had turned ordinary merchandise into a romantic symbol.

一棵常綠樹為以寧讀澄清稿的長椅遮蔭。 一件童裝把柔軟鯊魚鰭做成帽子的趣味裝飾。 工作室窗框掉下一小片舊漆。 報紙照片把普通商品變成了戀愛象徵。

That evening Yining called him, not about the photograph but about the courage of the store employee who had first reported hidden repair prices. She wanted the company to protect that employee from retaliation. Yancheng agreed to put the protection in writing, with an external reporting channel. After a pause he asked whether she was frightened by the publicity. She admitted that she was angry more than frightened. He listened without telling her how she should feel.

當晚以寧打電話給他，談的不是照片，而是第一位舉報隱藏維修價格的店員。她要求公司防止店員遭報復。硯承同意以書面建立保護措施和外部申訴管道。停頓片刻後，他問她是否被輿論嚇到。她承認自己更多是憤怒。他聽著，沒有教她應該怎麼感受。

Yining missed the comforting monotony of normal pattern cutting. Sketching birds remained her favorite private pastime. The contract forbade using the scandal as an excuse for sudden redundancy. Paparazzi were not permitted to roam through the private workshop.

以寧懷念平常裁版時令人安心的單調節奏。 畫鳥一直是她最喜歡的私人消遣。 合約禁止拿醜聞當藉口突然裁員。 狗仔不得在私人工作室裡四處遊走。

For the first time, the quiet between them held warmth rather than calculation. Yining stood by the window watching buses cross the river. She considered asking him to dinner, then stopped: one sincere feeling did not require an immediate decision. She thanked him for keeping the statement factual. He replied that he was learning that a strong leader could sometimes help most by stepping away from the center of the picture.

兩人第一次在沉默中感到溫暖，而不是計算。以寧站在窗邊看公車過河，想邀他吃晚餐，又停了下來：真實的好感不必立刻變成決定。她謝謝他讓聲明只談事實。他回答，自己正學著明白，強勢的領導者有時最有用的事，就是退離畫面中心。

Yining called the fabricated engagement story rubbish, but kept her response factual. A fantasy costume showed a heroine who chose not to slay the defeated dragon. The sly caption implied promises neither person had made. Warm vapor rose from her father's curry pot as she entered home.

以寧說假訂婚故事是胡扯，但回應仍只談事實。 一套奇幻服裝呈現選擇不殺敗北巨龍的女英雄。 狡猾的標題暗示兩人從未做過的承諾。 她回家時，父親的咖哩鍋冒出溫熱蒸氣。

Yancheng's public relations team sent the final correction to both companies before publication. Yining checked only the facts concerning her brand and declined to approve any line about his feelings. The rule felt almost painfully formal, yet it protected the very possibility of honest affection: neither person would have to wonder whether a private word had become a business obligation.

硯承的公關團隊發布前把最後澄清送給兩家公司。以寧只檢查涉及品牌的事實，拒絕核准任何描述他感情的字句。規則正式得有些生硬，卻恰好保護了真實情感的可能：兩人都不必擔心私話變成商業義務。
