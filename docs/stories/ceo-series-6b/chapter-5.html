A Ledger in Red

裴總，這份婚約不在收購清單裡 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The first trial results looked excellent until Mei noticed that the return rate was missing from the summary. Sales had risen, but many customers had sent back coats with poorly attached fasteners. Yining insisted that the data be released with an explanation, even if the figures hurt her brand. When she visited Pei headquarters, Lin Shuo suggested moving the problem costs into a separate account to protect the company's annual report. Yancheng rejected the suggestion in front of the finance team.

第一份試辦成果看起來相當亮眼，直到小美發現摘要沒有列退貨率。銷售增加了，但不少顧客把扣件縫製不良的外套退回。以寧堅持公開數據並說明原因，即使這會傷害品牌。她到裴氏總部時，林朔提議把問題成本移到另一個帳戶，以保護年報。硯承當著財務團隊的面否決了提議。

The defective fastener was sold as an accessory, not part of the main coat. The accountant requested a full breakdown of repair expenses. Lin's proposal would bypass the independent review process. No champagne was served at the tense finance meeting.

瑕疵扣件被當作配件販售，而非外套本體。 會計要求完整分項列出維修支出。 林朔的提議會繞過獨立審查程序。 緊張的財務會議沒有供應香檳。

A young accountant pointed out that the supplier's bank account had changed three days before the flawed shipment. The payment record led to a company controlled by a distant relative of Lin Shuo. This was a conflict worth investigating, not proof of theft. Yining arranged for an external auditor to check signatures and delivery documents. She refused a reporter's request to call Lin a criminal before the review had even begun.

年輕會計發現，瑕疵品出貨前三天，供應商的銀行帳戶曾變更。款項轉進林朔遠親控制的公司。這是應該調查的利益衝突，不等於已證明偷竊。以寧安排外部稽核核對簽名與交貨資料，也拒絕記者在調查前就把林朔稱為罪犯。

A chestnut-colored sample revealed uneven dye along the collar. Calling the whistleblower cowardly would only discourage future reports. The original sketch could evoke memories of Yining's first workshop. An illustrated farm collection included a bright fowl on its label.

一件栗色樣衣顯示領口染色不均。 稱檢舉者懦弱，只會阻止往後的人通報。 原始草圖能喚起以寧第一間工作室的回憶。 農場主題系列的標籤畫著鮮豔的家禽。

That afternoon Yancheng's car waited outside the workshop while he reviewed a contract on the pavement. Yining could see that he was exhausted. She offered him a chair and tea, then handed him the unpleasant return statistics. He read every page before thanking her. “If you expect me to protect my reputation by hiding your losses,” he said, “you have misunderstood me.” She answered that she would judge him by the next decision, not by the force of his voice.

下午硯承的車停在工作室外，他站在人行道上看合約。以寧看得出他很累，便遞給他椅子與茶，接著交上難看的退貨統計。他逐頁讀完才道謝。「如果你以為我會靠隱瞞你的損失保護名聲，那你誤會我了。」以寧說，她會看他下一步怎麼做，而不是聽他的聲音有多強勢。

The sales assistant was hoarse after explaining the recall all morning. The repair kit included lotion safe for handling rough synthetic material. Yining challenged the mentality that higher prices always meant better quality. The audit compared monetary losses with the cost of honest repairs.

銷售助理解釋召回一整個早上，嗓音已沙啞。 維修包裡附有處理粗糙合成材料後可用的乳液。 以寧挑戰價格愈高品質必然愈好的心態。 稽核比較金錢損失與誠實維修的成本。

During a staff meeting, the repair team argued that they needed authority to reject unsafe batches without permission from the sales department. Yining supported them. Yancheng agreed to a temporary stop rule, subject to an independent appeal. The change delayed the holiday launch and would cost real money. It also prevented the board from treating safety as an optional feature to be sacrificed whenever a profitable deadline appeared.

員工會議上，維修團隊要求有權拒收不安全批次，不必先經業務部門同意。以寧支持，硯承同意設立暫時停工規則，並提供獨立申訴程序。這使節慶上市延遲，確實增加成本；卻也避免董事會把安全當成趕上獲利期限時可以犧牲的選項。

Yancheng looked moody until he saw the detailed return figures. The repair team became the nucleus of a broader customer safety program. A short rap performance introduced the apprentices at the staff gathering. One executive wanted to slash repair costs despite the recorded defects.

硯承看起來心情陰鬱，直到看見詳細退貨數字。 維修團隊成為更廣泛顧客安全計畫的核心。 員工聚會用一段簡短饒舌表演介紹學徒。 一位高層即使知道瑕疵，仍想大砍維修成本。

At the end of the fifth week, the audit placed the suspicious payments under review and confirmed that a legal path existed for compensating customers. It did not declare anyone guilty. Yining had gained something smaller and more valuable than a dramatic victory: a written process that workers could use without asking either of the two powerful people for a favor. Yancheng watched her explain the process to the staff and realized he admired her most when she disagreed with him.

第五週結束時，稽核把可疑款項列入審查，也確認顧客可依法請求補償，卻沒有宣告誰有罪。以寧獲得的是比戲劇性勝利更小、卻更重要的成果：一套員工不用向兩位有權勢的人求情，也能自行使用的書面程序。硯承看她向員工說明，發現自己最欣賞她的時候，恰好是她不同意自己的時候。

An exhausted accountant began to snore during the overnight document review. The telecommunications contract covered secure calls from trial stores. The team would not unveil the revised coat before independent testing. Mei defended the workers' reporting rights with unexpected vigor.

徹夜審文件時，疲憊的會計開始打鼾。 電信合約涵蓋試辦門市的安全通訊。 獨立測試前，團隊不會揭曉改版外套。 小美以出人意料的活力捍衛員工通報權。

When the weekly staff meeting ended, Yining stayed to hear two repair workers explain why the defective fastener had been difficult to detect. Their explanation was precise and uncomfortable: management had shortened a quality check to save time. She wrote the lost inspection minutes into the next budget, treating them as necessary work rather than waste.

每週員工會議結束後，以寧留下來聽兩位維修工說明扣件瑕疵為何難以察覺。他們的答案精確而難堪：管理層為了省時間縮短了品管。她把被刪掉的檢驗分鐘數重新列入下一份預算，視為必要工作，而非浪費。
