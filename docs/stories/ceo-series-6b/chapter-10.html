Terms of the Trial

At the halfway review, the trial had produced both promising sales and embarrassing mistakes. The independent committee confirmed that the repair policy had improved, but the replacement lining and missing fabric shipment required further investigation. Lin Shuo argued for ending the entire partnership and offering Yining a cash settlement. She requested a narrower decision: keep the safe product lines available, suspend questionable batches, and disclose the risks to customers. The committee adopted her proposal for another thirty days.

中期檢討時，試辦同時帶來亮眼銷售與難堪錯誤。獨立委員會確認維修政策改善，卻認為替換內襯及失蹤布料仍須調查。林朔主張終止合作，給以寧一筆和解金。她要求較精準的決定：安全的款式繼續販售，有疑慮的批次停用，風險要告知顧客。委員會採納她的方案，再延長三十天。

A short film would animate the repair process for customers. Simple arithmetic exposed the missing return figures in the summary. A visitor called the accessible collection awesome after trying it. The riverside café served celery soup during the long meeting.

短片會以動畫呈現顧客維修流程。 簡單算術揭露了摘要漏列的退貨數據。 一位參觀者試穿後稱無障礙系列非常棒。 河邊咖啡館在漫長會議中供應芹菜湯。

During the hearing, a store manager admitted that the company had once rewarded employees for discouraging returns. The practice had been commonplace for years, although it was not written in the current policy. Yancheng ordered the bonus rule suspended and asked workers to participate in revising the system. He accepted that his own leadership had overlooked the incentives that shaped behavior. Yining did not praise him simply for admitting an obvious problem; she asked how the new rules would be enforced.

聽證時，一位店長坦承公司曾獎勵阻止退貨的員工。這個做法多年來很常見，卻沒寫進現行規定。硯承暫停獎金規則，邀請員工參與修訂，也承認自己的領導忽略了影響行為的誘因。以寧沒有只因他承認問題就稱讚，反而追問新制度如何落實。

The council checked whether the exhibition ramp's cement base was secure. A trade confederation offered advice on the nonexclusive distribution proposal. A sudden crackdown on returns would have punished honest customers. A playful crocodile costume appeared in the exhibition's children's section.

委員會檢查展示坡道的水泥底座是否牢固。 一個商業聯盟為非獨家通路提案提供意見。 突然嚴格限制退貨，反而會懲罰誠實顧客。 展覽兒童區出現一套俏皮鱷魚裝。

After the session, Yancheng invited her to walk along the river. It was the first invitation that had nothing to do with documents, stock, or cameras. Yining accepted for half an hour and told Mei where she would be. They watched the lights ripple under a bridge while he described learning the business from a father who never tolerated a mistake. Yining said understanding his past did not require her to excuse his present decisions. He said he knew.

會議後，硯承邀她去河邊散步。這是第一個與文件、存貨、鏡頭都無關的邀請。以寧答應半小時，先告訴小美行程。橋下燈光映在水面，他談起在不容犯錯的父親手中學經營。以寧說，理解過去不等於原諒現在每個決定。他說他明白。

The new policy served a dual purpose: safety and clear accountability. The store tried to equalize repair access across all customer groups. A faction of trustees opposed extending the trial. An unsafe launch would incur costs beyond the initial recall.

新政策有雙重目的：安全與責任明確。 門市試圖讓各類顧客都享有公平維修管道。 一派受託人反對延長試辦。 不安全上市會招致超過初次召回的成本。

He asked whether she had ever considered selling her company. She admitted that she had, during the year when her father was ill and her staff feared unpaid wages. But she had chosen to borrow less, grow slowly, and keep the design archive intact. His expression softened. “I thought you were refusing me,” he said. “I am refusing an ownership structure,” she answered. “That is not the same thing.” The distinction stayed with him.

他問她是否曾考慮賣掉公司。她承認父親生病、員工擔心領不到薪水那一年，她想過；最後仍選擇少借款、慢慢成長，保留設計檔案。他神情柔和了。「我以為你是在拒絕我。」他說。「我拒絕的是一種所有權安排，並不是同一件事。」她答。硯承把這個區別記在心裡。

Yining called hidden repair exclusions an injustice to customers. The finance dispute contained enough intrigue to attract newspapers. An unrealistically low price could lure customers into unfair terms. Yining stayed obstinate about retaining her design rights.

以寧稱隱藏維修排除條款對顧客不公平。 財務爭議充滿權謀色彩，足以吸引報紙。 不切實際的低價可能誘使顧客接受不公平條款。 在保留設計權上，以寧始終固執堅持。

That night Yining updated the trial ledger and signed the report under her own name. The committee's ruling gave the partnership time, not absolution. She expected more conflict before the final vote. Yet she also allowed herself a private thought: she wanted to see Yancheng again when neither of them had to negotiate. The wish felt dangerous because it was voluntary, not because anyone had threatened her independence. She left it unanswered for another day.

當晚以寧更新試辦帳本，以自己的名字簽署報告。委員會給的是更多時間，不是免責。她知道最終表決前還會有衝突。但她也容許自己有一個私人念頭：希望下次見硯承時，兩人不用談判。這個願望之所以危險，不是因為有人威脅她的自由，而是因為它出自她自己的心。她暫時不作答。

A pickpocket incident near the river prompted extra security at the event. Yancheng arrived punctual for their first walk outside work. Both lawyers praised the restraint shown during the public hearing. Exhaustion made Yancheng stagger slightly after the late review.

河邊發生扒手事件，活動因此加強保全。 第一次工作以外的散步，硯承準時到達。 雙方律師都稱讚公開聽證展現的克制。 深夜檢討後，疲勞讓硯承微微踉蹌。

The next day Yining rejected a celebratory press conference. The trial had earned a conditional extension, not an award. She wrote to customers explaining exactly what would remain on sale and which repairs were still being evaluated. The distinction disappointed her marketing manager, but it gave customers a usable answer instead of another perfect photograph.

隔天以寧拒絕舉辦慶祝記者會。試辦得到的是有條件延長，不是得獎。她寫信告知顧客哪些商品持續販售、哪些維修仍待評估。行銷主管有些失望，但顧客終於得到能使用的答案，而不是另一張完美照片。
