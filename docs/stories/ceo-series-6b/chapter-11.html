總裁身後的信託

The Trust Behind the Throne

The independent lawyer called Xinyi's warning justified. A dormant clause in the Pei family trust allowed a committee controlled by Lin Shuo's allies to replace Yancheng after a major financial loss. The clause was legal under the trust's old charter but had never been explained clearly to minority beneficiaries. Yancheng seemed less offended by the threat to his position than by the fact that employees might lose their jobs while powerful relatives traded votes in private.

獨立律師證實心儀的提醒有根據。裴家信託一項多年未動用的條款，容許林朔盟友控制的委員會在重大財務損失後撤換硯承。舊章程下這條合法，卻從未向小額受益人清楚說明。硯承最憤怒的不是總裁職位受威脅，而是有權勢的親戚私下交換票數，員工卻可能因此失業。

The family archive had won international acclaim before the payment questions emerged. An ambiguity in the trust charter gave the committee excessive discretion. The employee policy recognized bodily safety as a basic workplace obligation. A cold draught entered the boardroom through an unsealed window.

付款疑雲浮現前，家族檔案曾享有國際讚譽。 信託章程的一處歧義讓委員會有過大裁量權。 員工政策把身體安全列為工作場所的基本義務。 冷風從未密封的窗縫吹進董事會議室。

Yining refused to participate in the trust vote. She had no ownership stake and did not want a romantic rumor to become an excuse for political influence. Instead she submitted verified trial numbers to the independent reviewers, including returns and legal costs. The figures showed that the proposed buyout had been priced before the product defects became public. That timing raised questions about the fairness of the original offer, but it did not yet prove that anyone had planned a scheme.

以寧拒絕參與信託投票；她沒有股權，也不願讓戀情傳聞成為干涉決策的藉口。她只把經查核的試辦數字交給獨立審閱者，包括退貨和法律支出。數字顯示收購價格是在產品問題公開前就定好的。這使原始提案是否公平受到質疑，但尚未證明有人策畫陰謀。

The archive thermometer displayed temperature in Fahrenheit rather than Celsius. The trust records had been stored in a fireproof cabinet. The board reviewed the geographical reach of the trial stores. An outside hacker had not yet been ruled out in the access investigation.

檔案室溫度計顯示華氏，而非攝氏。 信託紀錄保存在防火櫃中。 董事會檢查試辦門市的地理分布。 存取調查仍未排除外部駭客的可能。

When Yancheng learned that a senior executive had told staff to prepare for a new president, he convened a meeting with elected employee representatives. He described the legal risk without promising that his own position was secure. One tailor asked what would happen to her pension if ownership changed. He did not improvise an answer. He promised to obtain a written opinion from an independent specialist and publish it with the staff committee's consent.

硯承得知高層已叫員工準備迎接新總裁，便召集經選出的員工代表。他說明法律風險，沒有保證自己的位置穩固。一名裁縫問收購後退休金怎麼辦。他沒有隨口承諾，而是答應請獨立專家出具書面意見，經員工委員會同意後公布。

Yining asked for an explanation a layman could understand. Transparent voting rules could liberate employees from private pressure. A mermaid design appeared in an old advertising illustration. The accountant separated miscellaneous expenses from documented repairs.

以寧要求一份外行人也看得懂的說明。 透明表決規則能使員工不再受私人壓力束縛。 舊廣告插畫裡有一幅人魚圖案。 會計把雜項支出與有單據的維修分開。

Late that night, Yining found him alone in a conference room, sleeves rolled up, an untouched meal beside the papers. She put a glass of water within reach. “You cannot solve exhaustion by acquiring more hours,” she said. He laughed at her imitation of his old language. She stayed only long enough to show him which financial assumptions the reviewers had challenged. Before leaving, she told him the legal specialist would call in the morning. He thanked her for the information, not for rescuing him.

深夜，以寧在會議室找到他。他捲起袖子，桌邊的飯一口未動。她放了杯水在旁邊。「你不能再併購幾個小時來解決疲勞。」她模仿他以前的口氣，他笑了。她只留下來指出審閱者質疑的財務假設，離開前提醒他法律專家隔天會來電。他謝的是資訊，不是她來救他。

The committee recorded every occurrence of unauthorized contract changes. A staff outing was postponed so employees could attend the trust hearing. Yining encouraged the team to persevere through the slow audit. Yancheng needed time to ponder the limits of inherited authority.

委員會記錄每次未授權修改合約的事件。 為讓員工參與信託聽證，部門出遊延期。 以寧鼓勵團隊堅持完成緩慢的稽核。 硯承需要時間思考繼承來的權力界線。

The trust committee set a date for the vote. Lin Shuo appeared confident, and Yancheng's mother advised him to reach a private compromise. He declined any arrangement that would waive the employees' ability to challenge the consequences. Yining heard the news at her workshop, where a sewing machine continued to hum behind her. She was beginning to believe that he might change, but she still waited for the vote. Principles became real only when they cost something.

信託委員會訂下投票日期。林朔很有把握，裴夫人勸兒子私下妥協。他拒絕任何要員工放棄質疑權利的安排。以寧在工作室得知消息，身後縫紉機仍嗡嗡運轉。她開始相信硯承可能真的在改變，卻仍等著看表決結果。原則只有在必須付出代價時，才算真正受到考驗。

The committee's questions continued to revolve around the missing payments. A metal utensil lay beside Yancheng's untouched lunch. Yining refused to assign a villain before the evidence was complete. Xinyi brought plain yogurt to the late meeting as a quick snack.

委員會的問題始終圍繞失蹤款項。 硯承未吃的午餐旁放著一件金屬餐具。 證據完整前，以寧拒絕先指定反派。 心儀帶原味優格到深夜會議當點心。

During a private briefing, Xinyi told Yancheng that the reform might reduce his personal authority even if he survived the vote. He answered that a system which worked only because he happened to be president was already fragile. He asked her to document the risks plainly, including those that could reflect badly on him.

私下簡報時，心儀告訴硯承，即使他表決後留任，改革也可能減少其個人權力。他說一套只因他剛好當總裁才運作的制度，本來就很脆弱。他請她清楚寫出風險，連可能使自己難堪的也要列明。
