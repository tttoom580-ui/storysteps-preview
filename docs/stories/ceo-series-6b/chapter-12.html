沒有鏡頭的夜晚

An Evening Without Cameras

裴總，這份婚約不在收購清單裡 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The workshop's apprentice program held its first open evening in a neighborhood community center. Yining invited families to see how clothing could be altered for different bodies. There was no exclusive brand display and no compulsory purchase. A teenage visitor asked whether the skill would help her find work. Yining explained the training path, the wages, and its limitations. Yancheng attended as a guest and carried folding chairs when a volunteer asked for help.

工作室的學徒課程在社區中心舉行第一次開放夜。以寧邀請家庭來看服裝如何依不同身形修改，沒有獨家品牌展示，也不要求購買。一位少女問這項技能能否幫她找工作。以寧解釋訓練管道、薪資與限制。硯承以訪客身分出席，有志工請求時便去搬折疊椅。

A community center attendant helped visitors find the accessible entrance. The cooking class showed how batter could stain a cotton apron. A local census helped estimate demand for accessible clothing. A textile chemist explained how different dyes affected sensitive skin.

社區中心服務員協助來賓找到無障礙入口。 烹飪課示範麵糊如何弄髒棉圍裙。 地方人口普查有助估算無障礙服裝需求。 紡織化學師說明不同染劑如何影響敏感皮膚。

A sudden power failure interrupted the demonstration. People instinctively turned toward the president, assuming he would order someone to fix it. He looked instead at the community center manager, who knew where the emergency equipment was stored. While volunteers placed safe lamps along the corridor, Yining continued the lesson by hand. The interruption revealed an unexpected strength of the program: the apprentices understood the garments well enough to work without expensive machines.

突然停電打斷示範。大家下意識看向總裁，以為他會命令人處理。他卻先看向熟悉緊急設備位置的社區中心管理員。志工在走廊擺好安全照明，以寧便改用手工繼續教學。中斷反而顯出課程的優點：學徒夠了解衣服，不靠昂貴機器也能工作。

Birds began to chirp outside when the power returned. The center would dispense emergency supplies only through trained staff. A volunteer gave a tired groan while carrying the last folding chair. An apprentice compared hidden production costs to the unseen part of an iceberg.

電力恢復時，窗外鳥兒開始啁啾。 中心只由受訓人員發放緊急用品。 搬最後一張折疊椅時，志工疲倦地呻吟。 一位學徒把隱藏成本比作冰山看不見的部分。

After the event, Yancheng and Yining walked past a small food market. He asked whether he could buy her supper. This time she said yes. They found an ordinary table beneath an awning and ordered noodles from a woman who had never heard of his company. When the vendor asked how spicy they wanted the food, Yancheng waited for Yining to answer for herself. She noticed and smiled, though neither mentioned the change.

活動後，兩人走過小食市場。硯承問能不能請她吃宵夜，這次她答應了。他們在雨棚下找張普通桌子，向一位沒聽過裴氏的老闆娘點麵。老闆娘問要多辣時，硯承等以寧自己回答。她注意到這個小變化，笑了笑，兩人都沒特別提起。

The manager's calm directions proved invaluable during the outage. Yining preferred a simple supper to a lavish corporate banquet. The apprentices designed a logo that belonged to their training cooperative. A portable loudspeaker guided visitors to the safe exit.

停電時，管理員冷靜的指示非常寶貴。 以寧寧願吃簡單宵夜，也不要奢華企業晚宴。 學徒設計了一個屬於培訓合作社的標誌。 手提擴音器引導來賓走向安全出口。

He told her about studying tailoring for one summer before his father made him enter the finance department. She was surprised that he knew how to repair a hem. He admitted he was not very good at it. She described the first dress she had sold and the customer who had returned five years later asking for a new lining rather than a new garment. The stories were small, but they belonged to them, not to a press release or a negotiating table.

他說自己年輕時曾學過一個夏天裁縫，後來父親要他進財務部。她驚訝他竟會修裙邊。他承認技術不算好。她講起賣出的第一件洋裝，以及五年後回來要求更換內襯、不願買新衣的顧客。故事都很小，卻屬於他們，而不是公關稿或談判桌。

A martial arts teacher showed how flexible sleeves helped movement. The center reviewed its plan for a possible infectious outbreak. The street stall served oyster noodles beside the community center. Families began to populate the hall as the demonstration started.

武術老師示範有彈性的袖子如何幫助動作。 中心檢查可能發生傳染病爆發時的應變計畫。 社區中心旁的攤位供應蚵仔麵。 示範開始時，家庭陸續走進大廳。

At the station she said she had enjoyed the evening and would like to see him again outside work. She also reminded him that their companies would continue to negotiate through lawyers. He agreed to keep the two conversations separate. They did not kiss that night. When her train arrived, Yancheng stepped back from the door and watched her board. For once his most meaningful action was to make room rather than close a deal.

到車站時，以寧說自己喜歡今晚，也願意工作之外再見面，但兩家公司仍會透過律師談判。他同意把兩件事分開。那晚他們沒有接吻。列車進站時，硯承退開車門，看她上車。這一次他最有意義的行動不是完成交易，而是為另一個人留出空間。

The sudden power failure was a setback, not the end of the lesson. Yining remained skeptical of publicity claims that lacked actual training funds. Visitors could subscribe to updates without buying the collection. The charity promised not to withhold apprentice wages during an audit.

突然停電是挫折，卻不是課程終點。 沒有真正培訓經費的宣傳說法讓以寧保持懷疑。 來賓不必購買系列，也能訂閱更新資訊。 慈善機構承諾稽核期間不扣留學徒薪水。

On her way home, Yining remembered the apprentice who had asked whether tailoring offered a real career. She decided to invite that student to a paid workshop, not a ceremonial photo session. The modest fee would come from the program's published budget. She sent the invitation before allowing herself to think again about the meal by the river.

回家路上，以寧想起那位問裁縫能否成為職涯的學徒。她決定邀對方參加有薪工作坊，而不是拍照造勢。費用從公開的課程預算支付。她先寄出邀請，才容許自己再次想起河邊共餐。
