消失的貨櫃

The Missing Shipment

The warehouse inventory showed that six crates of premium fabric had been delivered. The staff on duty remembered only four. A video recording was missing precisely during the relevant hour, and the loading gate had been left open by a contractor whose badge had expired. Lin Shuo insisted that Yining's team must be responsible. She demanded a shared inspection, refusing both his accusation and the idea that her own workers should be immune from questioning.

倉庫系統記載六箱高級布料已入庫，值班員工卻只記得四箱。相關時段的監視影像正好遺失，裝卸門還曾由識別證過期的承包商開啟。林朔堅稱是以寧團隊的責任。她要求共同檢查，既拒絕林朔的指控，也不主張自己員工不應被查。

The warehouse nurse stored an antibiotic prescription in a locked cabinet. Staff chose to applaud the worker who had preserved the shipping record. An astronomer lent the showroom a star chart for its ceiling projection. The group promised to computerize the warehouse register with individual logins.

倉庫護理師把抗生素處方保存在上鎖櫃中。 員工為保存出貨紀錄的同事鼓掌。 一位天文學家借給展示廳星圖作天花板投影。 集團承諾把倉庫登記電腦化，並設個別登入帳號。

An independent technician recovered part of the gate's electronic log. The missing crates had been loaded onto a truck assigned to a different department. The delivery address belonged to a temporary showroom scheduled to open for a rival collection. Yining recognized the name of a designer who had previously offered to buy her first sketches. She made the investigators aware of that history but did not call the rival a thief. Her memory was a lead, not a verdict.

獨立技師恢復了部分電子閘門紀錄。失蹤的箱子被裝上另一部門的貨車，送往準備開設競品展示廳的臨時地址。以寧認出一位曾想收購自己初稿的設計師。她告訴調查員這段往事，卻沒有稱對方為小偷；記憶只是線索，不是判決。

The damaged gate could disable the automatic alarm without warning. The technician told everyone to evacuate the loading area during inspection. Yining requested a detailed record of warehouse security expenditure. Lin Shuo grew irritable when the court asked for original delivery logs.

損壞的閘門可能在無預警下使自動警報失效。 技師要求檢查期間所有人撤離裝卸區。 以寧要求完整記錄倉庫保全支出。 法院要求原始交貨紀錄時，林朔變得易怒。

Yancheng suggested visiting the showroom immediately. Yining objected because an unscheduled confrontation might destroy evidence. They chose instead to ask a court for preservation of the shipment and to notify the insurer. Yancheng initially looked impatient, then admitted that her slower plan protected both firms. He remained at the warehouse to question his own security procedures while she went back to reassure employees whose wages depended on the delayed collection.

硯承提議立刻去展示廳，以寧反對，因為突襲對質可能讓證據消失。他們改向法院聲請保全貨物並通知保險公司。硯承一開始顯得急躁，後來承認較慢的作法能保護雙方。他留在倉庫檢視集團保全制度，她則回工作室安定那些薪水仰賴新品上市的員工。

The investigation asked whether the intermediary was Lin Shuo's kin. Mei recommended a short meditation break after the stressful inspection. A loose nickel coin was found near the warehouse loading scale. The investigator used the floor map to orient new witnesses.

調查要查明中間商是否為林朔親屬。 緊張檢查後，小美建議短暫靜坐。 倉庫裝卸秤旁發現一枚鬆落的五分鎳幣。 調查員用樓層圖協助新證人辨明方向。

During a late call he asked why she never sounded impressed by his power. She answered that his power was simply a fact; the interesting question was what he did with it. Her frankness made him laugh for once. He asked whether he could buy her dinner after the investigation. She said he could ask again when their work was not under emergency review. She was not refusing dinner forever. She was refusing to make a business crisis into a romantic shortcut.

深夜通話時，硯承問她為何從不對他的權勢表示驚嘆。她說權勢只是事實，有趣的是他如何使用它。他難得笑了，問調查後能否請她吃飯。她答應他等緊急審查結束再問一次。她不是永遠拒絕晚餐，而是不讓商業危機成為感情的捷徑。

A historical label used the outdated word oriental, which the curator replaced. A former driver recovering from pneumonia gave testimony by video. The missing prototype was more valuable than the damaged fabric. The employee clinic offered voluntary psychiatry referrals after severe workplace stress.

歷史標籤用了過時的 oriental 一詞，館員將它更正。 一位從肺炎恢復中的前司機透過視訊作證。 失蹤的原型樣衣比受損布料更有價值。 員工診所為遭遇嚴重職場壓力者提供自願精神科轉介。

The recovered truck log became the first hard link between the false inventory and Lin Shuo's purchasing network. It was not yet clear who had ordered the transfer. By the end of the day the insurer had sealed the crates and the trial stores had been notified of the possible delays. Yining recorded both the progress and its limits in a message to her staff. A company could survive uncertainty better than it could survive a carefully polished lie.

恢復的貨車紀錄首次把假庫存與林朔的採購網絡連在一起，但下令轉運的人尚未查明。當晚保險公司封存貨箱，試辦門市也收到延遲通知。以寧向員工報告進展，也坦白限制。公司能承受不確定，比承受一個被修飾得很漂亮的謊言容易得多。

The revised access system needed robust records that could survive an outage. A sovereign emblem on the display wall was unrelated to warehouse ownership. The company spokesperson confirmed the shipment delay without naming suspects. The missing cloth was intended for a modular travel wardrobe.

修訂後門禁系統需要即使停電也可靠的紀錄。 展示牆上的主權徽章與倉庫所有權無關。 公司發言人確認出貨延遲，卻沒有公布嫌疑人姓名。 失蹤布料原本要做成可組合的旅行衣櫃系列。

The warehouse workers formed a small committee to review the next shipment's arrival. Instead of promising that no theft would ever happen again, they wrote down who would sign for each crate and where disputed records would be preserved. Yining endorsed the plan because it could be tested on an ordinary day, not merely praised after a crisis.

倉庫員工成立小組，檢查下一批貨物入庫。大家沒有保證以後絕不再失竊，而是寫明每箱由誰簽收、爭議紀錄保存在哪裡。以寧支持這個計畫，因為它能在平常日子被實際測試，不只是危機後拿來稱讚。
