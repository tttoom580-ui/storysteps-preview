被偷走的版型

The Stolen Pattern

裴總，這份婚約不在收購清單裡 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The trial began with a surprise. A discount retailer displayed a coat almost identical to Yining's signature design, including a hidden seam that allowed a seated customer to reach a pocket. The imitation was poorly cut, but its pattern could not have come from a photograph alone. Mei found an old archive of sample requests; one belonged to a contractor associated with Pei Luxury Group. Yining asked for the raw records before making any public accusation.

試辦剛開始就出現意外。折扣零售商展示了一件幾乎和以寧招牌設計相同的外套，連方便坐姿取物的隱藏縫線都一模一樣。仿品裁剪粗糙，但那種版型不可能只靠照片複製。小美從舊樣衣申請紀錄找到一名與裴氏有關的承包商。以寧要求先查原始紀錄，暫不公開指控。

The cashier preserved a receipt instead of obeying the supervisor's demand. The factory offered a voluntary checkup after workers reported skin irritation. Yining wanted to curb unauthorized access to the pattern archive. The factory alarm was loud enough to deafen anyone standing nearby.

收銀員保存了收據，沒有照主管要求銷毀。 工人反映皮膚刺激後，工廠提供自願健康檢查。 以寧希望遏止未授權存取版型檔案。 工廠警報大得足以震聾站在附近的人。

At the factory inspection, Yancheng arrived without announcing himself. A supervisor urged the staff to conceal damaged stock, but a young cashier handed over a properly dated receipt. Yining examined the stitching against her own signed sample. The counterfeit was not manufactured on the date stated in the distributor's complaint. Yancheng asked his internal audit office to preserve the shipping logs; he also removed Lin Shuo from direct access to the evidence until the review could be independently managed.

工廠稽查時，硯承沒有事先通報。主管想叫員工藏起瑕疵品，一位年輕收銀員卻交出有日期的收據。以寧比對縫線與自己簽名的樣衣，發現仿品不可能在經銷商申訴所寫的日期製造。硯承請內部稽核保存出貨紀錄，也暫時撤除林朔直接接觸證物的權限，交由獨立審查。

The supervisor called the stolen design a disgrace to his department. The warehouse's disposal register did not mention the missing fabric. A technician recorded her dissent from the first inspection report. A former seamstress planned to emigrate after her final contract ended.

主管稱設計遭竊是部門的恥辱。 倉庫廢棄物處理簿沒有提到失蹤布料。 一位技師對首份檢驗報告提出並記錄異議。 一名資深裁縫計畫合約期滿後移居海外。

Yining grew angry when a senior lawyer advised her to settle quietly because a fight might embarrass a powerful company. “I am not asking for a favor,” she replied. “I am asking for my work to be identified correctly.” Yancheng watched her across the table. Later he apologized for the pressure created by his group's name, but he made no promise that his employees were innocent. Neither of them could yet know who had taken the pattern.

一名資深律師勸以寧私下和解，免得讓大公司難堪。她生氣地說：「我不是來求情，我是要求作品被正確署名。」硯承在桌子另一端看著她。稍後他為集團名聲造成的壓力道歉，卻沒有保證自己的員工無辜。此時誰偷走了版型，兩人都還不知道。

A fair apprenticeship could enrich the lives of junior designers. The investigator warned that a lengthy inquiry might delay the launch. Yining viewed textile design as a lifelong commitment to practical beauty. “Madam, please keep the receipt,” the cashier said quietly.

公平的學徒制度能豐富年輕設計師的人生。 調查員提醒，冗長調查可能延誤上市。 以寧把紡織設計視為畢生追求實用之美的承諾。 收銀員輕聲說：「女士，請保留這張收據。」

A former worker agreed to speak only if the interview would not appear online. Yining and the independent investigator accepted that limit. The worker remembered a late delivery, a van with no normal company marking, and a supervisor who had insisted that several boxes bypass the warehouse scanner. The clues narrowed the dates, but they did not identify the thief. Instead of rushing to the press, Yining prepared a preservation order with her lawyer.

一位前員工只願在訪談不公開上網的條件下作證，以寧與獨立調查員同意。他記得一次深夜送貨、一輛沒有正常公司標記的貨車，以及一名堅持讓幾箱貨繞過倉庫掃描器的主管。這些線索縮小了時間範圍，卻仍不能指認偷竊者。以寧沒有急著找媒體，而是請律師準備證據保全聲請。

A melancholy silence filled the room when the stolen sketches appeared. The lawyer checked whether a private interview was permissible under the order. Yining refused to preach integrity while ignoring her own archive security. The counterfeit stitching was reminiscent of her earliest student experiments.

被竊草圖出現時，房間籠罩著憂鬱的沉默。 律師查核依命令是否允許私下訪談。 以寧拒絕一邊宣講誠信，一邊忽略自身檔案安全。 仿品縫法令人想起她學生時代最早的試作。

After the inspection, Yancheng asked whether she regretted accepting the trial. She said she regretted not discovering the security weakness earlier, but that the trial had created records she could now examine. He offered to cover the legal costs. She declined the personal payment and proposed a transparent shared investigation fund instead. He agreed. That evening their disagreement felt less like a battle between buyer and seller and more like a difficult conversation between two people who wanted the truth.

稽查後，硯承問她是否後悔同意試辦。她說後悔自己沒有早點發現保全漏洞，但試辦也留下可追查的紀錄。他提議私人支付律師費，她拒絕，改提公開透明的共同調查基金。他同意了。那天的爭論不再像買賣雙方的攻防，而像兩個都想查清真相的人，正在進行一場困難的對話。

The rivalry between distributors made the evidence politically sensitive. Every payment would now face independent scrutiny. The staff waited in suspense for the court's preservation decision. Yining's registered trademark remained outside the proposed acquisition.

經銷商之間的競爭讓證據變得敏感。 今後每筆款項都要接受獨立審查。 員工懷著懸念等待法院的保全裁定。 以寧已註冊的商標仍不列入收購範圍。

Mei drew three columns on the evidence board: facts verified, statements awaiting confirmation, and questions nobody could yet answer. Yining added the suspicious shipment to the second column. She knew that an honest uncertainty could be uncomfortable in public, but a confident mistake could destroy somebody's career. Her firm would not make that exchange.

小美在證據板畫了三欄：已核實事實、待確認說法，以及尚無答案的問題。以寧把可疑貨運放進第二欄。她知道公開不確定性令人不安，但自信的錯誤可能毀掉人的職涯。她的公司不會做這種交換。
