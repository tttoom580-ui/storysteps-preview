九十天，不移轉所有權

Ninety Days, No Ownership

Yining arrived at Pei headquarters with her own lawyer and a folder of warranty claims. The conference room overlooked the river, but she kept her attention on the contract. Yancheng offered financing, international shops, and a team of experienced buyers. In return he wanted an option to purchase the label. She crossed out the option and proposed a ninety-day, nonexclusive distribution trial. “If your stores can respect our customers,” she said, “the figures will make the argument.”

以寧與自己的律師帶著售後保固申訴資料來到裴氏總部。會議室能俯瞰河流，她卻只看合約。硯承提出資金、國際門市和採購團隊，交換條件是品牌收購選擇權。她劃掉那一條，改提九十天非獨家通路試辦。「如果你的門市真正尊重顧客，數字自然會說話。」

Every store manager had to abide by the published repair policy. The employee asked Yancheng to affirm the policy in writing. A playful alligator appeared on an optional children's raincoat print. A low bass melody played softly in the waiting room.

每位店長都必須遵守公開的維修政策。 店員請硯承以書面確認政策。 兒童雨衣可選的圖樣上有隻俏皮鱷魚。 等候室輕輕播放著低沉的低音旋律。

Lin Shuo, the group's strategy chief, leaned back in his chair and called the proposal sentimental. He warned that the board disliked uncertain revenue. Yancheng asked him for the calculation behind that warning. Lin had no complete model, only a slide filled with ambitious estimates. Yining understood then that Yancheng's reputation for forceful decisions might conceal something useful: he was willing to challenge a convenient number when he could not verify its source.

集團策略長林朔靠在椅背上，說她的提案太感情用事，董事會不喜歡不確定收入。硯承請他拿出計算依據；林朔只有一張充滿樂觀預估的投影片，沒有完整模型。以寧第一次發現，總裁強勢的名聲或許也有另一面：遇到無法驗證的數字，他願意當面質疑。

Mei rewrote the return instructions in colloquial language customers understood. Hidden service charges had become commonplace in competing luxury shops. The temporary showroom permit would expire at the end of the quarter. A damaged fuse briefly disabled the sample room lights.

小美把退貨說明改寫成顧客聽得懂的口語。 隱藏服務收費在其他精品店裡已經十分常見。 臨時展示廳的許可將於季度末到期。 損壞的保險絲讓樣品室燈光短暫熄滅。

During lunch, an employee from the trial store called Yining directly. Her supervisors had told her to hide repair prices because the printed brochure looked untidy. Yining put the call on speaker only after asking the employee's permission. Yancheng listened as the woman described a customer who had traveled two hours for a replacement fastener. He ordered the trial team to publish service prices and gave them forty-eight hours to correct the display.

午餐時，試辦門市一名員工直接打電話給以寧。主管要求她把維修價格藏起來，因為印在型錄上不好看。以寧先徵得同意才開擴音。硯承聽她說，一位顧客搭車兩小時只為換一顆扣件。他要求試辦團隊公開服務價格，並在四十八小時內修正展示。

Yancheng rejected hospitality gifts offered by a contractor under review. The consultant reminded staff that an infectious illness required safe leave arrangements. The dye workshop used a measured liter of water for each test. A novice assistant discovered the missing price information.

硯承拒絕接受正在受查的承包商所送的招待禮品。 顧問提醒員工，傳染性疾病需要安全的請假安排。 染色工作坊每次測試使用精確量好的一公升水。 一名新手助理發現價格資訊遺漏。

The afternoon meeting turned unexpectedly personal when a director asked whether Yining could be persuaded to accompany the president to a charity dinner. She said she would attend only as a designer presenting an accessibility collection. Yancheng accepted her condition without bargaining. Outside the building, rain had begun. His driver offered her a ride; she declined because she wanted to call Mei privately. He stepped aside, leaving the covered entrance to her.

下午有董事突然問，能否請以寧陪總裁出席慈善晚宴。她表示只會以展示無障礙服裝的設計師身分出席。硯承沒有討價還價。走出大樓時下起雨，司機提議送她一程，她想私下打電話給小美便拒絕了。硯承退開一步，把有雨棚的入口留給她。

Yining warned that a rash decision could end the trial unfairly. The finance team had to reconcile sales records with customer returns. An old, shabby coat became the best example of affordable repairs. A narrow spotlight illuminated the garment's adjustable fasteners.

以寧提醒，草率決定可能讓試辦不公平地中止。 財務團隊必須核對銷售紀錄與顧客退貨資料。 一件破舊外套反而成為平價維修的最佳示例。 一束聚光燈照亮了衣服的可調扣件。

By evening both legal teams had a provisional agreement: no transfer of design rights, independent auditing, published repair terms, and an exit that either party could exercise without a personal penalty. The agreement was not romantic, and Yining preferred it that way. Yet when the revised document arrived with every condition she had demanded, she caught herself wondering what sort of man took a refusal seriously enough to improve his own proposal.

傍晚雙方法務完成暫行協議：設計權不移轉、獨立稽核、維修條件公開，任一方可退出而不承擔私人懲罰。協議毫不浪漫，以寧反而喜歡。只是修訂文件真的納入她提出的每項條件時，她忍不住想：究竟是怎樣的人，會認真看待一句拒絕，甚至改善自己的提案？

The café served warm tea with a little ginger syrup. The display stand began to tilt until a technician stabilized it. At twilight, Yining reviewed the agreement beside the river window. Yancheng declined whiskey at lunch to keep the negotiations clear.

咖啡廳供應加少量薑糖漿的熱茶。 展示架開始傾斜，直到技師把它固定。 暮色中，以寧在河景窗邊重讀協議。 硯承午餐時婉拒威士忌，以保持談判清醒。

The revised contract had one more practical effect: store workers could report a repair problem without needing permission from the sales director. Yining insisted on testing that promise before the first shipment. She asked a volunteer to submit a sample complaint, watched the message reach the independent channel, and recorded the response time.

修訂合約還有一項實際效果：門市員工不必取得業務主管同意，就能通報維修問題。以寧堅持首批出貨前測試承諾。她請志工送出模擬申訴，確認訊息能進入獨立管道，再記下回覆時間。
