喊停的模特兒

The Model Who Said Stop

裴總，這份婚約不在收購清單裡 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

During a new fitting, a model reported that a fastening strap pressed painfully against her shoulder. The launch director dismissed the complaint as ordinary discomfort caused by unfamiliar clothing. Yining stopped the session and invited the model to demonstrate the problem on her own terms. The test revealed that a supplier had replaced a soft lining with a cheaper synthetic material. Yancheng authorized a temporary withdrawal of the affected lot, although the change threatened a prestigious magazine cover.

新一輪試衣時，模特兒反映固定帶壓得肩膀疼痛。上市主管說那只是穿不習慣造成的普通不適。以寧中止測試，讓模特兒依自己的方式說明。檢查發現供應商把柔軟內襯換成便宜的合成材料。雖然會影響重要雜誌封面，硯承仍核准暫時撤回受影響批次。

A model with acne asked for camera lighting that respected her skin. The clinic would administer the follow-up program without sharing medical records. The recall brochure explained refunds in plain language. Yining rejected a campaign that portrayed disability as captivity.

一位有痘痘的模特兒要求尊重膚況的攝影燈光。 診所將管理追蹤計畫，但不分享醫療紀錄。 召回手冊以簡明文字說明退款方法。 以寧拒絕把身心障礙描繪成囚禁的宣傳。

A medical consultant recommended evaluating the affected customers, not simply mailing them a coupon. Yining insisted that the consultation remain confidential and that nobody's health information appear in marketing materials. The supplier complained that a full review would be expensive. She answered that an elegant garment was not elegant if the person wearing it was afraid to move. Several younger designers listened closely; they had never heard that principle stated in a board meeting.

醫療顧問建議評估受影響顧客，而不是只寄折價券。以寧堅持諮詢必須保密，健康資料不能用於行銷。供應商抱怨完整審查太貴。她回答，若穿著者害怕活動，再美的衣服也談不上優雅。幾位年輕設計師仔細聽著；他們從沒在董事會上聽過這樣的原則。

The director tried to cram the recalled coats into a storage cabinet. The technician showed how to detach the defective strap safely. The model's discomfort revealed a problem beyond ordinary fitting pressure. A forthcoming report would describe the replacement material in detail.

總監試圖把召回外套硬塞進儲藏櫃。 技師示範如何安全拆下瑕疵固定帶。 模特兒的不舒服揭露了超出普通試衣壓迫的問題。 即將發布的報告會詳細說明替換材料。

Outside the studio, Yancheng received a message from his mother warning that the press might turn the recall into a scandal. He replied that the recall would be announced accurately, with instructions for refunds and repairs. Yining watched him write the message without asking her to approve it. The distinction mattered: he was responsible for his company's public statement, while she remained responsible for her brand and its customers.

工作室外，硯承收到母親訊息，提醒媒體可能把召回事件炒成醜聞。他回答將如實公告，提供退款與維修指引。以寧看他寫下訊息，沒有要求自己批准。這個界線很重要：他負責集團的公開聲明，她則對自己的品牌與顧客負責。

A bright inspection lamp helped illuminate the hidden seam. A pale jade button was tested as an alternative decoration. The models hoped the corrected garments would support a joyous performance. The recall instructions were tested for different levels of reading literacy.

明亮檢查燈照亮了隱藏縫線。 一顆淡玉色鈕扣被測試作為替代裝飾。 模特兒希望修正後的服裝能讓演出更愉快。 召回說明依不同閱讀識字程度進行測試。

They spent the evening inspecting samples under bright lamps. Yancheng held fabric while Yining checked the seams. At one point their hands touched over a roll of material, and both withdrew slightly. He asked whether the contact had bothered her. She said no and, after a moment, placed the fabric back between them. It was an ordinary, respectful exchange, yet it changed the atmosphere more than any expensive gift could have done.

兩人晚上在強光下檢查樣品。硯承扶住布料，以寧查看縫線。有一次手在布卷上碰到，兩人都稍微縮回。他問她是否介意，她說不介意，停了一下又把布料放回兩人中間。這是一個平常又尊重彼此的互動，卻比昂貴禮物更能改變氣氛。

A narrator described the changes during the accessible product demonstration. A participant reported irritation near one nostril from a synthetic scent. The collection's travel photographs had been taken along a quiet peninsula. The dye consultant warned that a potent solvent required safer handling.

旁白員在無障礙產品示範時說明改動。 一位參與者說合成香味刺激了單側鼻孔附近。 系列旅行照片拍攝於寧靜的半島。 染劑顧問提醒，強效溶劑需要更安全的處理方式。

The recall report described the exact defect, the number of potentially affected coats, and a clear way to request help. It also recorded that the model's willingness to speak had prevented wider harm. Yining asked whether the model wanted to be named; she preferred anonymity. The report respected that choice. Yancheng sent Yining a final message: “Thank you for stopping the show.” She wrote back, “Thank the person who first said stop.”

召回報告列出確切瑕疵、可能受影響的外套數量，以及求助方法，也記載是模特兒勇於提出問題才避免更大傷害。以寧問她願不願署名，對方選擇匿名，報告便尊重這個決定。硯承傳訊息：「謝謝你叫停。」以寧回：「該謝的是第一個說停的人。」

The revised coat marked a revival of Yining's original design principles. The team rejected selective reporting of only favorable test results. One model's stepchild helped test the children's adjustable jacket. A small trout appeared on the sustainable fabric's printed pattern.

改版外套象徵以寧原始設計理念的復興。 團隊拒絕只挑有利測試結果報告。 一位模特兒的繼子女協助測試兒童可調外套。 永續布料的印花上有一條小鱒魚。

The model who had stopped the fitting later wrote that she would return only when the revised straps passed independent testing. Yining thanked her without attempting to negotiate a softer condition. The letter was attached to the product safety file with the model's permission. Her words would remain visible to the people responsible for making the next batch.

當初叫停試衣的模特兒後來寫信說，只有改版固定帶通過獨立測試，她才會回來。以寧道謝，沒有試圖要求放寬條件。取得同意後，信件附入產品安全檔案，讓下一批製作負責人看見她的原話。
