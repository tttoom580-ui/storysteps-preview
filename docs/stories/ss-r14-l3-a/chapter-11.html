The Torn Parcel

午夜種子庫的白色腳印 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A damaged parcel arrived from the cooperative's old accountant. It contained letters about seed loans, including promises that the garden would return samples whenever members needed them. The letters were personal and sometimes emotional, but each carried a date and a signature.

合作社前會計寄來一個受損包裹，裡面有關於種子借存的信件，包括園方承諾會員有需要時便歸還樣本。文字帶著個人感情，卻都附有日期和簽名。

One bloody fingerprint on the parcel came from a paper cut. Imani asked whose account had been missing from the discussion. A light breeze moved the loose letter across the desk. A sign with the wrong date could mislead someone years later. The original families deserved credit for protecting the seeds. For that reason, they marked the uncertain part instead of hiding it. Nora refused to label a clerk criminal before the investigation. Imani listened to a worker who had not spoken in the earlier meeting. They paused for water and checked the time. Nothing in the room would become clearer simply because they were frightened or impatient. Nora set a limit on the next test and asked Ben to confirm where the emergency exit would remain open.

包裹上一枚帶血指印來自紙張割傷。 伊曼妮問，討論中遺漏了誰的說法。 微風吹動桌上鬆散的信紙。 日期錯誤的標誌可能在多年後誤導別人。 原始家戶應因保護種子獲得肯定。 因此他們標示不確定的部分，而不是把它藏起來。 調查完成前，諾拉拒絕稱職員為罪犯。 伊曼妮傾聽一位先前會議未發言的員工。 大家停下來喝水、核對時間。恐懼或不耐煩都不會自動讓房間變得清楚。諾拉為下一次測試設定界線，並請班恩確認哪一處緊急出口會保持開放。

The family letters made Imani emotional but not careless. Imani kept her own copy so the families could challenge any future mistake. Renn had said the sale might bring the garden a fortune. The small details did not answer every question, but they narrowed the search. Old letters described hunger after a year of failed harvests. They returned the tools to their places so the next shift could work safely. Sal watched a cat lick water from a clean stone. They photographed the work area before putting anything back. Imani reminded Nora that records mattered to people beyond this room. Some families had saved these seeds through years when food was scarce. Others had trusted the garden with stories they did not want told in public. Both concerns belonged in the report.

家族信件讓伊曼妮動容，卻未使她失去謹慎。 伊曼妮留下自己的影本，讓家戶能質疑未來的錯誤。 雷恩曾說出售可能為園區帶來巨額財富。 這些小細節沒有回答所有問題，卻縮小了調查範圍。 舊信描述歉收之年後的飢餓。 他們把工具放回原位，讓下一班能安全工作。 薩爾看見貓從乾淨石頭上舔水。 把物品放回前，他們先拍下工作區。 伊曼妮提醒諾拉，這些紀錄關係到房間以外的人。有些家庭曾在糧食短缺時保存種子；另一些則把不願公開的故事託付園區。兩種顧慮都應寫進報告。

The accountant had packed a loaf of bread for the journey. Ben checked the emergency route one more time before closing the room. The parcel had been wrapped twice to protect the letters. Nora dated the paper before moving it to another table. A list of donated crops included several kinds of pepper. They could disagree about an explanation without changing the facts. The warehouse floor had a slight pitch toward the drain. A single change in the record could alter the meaning of a whole page. Nora checked the narrow margin of the paper under a lamp, then compared it with the copy. An ordinary error would have one history; a deliberate change would leave another. She did not know which explanation was true yet.

前會計為路程帶了一條麵包。 班恩關閉房間前再次檢查緊急通道。 包裹被包了兩層，以保護信件。 諾拉在移動紙張到另一張桌子前，先寫上日期。 捐贈作物清單包含數種辣椒。 他們可以對解釋有不同意見，但不能因此改動事實。 倉庫地板稍微傾斜，朝向排水口。 紀錄裡的一處修改，可能改變整頁的意思。 諾拉在燈下檢查紙張窄窄的邊緣，再與影本核對。普通錯誤會有一種形成過程，故意修改則會留下另一種痕跡。她還不知道哪一種解釋才是真的。

Nora would not pretend the letters proved every detail. The workers took a short break without abandoning the safety watch. Imani had to resist the urge to publish names immediately. The garden was quiet enough for the workers to hear the machinery. A torn sleeve caught on the edge of an old box. A visitor who saw the same evidence should be able to reach a similar conclusion. A slender green stem grew from one donated seed. Nora asked whether the same mark appeared on the original paper.

諾拉不會假裝這些信證明了所有細節。 員工短暫休息，但沒有中斷安全監控。 伊曼妮必須克制立刻公開姓名的衝動。 園區非常安靜，員工可以聽見機械聲。 破裂袖口勾住舊箱子邊緣。 看見相同證據的訪客應能得出相近結論。 一根纖細綠莖從捐贈的種子長出。 諾拉詢問原始紙本上是否也有相同記號。

The outer path followed a gentle slope to the storage room. For that reason, they marked the uncertain part instead of hiding it. There was a surprising twist in the dates on the letters. The team agreed to keep facts separate from their guesses. The workers' union asked to review the safety findings. The quiet pause gave everyone a chance to check the numbers again. Nora wore an identification band around her wrist. The disagreement mattered, because it could change who controlled the seeds.

外側小路沿緩坡通向儲藏室。 因此他們標示不確定的部分，而不是把它藏起來。 信件日期出現令人驚訝的轉折。 團隊同意把事實與猜測分開。 工會要求檢視安全調查結果。 短暫的安靜讓每個人有機會再次核對數字。 諾拉手腕上戴著識別手環。 這項分歧很重要，因為它可能改變誰有權控制種子。

Nora arranged the letters by date and saw a pattern: the company had stored the seeds but never received ownership. She scanned only the relevant passages and asked permission before sharing the private stories inside them.

諾拉按日期排列信件，看出公司只是代為保存，從未取得所有權。她只掃描相關段落，分享私人故事前也先取得同意。
