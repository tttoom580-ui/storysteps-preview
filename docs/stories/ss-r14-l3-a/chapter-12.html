The Basement Alphabet

In the basement, Imani found a wooden alphabet board used when the garden taught children to read seed labels. Behind it lay a thin notebook listing every family that contributed to the first collection. Some names had been crossed out, but the ink underneath was still visible.

伊曼妮在地下室找到舊木製字母板，當年園區用它教孩子讀種子標籤。字母板後面藏著一本薄冊，記錄第一批捐種家庭。部分姓名被劃掉，底下的墨水仍可辨識。

The wooden alphabet board once taught children seed names. Nobody was allowed to change the record without leaving a name. The basement held older files than the electronic archive. A careful comparison mattered more than a dramatic announcement. An empty cigarette box lay behind the wooden board. No one would benefit from a report that hid its own limits. A rusty clip held three old pages together. Nora placed the sealed note beside the related photograph. The garden held living material, not just numbers in a table. If a decision could damage the last sample, it needed more than a hurried signature. Imani asked the workers to record who had been consulted and who had not.

木製字母板過去用來教孩子讀種子名稱。 沒有人能在不留下姓名的情況下更動紀錄。 地下室保存著比電子檔案更早的文件。 仔細比較比戲劇性的宣告更重要。 木板後有一個空香菸盒。 一份隱瞞自身限制的報告不會真正幫助任何人。 一枚生鏽的夾子把三張舊紙夾在一起。 諾拉把封存筆記放在相關照片旁。 園區保存的是活的材料，不只是表格裡的數字。如果一個決定可能傷害最後一份樣本，就不能只靠倉促簽名。伊曼妮請員工記錄曾諮詢誰、又遺漏誰。

At dawn, a cock crowed from the farm beyond the road. Nora kept the original and the copy in different envelopes. The collection included more than two hundred seed varieties. Imani asked whose account had been missing from the discussion. The morning was foggy, making the outer garden hard to see. A missing signature was a reason to investigate, not to accuse. Renn had wanted to hire a private records consultant. Imani compared the date against a second independent source. Ben pointed to the safety drawing and asked the team to imagine arriving there for the first time. A good rule had to help a tired person take the correct action without knowing any of the rumors. Nora added that test to her notes.

黎明時，路對面農場的公雞啼叫。 諾拉把原件和影本放入不同信封。 收藏包含兩百多種種子品種。 伊曼妮問，討論中遺漏了誰的說法。 早晨霧氣濃重，外園難以看清。 缺少簽名是調查的理由，不是直接指控的理由。 雷恩曾想聘請私人檔案顧問。 伊曼妮將日期與第二個獨立來源核對。 班恩指著安全圖，請大家想像第一次到這裡的情況。好的規則應幫助疲憊的人採取正確行動，而不需先知道任何傳聞。諾拉把這項檢驗方式寫進筆記。

Imani asked every witness for honesty about uncertain memories. Ben stayed near the entrance so nobody would enter an unsafe area alone. A kindergarten class had donated drawings to the seed display. Ben asked if a photograph could be taken without opening the container. The old room had been designed for learning, not sales. Imani insisted that every family keep the right to tell its own story. The families wanted the liberty to request their own seeds. Imani explained why a correct name could matter to an entire family. They paused for water and checked the time. Nothing in the room would become clearer simply because they were frightened or impatient. Nora set a limit on the next test and asked Ben to confirm where the emergency exit would remain open.

伊曼妮請每位證人誠實說明不確定的記憶。 班恩守在入口，避免有人獨自進入危險區域。 一班幼稚園孩子曾捐畫給種子展。 班恩問能否不打開容器就完成拍照。 舊房間設計用途是學習，而不是販售。 伊曼妮堅持每個家戶都保有敘述自身故事的權利。 家戶希望有自由申請取回自家種子的權利。 伊曼妮說明正確姓名為何對整個家庭都很重要。 大家停下來喝水、核對時間。恐懼或不耐煩都不會自動讓房間變得清楚。諾拉為下一次測試設定界線，並請班恩確認哪一處緊急出口會保持開放。

The matching dates gave the search meaningful support. Nora placed the sealed note beside the related photograph. A little white powder from an old label fell onto the table. Nobody was allowed to change the record without leaving a name. The previous catalogue still named the original owners. The result was useful precisely because the procedure could be repeated. A queer mark on the page proved to be an old number. Ben refused to open a door while the warning light was active.

相符的日期為調查提供有意義的支持。 諾拉把封存筆記放在相關照片旁。 舊標籤上的少量白色粉末落到桌上。 沒有人能在不留下姓名的情況下更動紀錄。 前一版清冊仍列出原始所有人。 這項結果有用，正因程序能夠重複。 頁上一個奇特記號原來是舊數字。 警示燈亮著時，班恩拒絕開門。

The faded ink could reveal a name beneath the crossing line. They could disagree about an explanation without changing the facts. A forgotten bottle of shampoo sat in the staff washroom. Nora wrote the observation in a notebook rather than trusting memory. Families used to weave baskets to carry harvested seeds. Imani asked that private names be covered on the public copy. Sal said farming wisdom was stored in the letters too. The old building had answers in its records, but not all were easy to read.

褪色墨水可能顯露刪除線下的姓名。 他們可以對解釋有不同意見，但不能因此改動事實。 員工盥洗室留著一瓶被忘記的洗髮精。 諾拉把觀察寫進筆記本，而不是只靠記憶。 家戶過去會編織籃子搬運採收種子。 伊曼妮要求在公開影本上遮住私人姓名。 薩爾說信件也保存著農耕智慧。 老建築的紀錄裡藏著答案，卻不是每一筆都容易讀懂。

The notebook matched the metal gate plate and the donated receipts. Nora now had three independent sources for the same transfer date. She copied them carefully instead of celebrating too early.

筆記本與舊門金屬牌、捐贈收據相符。諾拉現在掌握三個互相獨立、指向同一移交日期的來源。她謹慎抄錄，沒有過早慶祝。
