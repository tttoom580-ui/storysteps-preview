The next morning the team studied a line of green footprints across the east hall. The marks were light, not paint: they vanished when Ben cut the electricity to the exhibition projector. Director Renn dismissed them as a harmless attraction, but the projected path ended at a service fan with no public guardrail.

隔天早上，團隊研究東廊的一列綠色腳印。那些痕跡是光，不是油漆；班恩切斷展覽投影機電源後，腳印便消失。雷恩主任說這只是無害的導覽設計，但投影路線的終點是一座沒有公共護欄的維修風扇。

A tree-lined avenue led visitors to the east exhibition. Imani reminded them that a collection had people behind every jar. A wooden cradle held a model of the first seed vault. The garden was quiet enough for the workers to hear the machinery. Volunteers had used paper leaves to decorate the entrance. A visitor who saw the same evidence should be able to reach a similar conclusion. The service elevator was locked after the final tour. Nora asked whether the same mark appeared on the original paper. The garden held living material, not just numbers in a table. If a decision could damage the last sample, it needed more than a hurried signature. Imani asked the workers to record who had been consulted and who had not.

一條林蔭大道引導遊客前往東側展區。 伊曼妮提醒他們，每個種子罐背後都有人。 木製搖籃托著第一代種子庫的模型。 園區非常安靜，員工可以聽見機械聲。 志工曾用紙葉子裝飾入口。 看見相同證據的訪客應能得出相近結論。 最後一場導覽後，貨梯便上鎖。 諾拉詢問原始紙本上是否也有相同記號。 園區保存的是活的材料，不只是表格裡的數字。如果一個決定可能傷害最後一份樣本，就不能只靠倉促簽名。伊曼妮請員工記錄曾諮詢誰、又遺漏誰。

The old exhibition projector made the green footprints. Nobody moved the last jar until its label and location matched. A photograph showed a firework above the garden festival. The team agreed to keep facts separate from their guesses. Ben measured one gallon of water from the leaking pipe. The quiet pause gave everyone a chance to check the numbers again. The owner's headquarters had approved the new visitor route. The disagreement mattered, because it could change who controlled the seeds. Ben pointed to the safety drawing and asked the team to imagine arriving there for the first time. A good rule had to help a tired person take the correct action without knowing any of the rumors. Nora added that test to her notes.

舊展覽投影機製造了綠色腳印。 最後一罐的標籤與位置相符前，沒有人移動它。 照片記錄了花園節慶上空的煙火。 團隊同意把事實與猜測分開。 班恩量出漏水管流出的一加侖水。 短暫的安靜讓每個人有機會再次核對數字。 業主總部曾核准新遊客動線。 這項分歧很重要，因為它可能改變誰有權控制種子。 班恩指著安全圖，請大家想像第一次到這裡的情況。好的規則應幫助疲憊的人採取正確行動，而不需先知道任何傳聞。諾拉把這項檢驗方式寫進筆記。

Nora said imagination could not replace a safety test. The group agreed that safety and ownership were separate questions. A painted leopard watched from a children's animal mural. They left the original object where it could be safely examined. In the dark, the projected path looked almost magical. Nora wrote down both what she knew and what remained untested. It was odd that the bright path ended at a dangerous fan. Ben kept the passage clear for workers carrying heavy equipment.

諾拉說想像力不能代替安全測試。 大家同意安全與所有權是兩個分開的問題。 兒童動物壁畫上有隻彩繪豹。 他們把原件留在安全且可受檢視的位置。 黑暗中，投影道路看起來幾乎像魔法。 諾拉同時記下已知和仍未測試的事情。 明亮道路竟通向危險風扇，這點很奇怪。 班恩讓通道保持暢通，方便員工搬運重型設備。

The side shop sold organic vegetables from nearby farms. Nora asked whether the same mark appeared on the original paper. A red ribbon marked the page missing from the route book. Imani reminded them that a collection had people behind every jar. The loose pipe could spit warm water across the passage. Ben checked the safety limits before anyone attempted another test. The white tiles avoided a steep ramp near the service door. The workers took a short break without abandoning the safety watch.

側邊商店販售附近農場的有機蔬菜。 諾拉詢問原始紙本上是否也有相同記號。 一條紅緞帶標出路線冊缺頁的位置。 伊曼妮提醒他們，每個種子罐背後都有人。 鬆動管線可能把溫水噴濺到通道上。 任何人嘗試下一次測試前，班恩先核對安全限制。 白色地磚避開了維修門旁的陡坡。 員工短暫休息，但沒有中斷安全監控。

Imani planned a survey of visitors who had used the route. Imani asked that private names be covered on the public copy. The open fan was a real threat, unlike the false ghost story. They checked the labels under steady light from two different angles. A tourist had photographed the footprints the day before. Nora made sure the temporary decision could be corrected later. Ben used a cloth to wipe the dust from the projector. They checked the condition of the box before signing the form.

伊曼妮打算調查曾走過那條路線的遊客。 伊曼妮要求在公開影本上遮住私人姓名。 裸露風扇是真正的威脅，不像虛構的鬼故事。 他們在穩定的光線下從兩個角度檢查標籤。 一名遊客前一天拍下了那些腳印。 諾拉確認臨時決定日後可以更正。 班恩用布擦掉投影機上的灰塵。 簽署表單前，他們先檢查箱子的狀況。

Nora followed the white floor numbers instead. They led to a locked reading room with an older map and a different route. Someone had left a bright ribbon between two books, pointing at a handwritten warning about the east hall. The rule against green footprints was not a ghost story; it described an actual wrong turn.

諾拉改沿白色地磚編號行走，來到一間上鎖的閱覽室，裡面有一張較舊、路線也不同的地圖。有人在兩本書之間留下亮色緞帶，指向一則手寫的東廊警告。禁止跟隨綠色腳印的規則不是鬼故事，而是在提醒人們不要走錯路。
