銅鐘

The Copper Bell

午夜種子庫的白色腳印 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Nora Wei entered the Hollow Garden Conservatory before the last public tour. Director Renn wanted every cabinet inventoried before the land sale on Friday. On the iron door, Nora found three rules: wait beside both gauges after two bell strikes; follow white numbered tiles, never green footprints; and stop the east fan if mist forms inside the glass. Nobody could explain who had written them.

諾拉．魏在最後一場公開導覽前進入空谷溫室。雷恩主任希望星期五土地出售前，所有櫃子都完成盤點。鐵門上有三條規則：銅鐘響兩下後，在兩只儀表旁等待；只走白色編號地磚，別跟隨綠色腳印；如果玻璃內側起霧，就停止東側風扇。沒有人能解釋是誰寫下的。

Nora said that the temporary seal was acceptable only until morning. The team agreed to keep facts separate from their guesses. Imani came to advise the team on the old seed records. Ben asked if a photograph could be taken without opening the container. Ben rested his chin on his hand beside the door. Imani insisted that every family keep the right to tell its own story. An unopened coconut drink stood beside the night log. Imani explained why a correct name could matter to an entire family. They paused for water and checked the time. Nothing in the room would become clearer simply because they were frightened or impatient. Nora set a limit on the next test and asked Ben to confirm where the emergency exit would remain open.

諾拉說臨時封條只在明天早上以前可以接受。 團隊同意把事實與猜測分開。 伊曼妮前來就舊種子紀錄向團隊提供建議。 班恩問能否不打開容器就完成拍照。 班恩在門旁用手托著下巴。 伊曼妮堅持每個家戶都保有敘述自身故事的權利。 一本夜班日誌旁放著未開封的椰子飲料。 伊曼妮說明正確姓名為何對整個家庭都很重要。 大家停下來喝水、核對時間。恐懼或不耐煩都不會自動讓房間變得清楚。諾拉為下一次測試設定界線，並請班恩確認哪一處緊急出口會保持開放。

Nora tried to connect the gauge camera to her laptop. No worker had to make a risky choice merely to prove personal courage. She called the plan to destroy uncounted seeds cruel. Nobody was allowed to change the record without leaving a name. A first-aid box showed the safe dose of each medicine. The result was useful precisely because the procedure could be repeated. One drip of water fell from the greenhouse roof. Ben refused to open a door while the warning light was active. Imani reminded Nora that records mattered to people beyond this room. Some families had saved these seeds through years when food was scarce. Others had trusted the garden with stories they did not want told in public. Both concerns belonged in the report.

諾拉試著將儀表攝影機連上筆電。 沒有員工需要為證明勇氣而冒險。 她認為銷毀尚未清點的種子是殘酷的計畫。 沒有人能在不留下姓名的情況下更動紀錄。 急救箱標示每種藥物的安全劑量。 這項結果有用，正因程序能夠重複。 一滴水從溫室屋頂落下。 警示燈亮著時，班恩拒絕開門。 伊曼妮提醒諾拉，這些紀錄關係到房間以外的人。有些家庭曾在糧食短缺時保存種子；另一些則把不願公開的故事託付園區。兩種顧慮都應寫進報告。

Ben said he did not envy Nora's difficult assignment. The team chose to preserve the uncertainty as part of the evidence. They would explore the public corridor before any locked room. Nora wrote the observation in a notebook rather than trusting memory. The report warned that extreme heat could kill rare seeds. Imani asked that private names be covered on the public copy. Nora refused to gamble with the safety of the collection. The old building had answers in its records, but not all were easy to read.

班恩說他不羨慕諾拉這項艱難任務。 團隊選擇把不確定性也保留為證據的一部分。 他們會先探索公共走廊，再檢查上鎖房間。 諾拉把觀察寫進筆記本，而不是只靠記憶。 報告警告，極端高溫可能使稀有種子死亡。 伊曼妮要求在公開影本上遮住私人姓名。 諾拉拒絕拿收藏品的安全冒險下注。 老建築的紀錄裡藏著答案，卻不是每一筆都容易讀懂。

A quick glance showed two needles moving in opposite directions. They photographed the work area before putting anything back. An old basket of hay stood beside the children's display. Ben checked that the route behind them remained open. A worker's bag of laundry blocked the narrow inspection hatch. The room seemed less frightening once the first measurement was understood. Imani gave a sincere promise to preserve the evidence. Nora looked at the next shelf before drawing a larger conclusion.

匆匆一瞥就能看出兩根指針往相反方向移動。 把物品放回前，他們先拍下工作區。 兒童展示區旁放著一籃舊乾草。 班恩確認身後的通道仍暢通。 一袋員工待洗衣物擋住狹窄的檢查孔。 第一筆量測得到解釋後，房間看起來不再那麼可怕。 伊曼妮真誠承諾會保全證據。 諾拉看過下一座層架，才準備下更大的結論。

Nora climbed onto a low stool to read the sign. Imani insisted that every family keep the right to tell its own story. The old donor file mentioned a former tobacco farm. Nora wanted the next reviewer to follow the same steps. Ben's twin brother had repaired the lamps above the blue door. The team did not treat an unusual sound as proof of a ghost. A sharp whistle sounded when the cleaning cycle began. The hall smelled of wet leaves, but the samples remained sealed.

諾拉踩上矮凳讀取標示。 伊曼妮堅持每個家戶都保有敘述自身故事的權利。 舊捐贈檔案提到一座過去種植菸草的農場。 諾拉希望下一位審查者能重做同樣步驟。 班恩的雙胞胎兄弟曾修理藍門上方的燈。 團隊沒有把異常聲音當成鬼魂存在的證據。 清潔循環開始時響起一聲尖銳哨音。 大廳聞得到濕葉味，但樣本仍保持封存。

At midnight the bell struck twice although no one touched it. A blue door sealed itself. Nora did not force the handle. She photographed both moving gauge needles, then marked the first safe place on her paper map. The sound was frightening, but she had already gained one reliable fact: the lock and the bell changed at exactly the same time.

午夜時銅鐘響了兩下，沒有人碰它，藍門卻自動鎖住。諾拉沒有硬拉門把；她拍下兩只仍在移動的儀表指針，並在紙本地圖標出第一處安全位置。聲音令人害怕，但她已掌握一項可靠事實：門鎖與鐘聲在同一時間發生變化。
