Nora and Imani finished their first complete comparison of paper labels, electronic records, and actual seed jars. Only one group of jars had been assigned to the wrong owner, but it contained the oldest local rice seeds. If the sale proceeded on the false list, the cooperative could lose access to its own collection.

諾拉與伊曼妮完成第一次完整比對：紙標籤、電子紀錄、實際種子罐。只有一組種子罐的所有人被標錯，卻是最古老的地方稻種。如果照錯誤清冊出售，合作社可能失去取用自家收藏的權利。

Ben held the evidence box against his chest. The old building had answers in its records, but not all were easy to read. Nora checked the narrow margin of the paper under a lamp, then compared it with the copy. An ordinary error would have one history; a deliberate change would leave another. She did not know which explanation was true yet.

一名退休運動員自願協助安全搬動沉重櫃子。 諾拉把觀察寫進筆記本，而不是只靠記憶。 合作社詢問誰將從出售中獲益。 諾拉把觀察寫進筆記本，而不是只靠記憶。 諾拉知道挑戰雷恩可能影響自己的職涯。 伊曼妮要求在公開影本上遮住私人姓名。 班恩把證據箱抱在胸前。 老建築的紀錄裡藏著答案，卻不是每一筆都容易讀懂。 諾拉在燈下檢查紙張窄窄的邊緣，再與影本核對。普通錯誤會有一種形成過程，故意修改則會留下另一種痕跡。她還不知道哪一種解釋才是真的。

The different jar numbers gave Nora her strongest clue. The garden had been built to preserve life, and the workers wanted that purpose back. They refused to drag the fragile shelves across the floor. Ben checked that the route behind them remained open. Imani was eager to compare each label with the agreement. The room seemed less frightening once the first measurement was understood. Someone had tried to erase the cooperative's name. Nora looked at the next shelf before drawing a larger conclusion. The garden held living material, not just numbers in a table. If a decision could damage the last sample, it needed more than a hurried signature. Imani asked the workers to record who had been consulted and who had not.

不同的罐子編號為諾拉提供最重要的線索。 溫室原為保存生命而建，員工希望找回這個目的。 他們拒絕把脆弱層架拖過地板。 班恩確認身後的通道仍暢通。 伊曼妮急著將每張標籤與協議核對。 第一筆量測得到解釋後，房間看起來不再那麼可怕。 有人試圖抹去合作社的名稱。 諾拉看過下一座層架，才準備下更大的結論。 園區保存的是活的材料，不只是表格裡的數字。如果一個決定可能傷害最後一份樣本，就不能只靠倉促簽名。伊曼妮請員工記錄曾諮詢誰、又遺漏誰。

A sack of flour sat in the festival kitchen, far from the seeds. They ended the shift with a clear list of what must be examined next. An old photograph showed the harbor where the seeds first arrived. Nora wanted the next reviewer to follow the same steps. Nora called the loss of the last seed samples a horrible risk. The team did not treat an unusual sound as proof of a ghost. A child's drawing of a knight hung above the donation desk. The hall smelled of wet leaves, but the samples remained sealed.

一袋麵粉放在遠離種子的節慶廚房。 他們以一張清楚的下一步待查清單結束輪班。 一張舊照片拍到種子最初抵達的港口。 諾拉希望下一位審查者能重做同樣步驟。 諾拉說失去最後一批種子樣本是可怕風險。 團隊沒有把異常聲音當成鬼魂存在的證據。 捐贈桌上方掛著一幅孩子畫的騎士。 大廳聞得到濕葉味，但樣本仍保持封存。

Only the safety officer could release the cold-room lock. Imani compared the date against a second independent source. A medal from the first harvest fair lay in the cabinet. A sign with the wrong date could mislead someone years later. The old pavement outside the vault was cracked by tree roots. For that reason, they marked the uncertain part instead of hiding it. The crate had rough edges that could tear a protective glove. Imani listened to a worker who had not spoken in the earlier meeting.

只有安全員能解除低溫室的門鎖。 伊曼妮將日期與第二個獨立來源核對。 第一屆採收市集的獎牌放在櫃子裡。 日期錯誤的標誌可能在多年後誤導別人。 種子庫外的舊人行道被樹根頂裂。 因此他們標示不確定的部分，而不是把它藏起來。 木箱邊緣粗糙，可能撕破防護手套。 伊曼妮傾聽一位先前會議未發言的員工。

Nora demanded scientific tests rather than a confident guess. A visitor who saw the same evidence should be able to reach a similar conclusion. A seed trader had offered Renn an unusually high price. The small details did not answer every question, but they narrowed the search. A letter mentioned a tribe that had shared farming knowledge. They returned the tools to their places so the next shift could work safely. The inventory listed the volume of each storage container. They photographed the work area before putting anything back.

諾拉要求科學測試，而非自信的猜測。 看見相同證據的訪客應能得出相近結論。 一名種子商曾向雷恩提出異常高的價格。 這些小細節沒有回答所有問題，卻縮小了調查範圍。 一封信提到曾分享農耕知識的部落。 他們把工具放回原位，讓下一班能安全工作。 清冊列出每個儲藏容器的容量。 把物品放回前，他們先拍下工作區。

They sealed the five shelves with numbered tape and invited an outside witness to sign the record. The director disliked the delay but could not deny the discrepancy. Nora wrote a simple result in the work log: the collection was not ready for sale.

她們以編號封條封存五座層架，並邀請外部見證人簽署紀錄。主任不喜歡延誤，卻無法否認差異。諾拉在工作日誌寫下一個簡單結論：這批收藏還不能出售。
