午夜種子庫的白色腳印 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Imani Chen, a local seed historian, recognized the mark on an empty storage tray. It belonged to the River Families Cooperative, not to the garden corporation. Nora compared the tray with a digital inventory and found that three ownership fields had been replaced with the word unknown. The change had a date but no author.

在地種子史研究者陳伊曼妮認出空托盤上的記號。那是河岸家族合作社的標記，不屬於溫室公司。諾拉將托盤與數位清冊核對，發現三欄所有權資料都被改成「不明」。變更有日期，卻沒有作者姓名。

The cooperative could not afford to replace its rare rice seeds. A careful comparison mattered more than a dramatic announcement. Imani tied an apron before handling the old seed trays. Nobody was allowed to change the record without leaving a name. A small bucket caught water beneath the damaged shelf. The result was useful precisely because the procedure could be repeated. The tilted lamp cast a shadow over the empty tray. Ben refused to open a door while the warning light was active. Imani reminded Nora that records mattered to people beyond this room. Some families had saved these seeds through years when food was scarce. Others had trusted the garden with stories they did not want told in public. Both concerns belonged in the report.

合作社無力負擔重新取得稀有稻種的費用。 仔細比較比戲劇性的宣告更重要。 伊曼妮處理舊種子盤前先繫上圍裙。 沒有人能在不留下姓名的情況下更動紀錄。 小水桶接住損壞層架下方的滴水。 這項結果有用，正因程序能夠重複。 傾斜的燈在空托盤上投下陰影。 警示燈亮著時，班恩拒絕開門。 伊曼妮提醒諾拉，這些紀錄關係到房間以外的人。有些家庭曾在糧食短缺時保存種子；另一些則把不願公開的故事託付園區。兩種顧慮都應寫進報告。

The workers left a simple fruit dessert for the late shift. They ended by placing a dated seal over the completed section. A trail of dirt led from the loose wall panel. Nora wrote the observation in a notebook rather than trusting memory. Renn gave a dramatic speech about the garden's future. Imani asked that private names be covered on the public copy. An energetic volunteer offered to carry the storage boxes. The old building had answers in its records, but not all were easy to read. Nora checked the narrow margin of the paper under a lamp, then compared it with the copy. An ordinary error would have one history; a deliberate change would leave another. She did not know which explanation was true yet.

員工為晚班留下一份簡單水果甜點。 最後，他們在完成的部分貼上有日期的封條。 一道泥土痕跡延伸到鬆動的牆板。 諾拉把觀察寫進筆記本，而不是只靠記憶。 雷恩對溫室未來發表一場戲劇性的演說。 伊曼妮要求在公開影本上遮住私人姓名。 一位精力充沛的志工主動搬運儲藏箱。 老建築的紀錄裡藏著答案，卻不是每一筆都容易讀懂。 諾拉在燈下檢查紙張窄窄的邊緣，再與影本核對。普通錯誤會有一種形成過程，故意修改則會留下另一種痕跡。她還不知道哪一種解釋才是真的。

The electronic entry showed an unknown owner for three jars. Before continuing, Nora repeated the rule aloud and checked it against the posted sign. Old ink began to fade beside the cooperative's symbol. Ben checked that the route behind them remained open. The greenhouse roof kept the new plants warm through winter. The room seemed less frightening once the first measurement was understood. An outside inspector was asked to witness the broken seal. Nora looked at the next shelf before drawing a larger conclusion.

電子條目將三個罐子的所有人標為不明。 繼續前，諾拉朗讀規則，並核對張貼的標示。 合作社符號旁的舊墨跡開始褪色。 班恩確認身後的通道仍暢通。 溫室屋頂讓新植株在冬天保持溫暖。 第一筆量測得到解釋後，房間看起來不再那麼可怕。 一名外部檢查員受邀見證破損的封條。 諾拉看過下一座層架，才準備下更大的結論。

A forgotten lollipop wrapper lay behind the visitor desk. Ben kept the passage clear for workers carrying heavy equipment. The ongoing sale made the inventory error especially serious. Nora wanted the next reviewer to follow the same steps. An old donation stamp showed an ox pulling a wooden cart. The team did not treat an unusual sound as proof of a ghost. A penguin drawing decorated a child's seed diary. The hall smelled of wet leaves, but the samples remained sealed.

遊客櫃檯後方有一張被忘記的棒棒糖包裝紙。 班恩讓通道保持暢通，方便員工搬運重型設備。 正在進行的出售程序使清冊錯誤格外嚴重。 諾拉希望下一位審查者能重做同樣步驟。 舊捐贈印章刻著一頭牛拉木車。 團隊沒有把異常聲音當成鬼魂存在的證據。 一本兒童種子日記上畫著企鵝。 大廳聞得到濕葉味，但樣本仍保持封存。

A shallow pit beneath the floor held a broken drainage pipe. The team did not treat an unusual sound as proof of a ghost. Ben removed the plug before opening the electrical box. A sign with the wrong date could mislead someone years later. A senior archivist recognized the original storage code. For that reason, they marked the uncertain part instead of hiding it. Nora refused to call anyone a spy without real evidence. Imani listened to a worker who had not spoken in the earlier meeting.

地板下方的淺坑藏著斷裂的排水管。 團隊沒有把異常聲音當成鬼魂存在的證據。 班恩打開電箱前先拔掉插頭。 日期錯誤的標誌可能在多年後誤導別人。 資深檔案員認出了原始保存代碼。 因此他們標示不確定的部分，而不是把它藏起來。 沒有真實證據，諾拉拒絕稱任何人是間諜。 伊曼妮傾聽一位先前會議未發言的員工。

The team found the paper version behind a loose wall panel. Its entries were clear, but one page had been carefully cut away. Imani asked Nora not to accuse anyone yet. A missing page might prove interference; it did not tell them who took it or why.

團隊在一塊鬆動的牆板後方找到紙本清冊。條目清楚，卻有一頁被仔細裁走。伊曼妮請諾拉暫時不要指控任何人。缺頁可能證明有人介入，卻不能單憑此事知道是誰拿走的、又為了什麼。
