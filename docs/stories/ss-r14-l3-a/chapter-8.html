The Painted Monster

午夜種子庫的白色腳印 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Ben discovered why visitors spoke of a monster walking through the east passage. An old projector painted huge shadows on the mist whenever the fan started. It had been installed for a festival, then forgotten. Nora repeated the test twice and filmed it from different angles.

班恩找出了遊客口中「東廊怪物」的原因。老舊投影機在風扇啟動時，把巨大影子投到霧上。它原本為節慶活動安裝，後來被遺忘。諾拉從不同角度重複測試兩次並錄影。

Ben felt an ache in his shoulder after moving the projector. The garden was quiet enough for the workers to hear the machinery. An empty bookcase stood against the far exhibition wall. Imani reminded them that a collection had people behind every jar. The visitor's complaint described a shadow taller than the door. Ben checked the safety limits before anyone attempted another test. Nora stayed conscious of the difference between fear and evidence. The workers took a short break without abandoning the safety watch. Imani reminded Nora that records mattered to people beyond this room. Some families had saved these seeds through years when food was scarce. Others had trusted the garden with stories they did not want told in public. Both concerns belonged in the report.

班恩搬完投影機後肩膀隱隱作痛。 園區非常安靜，員工可以聽見機械聲。 展覽廳遠牆旁立著一座空書櫃。 伊曼妮提醒他們，每個種子罐背後都有人。 遊客抱怨信描述一個比門還高的影子。 任何人嘗試下一次測試前，班恩先核對安全限制。 諾拉始終意識到恐懼與證據的不同。 員工短暫休息，但沒有中斷安全監控。 伊曼妮提醒諾拉，這些紀錄關係到房間以外的人。有些家庭曾在糧食短缺時保存種子；另一些則把不願公開的故事託付園區。兩種顧慮都應寫進報告。

The gift shop offered a discount on last year's guidebooks. Ben watched the equipment rather than the people arguing beside it. A grasshopper rested on the warm window ledge. They checked the labels under steady light from two different angles. A hasty explanation could make the team ignore real danger. Nora made sure the temporary decision could be corrected later. Nora asked Ben to inform visitors that the route was closed. They checked the condition of the box before signing the form. Nora checked the narrow margin of the paper under a lamp, then compared it with the copy. An ordinary error would have one history; a deliberate change would leave another. She did not know which explanation was true yet.

禮品店為去年的導覽書打折。 班恩注意設備，而非旁邊爭論的人。 一隻蚱蜢停在溫暖窗台。 他們在穩定的光線下從兩個角度檢查標籤。 倉促的解釋可能使團隊忽視真正危險。 諾拉確認臨時決定日後可以更正。 諾拉請班恩通知遊客路線封閉。 簽署表單前，他們先檢查箱子的狀況。 諾拉在燈下檢查紙張窄窄的邊緣，再與影本核對。普通錯誤會有一種形成過程，故意修改則會留下另一種痕跡。她還不知道哪一種解釋才是真的。

The projector had been installed for a leisure festival. The work took longer than planned, but no evidence was discarded to save time. They used the light beam to locate the hidden projector. A careful comparison mattered more than a dramatic announcement. A harmless shadow had become a monster in local stories. No one would benefit from a report that hid its own limits. Imani helped organize the original photographs by date. Nora placed the sealed note beside the related photograph. The garden held living material, not just numbers in a table. If a decision could damage the last sample, it needed more than a hurried signature. Imani asked the workers to record who had been consulted and who had not.

投影機原本是為休閒節慶裝設。 工作比預定久，卻沒有為了省時間而丟棄證據。 他們沿光束找出藏起來的投影機。 仔細比較比戲劇性的宣告更重要。 無害影子在地方故事裡變成怪物。 一份隱瞞自身限制的報告不會真正幫助任何人。 伊曼妮協助依日期整理原始照片。 諾拉把封存筆記放在相關照片旁。 園區保存的是活的材料，不只是表格裡的數字。如果一個決定可能傷害最後一份樣本，就不能只靠倉促簽名。伊曼妮請員工記錄曾諮詢誰、又遺漏誰。

A portrait of the founder hung near the control switch. Imani listened to a worker who had not spoken in the earlier meeting. A faded festival poster covered the old switch diagram. Imani asked whose account had been missing from the discussion. Nora agreed to release the test video after checking privacy. A missing signature was a reason to investigate, not to accuse. A model rocket from a science fair stood in the hall. Imani compared the date against a second independent source.

創辦人肖像掛在控制開關旁。 伊曼妮傾聽一位先前會議未發言的員工。 褪色節慶海報蓋住舊開關圖。 伊曼妮問，討論中遺漏了誰的說法。 諾拉同意檢查隱私後公開測試影片。 缺少簽名是調查的理由，不是直接指控的理由。 大廳立著科展的模型火箭。 伊曼妮將日期與第二個獨立來源核對。

The display included a paper swan folded by a child. A missing signature was a reason to investigate, not to accuse. The projector cable was pulled tight behind the curtain. Ben asked if a photograph could be taken without opening the container. The bright shadow had a unique shape caused by two lenses. Imani insisted that every family keep the right to tell its own story. Nora asked people not to answer rumors with violence. Imani explained why a correct name could matter to an entire family.

展示品包含一隻孩子摺的紙天鵝。 缺少簽名是調查的理由，不是直接指控的理由。 投影機線材在布幕後被拉得很緊。 班恩問能否不打開容器就完成拍照。 明亮影子因兩片鏡片形成獨特形狀。 伊曼妮堅持每個家戶都保有敘述自身故事的權利。 諾拉請大家不要以暴力回應謠言。 伊曼妮說明正確姓名為何對整個家庭都很重要。

The trick explained the shadows but not the altered records. Nora warned the team against turning one successful explanation into an answer for everything. A real fraud and a harmless illusion could exist in the same building.

這個機關解釋了影子，卻解釋不了被更改的清冊。諾拉提醒團隊，不要把一個成功的解釋當成所有問題的答案。真正的造假與無害的幻象可能同時存在於同一棟建築。
