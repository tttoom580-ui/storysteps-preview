The missing page required a creative but careful search. Imani checked that the public explanation left out no important condition. The number of disputed jars did not decrease after recounting. The fearful workers asked for a clear explanation. Nora placed the sealed note beside the related photograph. The garden held living material, not just numbers in a table. If a decision could damage the last sample, it needed more than a hurried signature. Imani asked the workers to record who had been consulted and who had not.

尋找缺頁需要有創意又謹慎的方法。 伊曼妮確認公開說明沒有遺漏重要條件。 重新清點後，有爭議的罐子數量沒有減少。 仔細比較比戲劇性的宣告更重要。 大廳回音讓夜間鐘聲聽來更響。 一份隱瞞自身限制的報告不會真正幫助任何人。 害怕的員工要求清楚說明。 諾拉把封存筆記放在相關照片旁。 園區保存的是活的材料，不只是表格裡的數字。如果一個決定可能傷害最後一份樣本，就不能只靠倉促簽名。伊曼妮請員工記錄曾諮詢誰、又遺漏誰。

The original donation was a historic event for the town. Nora reminded the others that a good question was better than an invented answer. The badge holder recorded a late-night entrance. Imani asked whose account had been missing from the discussion. A small leak had damaged the edge of one receipt. A missing signature was a reason to investigate, not to accuse. Nora stopped an angry mob from entering the archive. Imani compared the date against a second independent source. Ben pointed to the safety drawing and asked the team to imagine arriving there for the first time. A good rule had to help a tired person take the correct action without knowing any of the rumors. Nora added that test to her notes.

原始捐贈是小鎮的重要歷史事件。 諾拉提醒大家，好問題比捏造的答案更有價值。 識別證持有人留下深夜進入紀錄。 伊曼妮問，討論中遺漏了誰的說法。 一處小漏水損壞了收據邊緣。 缺少簽名是調查的理由，不是直接指控的理由。 諾拉阻止憤怒群眾闖入檔案室。 伊曼妮將日期與第二個獨立來源核對。 班恩指著安全圖，請大家想像第一次到這裡的情況。好的規則應幫助疲憊的人採取正確行動，而不需先知道任何傳聞。諾拉把這項檢驗方式寫進筆記。

The edited computer entry looked normal at first. The disagreement mattered, because it could change who controlled the seeds. A postcard of a distant palace was inside the receipt box. Ben asked if a photograph could be taken without opening the container. A single penny had been taped to a donor's old letter. Imani insisted that every family keep the right to tell its own story. Nora asked the board to postpone the sale pending an audit. Imani explained why a correct name could matter to an entire family.

修改過的電腦條目起初看來正常。 這項分歧很重要，因為它可能改變誰有權控制種子。 收據箱內有一張遠方宮殿明信片。 班恩問能否不打開容器就完成拍照。 一枚一分硬幣貼在捐贈者舊信上。 伊曼妮堅持每個家戶都保有敘述自身故事的權利。 諾拉要求董事會在稽核完成前延後出售。 伊曼妮說明正確姓名為何對整個家庭都很重要。

The receiver beside the door had logged a strange call. The room seemed less frightening once the first measurement was understood. An early founder had funded a farming scholarship. Nobody was allowed to change the record without leaving a name. Nobody could prove that the consultant meant to steal the seeds. The result was useful precisely because the procedure could be repeated. Ben closed the evidence bag with a strong zipper. Ben refused to open a door while the warning light was active.

門旁的接收器記錄了一通奇怪電話。 第一筆量測得到解釋後，房間看起來不再那麼可怕。 早期創辦人曾資助農業獎學金。 沒有人能在不留下姓名的情況下更動紀錄。 沒有人能證明顧問有意偷走種子。 這項結果有用，正因程序能夠重複。 班恩以牢固拉鍊拉上證據袋。 警示燈亮著時，班恩拒絕開門。

She requested the entry card record for the archive room. Ben found an unusual card swipe and a maintenance call at the same time. The names were different. Someone had been present without using the usual staff door.

她請班恩調出檔案室門禁刷卡紀錄，發現異常刷卡與維修通話發生在同一時間，卻是不同的人名。有人沒有走正常員工門，仍然進入了檔案室。
