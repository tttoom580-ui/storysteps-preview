贊助商的信

The Sponsor's Letter

午夜種子庫的白色腳印 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A letter arrived from the development sponsor demanding a clean ownership report within forty-eight hours. Renn said the garden needed the money for repairs. Imani replied that repairs could not be paid for by selling seeds that the company did not own. The pressure made every conversation sharper.

開發贊助商來信，要求四十八小時內交出沒有爭議的所有權報告。雷恩說溫室需要這筆錢維修。伊曼妮回答，不能靠出售公司不擁有的種子來支付維修費。壓力讓每段談話都變得尖銳。

Renn claimed the sale would achieve a full repair of the garden. Nora wanted the next reviewer to follow the same steps. A bright fruit cocktail remained untouched at the sponsor's reception. They left the original object where it could be safely examined. Imani agreed that a safe repair was desirable, but not any sale. Nora wrote down both what she knew and what remained untested. Ben joked that the devil was hiding in the agreement's final page. Ben kept the passage clear for workers carrying heavy equipment. They paused for water and checked the time. Nothing in the room would become clearer simply because they were frightened or impatient. Nora set a limit on the next test and asked Ben to confirm where the emergency exit would remain open.

雷恩聲稱出售能達成溫室全面修復。 諾拉希望下一位審查者能重做同樣步驟。 贊助商招待會上的鮮豔水果調飲無人碰觸。 他們把原件留在安全且可受檢視的位置。 伊曼妮同意安全修繕值得追求，卻不代表能任意出售。 諾拉同時記下已知和仍未測試的事情。 班恩開玩笑說魔鬼藏在協議最後一頁。 班恩讓通道保持暢通，方便員工搬運重型設備。 大家停下來喝水、核對時間。恐懼或不耐煩都不會自動讓房間變得清楚。諾拉為下一次測試設定界線，並請班恩確認哪一處緊急出口會保持開放。

Nora was doubtful that a photocopy proved complete ownership. Their next decision depended on evidence that could still be checked. The contractor wanted to dump broken cabinets behind the fence. Imani reminded them that a collection had people behind every jar. The former governor had attended the garden's opening ceremony. Ben checked the safety limits before anyone attempted another test. A nearby inn offered rooms to the visiting auditors. The workers took a short break without abandoning the safety watch. Imani reminded Nora that records mattered to people beyond this room. Some families had saved these seeds through years when food was scarce. Others had trusted the garden with stories they did not want told in public. Both concerns belonged in the report.

諾拉懷疑影本能否證明完整所有權。 下一項決定取決於仍能查證的證據。 承包商想把壞掉的櫃子丟在圍籬後。 伊曼妮提醒他們，每個種子罐背後都有人。 前任州長曾參與溫室開幕典禮。 任何人嘗試下一次測試前，班恩先核對安全限制。 附近旅店提供房間給來訪稽核員。 員工短暫休息，但沒有中斷安全監控。 伊曼妮提醒諾拉，這些紀錄關係到房間以外的人。有些家庭曾在糧食短缺時保存種子；另一些則把不願公開的故事託付園區。兩種顧慮都應寫進報告。

Ben brought a small jar of berry jelly for the long shift. They could now explain one part of the mystery without pretending to know the rest. Sal called the night guard an old pal from farming days. They checked the labels under steady light from two different angles. A pile of repair bills lay on Renn's desk. Nora made sure the temporary decision could be corrected later. The survey map marked an iron pole at the old garden gate. They checked the condition of the box before signing the form. Nora checked the narrow margin of the paper under a lamp, then compared it with the copy. An ordinary error would have one history; a deliberate change would leave another. She did not know which explanation was true yet.

班恩為長班帶了一罐莓果果凍。 他們現在能解釋謎團的一部分，卻不假裝已知道其餘答案。 薩爾稱夜班警衛是耕作年代的老朋友。 他們在穩定的光線下從兩個角度檢查標籤。 雷恩桌上堆著一疊維修帳單。 諾拉確認臨時決定日後可以更正。 測繪圖在舊園門處標記一根鐵柱。 簽署表單前，他們先檢查箱子的狀況。 諾拉在燈下檢查紙張窄窄的邊緣，再與影本核對。普通錯誤會有一種形成過程，故意修改則會留下另一種痕跡。她還不知道哪一種解釋才是真的。

Nora would not rank money above the cooperative's rights. Ben refused to open a door while the warning light was active. An old razor lay in a box of abandoned staff items. A careful comparison mattered more than a dramatic announcement. A reliable signature mattered more than an impressive speech. No one would benefit from a report that hid its own limits. The sponsor owned a new office in the western suburb. Nora placed the sealed note beside the related photograph.

諾拉不願把金錢排在合作社權利之上。 警示燈亮著時，班恩拒絕開門。 員工遺留物品盒裡有一把舊刮鬍刀。 仔細比較比戲劇性的宣告更重要。 可信的簽名比動聽演說更重要。 一份隱瞞自身限制的報告不會真正幫助任何人。 贊助商在西郊有一間新辦公室。 諾拉把封存筆記放在相關照片旁。

Renn argued that the new facilities would be superior to the old. Nora wrote down both what she knew and what remained untested. A muddy trail led toward the abandoned service gate. Imani asked whose account had been missing from the discussion. Imani said unity required a fair vote, not silent agreement. A missing signature was a reason to investigate, not to accuse. An old register noted that two early donors would wed in spring. Imani compared the date against a second independent source.

雷恩主張新設施會比舊設施更優越。 諾拉同時記下已知和仍未測試的事情。 一道泥濘小徑通往廢棄維修門。 伊曼妮問，討論中遺漏了誰的說法。 伊曼妮說團結需要公平投票，而非沉默同意。 缺少簽名是調查的理由，不是直接指控的理由。 舊登記冊記載兩名早期捐贈者將在春天結婚。 伊曼妮將日期與第二個獨立來源核對。

Nora asked for the original donation agreement. Renn produced a photocopy without the last page. The missing page was the one that normally carried the conditions of custody. Nora refused to sign the sale inventory without it.

諾拉要求查看原始捐贈協議。雷恩拿出影本，最後一頁卻不見了。那一頁通常記載保管條件。諾拉拒絕在缺少文件的出售清冊上簽名。
