幽靈的三條規則

Three Ghost Rules

放學後的幽靈社團 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Sora learned ghosts could be seen by only a few people, could move no heavy objects, and could choose when to leave. At first, Eli thought the problem could be solved before the next bell. A closer look showed that someone else's wishes mattered too. Sora listened without interrupting, while Ms. Park watched the group from nearby. They did not have to agree on everything immediately. There was time to separate a real observation from a story someone had only imagined. Eli took out the music archive and asked what should be checked first.

空發現幽靈只有少數人能看見、不能移動重物，也能自己決定何時離開。 起初，艾里以為下次鐘響前就能解決問題。但仔細看過後才發現，別人的願望也很重要。空沒有打斷，靜靜傾聽，朴老師則在附近看著大家。眾人不必立刻同意每件事，還有時間分清真實觀察與想像中的說法。艾里拿出音樂檔案，問大家應先確認什麼。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Eli raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Sora repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The ghosts’ requests remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. Eli read all aloud, then checked the meaning with Sora. Another card carried area and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word basketball gave the group a new example to discuss. Sora put begin on the board without turning it into a test. They found define in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。艾里舉起手，提議輪流說話。空用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。幽靈們的請求仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 艾里朗讀「all」（全部），再和空確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「area」（區域），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「basketball」（籃球）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 空把「begin」（開始）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「define」（定義）。

Eli wrote the three rules where every club member could read them. Once everyone understood the choice, the next step was smaller than it had seemed. Eli spoke first and waited for an answer. Sora watched the other person's face instead of trying to hurry the talk. There was room to say no, to ask for an explanation, or to think for another minute. The group used the music archive as a guide, not as an order that could never be changed. Eli drew a tiny sign beside email so it would be easier to remember. Sora chose eraser as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed few, which Eli copied beside today's notes. Sora asked how to use lamp in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, leave appeared on the next line.

艾里把三條規則寫在社員都能看見的地方。 當所有人都了解選擇後，下一步其實沒有想像中困難。艾里先開口，然後等待回答。空注意對方的表情，沒有催促。大家可以說不、要求解釋，或多想一分鐘。小組把音樂檔案當作指引，而不是絕不能改變的命令。 艾里在「email」（電子郵件）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 空選了「eraser」（橡皮擦），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「few」（少數），艾里把它抄在今日筆記旁。 空問起，如何用「lamp」（燈）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「leave」（離開）。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Eli wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Sora suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the ghosts’ requests easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. Eli read less aloud, then checked the meaning with Sora. Another card carried mail and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word modern gave the group a new example to discuss. Sora put night on the board without turning it into a test. They found orange in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但艾里還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。空建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論幽靈們的請求便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 艾里朗讀「less」（較少），再和空確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「mail」（郵件），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「modern」（現代化的）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 空把「night」（夜晚）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「orange」（橘色）。

The ghost added that he did not want to be treated like a puzzle prize. Before calling the task finished, Eli went back over the facts. One detail had changed, and another still needed an answer. Sora wrote both down rather than hiding the uncertain part. They could be pleased with progress without pretending that every worry had disappeared. By comparing the new result with the first note, they could see what had actually improved. The helping with care still mattered, but now the group knew one more way to protect it. Eli drew a tiny sign beside plant so it would be easier to remember. Sora chose p.m. as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed quarter, which Eli copied beside today's notes. Sora asked how to use traffic in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, will appeared on the next line.

幽靈補充說，自己不想被當成解謎獎品。 在宣布完成前，艾里重新檢查事實。有一個細節改變了，另一個仍有待解答。空把兩者都寫下，沒有隱藏不確定之處。大家可以為進展高興，卻不必假裝所有擔憂都消失。比對新結果與第一張筆記後，他們看出真正改善的部分。謹慎地幫助別人依然重要，現在小組又多學會一種守護它的方法。 艾里在「plant」（盆栽；植物）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 空選了「p.m.」（下午（時間標記）），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「quarter」（一刻鐘），艾里把它抄在今日筆記旁。 空問起，如何用「traffic」（往來人流；交通）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「will」（將會）。

The hallway grew quiet, and Eli finally had time to think. A good friend had not supplied every answer. Instead, Sora had left space for another idea and had stayed when the first idea failed. That was harder to notice than a loud victory, but it was real. Eli made a promise to remember the person as well as the task. The helping with care was important; so were the people who helped shape it. With that thought, the next chapter of their ordinary and extraordinary days felt possible.

走廊逐漸安靜，艾里終於有時間思考。好朋友不是替自己提供每個答案；空反而留下思考空間，也在第一個想法失敗時陪在身旁。這比熱鬧的勝利難以察覺，卻是真實的。艾里答應自己，不只記得任務，也要記得參與的人。謹慎地幫助別人重要，幫忙塑造它的人也同樣重要。帶著這個念頭，他們平凡又不平凡的日子可以繼續寫下去。
