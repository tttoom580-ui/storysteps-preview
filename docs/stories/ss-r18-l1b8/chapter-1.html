三個人看見同一隻幽靈

Three People See the Same Ghost

放學後的幽靈社團 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Eli, Sora, and Jun saw a small ghost sitting on an empty piano bench. Nobody had planned for this moment. The room was full of ordinary sounds, yet Eli felt that something important had changed. Sora waited beside the door and let the first surprise settle. It was tempting to find an easy answer before anyone knew the whole story. Instead, they looked carefully at what was truly in front of them. Eli opened the music archive and made a small note about the day.

艾里、空與俊看見一隻小幽靈坐在空鋼琴椅上。 沒有人預料到這一刻。學校社團教室充滿平常的聲音，艾里卻感覺有件重要的事改變了。空站在門旁，讓最初的驚訝慢慢沉澱。大家很容易在弄清事情之前就急著下結論，但他們決定先看清眼前真正發生的事。艾里翻開音樂檔案，記下今天的小小發現。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Sora thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Eli thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Ms. Park reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. Sora drew a small picture of bug in the margin of the class story page. In the English practice book, camera appeared on the next line. Eli read fact aloud, then checked the meaning with Sora. Another card carried friendly and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word future gave the group a new example to discuss.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。空想到還沒發言的人，艾里則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。朴老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 空在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「bug」（小蟲）的圖。 英文練習本的下一行出現「camera」（相機）。 艾里朗讀「fact」（事實），再和空確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「friendly」（親切的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「future」（未來）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

They asked each other what they saw before telling anyone else. Eli chose the part of the work that could be done carefully. Sora handled a different task and promised to report back before making any large change. A short question from Ms. Park caught an error in their first version. Instead of hiding it, they made a neat correction on the page. That correction did not erase their effort. It showed that the group was learning how to be responsible for its own work. Sora put juice on the board without turning it into a test. They found lie in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Eli drew a tiny sign beside middle so it would be easier to remember. Sora chose north as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed notice, which Eli copied beside today's notes.

三人先互相確認看到的景象，沒有立刻告訴別人。 艾里選擇自己能謹慎完成的工作，空負責另一項任務，並答應在重大更改前先回報。朴老師的一個簡短問題，讓大家發現第一版中的錯誤。他們沒有掩飾，而是在頁面上整齊修正。這項修正沒有抹去先前的努力，反而證明小組正在學習對自己的工作負責。 空把「juice」（果汁）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「lie」（謊言；說謊）。 艾里在「middle」（中間）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 空選了「north」（北方），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「notice」（注意），艾里把它抄在今日筆記旁。

Eli was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Sora answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Ms. Park nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. Sora asked how to use other in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, pet appeared on the next line. Sora drew a small picture of picnic in the margin of the class story page. Another card carried reach and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word red gave the group a new example to discuss.

艾里害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。空回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的朴老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 空問起，如何用「other」（其他）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「pet」（寵物）。 空在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「picnic」（野餐）的圖。 另一張圖卡上有「reach」（達到；到達），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「red」（紅色的）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

The ghost waved and whispered that he had lost his favorite song. Ms. Park asked the group to explain its choice in everyday words. Eli tried once, paused, and then gave a shorter answer. Sora added an important detail that had been left out. The explanation became clear enough for another person to check. That mattered more than making their decision sound grand. The helping with care could grow only if other people understood how it worked. Sora drew a small picture of star in the margin of the class story page. They found then in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Eli drew a tiny sign beside this so it would be easier to remember. Sora chose use as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed woman, which Eli copied beside today's notes.

幽靈揮手，低聲說自己弄丟了最喜歡的歌。 朴老師請小組用平常的話解釋選擇。艾里試了一次，停頓後改用更短的回答。空補充了一個漏掉的重要細節。解釋變得足夠清楚，讓別人也能核對。這比把決定說得偉大更重要。只有其他人理解做法，謹慎地幫助別人才有機會繼續成長。 空在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「star」（明星；星星）的圖。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「then」（然後）。 艾里在「this」（這個）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 空選了「use」（使用），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「woman」（女士），艾里把它抄在今日筆記旁。

Near the end of the day, Eli stayed beside Sora for one more minute. They remembered how strange the morning had felt. A problem that once seemed to fill the whole room had become several smaller questions. Each question could be handled in turn. They did not know what the following day would bring, and that was all right. What they had learned was not a magic answer but a habit: notice a change, ask with care, and let people choose for themselves. Eli closed the music archive and left with a lighter heart.

一天快結束時，艾里在空身邊多待了一分鐘。他們想起早上的奇怪感受。原本像占滿整間房間的難題，現在分成幾個較小的問題，可以逐一處理。他們不知道明天會帶來什麼，但這沒有關係。今天學到的不是神奇答案，而是一種習慣：留意變化、仔細發問，也讓別人自己選擇。艾里闔上音樂檔案，帶著較輕鬆的心情離開。
