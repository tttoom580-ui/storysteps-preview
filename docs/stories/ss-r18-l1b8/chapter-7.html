走廊上的第二隻幽靈

A Second Ghost in the Hall

A new ghost appeared beside the hallway clock with a worried expression. At first, Eli thought the problem could be solved before the next bell. A closer look showed that someone else's wishes mattered too. Sora listened without interrupting, while Ms. Park watched the group from nearby. They did not have to agree on everything immediately. There was time to separate a real observation from a story someone had only imagined. Eli took out the music archive and asked what should be checked first.

另一隻幽靈出現在走廊時鐘旁，神情擔憂。 起初，艾里以為下次鐘響前就能解決問題。但仔細看過後才發現，別人的願望也很重要。空沒有打斷，靜靜傾聽，朴老師則在附近看著大家。眾人不必立刻同意每件事，還有時間分清真實觀察與想像中的說法。艾里拿出音樂檔案，問大家應先確認什麼。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Eli wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Sora suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the ghosts’ requests easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. They found by in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Eli drew a tiny sign beside chocolate so it would be easier to remember. Sora chose class as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed collect, which Eli copied beside today's notes. Sora asked how to use course in a sentence about their day.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但艾里還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。空建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論幽靈們的請求便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「by」（經由；在旁）。 艾里在「chocolate」（巧克力）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 空選了「class」（課堂），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「collect」（收集），艾里把它抄在今日筆記旁。 空問起，如何用「course」（課程）寫一句與今天有關的話。

Sora asked whether she wanted help before starting any search. The plan became real only when someone took the first small action. Eli checked the time, Sora checked the shared materials, and Ms. Park confirmed which steps needed adult approval. The group left a clear record so that a person arriving later could understand the decision. They also agreed on what to do if something went wrong. It was not exciting in the way stories often promised, but it let everyone move forward with confidence. In the English practice book, flower appeared on the next line. Eli read hour aloud, then checked the meaning with Sora. Another card carried housewife and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word large gave the group a new example to discuss. Sora put let on the board without turning it into a test.

空先問她想不想被幫忙，才考慮調查。 有人做出第一個小小行動時，計畫才真正開始。艾里確認時間，空檢查共用材料，朴老師確認哪些步驟需要大人同意。小組留下清楚紀錄，讓稍後到的人也能理解決定。他們還約好若事情出錯該怎麼辦。這不像故事裡常見的刺激冒險，卻讓每個人都更有信心往前走。 英文練習本的下一行出現「flower」（花）。 艾里朗讀「hour」（小時），再和空確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「housewife」（家庭主婦），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「large」（大的）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 空把「let」（讓）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Eli raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Sora repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The ghosts’ requests remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. They found list in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Eli drew a tiny sign beside main so it would be easier to remember. Sora chose note as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed popular, which Eli copied beside today's notes. Sora asked how to use rabbit in a sentence about their day.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。艾里舉起手，提議輪流說話。空用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。幽靈們的請求仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「list」（清單）。 艾里在「main」（主要的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 空選了「note」（筆記；便條），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「popular」（受歡迎的），艾里把它抄在今日筆記旁。 空問起，如何用「rabbit」（兔子）寫一句與今天有關的話。

She asked the club to find a missing thank-you card. They gathered the papers and put the shared things back in their places. Eli could feel that the room had changed even though the desks had not moved. Sora pointed to the page where the group had written its first worry. The words were still there, but they no longer told the whole story. Now there was also an action, a result, and a reason to look forward to the next meeting. In the English practice book, real appeared on the next line. Eli read start aloud, then checked the meaning with Sora. Another card carried there and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word until gave the group a new example to discuss. Sora put west on the board without turning it into a test.

她希望社團幫忙尋找一張不見的感謝卡。 他們整理紙張，把共用物品放回原處。雖然桌子沒有移動，艾里卻覺得房間已經不同。空指出小組寫下第一個擔憂的那一頁。那些字還在，卻已無法概括整件事。現在多了行動、結果，以及期待下次見面的理由。 英文練習本的下一行出現「real」（真實的）。 艾里朗讀「start」（開始），再和空確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「there」（那裡），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「until」（直到）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 空把「west」（西方；西門）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

The hallway grew quiet, and Eli finally had time to think. A good friend had not supplied every answer. Instead, Sora had left space for another idea and had stayed when the first idea failed. That was harder to notice than a loud victory, but it was real. Eli made a promise to remember the person as well as the task. The helping with care was important; so were the people who helped shape it. With that thought, the next chapter of their ordinary and extraordinary days felt possible.

走廊逐漸安靜，艾里終於有時間思考。好朋友不是替自己提供每個答案；空反而留下思考空間，也在第一個想法失敗時陪在身旁。這比熱鬧的勝利難以察覺，卻是真實的。艾里答應自己，不只記得任務，也要記得參與的人。謹慎地幫助別人重要，幫忙塑造它的人也同樣重要。帶著這個念頭，他們平凡又不平凡的日子可以繼續寫下去。
