抽屜裡的旋律

A Melody in the Drawer

An old drawer held a music sheet with several notes crossed out. Nobody had planned for this moment. The room was full of ordinary sounds, yet Eli felt that something important had changed. Sora waited beside the door and let the first surprise settle. It was tempting to find an easy answer before anyone knew the whole story. Instead, they looked carefully at what was truly in front of them. Eli opened the music archive and made a small note about the day.

舊抽屜裡有張樂譜，幾個音符被劃掉。 沒有人預料到這一刻。學校社團教室充滿平常的聲音，艾里卻感覺有件重要的事改變了。空站在門旁，讓最初的驚訝慢慢沉澱。大家很容易在弄清事情之前就急著下結論，但他們決定先看清眼前真正發生的事。艾里翻開音樂檔案，記下今天的小小發現。

Eli was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Sora answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Ms. Park nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. The word bank gave the group a new example to discuss. Sora put farm on the board without turning it into a test. They found feed in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Eli drew a tiny sign beside letter so it would be easier to remember. Sora chose many as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

艾里害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。空回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的朴老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 「bank」（銀行）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 空把「farm」（農場）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「feed」（餵食）。 艾里在「letter」（信）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 空選了「many」（許多），準備耐心向初學者說明。

Sora asked the school if the sheet could be studied without damaging it. There was a quiet kind of courage in trying again. Eli noticed that the last attempt had taught them something, even without a perfect result. Sora offered practical help and asked which part would be most useful. They worked together for a short time and then stopped to check whether they were still following the rule. Ms. Park praised the careful check more than the speed of the work. A small study card showed moon, which Eli copied beside today's notes. Sora asked how to use movie in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, oil appeared on the next line. Eli read sell aloud, then checked the meaning with Sora. Another card carried slow and a simple drawing by a classmate.

空詢問校方，能否在不損傷樂譜的情況下研究。 願意再試一次，是一種安靜的勇氣。艾里發現，就算上次結果不完美，大家仍學到了一些事。空提供實際協助，先問哪一部分最有用。他們一起工作一小段時間，再停下來確認是否仍遵守規則。朴老師稱讚的不是速度，而是這份細心。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「moon」（月亮），艾里把它抄在今日筆記旁。 空問起，如何用「movie」（電影）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「oil」（油）。 艾里朗讀「sell」（販賣），再和空確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「slow」（緩慢地），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Sora thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Eli thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Ms. Park reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. The word straight gave the group a new example to discuss. Sora put strange on the board without turning it into a test. They found string in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Eli drew a tiny sign beside teenager so it would be easier to remember. Sora chose think as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。空想到還沒發言的人，艾里則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。朴老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 「straight」（直接地）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 空把「strange」（奇怪）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「string」（線）。 艾里在「teenager」（青少年）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 空選了「think」（思考），準備耐心向初學者說明。

The ghost hummed a tune that fit the first few notes. The answer made someone smile, but Eli still asked one final question: was everyone comfortable with what came next? Sora listened to the replies, including the quiet ones. They changed a small detail when one person needed something different. Nothing about the change made the story less meaningful. It helped the helping with care belong to more than one person, which was why the work had begun. A small study card showed through, which Eli copied beside today's notes. Sora asked how to use toe in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, topic appeared on the next line. Eli read where aloud, then checked the meaning with Sora. Another card carried yellow and a simple drawing by a classmate.

幽靈哼出一段旋律，和樂譜開頭幾個音符相合。 答案讓某個人笑了，但艾里仍問最後一個問題：大家都能接受下一步嗎？空聽取回答，包括輕聲說出的意見。有人有不同需求，他們就修改一個小細節。這種修改沒有讓故事失去意義，反而讓謹慎地幫助別人不只屬於一個人；這也正是大家當初開始的原因。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「through」（經過；透過），艾里把它抄在今日筆記旁。 空問起，如何用「toe」（腳趾）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「topic」（主題）。 艾里朗讀「where」（哪裡），再和空確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「yellow」（黃色），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

Near the end of the day, Eli stayed beside Sora for one more minute. They remembered how strange the morning had felt. A problem that once seemed to fill the whole room had become several smaller questions. Each question could be handled in turn. They did not know what the following day would bring, and that was all right. What they had learned was not a magic answer but a habit: notice a change, ask with care, and let people choose for themselves. Eli closed the music archive and left with a lighter heart.

一天快結束時，艾里在空身邊多待了一分鐘。他們想起早上的奇怪感受。原本像占滿整間房間的難題，現在分成幾個較小的問題，可以逐一處理。他們不知道明天會帶來什麼，但這沒有關係。今天學到的不是神奇答案，而是一種習慣：留意變化、仔細發問，也讓別人自己選擇。艾里闔上音樂檔案，帶著較輕鬆的心情離開。
