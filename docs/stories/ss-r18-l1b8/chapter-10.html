也幫活人的社團

A Club for the Living Too

放學後的幽靈社團 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A living student asked for a quiet place to practice music after school. The day began with a small question and soon became more interesting than anyone expected. Eli could hear other students talking, but did not hurry to join their guesses. Sora pointed out a detail that had been easy to miss. They stood together in the school club room, where the same familiar objects suddenly seemed to tell a different story. Eli kept the music archive close, not as a perfect answer, but as a place to begin.

一名還活著的學生希望放學後有安靜練琴的地方。 這一天從一個小疑問開始，後來卻比大家預想的更有趣。艾里聽到其他同學交談，沒有急著加入猜測。空指出了一個容易忽略的細節。他們站在學校社團教室裡，熟悉的東西忽然像在說另一段故事。艾里把音樂檔案帶在身邊，那不是完美答案，而是一個開始。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Sora thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Eli thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Ms. Park reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. A small study card showed around, which Eli copied beside today's notes. Sora asked how to use button in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, dark appeared on the next line. Eli read evening aloud, then checked the meaning with Sora. Another card carried fall and a simple drawing by a classmate.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。空想到還沒發言的人，艾里則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。朴老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「around」（周圍），艾里把它抄在今日筆記旁。 空問起，如何用「button」（按鈕）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「dark」（昏暗的）。 艾里朗讀「evening」（傍晚），再和空確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「fall」（落下），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

Jun helped find an approved rehearsal time with the teacher. Eli chose the part of the work that could be done carefully. Sora handled a different task and promised to report back before making any large change. A short question from Ms. Park caught an error in their first version. Instead of hiding it, they made a neat correction on the page. That correction did not erase their effort. It showed that the group was learning how to be responsible for its own work. The word have gave the group a new example to discuss. Sora put interesting on the board without turning it into a test. They found left in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Eli drew a tiny sign beside menu so it would be easier to remember. Sora drew a small picture of potato in the margin of the class story page.

俊和老師一起找出可使用的練習時間。 艾里選擇自己能謹慎完成的工作，空負責另一項任務，並答應在重大更改前先回報。朴老師的一個簡短問題，讓大家發現第一版中的錯誤。他們沒有掩飾，而是在頁面上整齊修正。這項修正沒有抹去先前的努力，反而證明小組正在學習對自己的工作負責。 「have」（有）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 空把「interesting」（有趣的）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「left」（左側）。 艾里在「menu」（菜單）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 空在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「potato」（馬鈴薯）的圖。

Eli was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Sora answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Ms. Park nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. A small study card showed rose, which Eli copied beside today's notes. Sora asked how to use smile in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, sound appeared on the next line. Eli read stay aloud, then checked the meaning with Sora. Another card carried student and a simple drawing by a classmate.

艾里害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。空回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的朴老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「rose」（玫瑰），艾里把它抄在今日筆記旁。 空問起，如何用「smile」（微笑）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「sound」（聲音）。 艾里朗讀「stay」（留下），再和空確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「student」（學生），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

The club realized it could help the living without using ghost secrets. Ms. Park asked the group to explain its choice in everyday words. Eli tried once, paused, and then gave a shorter answer. Sora added an important detail that had been left out. The explanation became clear enough for another person to check. That mattered more than making their decision sound grand. The helping with care could grow only if other people understood how it worked. The word sweet gave the group a new example to discuss. Sora put trouble on the board without turning it into a test. They found usually in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Eli drew a tiny sign beside watch so it would be easier to remember. Sora chose worry as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

社團發現，不靠幽靈祕密也能幫到活人。 朴老師請小組用平常的話解釋選擇。艾里試了一次，停頓後改用更短的回答。空補充了一個漏掉的重要細節。解釋變得足夠清楚，讓別人也能核對。這比把決定說得偉大更重要。只有其他人理解做法，謹慎地幫助別人才有機會繼續成長。 「sweet」（甜蜜的）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 空把「trouble」（困擾）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「usually」（通常）。 艾里在「watch」（觀看）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 空選了「worry」（擔心），準備耐心向初學者說明。

When the last bell sounded, Eli saw that Sora was waiting by the familiar door. The two had not said everything perfectly. They had even disagreed about a detail, yet the disagreement had made their plan clearer. The moment did not need a large speech. A small smile and one honest thank-you were enough. At home, Eli would remember the most surprising part of the day and write it on the next page. Tomorrow the ghosts’ requests might look different again. This time they would know how to begin.

最後一聲鈴響時，艾里看見空在熟悉的門旁等待。兩人不是每句話都說得完美，甚至為某個細節意見不同，但那場分歧讓計畫更清楚。這一刻不需要長篇大論，一個微笑和真誠的道謝就夠了。回家後，艾里會把今天最驚訝的事寫在下一頁。明天幽靈們的請求也許又有不同面貌，但這次他們知道如何開始。
