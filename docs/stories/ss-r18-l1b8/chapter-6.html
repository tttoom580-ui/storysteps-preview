Jun played the written ending, and the ghost shook his head hard. The day began with a small question and soon became more interesting than anyone expected. Eli could hear other students talking, but did not hurry to join their guesses. Sora pointed out a detail that had been easy to miss. They stood together in the school club room, where the same familiar objects suddenly seemed to tell a different story. Eli kept the music archive close, not as a perfect answer, but as a place to begin.

俊彈奏紙上寫的結尾，幽靈卻拼命搖頭。 這一天從一個小疑問開始，後來卻比大家預想的更有趣。艾里聽到其他同學交談，沒有急著加入猜測。空指出了一個容易忽略的細節。他們站在學校社團教室裡，熟悉的東西忽然像在說另一段故事。艾里把音樂檔案帶在身邊，那不是完美答案，而是一個開始。

Eli was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Sora answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Ms. Park nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. Sora put arm on the board without turning it into a test. They found convenient in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Eli drew a tiny sign beside deep so it would be easier to remember. Sora drew a small picture of desk in the margin of the class story page. A small study card showed do, which Eli copied beside today's notes.

艾里害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。空回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的朴老師點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 空把「arm」（手臂）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「convenient」（方便的）。 艾里在「deep」（深處的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 空在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「desk」（桌子）的圖。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「do」（做），艾里把它抄在今日筆記旁。

Eli asked him to explain what the music had once meant. There was a quiet kind of courage in trying again. Eli noticed that the last attempt had taught them something, even without a perfect result. Sora offered practical help and asked which part would be most useful. They worked together for a short time and then stopped to check whether they were still following the rule. Ms. Park praised the careful check more than the speed of the work. Sora asked how to use envelope in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, find appeared on the next line. Eli read fish aloud, then checked the meaning with Sora. Another card carried funny and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word history gave the group a new example to discuss.

艾里問幽靈，這首曲子過去代表什麼。 願意再試一次，是一種安靜的勇氣。艾里發現，就算上次結果不完美，大家仍學到了一些事。空提供實際協助，先問哪一部分最有用。他們一起工作一小段時間，再停下來確認是否仍遵守規則。朴老師稱讚的不是速度，而是這份細心。 空問起，如何用「envelope」（信封）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「find」（找到）。 艾里朗讀「fish」（魚），再和空確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「funny」（有趣），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「history」（歷史）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Sora thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Eli thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Ms. Park reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. Sora put however on the board without turning it into a test. They found may in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Eli drew a tiny sign beside mud so it would be easier to remember. Sora chose news as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed rise, which Eli copied beside today's notes.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。空想到還沒發言的人，艾里則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。朴老師提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 空把「however」（然而）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「may」（可能）。 艾里在「mud」（泥巴）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 空選了「news」（新聞），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「rise」（升起），艾里把它抄在今日筆記旁。

The ghost remembered playing it with a friend during a school festival. The answer made someone smile, but Eli still asked one final question: was everyone comfortable with what came next? Sora listened to the replies, including the quiet ones. They changed a small detail when one person needed something different. Nothing about the change made the story less meaningful. It helped the helping with care belong to more than one person, which was why the work had begun. Sora asked how to use seat in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, shop appeared on the next line. Eli read speak aloud, then checked the meaning with Sora. Another card carried surprised and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word welcome gave the group a new example to discuss.

幽靈想起昔日和朋友在學園祭合奏的時光。 答案讓某個人笑了，但艾里仍問最後一個問題：大家都能接受下一步嗎？空聽取回答，包括輕聲說出的意見。有人有不同需求，他們就修改一個小細節。這種修改沒有讓故事失去意義，反而讓謹慎地幫助別人不只屬於一個人；這也正是大家當初開始的原因。 空問起，如何用「seat」（座位）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「shop」（商店）。 艾里朗讀「speak」（說話），再和空確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「surprised」（驚訝），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「welcome」（歡迎）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

When the last bell sounded, Eli saw that Sora was waiting by the familiar door. The two had not said everything perfectly. They had even disagreed about a detail, yet the disagreement had made their plan clearer. The moment did not need a large speech. A small smile and one honest thank-you were enough. At home, Eli would remember the most surprising part of the day and write it on the next page. Tomorrow the ghosts’ requests might look different again. This time they would know how to begin.

最後一聲鈴響時，艾里看見空在熟悉的門旁等待。兩人不是每句話都說得完美，甚至為某個細節意見不同，但那場分歧讓計畫更清楚。這一刻不需要長篇大論，一個微笑和真誠的道謝就夠了。回家後，艾里會把今天最驚訝的事寫在下一頁。明天幽靈們的請求也許又有不同面貌，但這次他們知道如何開始。
