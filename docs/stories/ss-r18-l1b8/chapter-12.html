Names on the Back

The team photo had handwritten names on the back but one corner was torn. The light through the window looked ordinary, but the news did not. Eli stopped for a moment, feeling both curious and unsure. Sora did not laugh at the question, even when it sounded strange. They knew from earlier mistakes that moving too fast could make a simple problem harder. The school club room offered enough space for a calm talk. On the first page of the music archive, Eli wrote what was known and left a blank space for what remained uncertain.

球隊照片背面寫著姓名，卻有一角撕破了。 窗外的光線和平常一樣，消息卻一點也不平常。艾里停了一會兒，既好奇又不確定。就算問題聽來奇怪，空也沒有取笑。他們從先前的錯誤學到，行動太快反而可能讓小問題變複雜。學校社團教室有足夠空間讓大家平靜討論。艾里在音樂檔案第一頁寫下已知的事，也留下空白給尚未確定的部分。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Eli raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Sora repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The ghosts’ requests remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. In the English practice book, actor appeared on the next line. Eli read afternoon aloud, then checked the meaning with Sora. Another card carried band and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word celebrate gave the group a new example to discuss. Sora put corner on the board without turning it into a test.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。艾里舉起手，提議輪流說話。空用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。幽靈們的請求仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 英文練習本的下一行出現「actor」（演員）。 艾里朗讀「afternoon」（下午），再和空確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「band」（樂團），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「celebrate」（慶祝）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 空把「corner」（轉角）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

Sora asked the school archive for a copy instead of guessing the missing player. Once everyone understood the choice, the next step was smaller than it had seemed. Eli spoke first and waited for an answer. Sora watched the other person's face instead of trying to hurry the talk. There was room to say no, to ask for an explanation, or to think for another minute. The group used the music archive as a guide, not as an order that could never be changed. They found die in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Eli drew a tiny sign beside glasses so it would be easier to remember. Sora chose I as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed key, which Eli copied beside today's notes. Sora asked how to use last in a sentence about their day.

空向學校檔案室尋找副本，沒有亂猜遺漏的人。 當所有人都了解選擇後，下一步其實沒有想像中困難。艾里先開口，然後等待回答。空注意對方的表情，沒有催促。大家可以說不、要求解釋，或多想一分鐘。小組把音樂檔案當作指引，而不是絕不能改變的命令。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「die」（枯死）。 艾里在「glasses」（眼鏡）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 空選了「I」（我），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「key」（鑰匙），艾里把它抄在今日筆記旁。 空問起，如何用「last」（最後的）寫一句與今天有關的話。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Eli wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Sora suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the ghosts’ requests easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. In the English practice book, media appeared on the next line. Eli read more aloud, then checked the meaning with Sora. Another card carried o’clock and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word or gave the group a new example to discuss. Sora put package on the board without turning it into a test.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但艾里還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。空建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論幽靈們的請求便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 英文練習本的下一行出現「media」（媒體）。 艾里朗讀「more」（更多），再和空確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「o’clock」（整點鐘），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「or」（或）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 空把「package」（包裹）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

A copy showed that the missing name belonged to a student manager. Before calling the task finished, Eli went back over the facts. One detail had changed, and another still needed an answer. Sora wrote both down rather than hiding the uncertain part. They could be pleased with progress without pretending that every worry had disappeared. By comparing the new result with the first note, they could see what had actually improved. The helping with care still mattered, but now the group knew one more way to protect it. They found play in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Eli drew a tiny sign beside poor so it would be easier to remember. Sora chose supermarket as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed ticket, which Eli copied beside today's notes. Sora asked how to use tired in a sentence about their day.

副本顯示，少掉的是一位學生經理的名字。 在宣布完成前，艾里重新檢查事實。有一個細節改變了，另一個仍有待解答。空把兩者都寫下，沒有隱藏不確定之處。大家可以為進展高興，卻不必假裝所有擔憂都消失。比對新結果與第一張筆記後，他們看出真正改善的部分。謹慎地幫助別人依然重要，現在小組又多學會一種守護它的方法。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「play」（演奏）。 艾里在「poor」（不足的；昏暗的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 空選了「supermarket」（超市），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「ticket」（門票），艾里把它抄在今日筆記旁。 空問起，如何用「tired」（疲倦的）寫一句與今天有關的話。

Outside, the air was cooler than it had been at lunch. Eli looked back once and saw the school club room in the evening light. It seemed like an ordinary place, but there was more to it now: a remembered question, a mistake corrected, and a choice made together. Sora waved from a few steps away. They still had their separate dreams and small worries, yet they also shared one clear memory. Whatever happened next, they could return to that memory without needing to pretend the day had been perfect.

外面的空氣比午餐時涼了些。艾里回頭望了一眼晚光中的學校社團教室。它看似平凡，現在卻多了值得記住的問題、改正的錯誤，以及共同做出的選擇。空在幾步外揮手。他們仍有各自的夢想和小煩惱，也有一段共同的清晰回憶。無論接下來發生什麼，都不必假裝今天完美，就能想起它的價值。
