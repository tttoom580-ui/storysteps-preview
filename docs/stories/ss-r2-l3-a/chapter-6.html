旅館裡的名字

The Names at the Inn

這座圖書館，禁止替別人作答 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Ada told Mira to visit an inn in the suburb before the next opening. Its owner had once filed a complaint against the governor for approving secret payments to the library. The owner was gone, but her friend still kept the guest book. Each guest who had complained was assigned a number instead of a name. Mira recognized June’s handwriting next to one number and learned that her sister had traveled there on purpose.

艾達叫米拉在下次開館前去一趟郊區旅館。旅館老闆曾控告總督批准支付圖書館的秘密款項。老闆已離開，但她的朋友仍保留住客簿。每位投訴過的客人都被改用數字代替姓名。米拉在其中一個數字旁認出妹妹的字跡，得知小君是刻意前往的。

The woman at the desk would not answer the question registered under June’s name. She did, however, hand Mira an unsigned receipt and invite her to compare it with the official copies. There was no signature where one should have been. At the bottom, someone had marked a trail in small squares, suggesting that missing names had been moved through several offices rather than erased at once.

櫃檯女子不肯替小君回答已登記的問題，不過她拿出一張沒有簽名的收據，讓米拉與官方副本比較。該有簽名之處是空白的。收據底部有人用小方格畫出路線，暗示失去的姓名不是一次被抹除，而是在幾個辦公室之間轉移。

A visitor had struggled to achieve recognition for her town's first public garden. At an opening reception, officials offered a cocktail and called the garden a desirable improvement, then laughed when a child described the abandoned lot as the devil's playground. The visitor became doubtful of their promises after workers were ordered to dump building waste beside the flowers. She wrote to the governor, but the letter came back to the inn where she was staying. The innkeeper served jelly and tea while her closest pal helped her sort the papers into a careful pile. A wooden pole outside carried the city's official rank of approved projects, yet the garden had been left off the list. A barber offered a razor to cut the packing tape around an old box. Inside was a reliable plan for repairing the site at a reasonable cost. The papers described the suburb, marked a trail from the street to the garden, and compared the plan with an expensive design called superior by the council. Residents chose unity rather than another fight; two neighbors who planned to wed even held their ceremony beside the new trees. The file did not end with every demand granted. It ended with a clear record that the people, not the governor, had decided what the garden should become.

一名訪客一直努力讓家鄉第一座公共花園獲得認可。開幕招待會上，官員端出雞尾酒，稱花園是令人嚮往的改善；孩子把荒地叫作魔鬼的遊樂場時，他們卻大笑。工人奉命把建築廢料傾倒在花旁，讓訪客開始懷疑承諾。她寫信給總督，信卻被退回她住的旅館。旅館主人端出果凍和茶，她最好的朋友幫忙把文件堆整齊。外頭木桿貼著城市核准計畫的官方排名，花園竟不在名單上。理髮師借剃刀割開舊箱子的膠帶，裡頭有一份可靠、成本合理的修復計畫。文件描述郊區位置，標出從街道通往花園的小徑，並和議會稱為更優秀的昂貴設計比較。居民選擇團結，而不是再次爭吵；一對打算結婚的鄰居甚至在新樹旁舉行典禮。檔案結尾並非所有要求都被滿足，而是明確記下：花園的未來由居民決定，不由總督決定。

For several minutes, Mira considered leaving the town and searching somewhere no one knew her. The innkeeper's evidence made her understand how far the records could travel. Yet running without a plan might allow Hale to move the remaining files again. Tomas suggested preparing a public request that named a document and asked for its source. They could not compel an honest answer, but they could make the refusal visible. Mira drafted three questions, then removed the one that demanded to know Hale's private thoughts. Motives mattered, she knew, but paper could not reveal them on command. The more useful question was whether he possessed a signed order for the transfers. She registered it under her own name and kept the receipt. The act seemed small compared with the danger facing June. Still, it put a clear boundary around what Mira would accept: not a friendly promise, not a whispered story, but an answer she could check.

有好幾分鐘，米拉想乾脆離開城市，到沒人認識她的地方找妹妹。旅館證據讓她明白紀錄可能轉移得多遠，但毫無計畫地奔逃，或許只會讓黑爾再次搬走其他文件。托瑪斯建議提出公開申請，明確指出文件並詢問來源。他們無法強迫誠實回答，但能讓拒絕變得看得見。米拉草擬三個問題，再刪掉要求知道黑爾內心想法的那個。動機固然重要，紙張卻不會聽命揭示人心。更有用的問題是：他是否持有轉移文件的簽署命令？她用自己名字登記，留好收據。這項行動與小君面臨的危險相比似乎微小，卻畫出了明確界線：她要的不是友善承諾或耳語故事，而是能查證的答案。

The innkeeper asked Mira whether she would return with news. Mira said she could promise to report what the official documents showed, but could not promise when the case would end. The woman accepted that answer and gave her a clean envelope.

旅館主人問米拉會不會帶消息回來。米拉答應回報官方文件顯示的內容，卻無法保證案件何時結束。女子接受了，還給她一個乾淨信封。

Mira returned with more doubt but also a more reliable method: follow documents rather than rumors. Hale was waiting at the library entrance and asked her to sign a statement praising the system. She refused. A good system, she said, should not need a person to promise approval before seeing the evidence. Hale gave her an unreadable look and moved aside.

米拉帶回更多疑問，也找到更可靠的方法：跟著文件走，不跟著流言走。黑爾在入口等她，要求她簽署讚美制度的聲明。她拒絕了。她說，好的制度不該要求人在看過證據前，就先承諾贊同。黑爾的眼神難以讀懂，最後側身讓路。
