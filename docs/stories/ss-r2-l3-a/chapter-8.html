A Portrait With Two Dates

這座圖書館，禁止替別人作答 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

After the flood, Mira developed an ache in her shoulders and a serious complaint about the library’s safety. An old bookcase had shifted away from the wall, exposing a painted portrait. The woman in it wore a blue coat and held a key shaped like an open question mark. Under the painting were two dates. The first marked the library’s founding; the second marked the day Hale took control.

洪水之後，米拉肩膀痠痛，也對圖書館的安全有了嚴重不滿。一座舊書櫃移開，露出牆上的肖像。畫中的女人穿藍色外套，手握問號形狀的鑰匙。畫下有兩個日期：第一個是圖書館成立日，第二個是黑爾接管當天。

The founder, Elin Ward, had written that every citizen owned the right to ask and withdraw questions. The later plaque replaced “citizen” with “manager.” This was a small change in language with enormous consequences. Mira copied both inscriptions and asked Ada to locate the original charter. Ada said the charter existed, but its location had been hidden after a violent dispute about control of the town’s records.

創辦人伊琳・沃德曾寫道，每個市民都擁有提問和撤回問題的權利。後來的牌匾卻把「市民」改成「主管」。文字的小改動帶來巨大後果。米拉抄下兩段文字，請艾達找出原始章程。艾達說章程還在，但城市紀錄控制權曾引發激烈爭執，章程位置因此被隱藏。

A woman had written a complaint after an old bookcase fell and gave her a lasting ache. She remained conscious during the accident and described every step she had taken. A shop offered a discount on a replacement shelf, but the council refused to locate the company that installed the dangerous one. Her son found a grasshopper in the damaged room and made a hasty guess that insects had weakened the wood. A carpenter later proved the screws had been too short. The mother tried to inform the library, where she spent her leisure hours, but the manager called her a monster who wanted to ruin the town's good name. She began to organize neighbors and displayed a portrait of the injured workers beneath a simple poster asking for a fair investigation. The town offered to release a statement, then removed her name from it. At a festival, children built a paper rocket and watched a white swan cross the pond. The woman stood nearby, holding a tight bandage around her wrist. She said each person's account was unique, even when officials wanted a single easy story. Her neighbors rejected violence and chose to meet with the building inspector instead. The resulting repair order was quiet and ordinary, but it prevented the same shelf from hurting another reader.

一名女子寫下投訴：舊書櫃倒下，讓她留下長期痠痛。事故時她始終清醒，詳細描述自己走過的每一步。商店提出新書架折扣，議會卻不肯查出安裝危險書架的公司。兒子在損壞的房間找到蚱蜢，草率猜測昆蟲蛀弱了木頭。木匠後來證明螺絲太短。母親想通知自己閒暇時常去的圖書館，主管卻罵她是想毀掉城鎮名聲的怪物。她開始組織鄰居，在要求公平調查的海報下展示受傷工人的肖像。城鎮答應發布聲明，卻把她的名字刪掉。節慶時，孩子製作紙火箭，觀看白天鵝游過池塘。女子站在附近，手腕繃帶綁得很緊。她說即使官員想要單一而簡單的故事，每個人的經歷仍是獨特的。鄰居拒絕暴力，決定與建築檢查員會面。最後的修復命令安靜而平凡，卻讓同一座書架不會再傷害另一位讀者。

Mira asked Ada why the founder had drawn a key in the shape of a question. Ada replied that she could share what was written in the public history, but the founder's private intentions belonged to the realm of interpretation. The distinction made Mira smile. Even now, Ada resisted the urge to replace a missing fact with a pleasing story. Together they studied the portrait under a portable lamp. Someone had scratched the paint near the bottom edge, exposing a small line of older writing. The line did not reveal a secret password. It recorded the location of the original charter in the building's earliest catalogue. They compared the line with a surviving inventory and found a matching number. Mira wrote down the result and asked for a photograph from another angle. She wanted the future reviewers to see the mark without relying on her description alone. The discovery also showed how an attempt to hide history could leave the route to that history visible.

米拉問艾達，為什麼創辦人把鑰匙畫成問號。艾達說她可以分享公開歷史寫了什麼，但創辦人的私密意圖仍屬解釋範圍。米拉笑了；即使在此刻，艾達也拒絕用好聽故事填補缺少的事實。她們在移動燈下研究肖像，有人在底緣刮掉顏料，露出一行較舊的文字。那不是祕密密碼，而是最早目錄中原始章程的存放位置。她們把文字與保存下來的清單比對，找到對應編號。米拉記下結果，又要求從另一角度拍照。她希望未來審查者不用只相信她的描述，也能看到標記。這項發現還說明，企圖掩蓋歷史的人，有時反而留下找到歷史的路。

As the workers moved the bookcase back, Mira saw a small square of lighter paint behind it. Someone had once hung a notice in that place. She measured the space and wrote it in her notebook for later comparison with the original plans.

工人把書櫃移回時，米拉看見牆後一塊顏色較淺的方形油漆。那裡曾掛著公告。她量了尺寸，記在筆記本，準備日後與原始圖面比較。

For the first time, Mira understood that rescuing June and protecting the city could be the same task. She drew a diagram linking the portrait, the altered plaque, the receipts, and her sister’s warning. The lines did not yet make a complete answer. That was all right. A useful question was not something to rush through merely because the truth frightened someone powerful.

米拉第一次明白，救小君和保護城市也許是同一件事。她畫圖，把肖像、被更改的牌匾、收據和妹妹警告連起來。線條還不能形成完整答案，但沒有關係。真正有用的問題，不該因為真相令有權力的人害怕，就被急著草率解決。
