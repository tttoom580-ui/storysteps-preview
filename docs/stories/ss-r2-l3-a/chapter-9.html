The Brass Seal

這座圖書館，禁止替別人作答 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A retired designer brought Mira a recording of a former public hearing. The recorder had been sealed in porcelain dust and wax, making it clear that no one had opened the case recently. In the recording, Elin Ward explained that the library’s seal could not approve an answer without two independent witnesses. Hale’s modern forms showed only one. Mira asked the designer to identify the date and the original owner of the recording.

一名退休設計師帶給米拉一段早年公開聽證的錄音。錄音機外盒被瓷粉和蠟封住，顯然近期沒人打開。錄音中伊琳・沃德解釋，圖書館的印章不能在缺少兩名獨立見證人的情況下核准答案。黑爾的現代表格只留一名見證人。米拉請設計師確認錄音日期與原持有人。

Hale claimed that the old rule had been too costly and that technical improvements made a second witness unnecessary. Mira was tempted to call him a liar in front of the entire hall. Instead, she requested the official order that changed the rule. The clerk produced a blank page. A change that affected thousands of residents had apparently left no record.

黑爾聲稱舊制度成本太高，技術改進後已無須第二位見證人。米拉很想當眾說他說謊，最後仍選擇要求他出示修改規則的正式命令。職員交出的是一張空白頁。影響數千名市民的變更，竟然沒有留下紀錄。

A brass bell appeared in the ninth record beside a photograph of a crowded buffet. The hall had been expensive to rent, and a young designer had spent weeks preparing the event. Workers used a hammer to hang banners while a cook declared that the kitchen felt hotter than hell. Her humorous complaint made the others laugh even though the floor was icy near a broken cooler. The children's play included a kangaroo costume, but thick mist outside kept guests from seeing the stage through the windows. A fine porcelain cup was lost, and the manager accused a cleaner without evidence. A guest had brought a recorder, which captured the cleaner offering to scrub a stain and seal a broken box. The cleaner did not skip her shift or leave the stadium, as the official report claimed. She bought a tangerine from a street seller before going home. A technical inspection later showed the cup had cracked when its shelf moved. Nevertheless, the manager tried to threaten the cleaner with dismissal unless she signed a false statement. She refused and asked her union to help. A carpenter came to tighten the loose screws. The recording was imperfect, but its time stamp and the inspection together supported the cleaner's account far better than the manager's accusation.

第九份紀錄裡，一口黃銅鐘出現在擁擠自助餐會的照片旁。租用大廳費用高昂，年輕設計師花了好幾週準備。工人用鎚子掛布條，廚師說廚房熱得像地獄。她幽默的抱怨讓大家笑出來，雖然壞掉的冷藏櫃附近地板冰冷。孩子演出時穿袋鼠戲服，窗外濃霧卻讓賓客看不清舞台。精緻的瓷杯不見了，經理毫無證據地指控清潔員。有賓客帶來錄音機，錄到清潔員主動說要刷洗污漬、封好破箱。她沒有像報告說的那樣翹班或離開體育場，只是在回家前向攤販買了一顆橘子。後來技術檢查指出，杯子是因架子移動而裂開。經理仍試圖威脅清潔員，若不簽署虛假聲明就開除她。她拒絕並向工會求助。木匠來鎖緊鬆脫的螺絲。錄音並不完美，但時間戳記加上檢查結果，比經理的指控更能支持清潔員的說法。

The designer who owned the recording did not want to become famous. She asked that her name remain private until the inspector could verify its history. Mira accepted the condition and wrote it on the receipt. Later, when a reporter asked for the source, she explained the verification process without identifying the woman. Hale accused her of hiding evidence. Mira replied that protecting a witness was not the same as concealing a public rule; the recording could be examined by an independent reviewer. She knew the distinction would be tested. If the audio was edited, her conclusions would have to change. If it was original, the missing second signature would require an explanation from the office. In the meantime, the recording helped her ask a sharper question: who had authorized the change to the forms? She wrote that question in a new notebook section, separate from the questions already registered in June's name. The growing case demanded care with boundaries, even when she was exhausted.

錄音的持有人不想出名。她要求在調查員確認錄音歷史前保密姓名。米拉接受，把條件寫在收據上。後來記者詢問來源，米拉說明查核程序，但沒有指出女子身分。黑爾指控她隱藏證據。米拉回答，保護證人不等於隱瞞公共規則；錄音可交由獨立審查者檢查。她知道這項區分會受到考驗。如果音檔經過剪輯，她的結論就必須改；若是原始錄音，辦公室便要解釋為何少了第二位簽署人。此時錄音已幫她提出更精確的問題：是誰授權變更表格？她把問題寫進筆記本新的區塊，與登記在小君名下的問題分開。即使疲倦，這件日益龐大的案子仍要求她小心守住界線。

Ada reminded her that a recording could preserve a voice without preserving the entire situation. Mira wrote down that warning beside the evidence list. She wanted the committee to hear the audio, but also to know what it could and could not prove.

艾達提醒，錄音可以保存聲音，卻不會保存完整情境。米拉把警告寫在證據清單旁。她希望委員會聽錄音，也知道錄音能證明與不能證明什麼。

An older woman in the audience nodded once, then tucked a note into Mira’s hand. It said that June had spoken at a private hearing and had refused a proxy signature. The woman left before Mira could ask her name. Mira wrote the time and place of the encounter immediately. She had learned that even an anonymous clue deserved a clear record of how it arrived.

觀眾席上一名老婦點了點頭，把紙條塞進米拉手中。紙上寫著小君曾出席私人聽證，並拒絕代理簽名。米拉還來不及問姓名，她就離開了。米拉立刻記下相遇的時間與地點。她已學到，即使是匿名線索，也應清楚記錄取得過程。
