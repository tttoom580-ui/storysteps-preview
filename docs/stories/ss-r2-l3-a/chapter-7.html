淹水的走道

The Flooded Passage

這座圖書館，禁止替別人作答 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The city lost power during a storm. Lightning flashed above the library and water entered the basement through a damaged window. Tomas knew the building’s pipes and showed Mira how to close the main valve. The lower shelves contained unanswered questions from hundreds of residents, each protected by a thin paper cover. They moved the boxes to higher ground while the night bell remained silent.

暴風雨讓城市停電。閃電照亮圖書館，水從損壞的窗戶灌入地下室。托瑪斯熟悉管線，教米拉關閉總閥。他們看到下層書架放著數百名市民尚未得到回答的問題，每份都有薄紙封套。兩人把紙箱搬到高處，而夜鐘一直沒有響。

A committee had inspected the basement only a week earlier, but its report said there was no danger. Mira found the report and took a photograph beside the rising water line. Then a drawer slid open by itself, released by a swollen piece of wood, not by magic. Inside were red receipts with names that had been concealed. June’s was among them. Her question had been given to a proxy without her consent.

一個委員會一週前才檢查地下室，報告卻寫著沒有危險。米拉把報告和上升的水位線一起拍下。接著抽屜自行滑開，不是魔法，而是木頭泡水膨脹造成的。裡面是被藏起姓名的紅色收據，小君的也在其中。她的問題在未經同意下被交給代理人。

The seventh file described an automatic warning system built for the old riverside district. A mechanic arrived in an automobile carrying blankets to clothe people driven from their homes by rain. A committee had been formed to prevent anyone from drowning in a sudden flood. Volunteers cooked dumplings and checked each faucet for clean water. One farmer repaired a fence to keep his flock of sheep from wandering onto the road. Children ate juicy fruit while lightning flashed over the hills. The mayor said the storm had caused widespread mental distress, but offered no real support. In a small kitchen, a microwave had stopped working, leaving an old woman without a warm meal. The official photograph showed a naked tree in an empty square, not the people waiting for help. Under its branches, a bird protected a nest that had survived the storm. Residents asked the council not to pollute the river with damaged fuel tanks and demanded protection for the lower houses. A volunteer used a shovel to move mud away from a door. When the mayor called the district's poverty a sin caused by laziness, a teacher produced photographs showing that the poorest residents had done most of the rescue work. At breakfast, she cracked an egg and placed its bright yolk on a child's plate. The child remembered that kindness long after the official report had faded.

第七份檔案描述河畔舊區的自動警報系統。技工開著汽車抵達，帶毯子替被雨逼離家園的人添衣。一個委員會成立，目的是避免突發洪水使人溺斃。志工煮水餃，檢查每個水龍頭能否供應乾淨水。農夫修好圍籬，免得羊群跑上道路。孩子吃著多汁水果時，山上閃電不斷。市長說暴風雨造成廣泛的心理壓力，卻沒有提供實際援助。小廚房裡微波爐壞了，使老婦人吃不到熱食。官方照片只拍空廣場上一棵光禿禿的樹，沒有拍等待援助的人。枝葉間有鳥守護著經過暴雨仍存活的鳥巢。居民要求議會別讓損壞的燃料槽污染河流，並要求保護低窪住宅。一名志工用鏟子挖走門口的泥。市長把貧窮說成懶惰造成的罪惡，老師卻拿出照片，證明最貧窮的居民做了最多救援工作。早餐時，她敲開一顆蛋，把金黃蛋黃放進孩子的盤子。那份善意在官方報告淡去很久後仍留在孩子記憶裡。

While Tomas repaired the valve, Mira counted the boxes by row. She read the titles slowly so that the numbers on the shelves matched the photographs Ada took. When the lights returned, they found that one section of the basement had been marked as empty although it contained dozens of sealed folders. The error might be a poor inventory, or it might be deliberate. Mira chose not to decide before checking the older lists. A child from the neighboring apartment brought towels, and a shopkeeper offered dry cartons. They accepted the help but explained that the contents could not be opened casually. Some readers had asked painful questions in confidence. Protecting their papers did not mean publishing their lives. By the time the water stopped rising, everyone was tired. The inspector would arrive the next morning. Mira sat on the stairs with her notebook and understood that the library's safety rules and its question rules had the same purpose: a person who trusted a place should not be punished for that trust.

托瑪斯修總閥時，米拉逐排清點紙箱。她慢慢讀出標題，讓架上數字和艾達拍的照片一致。燈恢復後，他們發現地下室有一區明明裝了數十個密封檔案，清單卻標為空。可能只是糟糕的清點，也可能故意為之。米拉決定查過舊清單再下判斷。鄰近公寓的孩子帶來毛巾，店主提供乾紙箱。他們接受幫忙，也解釋不能隨意打開內容：有讀者曾私密地提出痛苦問題。保護紙張不等於公開別人的生活。等水終於停止上升，所有人都累了。調查員明早會到。米拉坐在階梯上看著筆記本，明白圖書館的安全規則和提問規則目的相同：人不應該因信任一個地方就受到懲罰。

Tomas suggested taking the box and leaving. Mira knew that protecting the papers mattered, but taking them secretly could destroy their value as evidence. They photographed the box, sealed it with a strip of tape, and asked Ada to witness the seal. The decision cost them precious minutes. Still, it meant Hale could not later claim that nobody had seen the contents before they moved.

托瑪斯提議直接抱走箱子。米拉知道保存文件重要，但偷偷帶走會破壞它的證據價值。他們拍照、貼上封條，請艾達見證封印。這項決定花了寶貴時間，卻也讓黑爾日後無法聲稱箱子移動前沒人見過內容。
