The Charter in the Basement

這座圖書館，禁止替別人作答 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Ada finally opened the oldest catalogue. Its alphabet was written by several hands, because the first librarians had invited readers to help arrange the collection. The basement held a wooden chest with the original charter inside. The chest was not hidden behind a strange spell. It had been labeled as a damaged collection during a period of staff changes. Mira photographed the label and every page before reading the document.

艾達終於打開最老的目錄。字母索引由好幾個人書寫，因為最早的館員邀請讀者一起整理館藏。地下室有一只木箱，裡面保存原始章程。箱子沒有被神奇咒語藏起來；它只是在人員更替時被標成損壞收藏。米拉先拍下標籤和每頁內容，才開始閱讀。

The charter made the three rules clear. A registered question belonged to the person who asked it. No manager could transfer that right without an independent witness and the reader’s written permission. An unresolved question could not simply be marked as answered. The correction office was meant to preserve disputed records until their owners returned. Hale had not created a new system; he had removed its safeguards.

章程把三條規則寫得很清楚：登記問題屬於提出問題的人；沒有獨立見證人和讀者書面許可，主管不得轉讓其權利；未解決的問題不能任意標為已回答。「更正辦公室」原本應保存爭議紀錄，直到原提問者回來。黑爾不是創造新制度，而是拆掉原制度的保護措施。

The oldest index began with a handwritten alphabet above a narrow basement staircase. A broken cigarette case lay beside a metal clip holding several pages together. Outside, a farmyard cock crowed even though the morning was foggy. The collection had been moved from a kindergarten that could no longer hire enough teachers. Its founder believed that honesty was the first rule of learning and that liberty included the freedom to question an adult. A teacher had brought a meaningful gift: a jar of colored powder that children used to mark different kinds of evidence. In previous years, the school had been accused of teaching queer ideas simply because it invited children to discuss unusual family arrangements. The original letters reveal that parents had asked for respectful explanations, not censorship. Workers used shampoo to wash paint from the brushes and taught pupils to weave paper strips into a shared picture. One child wrote, “Wisdom is knowing when I have not heard the whole story.” Mira paused over that sentence. The school had never claimed children were always right; it had taught them to check what they heard. When its teachers lost their jobs, the library had preserved their questions but allowed the public answers to be rewritten without their consent.

最舊索引在狹窄地下樓梯上方寫著手繪字母表。破掉的香菸盒旁有金屬夾子夾著幾頁紙。外頭天氣起霧，農場公雞仍鳴叫。這批收藏原本來自一所無力聘足教師的幼稚園。創辦人相信誠實是學習的第一條規則，自由也包括向大人提問的自由。老師帶來有意義的禮物：一罐彩色粉末，孩子用它標記不同種類的證據。早些年，學校只因邀請孩子討論不尋常的家庭安排，就被指控傳授怪異觀念。原始信件揭示，家長要求的是尊重的解釋，而不是審查。工人用洗髮精清洗畫筆上的顏料，老師教學生把紙條編織成共同畫作。一個孩子寫著：「智慧就是知道自己還沒聽完整個故事。」米拉在那句話前停住。學校從未宣稱孩子永遠正確，只教他們查核聽見的事情。教師失去工作後，圖書館雖保存他們的問題，卻讓公開答案在未經同意下被改寫。

The charter was written in language older than the forms at the front desk. Ada helped Mira compare the terms without pretending that every phrase meant precisely the same thing today. They noticed that the founders had included a place for readers who could not write. An independent witness could record their words, but the witness was forbidden to replace their choice. That provision had vanished from the modern policy. Mira thought of residents who had trusted a clerk simply because they needed assistance. She asked the inspector to preserve any forms completed for such readers, especially those bearing only a manager's signature. Ada said the original procedure had been slow, and not every librarian had liked it. Yet its slowness gave readers time to correct misunderstandings. Mira copied the provision into a public explanation with a note about where the original could be inspected. She did not announce that the charter solved every problem. It offered a fair starting point, and that mattered enough.

章程使用的文字比櫃檯表格更古老。艾達協助米拉比較用語，沒有假裝每一句在今天都完全同義。她們注意到創辦人替不會寫字的讀者設了程序：獨立見證人可以記錄對方的話，卻禁止代替對方選擇。現代政策刪掉了這條。米拉想起那些只因需要協助就信任職員的居民。她要求調查員保存為這類讀者填寫的所有表格，尤其是只有主管簽名的文件。艾達說原程序很慢，並非每位館員都喜歡；但慢也給讀者時間修正誤解。米拉把條文抄進公開說明，附上正本可供查閱的位置。她沒有宣稱章程解決所有問題；它提供公平起點，這已經很重要。

They left the basement together and locked the chest only after noting the key number and the names of everyone present. It was a simple habit, yet it made the next inspection possible. Mira was beginning to see procedure as an act of care.

兩人離開地下室前才鎖上箱子，並先記下鑰匙編號和在場者姓名。這是簡單習慣，卻使下一次檢查成為可能。米拉開始把程序看成一種照顧。

Mira felt both relief and fear. She had found the standard against which Hale’s actions could be judged, but the papers alone would not open a locked door. She asked Ada to make two certified copies while Tomas listed every official change to the rules. Their next step would be public: they needed readers willing to compare the old charter with the one displayed at the entrance.

米拉同時感到放心與害怕。她找到了衡量黑爾行為的依據，但紙張本身打不開鎖住的門。她請艾達製作兩份核證副本，托瑪斯則整理每一次官方規則修改。下一步必須公開進行：他們需要願意把舊章程與入口告示比較的讀者。
