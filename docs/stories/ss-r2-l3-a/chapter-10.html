不合鎖的鑰匙

The Key That Did Not Fit

這座圖書館，禁止替別人作答 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The tenth night began with a surprising offer. Hale promised Mira access to a scholarship fund if she stopped asking about June. He called it a chance to protect her future. Mira placed the offer beside the copy of June’s red receipt and noticed that both used the same unusual paper. The scholarship had been postponed for years, but the library had quietly kept the money.

第十晚一開始就有人提出意外條件。黑爾答應只要米拉不再追問小君，就讓她使用一筆獎學金。他說這是在保障她的未來。米拉把條件書放在小君的紅色收據旁，發現兩者使用同種罕見紙張。那筆獎學金多年來一直延期發放，資金卻始終被圖書館扣住。

Tomas tried a key from the old stage against the central archive door, but it would not turn. Mira examined the lock rather than forcing it. The key was a perfect copy of an older pattern, while the lock had been replaced recently. They looked for the original lock in the repair records. A handwritten note named the contractor and the day the work was done, giving them a real trail to follow.

托瑪斯拿舊舞台的鑰匙試開中央檔案室，鑰匙卻轉不動。米拉沒有硬扭，而是檢查鎖。鑰匙完全符合舊型號，鎖卻是最近換的。他們查找維修紀錄，發現手寫備註記下了承包商和施工日期，終於有了真正可以追查的線索。

A banker had once funded repairs to the old bay road. A narrow beam of light fell through his office window onto a photograph of a local champion. The athlete's creative training plan had helped decrease injuries, but the town newspaper let a false rumor echo for years. A fearful clerk said a historic building had been sold without approval. The holder of the property record could not explain why the official map failed to show a water leak beneath the road. A loud mob gathered outside the bank, but most residents wanted a normal meeting, not a fight. The council compared the building to a palace and insisted that repairs would cost too much. One child offered a penny from her pocket, and adults were embarrassed that their leaders had continued to postpone the decision. A radio receiver broadcast a discussion of a scholarship intended for young engineers. The banker denied trying to steal the money, yet would not show the ledger. At the meeting a woman opened her bag, pulled down its broken zipper, and produced a dated copy of an earlier payment order. The paper did not solve the whole dispute, but it gave the town a clear place to start checking the numbers. Mira noted the difference between a witness who could name a document and a speaker who merely sounded certain.

一名銀行家曾出資修理海灣舊路。一道狹長光束穿過辦公室窗戶，照在當地冠軍的照片上。運動員有創意的訓練計畫幫助減少傷害，但小鎮報紙多年來仍任由假流言迴盪。害怕的職員說一棟歷史建築未經批准就賣掉。持有產權紀錄的人無法解釋，官方地圖為何沒標出路底漏水。憤怒群眾聚集銀行外，多數居民卻只想正常開會，不想打架。議會把那棟建築比作宮殿，堅稱修復太貴。一個孩子掏出一便士，大人對領袖繼續延後決定感到難堪。無線電接收器播送一筆給年輕工程師的獎學金討論。銀行家否認企圖偷錢，卻不肯出示帳簿。會上，一名女子打開袋子，拉下壞掉的拉鍊，拿出較早付款命令的日期副本。文件不能解決所有爭議，卻讓城鎮有了清楚的數字查核起點。米拉記下：能說明文件來源的證人，與只是聽起來篤定的發言者，是不同的。

The contractor's record identified an address on the other side of the square. Mira walked there with Tomas in daylight. The shop had closed, but an ordinary notice on the door gave the date when its owner had retired. A neighbor remembered the work on the library because it had blocked traffic for two mornings. She showed them a dated photograph of the delivery truck. The truck carried two locks, although only one had been listed on the repair bill. That difference interested Mira more than Hale's promises. The neighbor let her photograph the image and wrote a statement describing where the original was kept. Back at the library, Mira compared the truck's number with the delivery sheet. She found a matching entry marked “correction office.” The chain was growing stronger: a documented lock change, a shipment, and a room where disputed answers were stored. She still could not enter the room, but she could explain why an independent inspector should be allowed to do so.

承包商紀錄指向廣場另一頭的地址。白天米拉和托瑪斯走去，店已關門，門上普通公告標著老闆退休日期。鄰居記得圖書館施工，因為連續兩早阻礙交通。她拿出一張有日期的送貨卡車照片。車上運了兩把鎖，維修帳單卻只列一把。米拉對這差異的興趣遠超過黑爾的承諾。鄰居准許她拍照，還寫下正本保存位置。回到圖書館，米拉把車牌和送貨單比對，找到一項標著「更正辦公室」的相符紀錄。證據鏈更穩固了：有文件的換鎖、送貨，以及存放爭議答案的房間。她仍不能進去，但可以解釋為何應讓獨立調查員進入。

She checked the contractor's photograph twice before leaving the shop. The number on the truck was clear, but the driver's face was not. She refused to name the driver in her report; a blurred image was not a fair basis for accusing a stranger.

她離開店前把承包商照片看了兩遍。卡車號碼清楚，司機面孔卻模糊。她拒絕在報告點名司機；模糊照片不足以公平指控陌生人。

Mira rejected Hale’s offer in writing and kept a dated copy. At the same time, she requested a public accounting of the scholarship fund. She could not prove theft merely by finding matching paper. But she could prove that questions about the money had been ignored. One documented fact at a time, the wall around Hale was becoming easier to measure.

米拉以書面拒絕黑爾的條件，並留下有日期的副本。同時，她要求公開獎學金基金帳目。紙張相同並不能證明竊盜，卻能證明關於資金的問題一直遭到忽視。一項項有文件支持的事實，正讓黑爾周圍的高牆變得可以丈量。
