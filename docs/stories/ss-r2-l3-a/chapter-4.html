The Play Behind the Shelf

這座圖書館，禁止替別人作答 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

An old theater program was folded inside the next ledger. Mira had wanted to become a novelist once, and she could recognize the shape of a story being edited to hide its ending. The program announced a concert that never happened. Its director had been dismissed after asking why the library required private signatures for public questions. Ada suggested that Mira visit the reading room where that director had worked. She added that hints were permitted; answering another person’s registered question was not.

下一本帳簿裡夾著一張舊劇院節目單。米拉曾想成為小說家，她看得出有人正改寫故事，刻意藏起結局。節目單宣布了一場從未舉行的音樂會。導演因質問圖書館為何要市民替公共問題留下私人簽名而遭解職。艾達建議米拉去看看導演工作的閱覽室，並提醒：提供線索可以，替他人回答登記問題不行。

The room held costumes, pictures, and a tiny stage. Tomas explained that the library used to host public readings so people could share evidence. Hale had closed the stage on the claim that public discussion caused confusion. Mira found a script with one scene repeated twice. In the first version a woman asked her own question; in the second a man spoke it for her and called his words a kindness. The second version ended with her name crossed out.

那間房裡有戲服、畫作和小舞台。托瑪斯說，圖書館以前會舉辦公開朗讀，讓大家共享證據。黑爾聲稱公共討論會引起混亂，於是封閉舞台。米拉找到一份劇本，其中同一場戲出現兩次：第一版是女人親自提問；第二版是男人替她說出問題，還稱這是好意。第二版結尾，女人的名字被劃掉了。

The fourth set of memories belonged to a traveling company whose ambition was to perform in every small town. Their stage had once been a barn. The leading actor wore a coat with a high collar and practiced for a concert under a dim lamp. The director tried not to confuse the audience with a sudden change of scene, but the manager made the actors freeze whenever a wealthy guest arrived. A young performer used humor to calm the room, while a junior painter repaired the background. The troupe had also hired a novelist to write a new ending. At dinner the actors shared an onion and a little roast meat, treating even a plain meal as precious. Mira noticed that the official response to their request for fair pay was missing. One worker described having to spray the painted wall before the colors would hold. Another had watched a squirrel hide beneath the steps. A third remembered a gentle tap on the door from a traveler who carried news of unpaid wages. The company leader had a vision of an open theater where every worker could speak. Instead, someone had changed the final page to say the workers were happy. The altered script was still attached to the original, and the two versions exposed the lie without requiring Mira to guess what anyone had intended.

第四組記憶來自一個巡演團，志向是走遍每個小鎮。他們曾在穀倉搭舞台。男主角穿高領外套，在昏暗燈下為音樂會練習。導演怕突然換景使觀眾困惑，但每當富有賓客到訪，經理就要演員立刻停住。年輕演員用幽默安撫眾人，初級畫師則修補背景。劇團還聘請小說家寫新結局。晚餐時大家分食洋蔥和少量烤肉，把簡單餐點也當成珍貴的東西。米拉注意到，他們要求公平薪資的官方回覆不見了。一個工人說，為了讓顏色附著，他得先噴濕牆面。另一人看見松鼠躲在階梯下。第三人記得有旅人帶來欠薪消息，輕敲房門。團長希望打造能讓每名工人發聲的開放劇院，最後卻有人修改劇本，寫成工人全都很快樂。被改過的劇本仍和原稿釘在一起，兩個版本就能揭露謊言，不必由米拉猜測任何人的意圖。

Mira read the altered scene aloud to herself, then read the original. The woman's question had not changed much. What changed was the space around it: in the revised version, other characters answered before she finished speaking. The audience would leave believing that she had agreed with them. A quiet trick, Mira thought, could do more damage than a loud threat. She wondered how often the same trick appeared in hearing records, newspaper reports, and the library's forms. The stage helped her see it because actors had to know exactly who spoke each line. Tomas found a pencil mark beside the changed passage. The mark matched the style of a correction on June's register. That was not enough to identify the writer, but it offered another detail for comparison. Mira kept the pages together so that nobody could claim a single removed sentence represented the full play. At midnight, the tiny stage remained dark, yet she felt as if the hidden audience had finally been given a chance to hear the first version.

米拉先把改過的戲朗讀一遍，再讀原稿。女人的問題本身幾乎沒變，變的是周圍的空間：改寫版裡，其他角色在她說完前就搶著回答。觀眾會以為女人已經同意他們。米拉想，安靜的小手段可能比大聲威脅造成更深傷害。這種手法是否也出現在聽證紀錄、新聞報導與圖書館表格？舞台讓她看出問題，因為演員必須清楚知道每一句由誰說。托瑪斯在改過的段落旁找到鉛筆記號，樣式與小君登記簿上的更正痕跡相似。還不足以確認寫者，但提供另一項可比較細節。米拉把兩版劇本留在一起，免得有人把抽出的單句說成整齣戲。午夜時小舞台仍然黑暗，她卻覺得隱形觀眾終於有機會聽見原版。

Mira did not mistake a play for proof, but its changes gave her an idea. She made copies of both versions and marked their dates. Whoever changed the script might also have altered the official record. At closing time she heard Hale tap on the door. He wanted to know whether she had made progress. “I have better questions,” she replied. His smile vanished for a moment.

米拉沒有把戲劇當成證據，卻從修改之處得到靈感。她複印兩個版本，標上日期。更改劇本的人也可能改過官方紀錄。閉館時黑爾敲門，問她有沒有進展。「我有更好的問題了。」她回答。他的笑容短暫消失。
