On the third evening, the head cataloguer Ada Voss invited Mira to inspect the original register. Ada was old enough to remember the library before Hale became its manager. She refused to answer Mira’s registered question, because the rules did not allow it. Instead, she placed two documents side by side and asked Mira what she noticed. One copy showed June entering at 8:14. Another showed her leaving at 8:13. Both carried the same official stamp. A minute had vanished from the record, but the error was visible.

第三晚，首席目錄員艾達・沃斯請米拉檢查原始登記簿。艾達年紀夠大，記得黑爾成為主管之前的圖書館。她不肯替米拉回答登記的問題，因為規則不允許。她只把兩份文件並排，請米拉自己觀察。一份寫著小君八點十四分進館；另一份寫著她八點十三分離開。兩份都蓋了同一個公章。紀錄少了一分鐘，但錯誤清清楚楚。

Ada then showed Mira the evidence procedure. Every accepted answer had a blue receipt with the reader’s own signature. A red receipt meant the answer had been submitted by a proxy and was under review. Hale had locked the red receipts away. Mira asked whether a reader could recover an answer. Ada pointed back to the second rule: the owner could withdraw any unanswered question before the final bell. “It protects a choice,” she said, “not a fact.” Mira understood that a question might be more important than its answer.

艾達接著展示證據程序。每個被接受的回答都附有藍色收據，上面必須是讀者親筆簽名。紅色收據表示答案由代理人送出，尚待審查。黑爾把紅色收據鎖了起來。米拉問讀者能否收回答案。艾達指向第二條規則：提問者可以在最後一聲鐘響前撤回未回答的問題。「這是在保護選擇，不是在保護某個事實。」她說。米拉明白，問題有時比答案更重要。

The files in the third drawer concerned a school that could not afford new equipment. A cook in an apron carried a bucket across a floor covered with dirt. A teacher had been asked to cast a play about a generous king, but the children wanted a drama about ordinary workers instead. Their dramatic performance drew a crowd, although the official review was unkind. Their dessert after rehearsal was a small piece of fruit, and their energetic voices filled the hall. The official entry called their show a failure. A photograph, however, showed a crowded audience and an excited teacher whose smile did not fade until long after the curtain fell. The school used a greenhouse as a classroom and invited an inspector to examine its cracked windows. Someone had placed a lollipop on the inspector's desk as a joke. An ongoing argument followed: was the gift innocent, or an attempt to influence the report? One boy said an ox had damaged the fence; another held up a toy penguin found near the broken boards. Later, workers discovered a pit beneath the path and used a wooden plug to keep muddy water from filling it. A senior engineer said the damage had begun years earlier. Hale's report had blamed a spy for the accident, though no evidence supported that claim. Mira wrote the accounts in three columns: observation, possible explanation, and unsupported accusation. The distinction gave her a practical way to inspect the library's own reports.

第三個抽屜收的是一所買不起新設備的學校。穿圍裙的廚師提著水桶，走過滿是泥土的地板。老師被要求替一齣慷慨國王的戲選角，孩子卻想演普通勞工的故事。他們戲劇性的演出吸引了群眾，儘管官方評論很刻薄。排練後的甜點只是一小塊水果，但充滿活力的聲音響遍大廳。官方紀錄稱演出失敗，照片卻顯示觀眾席擠滿人，老師興奮的笑容直到謝幕很久後才消退。學校把溫室當教室，請檢查員查看裂開的窗戶。有人開玩笑，在檢查員桌上放一支棒棒糖。於是爭論持續發酵：那是無心的小禮物，還是試圖影響報告？一個男孩說牛弄壞柵欄，另一個男孩舉起在破木板附近找到的玩具企鵝。後來工人在小徑下發現坑洞，用木塞擋住泥水。一名資深工程師說損壞早在多年前就開始。黑爾的報告卻怪罪一名間諜，沒有任何證據支持。米拉把說法分成三欄：觀察、可能解釋、未受支持的指控。這項區分讓她有了檢驗圖書館報告的實用方法。

Ada gave Mira a pencil with a worn eraser and asked her to make two columns. In one, Mira wrote the facts supported by paper: both stamps existed, the times contradicted one another, and June had warned her not to answer Number 041. In the other, she wrote the possible explanations: a mistaken clock, a copied signature, or a person deliberately changing a record. She did not cross out possibilities simply because she disliked them. Ada nodded and said an archive could protect people only if it also protected uncertainty. A comfortable story was not always a true one. After the lesson, Mira walked to the public reading desk and compared the edges of the two stamped sheets. One bore a small cut, as if it had once been attached to another page. She sketched the cut rather than tearing at it. This was the first evening when she felt she could move forward without guessing what June had intended.

艾達給米拉一支橡皮擦磨舊的鉛筆，要她畫兩欄。一欄寫有紙本支持的事實：兩個印章確實存在、時間互相矛盾、小君警告她不要回答041號。另一欄寫可能解釋：時鐘出錯、簽名被複製、有人故意改紀錄。她沒有只因討厭某種可能，就把它劃掉。艾達點頭說，檔案要保護人，也必須保護不確定。讓人安心的故事不一定是真的。下課後，米拉到公共閱覽桌比較兩張蓋印紙的邊緣，其中一張有小切口，像原本附在別頁上。她把缺口畫下，沒有伸手撕扯。這晚，她第一次覺得自己不必猜妹妹的意圖，也能繼續往前。

Under the desk, Mira discovered a paper scrap carrying June’s familiar handwriting: “Do not answer Number 041, even if I ask you to.” That was the first certain proof that June had known danger was coming. Mira kept the scrap flat between two sheets of paper and wrote down where she had found it. Evidence without a clear origin could be dismissed. She would not make that mistake.

米拉在桌子下面找到一張紙片，上面是小君熟悉的字跡：「就算我開口要求，也不要替我回答041號。」這是第一個確定的證據，證明小君早知道危險將至。米拉把紙片夾在兩張紙中，記下發現位置。沒有清楚來源的證物可能被否認；她不能犯這種錯。
