通往陶土山的路

The Road to the Clay Hill

沒有火種的修窯師 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

To test a new tile, they needed fresh clay from a low hill across the valley. Lin was active before sunrise, but she was concerned about the distance. The road followed a flat bicycle track, then climbed beside a narrow stream. Wen carried a flag to indicate where carts should stop. His notebook contained one rule of the road: when a wheel slipped, nobody should push it forward without first finding the cause. A fall had occurred there last year. The valley was rich in stone and clay, but riches were no use if a worker could not return home safely.

為了試作新瓦，他們需要從山谷對面的低丘採來新陶土。林天未亮就開始忙碌，卻擔心距離太遠。道路先沿著平坦的自行車道，再順小溪爬坡。文帶著旗子，指示推車必須停下的位置。他的筆記本寫著一條道路規則：車輪滑動時，未查明原因前不得繼續向前推。去年那裡曾發生跌倒事故。山谷雖富含石材與陶土，工人若無法平安回家，再多資源也無用。

At the checkpoint a clerk checked a single permit and asked the workers to keep silence while he read it. His form still asked each person to state their sex, although the task depended on skill and training rather than that entry. Lin asked him to add a place for safety experience. He agreed to raise the concern at the next office meeting. A traveler in a bright suit said the permit cycle should be shorter for small teams. Another had lost his wallet beside the track. Lin helped return it before the group went on. Their own work was urgent, but that did not excuse ignoring someone else's trouble.

在檢查站，職員檢查唯一一張許可證，並請工人安靜等他閱讀。表格仍要求每人填寫生理性別，雖然工作真正需要的是技術與訓練。林請他增設安全經驗欄位；他答應在下次辦公會議提出。另一名穿亮色套裝的旅客認為小隊應有較短的審批週期。還有人在車道旁遺失錢包。林協助把錢包送還後才繼續前進。自己的工作很急，也不能成為忽視別人困難的理由。

The road guard checked the cart's brakes with Wen and invited the younger workers to do the same. At the steepest place, he showed them a deep mark cut into a roadside stone by a wheel many winters earlier. Lin imagined a cart sliding there with a worker behind it and felt cold in her hands. She asked to split the load between two carts, each light enough to stop. The trip took longer, and nobody received a special prize for caution. Yet every bag arrived without a tear. That evening Lin labeled the samples before she sat down to eat, because the place they came from mattered as much as their color.

道路守衛和文一起檢查推車煞車，也請較年輕的工人跟著操作。在最陡的地方，他指著路邊石頭上的深痕，那是許多個冬天前車輪留下的。林想像推車在那裡滑下、工人站在後方，手掌不禁發冷。她請大家把貨分裝兩車，每車都要輕得足以煞停。行程因此變長，沒有人為小心獲得特別獎品，但每袋材料都完整送到。當晚林先標好樣品才坐下吃飯，因為來源和顏色同樣重要。

The hill offered three kinds of clay. One was smooth but held too much water. One was full of sharp stones. The third could be shaped firmly after a little drying. Wen dug a small sample of each, and Lin wrote down the place and time instead of taking a cartload without proof. They returned before dark. At the gate, Ke had prepared a safe storage room where the samples could dry at the same temperature. He praised the simple choice to take less material. Careful work sometimes gained time by refusing to carry too much. Under town law, every cart needed a working brake. Another fall could occur if the rule was ignored.

山丘上有三種陶土：一種柔滑但含水太多，一種充滿尖石，第三種略乾燥後便能穩定塑形。文每種只挖一點樣品，林記下地點與時間，沒有在缺乏證據時就推走整車材料。他們在天黑前返回。柯已在山門準備一間安全的儲藏室，讓樣品在相同溫度下乾燥。他稱讚少量取材的選擇。謹慎工作有時正是靠不多拿，反而贏得時間。依城鎮法規，每輛推車都必須有有效煞車；若忽視規則，另一次跌落仍可能發生。

Wen set the clay samples on separate boards before they left the hill. Lin matched each small label to the point on their route map. One label had come loose, and she could no longer tell which side of the stream its clay came from. She placed that sample apart from the rest. They had carried it a long way, but the distance could not supply the missing information. On the return journey she left space in the cart for the boards instead of stacking them. At the gate, Qiao asked which sample they preferred. Lin showed her the map first. This time she could explain where each usable piece had begun.

離開陶土山前，文把樣本分放在不同木板上，林逐一把標籤對回路線圖。一張標籤鬆脫了，她已分不清那份土來自溪流哪邊，便把它與其他樣本分開。他們雖然搬了很遠，路程卻補不回遺失的資訊。回程時，她在車上為木板留出空間，不再把它們疊起來。到山門，喬問他們較喜歡哪份樣本。林先拿出地圖。這次，她能說明每份可用陶土從哪裡開始。
