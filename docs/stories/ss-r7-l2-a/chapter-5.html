一把火的代價

The Price of a Fire

沒有火種的修窯師 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

By the fifth day the burden of repair had become clear. A nearby college offered a small grant, but every expense had to be recorded. Wen built a double frame with one feature that made it easy to remove a hot tile without touching it. A fellow student asked whether the design would function under heavy snow. Lin said she seldom trusted a perfect drawing. Production should wait until they had tested one tile in real conditions. A lady from the winter shelter made a selection of blankets and brought a spare sweater for Lin, who had spent the previous night working in the cold.

到第五天，修繕的負擔已十分清楚。附近一所學院願意提供一筆小補助，但每項開銷都必須記錄。文做了一個雙層架，其中一項設計能讓人不用觸碰熱瓦就取下它。一位同學問這設計遇到大雪能否正常運作。林說她很少相信完美的圖紙；大量生產應等到一片瓦通過真實條件測試。冬季收容所的一位女士挑了幾條毛毯，還替通宵在寒風工作的林帶來一件備用毛衣。

Wen made a little space on the bench and laid out the tools in the order they would be used. He had once believed that a tidy bench was only a matter of pride. Now he understood that it also let a worker find a handle quickly when something went wrong. Lin checked the repair budget without making a guess about its purpose. She wanted to know what happened when one part moved and another stayed still. The question was small, but it kept her eyes on the real thing instead of the picture of success in her head.

文在工作檯空出一小塊地方，把工具依使用順序排好。他以前以為整理整齊只是面子問題，現在知道出錯時能迅速找到握柄也很重要。林檢查修繕預算，沒有憑想像猜它的用途。她想知道一個部件移動、另一個保持不動時會發生什麼。問題很小，卻使她專注眼前真實的東西，而非腦海裡成功的畫面。

The town square was busy with a soccer game and a festival. Players showed great spirit, and a child tried to swallow a large bite of food while running. His mother handed him a tissue. Across the square, a cleaner swept old ash from the road. People had begun to upload rumors about the kiln. One message claimed a terrorist group had broken it. Lin knew there was no evidence for that frightening claim. She asked the school paper to print the measured facts: blocked air, damaged clay, and a plan that might work but had not yet passed a hot test. Rumor could not repair a wall.

廣場上同時有足球賽與祭典。球員精神飽滿，一個孩子邊跑邊想吞下大口食物，母親趕緊遞給他一張面紙。廣場另一側，清潔工把路上的舊灰掃走。人們開始上傳關於陶窯的傳言，有訊息聲稱恐怖分子破壞了它。林知道那種可怕說法毫無證據。她請校報刊出已測得的事實：氣道受阻、陶土受損，以及一個可能有效但還未通過熱測試的計畫。流言無法修補牆壁。

A young accountant from the college checked their sums and found a simple mistake: Lin had counted the same rope twice. The amount was small, but Lin turned red with shame. She wanted the list to prove her care, and now it showed an error. The accountant smiled and handed her a fresh pencil. “The point of a record is to let someone find what you missed,” he said. They changed the number without tearing out the page. At the bottom Lin added a new line for unexpected costs. It was the first time she understood that a public budget could protect its writer as well as its readers.

一位來自學院的年輕會計核對總額時，發現一個簡單失誤：林把同一條繩子的費用算了兩次。金額不大，她卻羞紅了臉。她原想藉清單證明自己的仔細，如今它卻顯示錯誤。會計微笑著遞給她一支新鉛筆。「紀錄的目的就是讓別人能找到你漏看的地方，」他說。他們沒有撕掉紙，只把數字改正。林在底部新增意外支出欄，第一次明白公開預算既能保護閱讀的人，也能保護寫下它的人。

That evening Lin gathered the volunteers around a single table. She listed the price of wood, tools, meals, and protective cloth, then asked each person to choose a task that suited their time and skill. No one was told to give more than they could afford. Ke watched quietly from the doorway. He had expected the apprentices to ask for stronger fire magic, yet they asked for a scale and written receipts. When Lin added the figures, the first week's money was enough for three safe tests, not for a whole new kiln. They agreed to spend it slowly and make every failure useful. Before locking the shop, Wen volunteered to sweep the last dust from the floor.

傍晚，林把志工召集到一張桌旁，列出木材、工具、食物與防護布的價格，請每個人依時間和能力選擇工作。沒有人被要求付出超出負擔的費用。柯在門口安靜看著。他原以為學徒會要求更強的火術，沒想到他們想要的是磅秤與收據。林加總後發現，第一週經費只夠做三次安全測試，不夠重建整座陶窯。大家決定謹慎花費，讓每一次失敗也能帶來收穫。關上工坊前，文自願把地上的最後一點灰塵掃乾淨。

At the end of the shift, each worker spoke about one thing that had changed. Lin wrote their statements beside the plan to “publish the spending list.” She kept facts apart from hopes: the first kind could be measured, while the second explained why they bothered to measure at all. The room grew quieter as the list became longer. Even the workers who had disagreed earlier could see that their efforts had a common direction. Before the book was closed, Lin placed a mark beside every question that remained unanswered. Tomorrow's team would know where to begin.

班次結束時，每位工人都說出一項改變。林把發言寫在「公布支出清單」的計畫旁，並把事實與希望分開：前者可以測量，後者說明人為何值得去測量。清單越長，室內越安靜。即使先前意見不同的工人，也看得出大家正朝同一方向努力。合上本子前，林在尚未回答的問題旁都做了記號，讓明天的小隊知道從哪裡開始。
