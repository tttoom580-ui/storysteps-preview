一堂課的代價

The Cost of One Lesson

沒有火種的修窯師 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A beginner named Taro came to ask about the kiln. He had no money for the academy's high charge, and the fire guild had told him to chase another goal. Lin gave him a mug of warm grape tea and asked him to stay for the lesson. She wanted to emphasize the importance of iron tools, which could carry heat and therefore had to be handled with care. On the table lay a little gold-colored pin, the prize from her last school contest. She used it to hold a paper label, showing Taro that even a prize could serve an ordinary purpose. A crowd gathered because free teaching was still unusual in the town.

一位叫太郎的初學者來詢問陶窯。他付不起學院高昂的費用，火術公會要他改追其他目標。林遞給他一杯溫葡萄茶，請他留下聽課。她想強調鐵製工具的重要性：鐵會導熱，因此使用時必須小心。桌上有一枚金色的小別針，是她上次校內比賽的獎品。她用它固定紙標籤，讓太郎看見獎品也能有平凡用途。人群慢慢聚集，因為免費教學在鎮上仍不常見。

Outside, vendors sold jam and fresh bread near a small temple. The old priest spoke about his religion's custom of blessing a new fire, but he did not ask anyone to join it. Lin thanked him for respecting their different beliefs. Then Wen showed the class a simple safety trap: a metal rod placed where a careless person might touch it after heating. Nobody was allowed to shoot sparks at the rod as a joke. “The true success of a lesson,” Lin said, “is not a loud display. It is knowing when to stop.” Taro wrote the sentence down. Social respect, she thought, began with rules that kept every learner safe.

外面的小廟旁，攤販賣著果醬與新鮮麵包。老祭司提起他宗教中為新火祈福的習俗，但沒有要求任何人加入。林感謝他尊重不同信念。接著文示範一個簡單的安全陷阱：一根可能在加熱後被粗心的人碰到的金屬桿。沒有人可以拿它開玩笑，向它射出火花。「一堂課真正的成功，」林說，「不是華麗的展示，而是知道何時停下。」太郎記下這句話。她想，社會上的尊重從保護每個學習者的規則開始。

After class, Taro stayed to help put the metal pieces away. He said his parents had saved for a year to pay the academy, only to learn that he had no flame seed. Lin knew that news too well. She did not tell him his disappointment was silly. Instead, she showed him her first notebook, with its crooked drawings and words crossed out. “I started with more questions than answers,” she said. Taro asked if he could borrow the notebook. Lin offered him a blank one of his own. He would need to make his own mistakes, but he would not have to make them alone.

下課後，太郎留下幫忙收金屬零件。他說父母存了一年的錢準備學院費用，才發現自己沒有火種。林太熟悉這個消息了。她沒有說失望很可笑，而是拿出自己的第一本筆記，裡面畫滿歪斜的圖，也有被劃掉的字。「我一開始的問題遠多於答案，」她說。太郎問能否借那本筆記，林卻送他一本空白的新筆記。他必須犯自己的錯，但不必獨自面對。

Ke watched Taro repeat the cooling procedure without help. He offered the boy a paid training shift on the next day, and asked Lin to check that it did not take time away from school. This small offer was important. It proved an apprentice could learn a practical craft without pretending to have a flame seed. The class ended with a short test: every student had to identify a safe handle and a dangerous one. All passed, but Lin explained that a passed classroom test was only the start of careful work, never permission to forget the rules. Lin said simply that trust was earned through repeated safe work, not a gold prize. As usual, Wen wrote down the question that no one had answered yet.

柯看著太郎不需協助就完成冷卻程序，提議隔天給他一個有薪的訓練班次，也請林確認不會占用他的上課時間。這個小小的邀約很重要，證明沒有火種的人也能學實用工藝。課末每個學生都要分辨安全握柄和危險握柄，大家都通過了。但林解釋，通過教室測驗只是認真工作的起點，絕不是忘記規則的許可。林簡單地說，信任來自一次又一次安全的工作，而不是金色獎品。和平常一樣，文記下大家尚未答出的問題。

Taro waited until the others had left before showing Lin his test tile. He expected her to tell him whether he was good enough to return. Instead, she asked him to describe how he had let it cool. He could explain the first part but had not watched the last. They put the tile beside a new blank card and agreed to repeat that stage with someone present. Lin wrote down the time of the next free lesson. Taro folded the invitation into his coat without hiding it. When he left, his mug was empty, but his place at the next lesson had not been taken away by an uncertain result.

太郎等別人離開，才把試作瓦交給林。他以為她會判斷自己是否有資格再來，林卻請他說明瓦片如何冷卻。他講得出前半段，最後一段卻沒有看。他們把瓦放在空白卡旁，約好下次有人在場時重做這個步驟。林寫下下一堂免費課的時間。太郎把邀請摺進外套，沒有藏起來。離開時，杯子空了，但他下一堂課的位置，沒有因一次不確定的結果而被奪走。
