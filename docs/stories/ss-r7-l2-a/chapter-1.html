The Cold Kiln

沒有火種的修窯師 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Lin stood on the balcony above Red Clay Gate on a windy morning. The field below was white with frost, and an old seesaw stood beside the children's garden. The kiln had gone cold three days earlier. Without new heat tiles, the winter beds would lose their young plants. A male operator from the nation’s leading fire guild said that only a person born with a flame seed could approach the furnace. Lin was nineteen and had no such gift. Still, she held a different belief: a careful hand could sometimes do better work than a bright spell. She did not deny the challenge. She wanted to understand why the fire had stopped before anyone tried to light it again.

一個多風的早晨，林站在紅土門的陽臺上。下方田地覆著白霜，兒童花園旁有一座舊翹翹板。三天前陶窯熄了火；若沒有新的保溫瓦，冬季苗圃裡的幼苗就會凍死。來自國內頂尖火術公會的一名男性操作員說，只有生來具有火種的人才能靠近爐膛。十九歲的林沒有那種天賦，但她相信仔細的雙手有時能比耀眼的法術做得更好。她不否認這是挑戰，卻想先弄清楚火為什麼熄滅，再讓任何人重新點燃它。

At the stone base of the kiln, Lin found a crack beside the air door. She asked Wen, a young woodworker, to discuss what the crack meant. They had studied together when he repaired a model subway in the town museum. The museum's underground tracks were only a secondary exhibit, but its lesson about moving air had stayed with her. “For instance,” Lin said, “air must enter below before hot smoke can leave above.” Wen moved his hand slowly to show the path. Their teacher had called this a tradition of ordinary craft. Lin now wanted to develop it into a safe repair plan, not a secret trick.

在陶窯的石造底座上，林看見進氣門旁有一道裂縫。她請年輕木匠文一起討論裂縫的意義。他們以前在城鎮博物館修過一座地下鐵模型。地下軌道只是次要展品，但空氣流動的那堂課一直留在她心裡。「例如，」林說，「空氣必須從下面進去，熱煙才能從上面排出。」文慢慢揮手示意流動路徑。老師曾稱這是平凡工藝的傳統。現在林想把它發展成安全的修繕計畫，而非祕密的捷徑。

Lin remembered the day the academy tested her hands. The examiner had put a clear stone in her palm and waited for a red light. Nothing appeared. He had crossed her name off his list before she could ask another question. She had carried that silence for years. Now the winter beds were in danger, and the people who depended on them could not eat a bright flame. They needed steady warmth. As Wen waited for her answer, she placed the stone on a shelf beside ordinary nails and cloth. It was not broken; it was simply the wrong tool for the work before her.

林記得學院檢驗她雙手的那天。考官把一顆透明石頭放進她掌心，等待紅光亮起，結果什麼都沒有。他在她來得及再問問題前就把名字從名單劃掉。多年來，她一直背著那份沉默。如今冬季苗圃有危險，依靠苗圃生活的人不能靠明亮火焰填飽肚子；他們需要的是穩定暖意。文等她回答時，她把那顆石頭放到裝著普通鐵釘與布料的架子上。石頭沒壞，只是不適合眼前這項工作。

Foreman Ke asked about the speed of the work and pointed toward the frozen beds. He offered one night to inspect the kiln, provided no one used magic while the door was open. Lin accepted that limit. She wrote the first rule on a board: no fire until the air path was checked. Wen added a second rule about cooling metal before touching it. They left the kiln closed, even when people urged them to act quickly. The first real step was not to make a flame. It was to make a place where a flame could be used without hurting the people who depended on it.

工頭柯問修理的速度，並指向結冰的苗圃。他同意給他們一晚檢查陶窯，條件是在爐門開啟時不得施法。林接受這項限制，在木板上寫下第一條規則：沒有查清進氣路徑前，不得點火。文又加上一條，金屬必須冷卻才能碰觸。即使有人催促，他們也讓陶窯維持關閉。真正的第一步不是生出火焰，而是建立一個不會傷害依靠它生活之人的用火場所。

At the end of the shift, each worker spoke about one thing that had changed. Lin wrote their statements beside the plan to “draw the air path again.” She kept facts apart from hopes: the first kind could be measured, while the second explained why they bothered to measure at all. The room grew quieter as the list became longer. Even the workers who had disagreed earlier could see that their efforts had a common direction. Before the book was closed, Lin placed a mark beside every question that remained unanswered. Tomorrow's team would know where to begin.

班次結束時，每位工人都說出一項改變。林把發言寫在「重新畫出氣流路徑」的計畫旁，並把事實與希望分開：前者可以測量，後者說明人為何值得去測量。清單越長，室內越安靜。即使先前意見不同的工人，也看得出大家正朝同一方向努力。合上本子前，林在尚未回答的問題旁都做了記號，讓明天的小隊知道從哪裡開始。

Wen asked Lin what she would do if no one thanked the team when the repairs were finished. She thought about it while putting the last tool away. “I would still want the children to be warm,” she said. “But I would also want the next worker to know what we learned.” Wen nodded. The answer was different from a wish for fame. It gave a place to the future, when the original team would no longer be standing at the gate. Lin left the light low, checked that the kiln was shut, and went home before the night grew dangerous.

文問林，如果修理完成後沒有人感謝小隊，她會怎麼做。她一邊收起最後的工具一邊思考。「我還是希望孩子們暖和，」她說，「但也希望下一位工人知道我們學到了什麼。」文點頭。這答案不同於追求名聲，而是把未來放進考量：總有一天原來的小隊不會再站在山門前。林調暗燈、確認陶窯關好，便在夜晚變危險前回家。
