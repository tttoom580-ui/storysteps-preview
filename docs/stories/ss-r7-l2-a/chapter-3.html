第一次警報

The First Alarm

沒有火種的修窯師 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Before dawn the kiln gave a sharp alarm. Ke told everyone to stay calm while the young workers moved away from the iron door. A small air cell beside the chimney had filled with what looked like smoke. Lin pressed a wet cloth against its empty frame, but she did not press the main switch. She knew that a false sense of safety could be worse than no action at all. Her duty was to find the source. When the fair stalls opened later that morning, a child in a cowboy hat offered her a classic drawing of the kiln, made during a school tour. It showed a direct line from the lower door to the roof.

天亮前，陶窯忽然響起尖銳的警報。柯請大家保持冷靜，讓年輕工人遠離鐵門。煙囪旁的一個小氣室積滿看似煙霧的東西。林把濕布壓在空框上，卻沒有按下總開關。她知道虛假的安全感有時比不採取行動更糟。她的責任是找到煙的來源。當天上午稍晚，附近市集的攤位開張後，一個戴牛仔帽的小孩拿來學校導覽時畫的古典陶窯圖，圖上有一條從下方進氣門直通屋頂的路線。

The light changed across the yard as the afternoon passed. Lin found that the cause of the smoke alarm could be judged only when she looked from more than one side. Wen offered to hold the measuring line while she stepped back. They did not move anything that might still be hot, and they kept younger visitors outside the marked area. It was tempting to finish the task with one bold movement. Lin chose three small movements instead, each followed by a look at the result. The slower path gave her more chances to notice a mistake while it was still easy to fix.

午後陽光逐漸移過院子。林發現要判斷煙霧警報的原因，必須從不只一個方向觀察。文主動固定測量線，讓她往後退。兩人沒有移動可能仍熱的東西，也讓年幼訪客待在標線外。一個大膽動作就做完似乎很誘人，林卻選擇分三個小動作，每一步都回頭看結果。較慢的方法讓她更有機會在錯誤仍容易修正時發現它。

The town was preparing a trade fair beside the river. A lamb stood near a little boat with a white sail, while a dryer hummed behind a food tent. The fair manager had just had a haircut and worried that the alarm would ruin the whole event. He asked Ke to punish whoever had made the smoke. Lin objected. “We do not know if anyone did something wrong.” A volunteer wanted to strike the rusty pipe with a hammer. Wen stopped him and offered a different plan: one shot of cool air through the small opening, with every worker standing clear. They would watch the cloth, not guess at the result.

鎮上正準備在河邊舉辦交易市集。一頭小羊站在掛白帆的小船旁，食品棚後的烘乾機嗡嗡作響。剛剪過頭髮的市集經理擔心警報毀掉活動，要求柯處罰製造煙霧的人。林提出異議：「我們還不知道是否有人做錯。」一名志工想用鎚子敲打鏽管，文阻止了他，改提議只送進一小股冷空氣，所有人退到安全區，再觀察布條動向，而非憑猜測下結論。

After the alarm, the youngest helper, Bao, would not go near the kiln. Lin found him sitting behind the fair tent with his knees drawn up. She did not say that the danger was gone. Instead, she drew a small box in the dirt and asked him which side the wind had crossed. Bao pointed without speaking. Then he said he had heard the bell before the smoke appeared. That detail helped Lin put the events in order. She thanked him and told him he could help from outside the safety line. His hands stopped shaking when he learned that distance did not make him useless.

警報響過後，年紀最小的幫手寶再也不敢靠近陶窯。林發現他縮著膝蓋坐在市集帳篷後。她沒有說危險已經消失，而是在泥地上畫了一個小方框，問風從哪一側吹過。寶沉默地指出方向，接著說他在煙出現前就聽見鐘響。這個細節幫林排出事件順序。她向他道謝，告訴他可以在安全線外幫忙。當寶知道站得遠不代表沒用時，手終於不再發抖。

The cloth bent toward the wall instead of toward the chimney. That tiny movement gave them their first useful answer. Air was returning through a side gap, carrying old dust into the alarm cell. The kiln was not ready for a fire, but the cause of that morning's alarm was no longer a mystery: the cloud had been dust, not smoke from a fire. Lin wrote the result where everyone could read it. Ke admitted that he had expected a much longer search. He let the apprentices keep the workshop key for another day, on the condition that every later test would be announced before it began.

布條朝牆面彎去，而不是朝煙囪。這個微小動作給了他們第一個有用答案：空氣從側邊裂隙倒流，把積塵帶進警報氣室。陶窯仍不能點火，但今晨警報的原因終於有了答案：那團雲霧是積塵，不是燃燒產生的煙。林把結果寫在每個人都看得見的地方。柯坦承他原以為要查更久，於是讓學徒多保管一天工坊鑰匙，條件是往後每次測試都必須事先公告。

The team had a rule about their notebook: anyone could read the task list, but no one could erase an earlier result because it was embarrassing. Lin wrote “repeat the cold-air test” on the new page and placed the older test beside it for comparison. The marks were not beautiful. A few numbers had been corrected, and some lines leaned to one side. They were still valuable. A drawing that looked perfect might hide the path by which it had been made. A record showing errors could teach the next worker how to avoid them.

小隊的筆記本有個規矩：任何人都能讀工作清單，但不能因結果丟臉就擦掉以前的紀錄。林在新頁寫下「重做冷空氣測試」，把舊試驗放在旁邊對照。記號不漂亮，有些數字曾修正，線條也歪斜，卻仍有價值。完美的圖可能藏起製作過程，留下錯誤的紀錄反而能教下一位工人如何避開它們。

Later, when the others were eating, Lin remained by the window for a few quiet minutes. She could see the nursery roofs below the hill and thought of the families who would sleep there tonight. She wanted to tell them that everything was nearly fixed, yet she knew that a comforting promise was not the same as a safe result. Wen came to call her inside. They shared a small meal and spoke about ordinary things: the cold wind, the school garden, and tomorrow's tools. In the end, the work had to fit inside an ordinary life, or no one could keep doing it for long.

稍晚大家吃飯時，林在窗邊多留了幾分鐘。她看見山下苗圃的屋頂，想到今晚會在那裡睡的人家。她很想告訴他們一切快修好了，卻知道安慰人的承諾不等於安全結果。文過來叫她進屋，兩人分享簡單晚餐，聊冷風、學校花園與明天的工具等平凡事。到頭來，修理工作必須能融入普通生活，不然沒有人能長久做下去。
