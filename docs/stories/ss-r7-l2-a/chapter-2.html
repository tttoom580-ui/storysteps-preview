The Book at the Market

沒有火種的修窯師 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next day Lin carried a board with a drawing of the kiln through the market. A barber studied the angle of the chimney and asked why it pointed toward the school windows. Lin could not answer. Above his shop a dove watched the crowd, and a deer from the nearby woods crossed the empty road. The barber showed her a huge lantern that had once hung over the town's first kiln. Inside its case lay an old edition of a fire textbook. The pages described a particular kind of chimney built before modern spells. Lin began to appreciate the book, not because it was old, but because each instruction had a reason.

第二天，林帶著畫有陶窯構造的木板穿過市集。一位理髮師看著煙囪的角度，問它為何朝向學校窗戶；林答不出來。鴿子停在店上看人群，一頭來自附近樹林的鹿則走過空路。理髮師拿出曾掛在鎮上第一座陶窯上方的巨大提燈，燈箱裡藏著一本舊版火工教科書。書頁記錄了現代法術出現前的一種特殊煙囪。林開始珍惜這本書，不只是因為它古老，而是每一步操作都有理由。

A local organization had once cared for the kiln, but the group no longer seemed to exist. Its former president, Mrs. Dai, lived near the north orchard. She told Lin a painful truth: a minority of families had paid for repairs that never reached their homes. She was sour about the memory, yet she did not blame the apprentices. “Avoid the easy answer,” she said. “A broken tool may have more than one cause.” She placed a dark spot on Lin's drawing, showing where a wall had been built across the old air channel. The new wall was not on the guild's map.

一個地方組織曾管理陶窯，如今似乎已經解散。前會長戴太太住在北方果園旁，她告訴林一個痛苦的事實：少數家庭曾付出修繕費，卻沒有得到相應的服務。她對往事仍有苦澀，卻不責怪學徒。「別急著接受簡單答案，」她說，「壞掉的工具可能有不只一個原因。」她在林的圖上點出一個黑點，指出舊進氣道被新牆堵住的位置；那道牆不在公會的地圖上。

Mrs. Dai opened an old box and took out three requests for repairs. Each had a different address, but all three carried the same date. She could not remember whether the work had been completed. “I was angry then,” she told Lin, “and anger makes some details larger than they were.” Lin respected the warning. She asked permission to copy the papers and promised not to write anyone's name on the public board. On the walk home Wen said the village had trusted important people for so long that ordinary questions sounded rude. Lin said a question could be polite and still be necessary.

戴太太打開舊箱，拿出三張修繕申請。三張地址不同，日期卻一樣。她不記得工作究竟做完沒有。「那時我很生氣，」她告訴林，「憤怒會把某些細節放大。」林尊重這個提醒，徵求同意後才抄寫文件，也答應不把任何姓名寫上公共告示板。回家路上，文說村民太習慣相信重要人物，普通問題聽起來反而像失禮。林說，一個問題可以有禮貌，仍然有提出的必要。

Lin did not treat this information as proof. A mark on a drawing could be mistaken, and a memory might change over many years. She thanked Mrs. Dai and went to the place itself with Wen. They found the wall, but they also saw a small opening beneath it. That opening might still carry air. The truth would need a test. Wen promised to build a wooden frame to hold a safe measuring ribbon. Lin closed the textbook gently and carried it home, determined not to confuse a strong story with a checked fact.

林沒有把這些資訊直接當作證據。圖上的記號可能畫錯，人們的記憶也會隨歲月改變。她向戴太太道謝，和文親自去看。他們找到了那道牆，也發現牆底有個小洞，也許仍能讓空氣通過。真相需要測試。文答應做一個木框，固定安全的測量布帶。林輕輕合上教科書帶回家，決心不把動人的說法誤當成核實過的事實。

Master Qiao, who supervised the apprentices, asked to see the task in writing before giving her approval. Lin showed the line marked “check the wall in person” and explained which tools were needed. Qiao did not sign at once. She asked whether the same test could be repeated by another apprentice who was not close to Lin. That question bothered Lin for a moment. She wanted her own care to be trusted. Then she understood: if the method worked only when she stood nearby, it was not ready to help the whole town. She made the instructions easier to follow and asked Wen to try them without a hint.

負責指導學徒的喬師傅要求先看到書面工作內容才願批准。林拿出標著「親自檢查牆面」的那一行，說明需要哪些工具。喬沒有立刻簽字，而是問一個與林不熟的學徒是否也能重做同樣測試。林一度有些難受，她希望自己的細心被信任。隨後她明白，若方法只有她在旁邊才有效，就還不能幫助全鎮。她把指示改得更容易遵循，請文在不接受提示的情況下自行試做一次。

Lin closed the old textbook with one finger marking the chimney drawing. From the market she could still see the wall Mrs. Dai had described. She asked Wen to walk back that way with her before they returned to the gate. The book showed how the chimney had once been built; the wall might show what had changed. They would need both. At the corner, Lin stopped to sketch the place from the ground rather than copy the page again. The drawing took longer with the wind lifting her paper. When she finished, she had not learned a new spell. She had learned what she needed to look at next.

林合上舊課本，用一根手指夾住煙囪圖那頁。從市集，她還看得到戴太太提到的牆。她請文先陪自己往那邊走，再回山門。書上畫的是煙囪原來怎麼建；現場牆壁則可能顯示後來改了什麼。兩者都需要。到了轉角，林停下，從地面畫現況，不再照抄書頁。風一直掀紙，畫得比預期慢。完成時，她沒有學會新法術，卻知道下一步該親眼查看什麼。
