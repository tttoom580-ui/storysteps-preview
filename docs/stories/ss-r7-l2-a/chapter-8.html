比山門更老的刻痕

Marks Older Than the Gate

沒有火種的修窯師 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

An elder farmer brought Lin an ancient wooden mold. His family had used it for nearly a century, long before the academy gave its fire classes an official identity. He asked whether an adult could learn the same craft after working in the grain fields all day. Lin said yes, although the design required patience and clear instruction. The old man showed a cut on the mold that looked backward to her. Then he turned it over in his lap, and the mark made sense. It was a guide for placing the clay, not a sign of who owned the tool.

一位年長農夫帶來古老的木模。他家使用這個模具近一個世紀，遠在學院正式設立火工課之前。他問成年人白天在穀田工作後，還能否學這項工藝。林說可以，只是模具設計需要耐心和清楚的說明。老人指著模具上一道在林看來方向相反的刻痕，接著把模具在膝上翻過來，刻痕便清楚了。那是放置陶土的導引，不是所有權記號。

At the river, volunteers offered aid to a stranger who had developed a fever. A child tried to swim toward the bank with a bag of peaches, but an aunt called him back before he reached deep water. The old man tore a piece from his spare cloth to cool the visitor's face. It was a typical kindness in the valley. Lin saw the used cloth and understood that the same principle applied to tools: maintain them before they failed, and never ask a weak thing to carry more weight than it could bear. Care was not an answer written only in a book.

河邊，志工幫助一位發燒的陌生人。一個孩子提著一袋桃子想游向對岸，姑姑在他進入深水前叫住他。老人從備用布上撕下一條替訪客敷額頭。這是山谷裡典型的善意。林看著用過的布，理解相同原則也適用工具：要在壞掉前保養，不能要求脆弱的東西承擔超過能力的重量。照顧不是只寫在書裡的答案。

The farmer's granddaughter came to watch the comparison. She said her grandfather had always kept the mold in a wooden box, although the rest of the family considered it a useless old thing. When Lin asked why he had kept it, the girl replied, “Because his mother taught him with it.” The answer changed Lin's attention. The mold was not only an object from the past; it was a memory of someone learning patiently from someone else. She took two clear pictures of the marks and promised the family a copy. Their story would stay with the object even if the new design proved better.

農夫的孫女來看比較試驗。她說祖父一直把模具收在木箱裡，雖然其他家人認為它毫無用處。林問為何仍保留，女孩回答：「因為他的母親曾用它教他。」這句話改變了林的注意方向。模具不只是過去的物件，也是一個人耐心向另一個人學習的記憶。她把刻痕清楚拍了兩張照片，答應送家人一份。即使新設計證明比較好，他們的故事仍會和模具一起留下。

They posed the wooden mold beside their newest clay sample and compared the shapes. Qiao noticed that the old mark kept a little air under the tile while it dried. The modern mold was smooth and easy to use, but its flat bottom could trap water. They would test both instead of declaring a winner by age or fame. Lin asked the farmer to sign the record as the source of the idea. He laughed and said his family had almost thrown the mold away. Now it might help keep another family's winter garden warm. The elder asked Lin not to tear the old instruction page, even if its corner was already weak.

他們把木模擺在最新的陶土樣品旁比較形狀。喬發現舊刻痕能在瓦片乾燥時保留少量底部空氣。新式模具雖然平滑好用，平底卻可能困住水分。他們決定兩種都測，而非因年代或名聲選勝者。林請農夫在紀錄上簽名，確認構想來源。老人笑說家人幾乎把木模丟掉，如今它也許能替另一戶人家的冬季花園留住暖意。長者請林不要撕破舊說明頁，即使那頁的邊角早已脆弱。

The old mold and the newer one stood side by side when the farmer returned. Lin asked him to point out the edge that his family always checked before using theirs. He ran a finger near a small mark but did not touch the test pieces. Wen copied its shape onto the board. They would compare both molds under the same conditions before choosing one. The farmer stayed to watch them prepare. He had arrived asking whether someone his age could still learn; now a younger worker was asking him to explain something. Lin pulled another stool to the table and made room for his answer.

農人回來時，舊模具與新模具並排放著。林請他指出，家人每次使用前一定會檢查哪條邊。他的手指停在一道小記號旁，沒有碰試件。文把形狀抄到板上。他們會在相同條件下比較兩個模具，再決定用哪個。農人留下來看準備工作。他原先來問自己這個年紀是否還能學，現在卻有年輕工人請他說明一件事。林拉來另一張凳子，為他的答案騰出位置。
