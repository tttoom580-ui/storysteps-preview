岸邊的水泵

The Pump by the Shore

沒有火種的修窯師 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The next repair concerned water rather than fire. During the annual badminton match, the pump engine by the shore stopped without warning. The kitchen had enough cabbage for one meal, but no clean water to wash it. A clever neighbor told Lin that the pump was a private affair and none of her business. She disagreed: a kiln could not be tested safely without water nearby. The crisis made the whole street uneasy. A fireman showed the apprentices how to shut the engine down before opening it. He said the most useful part of safety culture was not a heroic spell but the habit of asking for help early.

下一項修繕不是火，而是水。年度羽球賽進行時，岸邊的水泵引擎突然停了。廚房的高麗菜還夠做一餐，卻沒有乾淨的水可以清洗。一位聰明的鄰居說水泵是私人的事，與林無關。林不同意：旁邊沒有備用水，陶窯就無法安全測試。危機讓整條街不安。一名消防員教學徒如何在拆開機器前關閉引擎。他說安全文化最重要的不是英雄法術，而是及早求助的習慣。

Wen opened his journal and drew a line from the pump to the kiln, then another to the shelter. Every individual who wanted to participate had a human reason for needing water: washing hands, drinking tea, or keeping a sick child cool. The local council could govern the public pipe, but it could not order a volunteer to work without rest. Lin brought a pillow and dry socks to the watch station. A museum across the road displayed the history of an old prison, where people had once been denied clean water. The story excited no one; it reminded them how serious the problem was.

文打開日誌，畫出水泵通往陶窯與收容所的兩條線。每個想參與的人都有人性的需要：洗手、喝茶，或替生病的孩子降溫。地方議會可以管理公共管線，卻不能命令志工不休息地工作。林帶了一個枕頭和乾襪子到守望站。馬路對面的博物館展出一座舊監獄的歷史，那裡的人曾被剝奪乾淨的水。這段往事並不令人興奮，反而提醒大家問題有多嚴重。

The fireman taught them to listen to the pump before opening it. A weak, slow knock meant the wheel was meeting something it should not touch. Wen had always been quick with a wrench, but this time he folded his arms and waited. Lin placed her palm on the cold outer case and counted the knocks until the engine stopped. That count became part of the repair record. When the water returned, the kitchen workers cheered from the window. Lin allowed herself a smile, then placed a paper notice beside the pump: temporary repair, cover not yet replaced. Relief was welcome; forgetting the unfinished work was not.

消防員教他們在拆開水泵前先聽聲音。低沉緩慢的敲響，代表轉輪碰到了不該碰的東西。文平常拿起扳手就動作很快，這次卻抱著手臂等待。林把手掌貼在冰冷外殼上，數著敲擊聲直到引擎停止，並把次數寫進紀錄。水重新流出時，廚房工人從窗邊歡呼。林終於笑了，接著在水泵旁貼上通知：「暫時修復，護罩尚未更換。」鬆一口氣可以，但不能忘記尚未完成的工作。

They worked slowly back and forth along the pipe and found a stone lodged under the wheel. Wen removed it only after the power was off. The pump began to turn again, but Lin marked the repair as temporary. A loose guard might let another stone enter. Ke agreed to send a worker for a proper cover. The school kitchen served warm soup by evening, and the apprentices returned to the cold kiln knowing that its next hot test now had one more needed protection in place.

他們沿管線來回檢查，終於在轉輪下找到卡住的石頭。文確定電源關閉後才把石頭移走。水泵再次轉動，但林把這次修理記為「暫時」，因為鬆動的護罩仍可能讓其他石頭掉進去。柯答應派人製作合格的蓋板。傍晚學校廚房重新端出熱湯，學徒回到冷陶窯時，知道下一次熱測試又多了一道必要的保護。

Wen read the pump-cover instructions without looking up for help. At the second step he stopped: the drawing did not show which way the cover should turn. Lin almost pointed, then handed him a pencil instead. He marked the missing edge, and she compared it with the actual part. Water was running again, but the uncovered pump still needed a barrier before ordinary use. They left the warning in place while revising the drawing. Across the yard, the kitchen workers were washing the cabbage. Lin could hear water falling into their bowls. That sound was a useful beginning, not a reason to hide the repair that remained.

文低頭閱讀水泵護罩的說明，沒有抬頭求提示。到了第二步，他停住：圖上沒畫護罩該朝哪邊轉。林差點伸手指，最後改遞給他一支筆。他標出缺漏的邊，她再與實物比對。水重新流動了，但沒有護罩的水泵，在一般使用前仍需要隔離防護。他們改圖時，把警告留在原處。院子另一邊，廚房人員正在洗高麗菜。林聽得見水落進碗裡。那是有用的開始，不是遮掩未完修理的理由。
