The Glass Cup Test

沒有火種的修窯師 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Lin placed a clear glass cup on the worktable and read the title of the day's lesson aloud: heat must have an exit. Through a narrow tube she poured a little liquid into a closed model. When Wen warmed the lid, a bubble burst from the side. It was a silly sight, and some children laughed, but the result expressed something important. A closed space could build pressure even when its outside looked calm. Lin drew an image of the tube and marked the position of the weak joint. Her solution was to improve the escape path, not to hide the crack with paint.

林把透明玻璃杯放在工作桌上，大聲念出今天的課名：「熱必須有出口。」她用細管把少量液體倒進封閉模型。文加熱杯蓋時，一個氣泡從側邊爆開。畫面有點滑稽，幾個孩子笑了，但結果表達了重要的道理：密閉空間即使外觀看來平靜，也可能累積壓力。林畫下細管的圖，標出薄弱接縫的位置。她提出的解法是改善排氣路徑，而非用油漆掩蓋裂縫。

A visiting cook arrived with delicious soup and told a story about a shark trapped in a fishing net. The animal had remained alive only because a boat crew cut the right opening. Lin liked the story's character, a young fisher who first wanted to keep the valuable net. He had to forgive himself for losing money before he could help the animal. Wen thought this was similar to their choice. They could keep the old kiln's beautiful outer wall, or open one part of it to give the heat a safe exit. The wall could be rebuilt; a hurt person could not be repaired so simply.

一名來訪廚師帶著美味的湯，講起一條鯊魚困在漁網裡的故事。船員剪開正確的出口，牠才得以活著。林喜歡故事裡的角色：一名年輕漁夫原本捨不得昂貴的網，必須先原諒自己損失金錢，才能救助動物。文覺得他們也面臨類似選擇：保留舊陶窯漂亮的外牆，還是打開一部分讓熱安全排出。牆可以重建，受傷的人卻不能那麼容易修復。

Before cutting the inspection square, Lin asked Ke to name what they would lose. He pointed to the painted stone border, which had been part of the gate for forty years. Its color could not be copied exactly. Lin listened, then asked him to name what they might gain: a chance to find the hidden channel and protect the next worker from smoke. Ke stayed silent for a while. At last he fetched a box and placed the painted pieces inside as they were removed. The team numbered every stone so the border could be rebuilt later, even if its history would never be perfectly restored.

打開檢查口前，林請柯說出他們將失去什麼。柯指著彩繪石邊，那是山門四十年的一部分，顏色無法完全複製。林聽完，又請他說出可能得到的：找到隱藏氣道的機會，並保護下一位工人不受煙害。柯沉默許久，最後拿來箱子，讓拆下的彩石依序放進去。小隊替每塊石頭編號，以便日後重建邊框，即使那段歷史再也無法百分之百恢復。

Ke agreed to let the team open a small inspection square, but only after a stronger frame was installed. Lin marked the place with blue chalk and asked everyone to move their tools away. The first stone came free without trouble. Behind it they found a dry pocket of ash, not the open channel shown in the guild drawing. For the first time Ke looked at the old book without making a joke. He said they had been repairing the wrong route for years. Lin recorded his statement, then asked whether anyone could confirm it before they changed the whole plan. Lin chose a small blue circle as the symbol for the safe air exit on the new drawing.

柯同意讓小隊打開一塊檢查口，但必須先裝好較強的支架。林用藍色粉筆標記位置，請眾人把工具移開。第一塊石頭順利取下，後面卻是積著乾灰的空腔，不是公會圖上的通道。柯第一次認真看舊書，說他們多年來可能都在修錯位置。林記下他的話，接著問是否有人能確認，再決定是否更動整個計畫。林在新圖上選了一個小藍圓圈，作為安全排氣出口的符號。

After the cup had cooled, Lin set it beside the drawing of the kiln. The little opening in the model was easy to see. Finding a useful inspection point in the thick wall would be harder. She asked Wen to mark the place where a worker could look without reaching across a hot surface. His first mark was too high for her to see through. He lowered it, then asked her to try again. They kept the inspection opening covered while the kiln was out of use. On the table, a washed glass cup caught the evening light. The revised drawing would stay with the inspection notes, so the next worker could find the same opening.

杯子冷卻後，林把它放在陶窯圖旁。模型的小開口一眼就看得到，要在厚窯壁找出好用的檢查位置，卻困難得多。她請文標出一處不用跨過灼熱表面就能查看的位置。他最初畫得太高，她看不到，於是他往下改，再請她試一次。陶窯停用時，他們仍把檢查開口遮好。桌上，洗淨的玻璃杯映著夕光。修訂圖會和檢查紀錄放在一起，讓下一位工人也找得到同一個開口。
