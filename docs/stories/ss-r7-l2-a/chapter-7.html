日曆上的七天

Seven Days on the Calendar

沒有火種的修窯師 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Master Qiao introduced the next trial with a calendar. They had one short period to make progress before the first hard snow. The climate of the rocky valley could change in a day, so her ideal plan had room for delay. “Advance one step only when the last step is safe,” she said. The simple principle sounded humble beside the great fire spells taught by the academy. Yet Lin understood that a living being could be hurt by a careless choice. She wrote down what each test must contain: a question, a method, a result, and a person responsible for the equipment.

喬師傅拿出日曆宣布下一次考驗。第一場大雪到來前，他們只有很短的一段時間能取得進展。岩石山谷的氣候一天就可能改變，因此她心目中理想的計畫必須容許延誤。「上一步安全了，才能往前一步，」她說。這個樸素原則在學院華麗的火術旁顯得謙遜，但林明白一個活生生的人可能因粗心的選擇受傷。她寫下每次測試都必須包括：問題、方法、結果，以及設備負責人。

To introduce the project to younger students, Lin built a small display in the schoolyard. A turtle moved across a shallow pool while children jogged around the path. A boy held a toothbrush in one hand and a wet mop in the other, asking which was better for cleaning tiny cracks. Lin showed him why the soft brush reached the narrow lines and the mop did not. A map on the wall included a far desert where clay would dry too quickly. Thus the students saw that the same tool could work differently in another place. Their first little victory was a question asked carefully, not a prize won in public.

為了向年幼學生介紹計畫，林在校園做了小展示。烏龜穿過淺水池，孩子們沿小路慢跑。一個男孩一手拿牙刷、一手拿濕拖把，問哪個更適合清理細小裂縫。林示範柔軟刷毛能進入細縫，拖把卻不行。牆上的地圖還標出遙遠的沙漠，那裡的陶土會乾得太快。學生因此知道，同一工具換個地方可能有不同效果。他們最初的小勝利，是認真提出問題，而不是在眾人面前贏得獎品。

In the schoolroom, Qiao asked why the plan included only seven days. One student said the academy's calendar had named the trial date; another said snow would arrive whether they were ready or not. Qiao agreed that both answers were true, but neither explained how to protect people if the work took longer. Lin drew a second line under the seven days: keep the small emergency stoves ready. They would not save every plant, but they could warm the smallest nursery rooms while repairs continued. Qiao nodded. An honest plan contained not just the road to success, but a place to stand if success was late.

在教室裡，喬問計畫為何只有七天。一名學生說學院日曆訂好了考核日期，另一名學生說不管準備好沒有，雪都會降下來。喬同意兩種答案都對，卻都沒說明工作延遲時如何保護人。林在七日計畫下又畫出一條線：保持小型緊急暖爐可用。暖爐救不了所有植物，卻能在修繕期間先溫暖最小的育苗房。喬點頭。誠實計畫不只要有通往成功的路，還得預備成功遲到時可以站穩的地方。

That night Qiao checked the seven-day schedule. Some tasks could happen at once: carrying sand, drying test pieces, and asking farmers about the greenhouse. Other tasks had to wait, especially heating a tile that still held water. Lin crossed out one proud but impossible promise from her list. It felt strange to make a plan smaller when the danger was growing, but Wen reminded her that a smaller honest plan could be finished. They kept two spare days for failures. Neither called that a loss of courage. After class, the children asked if they could jog along the safe marked path, and Lin agreed.

當晚，喬師傅檢查七天計畫。有些工作可以同時進行：搬運沙、晾乾試片、詢問農民苗圃的情況；另一些則必須等待，尤其不能加熱仍含水的瓦。林從清單刪掉一項漂亮卻不可能完成的承諾。危險正在增加，計畫反而縮小，令她有些不安。文提醒她，規模較小但誠實的計畫才有機會完成。他們保留兩天應付失敗，沒有一個人把這稱作膽小。下課後，孩子們問能否沿安全標線慢跑，林答應了。

Lin drew a narrow blue line across the seventh day, then stepped back to see the whole calendar. It was not another task. It was time they would keep free if snow closed the road or a test had to be repeated. Wen looked at the line and asked whether people would think they were doing less. Lin thought of the cold beds below the gate. A full calendar would not keep those plants alive if the first delay broke the whole plan. She placed the calendar where the growers could see it, then went down to ask which deliveries could wait. For the first time, the empty part of her plan had a job.

林在第七天畫下一條細藍線，再後退一步，看完整張日曆。那不是新工作，而是預留的時間，萬一下雪封路，或試驗必須重做，就能使用。文望著線，問大家會不會以為他們做得較少。林想起山門下冰冷的苗床。如果第一次延誤就能打亂全部安排，再滿的日曆也保不住植物。她把日曆放在種植者看得到的地方，再下樓詢問哪些交貨可以延後。計畫裡空白的部分，第一次有了自己的工作。
