沒有編號的收據

The Receipt with No Number

沒有火種的修窯師 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 1000–2000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A customer from the countryside came with a blank repair receipt. She had bought clay from a trader in the capital and feared that she might be blamed for a wrong delivery. The page had ink marks but no number and no clear direction for where the goods should go. Lin regarded it as a warning rather than proof of a crime. She took a careful measurement of the bags still in storage and found that two were heavier than the others. The customer was scared of losing her savings. Lin promised to treat her with respect and check the facts before anyone spoke of guilt.

一位鄉間顧客帶著沒有內容的修繕收據來求助。她向首都的商人買了陶土，害怕因送錯貨而被怪罪。紙上有墨跡，卻沒有編號，也沒有清楚的交貨方向。林把它當成警訊而非犯罪證據。她仔細測量倉庫裡的袋子，發現兩袋比其他的重。顧客害怕失去積蓄，林答應尊重她，在查明事實前不談任何人的罪過。

A sudden storm trapped the group in the market hall. A dentist who was closing his office warned them about the danger of slippery steps. A child in slippers nearly fell while carrying fries and a bowl of pudding to her grandmother. Nearby a shopkeeper brought a teapot so they could wait in warmth. The storm hardly lasted an hour, but it gave Lin time to compare the trader's mark with the official settlement book. She found a missing page number, not a false weight. That difference mattered. A clerk had copied the order onto the wrong form when the first book became wet.

一場突來的風雨把大家困在市集大廳。準備關門的牙醫提醒他們，小心濕滑的階梯。一個穿拖鞋的孩子端著薯條和布丁，差點在送去給祖母的路上摔倒。旁邊店家拿來茶壺，讓大家暖暖地等雨停。暴雨不到一小時，卻讓林有時間把商人的印記和正式的結算簿對照。她找到的是遺漏的頁碼，而不是假的重量。兩者差別很大：第一本簿子被雨淋濕時，職員把訂單抄到了錯誤表格上。

The customer returned the next afternoon with a correct receipt and two bags of usable clay. She had expected Lin to ask for a reward, but Lin only asked whether the trader had treated her fairly. The woman said he had apologized and paid for the extra cart trip. She then offered to teach the apprentices how to knot bags so that rain could not enter through the top. Wen tried the knot and failed twice before learning it. They all laughed, not at his weakness, but at the idea that a clever woodworker might still need a lesson from a farmer who had never seen the inside of an academy.

隔天下午，那位顧客帶著正確收據與兩袋可用陶土回來。她以為林會要求報酬，林卻只問商人是否公平對待她。女子說對方已道歉，還支付了多跑一趟的車費。接著她主動教學徒如何打結，讓雨水不會從袋口滲入。文試了兩次都失敗，第三次才學會。眾人笑的不是他笨，而是一位聰明木匠仍得向從沒進過學院的農民學習，這件事十分有趣。

The trader agreed to replace the two bags and issue a numbered receipt. Lin wrote a short public note about the error, leaving the customer's name out of it. She did not want a simple office mistake to become an excuse for frightening people who had already paid. The incident also changed the kiln plan. Every future supply would be checked by weight, date, and source before use. Wen said paper could seem dull beside a great fire. Lin answered that without reliable paper, a repair team might heat the wrong material and discover the error too late. The final measure was made with a clean scale, and the settlement would list both the error and its correction.

商人同意換掉那兩袋材料，並開出有編號的收據。林寫了一份簡短的公開說明，沒有透露顧客姓名。她不希望普通的行政失誤變成威嚇已付款者的理由。這件事也改變了陶窯計畫：今後每批材料使用前，都要核對重量、日期與來源。文說紙張在大火面前顯得無聊。林回答，若沒有可靠紀錄，修理隊可能加熱錯誤材料，等發現時就太晚了。最後一次測量使用乾淨的磅秤，結算紀錄會同時列出錯誤與修正方式。

The customer watched Lin place the unnumbered receipt beside the delivery record. Neither page could answer every question by itself. Wen read the dates aloud while Lin checked the weights, keeping a separate line for the figures that did not match. The customer finally stopped holding the edge of her coat. She had expected to be asked to prove she was honest before anyone looked at the goods. Lin gave her a copy of the comparison and showed her where the next answer would be added. The original receipt stayed in its envelope. Its missing number was a problem to investigate, not a name to give its owner.

顧客看著林把無編號收據放在交貨紀錄旁。兩張紙各自都答不完所有問題。文念日期，林核對重量，把不相符的數字另列一行。顧客終於鬆開一直抓著的衣角。她原以為，大家會先要她證明誠實，才肯查看貨物。林給她一份比對副本，指出下個答案會補在哪裡。原始收據仍收在信封內。缺少編號是一個待查問題，不是拿來定義收據主人身分的名稱。
