加錯的凱

The Wrong Kai

誤把校霸加入讀書群 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Annie added Kai, the campus's most feared student, to her small English study chat. The day began with a small question and soon became more interesting than anyone expected. Annie could hear other students talking, but did not hurry to join their guesses. Kai pointed out a detail that had been easy to miss. They stood together in the college library, where the same familiar objects suddenly seemed to tell a different story. Annie kept the red English notebook close, not as a perfect answer, but as a place to begin.

安琪把全校最令人不敢接近的凱，誤加進英文讀書群。 這一天從一個小疑問開始，後來卻比大家預想的更有趣。安琪聽到其他同學交談，沒有急著加入猜測。凱指出了一個容易忽略的細節。他們站在大學圖書館裡，熟悉的東西忽然像在說另一段故事。安琪把紅色英文筆記本帶在身邊，那不是完美答案，而是一個開始。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Annie raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Kai repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The study group plan remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. Kai drew a small picture of bug in the margin of the class story page. In the English practice book, camera appeared on the next line. Annie read fact aloud, then checked the meaning with Kai. Another card carried friendly and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word future gave the group a new example to discuss.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。安琪舉起手，提議輪流說話。凱用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。讀書群計畫仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 凱在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「bug」（小蟲）的圖。 英文練習本的下一行出現「camera」（相機）。 安琪朗讀「fact」（事實），再和凱確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「friendly」（親切的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「future」（未來）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

She sent a polite apology and offered to remove him. Once everyone understood the choice, the next step was smaller than it had seemed. Annie spoke first and waited for an answer. Kai watched the other person's face instead of trying to hurry the talk. There was room to say no, to ask for an explanation, or to think for another minute. The group used the red English notebook as a guide, not as an order that could never be changed. Kai put juice on the board without turning it into a test. They found lie in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Annie drew a tiny sign beside middle so it would be easier to remember. Kai chose north as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed notice, which Annie copied beside today's notes.

她禮貌道歉，並表示可以移除他。 當所有人都了解選擇後，下一步其實沒有想像中困難。安琪先開口，然後等待回答。凱注意對方的表情，沒有催促。大家可以說不、要求解釋，或多想一分鐘。小組把紅色英文筆記本當作指引，而不是絕不能改變的命令。 凱把「juice」（果汁）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「lie」（謊言；說謊）。 安琪在「middle」（中間）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 凱選了「north」（北方），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「notice」（注意），安琪把它抄在今日筆記旁。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Annie wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Kai suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the study group plan easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. Kai asked how to use other in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, pet appeared on the next line. Kai drew a small picture of picnic in the margin of the class story page. Another card carried reach and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word red gave the group a new example to discuss.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但安琪還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。凱建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論讀書群計畫便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 凱問起，如何用「other」（其他）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「pet」（寵物）。 凱在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「picnic」（野餐）的圖。 另一張圖卡上有「reach」（達到；到達），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「red」（紅色的）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

Kai replied, I can help with the grammar exercise if you want. Before calling the task finished, Annie went back over the facts. One detail had changed, and another still needed an answer. Kai wrote both down rather than hiding the uncertain part. They could be pleased with progress without pretending that every worry had disappeared. By comparing the new result with the first note, they could see what had actually improved. The fair group study still mattered, but now the group knew one more way to protect it. Kai drew a small picture of star in the margin of the class story page. They found then in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Annie drew a tiny sign beside this so it would be easier to remember. Kai chose use as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed woman, which Annie copied beside today's notes.

凱回覆：「如果你願意，我可以幫忙看文法練習。」 在宣布完成前，安琪重新檢查事實。有一個細節改變了，另一個仍有待解答。凱把兩者都寫下，沒有隱藏不確定之處。大家可以為進展高興，卻不必假裝所有擔憂都消失。比對新結果與第一張筆記後，他們看出真正改善的部分。公平的讀書會依然重要，現在小組又多學會一種守護它的方法。 凱在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「star」（明星；星星）的圖。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「then」（然後）。 安琪在「this」（這個）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 凱選了「use」（使用），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「woman」（女士），安琪把它抄在今日筆記旁。

When the last bell sounded, Annie saw that Kai was waiting by the familiar door. The two had not said everything perfectly. They had even disagreed about a detail, yet the disagreement had made their plan clearer. The moment did not need a large speech. A small smile and one honest thank-you were enough. At home, Annie would remember the most surprising part of the day and write it on the next page. Tomorrow the study group plan might look different again. This time they would know how to begin.

最後一聲鈴響時，安琪看見凱在熟悉的門旁等待。兩人不是每句話都說得完美，甚至為某個細節意見不同，但那場分歧讓計畫更清楚。這一刻不需要長篇大論，一個微笑和真誠的道謝就夠了。回家後，安琪會把今天最驚訝的事寫在下一頁。明天讀書群計畫也許又有不同面貌，但這次他們知道如何開始。
