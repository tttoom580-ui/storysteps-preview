Annie did badly on a practice test and wanted to hide the result. At first, Annie thought the problem could be solved before the next bell. A closer look showed that someone else's wishes mattered too. Kai listened without interrupting, while Professor Wu watched the group from nearby. They did not have to agree on everything immediately. There was time to separate a real observation from a story someone had only imagined. Annie took out the red English notebook and asked what should be checked first.

安琪模擬考成績不好，想把分數藏起來。 起初，安琪以為下次鐘響前就能解決問題。但仔細看過後才發現，別人的願望也很重要。凱沒有打斷，靜靜傾聽，吳教授則在附近看著大家。眾人不必立刻同意每件事，還有時間分清真實觀察與想像中的說法。安琪拿出紅色英文筆記本，問大家應先確認什麼。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Annie wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Kai suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the study group plan easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. Kai put arm on the board without turning it into a test. They found convenient in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Annie drew a tiny sign beside deep so it would be easier to remember. Kai drew a small picture of desk in the margin of the class story page. A small study card showed do, which Annie copied beside today's notes.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但安琪還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。凱建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論讀書群計畫便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 凱把「arm」（手臂）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「convenient」（方便的）。 安琪在「deep」（深處的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 凱在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「desk」（桌子）的圖。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「do」（做），安琪把它抄在今日筆記旁。

Kai helped her sort mistakes by type without teasing her. The plan became real only when someone took the first small action. Annie checked the time, Kai checked the shared materials, and Professor Wu confirmed which steps needed adult approval. The group left a clear record so that a person arriving later could understand the decision. They also agreed on what to do if something went wrong. It was not exciting in the way stories often promised, but it let everyone move forward with confidence. Kai asked how to use envelope in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, find appeared on the next line. Annie read fish aloud, then checked the meaning with Kai. Another card carried funny and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word history gave the group a new example to discuss.

凱幫她依類型整理錯誤，沒有取笑。 有人做出第一個小小行動時，計畫才真正開始。安琪確認時間，凱檢查共用材料，吳教授確認哪些步驟需要大人同意。小組留下清楚紀錄，讓稍後到的人也能理解決定。他們還約好若事情出錯該怎麼辦。這不像故事裡常見的刺激冒險，卻讓每個人都更有信心往前走。 凱問起，如何用「envelope」（信封）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「find」（找到）。 安琪朗讀「fish」（魚），再和凱確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「funny」（有趣），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「history」（歷史）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Annie raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Kai repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The study group plan remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. Kai put however on the board without turning it into a test. They found may in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Annie drew a tiny sign beside mud so it would be easier to remember. Kai chose news as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed rise, which Annie copied beside today's notes.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。安琪舉起手，提議輪流說話。凱用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。讀書群計畫仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 凱把「however」（然而）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「may」（可能）。 安琪在「mud」（泥巴）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 凱選了「news」（新聞），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「rise」（升起），安琪把它抄在今日筆記旁。

The notebook showed a clear path forward even though the score stayed low. They gathered the papers and put the shared things back in their places. Annie could feel that the room had changed even though the desks had not moved. Kai pointed to the page where the group had written its first worry. The words were still there, but they no longer told the whole story. Now there was also an action, a result, and a reason to look forward to the next meeting. Kai asked how to use seat in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, shop appeared on the next line. Annie read speak aloud, then checked the meaning with Kai. Another card carried surprised and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word welcome gave the group a new example to discuss.

儘管分數仍低，筆記本已指出清楚的進步方向。 他們整理紙張，把共用物品放回原處。雖然桌子沒有移動，安琪卻覺得房間已經不同。凱指出小組寫下第一個擔憂的那一頁。那些字還在，卻已無法概括整件事。現在多了行動、結果，以及期待下次見面的理由。 凱問起，如何用「seat」（座位）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「shop」（商店）。 安琪朗讀「speak」（說話），再和凱確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「surprised」（驚訝），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「welcome」（歡迎）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。

The hallway grew quiet, and Annie finally had time to think. A good friend had not supplied every answer. Instead, Kai had left space for another idea and had stayed when the first idea failed. That was harder to notice than a loud victory, but it was real. Annie made a promise to remember the person as well as the task. The fair group study was important; so were the people who helped shape it. With that thought, the next chapter of their ordinary and extraordinary days felt possible.

走廊逐漸安靜，安琪終於有時間思考。好朋友不是替自己提供每個答案；凱反而留下思考空間，也在第一個想法失敗時陪在身旁。這比熱鬧的勝利難以察覺，卻是真實的。安琪答應自己，不只記得任務，也要記得參與的人。公平的讀書會重要，幫忙塑造它的人也同樣重要。帶著這個念頭，他們平凡又不平凡的日子可以繼續寫下去。
