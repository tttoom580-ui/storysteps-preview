Annie recorded her speech and hated the sound of her own voice. Nobody had planned for this moment. The room was full of ordinary sounds, yet Annie felt that something important had changed. Kai waited beside the door and let the first surprise settle. It was tempting to find an easy answer before anyone knew the whole story. Instead, they looked carefully at what was truly in front of them. Annie opened the red English notebook and made a small note about the day.

安琪錄下自己的演講，卻討厭自己的聲音。 沒有人預料到這一刻。大學圖書館充滿平常的聲音，安琪卻感覺有件重要的事改變了。凱站在門旁，讓最初的驚訝慢慢沉澱。大家很容易在弄清事情之前就急著下結論，但他們決定先看清眼前真正發生的事。安琪翻開紅色英文筆記本，記下今天的小小發現。

Annie was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Kai answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Professor Wu nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. In the English practice book, actor appeared on the next line. Annie read afternoon aloud, then checked the meaning with Kai. Another card carried band and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word celebrate gave the group a new example to discuss. Kai put corner on the board without turning it into a test.

安琪害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。凱回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的吳教授點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 英文練習本的下一行出現「actor」（演員）。 安琪朗讀「afternoon」（下午），再和凱確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「band」（樂團），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「celebrate」（慶祝）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 凱把「corner」（轉角）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

Kai asked her to notice one part she had improved before replaying mistakes. There was a quiet kind of courage in trying again. Annie noticed that the last attempt had taught them something, even without a perfect result. Kai offered practical help and asked which part would be most useful. They worked together for a short time and then stopped to check whether they were still following the rule. Professor Wu praised the careful check more than the speed of the work. They found die in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Annie drew a tiny sign beside glasses so it would be easier to remember. Kai chose I as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed key, which Annie copied beside today's notes. Kai asked how to use last in a sentence about their day.

凱請她在重聽錯誤之前先找出一個進步之處。 願意再試一次，是一種安靜的勇氣。安琪發現，就算上次結果不完美，大家仍學到了一些事。凱提供實際協助，先問哪一部分最有用。他們一起工作一小段時間，再停下來確認是否仍遵守規則。吳教授稱讚的不是速度，而是這份細心。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「die」（枯死）。 安琪在「glasses」（眼鏡）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 凱選了「I」（我），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「key」（鑰匙），安琪把它抄在今日筆記旁。 凱問起，如何用「last」（最後的）寫一句與今天有關的話。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Kai thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Annie thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Professor Wu reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. In the English practice book, media appeared on the next line. Annie read more aloud, then checked the meaning with Kai. Another card carried o’clock and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word or gave the group a new example to discuss. Kai put package on the board without turning it into a test.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。凱想到還沒發言的人，安琪則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。吳教授提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 英文練習本的下一行出現「media」（媒體）。 安琪朗讀「more」（更多），再和凱確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「o’clock」（整點鐘），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「or」（或）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 凱把「package」（包裹）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。

She heard that her ending had become clear and steady. The answer made someone smile, but Annie still asked one final question: was everyone comfortable with what came next? Kai listened to the replies, including the quiet ones. They changed a small detail when one person needed something different. Nothing about the change made the story less meaningful. It helped the fair group study belong to more than one person, which was why the work had begun. They found play in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Annie drew a tiny sign beside poor so it would be easier to remember. Kai chose supermarket as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed ticket, which Annie copied beside today's notes. Kai asked how to use tired in a sentence about their day.

她發現自己的結尾已變得清晰而穩定。 答案讓某個人笑了，但安琪仍問最後一個問題：大家都能接受下一步嗎？凱聽取回答，包括輕聲說出的意見。有人有不同需求，他們就修改一個小細節。這種修改沒有讓故事失去意義，反而讓公平的讀書會不只屬於一個人；這也正是大家當初開始的原因。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「play」（演奏）。 安琪在「poor」（不足的；昏暗的）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 凱選了「supermarket」（超市），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「ticket」（門票），安琪把它抄在今日筆記旁。 凱問起，如何用「tired」（疲倦的）寫一句與今天有關的話。

Near the end of the day, Annie stayed beside Kai for one more minute. They remembered how strange the morning had felt. A problem that once seemed to fill the whole room had become several smaller questions. Each question could be handled in turn. They did not know what the following day would bring, and that was all right. What they had learned was not a magic answer but a habit: notice a change, ask with care, and let people choose for themselves. Annie closed the red English notebook and left with a lighter heart.

一天快結束時，安琪在凱身邊多待了一分鐘。他們想起早上的奇怪感受。原本像占滿整間房間的難題，現在分成幾個較小的問題，可以逐一處理。他們不知道明天會帶來什麼，但這沒有關係。今天學到的不是神奇答案，而是一種習慣：留意變化、仔細發問，也讓別人自己選擇。安琪闔上紅色英文筆記本，帶著較輕鬆的心情離開。
