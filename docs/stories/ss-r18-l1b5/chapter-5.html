窗邊的座位

A Seat by the Window

誤把校霸加入讀書群 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Annie saved the quiet window seat for the group study meeting. The day began with a small question and soon became more interesting than anyone expected. Annie could hear other students talking, but did not hurry to join their guesses. Kai pointed out a detail that had been easy to miss. They stood together in the college library, where the same familiar objects suddenly seemed to tell a different story. Annie kept the red English notebook close, not as a perfect answer, but as a place to begin.

安琪替讀書會保留安靜的窗邊座位。 這一天從一個小疑問開始，後來卻比大家預想的更有趣。安琪聽到其他同學交談，沒有急著加入猜測。凱指出了一個容易忽略的細節。他們站在大學圖書館裡，熟悉的東西忽然像在說另一段故事。安琪把紅色英文筆記本帶在身邊，那不是完美答案，而是一個開始。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Annie wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Kai suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the study group plan easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. The word bank gave the group a new example to discuss. Kai put farm on the board without turning it into a test. They found feed in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Annie drew a tiny sign beside letter so it would be easier to remember. Kai chose many as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但安琪還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。凱建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論讀書群計畫便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 「bank」（銀行）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 凱把「farm」（農場）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「feed」（餵食）。 安琪在「letter」（信）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 凱選了「many」（許多），準備耐心向初學者說明。

Kai asked if everyone would rather meet somewhere less visible. The plan became real only when someone took the first small action. Annie checked the time, Kai checked the shared materials, and Professor Wu confirmed which steps needed adult approval. The group left a clear record so that a person arriving later could understand the decision. They also agreed on what to do if something went wrong. It was not exciting in the way stories often promised, but it let everyone move forward with confidence. A small study card showed moon, which Annie copied beside today's notes. Kai asked how to use movie in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, oil appeared on the next line. Annie read sell aloud, then checked the meaning with Kai. Another card carried slow and a simple drawing by a classmate.

凱問大家是否想在不那麼顯眼的地方碰面。 有人做出第一個小小行動時，計畫才真正開始。安琪確認時間，凱檢查共用材料，吳教授確認哪些步驟需要大人同意。小組留下清楚紀錄，讓稍後到的人也能理解決定。他們還約好若事情出錯該怎麼辦。這不像故事裡常見的刺激冒險，卻讓每個人都更有信心往前走。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「moon」（月亮），安琪把它抄在今日筆記旁。 凱問起，如何用「movie」（電影）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「oil」（油）。 安琪朗讀「sell」（販賣），再和凱確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「slow」（緩慢地），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Annie raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Kai repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The study group plan remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. The word straight gave the group a new example to discuss. Kai put strange on the board without turning it into a test. They found string in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Annie drew a tiny sign beside teenager so it would be easier to remember. Kai chose think as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。安琪舉起手，提議輪流說話。凱用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。讀書群計畫仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 「straight」（直接地）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 凱把「strange」（奇怪）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「string」（線）。 安琪在「teenager」（青少年）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 凱選了「think」（思考），準備耐心向初學者說明。

The group chose the public library table so everyone felt comfortable. They gathered the papers and put the shared things back in their places. Annie could feel that the room had changed even though the desks had not moved. Kai pointed to the page where the group had written its first worry. The words were still there, but they no longer told the whole story. Now there was also an action, a result, and a reason to look forward to the next meeting. A small study card showed through, which Annie copied beside today's notes. Kai asked how to use toe in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, topic appeared on the next line. Annie read where aloud, then checked the meaning with Kai. Another card carried yellow and a simple drawing by a classmate.

大家選擇圖書館公共桌，讓所有人都自在。 他們整理紙張，把共用物品放回原處。雖然桌子沒有移動，安琪卻覺得房間已經不同。凱指出小組寫下第一個擔憂的那一頁。那些字還在，卻已無法概括整件事。現在多了行動、結果，以及期待下次見面的理由。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「through」（經過；透過），安琪把它抄在今日筆記旁。 凱問起，如何用「toe」（腳趾）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「topic」（主題）。 安琪朗讀「where」（哪裡），再和凱確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「yellow」（黃色），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

When the last bell sounded, Annie saw that Kai was waiting by the familiar door. The two had not said everything perfectly. They had even disagreed about a detail, yet the disagreement had made their plan clearer. The moment did not need a large speech. A small smile and one honest thank-you were enough. At home, Annie would remember the most surprising part of the day and write it on the next page. Tomorrow the study group plan might look different again. This time they would know how to begin.

最後一聲鈴響時，安琪看見凱在熟悉的門旁等待。兩人不是每句話都說得完美，甚至為某個細節意見不同，但那場分歧讓計畫更清楚。這一刻不需要長篇大論，一個微笑和真誠的道謝就夠了。回家後，安琪會把今天最驚訝的事寫在下一頁。明天讀書群計畫也許又有不同面貌，但這次他們知道如何開始。
