The Rumor by the Stairs

A classmate warned Annie that Kai had once fought another student. The light through the window looked ordinary, but the news did not. Annie stopped for a moment, feeling both curious and unsure. Kai did not laugh at the question, even when it sounded strange. They knew from earlier mistakes that moving too fast could make a simple problem harder. The college library offered enough space for a calm talk. On the first page of the red English notebook, Annie wrote what was known and left a blank space for what remained uncertain.

一位同學警告安琪，凱曾和別的學生打架。 窗外的光線和平常一樣，消息卻一點也不平常。安琪停了一會兒，既好奇又不確定。就算問題聽來奇怪，凱也沒有取笑。他們從先前的錯誤學到，行動太快反而可能讓小問題變複雜。大學圖書館有足夠空間讓大家平靜討論。安琪在紅色英文筆記本第一頁寫下已知的事，也留下空白給尚未確定的部分。

Annie was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Kai answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Professor Wu nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. Annie read all aloud, then checked the meaning with Kai. Another card carried area and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word basketball gave the group a new example to discuss. Kai put begin on the board without turning it into a test. They found define in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

安琪害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。凱回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的吳教授點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 安琪朗讀「all」（全部），再和凱確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「area」（區域），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「basketball」（籃球）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 凱把「begin」（開始）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「define」（定義）。

Annie refused to treat a rumor as a proven fact. There was a quiet kind of courage in trying again. Annie noticed that the last attempt had taught them something, even without a perfect result. Kai offered practical help and asked which part would be most useful. They worked together for a short time and then stopped to check whether they were still following the rule. Professor Wu praised the careful check more than the speed of the work. Annie drew a tiny sign beside email so it would be easier to remember. Kai chose eraser as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed few, which Annie copied beside today's notes. Kai asked how to use lamp in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, leave appeared on the next line.

安琪拒絕把傳聞當成已證實的事。 願意再試一次，是一種安靜的勇氣。安琪發現，就算上次結果不完美，大家仍學到了一些事。凱提供實際協助，先問哪一部分最有用。他們一起工作一小段時間，再停下來確認是否仍遵守規則。吳教授稱讚的不是速度，而是這份細心。 安琪在「email」（電子郵件）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 凱選了「eraser」（橡皮擦），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「few」（少數），安琪把它抄在今日筆記旁。 凱問起，如何用「lamp」（燈）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「leave」（離開）。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Kai thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Annie thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Professor Wu reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. Annie read less aloud, then checked the meaning with Kai. Another card carried mail and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word modern gave the group a new example to discuss. Kai put night on the board without turning it into a test. They found orange in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。凱想到還沒發言的人，安琪則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。吳教授提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 安琪朗讀「less」（較少），再和凱確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「mail」（郵件），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「modern」（現代化的）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 凱把「night」（夜晚）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「orange」（橘色）。

She discovered that the event had been a dispute over a broken club cabinet. The answer made someone smile, but Annie still asked one final question: was everyone comfortable with what came next? Kai listened to the replies, including the quiet ones. They changed a small detail when one person needed something different. Nothing about the change made the story less meaningful. It helped the fair group study belong to more than one person, which was why the work had begun. Annie drew a tiny sign beside plant so it would be easier to remember. Kai chose p.m. as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed quarter, which Annie copied beside today's notes. Kai asked how to use traffic in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, will appeared on the next line.

她得知那件事其實是社團櫃子損壞引起的爭執。 答案讓某個人笑了，但安琪仍問最後一個問題：大家都能接受下一步嗎？凱聽取回答，包括輕聲說出的意見。有人有不同需求，他們就修改一個小細節。這種修改沒有讓故事失去意義，反而讓公平的讀書會不只屬於一個人；這也正是大家當初開始的原因。 安琪在「plant」（盆栽；植物）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 凱選了「p.m.」（下午（時間標記）），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「quarter」（一刻鐘），安琪把它抄在今日筆記旁。 凱問起，如何用「traffic」（往來人流；交通）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「will」（將會）。

Outside, the air was cooler than it had been at lunch. Annie looked back once and saw the college library in the evening light. It seemed like an ordinary place, but there was more to it now: a remembered question, a mistake corrected, and a choice made together. Kai waved from a few steps away. They still had their separate dreams and small worries, yet they also shared one clear memory. Whatever happened next, they could return to that memory without needing to pretend the day had been perfect.

外面的空氣比午餐時涼了些。安琪回頭望了一眼晚光中的大學圖書館。它看似平凡，現在卻多了值得記住的問題、改正的錯誤，以及共同做出的選擇。凱在幾步外揮手。他們仍有各自的夢想和小煩惱，也有一段共同的清晰回憶。無論接下來發生什麼，都不必假裝今天完美，就能想起它的價值。
