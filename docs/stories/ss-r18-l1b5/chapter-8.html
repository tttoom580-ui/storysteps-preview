Professor Wu announced a short English speech contest for college students. Nobody had planned for this moment. The room was full of ordinary sounds, yet Annie felt that something important had changed. Kai waited beside the door and let the first surprise settle. It was tempting to find an easy answer before anyone knew the whole story. Instead, they looked carefully at what was truly in front of them. Annie opened the red English notebook and made a small note about the day.

吳教授宣布為大學生舉辦英文短講比賽。 沒有人預料到這一刻。大學圖書館充滿平常的聲音，安琪卻感覺有件重要的事改變了。凱站在門旁，讓最初的驚訝慢慢沉澱。大家很容易在弄清事情之前就急著下結論，但他們決定先看清眼前真正發生的事。安琪翻開紅色英文筆記本，記下今天的小小發現。

They began by asking who might be affected by the next step. Kai thought about the people who had not spoken yet, and Annie thought about the rule they had already promised to follow. One person offered a quick answer, but the group asked for a reason. A good question was not an attack. It was a way to make the answer safer and kinder. Professor Wu reminded them that everyone could ask for a pause. Kai drew a small picture of book in the margin of the class story page. Kai chose boring as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed dangerous, which Annie copied beside today's notes. Kai asked how to use dollar in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, each appeared on the next line.

大家先問，下一步會影響到誰。凱想到還沒發言的人，安琪則想到自己早已承諾遵守的規則。有人很快提出答案，其他人卻請他說明理由。好問題不是攻擊，而是讓答案更安全、更體貼的方法。吳教授提醒大家，每個人都可以要求暫停。 凱在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「book」（書）的圖。 凱選了「boring」（無聊的），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「dangerous」（危險的），安琪把它抄在今日筆記旁。 凱問起，如何用「dollar」（美元）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「each」（每個）。

Annie decided to enter with a talk about learning from mistakes. Annie chose the part of the work that could be done carefully. Kai handled a different task and promised to report back before making any large change. A short question from Professor Wu caught an error in their first version. Instead of hiding it, they made a neat correction on the page. That correction did not erase their effort. It showed that the group was learning how to be responsible for its own work. Annie read else aloud, then checked the meaning with Kai. Another card carried file and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word fork gave the group a new example to discuss. Kai put full on the board without turning it into a test. They found idea in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

安琪決定以從錯誤中學習為題參賽。 安琪選擇自己能謹慎完成的工作，凱負責另一項任務，並答應在重大更改前先回報。吳教授的一個簡短問題，讓大家發現第一版中的錯誤。他們沒有掩飾，而是在頁面上整齊修正。這項修正沒有抹去先前的努力，反而證明小組正在學習對自己的工作負責。 安琪朗讀「else」（其他），再和凱確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「file」（檔案），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「fork」（叉子）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 凱把「full」（滿的）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「idea」（點子）。

Annie was afraid that saying the wrong thing would make the day worse. Kai answered that staying silent could also leave a misunderstanding in place. They practiced one honest sentence and checked that it did not blame anyone unfairly. This took longer than making a joke, but the result was clearer. Near the table, Professor Wu nodded and let the young people try their own words before offering advice. Annie drew a tiny sign beside listen so it would be easier to remember. Kai chose mistake as a word to explain kindly to a new learner. A small study card showed museum, which Annie copied beside today's notes. Kai asked how to use noon in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, queen appeared on the next line.

安琪害怕說錯話會讓事情變糟。凱回答，保持沉默有時也會讓誤會繼續存在。他們練習說出一句誠實的話，再確認沒有不公平地責怪任何人。這比開玩笑花時間，結果卻更清楚。桌旁的吳教授點頭，先讓年輕人用自己的話嘗試，再提供建議。 安琪在「listen」（聽）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 凱選了「mistake」（錯誤），準備耐心向初學者說明。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「museum」（博物館），安琪把它抄在今日筆記旁。 凱問起，如何用「noon」（中午）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「queen」（女王）。

Kai offered to practice as a listener, not to write her speech. Professor Wu asked the group to explain its choice in everyday words. Annie tried once, paused, and then gave a shorter answer. Kai added an important detail that had been left out. The explanation became clear enough for another person to check. That mattered more than making their decision sound grand. The fair group study could grow only if other people understood how it worked. Annie read roll aloud, then checked the meaning with Kai. Another card carried successful and a simple drawing by a classmate. The word tell gave the group a new example to discuss. Kai drew a small picture of train in the margin of the class story page. They found you in a short reading exercise near the end of the page.

凱願意充當聽眾練習，而不是替她代寫。 吳教授請小組用平常的話解釋選擇。安琪試了一次，停頓後改用更短的回答。凱補充了一個漏掉的重要細節。解釋變得足夠清楚，讓別人也能核對。這比把決定說得偉大更重要。只有其他人理解做法，公平的讀書會才有機會繼續成長。 安琪朗讀「roll」（捲；滾動），再和凱確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「successful」（成功的），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。 「tell」（告訴）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 凱在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「train」（火車）的圖。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「you」（你；你的）。

Near the end of the day, Annie stayed beside Kai for one more minute. They remembered how strange the morning had felt. A problem that once seemed to fill the whole room had become several smaller questions. Each question could be handled in turn. They did not know what the following day would bring, and that was all right. What they had learned was not a magic answer but a habit: notice a change, ask with care, and let people choose for themselves. Annie closed the red English notebook and left with a lighter heart.

一天快結束時，安琪在凱身邊多待了一分鐘。他們想起早上的奇怪感受。原本像占滿整間房間的難題，現在分成幾個較小的問題，可以逐一處理。他們不知道明天會帶來什麼，但這沒有關係。今天學到的不是神奇答案，而是一種習慣：留意變化、仔細發問，也讓別人自己選擇。安琪闔上紅色英文筆記本，帶著較輕鬆的心情離開。
