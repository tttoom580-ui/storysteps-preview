The Hallway Story

A group photograph showed Kai beside the broken cabinet on the day of the dispute. At first, Annie thought the problem could be solved before the next bell. A closer look showed that someone else's wishes mattered too. Kai listened without interrupting, while Professor Wu watched the group from nearby. They did not have to agree on everything immediately. There was time to separate a real observation from a story someone had only imagined. Annie took out the red English notebook and asked what should be checked first.

一張合照拍到爭執當天，凱站在壞掉的櫃子旁。 起初，安琪以為下次鐘響前就能解決問題。但仔細看過後才發現，別人的願望也很重要。凱沒有打斷，靜靜傾聽，吳教授則在附近看著大家。眾人不必立刻同意每件事，還有時間分清真實觀察與想像中的說法。安琪拿出紅色英文筆記本，問大家應先確認什麼。

For a minute, the group spoke all at once. Annie raised a hand and suggested taking turns. Kai repeated the question in simpler words, so that even a quiet student could follow the talk. Nobody needed to win an argument. They needed to learn which part of the problem could be handled now and which part needed more help. The study group plan remained difficult, but it no longer felt impossible. A small study card showed around, which Annie copied beside today's notes. Kai asked how to use button in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, dark appeared on the next line. Annie read evening aloud, then checked the meaning with Kai. Another card carried fall and a simple drawing by a classmate.

有一分鐘，大家幾乎同時開口。安琪舉起手，提議輪流說話。凱用更簡單的詞重述問題，讓安靜的學生也能跟上。沒有人需要爭贏；重要的是分清哪些事現在能做、哪些事需要更多協助。讀書群計畫仍然困難，卻不再讓人覺得無法處理。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「around」（周圍），安琪把它抄在今日筆記旁。 凱問起，如何用「button」（按鈕）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「dark」（昏暗的）。 安琪朗讀「evening」（傍晚），再和凱確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「fall」（落下），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

Annie asked the club adviser for the official account rather than spreading the image. Once everyone understood the choice, the next step was smaller than it had seemed. Annie spoke first and waited for an answer. Kai watched the other person's face instead of trying to hurry the talk. There was room to say no, to ask for an explanation, or to think for another minute. The group used the red English notebook as a guide, not as an order that could never be changed. The word have gave the group a new example to discuss. Kai put interesting on the board without turning it into a test. They found left in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Annie drew a tiny sign beside menu so it would be easier to remember. Kai drew a small picture of potato in the margin of the class story page.

安琪向社團指導老師查證，而不是轉傳照片。 當所有人都了解選擇後，下一步其實沒有想像中困難。安琪先開口，然後等待回答。凱注意對方的表情，沒有催促。大家可以說不、要求解釋，或多想一分鐘。小組把紅色英文筆記本當作指引，而不是絕不能改變的命令。 「have」（有）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 凱把「interesting」（有趣的）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「left」（左側）。 安琪在「menu」（菜單）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 凱在班級故事頁的空白處畫了「potato」（馬鈴薯）的圖。

The first idea sounded simple, and that was useful. Still, Annie wanted to know whether it respected everyone's choice. Kai suggested changing only one part rather than throwing the whole plan away. They drew two small boxes on a page: what we can do and what we must ask about. The list made the study group plan easier to discuss without turning a friend into an enemy. A small study card showed rose, which Annie copied beside today's notes. Kai asked how to use smile in a sentence about their day. In the English practice book, sound appeared on the next line. Annie read stay aloud, then checked the meaning with Kai. Another card carried student and a simple drawing by a classmate.

第一個想法聽起來很簡單，這是好事。但安琪還想確認是否尊重每個人的選擇。凱建議只修改其中一部分，不必推翻整個計畫。他們在紙上畫兩個方框：我們能做的事，以及必須先詢問的事。有了這份清單，討論讀書群計畫便容易多了，也不會把朋友當成敵人。 一張小小練習卡上寫著「rose」（玫瑰），安琪把它抄在今日筆記旁。 凱問起，如何用「smile」（微笑）寫一句與今天有關的話。 英文練習本的下一行出現「sound」（聲音）。 安琪朗讀「stay」（留下），再和凱確認意思。 另一張圖卡上有「student」（學生），旁邊是同學的簡筆畫。

The adviser confirmed that Kai had reported damage, not caused it. Before calling the task finished, Annie went back over the facts. One detail had changed, and another still needed an answer. Kai wrote both down rather than hiding the uncertain part. They could be pleased with progress without pretending that every worry had disappeared. By comparing the new result with the first note, they could see what had actually improved. The fair group study still mattered, but now the group knew one more way to protect it. The word sweet gave the group a new example to discuss. Kai put trouble on the board without turning it into a test. They found usually in a short reading exercise near the end of the page. Annie drew a tiny sign beside watch so it would be easier to remember. Kai chose worry as a word to explain kindly to a new learner.

指導老師證實，凱是通報損壞的人，不是肇事者。 在宣布完成前，安琪重新檢查事實。有一個細節改變了，另一個仍有待解答。凱把兩者都寫下，沒有隱藏不確定之處。大家可以為進展高興，卻不必假裝所有擔憂都消失。比對新結果與第一張筆記後，他們看出真正改善的部分。公平的讀書會依然重要，現在小組又多學會一種守護它的方法。 「sweet」（甜蜜的）這個字讓小組多了一個可以討論的例子。 凱把「trouble」（困擾）寫在板上，卻沒有把它變成考試。 他們在頁面末尾的短文練習裡找到「usually」（通常）。 安琪在「watch」（觀看）旁畫上小記號，幫助記憶。 凱選了「worry」（擔心），準備耐心向初學者說明。

The hallway grew quiet, and Annie finally had time to think. A good friend had not supplied every answer. Instead, Kai had left space for another idea and had stayed when the first idea failed. That was harder to notice than a loud victory, but it was real. Annie made a promise to remember the person as well as the task. The fair group study was important; so were the people who helped shape it. With that thought, the next chapter of their ordinary and extraordinary days felt possible.

走廊逐漸安靜，安琪終於有時間思考。好朋友不是替自己提供每個答案；凱反而留下思考空間，也在第一個想法失敗時陪在身旁。這比熱鬧的勝利難以察覺，卻是真實的。安琪答應自己，不只記得任務，也要記得參與的人。公平的讀書會重要，幫忙塑造它的人也同樣重要。帶著這個念頭，他們平凡又不平凡的日子可以繼續寫下去。
