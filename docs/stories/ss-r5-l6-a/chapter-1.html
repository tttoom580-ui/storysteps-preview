死者的姓名

The Name of a Dead Woman

王庭第七份證詞 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

On the morning the royal archive burned, Investigator Serin Ash was sent not to the fire but to a hearing room. Six witnesses had accused a young copyist, Tavi, of starting the blaze. The seventh statement was stranger: it carried the name of Lady Elda Mire, who had been buried three winters earlier. The senior clerk proposed that they abbreviate her name to E. Mire and proceed. Serin refused. In the Kingdom of Leth, a seal confirmed that a document had entered official custody; it did not confirm that its contents were true. That distinction was the anchor of the Witness Code, applicable even to evidence submitted by a minister. Serin rubbed her tired eyelid and requested the original registration book. Everyone expected her to pronounce the copyist guilty. Instead, she asked when a dead woman had learned to speak.

王室檔案庫失火的早晨，調查官瑟琳・艾許沒有被派往火場，而是走進一間聽證室。六名證人指控年輕抄寫員塔維縱火；第七份證詞卻更奇怪，署名竟是三個冬天前已下葬的艾妲・米爾夫人。資深書記建議把她的名字縮寫成 E. Mire，照常辦理。瑟琳拒絕了。在雷斯王國，封印只能證明文件已進入官方保管程序，不能證明內容屬實。這項區別是《證人法》的核心，即使證據由大臣呈交也一樣適用。瑟琳揉了揉疲憊的眼皮，要求調閱原始登記簿。所有人都以為她會宣布抄寫員有罪，她卻問了一個問題：死人究竟何時學會開口說話？

Beyond the hearing chamber, the palace kitchen had begun to broil river fish for the ministers. A seamstress called Nessa waited near the stove, carrying a folded brassiere, or bra, meant for an injured laundress. She pressed the bundle to her bosom when Serin passed. Inside the cloth was a note: a palace messenger had offered Nessa a bribe to say she had seen Tavi carrying oil. She had not seen him. A hired gangster had watched her door since she declined. Serin could not arrest the messenger on a whispered account, but she could record the offer and protect the witness. She led Nessa to a public desk where two independent clerks could observe the testimony. The clerk who had originally ignored her now watched with the fixed smile of a man who knew the story was slipping away.

聽證室外，王宮廚房正替大臣們炙烤河魚。名叫妮莎的女裁縫等在爐灶旁，手裡拿著一件折好的胸罩，準備送給受傷的洗衣婦。瑟琳經過時，她把布包緊貼胸前。布料裡藏著一張字條：一名王宮信差提出賄賂，要她聲稱看見塔維攜帶燈油。她其實沒有看見。她拒絕後，一名受雇的黑幫打手就一直守在家門外。瑟琳不能僅憑耳語拘捕信差，但可以記錄利誘並保護證人。她帶妮莎到公開辦事桌，讓兩名獨立書記見證陳述。先前忽視妮莎的書記此刻掛著僵硬的笑，彷彿已察覺原本安排好的故事正在脫離掌控。

Serin performed a comparative examination of the seven pages. Six used the same unusual phrase, “the eastern window swallowed the moon,” although the witnesses supposedly came from different streets. The seventh page used a copied description of a virgin sheet of parchment, untouched by prior writing. Yet the paper showed faint lines beneath the fresh ink. Tavi, a twenty-three-year-old apprentice, asked to narrate his own movements, but the presiding officer ordered silence. Serin reminded him that the available evidence was finite; he could not invent missing facts merely because the court wished to finish before lunch. A conviction required independent testimony and a material finding, not a beautiful arrangement of sentences. The officer granted her one day to examine the registry. One day was an insulting allowance, but it was better than no investigation at all.

瑟琳比較了七張證詞。前六張都使用同一句罕見的話：「東窗吞下月亮」，儘管證人據稱來自不同街道。第七張把羊皮紙描述為從未書寫過的全新紙張，紙面上卻隱約浮出舊筆畫。二十三歲的學徒塔維要求敘述自己的行蹤，主持官卻命他安靜。瑟琳提醒對方，現有證據是有限的；不能因為法庭想在午餐前結案，就憑空補上缺失的事實。定罪需要獨立證詞與實物證據，不是把漂亮句子排列整齊。主持官只准她花一天查驗登記簿。一天的時間少得近乎侮辱，但總比完全沒有調查好。

At the north junction, a palace orderly guided Serin toward the registry bank, where witnesses deposited sealed statements. An elderly teller showed her an entry for Lady Elda made after the funeral. The man who had brought it walked with a limp, but his signature was disguised as a harmless delivery mark. The bank had asked for a warrant before releasing the original, and a junior judge had refused the request without explanation. Serin wrote down the sequence instead of announcing a conspiracy. She knew how quickly public anger could flourish into certainty, and certainty was not proof. She obtained an inspection order limited to the page and its custody history. Then she told Tavi he would remain under lawful supervision rather than be executed that evening. The young man looked more frightened by that narrow hope than he had by the accusations.

在北側路口，一名宮廷勤務員引領瑟琳前往登記銀行，證人會把封存的陳述交到這裡。一名年老出納拿出艾妲夫人葬禮之後才建立的登記紀錄。送件男子走路一跛一跛，簽名卻被偽裝成不起眼的遞送記號。銀行曾要求取得司法調閱令，才肯交出正本，但一位初級法官不說理由就駁回了申請。瑟琳先記下事情的先後順序，沒有立即宣布有人陰謀作案。她明白公眾的怒火多快就能滋長成篤定，而篤定不等於證據。她取得一份範圍有限的檢查命令，只能調閱該頁和保管歷程。隨後她告訴塔維，今晚他不會被處決，而是繼續依法接受監督。年輕人面對這一線希望，似乎比被控告時更加害怕。

That evening Serin locked the copied record inside a box with two keys, one held by the court and one by the independent witness office. She would not risk losing the only evidence through impatience. Regent Aven had promised that learning and honest service could flourish under her rule; now the promise had reached a practical test. If Serin followed the easiest explanation, she might please the palace and silence a frightened apprentice. If she followed the paper, she would have to question the people who supervised her own department. She wrote the first unanswered question in her notebook: Who registered a statement on behalf of a woman already dead? Beneath it she added a second question, harder to face: Who made it so easy for the court to accept the statement without asking? For the first time that day, she understood the scale of the work ahead.

當晚，瑟琳把紀錄副本鎖進雙鑰箱，一把鑰匙由法庭保管，另一把交給獨立證人事務所。她不願因急躁而失去唯一的證據。攝政王艾雯曾承諾，在她治下，學識與誠實服務都能興盛；如今這項承諾迎來實際考驗。如果瑟琳選擇最簡單的解釋，她或許能討好宮廷，並讓受驚的學徒永遠閉嘴。如果她跟著文件追查，就必須質疑監督自己部門的人。她在筆記本寫下第一個未解問題：誰替一名已死的女人登記證詞？接著又添上一個更難面對的問題：是誰讓法庭如此輕易地接受證詞，甚至不肯多問一句？這天，她第一次真正理解眼前工作的規模。
