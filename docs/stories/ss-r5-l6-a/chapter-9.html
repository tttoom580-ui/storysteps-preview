不受歡迎證人的旅舍

The Lodge of Unwanted Witnesses

王庭第七份證詞 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first five witnesses who corrected their accounts could no longer safely remain at home. Serin arranged temporary rooms in a modest lodge by the maple grove, away from the streets where crowds gathered. The building had a waterproof roof, a small kitchen smelling of jasmine tea, and a caretaker who refused to gossip about guests. When one witness spilled chili sauce on a signed receipt, Jori used a clean blot of paper to absorb the stain and preserved the page. Serin feared the protection order might alienate the neighbors, who had been told the residents were dangerous conspirators. She asked the lodge keeper to explain only the public safety rules, not the identities of the guests. Privacy, she argued, did not need to become secrecy about the law itself. The arrangement would last for a defined span, with periodic review by the court.

首批更正證詞的五名證人已無法安全留在家中。瑟琳把他們暫時安置在楓樹林旁一間樸素旅舍，遠離群眾聚集的街道。旅舍屋頂防水，廚房飄著茉莉茶香，管理員拒絕向外談論住客。一名證人把辣椒醬灑在簽署收據上時，喬里用一張乾淨吸墨紙吸乾污漬，再保存原頁。瑟琳擔心保護令會疏遠鄰居，因為他們已被告知旅舍住的是危險陰謀者。她請店主只說明公開的安全規則，不透露住客身分。她認為，尊重隱私不必變成對法律本身保密。安排有明確期限，並由法庭定期審查。

A newspaper tried to describe one female witness as a minister's mistress, implying that her evidence had been purchased through intimacy. She was in fact a widowed schoolteacher who had never met the minister. Serin demanded a correction without disclosing the woman's location. Another paper called the lodge a socialist meeting place because two residents supported a workers' association. The caretaker replied that people could socialize over tea without joining a political movement. Serin admired her persistence. She also knew that moral outrage could damage the continuity of her case if she abandoned careful documentation. She asked a court officer to record each threat separately, identifying who made it and whether it could be confirmed. False descriptions mattered, and none of the affected people were dispensable, but the errors were not all crimes. Each allegation required its own evidence.

一家報紙試圖把某位女證人寫成大臣的情婦，暗示她靠私密關係換來證詞。她其實是喪偶的女教師，從未見過那位大臣。瑟琳要求更正，沒有洩漏女子的位置。另一家報紙稱旅舍是社會主義者聚會處，只因兩位住客支持工人協會。管理員回答，大家喝茶聊天不代表加入政治運動。瑟琳佩服她的堅持，也知道若自己放棄謹慎紀錄，道德憤怒反而會破壞案件的連續性。她請法庭官員分別記下每項威脅，辨明說話者及是否可驗證。錯誤描寫很重要，受影響的人沒有誰是可以被犧牲的，但並非每一項錯誤都構成犯罪；每項指控都要有自己的證據。

The witnesses took a short trek through the grove with a guard at a respectful distance. One had been too frightened to leave her room; the vigorous morning walk helped her regain enough calm to speak. During a rest, Bren began to muse aloud about the strange phrase all six statements shared. A visiting teacher suggested that the phrase might have come from a famous play. Jori checked the scripts and found it had been written only three months earlier for an instructional recital. That date was decisive. The phrase could not be an ancient figure of speech that all witnesses independently remembered from childhood. The teacher showed them the playwright's original notes. Serin arranged to obtain a certified copy, then told the group not to speculate publicly until the author could confirm where the script had traveled.

證人在警衛保持適當距離的陪同下，沿著楓林短途健行。有一人原本嚇得不敢離開房間，活力十足的晨間步行讓她恢復足夠平靜，終於願意說話。休息時，布倫開始思索六份證詞共有的奇怪句子。一名來訪教師建議，那可能出自一齣名劇。喬里查閱劇本，發現那句話三個月前才為教學朗誦寫成。這個日期十分關鍵：它不可能是六名證人各自從童年記起的古老修辭。教師展示劇作家的原始筆記。瑟琳安排取得認證副本，並請眾人不要在作者確認劇本流向前公開猜測。

Serin now saw a cumulative pattern. Identical language, altered times, a replaced index leaf, and the courier's badge all pointed toward preparation outside the courtroom. Yet the pattern did not tell her who had approved it. Jori wanted to mobilize the public, arguing that a vigorous campaign would force the minister to answer. Serin refused to turn protected witnesses into symbols. The lodge's purpose was to keep them safe while the investigation matured. She instructed officers to separate security decisions from publicity decisions and to review each restriction with the guests themselves. Several demanded the freedom to contact family, and she agreed to supervised arrangements that respected their choices. A protected witness was not a captive. The plan was slower and less satisfying than a grand protest, but it preserved consent.

瑟琳如今看見累積的模式：相同語句、變動時間、被換掉的索引頁、信差徽章，都指向法庭外事先安排的程序。但模式還不能告訴她誰核准了整件事。喬里希望動員公眾，認為強大的運動能迫使大臣回答。瑟琳拒絕把受保護證人變成象徵。旅舍的目的，是在調查成熟前確保他們安全。她命令官員將安全決策與宣傳決策分開，每項限制都要和住客本人討論。幾名證人要求聯繫家人，她同意安排在尊重選擇前提下受到適當監督的探訪。受保護證人不是囚犯。這個方案比大型示威慢，也不那麼痛快，卻保住了當事人的同意。

Before Serin returned to the capital, the playwright sent a reply. He had taught the phrase at the studio rehearsal as an example of exaggerated testimony, not as a line to use in court. A palace secretary had attended the lesson and taken copies. The author remembered the secretary's name but could not identify who later distributed the pages. Serin placed the statement beside Bren's correction. Two independent sources now linked the suspicious wording to the same rehearsal. That was a stronger finding than anything the angry newspapers had printed. She looked back at the lodge, its windows warm beneath the trees, and thought of the people who had risked their comfort to correct a lie. Their courage was not a resource for her to spend without permission. She would have to protect it by making the next step worthy of their trust.

瑟琳返城前，劇作家送來回信。他當初在排練室把那句話當作誇張證詞的範例教授，不是要人拿去法庭使用。一名王宮祕書曾上課並取走副本。作者記得祕書姓名，卻不知道誰後來分發文件。瑟琳把陳述放在布倫的更正旁。兩個獨立來源現在都把可疑句子連到同一次排練，這比憤怒報紙的任何說法都更有力。她回望樹下窗戶溫暖的旅舍，想到那些冒著風險更正謊言的人。他們的勇氣不是她可以未經同意任意消耗的資源。她必須用值得信賴的下一步，來保護他們交出的信任。
