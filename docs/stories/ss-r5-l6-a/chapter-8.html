消失的索引頁

The Missing Index Leaf

王庭第七份證詞 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The registry bank occupied a low stone building beside the marsh, where lotus flowers grew in shallow water. Its manager, a blond woman named Elsen, had completed the most difficult qualification examinations in the province. She agreed to help if Serin obtained proper clearance and did not disrupt ordinary customers. The ledger showed a steady accumulation of statements in the week before the fire. Each was supposed to receive a number that could never be reused. Yet the page for Elda's testimony appeared between two older entries, as though it had been inserted after the book was bound. Elsen would not certify Serin's first impression. She asked them to retrieve the registration ruler and compare the spacing of the holes. A small difference might be a printing defect, not evidence of wrongdoing. Serin welcomed the caution and ordered refreshments for the exhausted clerks.

登記銀行位於沼澤旁的低矮石屋，淺水裡長著蓮花。經理艾爾森是位金髮女子，通過了省內最艱難的資格考試。她同意協助，但要求瑟琳取得正式查驗許可，且不得干擾一般客戶。帳冊顯示火災前一週已有大量陳述累積，每一份都應取得不能重複使用的編號。然而艾妲的證詞頁卻夾在兩份更舊的登記之間，彷彿帳本裝訂完成後才插入。艾爾森不願立即替瑟琳的第一印象背書，而是請他們取出登記尺，比較紙孔間距。細微差異可能只是印刷瑕疵，不一定代表舞弊。瑟琳歡迎這份謹慎，並替疲憊的書記安排茶點。

An assistant lieutenant from the palace guard arrived with instructions to take the book away. He called the investigation a marginal dispute and said his commander had more heroic duties to perform. Serin asked him to show an order signed by a judge. He could not. Elsen calmly explained that a custody seal did not authorize removal by anyone who happened to wear a uniform. Her staff had been trained to exert no pressure on witnesses, even when a senior official became impatient. The lieutenant left without the volume. Behind him, a literate apprentice whispered that someone had recently deducted a page count from the monthly audit. The adjustment was recorded as a “clerical correction,” but no supervisor had explained it. Serin did not ask the apprentice to accuse his manager; she invited him to submit the note through the bank's protected reporting channel.

一名宮廷衛隊副官帶著取走帳簿的指示趕到。他說這只是邊緣小爭議，自己的長官有更英雄的任務。瑟琳要求出示法官簽署的命令，他拿不出來。艾爾森平靜地說明，保管封印不代表穿制服的人都能把文件帶走。她的員工接受過訓練，即使高級官員不耐煩，也不得對證人施壓。副官空手離開。他走後，一名識字的學徒悄聲說，最近有人從每月稽核總頁數中扣除了一頁。變更被登記為「文書更正」，卻沒有主管說明原因。瑟琳沒有要求學徒指控經理，而是請他透過銀行受保護的通報管道提交紀錄。

Elsen offered a beverage and opened the monthly audit in front of two witnesses. The altered number did not overlap with any standard deletion period. A provincial courier had transported the book that month, but his receipt showed no interruption or damage. The omission therefore belonged either to the bank before shipment or to the court after delivery. Serin asked for a second certification of the paper stock, knowing that the original pages had been imported from a mill beside the swamp. Jori traced the invoice and found that the replacement leaf came from a newer batch. Its fibers were not dangerous or mysterious; they were simply different. For a moment Serin felt destined to solve the case by this single clue. Then Elsen reminded her that a new sheet could have been inserted for an innocent repair. Motive and authority still mattered.

艾爾森端上飲料，當著兩名證人的面打開月度稽核表。遭修改的數字不屬於任何正常的刪頁期間。當月一名省府信差運送過帳簿，但回執沒有中斷或損毀紀錄。因此遺漏可能發生在銀行出貨前，也可能發生在法庭收件後。瑟琳要求再次鑑定紙張來源，因為原始頁面購自沼澤旁的紙坊。喬里追查發票，發現替換頁來自較新的批次。纖維沒有危險或神祕之處，只是不同而已。瑟琳一瞬間以為命運注定讓她靠這個線索解案，艾爾森卻提醒，新頁也可能是善意修補時插入。動機與權限仍然重要。

After sunset, Serin met the bank's independent auditor. She asked whether the missing index number could be restored without changing the rest of the register. He said it could, provided that all corrections remained visible and the original sequence was preserved. That answer brought relief: proving a document false did not require destroying the institution that stored it. Meanwhile, Tavi had begun a vocation course in bookbinding under supervision, hoping to return to ordinary work. He sent Serin a note explaining how thin glue could hold a replacement leaf without disturbing adjacent pages. She passed the technical suggestion to Elsen without telling the bank to accept it as truth. The next morning they would test the adhesive against a verified sample. A young defendant could offer knowledge, but the investigation still needed an independent examination.

日落後，瑟琳與銀行獨立稽核員會面，詢問能否在不改動其餘登記的情況下恢復缺失編號。他說可以，只要更正始終可見、原始順序也保留。這答案使她鬆了口氣：證明某份文件有假，不必摧毀保管它的制度。同時，塔維在監督下開始學習裝訂的職業課程，希望能重返普通工作。他寫信說明，薄膠可以把替換頁黏進書裡，而不破壞相鄰頁面。瑟琳把這項技術建議轉給艾爾森，沒有要求銀行直接當成真理。翌日，他們將以已驗證樣本測試黏著劑。年輕被告可以提供知識，但調查仍需獨立檢驗。

Elsen's final statement was cautious. The index leaf was almost certainly replaced; the person who ordered the replacement remained unknown. She signed that conclusion before the witnesses and refused to guess beyond it. Serin thanked her, then copied the bank's procedures into her own notes. The more transparent the process became, the harder it would be for anyone to declare a verdict by rank alone. On her way out she met the young lieutenant, now standing without his guards. He apologized for attempting to seize the book. Serin accepted the apology but still filed a report of the incident. Courtesy could not substitute for a record. The lieutenant considered this, then offered to testify about the order he had received. For the first time, an instruction from the palace might be examined at its actual source.

艾爾森的最終陳述十分謹慎。索引頁幾乎可以確定遭到替換，但下令者尚不明。她在見證人面前簽署結論，拒絕越界猜測。瑟琳向她道謝，並把銀行程序抄進筆記。程序越透明，任何人就越難單靠職級宣布判決。離開時，她遇到那位年輕副官；這次他沒有帶守衛。他為試圖奪走帳簿道歉。瑟琳接受道歉，仍然正式記錄事件。禮貌不能代替紀錄。副官思索後，主動表示願意說明自己接到的命令。第一次，一項來自王宮的指示也許能被追查到真正來源。
