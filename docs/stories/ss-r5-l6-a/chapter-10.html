大臣的完美故事

The Minister's Perfect Story

Minister Odrin Vale finally agreed to meet Serin, but only in a gallery of great splendor where visiting diplomats usually admired royal paintings. He called her cynical for questioning statements that had passed through his department. She answered that a court should not prosecute a person by default merely because a minister was offended. Odrin offered a concession: he would release a copy of the rehearsal contracts if she stopped questioning the palace's handling of the fire. Serin refused to trade investigative independence for paperwork that already belonged in the public archive. A jockey outside the gallery led a restless horse past a delivery cart while the minister's adviser attempted to intrude on their conversation. Serin requested that he remain as an official observer instead of whispering from behind a curtain. Odrin's pleasant expression began to crack.

奧德林・維爾大臣終於同意見瑟琳，但地點設在充滿奢華光彩、供外國使節欣賞王室畫作的畫廊。他稱她憤世嫉俗，竟質疑已經過自己部門的證詞。瑟琳回答，法庭不能只因大臣不悅，就預設某人有罪並加以起訴。奧德林提出讓步：只要她不再追問宮廷如何處理火災，就交出排練合約副本。瑟琳拒絕用調查獨立性去交換原本就應公開的文件。畫廊外，一名賽馬騎師牽著不安的馬走過運貨車；大臣顧問試圖在幕後插話。瑟琳請他以正式旁聽者身分留下，不要躲在簾後耳語。奧德林和善的表情開始破裂。

The minister described the fire as a spontaneous disaster and repeated the literal words of the seven statements. Serin asked why an anonymous secretary had been allowed to copy the same unusual sentence into all of them. Odrin retorted that many frightened witnesses speak alike. She did not argue about human nature. Instead, she placed the original rehearsal program on the table and showed that the sentence had been written months before the fire. Odrin said the program was a forgery. Serin invited an independent examiner to compare signatures, paper, and ink. The minister tried to stutter through an answer, then recovered his composure. He promised that the Crown would subsidize any investigation he considered appropriate. Serin replied that independent review meant neither she nor he could control the result. His next smile looked almost painful.

大臣把火災描述成突發災難，又逐字重複七份證詞。瑟琳問為何一名匿名祕書能把同一句罕見話寫進所有文件。奧德林反駁，受驚的證人說話本來就相似。瑟琳沒有爭論人性，而是把排練節目原稿放上桌，證明那句話在火災數月前就已寫成。奧德林稱節目單是偽造。瑟琳邀請獨立檢驗員比較署名、紙張與墨水。大臣一時結巴，隨後恢復鎮定。他承諾王室會補助任何他認為適當的調查。瑟琳回答，獨立審查意味著結果既不能由她操控，也不能由他決定。他再次露出的笑容幾乎像在忍痛。

At the academy laboratory that evening, a technician tested the pigment found on Elda's replacement page. The sample carried a faint fragrance of resin and a synthetic dye used in new government stationery. Another luminous pigment in the same cabinet was marked radioactive and kept under special controls; the technician made clear it had not been detected in the evidence. Serin appreciated the distinction because sensational claims could deplete public trust as surely as hidden corrections. The dye found on the page was ordinary, but its supply had been limited to the minister's records office. Jori brought a screwdriver to open the registered sample case under supervision. The official count showed a missing bottle, yet the discrepancy might have come from waste or an accounting error. Serin ordered a stock audit rather than an arrest.

當晚在學院實驗室，技術員檢驗艾妲替換頁上的顏料。樣本帶著淡淡樹脂香，也含有新式政府文具使用的合成染料。櫃中另有一種發光顏料標示為放射性物質，受到特別管制；技術員清楚說明，證物裡沒有檢出它。瑟琳重視這項區別，因為聳動指控和隱瞞更正一樣，都會耗損公眾信任。替換頁上的染料很普通，但供應範圍限於大臣紀錄室。喬里在監督下用螺絲起子打開登記樣本箱。官方清單少了一瓶，但差額也可能是廢棄或帳務錯誤。瑟琳要求清點庫存，而非立即拘捕。

On the return carriage, Jori's stepfather, a retired cook, arrived with a covered serving dish and insisted the investigators eat something warm. The food was modest, its fragrance more welcome than the gallery's perfume. He had spent years serving officials who spoke of fairness while demanding special treatment. “You need morale more than another argument,” he told Serin. She laughed despite her fatigue. Then he mentioned that the minister's office had recently ordered a private banquet after the archive incident, even as the city mourned its losses. Serin thanked him but refused to turn kitchen gossip into proof of wrongdoing. She asked whether he could identify a written purchase order. He could not, so the story remained a lead. Jori watched her set the note aside and understood why her work frustrated both allies and enemies.

回程馬車旁，喬里的繼父——一名退休廚師——帶著蓋好的餐盤出現，堅持讓調查員吃點熱食。食物樸素，香氣卻比畫廊香水更令人歡迎。他服侍官員多年，聽過太多人高談公平，卻要求自己獲得特權。「你們現在需要士氣，不是另一場爭辯。」他對瑟琳說。她累得發笑。廚師接著提起，火災後大臣辦公室曾訂下一場私人宴會，當時全城還在哀悼損失。瑟琳道謝，卻拒絕把廚房閒話當作不法證據。她問能否找出書面採購單，他說不能。於是故事暫時只是一條線索。喬里看她把筆記放到一旁，明白她的工作為何讓盟友與敵人都感到挫折。

The next morning Serin sent the Regent a limited report. It described the repeated script, replaced index leaf, and ministerial dye supply, but it did not accuse Odrin of ordering the fire. The Regent wrote back that the report was useful precisely because it named what remained unknown. She appointed an external auditor to review the supply records and promised not to interfere with witness interviews. Serin knew promises could be broken, so she asked that the appointment be registered publicly. Tavi's case was still unresolved, and five witnesses still needed protection. Yet they had reached a turning point: the investigation could now trace a plausible path from rehearsal room to registry bank to ministerial office. The path was not a verdict, but it was something stronger than outrage. It was a sequence another person could attempt to verify.

翌日早晨，瑟琳向攝政王呈交有限報告。內容列出重複劇本、替換索引頁與大臣部門染料供應，但沒有指控奧德林下令縱火。攝政王回信說，報告之所以有用，正因明確列出尚未知曉的事。她任命外部稽核員檢查用品紀錄，並承諾不干涉證人訪談。瑟琳知道承諾可能被違背，便要求把任命公開登記。塔維案件仍懸而未決，五名證人仍需要保護。但他們已抵達轉折點：調查終於能描出一條合理路徑，從排練室到登記銀行，再到大臣紀錄室。這條路徑還不是判決，卻比憤怒更有力量，因為其他人可以試著逐步驗證。
