聽見兩次鐘聲的孩子

The Children Who Heard Two Bells

王庭第七份證詞 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Serin visited the Royal Orphanage because the third witness claimed to have heard the archive bell from its courtyard. The house stood beside a recreational garden, where a small shrub hid the opening of an old drainage pipe. The children were being given cucumber slices and bread when she arrived. Sister Halin, the director, belonged to an advisory board that had survived two changes of monarch. She welcomed questions but warned Serin not to turn frightened children into performers. The palace had long described the northern provinces as savage lands, an old habit dating from their conquest. Such language made it easier to dismiss the testimony of families who had fled there. Serin promised to interview only volunteers, with a guardian present and a written explanation of their rights. One girl, Mina, raised her hand and said she had heard two bells, not one.

瑟琳造訪王家育幼院，因為第三名證人聲稱自己在院子裡聽到檔案庫的鐘聲。院舍旁是一座休憩花園，一叢小灌木遮住舊排水管的入口。她抵達時，孩子們正在吃小黃瓜片和麵包。院長哈琳修女曾在一個歷經兩任君主的諮詢委員會任職。她歡迎提問，卻警告瑟琳不要把受驚的孩子當成表演者。王宮長期把北方省分描繪成野蠻之地，這種說法可以追溯到當年的征服戰爭，也使人們更容易忽略逃離當地家庭的證詞。瑟琳承諾只訪談自願參加的孩子，必須有監護人在場，並以文字說明他們的權利。一名叫米娜的女孩舉手說，她聽到的是兩次鐘聲，不是一次。

A second boy said that a firecracker had burst during the festival rehearsal and briefly drowned out the first bell. A feeble elderly groundskeeper remembered smelling grease near the bell's wheel that morning. He had seen an unfamiliar man wearing a cloth liner inside his coat, but could not identify his face. None of these details alone proved a conspiracy, and Serin would not pretend they did. She arranged to examine the bell mechanism before any cleaning took place. Jori found a stain beside the wheel and sealed a sample in a small bottle. The gardener had been trying to lessen the noise with ordinary oil, but the new mark contained a darker material. Even if the sample proved harmless, the second bell mattered: the court statements all described a single sound precisely when the fire began.

另一名男孩說，節慶彩排時一枚爆竹炸開，短暫蓋過第一次鐘聲。年邁而虛弱的園丁記得當天早晨鐘輪附近有油脂味。他還看見一名陌生男子，外套裡縫著布質內襯，但無法辨認對方臉孔。任何單一細節都不能證明陰謀，瑟琳也不會假裝可以。她安排在鐘輪清洗前檢查機械。喬里發現輪旁污痕，並把樣本封進小瓶。園丁平日會用普通油脂減低噪音，但新痕跡的顏色更深。即使最後證明樣本無害，第二次鐘聲仍然重要：法庭全部證詞都說，火災開始的那一刻只響過一次鐘。

In the courtyard, Mina showed Serin a tapestry stitched from donated textile scraps. A painted bird and a small bell were meant to symbolize safe refuge. The pattern had been taught by Elda Mire years before her death, during a program for children left parentless by the winter plague. Serin listened as Mina explained the nursery slang they used for the two bells: the first was “come home,” and the second was “stay away.” The pair had different rhythms. Serin asked whether any child would speak about that difference at a formal hearing. Halin insisted that the children could decline, and Serin agreed. Protecting them would surpass any immediate advantage of a dramatic courtroom scene. She noted the rhythm in her own words and left the original artwork where it belonged.

在院子裡，米娜展示一幅用捐贈布料碎片縫成的掛毯。畫上的鳥和小鐘象徵安全庇護。多年前，冬季瘟疫讓一些孩子失去雙親，艾妲・米爾曾在育幼院計畫中教他們這個花樣。瑟琳聽米娜解釋，院裡孩子用俚語稱呼兩次鐘聲：第一次叫「回家」，第二次叫「別靠近」，兩者節奏不同。瑟琳詢問是否有人願意在正式聽證上說明差異，哈琳堅持孩子有權拒絕，瑟琳也同意。保護他們比換取一場精彩法庭戲更重要。她用自己的文字記下節奏，並把原作留在原本的位置。

Halin then produced a vaccination ledger showing that several children had received a new vaccine at the infirmary on the morning of the fire. Their appointment times showed that the first bell must have sounded before the six adult witnesses said it did. The ledger was not a magical answer; it required independent confirmation from the nurse and the clinic clock. Nevertheless, it gave Serin a direction that the copied statements had overlooked. Outside, fumes from the kitchen drifted across the garden. Jori watched a nurse wheel supplies past the orphanage gate and asked why the court had ignored so many ordinary schedules. Serin answered that a fabricated story often looks complete only because it excludes inconvenient people. She would return to the infirmary, not to seek an emotional endorsement, but to test the clock against another institution.

哈琳隨後拿出疫苗接種登記簿。紀錄顯示火災當天早晨，有幾名孩子在醫務室接種新疫苗。預約時間表示第一次鐘聲響起時，應早於六名成年證人所說的時刻。這份簿冊不是神奇答案；仍需由護理師和診所時鐘獨立確認。但它給瑟琳一條遭到抄寫證詞忽略的追查方向。戶外，廚房油煙飄過花園。喬里看見一名護理師推著用品經過育幼院大門，詢問法庭為何忽略這麼多平凡的時程。瑟琳回答，捏造的故事往往只因排除了不方便的人而顯得完整。她會前往醫務室，不是尋找情感上的支持，而是用另一個機構的紀錄檢驗時間。

The next morning, a local newspaper called the investigation an insult to the Crown and compared Serin with rebellious agitators. Its language was cruel, but she did not fume in reply. She sent the editor a factual correction: she had questioned the timing of the fire, not declared the palace guilty. Tavi's detention remained lawful for the moment, though the prosecution could no longer rely on the disputed statements without review. His mother came to thank Serin, then asked when her son would be free. Serin could offer no honest date. She said only that they now had a way to test what happened rather than argue about reputations. The mother looked disappointed, but she accepted the answer. Serin kept the disappointment with her as a reminder of whom delayed justice actually harmed.

隔天早晨，一份地方報紙稱調查侮辱王權，還把瑟琳比作煽動叛亂的人。措辭殘酷，但她沒有憤怒回擊，只寄給編輯一份事實更正：她質疑的是火災時間，並未宣稱宮廷有罪。塔維目前仍依法受拘留，但檢方不能再不經審查就依賴那些有爭議的證詞。他的母親來向瑟琳道謝，接著問兒子何時能獲釋。瑟琳無法給出誠實的日期，只說現在終於有方法檢驗事件，而不是爭論誰的名聲比較好。母親露出失望，仍接受了答案。瑟琳把那份失望記在心裡，提醒自己拖延的正義究竟傷害了誰。
