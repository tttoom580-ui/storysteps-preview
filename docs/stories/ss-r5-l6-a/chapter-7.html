岔路上的信差

The Messenger at the Fork

The studio rental book named a former palace courier, Caspar Den, who had left the city after a disciplinary hearing. Serin found him on a three-acre orchard near the northern intersection. The orchard covered more than one acre of land, where a landslide had cut off the main road. He was an emigrant from the border provinces, a bachelor who taught boxing to young villagers for a small fee. The orchard's fertility was poor, and he supplemented his income by repairing fences. A woodpecker tapped above the gate as Serin approached. Caspar greeted her with visible gloom. “You think a man who carried letters must know the secrets inside,” he said. Serin explained that she did not equate delivery with guilt. She wanted to know who had given him the unusual badge and why he had been dismissed. Caspar studied her papers for several minutes before inviting her in.

排練室租借簿記著一名前王宮信差卡斯帕・登，曾在紀律聽證後離城。瑟琳在北方交叉路口附近一座三英畝果園找到他。那片果園面積遠不只一英畝；山崩切斷了主要道路。他是來自邊境省分的移民，也是單身漢，靠教村裡青年拳擊賺取微薄收入。果園土壤肥力不好，他還得兼做修柵欄的工作。瑟琳走近大門時，一隻啄木鳥在樹上敲個不停。卡斯帕滿臉陰鬱地迎接她。「妳以為送信的人就知道信裡所有祕密？」他問。瑟琳解釋，她不會把遞送行為等同罪責；她只想知道誰給他陌生徽章，以及他為何遭解職。卡斯帕看了她的文件好幾分鐘，才請她入內。

Caspar had once refused to forsake a fellow courier accused of disobeying an orthodox palace rule. Both men were punished, although the council later issued a provisional finding that the rule had never been properly published. Caspar's voice began to falter as he recalled the hearing. The official who supervised it told him that a unanimous verdict was necessary to protect the court's vanity, and that any dissent would be recorded as betrayal. Serin wrote the exact quotation without adopting his interpretation. He admitted carrying a sealed package to the registry bank on the morning after the fire. The label contained Elda Mire's name, but he had not opened it. A guard with a limp collected the receipt. The courier could describe the route, and he still possessed the stamped return slip.

卡斯帕曾拒絕拋棄一名被控違反正統宮廷規則的同事。兩人都受到懲罰，儘管委員會後來初步認定那項規則根本沒有正式公布。回想聽證時，他的聲音開始顫抖。主持官員告訴他，為了維護法庭的虛榮，裁決必須一致；任何異議都會被記為背叛。瑟琳逐字記下這番話，沒有直接接受他的解釋。他承認火災隔天早晨曾把一個密封包裹送到登記銀行。標籤上寫著艾妲・米爾的姓名，但他沒有開封。一名跛腳守衛取走回執。卡斯帕能描述路線，也仍保有蓋章的退件存根。

The orchard family had asked Caspar to teach their eldest daughter how to defend herself on the road. She was a gay young woman who had been mocked by a traveling preacher. Caspar spoke of her with respect and made clear that her private life had no connection to the investigation. Serin agreed. When the daughter brought bread and soup, she said Caspar's lessons had helped nourish her confidence without encouraging fights. That afternoon he showed Serin the route through the landslide debris. The path was narrow, but not impossible. A local prohibition on night travel had recently been lifted, something the official schedules had ignored. Serin realized the messenger could have reached the bank on time. That supported his account, not the contents of the letter. She thanked him for the distinction and copied the slip in his presence.

果園主人曾請卡斯帕教長女如何在路上自我保護。那位年輕女子是女同性戀者，曾遭巡迴傳教者嘲笑。卡斯帕尊重地談起她，並清楚表示她的私人生活與調查無關。瑟琳同意。女孩端來麵包與湯，說卡斯帕的課程使她更有自信，卻不鼓勵打架。當天下午，他領瑟琳穿過山崩碎石間的小路。路徑狹窄，但並非無法通行。地方禁止夜間出行的規定最近已取消，官方時程卻沒有更新。瑟琳因此明白，信差確實可能準時抵達銀行。這支持他的運送說法，並不證明信件內容。她感謝卡斯帕澄清，並在他面前複印存根。

Before she left, Caspar told Serin that he had once hoped the palace would acknowledge his mistake and let him return. Now he wanted only his name removed from the disciplinary list. He asked whether she could make the court agree. She could not. The law allowed him to appeal, and she promised to give him the relevant forms, not an outcome. The lack of a dramatic bargain disappointed him, but he accepted it. Jori noticed that the return slip bore two different kinds of ink. The payment figure was ordinary; the registrar's initials had been added later. If the initials proved false, someone inside the bank had assisted in transferring the envelope into the court file. The inquiry was no longer simply about a lying witness. It was about the path a paper traveled after a witness had supposedly spoken.

離開前，卡斯帕告訴瑟琳，他過去希望王宮承認處置錯誤、讓他回任，如今只想從紀律名單刪去姓名。他問瑟琳能否讓法庭同意。她不能保證。法律允許他申訴，她承諾提供適當表格，而不是承諾結果。沒有戲劇性交換條件讓卡斯帕失望，但他仍接受。喬里注意到存根上有兩種墨色。支付金額是正常書寫，登記官縮寫則是稍後才加上的。如果縮寫確屬偽造，便表示銀行內有人協助把信封送入法庭卷宗。調查不再只是關於說謊的證人，也關於一張紙在證人所謂「說完話」之後究竟經過哪些人的手。

On the ride south, Serin considered the different people who had cooperated with her: a widower, a nurse, a sculptor, and a courier with a damaged career. None agreed on politics, and several distrusted the palace for good reasons. Their statements did not form a perfect story. That imperfection was what made them valuable. Each could correct the others where times, places, and motives overlapped. At the city gate, Serin asked Jori to arrange separate custody for the courier's slip and the studio accounts. If the same official could quietly change both, their independence would be lost. She was exhausted, but she could finally see an outline of the machinery behind the false evidence. It had hands, schedules, and signatures; that meant it could be examined, rather than merely feared.

南返途中，瑟琳想著與她合作的不同人：鰥夫、護理師、雕刻家，以及事業受損的信差。他們政治立場並不一致，有幾位也有充分理由不信任宮廷。他們的陳述沒有組成完美故事，而正是這種不完美使它們有價值。每個人都能在時間、地點與動機交疊處修正其他人的說法。抵達城門時，瑟琳請喬里把信差存根與排練室帳冊分別保管。如果同一官員能悄悄更動兩者，它們的獨立性便會喪失。她筋疲力竭，卻終於看見製造偽證的機器輪廓。它有手、有時間表、有簽名，表示它可以被檢驗，而不只能被害怕。
