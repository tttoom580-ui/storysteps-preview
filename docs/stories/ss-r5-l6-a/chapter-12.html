消失的赦免令

The Pardon That Vanished

王庭第七份證詞 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

A clerk with failing eyesight from the royal treasury approached Serin beside a brook running through the palace garden. He had found a lease agreement and an expiration notice among the burned archive's surviving documents. The agreement concerned a private company allowed to transport court papers between the inland capital and the islands beyond the coral coast. Its contract was due to expire the day after the fire. The clerk believed the transport firm had removed a sealed chest before any flames appeared. Serin listened but refused to elevate the claim above its evidence. The firm's invoices were available; the chest's contents were not. A majestic statue of the former monarch stood over the stream, and birds hovered near its crown. The setting looked peaceful, but the documents suggested that the fire might have concealed a theft rather than merely destroyed an accident's records.

一名視力衰退的王室財政書記在流經宮廷花園的小溪旁接近瑟琳。他在火災後倖存的檔案裡找到租約和到期通知。合約涉及一家私人運輸公司，獲准在內陸首都與珊瑚海岸外的群島之間運送法庭文件，期限恰好在火災隔天結束。書記相信那家公司在火勢出現前就搬走一只密封箱。瑟琳聽完，卻不肯把推論提高到超過證據的地位。運輸發票可以調閱，箱中內容仍未知。前任君主的雄偉雕像俯視溪流，鳥兒在王冠附近盤旋。景色平和，文件卻暗示，火災可能掩蓋了一場竊取，而不只是一場意外留下的損失。

The missing chest was linked to a document called the Winter Pardon. It would have restored the rights of people imprisoned solely on uncorroborated testimony, including many whose offspring now lived in difficult circumstances. A legislator had proposed the reform years earlier, arguing that the Crown must not deprive families of liberty without verifiable evidence. Odrin's ministry had repeatedly requested more time to study it. Serin felt the investigation gain momentum, but her colleague reminded her that a motive for theft was not proof of theft. At lunch they ate a hearty stew while the palace physician discussed a surgical operation scheduled on the night of the fire. That appointment created another independent clock record. Its timing showed that a supply wagon left the archive courtyard earlier than the ministry's report admitted.

失蹤的箱子與一份名為《冬季赦免令》的文件有關。它原本會恢復那些僅憑缺乏佐證的供述而入獄者的權利，其中許多人的子女至今仍在艱困環境中生活。多年前，一名議員提出改革，主張王權不能在沒有可驗證證據時剝奪家庭自由。奧德林的部門卻一再要求更多研究時間。瑟琳感覺調查開始加速，但喬里提醒，有竊取動機不代表已證明竊取。午餐時，他們吃著豐盛燉菜，王宮醫師談起火災夜裡安排的一場手術。那項預約形成另一份獨立時鐘紀錄，顯示一輛物資馬車離開檔案庫庭院的時間，比大臣報告承認的更早。

Serin visited the transport yard and watched mechanics rotate a wagon wheel to check its axle. A drop of petrol on the floor suggested nothing illegal; the yard used fuel routinely. What interested her was the written insistence that only the minister's private seal could authorize removal of the chest. No one could explain why a public pardon had been placed under that seal. The company's manager said the shipment remained untouched on a small island depot. Serin asked for a custody history and a court order before anyone opened it. She refused a captain's suggestion that they simply seize the chest at dawn. That might secure the papers quickly, but it could also strangle the evidence by making its later handling impossible to verify. The legal route was slower, and it required cooperation from island authorities.

瑟琳造訪運輸場，看技師轉動貨車輪檢查車軸。地上一滴汽油不能代表非法行為，車場本來就使用燃料。真正引起她注意的是書面規定：只有大臣私人封印能核准搬走那只箱子。沒有人能解釋為何一份公共赦免令要受那個封印控制。運輸公司經理說貨物仍原封不動地留在小島倉庫。瑟琳要求先取得保管歷程和法庭命令，才准任何人開箱。她拒絕船長建議黎明直接奪箱的作法。那或許能迅速取得文件，也可能因後續保管無法驗證而扼殺證據。合法路徑更慢，還需要島上當局合作。

Meanwhile, the Regent was facing growing demands to make the pardon public immediately. An opposition speaker warned that delaying its release would make the monarchy appear indifferent to imprisoned families. Serin understood the urgency, but she could not promise that the missing chest contained the final signed text. A draft might differ from the approved decree; a copied signature might be false. She asked the legislature to publish the known stages of the pardon process: proposal, review, final assent, and registration. The records showed the first three steps had been completed. Only the public registration was missing. That finding changed the political conversation. The question was no longer whether the pardon deserved consideration. It was why an approved act had failed to enter the register where every citizen could read it.

同時，攝政王面臨愈來愈強烈的壓力，要求立即公布赦免令。反對派發言者警告，延後發布會讓王室顯得漠視被囚家庭。瑟琳理解迫切性，卻不能保證箱中裝的是最終簽署版本。草案可能與核准法令不同，複製簽名也可能是假。她要求議會公開赦免令已知的程序：提案、審議、最終御准與登記。紀錄顯示前三步已完成，唯獨缺少公共登記。這項發現改變了政治討論。問題不再是赦免令值不值得考慮，而是一項已核准的法令為何沒有進入所有公民都能閱讀的登記簿。

At the end of the week, the island council agreed to preserve the chest until a joint inspection could occur. Its message named three independent custodians and required a record of each movement. Serin welcomed the arrangement. She could almost feel the case shifting beneath her feet: it had begun with a suspicious witness statement and now reached a missing act of mercy. Yet she did not want the broader public issue to erase Tavi's immediate predicament. She arranged for his lawyer to receive all evidence bearing on the timing of the fire, including material that might weaken the prosecution. Then she asked Jori to prepare a list of families who could benefit if the pardon proved genuine, without publishing their names. The search was still about individual people, not merely the prestige of a palace.

週末，島議會同意在聯合檢查前保全箱子。訊息列出三名獨立保管人，要求記錄每一次移動。瑟琳歡迎這項安排。她幾乎感覺案件在腳下移動：原本是可疑證詞，如今已牽涉一份消失的恩赦法令。但她不願讓廣泛公共議題抹去塔維眼前的困境。她安排律師取得所有關於火災時間的證據，包括可能削弱檢方主張的資料。接著請喬里列出如果赦免令屬實，可能因此受益的家庭，但不要公開姓名。尋找真相仍然是為了具體的人，不只是宮廷聲望。
