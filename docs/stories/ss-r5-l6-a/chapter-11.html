假印的形狀

The Shape of a False Seal

王庭第七份證詞 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 5000–6000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

The academy's examiner found friction marks along the edge of the replacement page, where it had been pressed against a metal guide. At twenty degrees Celsius, a sample of the new ink dried with an unusual shine; the older ink remained dull. Serin resisted the temptation to simplify the result into an accusation against Minister Odrin. The dye's distribution list included three departments, and a superficial similarity in a supply record was not proof that a particular hand had written the forged statement. An experimental pianist who played for the academy offered to demonstrate a machine used to align printed forms. Its rollers made a low growl, and the repeated pattern on the paper showed near-perfect symmetry. Jori noticed that the same machine could reproduce a signature token if a template had been stolen. The examiner called that a possibility requiring a controlled test, not a conclusion.

學院檢驗員在替換頁邊緣發現摩擦痕，顯示紙張曾壓在金屬導軌上。在攝氏二十度時，新墨水樣本乾燥後帶有異常光澤，舊墨卻保持暗沉。瑟琳克制住把結果簡化為奧德林大臣有罪的衝動。染料配發清單涵蓋三個部門，領料紀錄表面的相似之處不能證明是某一隻手寫下偽造供述。一名在學院演出的實驗鋼琴家自願示範用來對齊印刷表格的機器。滾輪發出低沉轟聲，紙上重複圖案呈現近乎完美的對稱性。喬里注意到，只要有人偷走模板，同一台機器也能複製署名記號。檢驗員稱這是需要受控測試的可能性，還不能當成結論。

A peacock wandered across the laboratory courtyard while a guard delivered an urgent summons emphasizing the urgency of an immediate response. The palace wanted all disputed documents transferred to its internal office. Serin refused because the court's preservation order granted custody to three separate institutions. Transferring everything to the same desk would suppress the safeguards designed to prevent alteration. An adviser criticized her as shortsighted, saying she was dividing the kingdom when public unity was most important. Serin replied that to unify a country under trustworthy laws, officials must first accept limits on their own authority. She sent the adviser the applicable order and offered to discuss practical storage arrangements with representatives of the respective departments. The dispute was not personal theater; the documents could be destroyed or changed if no one could independently verify what had happened to them.

一隻孔雀穿過實驗室院子時，守衛送來一份強調必須立刻回應的緊急傳喚令。王宮要求把所有有爭議的文件轉交內部辦公室。瑟琳拒絕，因為法庭的保全令指定由三個不同機構分別保管。若全部移到同一張桌上，就會壓制原本用來防止篡改的保障措施。顧問批評她目光短淺，認為公眾最需要團結時，她卻在分裂國家。瑟琳回答，要讓國家在可信法律下統一，官員首先必須接受自己權力的限制。她把適用命令寄給顧問，也表示願與各部門代表討論實際保管安排。這不是私人表演；一旦失去獨立查證的能力，文件就可能遭毀損或竄改。

The next probe took Serin to a warehouse beside a low mound of earth near the city strait. Workers inhabited rooms above the storage sheds, and several had watched the minister's couriers deliver boxes after midnight. One woman offered a precise description of the cart, but she could not identify the driver. Serin asked whether threats had induced her to remain silent earlier. She said no; she had simply assumed the shipments were ordinary. That answer was inconvenient to those seeking a dramatic tale of terror, but it was honest. A carpenter found a missing set of printed forms in the warehouse inventory. The forms were blank, yet their margins carried the same machine marks as Elda's statement. Serin arranged independent measurements and photographed the page order. The evidence now suggested a process for manufacturing credible-looking records, not merely the act of an individual liar.

下一步調查把瑟琳帶到城市海峽旁一座低土丘附近的倉庫。工人住在儲物棚上方的房間，幾人曾看見大臣信差半夜運來箱子。一名女子精確描述馬車，卻辨不出駕車者。瑟琳問是否有人威脅她先前保持沉默。她說沒有，只是以為貨物平常無奇。這答案讓渴望恐怖情節的人失望，卻很誠實。一名木匠在倉庫清單中找到失蹤的印刷表格。表格仍然空白，但邊緣機械痕跡與艾妲證詞相同。瑟琳安排獨立測量，並拍攝頁面順序。證據如今指出一套製造看似可信紀錄的程序，不再只是某個人說謊。

Late that night, the Regent invited Serin to explain the findings in private. The meeting was polite but tense. Aven asked whether the investigator believed Odrin had organized the entire operation. Serin answered that she believed some officer had used official supplies to prepare false statements, and that the chain of authorization reached the minister's office. It would be ironic, she said, to condemn a man on inference while investigating a system that condemned people on inference. The Regent accepted the distinction and approved an external inquiry into supply orders. She also warned that the palace might lose the public's confidence before the inquiry ended. Serin did not offer reassurance she could not support. She proposed a practical timetable with weekly disclosures of verified facts and clear explanations of what remained uncertain.

深夜，攝政王私下邀瑟琳解釋發現。會談禮貌卻緊繃。艾雯問她是否相信奧德林組織了整場行動。瑟琳回答，她相信有官員利用官方物資準備假供述，授權鏈也通到大臣辦公室。她說，若在調查一套憑推論定罪的制度時，自己也憑推論判人有罪，那會十分諷刺。攝政王接受這項區別，同意成立外部用品採購調查，也警告宮廷可能在調查結束前就失去公眾信任。瑟琳沒有給出無憑據的保證，只提出每週公布已證實事實的實際時程，並清楚說明仍存的疑點。

Alone afterward, Serin drew two columns in her notebook. In one she recorded what the witnesses had directly observed: paper, a cart, a rehearsed phrase, a changed index. In the other she wrote the claims they could not yet prove: who authorized the substitution and why the archive had burned. The second column was longer, and its length frightened her. She had once believed the work of an investigator was to uncover a hidden truth in a single brilliant moment. Now she understood that her vocation consisted mainly of preventing the brilliant moment from misleading her. Even if she never discovered the highest official responsible, she could still protect the people harmed by the false records and repair the procedures that allowed them. The question was whether the palace would permit that repair when it began to reach powerful names.

之後獨處時，瑟琳在筆記本畫出兩欄。一欄列出證人親眼所見的東西：紙張、馬車、排練過的句子、遭更改的索引。另一欄列出尚無法證明的主張：誰授權替換，以及檔案庫為何燃燒。第二欄更長，使她害怕。她曾以為調查官的工作，是在某個靈光乍現的瞬間揭開隱藏真相，如今卻明白，這份志業主要在於防止靈光一現把自己帶錯方向。即使永遠找不到地位最高的責任者，她仍可以保護遭假紀錄傷害的人，並修補讓偽造得逞的程序。問題是，當修補開始觸及有權有勢的名字時，宮廷是否還願意允許。
