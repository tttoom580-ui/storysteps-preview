比基地更古老的訊號

A Signal Older Than the Colony

In 2149, lunar communications engineer Lin Yue received a distress call during the south-pole colony's night shift. The first sign of trouble was not a scream. It was a pause in an otherwise predictable routine, long enough for a careful person to notice. Others kept moving, and their movement made it tempting to do the same, but the pause deserved an explanation. Nara listened to the ancient pulses through a filter that stripped away modern noise. A witness mentioned acid during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to assembly near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable. The broom appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it.

2149年，月球南極殖民地通訊工程師林玥在夜班收到一段求救訊號。第一個麻煩徵兆不是尖叫，而是原本可預期的流程忽然停頓，長到足以讓細心的人察覺。其他人繼續前進，令人忍不住跟上，可那次停頓需要解釋。奈良隔著濾除現代雜訊的裝置聽古代脈衝。見證人在覆核時提到「胃酸」，奈良保留了原話。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「集會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊訊號紀錄出現了「掃帚」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。

Its carrier emerged from beneath the crater, and its internal timestamp was almost two thousand years old. The records did not tell a single story. One column described an intention; another showed what happened after real people entered the space. The difference was inconvenient, especially for those who had already spent money defending the original plan. Inconvenience was not a reason to erase it. Fine lunar dust settled over the rover tracks and made the distance seem endless. Before they could construct a safe model, the group needed a witness who was free to object. One instruction told the team to cooperate with independent witnesses; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned copper during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to counter near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable.

載波來自隕石坑下方，內部時間戳記接近兩千年前。紀錄沒有說出單一故事。一欄描述意圖，另一欄顯示真實的人進入空間後發生了什麼。差異令人不便，尤其是那些已花錢維護原計畫的人。不便卻不是刪掉差異的理由。細月塵落在探測車軌上，讓距離看似無盡。在能夠「建構」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。一項指令要求團隊「合作」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「銅製的」，奈良保留了原話。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「櫃臺」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

She saved the raw waveform instead of broadcasting the extraordinary claim to Earth. The most powerful person present could have ended the argument with an order. That might even have made the next hour more efficient. It would not have made the order wise. Instead, questions were allowed to remain questions long enough for another person to object. The ancient chamber was quiet enough to hear the suit fans turning. The coward appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could entertain the waiting visitors, the group needed a witness who was free to object. Lin Yue disliked the word facial in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. Nara proposed they interfere with the signal; Lin Yue wanted the risk written down first.

她先保存原始波形，沒有立刻向地球宣告驚人發現。在場最有權力的人本可一聲令下結束爭論，甚至讓下一小時更有效率，但命令不會因此變明智。他們讓問題繼續存在，給另一個人提出異議的時間。古老艙室安靜得聽得見太空衣風扇轉動。舊訊號紀錄出現了「膽小鬼」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「娛樂」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。林玥不喜歡摘要中的「臉部的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。奈良提議「干擾」，林玥希望先把風險寫清楚。

The signal repeated one clear request: open the chamber before the last lamp fails. For a moment the whole plan seemed to fail. The expected signal never arrived, and the people waiting outside grew impatient. Then a smaller detail emerged, one that would have been lost if the group had covered the mistake with a confident announcement. A caution light shone along the rim of the unpressurized tunnel. They found a reference to ministry near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable. The opera appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it. The warning sounded persuasive to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain.

訊號重複一項清楚請求：最後一盞燈熄滅前打開艙室。有一刻整個計畫像要失敗。預期訊號沒有來，外面等候的人越來越不耐。隨後出現更小的細節；若團隊用自信公告掩蓋錯誤，那個細節就會消失。一盞警示燈沿著無氣壓隧道邊緣亮起。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「部會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊訊號紀錄出現了「歌劇院」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「有說服力的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。

A small act of care interrupted the discussion. It solved no mystery and changed no official ruling. Yet it reminded them that the people named in the records were not pieces on a board; they had bodies, families and the right to decide what came next. Lin Yue glanced toward Nara and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: rescue cannot demand ownership. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Lin Yue checked the oxygen gauge before looking at the extraordinary signal again. The team considered the phenomenon a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe. A witness mentioned route during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk shady, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted.

一個小小的照顧動作打斷討論。它沒有解開謎，也沒改變裁決，卻提醒大家紀錄裡的人不是棋子；他們有身體、家庭，也有決定下一步的權利。林玥望向奈良，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：救援不該要求所有權。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。林玥再次看驚人的訊號前，先確認氧氣錶。團隊把「現象」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「路線」，奈良保留了原話。報告把風險標為「可疑的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

Before leaving, they checked the names once more. Names were easier to lose than equipment, especially when a system reduced people to numbers. The check took little time. It kept the next chapter of the problem from beginning with a convenient omission. Lin Yue knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The signal log would preserve one more question, and Nara would be there to challenge the first easy answer. No one could replace a broken seal simply by wishing the rescue to succeed. The slogan appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the usage; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin. Lin Yue disliked the word youthful in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

離開前，他們再次核對名字。名字比設備更容易消失，尤其當系統把人簡化成數字。檢查花不了多少時間，卻避免問題的下一章從方便的遺漏開始。林玥知道明天不會從零開始。訊號紀錄將保存又一個問題，奈良也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。沒有人能光靠盼望救援成功，就取代破損的密封圈。舊訊號紀錄出現了「口號」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「使用情況」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。林玥不喜歡摘要中的「年輕的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。
