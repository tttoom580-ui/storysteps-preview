The corporate drilling crew arrived with machinery capable of removing the entire chamber. The plan looked respectable at first glance. It had dates, names, and a signature in the proper corner. But paper can be complete without being honest, and a polished surface does not remove the cost carried by those who have no place to object. Fine lunar dust settled over the rover tracks and made the distance seem endless. The acceptance appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it. The warning sounded ambitious to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the atmosphere a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe.

企業鑽探隊帶著能搬走整座艙室的重型機具抵達。計畫乍看很正式，有日期、姓名，簽名也在正確角落。然而文件可以完整卻不誠實；漂亮的外觀不能消除無處提出異議的人所承擔的代價。細月塵落在探測車軌上，讓距離看似無盡。舊訊號紀錄出現了「接受；同意」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「有企圖心的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「氣氛」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。

Moving the chamber would sever the ancient cooling pipes and destroy the seed bank. A neat explanation would have spared everyone an argument. Instead, the evidence refused to be neat. It contained a gap large enough to change the meaning of what came before it. Filling the gap with a guess would have been quick, and unkind to anyone trusting the answer. The ancient chamber was quiet enough to hear the suit fans turning. A witness called the result authentic; Nara wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. It might help to classify the report, but Lin Yue refused to mistake possibility for proof. The plan called for them to differ from the first account, and Lin Yue asked who would check the result. Nobody disputed the dignity; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin.

搬走艙室會切斷古老冷卻管，毀掉種子庫。整齊的解釋本可省去爭吵，但證據偏偏不整齊。裡面有一道足以改變先前意義的缺口。拿猜測補起來雖快，對信任答案的人卻不公平。古老艙室安靜得聽得見太空衣風扇轉動。見證人稱結果「真實可靠的」，奈良把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。「分類」或許有幫助，林玥卻拒絕把可能當成證明。計畫要求他們「有所不同」，林玥追問由誰查核結果。沒人質疑「尊嚴」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

Lin Yue issued a documented safety objection instead of confronting the crew physically. The decision required a kind of patience that was easily mistaken for fear. Their tools were limited and the timetable already slipping. Yet the people affected had not agreed to be used as proof that an ambitious plan could work. A caution light shone along the rim of the unpressurized tunnel. One instruction told the team to embarrass the staff publicly; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned facility during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk genuine, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The public account described the situation as offensive, but Lin Yue asked for the observation behind that claim.

林玥提出有紀錄的安全異議，而非直接與工人衝突。這個決定需要一種容易被誤認為害怕的耐心。工具有限，時程也在延後，但受影響的人並沒有同意當作證明野心計畫可行的材料。一盞警示燈沿著無氣壓隧道邊緣亮起。一項指令要求團隊「使人難堪」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「設施」，奈良保留了原話。報告把風險標為「真實的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。公開說法把情況形容為「惡意的」，林玥要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。

Work paused for one lunar day while an independent review examined the risk. For a moment the whole plan seemed to fail. The expected signal never arrived, and the people waiting outside grew impatient. Then a smaller detail emerged, one that would have been lost if the group had covered the mistake with a confident announcement. Lin Yue checked the oxygen gauge before looking at the extraordinary signal again. Nobody disputed the option; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin. The team considered the perfume a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe. A witness mentioned promotion during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording.

工作暫停一個月球日，由獨立審查檢視風險。有一刻整個計畫像要失敗。預期訊號沒有來，外面等候的人越來越不耐。隨後出現更小的細節；若團隊用自信公告掩蓋錯誤，那個細節就會消失。林玥再次看驚人的訊號前，先確認氧氣錶。沒人質疑「選項」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「香水」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「行銷」，奈良保留了原話。

The work left marks that a final report would never show: a dented cup, an unfinished meal, a message that had to be sent to somebody waiting at home. These things did not prove a theory. They explained why getting the theory right mattered. Lin Yue glanced toward Nara and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: rescue cannot demand ownership. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. No one could replace a broken seal simply by wishing the rescue to succeed. They found a reference to publisher near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable. The refugee appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the rescue; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin.

工作留下最終報告不會顯示的痕跡：凹掉的杯子、沒吃完的飯、必須傳給在家等候者的訊息。這些不能證明理論，卻說明理論為什麼必須正確。林玥望向奈良，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：救援不該要求所有權。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。沒有人能光靠盼望救援成功，就取代破損的密封圈。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「出版社」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊訊號紀錄出現了「難民」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「救援」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

Before leaving, they checked the names once more. Names were easier to lose than equipment, especially when a system reduced people to numbers. The check took little time. It kept the next chapter of the problem from beginning with a convenient omission. Lin Yue knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The signal log would preserve one more question, and Nara would be there to challenge the first easy answer. The cooling pipes ran beneath the dust like veins in a sleeping machine. The team considered the resignation a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe. A witness mentioned stocking during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording. It might help to transform the flawed arrangement, but Lin Yue refused to mistake possibility for proof.

離開前，他們再次核對名字。名字比設備更容易消失，尤其當系統把人簡化成數字。檢查花不了多少時間，卻避免問題的下一章從方便的遺漏開始。林玥知道明天不會從零開始。訊號紀錄將保存又一個問題，奈良也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。冷卻管在塵土下延伸，像沉睡機械的血管。團隊把「辭職；辭職信」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「絲襪；長襪」，奈良保留了原話。「轉變」或許有幫助，林玥卻拒絕把可能當成證明。
