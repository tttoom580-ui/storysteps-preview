An unmanned craft appeared over the horizon and stopped outside the colony's protected airspace. The first sign of trouble was not a scream. It was a pause in an otherwise predictable routine, long enough for a careful person to notice. Others kept moving, and their movement made it tempting to do the same, but the pause deserved an explanation. Omar refused to trust a beautiful diagram until the pressure readings agreed. The warning sounded absolute to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the discipline a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe. A witness mentioned distribution during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording.

一艘無人飛行器出現在地平線，停在基地保護空域外。第一個麻煩徵兆不是尖叫，而是原本可預期的流程忽然停頓，長到足以讓細心的人察覺。其他人繼續前進，令人忍不住跟上，可那次停頓需要解釋。奧瑪不肯相信漂亮圖面，除非壓力讀值相符。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「絕對的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「紀律」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「分布」，奈良保留了原話。

It carried the same ancient symbol as the archive but did not respond to spoken commands. The easiest part of the investigation was drawing a line between two points. The difficult part was deciding what that line meant. It might show a connection, a coincidence, or an error copied from an older file. No one wanted to spend another day checking. Nara listened to the ancient pulses through a filter that stripped away modern noise. They found a reference to dread near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable. The efficiency appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could fasten the loose cover, the group needed a witness who was free to object. Lin Yue disliked the word gracious in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

它帶著與檔案相同的古老符號，卻不回應口語命令。調查最容易的部分，是在兩個點之間畫線。困難的是決定那條線的意思。它可能代表關聯、巧合，也可能是從舊檔複製的錯誤。沒有人想再花一天查證。奈良隔著濾除現代雜訊的裝置聽古代脈衝。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「恐懼」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊訊號紀錄出現了「效率」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「固定」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。林玥不喜歡摘要中的「親切的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。

Lin Yue transmitted the validated pulse alphabet and waited without firing a defensive shot. They argued quietly because shouting would only give the nearby cameras and strangers another story to tell. Beneath the disagreement lay a practical question: who would bear the consequences if the confident person turned out to be mistaken? No one volunteered to answer for somebody else. Fine lunar dust settled over the rover tracks and made the distance seem endless. A witness mentioned grief during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to hardware near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable. The hawk appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the illustration; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin.

林玥發送已驗證的脈衝字母，等待而沒有開火。他們壓低聲音爭辯，因為大喊只會讓旁邊的鏡頭與陌生人又多一個故事。爭論底下是實際問題：自信的人若弄錯，誰承擔後果？沒有人願意代替別人回答。細月塵落在探測車軌上，讓距離看似無盡。見證人在覆核時提到「悲傷」，奈良保留了原話。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「電子硬體」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊訊號紀錄出現了「老鷹」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「插圖」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The visitor projected a map of the missing cooling junctions. A surprising number of people had been waiting for that small result. Some had hoped to hear reassuring words; others needed permission to act. The result offered neither comfort nor permission by itself. It gave them a narrower, more honest problem to solve. The ancient chamber was quiet enough to hear the suit fans turning. One instruction told the team to insert the missing page; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned miner during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk oval, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted.

訪客投射出缺失冷卻接點的地圖。有令人意外的多人一直等著這個小結果。有人想聽安慰，有人需要行動許可。但結果本身既不提供安慰也不提供許可，只讓問題縮小、也更誠實。古老艙室安靜得聽得見太空衣風扇轉動。一項指令要求團隊「插入」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「礦工」，奈良保留了原話。報告把風險標為「橢圓形的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

A small act of care interrupted the discussion. It solved no mystery and changed no official ruling. Yet it reminded them that the people named in the records were not pieces on a board; they had bodies, families and the right to decide what came next. Lin Yue glanced toward Nara and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: rescue cannot demand ownership. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. A caution light shone along the rim of the unpressurized tunnel. The perfection appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it. The warning sounded realistic to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the recognition a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe.

一個小小的照顧動作打斷討論。它沒有解開謎，也沒改變裁決，卻提醒大家紀錄裡的人不是棋子；他們有身體、家庭，也有決定下一步的權利。林玥望向奈良，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：救援不該要求所有權。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。一盞警示燈沿著無氣壓隧道邊緣亮起。舊訊號紀錄出現了「完美」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「實際可行的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「辨識」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。

They filed the result beside the earlier mistakes, not above them. Tomorrow would require a different question and perhaps a different kind of courage. For now, the important thing was that the people most affected could read what had happened and challenge the account if it was wrong. Lin Yue knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The signal log would preserve one more question, and Nara would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Lin Yue checked the oxygen gauge before looking at the extraordinary signal again. Nara proposed they renew the inspection; Lin Yue wanted the risk written down first. It might help to resolve the dispute fairly, but Lin Yue refused to mistake possibility for proof. The vessel appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it.

他們把結果放在先前的錯誤旁邊，而不是蓋過錯誤。明天需要不同的問題，也許需要不同的勇氣。眼下最重要的是受影響者能讀到經過，也能在紀錄錯誤時提出挑戰。林玥知道明天不會從零開始。訊號紀錄將保存又一個問題，奈良也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。林玥再次看驚人的訊號前，先確認氧氣錶。奈良提議「更新；恢復」，林玥希望先把風險寫清楚。「決定」或許有幫助，林玥卻拒絕把可能當成證明。舊訊號紀錄出現了「容器」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。
