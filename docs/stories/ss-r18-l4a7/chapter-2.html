塵土下的時鐘

The Clock Beneath the Dust

Lin Yue and geologist Omar drove a rover toward a fissure near an abandoned survey post. It began with something that could be held in one hand, a small object too ordinary to attract attention. Yet the longer they looked, the less ordinary it became. The real problem was not its appearance but the decision someone had already made on its behalf. Omar refused to trust a beautiful diagram until the pressure readings agreed. Nobody disputed the autograph; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to carve a careful mark; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness mentioned convenience during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording.

林玥與地質學家奧瑪駕車前往廢棄測站附近的裂縫。事情從一個單手能拿起的小物件開始，普通得幾乎沒人會注意。可看得越久，它就越不尋常。真正的問題不在外表，而是有人早已代替它做出了決定。奧瑪不肯相信漂亮圖面，除非壓力讀值相符。沒人質疑「親筆簽名」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「刻出」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人在覆核時提到「便利」，奈良保留了原話。

A quartz clock buried in the dust ticked without any visible solar source. The easiest part of the investigation was drawing a line between two points. The difficult part was deciding what that line meant. It might show a connection, a coincidence, or an error copied from an older file. No one wanted to spend another day checking. Nara listened to the ancient pulses through a filter that stripped away modern noise. They found a reference to cruelty near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable. The plan called for them to disguise the source of the message, and Lin Yue asked who would check the result. The warning sounded elastic to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. Lin Yue disliked the word explosive in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

埋在塵土中的石英時鐘沒有可見太陽能來源，卻持續走動。調查最容易的部分，是在兩個點之間畫線。困難的是決定那條線的意思。它可能代表關聯、巧合，也可能是從舊檔複製的錯誤。沒有人想再花一天查證。奈良隔著濾除現代雜訊的裝置聽古代脈衝。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「殘忍」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。計畫要求他們「偽裝」，林玥追問由誰查核結果。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「有彈性的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。林玥不喜歡摘要中的「爆炸性的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。

They marked a safe perimeter and collected remote samples before approaching the clock. They argued quietly because shouting would only give the nearby cameras and strangers another story to tell. Beneath the disagreement lay a practical question: who would bear the consequences if the confident person turned out to be mistaken? No one volunteered to answer for somebody else. Fine lunar dust settled over the rover tracks and made the distance seem endless. A witness mentioned feedback during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to immigrant near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable. The public account described the situation as initial, but Lin Yue asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the mischief; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin.

兩人先畫出安全範圍、遠距採樣，才接近時鐘。他們壓低聲音爭辯，因為大喊只會讓旁邊的鏡頭與陌生人又多一個故事。爭論底下是實際問題：自信的人若弄錯，誰承擔後果？沒有人願意代替別人回答。細月塵落在探測車軌上，讓距離看似無盡。見證人在覆核時提到「回饋」，奈良保留了原話。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「移民」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。公開說法把情況形容為「最初的」，林玥要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「惡作劇」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

A second signal answered their probe with a pattern that matched an ancient lunar eclipse. The first result was neither victory nor defeat. It removed one false assumption and exposed another problem that had been concealed beneath it. A witness asked whether the discovery could be trusted. The answer was that the test could be repeated, not that the investigators were special. The ancient chamber was quiet enough to hear the suit fans turning. The team considered the paw a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe. A witness mentioned prevention during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to privilege near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable.

第二段訊號以古代月食的模式回應探測器。第一個結果既非勝利也非失敗。它排除一個錯誤假設，也露出下面藏著的另一個問題。見證人問發現能否相信；答案是測試可以重複，不是調查者有什麼特權。古老艙室安靜得聽得見太空衣風扇轉動。團隊把「爪子」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「預防」，奈良保留了原話。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「特權」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

Later, when the noise softened, the hardest detail was not written in the official account. It was the hesitation before an ordinary question, the way someone waited for permission to say they were tired. No one in the group could give back every hour already lost. Lin Yue glanced toward Nara and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: rescue cannot demand ownership. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. A caution light shone along the rim of the unpressurized tunnel. The public account described the situation as prominent, but Lin Yue asked for the observation behind that claim. Before they could prosper without taking unfair advantage, the group needed a witness who was free to object. The team considered the remark a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe.

後來噪音緩下來，最難忘的細節不在正式報告裡，而是平凡提問前的遲疑，以及有人等著被允許說自己累了的模樣。團隊沒有人能還回失去的每個小時。林玥望向奈良，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：救援不該要求所有權。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。一盞警示燈沿著無氣壓隧道邊緣亮起。公開說法把情況形容為「醒目的」，林玥要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。在能夠「繁榮」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。團隊把「評論；言語」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。

There would be easier stories to tell about this day. In those stories a brave person would find one clue, name one enemy and fix everything at once. The truth they carried was more difficult: a choice could be necessary and still leave work for the morning. Lin Yue knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The signal log would preserve one more question, and Nara would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Lin Yue checked the oxygen gauge before looking at the extraordinary signal again. Nara proposed they ruin the fragile evidence; Lin Yue wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to shift near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable. The timetable appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it.

關於今天，可以講得更簡單：勇敢的人找到一個線索，指出一個敵人，一切立刻解決。他們帶走的真相更難：一個選擇可以是必要的，也仍會留下明早要處理的工作。林玥知道明天不會從零開始。訊號紀錄將保存又一個問題，奈良也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。林玥再次看驚人的訊號前，先確認氧氣錶。奈良提議「毀壞」，林玥希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「輪班」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊訊號紀錄出現了「時刻表」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。
