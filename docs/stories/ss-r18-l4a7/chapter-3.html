The Calendar That Should Not Exist

月球殖民地收到古代求救訊號 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Historian Nara compared the eclipse pattern with observations recorded on Earth nearly two millennia earlier. Before anyone spoke, the room was divided between those who had been promised safety and those expected to provide it. The distinction was familiar, and that was precisely why it worried the people who had to act. Familiar arrangements could hide old mistakes remarkably well. The colony windows reflected the lights of a home still being built. The report labeled the risk accidental, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The barrier appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it. Before they could blink at the sudden light, the group needed a witness who was free to object.

歷史學者奈良把月食模式與地球近兩千年前的觀測紀錄比對。尚未有人開口，房間便分成被承諾安全的人，以及被期待提供安全的人。這種分別太熟悉，反而令必須行動的人不安。看似理所當然的安排，很容易藏住 오래以前的錯誤。基地窗戶反射出一個仍在建造中的家園燈光。報告把風險標為「意外的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。舊訊號紀錄出現了「柵欄」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。在能夠「眨眼」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。

The dates aligned, but the signal described a lunar shadow from the wrong side of the Moon. They compared the new account with what could be measured independently. It was an unglamorous exercise, made of repeated readings and questions that did not impress an audience. The result mattered precisely because it might prove the people asking those questions were wrong. Omar refused to trust a beautiful diagram until the pressure readings agreed. Lin Yue disliked the word brutal in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. Nara proposed they choke on the thick dust; Lin Yue wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to composition near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable. The plan called for them to evaluate the new measurements, and Lin Yue asked who would check the result.

日期吻合，訊號卻從月球背面描述月影。他們把新說法與能獨立測量的事情比較。這不光鮮，只是重複讀值與不會取悅觀眾的提問。結果重要，正因為它也可能證明提問者自己錯了。奧瑪不肯相信漂亮圖面，除非壓力讀值相符。林玥不喜歡摘要中的「粗獷而堅固的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。奈良提議「阻斷」，林玥希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「結構；組成」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。計畫要求他們「評估」，林玥追問由誰查核結果。

She proposed a second astronomical check rather than calling the artifact human or alien. The most powerful person present could have ended the argument with an order. That might even have made the next hour more efficient. It would not have made the order wise. Instead, questions were allowed to remain questions long enough for another person to object. Nara listened to the ancient pulses through a filter that stripped away modern noise. Nobody disputed the gratitude; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin. The team considered the grave a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe. A witness mentioned intelligence during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk mild, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted.

她要求再做一次天文核對，不急著把遺物稱為人類或外星造物。在場最有權力的人本可一聲令下結束爭論，甚至讓下一小時更有效率，但命令不會因此變明智。他們讓問題繼續存在，給另一個人提出異議的時間。奈良隔著濾除現代雜訊的裝置聽古代脈衝。沒人質疑「感激」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「嚴重的」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「情報」，奈良保留了原話。報告把風險標為「溫和的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

The check confirmed that something had been observing from the Moon in antiquity. They repeated the procedure with a different observer. It took longer and yielded a less dramatic account than the first version. That was an advantage: a discovery dependent on one person’s performance would have been too fragile to guide the next dangerous step. Fine lunar dust settled over the rover tracks and made the distance seem endless. The public account described the situation as mountainous, but Lin Yue asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the neglect; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to offend the people who trusted them; nobody could explain why it was urgent.

驗證證實古代確實曾有某種存在從月球觀察。他們換一位觀察者重做程序。耗時更久，結果也不如第一版戲劇化。這反而有利：如果發現只依靠一個人的表現，就太脆弱，不足以指引下一個危險步驟。細月塵落在探測車軌上，讓距離看似無盡。公開說法把情況形容為「山區的」，林玥要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「疏忽」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「冒犯」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。

Later, when the noise softened, the hardest detail was not written in the official account. It was the hesitation before an ordinary question, the way someone waited for permission to say they were tired. No one in the group could give back every hour already lost. Lin Yue glanced toward Nara and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: rescue cannot demand ownership. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. The ancient chamber was quiet enough to hear the suit fans turning. A witness mentioned philosopher during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to reform near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable. The revenge appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it.

後來噪音緩下來，最難忘的細節不在正式報告裡，而是平凡提問前的遲疑，以及有人等著被允許說自己累了的模樣。團隊沒有人能還回失去的每個小時。林玥望向奈良，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：救援不該要求所有權。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。古老艙室安靜得聽得見太空衣風扇轉動。見證人在覆核時提到「哲學家」，奈良保留了原話。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「改革」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊訊號紀錄出現了「報復」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。

Before leaving, they checked the names once more. Names were easier to lose than equipment, especially when a system reduced people to numbers. The check took little time. It kept the next chapter of the problem from beginning with a convenient omission. Lin Yue knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The signal log would preserve one more question, and Nara would be there to challenge the first easy answer. A caution light shone along the rim of the unpressurized tunnel. Nobody disputed the tendency; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin. The team considered the twig a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe. A witness mentioned urge during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording.

離開前，他們再次核對名字。名字比設備更容易消失，尤其當系統把人簡化成數字。檢查花不了多少時間，卻避免問題的下一章從方便的遺漏開始。林玥知道明天不會從零開始。訊號紀錄將保存又一個問題，奈良也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。一盞警示燈沿著無氣壓隧道邊緣亮起。沒人質疑「傾向」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「樹枝」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「催促」，奈良保留了原話。
