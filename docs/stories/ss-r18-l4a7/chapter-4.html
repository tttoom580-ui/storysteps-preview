一小時氧氣

One Hour of Oxygen

月球殖民地收到古代求救訊號 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 3000–4000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A rover seal cracked as the team prepared to enter the buried service tunnel. The plan looked respectable at first glance. It had dates, names, and a signature in the proper corner. But paper can be complete without being honest, and a polished surface does not remove the cost carried by those who have no place to object. Earth hung above the horizon like a promise nobody on the Moon could take for granted. One instruction told the team to accomplish the limited repair; nobody could explain why it was urgent. Nara proposed they amuse the tired children; Lin Yue wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to approval near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable.

團隊準備進入地下維修隧道時，探測車密封圈破裂。計畫乍看很正式，有日期、姓名，簽名也在正確角落。然而文件可以完整卻不誠實；漂亮的外觀不能消除無處提出異議的人所承擔的代價。地球懸在地平線上，像月球上沒有人能理所當然得到的承諾。一項指令要求團隊「成就；完成」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。奈良提議「逗樂；令人發笑」，林玥希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「核准」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

The colony had spare oxygen for the return trip but not for an improvised excavation. The records did not tell a single story. One column described an intention; another showed what happened after real people entered the space. The difference was inconvenient, especially for those who had already spent money defending the original plan. Inconvenience was not a reason to erase it. The colony windows reflected the lights of a home still being built. The plan called for them to bounce off the sealed door, and Lin Yue asked who would check the result. The warning sounded continual to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. One instruction told the team to disappoint the volunteers; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness called the result earnest; Nara wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name.

基地備用氧氣足夠返程，卻不足以臨時開挖。紀錄沒有說出單一故事。一欄描述意圖，另一欄顯示真實的人進入空間後發生了什麼。差異令人不便，尤其是那些已花錢維護原計畫的人。不便卻不是刪掉差異的理由。基地窗戶反射出一個仍在建造中的家園燈光。計畫要求他們「彈跳」，林玥追問由誰查核結果。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「持續的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。一項指令要求團隊「失望」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人稱結果「認真的」，奈良把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。

Lin Yue ordered everyone back and sent a smaller robotic probe instead. They argued quietly because shouting would only give the nearby cameras and strangers another story to tell. Beneath the disagreement lay a practical question: who would bear the consequences if the confident person turned out to be mistaken? No one volunteered to answer for somebody else. Omar refused to trust a beautiful diagram until the pressure readings agreed. They found a reference to embassy near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable. The emphasis appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the librarian; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin. The team considered the margin a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe.

林玥要求所有人返回，改派小型機器探針。他們壓低聲音爭辯，因為大喊只會讓旁邊的鏡頭與陌生人又多一個故事。爭論底下是實際問題：自信的人若弄錯，誰承擔後果？沒有人願意代替別人回答。奧瑪不肯相信漂亮圖面，除非壓力讀值相符。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「大使館」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊訊號紀錄出現了「重點」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「圖書管理員」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「頁邊」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。

The probe transmitted a drawing of a chamber surrounded by intact cryogenic containers. They repeated the procedure with a different observer. It took longer and yielded a less dramatic account than the first version. That was an advantage: a discovery dependent on one person’s performance would have been too fragile to guide the next dangerous step. Nara listened to the ancient pulses through a filter that stripped away modern noise. A witness mentioned murderer during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to nonsense near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable. The orbit appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it.

探針傳回一幅艙室圖，周圍排列著完整的低溫容器。他們換一位觀察者重做程序。耗時更久，結果也不如第一版戲劇化。這反而有利：如果發現只依靠一個人的表現，就太脆弱，不足以指引下一個危險步驟。奈良隔著濾除現代雜訊的裝置聽古代脈衝。見證人在覆核時提到「殺人犯」，奈良保留了原話。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「荒謬的事」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊訊號紀錄出現了「運行軌道」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。

The place was still full of ordinary life. Someone laughed at a poor joke, someone swept a corner, and a tired worker counted the minutes to the next shift. Those details made the danger harder to ignore, because the people involved had lives outside this puzzle. Lin Yue glanced toward Nara and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: rescue cannot demand ownership. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Fine lunar dust settled over the rover tracks and made the distance seem endless. The warning sounded passive to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain. The team considered the possession a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe. A witness mentioned researcher during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording.

這地方仍充滿平凡生活。有人為難笑話發笑，有人掃地，疲憊的工作者算著距離換班還有幾分鐘。這些細節讓危險更難忽略，因為受影響者在謎題之外還有人生。林玥望向奈良，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：救援不該要求所有權。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。細月塵落在探測車軌上，讓距離看似無盡。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「被動的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。團隊把「擁有權」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「研究員」，奈良保留了原話。

There would be easier stories to tell about this day. In those stories a brave person would find one clue, name one enemy and fix everything at once. The truth they carried was more difficult: a choice could be necessary and still leave work for the morning. Lin Yue knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The signal log would preserve one more question, and Nara would be there to challenge the first easy answer. The ancient chamber was quiet enough to hear the suit fans turning. They found a reference to rhyme near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable. The translator appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it. The warning sounded urgent to some listeners, although the actual measurements remained uncertain.

關於今天，可以講得更簡單：勇敢的人找到一個線索，指出一個敵人，一切立刻解決。他們帶走的真相更難：一個選擇可以是必要的，也仍會留下明早要處理的工作。林玥知道明天不會從零開始。訊號紀錄將保存又一個問題，奈良也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。古老艙室安靜得聽得見太空衣風扇轉動。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「童謠」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊訊號紀錄出現了「翻譯員」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。對部分聽眾而言，警告聽起來「緊急的」，實際測量卻仍有疑點。
