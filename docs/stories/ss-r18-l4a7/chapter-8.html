A Journey Written in Ice

Inside a sealed ice layer, Nara discovered a star map etched with an unfamiliar route. Nobody in the room knew that this would become a turning point. The ordinary sounds of work continued around them, almost offensively calm, while an uncertain fact asked to be checked. A confident announcement would have been easier than the slower work of asking who might be hurt. A caution light shone along the rim of the unpressurized tunnel. They found a reference to ancestor near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable. The contrary appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the craft; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin.

奈良在封住的冰層裡發現一張刻著陌生航路的星圖。房裡沒有人知道這將是轉折。周圍平凡的工作聲仍持續著，平靜得幾乎令人不安，而一個未經確定的事實正等待查核。高聲宣布結論遠比緩慢詢問誰可能受傷來得容易。一盞警示燈沿著無氣壓隧道邊緣亮起。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「祖先」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊訊號紀錄出現了「正好相反」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「手工技藝」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The route described a small craft departing the Moon for Earth nearly two thousand years earlier. They compared the new account with what could be measured independently. It was an unglamorous exercise, made of repeated readings and questions that did not impress an audience. The result mattered precisely because it might prove the people asking those questions were wrong. Lin Yue checked the oxygen gauge before looking at the extraordinary signal again. The team considered the crush a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe. A witness called the result defensible; Nara wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. The report labeled the risk distinguished, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The plan called for them to equip the rescue team, and Lin Yue asked who would check the result.

路線記載近兩千年前有艘小型飛船從月球前往地球。他們把新說法與能獨立測量的事情比較。這不光鮮，只是重複讀值與不會取悅觀眾的提問。結果重要，正因為它也可能證明提問者自己錯了。林玥再次看驚人的訊號前，先確認氧氣錶。團隊把「擠壓；造成壓傷」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。見證人稱結果「有充分理由的」，奈良把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。報告把風險標為「傑出的；精湛的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。計畫要求他們「器材」，林玥追問由誰查核結果。

She compared the design with known history without claiming that any ancient civilization had built the craft. A refusal sounded small compared with the danger outside. Even so, it changed what the group understood about its limits. No one should have to give up a private history, a safe route, or an honest answer merely to prove they were cooperative. No one could replace a broken seal simply by wishing the rescue to succeed. Nobody disputed the guilt; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin. The team considered the handwriting a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe. A witness mentioned hose during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording. The report labeled the risk identical, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted.

她把設計與已知歷史比較，不宣稱某個古文明建造過飛船。面對外面的危險，一次拒絕聽來很微小，卻改變了團隊對界線的理解。沒有人必須放棄私人經歷、安全路線或誠實答案，才能證明自己願意合作。沒有人能光靠盼望救援成功，就取代破損的密封圈。沒人質疑「愧疚」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「字跡」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「水管」，奈良保留了原話。報告把風險標為「完全相同的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。

The caretaker revealed that its builders were travelers from another system, not humans of that era. The room did not erupt in celebration. A few people checked the figures again, others examined the door, and somebody quietly asked who would pay for the repairs. That question belonged with the evidence. A clever answer was of little use if nobody could maintain it. The cooling pipes ran beneath the dust like veins in a sleeping machine. The plan called for them to involve the people affected, and Lin Yue asked who would check the result. Before they could launch the review, the group needed a witness who was free to object. Lin Yue disliked the word lousy in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

照護程式揭露建造者是來自其他星系的旅人，並非當時的人類。房間沒有爆發歡呼。幾人重新核對數字，其他人檢查門，還有人低聲問誰出維修費。那問題和證據同樣重要。若無人能維持，再聰明的答案也沒有用。冷卻管在塵土下延伸，像沉睡機械的血管。計畫要求他們「涉及；參與」，林玥追問由誰查核結果。在能夠「推出；啟動」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。林玥不喜歡摘要中的「品質差的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。

After the argument, a long silence settled around the group. It was not agreement. People were calculating what the next attempt might take from them, and whether anyone would notice the loss. Their caution was an ordinary human response, not a flaw in their courage. Lin Yue glanced toward Nara and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: rescue cannot demand ownership. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Earth hung above the horizon like a promise nobody on the Moon could take for granted. Nara proposed they misunderstand the signal; Lin Yue wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to pregnancy near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable. The riddle appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it.

爭論後，長久沉默籠罩大家。那不是同意。人們正在估算下一次嘗試會奪走什麼、又會不會有人注意。謹慎是平凡的人性反應，不是勇氣的缺點。林玥望向奈良，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：救援不該要求所有權。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。地球懸在地平線上，像月球上沒有人能理所當然得到的承諾。奈良提議「誤會」，林玥希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「懷孕」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊訊號紀錄出現了「謎題」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。

There would be easier stories to tell about this day. In those stories a brave person would find one clue, name one enemy and fix everything at once. The truth they carried was more difficult: a choice could be necessary and still leave work for the morning. Lin Yue knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The signal log would preserve one more question, and Nara would be there to challenge the first easy answer. The colony windows reflected the lights of a home still being built. Nobody disputed the stroke; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin. The team considered the suggestion a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe. A witness mentioned technician during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording.

關於今天，可以講得更簡單：勇敢的人找到一個線索，指出一個敵人，一切立刻解決。他們帶走的真相更難：一個選擇可以是必要的，也仍會留下明早要處理的工作。林玥知道明天不會從零開始。訊號紀錄將保存又一個問題，奈良也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。基地窗戶反射出一個仍在建造中的家園燈光。沒人質疑「一筆」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「建議」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「技術員」，奈良保留了原話。
