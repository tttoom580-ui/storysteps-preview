The probe translated repeated pulses into a request for visible light rather than more electrical power. Nobody in the room knew that this would become a turning point. The ordinary sounds of work continued around them, almost offensively calm, while an uncertain fact asked to be checked. A confident announcement would have been easier than the slower work of asking who might be hurt. No one could replace a broken seal simply by wishing the rescue to succeed. A witness mentioned anniversary during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording. It might help to assure the worried families, but Lin Yue refused to mistake possibility for proof. The campaign appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it.

An old optical receiver inside the chamber could not distinguish sunlight from a powered lamp. A neat explanation would have spared everyone an argument. Instead, the evidence refused to be neat. It contained a gap large enough to change the meaning of what came before it. Filling the gap with a guess would have been quick, and unkind to anyone trusting the answer. The team delivered light through a fiber cable while preserving the chamber's temperature. The most powerful person present could have ended the argument with an order. That might even have made the next hour more efficient. It would not have made the order wise. Instead, questions were allowed to remain questions long enough for another person to object. Earth hung above the horizon like a promise nobody on the Moon could take for granted. The public account described the situation as courageous, but Lin Yue asked for the observation behind that claim. Before they could devise a more careful test, the group needed a witness who was free to object. Lin Yue disliked the word diligent in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. 團隊用光纖送入光線，維持艙內溫度。在場最有權力的人本可一聲令下結束爭論，甚至讓下一小時更有效率，但命令不會因此變明智。他們讓問題繼續存在，給另一個人提出異議的時間。地球懸在地平線上，像月球上沒有人能理所當然得到的承諾。公開說法把情況形容為「勇敢的」，林玥要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。在能夠「想出」之前，團隊需要一位能自由提出異議的見證人。林玥不喜歡摘要中的「勤勉的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「世代；王朝」，奈良保留了原話。

The receiver woke a caretaker program that addressed them as late rescuers. They repeated the procedure with a different observer. It took longer and yielded a less dramatic account than the first version. That was an advantage: a discovery dependent on one person’s performance would have been too fragile to guide the next dangerous step. The place was still full of ordinary life. Someone laughed at a poor joke, someone swept a corner, and a tired worker counted the minutes to the next shift. Those details made the danger harder to ignore, because the people involved had lives outside this puzzle. 這地方仍充滿平凡生活。有人為難笑話發笑，有人掃地，疲憊的工作者算著距離換班還有幾分鐘。這些細節讓危險更難忽略，因為受影響者在謎題之外還有人生。林玥望向奈良，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：救援不該要求所有權。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。奧瑪不肯相信漂亮圖面，除非壓力讀值相符。團隊把「模仿」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「體力勞動」，奈良保留了原話。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「礦物粉末中的礦物」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

The question that followed was smaller and more frightening than the one they had brought to the room. It asked not whether they could win, but whether their answer would remain safe after they were gone. Nobody pretended to know yet, and that honesty held them together. Lin Yue knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The signal log would preserve one more question, and Nara would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Nara listened to the ancient pulses through a filter that stripped away modern noise. The public account described the situation as needy, but Lin Yue asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the psychology; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin. One instruction told the team to reflect on their own mistake; nobody could explain why it was urgent.

後來的問題比進門時更小、也更可怕：不是能否獲勝，而是離開後答案是否仍安全。沒有人假裝已經知道，而這份誠實使大家繼續團結。林玥知道明天不會從零開始。訊號紀錄將保存又一個問題，奈良也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。奈良隔著濾除現代雜訊的裝置聽古代脈衝。公開說法把情況形容為「需要幫助的」，林玥要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「心理學；心理」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。一項指令要求團隊「反射；反思」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。
