沒有國籍的艙室

The Chamber Without a Nation

The colony council demanded exclusive ownership of the chamber for the corporation that financed the base. The first sign of trouble was not a scream. It was a pause in an otherwise predictable routine, long enough for a careful person to notice. Others kept moving, and their movement made it tempting to do the same, but the pause deserved an explanation. The cooling pipes ran beneath the dust like veins in a sleeping machine. The plan called for them to adopt a safer rule, and Lin Yue asked who would check the result. Nobody disputed the agency; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin. The team considered the behavior a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe.

基地議會要求把艙室獨占權交給出資公司。第一個麻煩徵兆不是尖叫，而是原本可預期的流程忽然停頓，長到足以讓細心的人察覺。其他人繼續前進，令人忍不住跟上，可那次停頓需要解釋。冷卻管在塵土下延伸，像沉睡機械的血管。計畫要求他們「採用」，林玥追問由誰查核結果。沒人質疑「機構」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「行為」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。

Nara pointed out that no living human government could claim authorship of the two-thousand-year-old structures. A neat explanation would have spared everyone an argument. Instead, the evidence refused to be neat. It contained a gap large enough to change the meaning of what came before it. Filling the gap with a guess would have been quick, and unkind to anyone trusting the answer. Earth hung above the horizon like a promise nobody on the Moon could take for granted. Nara proposed they cherish the last message; Lin Yue wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to congress near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable. The conscience appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the decoration; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin.

奈良指出，現存人類政府沒有一個能宣稱建造兩千年前的建築。整齊的解釋本可省去爭吵，但證據偏偏不整齊。裡面有一道足以改變先前意義的缺口。拿猜測補起來雖快，對信任答案的人卻不公平。地球懸在地平線上，像月球上沒有人能理所當然得到的承諾。奈良提議「珍惜」，林玥希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「議會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊訊號紀錄出現了「良心」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「裝飾」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

Lin Yue argued for independent scientific custody and a temporary prohibition on extraction. The most powerful person present could have ended the argument with an order. That might even have made the next hour more efficient. It would not have made the order wise. Instead, questions were allowed to remain questions long enough for another person to object. The colony windows reflected the lights of a home still being built. One instruction told the team to discourage a dangerous shortcut; nobody could explain why it was urgent. A witness called the result fundamental; Nara wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name. It might help to horrify the witnesses with careless claims, but Lin Yue refused to mistake possibility for proof. The public account described the situation as influential, but Lin Yue asked for the observation behind that claim.

林玥主張由獨立科學團隊保管，暫時禁止取走任何物品。在場最有權力的人本可一聲令下結束爭論，甚至讓下一小時更有效率，但命令不會因此變明智。他們讓問題繼續存在，給另一個人提出異議的時間。基地窗戶反射出一個仍在建造中的家園燈光。一項指令要求團隊「使卻步」，卻沒有人能解釋為何急迫。見證人稱結果「根本的；基本的」，奈良把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。「使驚恐」或許有幫助，林玥卻拒絕把可能當成證明。公開說法把情況形容為「有影響力的」，林玥要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。

The council granted only seventy-two hours before a commercial drilling crew arrived. The room did not erupt in celebration. A few people checked the figures again, others examined the door, and somebody quietly asked who would pay for the repairs. That question belonged with the evidence. A clever answer was of little use if nobody could maintain it. Omar refused to trust a beautiful diagram until the pressure readings agreed. Nobody disputed the interaction; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin. The team considered the outcome a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe. A witness called the result primitive; Nara wrote the adjective beside the witness’s name.

議會只給七十二小時，商業鑽探隊就會到場。房間沒有爆發歡呼。幾人重新核對數字，其他人檢查門，還有人低聲問誰出維修費。那問題和證據同樣重要。若無人能維持，再聰明的答案也沒有用。奧瑪不肯相信漂亮圖面，除非壓力讀值相符。沒人質疑「相互作用」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「結果」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。見證人稱結果「古早的」，奈良把形容詞記在見證人姓名旁。

A small act of care interrupted the discussion. It solved no mystery and changed no official ruling. Yet it reminded them that the people named in the records were not pieces on a board; they had bodies, families and the right to decide what came next. Lin Yue glanced toward Nara and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: rescue cannot demand ownership. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Nara listened to the ancient pulses through a filter that stripped away modern noise. They found a reference to reception near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable. The robber appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the scenery; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin.

一個小小的照顧動作打斷討論。它沒有解開謎，也沒改變裁決，卻提醒大家紀錄裡的人不是棋子；他們有身體、家庭，也有決定下一步的權利。林玥望向奈良，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：救援不該要求所有權。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。奈良隔著濾除現代雜訊的裝置聽古代脈衝。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「接待會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。舊訊號紀錄出現了「強盜」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「舞臺布景」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

The last light in the room was not a sign of triumph. It was a reminder that somebody would return to check the work, and that a new person might notice something the experts had missed. They left the account open to correction rather than locking it inside a victory speech. Lin Yue knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The signal log would preserve one more question, and Nara would be there to challenge the first easy answer. Fine lunar dust settled over the rover tracks and made the distance seem endless. Lin Yue disliked the word shameful in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. A witness mentioned terror during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to witch near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable.

房裡最後的燈不是勝利標誌，而是提醒有人會回來檢查，也可能有新人看見專家漏掉的事。他們讓紀錄保持可更正，而不是鎖進勝利演說裡。林玥知道明天不會從零開始。訊號紀錄將保存又一個問題，奈良也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。細月塵落在探測車軌上，讓距離看似無盡。林玥不喜歡摘要中的「可恥的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。見證人在覆核時提到「恐懼」，奈良保留了原話。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「巫師」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。
