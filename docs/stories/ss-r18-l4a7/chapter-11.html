Nara found that the caretaker's language encoded relationships rather than simple names. The first sign of trouble was not a scream. It was a pause in an otherwise predictable routine, long enough for a careful person to notice. Others kept moving, and their movement made it tempting to do the same, but the pause deserved an explanation. Nara listened to the ancient pulses through a filter that stripped away modern noise. Nara proposed they accuse a person without proof; Lin Yue wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to assurance near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable. The plan called for them to boast about an untested result, and Lin Yue asked who would check the result.

奈良發現照護程式的語言編碼的是關係，不只是名稱。第一個麻煩徵兆不是尖叫，而是原本可預期的流程忽然停頓，長到足以讓細心的人察覺。其他人繼續前進，令人忍不住跟上，可那次停頓需要解釋。奈良隔著濾除現代雜訊的裝置聽古代脈衝。奈良提議「指控」，林玥希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「保證」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。計畫要求他們「吹噓」，林玥追問由誰查核結果。

A recurring symbol meant both shelter and obligation, depending on the sequence. An attractive theory formed almost immediately. It explained the loudest clue and gave everyone someone to blame. Yet one quiet observation remained outside it. Rather than hide that observation, the group put it where every later explanation would have to face it. Fine lunar dust settled over the rover tracks and made the distance seem endless. Nobody disputed the commerce; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin. Lin Yue disliked the word consistent in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made. Nara proposed they contribute to the shared plan; Lin Yue wanted the risk written down first. They found a reference to convention near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable.

同一個符號依序列不同，可能代表庇護也可能代表義務。一個吸引人的理論幾乎立刻成形。它解釋了最顯眼的線索，也給大家一個可以責怪的人。但一項安靜的觀察留在理論之外。團隊沒有藏起它，而是把它放在所有後續解釋都必須面對的位置。細月塵落在探測車軌上，讓距離看似無盡。沒人質疑「商業」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。林玥不喜歡摘要中的「前後一致的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。奈良提議「貢獻」，林玥希望先把風險寫清楚。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「大會；集會」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

The team published their translation uncertainty and requested peer review from Earth. They argued quietly because shouting would only give the nearby cameras and strangers another story to tell. Beneath the disagreement lay a practical question: who would bear the consequences if the confident person turned out to be mistaken? No one volunteered to answer for somebody else. The ancient chamber was quiet enough to hear the suit fans turning. The plan called for them to expose a private witness, and Lin Yue asked who would check the result. Nobody disputed the hurricane; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin. The team considered the hydrogen a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe. A witness mentioned ignorance during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording.

團隊公開翻譯的不確定性，向地球尋求同儕審查。他們壓低聲音爭辯，因為大喊只會讓旁邊的鏡頭與陌生人又多一個故事。爭論底下是實際問題：自信的人若弄錯，誰承擔後果？沒有人願意代替別人回答。古老艙室安靜得聽得見太空衣風扇轉動。計畫要求他們「揭露」，林玥追問由誰查核結果。沒人質疑「颶風」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。團隊把「氫」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「無知」，奈良保留了原話。

The response corrected a dangerous error in the coolant instructions. They repeated the procedure with a different observer. It took longer and yielded a less dramatic account than the first version. That was an advantage: a discovery dependent on one person’s performance would have been too fragile to guide the next dangerous step. A caution light shone along the rim of the unpressurized tunnel. The report labeled the risk informative, a judgment that needed checking before anyone acted. The public account described the situation as loyal, but Lin Yue asked for the observation behind that claim. Nobody disputed the manufacture; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin.

回覆修正了冷卻指示中的一個危險錯誤。他們換一位觀察者重做程序。耗時更久，結果也不如第一版戲劇化。這反而有利：如果發現只依靠一個人的表現，就太脆弱，不足以指引下一個危險步驟。一盞警示燈沿著無氣壓隧道邊緣亮起。報告把風險標為「提供資訊的」，這項判斷須經核查才能行動。公開說法把情況形容為「忠於」，林玥要求查看支撐判斷的觀察。沒人質疑「製造」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。

After the argument, a long silence settled around the group. It was not agreement. People were calculating what the next attempt might take from them, and whether anyone would notice the loss. Their caution was an ordinary human response, not a flaw in their courage. Lin Yue glanced toward Nara and remembered the principle they had been arguing about: rescue cannot demand ownership. It sounded simple until it demanded another hour of work. Lin Yue checked the oxygen gauge before looking at the extraordinary signal again. The team considered the misfortune a separate detail, not proof that a fragile cooling circuit was safe. A witness mentioned recovery during the review, so Nara preserved the exact wording. They found a reference to relaxation near the damaged signal log, but its date was unreadable.

爭論後，長久沉默籠罩大家。那不是同意。人們正在估算下一次嘗試會奪走什麼、又會不會有人注意。謹慎是平凡的人性反應，不是勇氣的缺點。林玥望向奈良，想起兩人不斷爭論的原則：救援不該要求所有權。話雖簡單，真正實踐又需要多一小時工作。林玥再次看驚人的訊號前，先確認氧氣錶。團隊把「災禍；不幸」視為獨立細節，不當成脆弱的冷卻電路安全的證據。見證人在覆核時提到「復原」，奈良保留了原話。他們在受損訊號紀錄旁找到提及「休息時間」的紀錄，日期卻看不清。

There would be easier stories to tell about this day. In those stories a brave person would find one clue, name one enemy and fix everything at once. The truth they carried was more difficult: a choice could be necessary and still leave work for the morning. Lin Yue knew the next day would not begin from a clean slate. The signal log would preserve one more question, and Nara would be there to challenge the first easy answer. No one could replace a broken seal simply by wishing the rescue to succeed. The resistance appeared in the older signal log, and Lin Yue asked why the newer version omitted it. Nobody disputed the skyscraper; what troubled Lin Yue was the silence around its origin. Lin Yue disliked the word troublesome in the summary because it hid a decision someone had made.

關於今天，可以講得更簡單：勇敢的人找到一個線索，指出一個敵人，一切立刻解決。他們帶走的真相更難：一個選擇可以是必要的，也仍會留下明早要處理的工作。林玥知道明天不會從零開始。訊號紀錄將保存又一個問題，奈良也會在場質疑第一個容易的答案。沒有人能光靠盼望救援成功，就取代破損的密封圈。舊訊號紀錄出現了「阻力」，林玥追問新版為何漏掉。沒人質疑「摩天大樓」，讓林玥困擾的是它的來歷沒有人提。林玥不喜歡摘要中的「棘手的」，因為它掩藏了某個人的決定。
