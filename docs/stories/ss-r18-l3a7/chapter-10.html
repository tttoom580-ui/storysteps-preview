The first account was untidy and sometimes embarrassing. That was a reason to ask careful questions, not a reason to throw it away. Outside, footsteps hurried past the door. A messenger came from Inspector Crow with an offer that sounded generous until Finn read the last line. The offer protected the person making it, not the people who would carry out the work. Mira reached for the care ledger and wrote down the missing condition. They were not refusing cooperation. They were refusing to confuse cooperation with obedience. That distinction changed the way the next conversation began. Finn found champion on the list of details that the first report had left unexplained. 克洛督察派來使者提出一項看似大方的條件，直到芬恩讀到最後一行才發現問題。條件保護的是提出者，不是實際工作的人。米拉拿起照護紀錄記下缺少的前提。他們並非拒絕合作，而是拒絕把合作和服從混為一談。這個區別改變了下一場談話的方式。 芬恩在第一份報告沒有說明的細節清單上，找到「冠軍」。起初這項變化看來「有創意的」，第二次觀察卻讓團隊不再那麼確定。計畫提到需要「減少」；兩人都不願毫無理由便接受這項指示。

In a quieter corner, Finn and Mira tried to understand each other without an audience. One favored action because people needed help now; the other feared that a rushed action could shut the safest route. Both concerns were real. Finn admitted that the care ledger did not answer every question. Mira admitted that waiting forever was not a plan either. They decided to separate the urgent work from the larger decision. The silver fox opened one golden eye and studied Finn without leaving its bed of leaves. He placed the food on the ground and backed away rather than asking the beast to perform. 到了安靜角落，芬恩與米拉試著在沒有觀眾的情況下理解彼此。一人主張立刻行動，因為有人正在求助；另一人擔心匆忙行動會堵住最安全的路。兩種擔憂都真實。芬恩承認照護紀錄無法回答一切，米拉也承認無限等待不是計畫。兩人決定把緊急工作和較大的決策分開。銀狐睜開一隻金色眼睛，在樹葉床上觀察芬恩。他把食物放在地上便後退，沒有要求魔獸表演。 一名證人在匆忙的敘述中提到「迴盪」；芬恩先記下來，沒有猜測它有多重要。起初這項變化看來「害怕的」，第二次觀察卻讓團隊不再那麼確定。米拉在初步提案旁寫下「歷史的」，又加了問號，因為意見並不一致。

The test seemed almost foolish at first. They used plain tools, asked a witness to describe what could be seen, and repeated the same action twice. Finn kept the care ledger open so that Mira could challenge each step. On the second attempt, a detail changed. That difference did not solve the whole mystery, but it ruled out the easiest explanation. For the first time that day, they had something firmer than a rumor. Finn watched the animals for signs of fear before changing their shelter. Someone asked whether holder was connected to the trouble, and the room fell quiet for a moment. 起初，測試看起來幾乎有點傻。他們只使用簡單工具，請證人描述眼前的情形，並把相同動作重做兩次。芬恩攤開照護紀錄，讓米拉可以質疑每一步。第二次嘗試時，一個細節改變了。那不能解開整個謎團，卻排除了最簡單的解釋。他們終於握有比流言更可靠的東西。芬恩先觀察動物是否害怕，才改動牠們的住處。 有人問「持有人」是否與麻煩有關，房間一時間安靜下來。米拉圈起「漏水」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。照護紀錄的頁邊提到了「群眾」，米拉做上記號，準備日後查證。

米拉朗讀最後一行。芬恩核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 米拉在初步提案旁寫下「正常的」，又加了問號，因為意見並不一致。照護紀錄的頁邊提到了「宮殿」，米拉做上記號，準備日後查證。

Someone suggested that Finn should keep the discovery secret until the whole argument was over. Mira objected: a secret about danger could also harm people who never had a chance to ask questions. They separated private names from public facts and prepared a short notice. The notice was less dramatic than the rumor, but it explained what could be used now and what still needed an independent check. A small bird settled on the repaired perch without making a sound. That quiet choice meant more to Finn than any show ticket or promise of a crowded audience. Mira circled the entry for penny and asked whether the detail belonged to this incident. Mira wanted to postpone, but agreed to wait for one more safe test.

有人建議芬恩等爭議結束再公開發現。米拉反對：危險的祕密也可能傷害那些根本沒有機會提問的人。他們把私人姓名與公共事實分開，準備簡短公告。公告不如流言驚人，卻清楚說明現在能使用什麼、哪些仍需獨立檢查。一隻小鳥安靜地停在修好的棲木上。對芬恩而言，這份安靜的選擇比表演門票或擁擠觀眾的承諾更重要。 米拉圈起「便士」那一欄，問這個細節是否真的屬於本次事件。米拉想要「延後」，但同意先等一次安全測試的結果。

Finn left the care ledger open so Mira could add the next finding. Neither could see the whole road yet. Mira wanted to steal, but agreed to wait for one more safe test. The margin of the care ledger contained a reference to zipper, which Mira marked for a later check.
