空麻袋的金字塔

A Pyramid of Empty Sacks

萬味渡口：與你煮一碗回家的湯 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The first five ports had not given Gu Wen and Lin Zhou an easy answer, but they had shown what a mistake could cost. They carried fewer assumptions now and more accurately labeled samples. At the Old Salt Pyramid, they faced a problem that the market announcements had described too simply: a sealed warehouse held sacks labeled as medicinal salt although the contents were industrial waste. People had gathered before sunrise, not because they expected a famous hero, but because the next meal might depend on what happened here. Some brought bowls; others carried questions they had asked for days. Gu Wen noticed how carefully the smallest children watched the adults. A promise that sounded generous from the council table might mean something very different to a family standing at the end of a queue.

前五港沒有給顧聞與林舟簡單答案，卻讓他們看見錯誤的代價。如今他們帶著更少的成見，以及標記更準確的樣本。在舊鹽塔，兩人面對一件市場公告說得過於簡單的難題：封閉倉庫裡的麻袋標成藥用鹽，內裝卻是工業廢料。日出前人們便聚集起來，不是期待名震天下的英雄，而是知道下一餐可能取決於這裡發生的事。有人帶來碗，有人帶著問了好幾天的疑惑。顧聞注意到最小的孩子如何仔細觀察大人。議會桌上聽來慷慨的承諾，對排在隊伍末尾的家庭可能完全不同。

Lin Zhou prepared two separate sample jars before he would discuss a result. One held material from the suspicious route; the other held a control sample obtained with a witness watching. They began with something ordinary that could be checked by anyone willing to look: a valid inventory had been patched with a second page and a false residence code. The first observation did not explain everything. Gu Wen marked the position in his notebook, while Lin Zhou asked a second person to repeat the measurement with fresh tools. A different result would have mattered as much as a matching one. Their copper pot might amplify a real aroma, but no enchantment could replace grain that had never been harvested. That distinction kept the work focused on actual supplies and actual people.

林舟在討論結果前，先備好兩個不同樣本罐。一罐裝可疑貨路的材料，一罐裝見證人監看下取得的對照樣本。兩人從一項人人只要願意細看就能核對的普通線索開始：一份有效清單被補入第二頁，並添上假的住址代碼。第一次觀察不能解釋一切。顧聞在筆記本標記位置，林舟則請第二個人用新工具重做測量。不同的結果和相同的結果一樣重要。古銅鍋可以放大真實香味，卻沒有任何法術能取代從未收成的穀物。這個區別讓工作始終聚焦於真正的物資與人。

An investigator entered the old pyramid with a bodyguard and a donkey, which the porters jokingly called an ass. Their official boundary map claimed the warehouse would comprise only ordinary goods. In reality its fluid contents had been patched into invoices from three companies. The investigator was passionate about a valid chain of records and refused to discriminate against the workers who lived in the adjacent residence. A young porter in a broken sandal offered a souvenir label, hoping it might be incorporated into the next version of the inventory. A therapist visiting vulnerable families confirmed that several names had been erased. Gu Wen wanted to undo the damage immediately; Lin Zhou reminded him that eligible witnesses must first decide how their words would be used. The map was not proof of guilt by itself, but the false route code was a place to begin. They could incorporate the new witness statement without changing the original page. A patch on the sack showed where its first label had been removed.

一名調查員帶著保鑣和驢子走進舊鹽塔，搬運工開玩笑地把那驢叫作「笨驢」。官方邊界圖聲稱倉內只有普通貨品，實際上液態貨物卻被補進三家公司的發票。調查員重視有效而連續的紀錄鏈，拒絕歧視住在旁邊宿舍的工人。一名穿破涼鞋的年輕搬運工遞上一張紀念標籤，希望能把它納入下一版庫存清單。探訪弱勢家庭的治療師證實，有幾個姓名被刪掉。顧聞想立即挽回損失；林舟提醒他，符合資格的見證人必須先決定證詞如何使用。地圖本身不是有罪證明，但假的路線代碼提供了一個調查起點。他們可以納入新的證詞，而不改動原始頁面。麻袋上的補丁顯示第一張標籤曾被拆下。

Every answer produced a new question about responsibility. It was easy to identify a flawed tool and much harder to decide who deserved help first when supplies were limited. After listening to workers and residents, Gu Wen proposed that they copy the record openly rather than sneak the original away. Some listeners objected. They had already lost wages, time, and confidence; a cautious solution sounded unbearably slow. Lin Zhou did not accuse them of being unreasonable. He showed them which part of the result was known and which part still had to be tested. When Gu Wen asked if the plan was fair, Lin Zhou answered that fairness had to survive the journey from a meeting room to a kitchen door.

每個答案又帶來新的責任問題。找出工具缺陷很容易，物資有限時決定誰該優先受助卻艱難得多。聽完工人與居民的意見後，顧聞提出：公開抄錄紀錄，不暗中偷走原件。有人反對。他們已失去工資、時間和信心，謹慎的方法聽起來慢得難以忍受。林舟沒有指責他們不理性，只是指出哪些結果已知、哪些仍待檢驗。顧聞問方案是否公平，林舟回答：公平必須經得起從議事廳走到廚房門口的考驗。

The workshop had no splendid banner and needed none. One person fetched water, another tested the jars, and a third wrote down the objections so that even those who arrived late could understand the decision. By the time the lamps were lit, one change could be seen: Gu Wen and Lin Zhou disagreed over speed, then agreed that vulnerable workers needed anonymity. It was a limited victory. Nobody claimed the entire river had been healed, and the records were left where the people who had helped could check them. The work had names, dates, and mistakes recorded plainly instead of hidden behind a heroic speech. For the first time that day, Gu Wen allowed himself a mouthful of warm food. It tasted plain and honest, which seemed better than any spell he could perform.

工坊沒有華麗橫幅，也不需要。有人取水，有人測罐，另有人記下反對意見，讓遲來的人也能理解決定。燈火亮起時，已有一項可見的改變：顧聞與林舟對速度意見不同，最後同意先保障弱勢工人的匿名權。這只是有限的勝利。沒有人宣稱整條河已痊癒；紀錄也留在協助者可以查閱的地方。工作有姓名、日期，錯誤也明白記下，不藏在英雄式演說後。那天顧聞第一次讓自己吃上一口熱食。味道樸素而真實，比他能施展的任何法術都好。

By dusk, the noise of the workshop had softened into the rattle of dishes and distant conversation. The two men finally found time to speak about something besides the mineral. They argued outside the storehouse about whether to take the papers and run. Gu Wen wanted speed; Lin Zhou wanted the workers protected. After a long pause they drew up a plan that did both imperfectly but honestly. It was the first disagreement neither man tried to win. Their next direction arrived in an unexpected form: a riverside clinic reported unexplained food reactions. They folded the new information into their map instead of calling it proof. At dawn they would travel again, but this time neither man would have to pretend he was walking alone.

黃昏時，工坊的喧鬧變成洗碗聲與遠處談話。兩人終於有空談些礦物以外的事。兩人在鹽倉外爭執是否該拿著文件逃走。顧聞想求快，林舟想保護工人。沉默良久後，他們擬出一份兼顧兩者、雖不完美卻誠實的方案。這是兩人第一次沒有試圖贏下的爭論。下一步的方向以意料之外的形式出現：河岸診所通報了原因不明的食物反應。兩人把新訊息記上地圖，沒有直接稱它為證據。黎明時他們將再次啟程，但這次誰都不必假裝自己孤身上路。
