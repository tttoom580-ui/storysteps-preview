苦蒸氣的水庫

The Reservoir of Bitter Steam

萬味渡口：與你煮一碗回家的湯 · 第 13 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

At the middle of their journey, Gu Wen discovered that a route on paper could look perfectly safe while the people walking it were afraid. Lin Zhou began every visit by asking who lived with its consequences. At the Upper Reservoir, they faced a problem that the market announcements had described too simply: a water laboratory found that the same mineral appeared in the filter beds. People had gathered before sunrise, not because they expected a famous hero, but because the next meal might depend on what happened here. Some brought bowls; others carried questions they had asked for days. Gu Wen noticed how carefully the smallest children watched the adults. A promise that sounded generous from the council table might mean something very different to a family standing at the end of a queue.

到了旅程中段，顧聞發現紙上看似安全的路線，走在上面的人仍可能害怕。林舟每到一處便先問，究竟是誰承受後果。在上游水庫，兩人面對一件市場公告說得過於簡單的難題：水質實驗室發現過濾層裡也有相同礦物。日出前人們便聚集起來，不是期待名震天下的英雄，而是知道下一餐可能取決於這裡發生的事。有人帶來碗，有人帶著問了好幾天的疑惑。顧聞注意到最小的孩子如何仔細觀察大人。議會桌上聽來慷慨的承諾，對排在隊伍末尾的家庭可能完全不同。

Gu Wen learned to recognize Lin Zhou’s careful silence: the herbalist was usually considering the possibility he most wanted to reject. The copper spoon was useful only when its sound could be compared with a known sample. They began with something ordinary that could be checked by anyone willing to look: the reservoir was safe in its lower chamber but dangerous when the upper vents heated the sediment. The first observation did not explain everything. Gu Wen marked the position in his notebook, while Lin Zhou asked a second person to repeat the measurement with fresh tools. A different result would have mattered as much as a matching one. A sample without its location and date could mislead an entire port. Gu Wen wrote both on the label while Lin Zhou counted the tools, taking care not to confuse an attractive result with a trustworthy one.

顧聞學會辨認林舟謹慎的沉默：藥師通常在思考他最不願接受的可能。銅匙的聲響必須與已知樣本比較才有用。兩人從一項人人只要願意細看就能核對的普通線索開始：水庫下層安全，上層排氣口加熱沉積物時卻有危險。第一次觀察不能解釋一切。顧聞在筆記本標記位置，林舟則請第二個人用新工具重做測量。不同的結果和相同的結果一樣重要。缺少地點與日期的樣本可能誤導整座港口。顧聞把兩者寫上標籤，林舟清點工具，提醒自己不能把漂亮結果誤當可信結果。

An administrator with a strong bias toward the salt guild oversaw the reservoir test. A documentary crew stood by a newly erected platform, filming a sphere of pale vapor above the water. The camera could flip to another angle, but it could not alter gravity or the dimensions of the upper vent. A doctor warned of acute reactions, though the condition was not contagious. The defendant company tried to overturn the committee's premise, claiming that olive oil in the dishes had caused the trouble. Lin Zhou answered with therapy records and a toll receipt showing when the mineral arrived. He urged legislation against untested additives, while Gu Wen called it weird to punish cooks who had never known the danger. Their measurements exposed a faulty heating sequence, and the administrator could no longer ignore the evidence. The dimension of each vent mattered. Workers would erect barriers while independent observers oversee the cooling process.

一名明顯偏袒鹽商會的行政官監督水庫測試。紀錄片團隊站在新架設的平台旁，拍攝水面上方的淡色蒸氣球。攝影機可以翻轉角度，卻不能改變重力，也不能改變上層排氣口的尺寸。醫師警告可能發生急性反應，但這不是傳染病。被告公司想推翻委員會的前提，聲稱問題出在菜餚使用的橄欖油。林舟拿出治療紀錄與通行費收據，證明礦物抵達的時間。他主張立法禁止未測試的添加物；顧聞則認為，懲罰根本不知道危險的廚師十分古怪。量測結果揭露錯誤的加熱順序，行政官再也無法忽視。每個排氣口的尺寸都很重要。工人將架起圍欄，獨立觀察員則監督冷卻過程。

The evidence now had to survive a public challenge. Some people wanted immediate punishment, others wanted the work stopped, and the cooks still needed to prepare supper before the lamps went out. After listening to workers and residents, Gu Wen proposed that they close only the affected vents while keeping drinking water available. Some listeners objected. They had already lost wages, time, and confidence; a cautious solution sounded unbearably slow. Lin Zhou did not accuse them of being unreasonable. He showed them which part of the result was known and which part still had to be tested. When Gu Wen asked if the plan was fair, Lin Zhou answered that fairness had to survive the journey from a meeting room to a kitchen door.

證據如今必須接受公開質疑。有人要求立即懲罰，有人想停工，但廚師仍須在熄燈前準備晚餐。聽完工人與居民的意見後，顧聞提出：只關閉受影響的排氣口，仍維持居民飲水。有人反對。他們已失去工資、時間和信心，謹慎的方法聽起來慢得難以忍受。林舟沒有指責他們不理性，只是指出哪些結果已知、哪些仍待檢驗。顧聞問方案是否公平，林舟回答：公平必須經得起從議事廳走到廚房門口的考驗。

A group of residents stayed after the official discussion and tried the procedure with their own hands. Their questions were specific: what was safe to touch, who would keep the key, and how could a failed test be reported? By the time the lamps were lit, one change could be seen: Lin Zhou and Gu Wen trusted a shared set of measurements more than either one's intuition. It was a limited victory. Nobody claimed the entire river had been healed, and the records were left where the people who had helped could check them. The work had names, dates, and mistakes recorded plainly instead of hidden behind a heroic speech. For the first time that day, Gu Wen allowed himself a mouthful of warm food. It tasted plain and honest, which seemed better than any spell he could perform.

正式討論結束後，一群居民留下來親手試做程序。他們的問題很具體：什麼可以碰、誰保管鑰匙、檢測失敗要如何通報？燈火亮起時，已有一項可見的改變：林舟和顧聞開始信任共同量測的結果，不再只憑個人直覺。這只是有限的勝利。沒有人宣稱整條河已痊癒；紀錄也留在協助者可以查閱的地方。工作有姓名、日期，錯誤也明白記下，不藏在英雄式演說後。那天顧聞第一次讓自己吃上一口熱食。味道樸素而真實，比他能施展的任何法術都好。

The lamps made soft pools of light along the river. Gu Wen and Lin Zhou walked at the edge of the crowd, close enough to hear one another without having to compete with the cheering. They signed their joint observation with two names of equal size. Gu Wen noticed that Lin Zhou no longer put his own work first out of habit; Lin Zhou noticed that Gu Wen had stopped apologizing for needing help. Neither mentioned the change, but both understood it. Their next direction arrived in an unexpected form: a charter council was preparing a closed vote on the salt trade. They folded the new information into their map instead of calling it proof. At dawn they would travel again, but this time neither man would have to pretend he was walking alone.

燈光沿河映出柔和的光圈。顧聞與林舟走在人群邊緣，距離近得能聽見彼此，不必與歡呼聲較勁。兩人把共同觀察紀錄簽上同樣大小的名字。顧聞發現林舟不再習慣性把自己的工作放在最前面；林舟也發現顧聞不再為需要幫忙而道歉。誰都沒提這個變化，心裡卻都明白。下一步的方向以意料之外的形式出現：章程議會準備對鹽貿易進行閉門表決。兩人把新訊息記上地圖，沒有直接稱它為證據。黎明時他們將再次啟程，但這次誰都不必假裝自己孤身上路。
