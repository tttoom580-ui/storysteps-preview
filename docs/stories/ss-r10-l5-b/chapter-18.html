舊對手的果園

The Orchard of Old Rivals

萬味渡口：與你煮一碗回家的湯 · 第 18 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

After the first honest feast, the travelers had to deal with success as carefully as failure. A generous offer might conceal a demand for control, while an old friendship could reveal a painful omission. At the Stone Orchard, they faced a problem that the market announcements had described too simply: the former supplier's assistant revealed how the gray mineral had entered legal shipments. People had gathered before sunrise, not because they expected a famous hero, but because the next meal might depend on what happened here. Some brought bowls; others carried questions they had asked for days. Gu Wen noticed how carefully the smallest children watched the adults. A promise that sounded generous from the council table might mean something very different to a family standing at the end of a queue.

第一場誠實宴席之後，兩人發現成功也必須像失敗一樣謹慎看待。慷慨提議可能藏著控制要求，舊日情誼也可能揭露痛苦的疏漏。在石果園，兩人面對一件市場公告說得過於簡單的難題：供應商的前助手揭露灰色礦物如何混進合法貨運。日出前人們便聚集起來，不是期待名震天下的英雄，而是知道下一餐可能取決於這裡發生的事。有人帶來碗，有人帶著問了好幾天的疑惑。顧聞注意到最小的孩子如何仔細觀察大人。議會桌上聽來慷慨的承諾，對排在隊伍末尾的家庭可能完全不同。

They separated questions about ingredients from questions about ownership. A cooking method could be taught openly without forcing anyone to disclose the story of an illness or a family recipe. They began with something ordinary that could be checked by anyone willing to look: a false shortage had raised prices and made the river villages dependent on one trader. The first observation did not explain everything. Gu Wen marked the position in his notebook, while Lin Zhou asked a second person to repeat the measurement with fresh tools. A different result would have mattered as much as a matching one. As the political dispute grew, their rule about consent became more important. Witnesses could choose a private hearing; cooks could contribute food without handing over every secret in their family notebooks.

兩人將食材問題與所有權問題分開。烹法可以公開教授，卻不該因此強迫誰揭露病情經歷或家傳食譜。兩人從一項人人只要願意細看就能核對的普通線索開始：假的缺貨消息抬高價格，讓河岸村落依賴單一商人。第一次觀察不能解釋一切。顧聞在筆記本標記位置，林舟則請第二個人用新工具重做測量。不同的結果和相同的結果一樣重要。政治爭議愈大，兩人關於同意的規則就愈重要。證人可以選擇非公開聽證；廚師也能提供食物，而不必交出家族筆記裡的每個祕密。

An administration officer brought an antique seal to the orchard, hoping to conceal a bizarre trail of payments. The framework of the false deal was simple: a gross price increase, a subsidy for the rival company, and a speech blaming everyone else for corruption. The agent's hostility made the workers nervous. A faded icon on a fabric sack proved the cargo had passed through the same warehouse, while a former clerk offered to nominate a neutral inspector. The crowd wanted to rally against the merchant, but Gu Wen said that anger should not undermine the safety of the witness. In the end, evidence prevailed over the attempted distraction. Lin Zhou felt a thrill at the discovery, yet the real venture was still ahead: they had to keep people fed while the false supply chain was dismantled. The clerk could not pay his tuition with apologies alone. Whether fairness would prevail depended on what the port did next.

一名行政官帶著古老印章來到果園，想掩蓋一條古怪的付款路線。假交易架構很簡單：大幅漲價、補貼競爭公司，再用演說把腐敗怪到別人身上。代理人的敵意使工人緊張。布袋上褪色的圖示證明貨物經過同一座倉庫，前任書記則提議提名一名中立稽查員。群眾想集會反對商人，但顧聞說，怒氣不能損害證人的安全。最後，證據勝過轉移焦點的企圖。林舟為新發現感到振奮，真正的冒險卻仍在前方：必須在拆解虛假供應鏈的同時，讓人們有飯可吃。書記不能只靠道歉支付學費。正義是否能勝出，要看港口接下來怎麼做。

An apparent victory made the choice more delicate. The market wanted a triumphant announcement, while the families asked whether the repair would last beyond the festival. After listening to workers and residents, Gu Wen proposed that they offer the assistant protection but require records before offering forgiveness. Some listeners objected. They had already lost wages, time, and confidence; a cautious solution sounded unbearably slow. Lin Zhou did not accuse them of being unreasonable. He showed them which part of the result was known and which part still had to be tested. When Gu Wen asked if the plan was fair, Lin Zhou answered that fairness had to survive the journey from a meeting room to a kitchen door.

表面上的勝利反而使選擇更微妙。市場想聽凱旋宣言，家庭卻關心修復能否撐過節慶以後。聽完工人與居民的意見後，顧聞提出：先保護助手，但必須有紀錄才能談原諒。有人反對。他們已失去工資、時間和信心，謹慎的方法聽起來慢得難以忍受。林舟沒有指責他們不理性，只是指出哪些結果已知、哪些仍待檢驗。顧聞問方案是否公平，林舟回答：公平必須經得起從議事廳走到廚房門口的考驗。

The common hearth became a place where arguments could turn into work. Some washed vegetables; some repaired the benches; others kept the accounts. Each task gave a different answer to the question of who owned the feast. By the time the lamps were lit, one change could be seen: Gu Wen recognized his own habit of judging people by the worst decision they had made. It was a limited victory. Nobody claimed the entire river had been healed, and the records were left where the people who had helped could check them. The work had names, dates, and mistakes recorded plainly instead of hidden behind a heroic speech. For the first time that day, Gu Wen allowed himself a mouthful of warm food. It tasted plain and honest, which seemed better than any spell he could perform.

公共灶成了讓爭論轉化為工作的地方。有人洗菜，有人修長椅，有人管帳。每份工作都為「宴席屬於誰」提出不同答案。燈火亮起時，已有一項可見的改變：顧聞看見自己總以他人最糟的決定判人。這只是有限的勝利。沒有人宣稱整條河已痊癒；紀錄也留在協助者可以查閱的地方。工作有姓名、日期，錯誤也明白記下，不藏在英雄式演說後。那天顧聞第一次讓自己吃上一口熱食。味道樸素而真實，比他能施展的任何法術都好。

Once the last worker had gone home, the two men remained to check the hearth. It was the quiet work afterward, not the speeches before, that let them understand each other. The former assistant asked whether one bad decision would follow him forever. Gu Wen almost answered harshly, then remembered his own childhood hunger. Afterward he admitted to Lin Zhou that mercy frightened him because it could become an excuse. Lin Zhou answered that mercy and evidence did not have to be enemies. Their next direction arrived in an unexpected form: a caravan of displaced families was approaching the central port. They folded the new information into their map instead of calling it proof. At dawn they would travel again, but this time neither man would have to pretend he was walking alone.

最後一名工人回家後，兩人仍留下檢查爐灶。讓他們了解彼此的，是演說之後安靜的工作，而不是先前的漂亮話。前助手問，一個壞決定會不會跟著自己一輩子。顧聞差點嚴厲回話，卻想起童年挨餓的記憶。之後他向林舟坦承，自己害怕仁慈變成藉口。林舟說，仁慈與證據不必互相為敵。下一步的方向以意料之外的形式出現：一支流離失所家庭的車隊正前往中央港口。兩人把新訊息記上地圖，沒有直接稱它為證據。黎明時他們將再次啟程，但這次誰都不必假裝自己孤身上路。
