The Charter at Noon

萬味渡口：與你煮一碗回家的湯 · 第 14 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

At the middle of their journey, Gu Wen discovered that a route on paper could look perfectly safe while the people walking it were afraid. Lin Zhou began every visit by asking who lived with its consequences. At the Hall of Open Tables, they faced a problem that the market announcements had described too simply: the merchants proposed a law that would give one family ownership of every public cooking method. People had gathered before sunrise, not because they expected a famous hero, but because the next meal might depend on what happened here. Some brought bowls; others carried questions they had asked for days. Gu Wen noticed how carefully the smallest children watched the adults. A promise that sounded generous from the council table might mean something very different to a family standing at the end of a queue.

到了旅程中段，顧聞發現紙上看似安全的路線，走在上面的人仍可能害怕。林舟每到一處便先問，究竟是誰承受後果。在公開餐桌議廳，兩人面對一件市場公告說得過於簡單的難題：商人提案讓單一家族取得所有公共烹法的所有權。日出前人們便聚集起來，不是期待名震天下的英雄，而是知道下一餐可能取決於這裡發生的事。有人帶來碗，有人帶著問了好幾天的疑惑。顧聞注意到最小的孩子如何仔細觀察大人。議會桌上聽來慷慨的承諾，對排在隊伍末尾的家庭可能完全不同。

Gu Wen learned to recognize Lin Zhou’s careful silence: the herbalist was usually considering the possibility he most wanted to reject. The copper spoon was useful only when its sound could be compared with a known sample. They began with something ordinary that could be checked by anyone willing to look: the draft quietly confused the right to sell a recipe with the right to deny others clean salt. The first observation did not explain everything. Gu Wen marked the position in his notebook, while Lin Zhou asked a second person to repeat the measurement with fresh tools. A different result would have mattered as much as a matching one. A sample without its location and date could mislead an entire port. Gu Wen wrote both on the label while Lin Zhou counted the tools, taking care not to confuse an attractive result with a trustworthy one.

顧聞學會辨認林舟謹慎的沉默：藥師通常在思考他最不願接受的可能。銅匙的聲響必須與已知樣本比較才有用。兩人從一項人人只要願意細看就能核對的普通線索開始：草案偷偷混淆出售食譜的權利與剝奪他人取得乾淨食鹽的權利。第一次觀察不能解釋一切。顧聞在筆記本標記位置，林舟則請第二個人用新工具重做測量。不同的結果和相同的結果一樣重要。缺少地點與日期的樣本可能誤導整座港口。顧聞把兩者寫上標籤，林舟清點工具，提醒自己不能把漂亮結果誤當可信結果。

Outside the cathedral, the legislative clerk said the council would amend its draft but refused to abolish the old cooking law outright. An auction broker wanted to deceive the public with a grant for 'clean' salt, while the smoke emission from the plant remained untested. Gu Wen made an articulate case for compassionate compliance: kitchens could combat contamination without surrendering every family recipe. The crowd was enthusiastic, but Lin Zhou reminded them that the juvenile workers deserved their own voices. A little boy on a porch had made a wig for the pageant from reeds; nearby, a predator bird carried a skull toward its nest. Neither detail proved anything about the company, yet both made the prospect of a poisoned river feel painfully real. The council voted to postpone exclusive ownership until the tests were independent. An unlocked storage room made it too easy to replace test samples without a record.

大教堂外，立法書記說議會將修訂草案，卻不願立即廢除舊烹法。拍賣商想用一筆「乾淨食鹽」補助款欺騙民眾，工廠排放卻仍未經檢驗。顧聞清楚提出兼顧同理與遵法的主張：廚房可以對抗污染，不必交出每家的食譜。群眾熱情響應，但林舟提醒，年少工人也有自己的發言權。走廊上的男孩用蘆葦做出一頂表演假髮；附近一隻掠食鳥正把頭骨叼回巢裡。這兩件事不能證明公司有罪，卻讓河流受污染的前景變得痛切真實。議會最後投票，在獨立測試完成前暫緩獨占所有權。未上鎖的儲藏室讓人容易在不留紀錄的情況下更換測試樣本。

The evidence now had to survive a public challenge. Some people wanted immediate punishment, others wanted the work stopped, and the cooks still needed to prepare supper before the lamps went out. After listening to workers and residents, Gu Wen proposed that they offer testimony without demanding that every household reveal its private recipes. Some listeners objected. They had already lost wages, time, and confidence; a cautious solution sounded unbearably slow. Lin Zhou did not accuse them of being unreasonable. He showed them which part of the result was known and which part still had to be tested. When Gu Wen asked if the plan was fair, Lin Zhou answered that fairness had to survive the journey from a meeting room to a kitchen door.

證據如今必須接受公開質疑。有人要求立即懲罰，有人想停工，但廚師仍須在熄燈前準備晚餐。聽完工人與居民的意見後，顧聞提出：作證但不要求所有家庭公開私傳食譜。有人反對。他們已失去工資、時間和信心，謹慎的方法聽起來慢得難以忍受。林舟沒有指責他們不理性，只是指出哪些結果已知、哪些仍待檢驗。顧聞問方案是否公平，林舟回答：公平必須經得起從議事廳走到廚房門口的考驗。

A group of residents stayed after the official discussion and tried the procedure with their own hands. Their questions were specific: what was safe to touch, who would keep the key, and how could a failed test be reported? By the time the lamps were lit, one change could be seen: a limited charter passed, protecting shared kitchens until independent tests were complete. It was a limited victory. Nobody claimed the entire river had been healed, and the records were left where the people who had helped could check them. The work had names, dates, and mistakes recorded plainly instead of hidden behind a heroic speech. For the first time that day, Gu Wen allowed himself a mouthful of warm food. It tasted plain and honest, which seemed better than any spell he could perform.

正式討論結束後，一群居民留下來親手試做程序。他們的問題很具體：什麼可以碰、誰保管鑰匙、檢測失敗要如何通報？燈火亮起時，已有一項可見的改變：一份有限章程通過，保障共享廚房直到獨立檢測完成。這只是有限的勝利。沒有人宣稱整條河已痊癒；紀錄也留在協助者可以查閱的地方。工作有姓名、日期，錯誤也明白記下，不藏在英雄式演說後。那天顧聞第一次讓自己吃上一口熱食。味道樸素而真實，比他能施展的任何法術都好。

The lamps made soft pools of light along the river. Gu Wen and Lin Zhou walked at the edge of the crowd, close enough to hear one another without having to compete with the cheering. Speaking in public frightened Lin Zhou more than examining a dangerous mineral. Gu Wen stood nearby without taking over his testimony. When it ended, Lin Zhou reached out and touched his sleeve, a brief thanks he did not need to explain. Their next direction arrived in an unexpected form: the harvest festival could finally test whether safe salt could feed the river villages. They folded the new information into their map instead of calling it proof. At dawn they would travel again, but this time neither man would have to pretend he was walking alone.

燈光沿河映出柔和的光圈。顧聞與林舟走在人群邊緣，距離近得能聽見彼此，不必與歡呼聲較勁。對林舟而言，公開發言比檢查危險礦物還可怕。顧聞站在附近，沒有搶走他的發言。結束後，林舟伸手碰了碰他的衣袖，那是不用說明的短短謝意。下一步的方向以意料之外的形式出現：豐收節將驗證安全食鹽能否供應河岸村莊。兩人把新訊息記上地圖，沒有直接稱它為證據。黎明時他們將再次啟程，但這次誰都不必假裝自己孤身上路。
