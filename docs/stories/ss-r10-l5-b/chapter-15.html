第一場誠實的宴席

The First Honest Feast

萬味渡口：與你煮一碗回家的湯 · 第 15 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

At the middle of their journey, Gu Wen discovered that a route on paper could look perfectly safe while the people walking it were afraid. Lin Zhou began every visit by asking who lived with its consequences. At the Reed Harvest Festival, they faced a problem that the market announcements had described too simply: five villages carried their own ingredients to a great open hearth. People had gathered before sunrise, not because they expected a famous hero, but because the next meal might depend on what happened here. Some brought bowls; others carried questions they had asked for days. Gu Wen noticed how carefully the smallest children watched the adults. A promise that sounded generous from the council table might mean something very different to a family standing at the end of a queue.

到了旅程中段，顧聞發現紙上看似安全的路線，走在上面的人仍可能害怕。林舟每到一處便先問，究竟是誰承受後果。在蘆原豐收節，兩人面對一件市場公告說得過於簡單的難題：五座村莊把各自食材帶到巨大的公共灶。日出前人們便聚集起來，不是期待名震天下的英雄，而是知道下一餐可能取決於這裡發生的事。有人帶來碗，有人帶著問了好幾天的疑惑。顧聞注意到最小的孩子如何仔細觀察大人。議會桌上聽來慷慨的承諾，對排在隊伍末尾的家庭可能完全不同。

Gu Wen learned to recognize Lin Zhou’s careful silence: the herbalist was usually considering the possibility he most wanted to reject. The copper spoon was useful only when its sound could be compared with a known sample. They began with something ordinary that could be checked by anyone willing to look: the old copper pot reflected only the flavors already present and proved no secret blessing was needed. The first observation did not explain everything. Gu Wen marked the position in his notebook, while Lin Zhou asked a second person to repeat the measurement with fresh tools. A different result would have mattered as much as a matching one. A sample without its location and date could mislead an entire port. Gu Wen wrote both on the label while Lin Zhou counted the tools, taking care not to confuse an attractive result with a trustworthy one.

顧聞學會辨認林舟謹慎的沉默：藥師通常在思考他最不願接受的可能。銅匙的聲響必須與已知樣本比較才有用。兩人從一項人人只要願意細看就能核對的普通線索開始：古銅鍋只映出原有味道，證明不需要什麼祕密神賜。第一次觀察不能解釋一切。顧聞在筆記本標記位置，林舟則請第二個人用新工具重做測量。不同的結果和相同的結果一樣重要。缺少地點與日期的樣本可能誤導整座港口。顧聞把兩者寫上標籤，林舟清點工具，提醒自己不能把漂亮結果誤當可信結果。

A collector of old pots arrived at the festival with a comedian who kept the children laughing. Their compound stove was accessible from both sides, allowing wheelchair users to cook without assistance. The conception was simple: foster skills that families could inherit, rather than watch their heritage decay. A patrol officer tested the interval between each cooking round with a metal rod; the sheriff's questionnaire asked whether the new system would yield enough meals. Gu Wen resumed work in a guest suite, but noticed a subtle error in how the totals were underlined. Lin Zhou's perception was sharper: a vendor had counted the same flour twice. They corrected it before the feast. The result was not superb because of a spell; it was superb because people were willing to admit errors and make room for one another. After the break, they would resume cooking and underline every correction on the public tally.

一名舊鍋收藏家帶著喜劇演員來到節慶現場，逗得孩子們哈哈大笑。他們的複合爐灶兩側都能使用，坐輪椅的人無須別人協助也可以煮食。構想很簡單：培養能由家人繼承的技能，而不是看著傳統逐漸腐朽。巡邏員用金屬桿測量每輪烹調的間隔；警長的問卷則詢問新系統能否產出足夠餐點。顧聞在客房套間繼續工作，卻注意到總數的底線標示有一處細微錯誤。林舟看得更清楚：攤販把同一袋麵粉算了兩次。兩人在開宴前修正。成果出色不是因為法術，而是人們願意承認錯誤，替彼此留出空間。休息過後，他們會恢復烹煮，並在公開統計表上替每處修正畫底線。

The evidence now had to survive a public challenge. Some people wanted immediate punishment, others wanted the work stopped, and the cooks still needed to prepare supper before the lamps went out. After listening to workers and residents, Gu Wen proposed that they share a recipe while allowing each family to keep its unique seasoning. Some listeners objected. They had already lost wages, time, and confidence; a cautious solution sounded unbearably slow. Lin Zhou did not accuse them of being unreasonable. He showed them which part of the result was known and which part still had to be tested. When Gu Wen asked if the plan was fair, Lin Zhou answered that fairness had to survive the journey from a meeting room to a kitchen door.

證據如今必須接受公開質疑。有人要求立即懲罰，有人想停工，但廚師仍須在熄燈前準備晚餐。聽完工人與居民的意見後，顧聞提出：共享基本烹法，也讓每戶保有獨特調味。有人反對。他們已失去工資、時間和信心，謹慎的方法聽起來慢得難以忍受。林舟沒有指責他們不理性，只是指出哪些結果已知、哪些仍待檢驗。顧聞問方案是否公平，林舟回答：公平必須經得起從議事廳走到廚房門口的考驗。

A group of residents stayed after the official discussion and tried the procedure with their own hands. Their questions were specific: what was safe to touch, who would keep the key, and how could a failed test be reported? By the time the lamps were lit, one change could be seen: the festival meal restored confidence without pretending every polluted field had recovered. It was a limited victory. Nobody claimed the entire river had been healed, and the records were left where the people who had helped could check them. The work had names, dates, and mistakes recorded plainly instead of hidden behind a heroic speech. For the first time that day, Gu Wen allowed himself a mouthful of warm food. It tasted plain and honest, which seemed better than any spell he could perform.

正式討論結束後，一群居民留下來親手試做程序。他們的問題很具體：什麼可以碰、誰保管鑰匙、檢測失敗要如何通報？燈火亮起時，已有一項可見的改變：節慶餐點重新建立信心，卻沒有假裝污染田地都已復原。這只是有限的勝利。沒有人宣稱整條河已痊癒；紀錄也留在協助者可以查閱的地方。工作有姓名、日期，錯誤也明白記下，不藏在英雄式演說後。那天顧聞第一次讓自己吃上一口熱食。味道樸素而真實，比他能施展的任何法術都好。

The lamps made soft pools of light along the river. Gu Wen and Lin Zhou walked at the edge of the crowd, close enough to hear one another without having to compete with the cheering. A child insisted they sit down and eat from the same pot they had inspected all afternoon. Gu Wen passed Lin Zhou the first spoonful, and Lin Zhou offered him the crisp edge of the bread. The small exchange felt more important than the applause from the festival square. Their next direction arrived in an unexpected form: the sponsor arrived with an offer to purchase the hearth itself. They folded the new information into their map instead of calling it proof. At dawn they would travel again, but this time neither man would have to pretend he was walking alone.

燈光沿河映出柔和的光圈。顧聞與林舟走在人群邊緣，距離近得能聽見彼此，不必與歡呼聲較勁。一名孩子堅持要兩人坐下，吃他們整個下午都在檢驗的同一鍋湯。顧聞先替林舟盛了一匙，林舟則分給他麵包酥脆的邊。這個小小交換，比節慶廣場上的掌聲更重要。下一步的方向以意料之外的形式出現：贊助商帶著收購整座公共灶的提議出現。兩人把新訊息記上地圖，沒有直接稱它為證據。黎明時他們將再次啟程，但這次誰都不必假裝自己孤身上路。
