不是詛咒的發燒

A Fever Without a Curse

萬味渡口：與你煮一碗回家的湯 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The first five ports had not given Gu Wen and Lin Zhou an easy answer, but they had shown what a mistake could cost. They carried fewer assumptions now and more accurately labeled samples. At the Riverside Clinic, they faced a problem that the market announcements had described too simply: families blamed a new illness on a river spirit after several people became sick. People had gathered before sunrise, not because they expected a famous hero, but because the next meal might depend on what happened here. Some brought bowls; others carried questions they had asked for days. Gu Wen noticed how carefully the smallest children watched the adults. A promise that sounded generous from the council table might mean something very different to a family standing at the end of a queue.

前五港沒有給顧聞與林舟簡單答案，卻讓他們看見錯誤的代價。如今他們帶著更少的成見，以及標記更準確的樣本。在河岸診所，兩人面對一件市場公告說得過於簡單的難題：幾戶人家病倒後，居民把新疾病怪罪給河靈。日出前人們便聚集起來，不是期待名震天下的英雄，而是知道下一餐可能取決於這裡發生的事。有人帶來碗，有人帶著問了好幾天的疑惑。顧聞注意到最小的孩子如何仔細觀察大人。議會桌上聽來慷慨的承諾，對排在隊伍末尾的家庭可能完全不同。

Lin Zhou prepared two separate sample jars before he would discuss a result. One held material from the suspicious route; the other held a control sample obtained with a witness watching. They began with something ordinary that could be checked by anyone willing to look: tests linked the symptoms to contaminated steam from commercial kitchens rather than contagious breath. The first observation did not explain everything. Gu Wen marked the position in his notebook, while Lin Zhou asked a second person to repeat the measurement with fresh tools. A different result would have mattered as much as a matching one. Their copper pot might amplify a real aroma, but no enchantment could replace grain that had never been harvested. That distinction kept the work focused on actual supplies and actual people.

林舟在討論結果前，先備好兩個不同樣本罐。一罐裝可疑貨路的材料，一罐裝見證人監看下取得的對照樣本。兩人從一項人人只要願意細看就能核對的普通線索開始：檢測顯示症狀與商用廚房的污染蒸氣有關，並非呼吸傳染。第一次觀察不能解釋一切。顧聞在筆記本標記位置，林舟則請第二個人用新工具重做測量。不同的結果和相同的結果一樣重要。古銅鍋可以放大真實香味，卻沒有任何法術能取代從未收成的穀物。這個區別讓工作始終聚焦於真正的物資與人。

The institute's preliminary report described a possible allergy, though the clinic counselor urged caution before anyone could cite it as fact. A drought had increased the bulk transport of cheap salt, and collaboration among kitchens became harder as patients crowded the terminal. The doctor explained digestion with a descriptive chart; a nurse could not help but exclaim when contaminated steam rose from the grill. Gu Wen felt overwhelmed by a thriller-like rumor about a wild spirit, but Lin Zhou proposed a rational test rather than a radical ritual. The wilderness beyond the city was no answer either: moving sick families there would compel them to abandon treatment. The most important variable was exposure to hot salt, not the patient's origin. They wrote this clearly on the clinic door and began preparing safe meals. The growing crowd could overwhelm the nurses, so the cooks organized a separate waiting area.

研究所的初步報告指出可能發生過敏，但診所輔導員提醒，不能急著把它引用為事實。乾旱增加了廉價食鹽的大宗運輸，病患擠滿終點站後，各廚房更難合作。醫師用圖表描述消化作用；污染蒸氣從烤架升起時，護士忍不住驚呼。顧聞被一則驚悚小說般的河靈傳言壓得喘不過氣，但林舟主張理性檢測，而非激進儀式。城外荒野也不是解方，把生病家庭移去那裡等於強迫他們放棄治療。最重要的變因是接觸加熱過的污染鹽，而不是病患出身。兩人在診所門上清楚寫下這點，開始準備安全餐食。愈來愈多的人可能使護理師不堪負荷，因此廚師們另設等候區。

Every answer produced a new question about responsibility. It was easy to identify a flawed tool and much harder to decide who deserved help first when supplies were limited. After listening to workers and residents, Gu Wen proposed that they separate the ill from the source of exposure without isolating them from care. Some listeners objected. They had already lost wages, time, and confidence; a cautious solution sounded unbearably slow. Lin Zhou did not accuse them of being unreasonable. He showed them which part of the result was known and which part still had to be tested. When Gu Wen asked if the plan was fair, Lin Zhou answered that fairness had to survive the journey from a meeting room to a kitchen door.

每個答案又帶來新的責任問題。找出工具缺陷很容易，物資有限時決定誰該優先受助卻艱難得多。聽完工人與居民的意見後，顧聞提出：讓病患遠離暴露來源，卻不把他們隔離在照護之外。有人反對。他們已失去工資、時間和信心，謹慎的方法聽起來慢得難以忍受。林舟沒有指責他們不理性，只是指出哪些結果已知、哪些仍待檢驗。顧聞問方案是否公平，林舟回答：公平必須經得起從議事廳走到廚房門口的考驗。

The workshop had no splendid banner and needed none. One person fetched water, another tested the jars, and a third wrote down the objections so that even those who arrived late could understand the decision. By the time the lamps were lit, one change could be seen: Lin Zhou used his training to make a simple treatment plan, and Gu Wen organized safe meals. It was a limited victory. Nobody claimed the entire river had been healed, and the records were left where the people who had helped could check them. The work had names, dates, and mistakes recorded plainly instead of hidden behind a heroic speech. For the first time that day, Gu Wen allowed himself a mouthful of warm food. It tasted plain and honest, which seemed better than any spell he could perform.

工坊沒有華麗橫幅，也不需要。有人取水，有人測罐，另有人記下反對意見，讓遲來的人也能理解決定。燈火亮起時，已有一項可見的改變：林舟運用訓練擬出簡單照護方案，顧聞則組織安全餐食。這只是有限的勝利。沒有人宣稱整條河已痊癒；紀錄也留在協助者可以查閱的地方。工作有姓名、日期，錯誤也明白記下，不藏在英雄式演說後。那天顧聞第一次讓自己吃上一口熱食。味道樸素而真實，比他能施展的任何法術都好。

By dusk, the noise of the workshop had softened into the rattle of dishes and distant conversation. The two men finally found time to speak about something besides the mineral. Gu Wen stayed at the clinic after the last bowls were served, though nobody had asked him to. Lin Zhou found him cleaning the dishes beside exhausted nurses. The herbalist placed a cup of cool water at his elbow and sat down instead of telling him to go to sleep. Their next direction arrived in an unexpected form: the clinic director finally named the supplier who had authorized the salt. They folded the new information into their map instead of calling it proof. At dawn they would travel again, but this time neither man would have to pretend he was walking alone.

黃昏時，工坊的喧鬧變成洗碗聲與遠處談話。兩人終於有空談些礦物以外的事。最後一批湯送完後，顧聞仍留在診所，儘管沒有人要求。林舟找到他時，他正與疲憊的護理師一起洗碗。藥師在他手肘旁放下一杯涼水，坐下來陪他，而不是命令他去睡覺。下一步的方向以意料之外的形式出現：診所主任終於說出批准這批鹽的供應商。兩人把新訊息記上地圖，沒有直接稱它為證據。黎明時他們將再次啟程，但這次誰都不必假裝自己孤身上路。
