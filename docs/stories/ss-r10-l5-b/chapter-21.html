墓園旁的一只水壺

A Jug beside the Burial Ground

萬味渡口：與你煮一碗回家的湯 · 第 21 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

Near the end of the seven-port route, Gu Wen and Lin Zhou no longer mistook exhaustion for courage. They had learned to ask for help, identify safe limits, and let people decide which promises to accept. At the Quiet Burial Garden, they faced a problem that the market announcements had described too simply: a retired miner gave testimony beside the grave of his brother, who had warned about hollow salt years ago. Some brought bowls; others carried questions they had asked for days. Gu Wen noticed how carefully the smallest children watched the adults.

接近七港旅程終點時，顧聞與林舟已不再把疲憊誤當勇氣。他們學會求助、辨認安全界線，也讓人們自行決定接受哪些承諾。在寂靜墓園，兩人面對一件市場公告說得過於簡單的難題：退休礦工在兄長墓前作證，兄長多年前曾警告空鹽危險。有人帶來碗，有人帶著問了好幾天的疑惑。

A fair inspection required ordinary tools as well as trained senses. They brought clean cloth, fresh bottles, water, and a list of places where a second test could be repeated without permission from the merchant. They began with something ordinary that could be checked by anyone willing to look: the warning had been dismissed, not because evidence was absent, but because it would have disrupted a valuable contract. The first observation did not explain everything. Neither man proposed using a miracle to erase the damage. Heat could release a smell, and herbs could reveal a change, but toxic mineral still had to be contained, collected, and handled by trained people.

公平檢驗不只需要敏銳感官，也需要普通工具。他們帶著乾淨布料、新瓶子、水，以及不必取得商人許可便可複驗的地點清單。兩人從一項人人只要願意細看就能核對的普通線索開始：警告被忽略，不是沒有證據，而是會破壞利益龐大的合約。第一次觀察不能解釋一切。不同的結果和相同的結果一樣重要。兩人都沒有提議用神蹟抹除損害。熱能釋放氣味，藥草能顯示變化，但有毒礦物仍須由受過訓練的人隔離、收集與處理。

At the burial garden, affection and grief made an unlikely alliance. The old miner asked the commission to authorize the donation of his brother's field, though the land was barren and the stones appeared eternal. During a small carnival nearby, children carried a jug of water and shared mustard bread. A former convict explained the mechanism by which dangerous salt had entered the market, while a propaganda officer tried to proclaim the company innocent. The repair squad cared more about operational stability than speeches. They tested the water transmission line again, checking whether new consumption would overload it. No one could give a clear answer to every vague rumor. Lin Zhou held Gu Wen's hand as they listened to the miner's testimony, understanding that grief deserved a place at the table without being turned into another form of currency. The miner chose to donate his old measuring tools so that apprentices could learn the tests for themselves.

墓園裡，愛意與哀傷結成意想不到的聯盟。老礦工請委員會授權捐出兄長的田，儘管土地荒蕪，石頭看似永恆。附近的小嘉年華上，孩子們提著水壺，分食芥末麵包。一名曾服刑的人解釋危險食鹽進入市場的機制，宣傳官卻試圖宣告公司無罪。修復小隊比起演說，更在乎系統能否穩定運作。他們再次測試輸水管線，確認新用水量不會造成超載。沒人能回答每一則含糊傳聞。林舟握著顧聞的手，一起聽礦工作證，明白哀傷應在餐桌上有位置，而不該變成另一種貨幣。礦工決定捐出舊量測工具，好讓學徒自己學會檢測。

Near the final decision, caution meant more than a warning spoken from a safe distance. People needed an evacuation route, a usable kitchen, and a clear reason for every delay. After listening to workers and residents, Gu Wen proposed that they record the brother's contribution without making his death a tool for dramatic victory. Some listeners objected. Lin Zhou did not accuse them of being unreasonable.

接近最終決定時，謹慎已不只是站在安全處發出警告。人們需要撤離路線、可用廚房，以及每一次延誤的清楚理由。聽完工人與居民的意見後，顧聞提出：留下兄長的貢獻，不把他的死亡當作戲劇性勝利的工具。有人反對。

The repair crews moved with the deliberate pace of people who knew that a shortcut could put strangers in danger. Neither traveler stood above them giving orders from a dry and comfortable platform. By the time the lamps were lit, one change could be seen: Lin Zhou took Gu Wen's hand in public for the first time and asked whether he wished to walk together after the case. It was a limited victory.

修復工班按部就班，因為他們知道捷徑可能害陌生人陷入危險。兩位旅人都沒有站在乾燥舒服的高臺上對工人發號施令。燈火亮起時，已有一項可見的改變：林舟首次在眾人面前牽起顧聞的手，問他案件結束後願不願繼續同行。這只是有限的勝利。那天顧聞第一次讓自己吃上一口熱食。

They had once imagined that finishing the case would mean returning to separate lives. Now their maps lay side by side on the table, and both were learning how to make a future without taking the other for granted. Beside the old miner's brother's grave, Lin Zhou took Gu Wen's hand where everyone could see. He asked, quietly and without demanding an answer, whether Gu Wen might want to keep walking beside him after the case. Gu Wen squeezed his fingers and said that he wanted to discuss the road together. Their next direction arrived in an unexpected form: the commission prepared its final hearing.

他們曾以為案件結束就要回到各自生活。如今兩張地圖並排攤在桌上，兩人都在學習如何規劃未來，卻不把對方的陪伴視為理所當然。在老礦工兄長的墓旁，林舟當著所有人的面牽起顧聞的手。他輕聲問，案件結束後顧聞是否願意繼續同行，沒有要求立即回答。顧聞輕握他的手，說自己願意一起商量往後的路。下一步的方向以意料之外的形式出現：委員會準備召開最終聽證。
