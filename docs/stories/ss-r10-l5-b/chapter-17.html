未寫完食譜的閣樓

The Attic of Unfinished Recipes

萬味渡口：與你煮一碗回家的湯 · 第 17 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

After the first honest feast, the travelers had to deal with success as carefully as failure. A generous offer might conceal a demand for control, while an old friendship could reveal a painful omission. At the Lantern Guesthouse, they faced a problem that the market announcements had described too simply: Gu Wen found a notebook left by his mother, full of substitutions made during the last famine. People had gathered before sunrise, not because they expected a famous hero, but because the next meal might depend on what happened here. Some brought bowls; others carried questions they had asked for days. Gu Wen noticed how carefully the smallest children watched the adults. A promise that sounded generous from the council table might mean something very different to a family standing at the end of a queue.

第一場誠實宴席之後，兩人發現成功也必須像失敗一樣謹慎看待。慷慨提議可能藏著控制要求，舊日情誼也可能揭露痛苦的疏漏。在燈影客舍，兩人面對一件市場公告說得過於簡單的難題：顧聞找到母親留下的筆記，裡面滿是上次飢荒時採用的替代食材。日出前人們便聚集起來，不是期待名震天下的英雄，而是知道下一餐可能取決於這裡發生的事。有人帶來碗，有人帶著問了好幾天的疑惑。顧聞注意到最小的孩子如何仔細觀察大人。議會桌上聽來慷慨的承諾，對排在隊伍末尾的家庭可能完全不同。

They separated questions about ingredients from questions about ownership. A cooking method could be taught openly without forcing anyone to disclose the story of an illness or a family recipe. They began with something ordinary that could be checked by anyone willing to look: its final pages listed people who had shared meals when the family restaurant failed. The first observation did not explain everything. Gu Wen marked the position in his notebook, while Lin Zhou asked a second person to repeat the measurement with fresh tools. A different result would have mattered as much as a matching one. As the political dispute grew, their rule about consent became more important. Witnesses could choose a private hearing; cooks could contribute food without handing over every secret in their family notebooks.

兩人將食材問題與所有權問題分開。烹法可以公開教授，卻不該因此強迫誰揭露病情經歷或家傳食譜。兩人從一項人人只要願意細看就能核對的普通線索開始：末頁記著家族餐館倒閉時曾分食共餐的人。第一次觀察不能解釋一切。顧聞在筆記本標記位置，林舟則請第二個人用新工具重做測量。不同的結果和相同的結果一樣重要。政治爭議愈大，兩人關於同意的規則就愈重要。證人可以選擇非公開聽證；廚師也能提供食物，而不必交出家族筆記裡的每個祕密。

In the attic, the accounting ledger lay beside a legendary recipe, its paper texture rough from years of smoke. Gu Wen adored his mother's handwriting, but Lin Zhou asked to conduct a careful reading instead of turning the page into a confession. An old delegate described the destructive theft that had closed the restaurant, and the likelihood that the thief's motive had been debt rather than hatred. Through the doorway came a cold draft; Gu Wen began to shiver and sniff at the scent of dried citrus. He recognized the devotion hidden in his mother's small substitutions. No supreme authority had ordered her to feed strangers. The notebook itself would initiate no miracle, but it showed that the family had chosen generosity even while the unpaid bills caused torment. Gu Wen closed the book gently and asked Lin Zhou to read the final page with him. Gu Wen could still adore his mother without believing her life was comparable to his own. A clean filter lay inside the old stove, carefully wrapped for another season.

閣樓裡，帳本放在一本傳說中的食譜旁，紙張紋理被多年炊煙燻得粗糙。顧聞珍愛母親的筆跡，林舟卻請他先仔細閱讀，不要把每一頁都當成供詞。一名老代表談起當年使餐館關門的破壞性竊案，並認為竊賊很可能是因債務而非仇恨行事。冷風從門口吹入，顧聞打了個寒顫，嗅到柑橘乾的香氣。他讀懂母親在小小食材替換裡藏著的奉獻。沒有至高權威命令她餵養陌生人。筆記本不會啟動奇蹟，卻顯示一家人在未付帳單的折磨下，仍選擇慷慨。顧聞輕輕闔上書，邀林舟一起讀最後一頁。顧聞可以依然深愛母親，而不必認為她的人生與自己完全相同。舊爐內放著一片乾淨濾網，仔細包好留待下一季。

An apparent victory made the choice more delicate. The market wanted a triumphant announcement, while the families asked whether the repair would last beyond the festival. After listening to workers and residents, Gu Wen proposed that they read the unfinished pages aloud but keep the private names from public circulation. Some listeners objected. They had already lost wages, time, and confidence; a cautious solution sounded unbearably slow. Lin Zhou did not accuse them of being unreasonable. He showed them which part of the result was known and which part still had to be tested. When Gu Wen asked if the plan was fair, Lin Zhou answered that fairness had to survive the journey from a meeting room to a kitchen door.

表面上的勝利反而使選擇更微妙。市場想聽凱旋宣言，家庭卻關心修復能否撐過節慶以後。聽完工人與居民的意見後，顧聞提出：讀出未完成的食譜，卻不公開私人姓名。有人反對。他們已失去工資、時間和信心，謹慎的方法聽起來慢得難以忍受。林舟沒有指責他們不理性，只是指出哪些結果已知、哪些仍待檢驗。顧聞問方案是否公平，林舟回答：公平必須經得起從議事廳走到廚房門口的考驗。

The common hearth became a place where arguments could turn into work. Some washed vegetables; some repaired the benches; others kept the accounts. Each task gave a different answer to the question of who owned the feast. By the time the lamps were lit, one change could be seen: Lin Zhou showed Gu Wen the herb field he wanted to cultivate after the journey. It was a limited victory. Nobody claimed the entire river had been healed, and the records were left where the people who had helped could check them. The work had names, dates, and mistakes recorded plainly instead of hidden behind a heroic speech. For the first time that day, Gu Wen allowed himself a mouthful of warm food. It tasted plain and honest, which seemed better than any spell he could perform.

公共灶成了讓爭論轉化為工作的地方。有人洗菜，有人修長椅，有人管帳。每份工作都為「宴席屬於誰」提出不同答案。燈火亮起時，已有一項可見的改變：林舟帶顧聞看自己想在旅程結束後耕種的藥草田。這只是有限的勝利。沒有人宣稱整條河已痊癒；紀錄也留在協助者可以查閱的地方。工作有姓名、日期，錯誤也明白記下，不藏在英雄式演說後。那天顧聞第一次讓自己吃上一口熱食。味道樸素而真實，比他能施展的任何法術都好。

Once the last worker had gone home, the two men remained to check the hearth. It was the quiet work afterward, not the speeches before, that let them understand each other. Among his mother's recipes, Gu Wen found a blank page titled 'For the person who stays.' He showed it to Lin Zhou without a joke. Lin Zhou said he hoped to plant a small herb garden after their journey, but he did not ask Gu Wen to choose it as his own dream. Their next direction arrived in an unexpected form: a former rival asked to meet them at the orchard crossing. They folded the new information into their map instead of calling it proof. At dawn they would travel again, but this time neither man would have to pretend he was walking alone.

最後一名工人回家後，兩人仍留下檢查爐灶。讓他們了解彼此的，是演說之後安靜的工作，而不是先前的漂亮話。顧聞在母親食譜中找到一頁空白，標題是「留在身邊的人」。他沒有開玩笑，直接給林舟看。林舟說希望旅程結束後種一片小藥草園，但沒有要求顧聞把這當成自己的夢想。下一步的方向以意料之外的形式出現：一名舊日對手約他們在果園路口見面。兩人把新訊息記上地圖，沒有直接稱它為證據。黎明時他們將再次啟程，但這次誰都不必假裝自己孤身上路。
