熱浪下的灶帳

The Heat Wave Ledger

萬味渡口：與你煮一碗回家的湯 · 第 20 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

After the first honest feast, the travelers had to deal with success as carefully as failure. A generous offer might conceal a demand for control, while an old friendship could reveal a painful omission. At the Seven-Port Kitchens, they faced a problem that the market announcements had described too simply: extreme heat weakened food stores while the merchant demanded immediate repayment. People had gathered before sunrise, not because they expected a famous hero, but because the next meal might depend on what happened here. Some brought bowls; others carried questions they had asked for days. Gu Wen noticed how carefully the smallest children watched the adults. A promise that sounded generous from the council table might mean something very different to a family standing at the end of a queue.

第一場誠實宴席之後，兩人發現成功也必須像失敗一樣謹慎看待。慷慨提議可能藏著控制要求，舊日情誼也可能揭露痛苦的疏漏。在七港公共廚房，兩人面對一件市場公告說得過於簡單的難題：極端高溫破壞糧食儲藏，商人卻要求立即還款。日出前人們便聚集起來，不是期待名震天下的英雄，而是知道下一餐可能取決於這裡發生的事。有人帶來碗，有人帶著問了好幾天的疑惑。顧聞注意到最小的孩子如何仔細觀察大人。議會桌上聽來慷慨的承諾，對排在隊伍末尾的家庭可能完全不同。

They separated questions about ingredients from questions about ownership. A cooking method could be taught openly without forcing anyone to disclose the story of an illness or a family recipe. They began with something ordinary that could be checked by anyone willing to look: the safest route for water carts crossed the same warehouse lane that held the contaminated salt. The first observation did not explain everything. Gu Wen marked the position in his notebook, while Lin Zhou asked a second person to repeat the measurement with fresh tools. A different result would have mattered as much as a matching one. As the political dispute grew, their rule about consent became more important. Witnesses could choose a private hearing; cooks could contribute food without handing over every secret in their family notebooks.

兩人將食材問題與所有權問題分開。烹法可以公開教授，卻不該因此強迫誰揭露病情經歷或家傳食譜。兩人從一項人人只要願意細看就能核對的普通線索開始：最安全的水車路線必須穿過存有污染鹽的倉庫巷道。第一次觀察不能解釋一切。顧聞在筆記本標記位置，林舟則請第二個人用新工具重做測量。不同的結果和相同的結果一樣重要。政治爭議愈大，兩人關於同意的規則就愈重要。證人可以選擇非公開聽證；廚師也能提供食物，而不必交出家族筆記裡的每個祕密。

The heat wave brought a deadly complication: a narrow canal became a constraint on the water carts just as the old salt store began to erupt in dust. A graphic report showed the ratio of contaminated cargo to safe stock, but its interpretation remained disputed. A senator offered a dramatic intervention and promised unprecedented revenue if the port would accept another private contract. Gu Wen was tempted to agree until Lin Zhou showed him a fragment of black mineral beside the vacuum vent. They had to qualify the promise before trusting it. The elaborate plan might unfold beautifully on paper, yet a warrior could not carry drinking water through an unsafe market by courage alone. The mainstream rumor blamed the river; the actual cause required slower, less glorious repair. A false warning about radiation spread through the market. Gu Wen would not tempt anyone into ignoring a real danger just because that particular rumor was wrong.

熱浪帶來致命的複雜情況：狹窄運河限制水車通行，舊鹽倉卻開始爆出粉塵。圖表報告顯示污染貨物與安全庫存的比率，但如何解讀仍有爭議。一名參議員提出戲劇性的干預措施，承諾只要港口再簽私人合約，就能獲得前所未有的收入。顧聞差點心動，直到林舟在真空排氣口旁指給他看一塊黑色礦物碎片。承諾必須先經查核。繁複的計畫在紙上可能展開得很漂亮，然而再勇敢的戰士，也不能只靠勇氣穿越危險市集運送飲水。主流傳言責怪河流，真正原因卻需要慢而不耀眼的修復。關於輻射的假警報在市集傳開。顧聞不會因這則傳聞錯誤，就引誘別人忽略真正的危險。

An apparent victory made the choice more delicate. The market wanted a triumphant announcement, while the families asked whether the repair would last beyond the festival. After listening to workers and residents, Gu Wen proposed that they keep cooling stations running even if the public tasting had to be postponed. Some listeners objected. They had already lost wages, time, and confidence; a cautious solution sounded unbearably slow. Lin Zhou did not accuse them of being unreasonable. He showed them which part of the result was known and which part still had to be tested. When Gu Wen asked if the plan was fair, Lin Zhou answered that fairness had to survive the journey from a meeting room to a kitchen door.

表面上的勝利反而使選擇更微妙。市場想聽凱旋宣言，家庭卻關心修復能否撐過節慶以後。聽完工人與居民的意見後，顧聞提出：維持降溫站運作，即使公開試味必須延後。有人反對。他們已失去工資、時間和信心，謹慎的方法聽起來慢得難以忍受。林舟沒有指責他們不理性，只是指出哪些結果已知、哪些仍待檢驗。顧聞問方案是否公平，林舟回答：公平必須經得起從議事廳走到廚房門口的考驗。

The common hearth became a place where arguments could turn into work. Some washed vegetables; some repaired the benches; others kept the accounts. Each task gave a different answer to the question of who owned the feast. By the time the lamps were lit, one change could be seen: Gu Wen handed command of the kitchen to a younger cook so he could help Lin Zhou protect the wells. It was a limited victory. Nobody claimed the entire river had been healed, and the records were left where the people who had helped could check them. The work had names, dates, and mistakes recorded plainly instead of hidden behind a heroic speech. For the first time that day, Gu Wen allowed himself a mouthful of warm food. It tasted plain and honest, which seemed better than any spell he could perform.

公共灶成了讓爭論轉化為工作的地方。有人洗菜，有人修長椅，有人管帳。每份工作都為「宴席屬於誰」提出不同答案。燈火亮起時，已有一項可見的改變：顧聞把廚房指揮交給年輕廚師，自己協助林舟守護水井。這只是有限的勝利。沒有人宣稱整條河已痊癒；紀錄也留在協助者可以查閱的地方。工作有姓名、日期，錯誤也明白記下，不藏在英雄式演說後。那天顧聞第一次讓自己吃上一口熱食。味道樸素而真實，比他能施展的任何法術都好。

Once the last worker had gone home, the two men remained to check the hearth. It was the quiet work afterward, not the speeches before, that let them understand each other. The heat made Gu Wen's hands shake, and Lin Zhou asked him to rest. Gu Wen protested until he saw how tired Lin Zhou looked too. They traded jobs for one hour and discovered that caring for each other sometimes meant refusing to be indispensable. Their next direction arrived in an unexpected form: a message reported that the old salt vault had begun to crack. They folded the new information into their map instead of calling it proof. At dawn they would travel again, but this time neither man would have to pretend he was walking alone.

最後一名工人回家後，兩人仍留下檢查爐灶。讓他們了解彼此的，是演說之後安靜的工作，而不是先前的漂亮話。高溫使顧聞雙手發抖，林舟請他休息。顧聞原本抗議，直到看見林舟也累得厲害。兩人交換工作一小時，發現彼此照顧有時意味著不必凡事都親自上陣。下一步的方向以意料之外的形式出現：消息傳來，古鹽庫開始出現裂縫。兩人把新訊息記上地圖，沒有直接稱它為證據。黎明時他們將再次啟程，但這次誰都不必假裝自己孤身上路。
