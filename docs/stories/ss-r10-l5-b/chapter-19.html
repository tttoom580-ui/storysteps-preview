The Caravan of New Neighbors

After the first honest feast, the travelers had to deal with success as carefully as failure. A generous offer might conceal a demand for control, while an old friendship could reveal a painful omission. At the Central Exchange Port, they faced a problem that the market announcements had described too simply: families displaced by bad water arrived carrying recipes from distant valleys. People had gathered before sunrise, not because they expected a famous hero, but because the next meal might depend on what happened here. Some brought bowls; others carried questions they had asked for days. Gu Wen noticed how carefully the smallest children watched the adults. A promise that sounded generous from the council table might mean something very different to a family standing at the end of a queue.

第一場誠實宴席之後，兩人發現成功也必須像失敗一樣謹慎看待。慷慨提議可能藏著控制要求，舊日情誼也可能揭露痛苦的疏漏。在中央交換港，兩人面對一件市場公告說得過於簡單的難題：受污染飲水迫遷的家庭帶著遠方山谷食譜抵達。日出前人們便聚集起來，不是期待名震天下的英雄，而是知道下一餐可能取決於這裡發生的事。有人帶來碗，有人帶著問了好幾天的疑惑。顧聞注意到最小的孩子如何仔細觀察大人。議會桌上聽來慷慨的承諾，對排在隊伍末尾的家庭可能完全不同。

They separated questions about ingredients from questions about ownership. A cooking method could be taught openly without forcing anyone to disclose the story of an illness or a family recipe. They began with something ordinary that could be checked by anyone willing to look: the port had enough grain but no agreed way to distribute safe salt among newcomers. The first observation did not explain everything. Gu Wen marked the position in his notebook, while Lin Zhou asked a second person to repeat the measurement with fresh tools. A different result would have mattered as much as a matching one. As the political dispute grew, their rule about consent became more important. Witnesses could choose a private hearing; cooks could contribute food without handing over every secret in their family notebooks.

兩人將食材問題與所有權問題分開。烹法可以公開教授，卻不該因此強迫誰揭露病情經歷或家傳食譜。兩人從一項人人只要願意細看就能核對的普通線索開始：港口糧食足夠，卻沒有公認的辦法向新來者分配安全食鹽。第一次觀察不能解釋一切。顧聞在筆記本標記位置，林舟則請第二個人用新工具重做測量。不同的結果和相同的結果一樣重要。政治爭議愈大，兩人關於同意的規則就愈重要。證人可以選擇非公開聽證；廚師也能提供食物，而不必交出家族筆記裡的每個祕密。

A compact market plan required competence from every cook and careful coordination among the arriving families. On their behalf, Gu Wen arranged a donation of grain and a public debut for three young vendors. An opponent insisted that destiny should decide whose domain received the salt first, but the council preferred a clear output target. The migration had brought muscular porters alongside people with little strength; not all were immune to the illness that had troubled the last port. Lin Zhou attributed the growing trust to shared work, not to any one hero. At noon a spectacular meal almost failed when a runner had to scramble for a missing sponge and cracked floor tile. Gu Wen asked the vendor to keep the line moving while he repaired the corner. The meal succeeded because different kinds of work were valued together. It was unfair to attribute every error to migrants. The cooks needed to coordinate the final meal and execute the agreed safety steps.

緊湊的市場計畫需要每位廚師的能力，也需要仔細協調新抵達的家庭。顧聞代表大家安排糧食捐贈，讓三名年輕攤販首次公開營業。一名反對者堅稱命運應決定哪片領域先拿到鹽，議會卻偏好清楚的產出目標。遷徙人潮中有肌肉結實的搬運工，也有體力較差的人；不是每個人都對上一港的疾病免疫。林舟把逐漸建立的信任歸功於共同工作，而非某位英雄。正午的精彩餐食差點失敗：跑腿者四處奔找遺失的海綿與裂開的地磚。顧聞讓攤販維持隊伍，自己修好角落。餐點之所以成功，是因為不同的勞動都受到重視。把每個錯誤都歸咎於移民並不公平。廚師需要協調最後一餐，並執行已同意的安全步驟。

An apparent victory made the choice more delicate. The market wanted a triumphant announcement, while the families asked whether the repair would last beyond the festival. After listening to workers and residents, Gu Wen proposed that they let newcomers participate in planning rather than assign them a silent place in the queue. Some listeners objected. They had already lost wages, time, and confidence; a cautious solution sounded unbearably slow. Lin Zhou did not accuse them of being unreasonable. He showed them which part of the result was known and which part still had to be tested. When Gu Wen asked if the plan was fair, Lin Zhou answered that fairness had to survive the journey from a meeting room to a kitchen door.

表面上的勝利反而使選擇更微妙。市場想聽凱旋宣言，家庭卻關心修復能否撐過節慶以後。聽完工人與居民的意見後，顧聞提出：讓新來者參與規劃，而不是只能安靜排隊。有人反對。他們已失去工資、時間和信心，謹慎的方法聽起來慢得難以忍受。林舟沒有指責他們不理性，只是指出哪些結果已知、哪些仍待檢驗。顧聞問方案是否公平，林舟回答：公平必須經得起從議事廳走到廚房門口的考驗。

The common hearth became a place where arguments could turn into work. Some washed vegetables; some repaired the benches; others kept the accounts. Each task gave a different answer to the question of who owned the feast. By the time the lamps were lit, one change could be seen: Lin Zhou apologized for hiding his teacher's early involvement, and Gu Wen chose to listen rather than punish. It was a limited victory. Nobody claimed the entire river had been healed, and the records were left where the people who had helped could check them. The work had names, dates, and mistakes recorded plainly instead of hidden behind a heroic speech. For the first time that day, Gu Wen allowed himself a mouthful of warm food. It tasted plain and honest, which seemed better than any spell he could perform.

公共灶成了讓爭論轉化為工作的地方。有人洗菜，有人修長椅，有人管帳。每份工作都為「宴席屬於誰」提出不同答案。燈火亮起時，已有一項可見的改變：林舟為隱瞞老師早期參與一事道歉，顧聞選擇傾聽而非懲罰。這只是有限的勝利。沒有人宣稱整條河已痊癒；紀錄也留在協助者可以查閱的地方。工作有姓名、日期，錯誤也明白記下，不藏在英雄式演說後。那天顧聞第一次讓自己吃上一口熱食。味道樸素而真實，比他能施展的任何法術都好。

Once the last worker had gone home, the two men remained to check the hearth. It was the quiet work afterward, not the speeches before, that let them understand each other. Lin Zhou apologized for not telling Gu Wen sooner about his teacher. This time Gu Wen did not demand a promise that secrets would never return. He asked for a simpler promise: when a secret affected their work, they would speak before the danger reached strangers. Lin Zhou agreed. Their next direction arrived in an unexpected form: a fierce heat wave threatened every shared kitchen. They folded the new information into their map instead of calling it proof. At dawn they would travel again, but this time neither man would have to pretend he was walking alone.

最後一名工人回家後，兩人仍留下檢查爐灶。讓他們了解彼此的，是演說之後安靜的工作，而不是先前的漂亮話。林舟為沒早點說出老師的事道歉。這次顧聞沒有要求他保證以後永遠沒有祕密，只提出更簡單的約定：當祕密影響工作時，要在危險波及陌生人前說出來。林舟同意。下一步的方向以意料之外的形式出現：猛烈熱浪威脅所有共享廚房。兩人把新訊息記上地圖，沒有直接稱它為證據。黎明時他們將再次啟程，但這次誰都不必假裝自己孤身上路。
