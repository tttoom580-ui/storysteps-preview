慶典的價碼

The Festival Price

After the first honest feast, the travelers had to deal with success as carefully as failure. A generous offer might conceal a demand for control, while an old friendship could reveal a painful omission. At the Merchant's Pavilion, they faced a problem that the market announcements had described too simply: a wealthy buyer tried to purchase the common hearth and all future festival meals. Some brought bowls; others carried questions they had asked for days. Gu Wen noticed how carefully the smallest children watched the adults.

第一場誠實宴席之後，兩人發現成功也必須像失敗一樣謹慎看待。慷慨提議可能藏著控制要求，舊日情誼也可能揭露痛苦的疏漏。在商人展亭，兩人面對一件市場公告說得過於簡單的難題：一名富商試圖買下公共灶和未來所有節慶餐點。有人帶來碗，有人帶著問了好幾天的疑惑。

They separated questions about ingredients from questions about ownership. A cooking method could be taught openly without forcing anyone to disclose the story of an illness or a family recipe. They began with something ordinary that could be checked by anyone willing to look: the sale would have turned freely shared techniques into a private product. The first observation did not explain everything. As the political dispute grew, their rule about consent became more important. Witnesses could choose a private hearing; cooks could contribute food without handing over every secret in their family notebooks.

兩人將食材問題與所有權問題分開。烹法可以公開教授，卻不該因此強迫誰揭露病情經歷或家傳食譜。兩人從一項人人只要願意細看就能核對的普通線索開始：這筆買賣會把自由共享的技術變成私人商品。第一次觀察不能解釋一切。不同的結果和相同的結果一樣重要。政治爭議愈大，兩人關於同意的規則就愈重要。證人可以選擇非公開聽證；廚師也能提供食物，而不必交出家族筆記裡的每個祕密。

The enterprise displayed a bright banner on canvas outside a mansion, hoping to manipulate the festival into a private celebration. Its confidential curriculum promised conservation lessons but allocated no money to protect the river. A miniature hockey rink stood beside a pillar, an odd attraction for the children. The owner, an alcoholic official with an unshaken conviction of his own honor, declared it a dilemma: sell the public stove or abandon the feast. Gu Wen did not perceive those as the only choices. Lin Zhou asked the guests to consider an honorable sequence of repairs, with each cost recorded openly. The sudden crash of a tray startled the crowd, but neither man treated the noise as a sign demanding worship. They would not surrender the kitchen because someone with money had spoken loudly. The council must allocate funds fairly. A sudden crash could startle the children even when no one was hurt.

企業在豪宅外的帆布上掛出鮮亮橫幅，希望操控節慶，將其變成私人宴會。它的機密課程承諾教授保育，卻沒有分配任何經費保護河流。柱子旁有一座迷你曲棍球場，對孩子而言是古怪的遊樂設施。企業主人是名酗酒官員，堅信自己光榮正直，聲稱大家面臨兩難：賣掉公共灶，或放棄宴席。顧聞不認為只有這兩個選項。林舟請賓客思考正當的修復順序，逐筆公開記錄費用。托盤忽然摔落，嚇了人群一跳，但兩人沒有把聲響當成必須崇拜的徵兆。他們不會只因有錢人大聲說話就交出廚房。議會必須公平分配經費。突然的撞擊聲即使沒傷到人，也可能嚇著孩子。

An apparent victory made the choice more delicate. The market wanted a triumphant announcement, while the families asked whether the repair would last beyond the festival. After listening to workers and residents, Gu Wen proposed that they reject the offer without denying workers their wages for real labor. Some listeners objected. Lin Zhou did not accuse them of being unreasonable.

表面上的勝利反而使選擇更微妙。市場想聽凱旋宣言，家庭卻關心修復能否撐過節慶以後。聽完工人與居民的意見後，顧聞提出：拒絕收購，但不否定工人應得的勞動報酬。有人反對。

The common hearth became a place where arguments could turn into work. Some washed vegetables; some repaired the benches; others kept the accounts. Each task gave a different answer to the question of who owned the feast. By the time the lamps were lit, one change could be seen: Lin Zhou explained why he feared long promises; Gu Wen stopped demanding an immediate answer about their future. It was a limited victory.

公共灶成了讓爭論轉化為工作的地方。有人洗菜，有人修長椅，有人管帳。每份工作都為「宴席屬於誰」提出不同答案。燈火亮起時，已有一項可見的改變：林舟說出自己害怕長期承諾的原因，顧聞不再催促兩人立刻決定未來。這只是有限的勝利。那天顧聞第一次讓自己吃上一口熱食。

Once the last worker had gone home, the two men remained to check the hearth. It was the quiet work afterward, not the speeches before, that let them understand each other. When a sponsor tried to buy their future, Lin Zhou admitted that long commitments frightened him. Gu Wen wanted to say that he would never leave, but a promise made in anger would be cheap. Instead he told Lin Zhou that affection was not a contract signed under pressure. Their next direction arrived in an unexpected form: an old recipe notebook was discovered in the guesthouse attic.

最後一名工人回家後，兩人仍留下檢查爐灶。讓他們了解彼此的，是演說之後安靜的工作，而不是先前的漂亮話。贊助商想買下兩人的未來時，林舟坦承自己害怕長期承諾。顧聞想說自己永遠不會走，但在怒氣中許下的承諾太廉價。他只是告訴林舟：喜愛不是在壓力下簽的合約。下一步的方向以意料之外的形式出現：客舍閣樓發現一本古老的食譜筆記。
