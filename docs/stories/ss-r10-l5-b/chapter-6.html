銅島的醋香

Vinegar on Copper Island

萬味渡口：與你煮一碗回家的湯 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The first five ports had not given Gu Wen and Lin Zhou an easy answer, but they had shown what a mistake could cost. They carried fewer assumptions now and more accurately labeled samples. At Copper Island, they faced a problem that the market announcements had described too simply: a famous vinegar shed had lost half its workers after an alarming taste trial. Some brought bowls; others carried questions they had asked for days. Gu Wen noticed how carefully the smallest children watched the adults.

前五港沒有給顧聞與林舟簡單答案，卻讓他們看見錯誤的代價。如今他們帶著更少的成見，以及標記更準確的樣本。在銅島，兩人面對一件市場公告說得過於簡單的難題：一家知名醋坊在可疑試味後失去了半數工人。有人帶來碗，有人帶著問了好幾天的疑惑。

Lin Zhou prepared two separate sample jars before he would discuss a result. One held material from the suspicious route; the other held a control sample obtained with a witness watching. They began with something ordinary that could be checked by anyone willing to look: older ceramic jars preserved the smell that the new metal containers absorbed. The first observation did not explain everything. Their copper pot might amplify a real aroma, but no enchantment could replace grain that had never been harvested. That distinction kept the work focused on actual supplies and actual people.

林舟在討論結果前，先備好兩個不同樣本罐。一罐裝可疑貨路的材料，一罐裝見證人監看下取得的對照樣本。兩人從一項人人只要願意細看就能核對的普通線索開始：舊陶罐留住了新金屬容器吸走的氣味。第一次觀察不能解釋一切。不同的結果和相同的結果一樣重要。古銅鍋可以放大真實香味，卻沒有任何法術能取代從未收成的穀物。這個區別讓工作始終聚焦於真正的物資與人。

The municipal agenda contained an unusual genre of report: a metaphor comparing the river to a baseball inning, as if polluted water could be erased when the score changed. The federal inspector was the council's nominee, but he seemed unable to comprehend the organism growing inside the salt jars. Lin Zhou warned the crew to beware of toxic residue and not cling to a rigid theory. A fisherman displayed a fish skeleton with a damaged rib; a thin shield of vinegar had kept the mineral from reacting during the test. From the crowd came a sob, followed by arguments about theology and the old rebellion against quarry owners. Gu Wen refused to let the debate overtake the practical need to clean the cooking vessels. He asked the crew to test one jar, then another, and to record what each change achieved.

市府議程裡出現一種奇特的報告類型：它用棒球局數比喻河流，彷彿比分改變就能抹去污染。聯邦稽查員是議會提名人，卻似乎無法理解鹽罐裡滋生的生物。林舟警告工班要當心有毒殘留，別死守僵化理論。一位漁夫拿出一副魚骨，其中一根肋骨受損；測試時，一層薄薄的醋液曾阻隔礦物反應。人群裡先傳來啜泣，接著有人談起神學，以及昔日反抗礦主的叛亂。顧聞不願讓爭論蓋過清洗炊具的實際需求。他請工班逐罐測試，記錄每次改動的成效。

Every answer produced a new question about responsibility. It was easy to identify a flawed tool and much harder to decide who deserved help first when supplies were limited. After listening to workers and residents, Gu Wen proposed that they protect an apprentice's testimony rather than trade it for a dramatic accusation. Some listeners objected. Lin Zhou did not accuse them of being unreasonable.

每個答案又帶來新的責任問題。找出工具缺陷很容易，物資有限時決定誰該優先受助卻艱難得多。聽完工人與居民的意見後，顧聞提出：保護學徒的證詞，不拿它交換一場戲劇化指控。有人反對。

The workshop had no splendid banner and needed none. One person fetched water, another tested the jars, and a third wrote down the objections so that even those who arrived late could understand the decision. By the time the lamps were lit, one change could be seen: Gu Wen tasted two samples blindfolded while Lin Zhou measured the residue. It was a limited victory.

工坊沒有華麗橫幅，也不需要。有人取水，有人測罐，另有人記下反對意見，讓遲來的人也能理解決定。燈火亮起時，已有一項可見的改變：顧聞蒙眼試味兩份樣本，林舟量測殘留物。這只是有限的勝利。那天顧聞第一次讓自己吃上一口熱食。

By dusk, the noise of the workshop had softened into the rattle of dishes and distant conversation. The two men finally found time to speak about something besides the mineral. Lin Zhou finally mentioned the teacher who had once supported the gray mineral. Gu Wen asked why he had concealed that connection. The question hurt, but Lin Zhou did not lie. They agreed to visit the quarry together, even though their silence for the next mile was uncomfortable. Their next direction arrived in an unexpected form: an abandoned quarry lay beyond the orchard road.

黃昏時，工坊的喧鬧變成洗碗聲與遠處談話。兩人終於有空談些礦物以外的事。林舟終於提起曾支持灰色礦物的老師。顧聞問他為何隱瞞這段關係。這問題讓人難受，但林舟沒有說謊。兩人仍決定一起前往採石場，雖然接下來一里路的沉默非常不自在。下一步的方向以意料之外的形式出現：廢棄採石場就在果園道路另一端。
