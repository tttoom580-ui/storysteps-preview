渡口下的鹽庫

The Vault beneath the Ferry

萬味渡口：與你煮一碗回家的湯 · 第 24 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂完成

Near the end of the seven-port route, Gu Wen and Lin Zhou no longer mistook exhaustion for courage. They had learned to ask for help, identify safe limits, and let people decide which promises to accept. At the Lower Salt Vault, they faced a problem that the market announcements had described too simply: an old support beam broke and black salt began spilling toward the canal. People had gathered before sunrise, not because they expected a famous hero, but because the next meal might depend on what happened here. Some brought bowls; others carried questions they had asked for days. Gu Wen noticed how carefully the smallest children watched the adults. A promise that sounded generous from the council table might mean something very different to a family standing at the end of a queue.

接近七港旅程終點時，顧聞與林舟已不再把疲憊誤當勇氣。他們學會求助、辨認安全界線，也讓人們自行決定接受哪些承諾。在下層鹽庫，兩人面對一件市場公告說得過於簡單的難題：舊支撐樑斷裂，黑鹽開始往運河流去。日出前人們便聚集起來，不是期待名震天下的英雄，而是知道下一餐可能取決於這裡發生的事。有人帶來碗，有人帶著問了好幾天的疑惑。顧聞注意到最小的孩子如何仔細觀察大人。議會桌上聽來慷慨的承諾，對排在隊伍末尾的家庭可能完全不同。

A fair inspection required ordinary tools as well as trained senses. They began with something ordinary that could be checked by anyone willing to look: the emergency gate could stop the spill only if the market bridge was closed first. The first observation did not explain everything. Neither man proposed using a miracle to erase the damage. Heat could release a smell, and herbs could reveal a change, but toxic mineral still had to be contained, collected, and handled by trained people.

公平檢驗不只需要敏銳感官，也需要普通工具。兩人從一項人人只要願意細看就能核對的普通線索開始：緊急閘門只有先關閉市橋才能攔住外洩。第一次觀察不能解釋一切。兩人都沒有提議用神蹟抹除損害。熱能釋放氣味，藥草能顯示變化，但有毒礦物仍須由受過訓練的人隔離、收集與處理。

The confrontation began when a nasty blur of dust filled the lower vault. A miner used a low chord as the evacuation signal, and the crowd began to descend in a coherent line. The company offered a scheme of immediate execution, claiming the old doctrine required every gate to be opened at once. Gu Wen refused to speculate about hidden treasure while people were in agony. A riot almost started when someone slapped a porter, but Lin Zhou stepped between them and asked everyone to soften their voices. The two men reached an accord with the workers: close the market bridge, protect families, then perform the extensive repair. A clerk browsing the emergency index found a worthy route around the sealed canal. The danger did not vanish, but an orderly response gave the crews time to act. A clerk began to browse the spare maps while another tried to slap the dust from his coat. One worker asked whether his spouse had reached safety.

下層鹽庫充滿惡臭又模糊的粉塵，正面衝突就此開始。礦工用一聲低沉和弦當作撤離訊號，人群沿著有秩序的隊伍往下走。公司提出立即執行的方案，宣稱舊教條要求一次打開所有閘門。顧聞不願在眾人痛苦時猜測什麼隱藏寶藏。有人掌摑搬運工，暴動幾乎爆發；林舟站到兩人中間，請大家放低聲音。兩人與工班達成協議：先關市橋、保護家庭，再展開大規模修復。一名翻閱緊急索引的書記找到一條值得使用的替代路線。危險沒有憑空消失，但有序應對替工班爭取了時間。書記開始翻閱備用地圖，另一人拍掉外套上的灰塵。一名工人問自己的配偶是否已平安抵達。

Near the final decision, caution meant more than a warning spoken from a safe distance. People needed an evacuation route, a usable kitchen, and a clear reason for every delay. After listening to workers and residents, Gu Wen proposed that they warn the traders and evacuate the bridge before attempting a difficult repair. Some listeners objected. They had already lost wages, time, and confidence; a cautious solution sounded unbearably slow. Lin Zhou did not accuse them of being unreasonable. He showed them which part of the result was known and which part still had to be tested. When Gu Wen asked if the plan was fair, Lin Zhou answered that fairness had to survive the journey from a meeting room to a kitchen door.

接近最終決定時，謹慎已不只是站在安全處發出警告。人們需要撤離路線、可用廚房，以及每一次延誤的清楚理由。聽完工人與居民的意見後，顧聞提出：先警告攤販撤離市橋，再嘗試艱難修復。有人反對。他們已失去工資、時間和信心，謹慎的方法聽起來慢得難以忍受。林舟沒有指責他們不理性，只是指出哪些結果已知、哪些仍待檢驗。顧聞問方案是否公平，林舟回答：公平必須經得起從議事廳走到廚房門口的考驗。

The repair crews moved with the deliberate pace of people who knew that a shortcut could put strangers in danger. By the time the lamps were lit, one change could be seen: Gu Wen and Lin Zhou worked beside the crews rather than claiming a heroic shortcut. It was a limited victory. It tasted plain and honest, which seemed better than any spell he could perform.

修復工班按部就班，因為他們知道捷徑可能害陌生人陷入危險。燈火亮起時，已有一項可見的改變：顧聞與林舟與工班並肩作業，不宣稱有英雄式捷徑。這只是有限的勝利。味道樸素而真實，比他能施展的任何法術都好。

They had once imagined that finishing the case would mean returning to separate lives. In the emergency, Lin Zhou held the inspection rope and Gu Wen kept the crowd moving. They could not stand together, and neither demanded it. When they met after the gate closed, each checked the other's face before speaking about the repair. Their fear had become something they could share instead of hide. Their next direction arrived in an unexpected form: the final feast could begin only after independent inspectors cleared the water. At dawn they would travel again, but this time neither man would have to pretend he was walking alone.

他們曾以為案件結束就要回到各自生活。緊急事故裡，林舟守著檢測繩，顧聞則引導人群。兩人不能站在一起，誰也沒有強求。閘門關上後相見時，他們先查看對方的臉，才談修復結果。恐懼終於成了可以共享的感受，而不是必須隱瞞的東西。下一步的方向以意料之外的形式出現：最後的宴席只能在獨立稽查員確認水質後開始。黎明時他們將再次啟程，但這次誰都不必假裝自己孤身上路。
