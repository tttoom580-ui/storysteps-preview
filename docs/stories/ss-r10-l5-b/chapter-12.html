沒有贏家的比賽

The Contest Without a Winner

萬味渡口：與你煮一碗回家的湯 · 第 12 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

At the middle of their journey, Gu Wen discovered that a route on paper could look perfectly safe while the people walking it were afraid. Lin Zhou began every visit by asking who lived with its consequences. At the Great Tasting Arena, they faced a problem that the market announcements had described too simply: the sponsor offered a prize to anyone who could cook a fragrant feast with the gray salt. People had gathered before sunrise, not because they expected a famous hero, but because the next meal might depend on what happened here. Some brought bowls; others carried questions they had asked for days. Gu Wen noticed how carefully the smallest children watched the adults. A promise that sounded generous from the council table might mean something very different to a family standing at the end of a queue.

到了旅程中段，顧聞發現紙上看似安全的路線，走在上面的人仍可能害怕。林舟每到一處便先問，究竟是誰承受後果。在大試味場，兩人面對一件市場公告說得過於簡單的難題：贊助者懸賞誰能用灰鹽煮出香氣四溢的宴席。日出前人們便聚集起來，不是期待名震天下的英雄，而是知道下一餐可能取決於這裡發生的事。有人帶來碗，有人帶著問了好幾天的疑惑。顧聞注意到最小的孩子如何仔細觀察大人。議會桌上聽來慷慨的承諾，對排在隊伍末尾的家庭可能完全不同。

Gu Wen learned to recognize Lin Zhou’s careful silence: the herbalist was usually considering the possibility he most wanted to reject. The copper spoon was useful only when its sound could be compared with a known sample. They began with something ordinary that could be checked by anyone willing to look: competitors were forbidden to bring private testing equipment onto the stage. The first observation did not explain everything. Gu Wen marked the position in his notebook, while Lin Zhou asked a second person to repeat the measurement with fresh tools. A different result would have mattered as much as a matching one. A sample without its location and date could mislead an entire port. Gu Wen wrote both on the label while Lin Zhou counted the tools, taking care not to confuse an attractive result with a trustworthy one.

顧聞學會辨認林舟謹慎的沉默：藥師通常在思考他最不願接受的可能。銅匙的聲響必須與已知樣本比較才有用。兩人從一項人人只要願意細看就能核對的普通線索開始：參賽者被禁止把私人檢測設備帶上台。第一次觀察不能解釋一切。顧聞在筆記本標記位置，林舟則請第二個人用新工具重做測量。不同的結果和相同的結果一樣重要。缺少地點與日期的樣本可能誤導整座港口。顧聞把兩者寫上標籤，林舟清點工具，提醒自己不能把漂亮結果誤當可信結果。

The arena held a celebrity cook whose portfolio included expensive dishes but little nutrition. He intended to win the ultimate prize with a crystal spoon, until judges announced a ban on private equipment. Gu Wen believed the ban concealed fraud, yet a lawsuit would not help the hungry crowd that day. During the first session, a judge tried to poke the soup with a silver rod and shove the bowl back onto its rack. The judge's prescription for success was simple: reverse the heat and claim the flavor had returned. Gu Wen refused a random demonstration. He addressed the residential families in the audience in the plural, not as a single patient to be cured by magic. A noble sponsor called his intent insulting. The crowd, however, could see that the soup was still soup, and that an honest cook had the courage to lose. A defeat could depress a proud cook, but Gu Wen would rather lose honestly than deceive a crowd.

試味場裡有位名廚，作品集滿是昂貴菜餚，卻少有營養。評審宣布禁止私人器材前，他原本打算用水晶湯匙贏得終極大獎。顧聞懷疑禁令掩護詐欺，但當天提起訴訟也幫不了飢餓的群眾。第一場次中，一名評審拿銀棒戳湯，想把碗推回架上。評審給的成功處方很簡單：反轉火候，就宣稱香氣回來了。顧聞拒絕隨機胡亂示範。他對觀眾席裡一戶戶居民說話，把他們當成許多不同的人，而不是等著魔法治療的單一病人。貴族贊助人說他的意圖是在侮辱人。觀眾卻看得出來，湯還是湯，而誠實的廚師有勇氣輸掉比賽。失敗可能使驕傲的廚師沮喪，但顧聞寧可誠實落敗，也不願欺騙群眾。

The evidence now had to survive a public challenge. Some people wanted immediate punishment, others wanted the work stopped, and the cooks still needed to prepare supper before the lamps went out. After listening to workers and residents, Gu Wen proposed that they enter with an ordinary soup and explain the risk instead of performing a misleading miracle. Some listeners objected. They had already lost wages, time, and confidence; a cautious solution sounded unbearably slow. Lin Zhou did not accuse them of being unreasonable. He showed them which part of the result was known and which part still had to be tested. When Gu Wen asked if the plan was fair, Lin Zhou answered that fairness had to survive the journey from a meeting room to a kitchen door.

證據如今必須接受公開質疑。有人要求立即懲罰，有人想停工，但廚師仍須在熄燈前準備晚餐。聽完工人與居民的意見後，顧聞提出：帶著普通的湯參賽，解釋風險，不表演誤導人的奇蹟。有人反對。他們已失去工資、時間和信心，謹慎的方法聽起來慢得難以忍受。林舟沒有指責他們不理性，只是指出哪些結果已知、哪些仍待檢驗。顧聞問方案是否公平，林舟回答：公平必須經得起從議事廳走到廚房門口的考驗。

A group of residents stayed after the official discussion and tried the procedure with their own hands. Their questions were specific: what was safe to touch, who would keep the key, and how could a failed test be reported? By the time the lamps were lit, one change could be seen: the audience witnessed the cook choose an honest loss over a fraudulent victory. It was a limited victory. Nobody claimed the entire river had been healed, and the records were left where the people who had helped could check them. The work had names, dates, and mistakes recorded plainly instead of hidden behind a heroic speech. For the first time that day, Gu Wen allowed himself a mouthful of warm food. It tasted plain and honest, which seemed better than any spell he could perform.

正式討論結束後，一群居民留下來親手試做程序。他們的問題很具體：什麼可以碰、誰保管鑰匙、檢測失敗要如何通報？燈火亮起時，已有一項可見的改變：觀眾親眼看見廚師寧可真實落敗，也不願靠欺騙取勝。這只是有限的勝利。沒有人宣稱整條河已痊癒；紀錄也留在協助者可以查閱的地方。工作有姓名、日期，錯誤也明白記下，不藏在英雄式演說後。那天顧聞第一次讓自己吃上一口熱食。味道樸素而真實，比他能施展的任何法術都好。

The lamps made soft pools of light along the river. Gu Wen and Lin Zhou walked at the edge of the crowd, close enough to hear one another without having to compete with the cheering. After the contest, Gu Wen called himself foolish for surrendering the prize. Lin Zhou said he had seen a different kind of skill: knowing when not to perform. Gu Wen held his gaze longer than usual, then asked if they could cook something unremarkable together. Their next direction arrived in an unexpected form: a research letter invited them to examine the reservoir above the city. They folded the new information into their map instead of calling it proof. At dawn they would travel again, but this time neither man would have to pretend he was walking alone.

燈光沿河映出柔和的光圈。顧聞與林舟走在人群邊緣，距離近得能聽見彼此，不必與歡呼聲較勁。比賽過後，顧聞責怪自己愚蠢，竟放棄獎金。林舟說他看見另一種本事：知道何時不必表演。顧聞比平常更久地望著他，然後問能否一起煮點毫不起眼的東西。下一步的方向以意料之外的形式出現：研究信邀請兩人前往城上水庫調查。兩人把新訊息記上地圖，沒有直接稱它為證據。黎明時他們將再次啟程，但這次誰都不必假裝自己孤身上路。
