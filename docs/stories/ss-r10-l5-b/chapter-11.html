斷匙的守護人

The Keeper of the Broken Spoon

萬味渡口：與你煮一碗回家的湯 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

At the middle of their journey, Gu Wen discovered that a route on paper could look perfectly safe while the people walking it were afraid. Lin Zhou began every visit by asking who lived with its consequences. At the Cliffside Refuge, they faced a problem that the market announcements had described too simply: an elderly storekeeper confessed to hiding invoices after being threatened with losing his home. Some brought bowls; others carried questions they had asked for days. Gu Wen noticed how carefully the smallest children watched the adults.

到了旅程中段，顧聞發現紙上看似安全的路線，走在上面的人仍可能害怕。林舟每到一處便先問，究竟是誰承受後果。在崖邊避難所，兩人面對一件市場公告說得過於簡單的難題：年邁的倉管坦承曾藏起發票，因為有人威脅要收回他的住所。有人帶來碗，有人帶著問了好幾天的疑惑。

Gu Wen learned to recognize Lin Zhou’s careful silence: the herbalist was usually considering the possibility he most wanted to reject. The copper spoon was useful only when its sound could be compared with a known sample. They began with something ordinary that could be checked by anyone willing to look: his worn spoon carried the same stamped mark as Gu Wen's grandmother's kitchen chest. The first observation did not explain everything. A sample without its location and date could mislead an entire port. Gu Wen wrote both on the label while Lin Zhou counted the tools, taking care not to confuse an attractive result with a trustworthy one.

顧聞學會辨認林舟謹慎的沉默：藥師通常在思考他最不願接受的可能。銅匙的聲響必須與已知樣本比較才有用。兩人從一項人人只要願意細看就能核對的普通線索開始：他的舊匙與顧聞祖母廚箱上的印章相同。第一次觀察不能解釋一切。不同的結果和相同的結果一樣重要。缺少地點與日期的樣本可能誤導整座港口。顧聞把兩者寫上標籤，林舟清點工具，提醒自己不能把漂亮結果誤當可信結果。

At the cliff refuge, a clinical workshop drew an unlikely spectator: the heir to the salt company. He was fascinated by a scar on the old keeper's hand and gasped when the man described the theft of a recipe. A child in a wheelchair was recommended for an upgrade to the kitchen ramp, but the keeper said the repair should never depend on a celebrity donation. Outside, dogs began to howl as wind struck the sheer rock. An intact bowl stood on a shelf beneath an old rifle, a reminder of a time when the place had been defended by force. Gu Wen was apt to characterize the keeper as cowardly; Lin Zhou asked whether a refuge could remain safe if people treated every confession as entertainment. The keeper finally offered his own testimony and the name of the man who had threatened him. The casino owner tried to claim copyright over a communal recipe. His threats did not fascinate Gu Wen, and the keeper heard a spectator gasp when the proposal was read. Lin Zhou would recommend an independent review.

崖邊避難所舉辦了一場醫療工作坊，來了一位意料之外的旁觀者：鹽業公司的繼承人。他對年邁倉管手上的疤痕著迷，聽到有人偷走食譜時還倒抽一口氣。一名坐輪椅的孩子需要升級廚房坡道，但倉管說，這項修繕不該仰賴名人捐款。屋外，狗隻在強風吹打陡峭岩壁時長嚎。一只完好的碗放在舊步槍下的架子上，提醒人們此地曾靠武力保衛。顧聞很容易把倉管形容成懦夫；林舟反問，如果大家把每份供詞都當娛樂，避難所還能安全嗎？倉管終於交出自己的證詞，以及威脅過他的人的名字。賭場老闆試圖主張共享食譜的著作權。這種威脅沒有使顧聞著迷；提案唸出時，倉管聽見旁觀者倒抽一口氣。林舟則會建議獨立審查。

The evidence now had to survive a public challenge. Some people wanted immediate punishment, others wanted the work stopped, and the cooks still needed to prepare supper before the lamps went out. After listening to workers and residents, Gu Wen proposed that they hear the confession without treating fear as proof of innocence or guilt. Some listeners objected. Lin Zhou did not accuse them of being unreasonable.

證據如今必須接受公開質疑。有人要求立即懲罰，有人想停工，但廚師仍須在熄燈前準備晚餐。聽完工人與居民的意見後，顧聞提出：聽取供詞，但不把恐懼直接當成有罪或無罪的證明。有人反對。

A group of residents stayed after the official discussion and tried the procedure with their own hands. Their questions were specific: what was safe to touch, who would keep the key, and how could a failed test be reported? By the time the lamps were lit, one change could be seen: Gu Wen spoke about his mother's closed restaurant for the first time. It was a limited victory.

正式討論結束後，一群居民留下來親手試做程序。他們的問題很具體：什麼可以碰、誰保管鑰匙、檢測失敗要如何通報？燈火亮起時，已有一項可見的改變：顧聞第一次談起母親被迫關閉的餐館。這只是有限的勝利。那天顧聞第一次讓自己吃上一口熱食。

The lamps made soft pools of light along the river. Gu Wen and Lin Zhou walked at the edge of the crowd, close enough to hear one another without having to compete with the cheering. The keeper's spoon reminded Gu Wen of the restaurant his mother had lost. He spoke of her for the first time, stopping twice before he could finish. Lin Zhou asked no clever question. He simply waited, letting the unfinished story remain Gu Wen's own until he wished to tell more. Their next direction arrived in an unexpected form: the annual tasting arena offered a chance to confront the supplier in public.

燈光沿河映出柔和的光圈。顧聞與林舟走在人群邊緣，距離近得能聽見彼此，不必與歡呼聲較勁。倉管的湯匙讓顧聞想起母親失去的餐館。他第一次說起母親，途中停了兩次才說完。林舟沒有問什麼聰明問題，只是等待，讓未說完的故事仍屬於顧聞，直到他願意再講。下一步的方向以意料之外的形式出現：年度試味場提供公開質問供應商的機會。
