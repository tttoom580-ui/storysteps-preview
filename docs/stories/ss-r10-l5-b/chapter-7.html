正午藍光的採石場

The Quarry at Blue Noon

萬味渡口：與你煮一碗回家的湯 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照

The first five ports had not given Gu Wen and Lin Zhou an easy answer, but they had shown what a mistake could cost. They carried fewer assumptions now and more accurately labeled samples. At the abandoned Blue Quarry, they faced a problem that the market announcements had described too simply: stonecutters remembered a mineral that crackled when salt and heat were mixed. People had gathered before sunrise, not because they expected a famous hero, but because the next meal might depend on what happened here. Some brought bowls; others carried questions they had asked for days. Gu Wen noticed how carefully the smallest children watched the adults. A promise that sounded generous from the council table might mean something very different to a family standing at the end of a queue.

前五港沒有給顧聞與林舟簡單答案，卻讓他們看見錯誤的代價。如今他們帶著更少的成見，以及標記更準確的樣本。在廢棄的藍岩採石場，兩人面對一件市場公告說得過於簡單的難題：石匠記得一種遇鹽受熱便劈啪作響的礦石。日出前人們便聚集起來，不是期待名震天下的英雄，而是知道下一餐可能取決於這裡發生的事。有人帶來碗，有人帶著問了好幾天的疑惑。顧聞注意到最小的孩子如何仔細觀察大人。議會桌上聽來慷慨的承諾，對排在隊伍末尾的家庭可能完全不同。

Lin Zhou prepared two separate sample jars before he would discuss a result. One held material from the suspicious route; the other held a control sample obtained with a witness watching. They began with something ordinary that could be checked by anyone willing to look: a chipped mixing wheel contained black powder identical to the river deposit. The first observation did not explain everything. Gu Wen marked the position in his notebook, while Lin Zhou asked a second person to repeat the measurement with fresh tools. A different result would have mattered as much as a matching one. Their copper pot might amplify a real aroma, but no enchantment could replace grain that had never been harvested. That distinction kept the work focused on actual supplies and actual people.

林舟在討論結果前，先備好兩個不同樣本罐。一罐裝可疑貨路的材料，一罐裝見證人監看下取得的對照樣本。兩人從一項人人只要願意細看就能核對的普通線索開始：缺角混料輪裡有與河底沉積物相同的黑粉。第一次觀察不能解釋一切。顧聞在筆記本標記位置，林舟則請第二個人用新工具重做測量。不同的結果和相同的結果一樣重要。古銅鍋可以放大真實香味，卻沒有任何法術能取代從未收成的穀物。這個區別讓工作始終聚焦於真正的物資與人。

The acquisition of the blue quarry had been disguised as a bonus for the colonial corporation. In the backyard of the stone office, a stack of broken plates held a chunk of mineral rich in calcium. The sophomore apprentice could comprehend the implication better than his supervisor: the powder could penetrate cheap filters, so the old scrap disposal plan was risky. A symbolic diagram of the quarry's spine was posted beside a transit map, yet every useful route lay under surveillance. Gu Wen refused to disapprove of the workers simply for being afraid. Lin Zhou took the samples into a spacious side room and compared them with last year's records. A former republican official said the stone belonged to the mountain, not to the company that had bought a paper claim. No one knew if the council would accept that argument, but the wheel marks finally matched the sacks from the ridge.

藍岩採石場的收購被包裝成殖民公司發給工人的獎金。石屋後院堆著破盤子，其中一塊富含鈣質的礦物藏在碎片裡。那名二年級學徒比主管更懂其含意：粉末會穿透便宜濾網，舊廢料處理計畫因此十分危險。礦層脊線的象徵性圖示掛在運輸地圖旁，但所有有用路線都受到監視。顧聞拒絕只因工人害怕，就對他們表示不滿。林舟把樣本帶進寬敞的側室，與去年紀錄比對。一位前共和派官員說，山石屬於山，不屬於買下一紙權利的公司。沒人知道議會是否接受這種論點，但混料輪的痕跡終於與鹽脊麻袋吻合。

Every answer produced a new question about responsibility. It was easy to identify a flawed tool and much harder to decide who deserved help first when supplies were limited. After listening to workers and residents, Gu Wen proposed that they preserve the machine instead of destroying it in anger. Some listeners objected. They had already lost wages, time, and confidence; a cautious solution sounded unbearably slow. Lin Zhou did not accuse them of being unreasonable. He showed them which part of the result was known and which part still had to be tested. When Gu Wen asked if the plan was fair, Lin Zhou answered that fairness had to survive the journey from a meeting room to a kitchen door.

每個答案又帶來新的責任問題。找出工具缺陷很容易，物資有限時決定誰該優先受助卻艱難得多。聽完工人與居民的意見後，顧聞提出：不因憤怒而摧毀機器，而要把它留作證據。有人反對。他們已失去工資、時間和信心，謹慎的方法聽起來慢得難以忍受。林舟沒有指責他們不理性，只是指出哪些結果已知、哪些仍待檢驗。顧聞問方案是否公平，林舟回答：公平必須經得起從議事廳走到廚房門口的考驗。

The workshop had no splendid banner and needed none. One person fetched water, another tested the jars, and a third wrote down the objections so that even those who arrived late could understand the decision. By the time the lamps were lit, one change could be seen: Lin Zhou admitted that his former teacher had once recommended the mineral for emergency preservation. It was a limited victory. Nobody claimed the entire river had been healed, and the records were left where the people who had helped could check them. The work had names, dates, and mistakes recorded plainly instead of hidden behind a heroic speech. For the first time that day, Gu Wen allowed himself a mouthful of warm food. It tasted plain and honest, which seemed better than any spell he could perform.

工坊沒有華麗橫幅，也不需要。有人取水，有人測罐，另有人記下反對意見，讓遲來的人也能理解決定。燈火亮起時，已有一項可見的改變：林舟坦承前任老師曾建議用此礦物應急保存食物。這只是有限的勝利。沒有人宣稱整條河已痊癒；紀錄也留在協助者可以查閱的地方。工作有姓名、日期，錯誤也明白記下，不藏在英雄式演說後。那天顧聞第一次讓自己吃上一口熱食。味道樸素而真實，比他能施展的任何法術都好。

By dusk, the noise of the workshop had softened into the rattle of dishes and distant conversation. The two men finally found time to speak about something besides the mineral. Gu Wen looked at the broken wheel and understood why Lin Zhou had been ashamed of his teacher's advice. He did not excuse the old recommendation, but he stopped treating Lin Zhou as if he had made it. The distinction allowed them to speak normally again. Their next direction arrived in an unexpected form: the old teacher now worked at the Night Salon. They folded the new information into their map instead of calling it proof. At dawn they would travel again, but this time neither man would have to pretend he was walking alone.

黃昏時，工坊的喧鬧變成洗碗聲與遠處談話。兩人終於有空談些礦物以外的事。顧聞看著破損的混料輪，明白林舟為何對老師當年的建議感到羞愧。他沒有替舊建議開脫，卻不再把林舟當成下建議的人。這個區別使兩人終於能正常說話。下一步的方向以意料之外的形式出現：那位老師如今在夜間食坊工作。兩人把新訊息記上地圖，沒有直接稱它為證據。黎明時他們將再次啟程，但這次誰都不必假裝自己孤身上路。
