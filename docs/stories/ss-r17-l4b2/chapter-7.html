Rain, dust, and distant voices made the artificial intelligence exhibition hall feel smaller than it was. An Lin arrived with a plan, then saw a detail that the plan had not accounted for. ORACLE publicly demonstrated strategic moves that were genuinely strong even without stolen private data. The difference between guessing and checking suddenly seemed enormous. A person who sounded brave could still be mistaken; a person who admitted uncertainty could still save the group. An Lin chose the second kind of courage. The disputed notice used the word academic, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for accountant, and An Lin asked who had verified it. Coach Mei found a reference to admiration in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. The old manual told its readers to annoy, and Coach Mei wanted to know who had written that rule.

雨聲、灰塵與遠處的人聲，讓人工智慧展示廳顯得格外狹窄。安琳帶著計畫抵達，卻看見計畫沒有考慮到的細節。神諭在不使用被竊私人資料時，仍公開展示真正高明的策略。猜測和查證的差別突然變得很大。聽起來勇敢的人也可能犯錯，敢承認不確定的人反而可能救下整個團隊。安琳決定選擇後者。有爭議的通知使用「學術的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「會計師」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。梅教練在舊筆記裡找到關於「欣賞」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。舊手冊要求讀者「使困擾」，梅教練想知道這條規則由誰制定。

Coach Mei brought the next piece of information on a sheet that had been folded several times. Coach Mei confirmed several predictions followed from visible game states rather than hidden practice records. The team compared its date with what they had witnessed for themselves. One line was persuasive; another contradicted the first. An Lin asked for the original source instead of selecting whichever version made their work easier. Even a genuine clue could be dangerous when removed from its context. The evidence list included aquarium, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for basin, and An Lin asked who had verified it. Coach Mei found a reference to characteristic in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned chemistry during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it.

梅教練把下一份資料帶來，紙張已被折過好幾次。梅教練確認部分預測確實來自公開局勢，不是祕密練習紀錄。小隊把上面的日期與親眼所見逐項比較。一行文字很有說服力，下一行卻與它矛盾。安琳沒有挑比較方便的版本，而是要求查看原始來源。即使是真的線索，一旦脫離脈絡也可能造成危險。證物清單上有「水族館」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「水池」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。梅教練在舊筆記裡找到關於「特徵」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「化學反應」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。

An argument broke out before anyone could announce a solution. Director Luo treated legitimate skill as a reason to overlook every privacy violation. An Lin did not win it with a long speech. Instead, the team drew two columns: what the record established and what remained uncertain. Director Luo challenged their interpretation, which was uncomfortable but useful. A plan that collapsed under one honest question was not ready to protect anyone outside the room. The evidence list included contribution, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for defeated, and An Lin asked who had verified it. Coach Mei found a reference to expansion in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. Someone had marked the warning fluent; Coach Mei checked whether that description was accurate.

尚未有人宣布解法，爭論便已爆發。羅總監把真實能力當成忽略所有隱私違規的理由。安琳沒有用長篇演說壓倒對方，而是在紙上分成兩欄：紀錄已證實的事，以及仍然不確定的事。羅總監質疑他們的解讀，雖然令人不快，卻也有用。禁不起一次誠實提問的計畫，還不能拿去保護門外的人。證物清單上有「捐贈」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「擊敗」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。梅教練在舊筆記裡找到關於「擴張」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。有人把警告標成「流暢的」，梅教練則查證這種描述是否準確。

A machine could be a strong opponent and still have been given an unfair advantage by human organizers. An Lin did not want to pretend the AI had no skill; doing so would make any honest victory meaningless. Coach Mei marked the difference between ability and access on the practice board. Around them, spectators argued about which side deserved to win. The team returned to a simpler question: what conditions made the result worth trusting?

機器可以是強勁對手，卻也可能被人類主辦方給了不公平的優勢。安琳不想假裝AI毫無能力；那樣一來，任何誠實勝利也會失去意義。梅教練在練習板上分開標示能力與資料存取。周圍觀眾爭論誰更值得贏，戰隊卻回到更簡單的問題：什麼條件能讓結果值得信任？

Nothing in the trial worked exactly as predicted. An Lin praised the valid analysis while separating it from the illegal information channel. An Lin insisted on keeping the unsuccessful measurements instead of replacing them with cleaner numbers. That choice annoyed Director Luo, who wanted a quick announcement. But the failure established a boundary they had only guessed at before. A useful boundary was better than a promise built on wishful thinking. The disputed notice used the word fortunate, but its author had not signed the page. A written instruction told the staff to motivate before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. The report called the arrangement prime, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. Someone had marked the warning relevant; Coach Mei checked whether that description was accurate.

這次測試沒有一項完全照預測發展。安琳肯定合法分析，同時把它與違法資料通道分開處理。安琳堅持留下不成功的測量值，不用好看的數字替換。羅總監急著發公告，因此相當不滿；然而失敗讓他們找到了先前只能猜測的限制。清楚的界線，總比建立在一廂情願上的承諾可靠。有爭議的通知使用「幸運的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「激勵；促使」，卻沒有人記下結果。報告說這項安排「主開關」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。有人把警告標成「相關的」，梅教練則查證這種描述是否準確。

When evening arrived, the immediate danger had changed shape rather than vanished. A student spectator asked whether machines and people could compete under rules both understood. An Lin gathered the others and repeated only the facts they could support. Some people would need more time. Others had already decided to leave. Neither choice made anyone less worthy of protection. The next step belonged to those who would live with its consequences, not merely to the person speaking loudest. The evidence list included rhythm, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for shade, and An Lin asked who had verified it. An Lin asked whether the team could strive under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. The old manual told its readers to waken, and Coach Mei wanted to know who had written that rule.

傍晚到來時，眼前的危險只是改變形態，並未完全消失。一名學生觀眾問，機器與人是否能在雙方都理解的規則下競賽。安琳召集同伴，只重述有證據支持的事。有人還需要時間，也有人已決定離開；這兩種選擇都不會使任何人失去受保護的資格。下一步應由承擔後果的人決定，而不是交給聲音最大的人。證物清單上有「節奏」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「陰影」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。安琳詢問小隊能否依安全規則「努力」，答案仍不明朗。舊手冊要求讀者「喚醒」，梅教練想知道這條規則由誰制定。
