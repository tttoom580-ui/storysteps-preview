Rain, dust, and distant voices made a small training studio above the arcade feel smaller than it was. An Lin arrived with a plan, then saw a detail that the plan had not accounted for. The team built a practice environment without network microphones or hidden spectator links. The difference between guessing and checking suddenly seemed enormous. A person who sounded brave could still be mistaken; a person who admitted uncertainty could still save the group. An Lin chose the second kind of courage. A written instruction told the staff to accompany before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. Coach Mei found a reference to attraction in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned diagram during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included diplomat, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

雨聲、灰塵與遠處的人聲，讓電玩街樓上的小型訓練室顯得格外狹窄。安琳帶著計畫抵達，卻看見計畫沒有考慮到的細節。戰隊搭建一間沒有網路麥克風與隱藏觀眾連線的練習室。猜測和查證的差別突然變得很大。聽起來勇敢的人也可能犯錯，敢承認不確定的人反而可能救下整個團隊。安琳決定選擇後者。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「陪同」，卻沒有人記下結果。梅教練在舊筆記裡找到關於「賣點」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「圖表」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「外交官」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。

Coach Mei brought the next piece of information on a sheet that had been folded several times. Coach Mei measured the AI's accuracy in public matches and compared it with the suspicious private tests. The team compared its date with what they had witnessed for themselves. One line was persuasive; another contradicted the first. An Lin asked for the original source instead of selecting whichever version made their work easier. Even a genuine clue could be dangerous when removed from its context. The working file contained a separate entry for draft, and An Lin asked who had verified it. The report called the arrangement essential, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. Someone had marked the warning experimental; Coach Mei checked whether that description was accurate. One witness promised to flee if conditions improved, but An Lin would not treat hope as evidence.

梅教練把下一份資料帶來，紙張已被折過好幾次。梅教練比較AI在公開比賽與可疑私人測試的準確度。小隊把上面的日期與親眼所見逐項比較。一行文字很有說服力，下一行卻與它矛盾。安琳沒有挑比較方便的版本，而是要求查看原始來源。即使是真的線索，一旦脫離脈絡也可能造成危險。工作檔案為「草案」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「必要的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。有人把警告標成「實驗性的」，梅教練則查證這種描述是否準確。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「逃離」，安琳卻不能把希望當成證據。

An argument broke out before anyone could announce a solution. Director Luo refused to release training data, claiming its commercial secrecy outweighed player concerns. An Lin did not win it with a long speech. Instead, the team drew two columns: what the record established and what remained uncertain. Director Luo challenged their interpretation, which was uncomfortable but useful. A plan that collapsed under one honest question was not ready to protect anyone outside the room. The working file contained a separate entry for germs, and An Lin asked who had verified it. Coach Mei found a reference to greeting in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned hesitation during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included horizon, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

尚未有人宣布解法，爭論便已爆發。羅總監拒絕公布訓練資料，說商業機密比選手疑慮重要。安琳沒有用長篇演說壓倒對方，而是在紙上分成兩欄：紀錄已證實的事，以及仍然不確定的事。羅總監質疑他們的解讀，雖然令人不快，卻也有用。禁不起一次誠實提問的計畫，還不能拿去保護門外的人。工作檔案為「病菌」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。梅教練在舊筆記裡找到關於「問候」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「猶豫」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「地平線」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。

A machine could be a strong opponent and still have been given an unfair advantage by human organizers. An Lin did not want to pretend the AI had no skill; doing so would make any honest victory meaningless. Coach Mei marked the difference between ability and access on the practice board. Around them, spectators argued about which side deserved to win. The team returned to a simpler question: what conditions made the result worth trusting?

機器可以是強勁對手，卻也可能被人類主辦方給了不公平的優勢。安琳不想假裝AI毫無能力；那樣一來，任何誠實勝利也會失去意義。梅教練在練習板上分開標示能力與資料存取。周圍觀眾爭論誰更值得贏，戰隊卻回到更簡單的問題：什麼條件能讓結果值得信任？

Nothing in the trial worked exactly as predicted. An Lin asked the players' association to observe the comparison without seeing private team tactics. An Lin insisted on keeping the unsuccessful measurements instead of replacing them with cleaner numbers. That choice annoyed Director Luo, who wanted a quick announcement. But the failure established a boundary they had only guessed at before. A useful boundary was better than a promise built on wishful thinking. A written instruction told the staff to loosen before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. The report called the arrangement magnificent, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof. A witness mentioned manual during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included mechanic, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

這次測試沒有一項完全照預測發展。安琳請選手協會觀察比對，避免暴露隊內戰術。安琳堅持留下不成功的測量值，不用好看的數字替換。羅總監急著發公告，因此相當不滿；然而失敗讓他們找到了先前只能猜測的限制。清楚的界線，總比建立在一廂情願上的承諾可靠。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「鬆開」，卻沒有人記下結果。報告說這項安排「壯麗的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。見證人在訪談中提到「操作手冊」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「機械技師」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。

When evening arrived, the immediate danger had changed shape rather than vanished. The privacy-safe test reduced the AI's apparent ability to predict secret instructions. An Lin gathered the others and repeated only the facts they could support. Some people would need more time. Others had already decided to leave. Neither choice made anyone less worthy of protection. The next step belonged to those who would live with its consequences, not merely to the person speaking loudest. An inspector described the situation as outstanding; An Lin wanted measurements instead of adjectives. Coach Mei found a reference to recreation in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned satellite during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. One witness promised to strengthen if conditions improved, but An Lin would not treat hope as evidence.

傍晚到來時，眼前的危險只是改變形態，並未完全消失。保護隱私的測試使AI看穿祕密指令的能力明顯下降。安琳召集同伴，只重述有證據支持的事。有人還需要時間，也有人已決定離開；這兩種選擇都不會使任何人失去受保護的資格。下一步應由承擔後果的人決定，而不是交給聲音最大的人。檢查員把情況形容為「傑出的」，安琳卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。梅教練在舊筆記裡找到關於「休閒活動」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「衛星」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「加強」，安琳卻不能把希望當成證據。
