Rain, dust, and distant voices made a quiet team training room feel smaller than it was. An Lin arrived with a plan, then saw a detail that the plan had not accounted for. An Lin deliberately practiced moves that would lose a small advantage but create a new route later. The difference between guessing and checking suddenly seemed enormous. A person who sounded brave could still be mistaken; a person who admitted uncertainty could still save the group. An Lin chose the second kind of courage. A witness mentioned apology during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included bandage, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for bargain, and An Lin asked who had verified it. Coach Mei found a reference to civilization in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

雨聲、灰塵與遠處的人聲，讓安靜的戰隊訓練室顯得格外狹窄。安琳帶著計畫抵達，卻看見計畫沒有考慮到的細節。安琳刻意練習先放棄一點優勢、稍後才開出新路的行動。猜測和查證的差別突然變得很大。聽起來勇敢的人也可能犯錯，敢承認不確定的人反而可能救下整個團隊。安琳決定選擇後者。見證人在訪談中提到「道歉」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「繃帶」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「交易」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。梅教練在舊筆記裡找到關於「文明」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

Coach Mei brought the next piece of information on a sheet that had been folded several times. Coach Mei tracked the cost of each decision instead of praising uncertainty for its own sake. The team compared its date with what they had witnessed for themselves. One line was persuasive; another contradicted the first. An Lin asked for the original source instead of selecting whichever version made their work easier. Even a genuine clue could be dangerous when removed from its context. The old manual told its readers to congratulate, and Coach Mei wanted to know who had written that rule. The disputed notice used the word critical, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for explosion, and An Lin asked who had verified it. An Lin asked whether the team could forbid under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear.

梅教練把下一份資料帶來，紙張已被折過好幾次。梅教練追蹤每次選擇的代價，而不是只讚美不可預測。小隊把上面的日期與親眼所見逐項比較。一行文字很有說服力，下一行卻與它矛盾。安琳沒有挑比較方便的版本，而是要求查看原始來源。即使是真的線索，一旦脫離脈絡也可能造成危險。舊手冊要求讀者「恭賀」，梅教練想知道這條規則由誰制定。有爭議的通知使用「關鍵的／批判的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「爆炸」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。安琳詢問小隊能否依安全規則「禁止」，答案仍不明朗。

An argument broke out before anyone could announce a solution. A teammate feared that unpredictable play would damage the team's reputation. An Lin did not win it with a long speech. Instead, the team drew two columns: what the record established and what remained uncertain. Director Luo challenged their interpretation, which was uncomfortable but useful. A plan that collapsed under one honest question was not ready to protect anyone outside the room. The old manual told its readers to illustrate, and Coach Mei wanted to know who had written that rule. One witness promised to install if conditions improved, but An Lin would not treat hope as evidence. An inspector described the situation as mere; An Lin wanted measurements instead of adjectives. The report called the arrangement pregnant, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof.

尚未有人宣布解法，爭論便已爆發。隊友擔心難以預測的打法會傷害戰隊名聲。安琳沒有用長篇演說壓倒對方，而是在紙上分成兩欄：紀錄已證實的事，以及仍然不確定的事。羅總監質疑他們的解讀，雖然令人不快，卻也有用。禁不起一次誠實提問的計畫，還不能拿去保護門外的人。舊手冊要求讀者「圖解說明」，梅教練想知道這條規則由誰制定。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「安裝」，安琳卻不能把希望當成證據。檢查員把情況形容為「僅僅的」，安琳卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。報告說這項安排「懷孕的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。

A machine could be a strong opponent and still have been given an unfair advantage by human organizers. An Lin did not want to pretend the AI had no skill; doing so would make any honest victory meaningless. Coach Mei marked the difference between ability and access on the practice board. Around them, spectators argued about which side deserved to win. The team returned to a simpler question: what conditions made the result worth trusting?

機器可以是強勁對手，卻也可能被人類主辦方給了不公平的優勢。安琳不想假裝AI毫無能力；那樣一來，任何誠實勝利也會失去意義。梅教練在練習板上分開標示能力與資料存取。周圍觀眾爭論誰更值得贏，戰隊卻回到更簡單的問題：什麼條件能讓結果值得信任？

Nothing in the trial worked exactly as predicted. An Lin allowed every player to refuse the tactic if it depended on risks they had not agreed to take. An Lin insisted on keeping the unsuccessful measurements instead of replacing them with cleaner numbers. That choice annoyed Director Luo, who wanted a quick announcement. But the failure established a boundary they had only guessed at before. A useful boundary was better than a promise built on wishful thinking. Someone had marked the warning prosperous; Coach Mei checked whether that description was accurate. The evidence list included resolution, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for revolution, and An Lin asked who had verified it. Coach Mei found a reference to settlers in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

這次測試沒有一項完全照預測發展。安琳允許任何隊員拒絕未事先同意的高風險戰術。安琳堅持留下不成功的測量值，不用好看的數字替換。羅總監急著發公告，因此相當不滿；然而失敗讓他們找到了先前只能猜測的限制。清楚的界線，總比建立在一廂情願上的承諾可靠。有人把警告標成「繁榮的」，梅教練則查證這種描述是否準確。證物清單上有「決議」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「革命」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。梅教練在舊筆記裡找到關於「遷居者」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

When evening arrived, the immediate danger had changed shape rather than vanished. The trial revealed one safe surprise, and two ideas that needed to be discarded. An Lin gathered the others and repeated only the facts they could support. Some people would need more time. Others had already decided to leave. Neither choice made anyone less worthy of protection. The next step belonged to those who would live with its consequences, not merely to the person speaking loudest. A witness mentioned surgeon during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The disputed notice used the word suspicious, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for tomb, and An Lin asked who had verified it. Coach Mei found a reference to website in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

傍晚到來時，眼前的危險只是改變形態，並未完全消失。試驗找到一種安全的奇襲，也淘汰兩個不該使用的主意。安琳召集同伴，只重述有證據支持的事。有人還需要時間，也有人已決定離開；這兩種選擇都不會使任何人失去受保護的資格。下一步應由承擔後果的人決定，而不是交給聲音最大的人。見證人在訪談中提到「外科醫師」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。有爭議的通知使用「可疑的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「墓穴」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。梅教練在舊筆記裡找到關於「網站」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。
