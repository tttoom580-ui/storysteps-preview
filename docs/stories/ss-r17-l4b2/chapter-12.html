At the tournament disciplinary office, the first sign of trouble was not a scream or an alarm. It was a small disagreement about something everyone believed they understood. An Lin was threatened with a suspension for discussing security faults before the investigation concluded. An Lin listened to the surrounding voices before making a judgment. Rumors traveled faster than careful questions, especially when the public wanted a simple villain. The team needed a record that would remain useful even if their favorite explanation proved false. The working file contained a separate entry for agent, and An Lin asked who had verified it. Coach Mei found a reference to bin in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned bracelet during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included burglar, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

在賽事紀律辦公室，麻煩不是從尖叫或警報開始，而是從一件大家自認了解的小事產生分歧。安琳因在調查結束前談資安缺陷，遭威脅禁賽。安琳先聽周遭人的說法，沒有急著下判斷。群眾想找到簡單的壞人時，謠言總比仔細提問傳得快。即使最喜歡的推測最後被推翻，小隊也需要留下仍然有用的紀錄。工作檔案為「代理人」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。梅教練在舊筆記裡找到關於「箱子」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「手環」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「竊賊」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。

The oldest document on the table looked less important than the newest screen. That appearance was misleading. Coach Mei found the disciplinary rule protected honest safety reporting when supported by evidence. Coach Mei read the handwritten notes aloud while An Lin checked the sequence of events. Their different skills mattered more than either person's rank. When they found a contradiction, they preserved both versions. Destroying an inconvenient record would only make the next investigation harder. The working file contained a separate entry for comment, and An Lin asked who had verified it. An Lin asked whether the team could commit under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. Someone had marked the warning definite; Coach Mei checked whether that description was accurate. The evidence list included destruction, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered.

桌上最舊的文件看起來不如最新螢幕重要，但那只是表面。梅教練查出紀律規則其實保護有證據的安全通報。梅教練念出手寫備註，安琳逐項核對事件順序。此時兩人的不同專長，比誰的職位更高還重要。找到矛盾後，他們把兩個版本都保存下來。毀掉一份不方便的資料，只會讓下一次調查更加困難。工作檔案為「評論」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。安琳詢問小隊能否依安全規則「承諾」，答案仍不明朗。有人把警告標成「明確的」，梅教練則查證這種描述是否準確。證物清單上有「毀壞」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。

By afternoon, the discovery had reached the people who could lose the most. Director Luo demanded silence in exchange for reinstatement of the team's promotional benefits. Director Luo offered a quick settlement, but it required the group to stop asking questions. An Lin considered the frightened faces around the room. It would be easy to sound heroic and demand that everyone take the same risk. Instead, each person was allowed to describe what they were prepared to do and what they would refuse. An inspector described the situation as durable; An Lin wanted measurements instead of adjectives. An Lin asked whether the team could emerge under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. A witness mentioned era during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The disputed notice used the word faithful, but its author had not signed the page.

到了下午，這項發現已傳到最可能蒙受損失的人耳中。羅總監要求封口，才願恢復戰隊宣傳福利。羅總監提出快速和解，條件卻是所有人不准再追問。安琳看著房內不安的臉孔。此時說些英雄話、要求大家冒一樣的風險很容易；他們卻讓每個人自己說明願意做什麼，以及拒絕承擔什麼。檢查員把情況形容為「耐用的」，安琳卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。安琳詢問小隊能否依安全規則「走出」，答案仍不明朗。見證人在訪談中提到「年代」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。有爭議的通知使用「忠實的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。

An Lin once believed that being captain meant knowing the next move before anyone else. Working with Coach Mei taught her a harder skill: making room for a teammate to say that the plan was unsafe or simply wrong. Such conversations were less exciting than a winning highlight shown on television. They also saved the team from spending its strength on a strategy that looked good only when nobody dared question it.

安琳曾以為當隊長就是比所有人更早知道下一步。與梅教練合作讓她學到更難的本事：讓隊友有空間說計畫不安全，或根本是錯的。這種對話沒有電視精彩片段刺激，卻能避免全隊把力氣浪費在只有沒人敢質疑時才看似漂亮的戰術上。

The plan looked simple on paper and complicated the moment people began moving. An Lin kept reporting facts through the approved review channel instead of launching personal attacks. A delay forced An Lin to choose between keeping the schedule and checking the safety condition again. The group checked again. They had lost time, but a false success would have cost them much more. Coach Mei marked the revised sequence where everyone could see it. The working file contained a separate entry for Fame, and An Lin asked who had verified it. Coach Mei found a reference to hardship in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date. A witness mentioned inflation during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. One witness promised to occupy if conditions improved, but An Lin would not treat hope as evidence.

計畫寫在紙上很簡單，人一動起來就變得複雜。安琳繼續透過正式審查管道通報事實，沒有做人身攻擊。一次延誤讓安琳必須在守住時間表與重新檢查安全條件之間選擇。小隊決定再查一次。雖然損失時間，虛假的成功卻可能造成更大代價。梅教練把修改後的順序標在所有人都看得見的位置。工作檔案為「名聲」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。梅教練在舊筆記裡找到關於「困境」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。見證人在訪談中提到「通貨膨脹」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「佔據」，安琳卻不能把希望當成證據。

The streets outside the tournament disciplinary office were quieter, but quiet was not proof of safety. The suspension was held pending the audit, and other players began submitting their own records. An Lin checked that everyone knew how to ask for help before the group separated. They had gained one answer and inherited two new responsibilities. Tomorrow's work would require patience from people who had every reason to be angry. For the first time that day, the team had a direction worth following. The working file contained a separate entry for procedure, and An Lin asked who had verified it. An Lin asked whether the team could pursue under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear. Someone had marked the warning tragic; Coach Mei checked whether that description was accurate. The disputed notice used the word tribal, but its author had not signed the page.

賽事紀律辦公室外的街道變安靜了，但安靜不代表安全。禁賽決定暫緩，其他選手也開始提交紀錄。安琳先確認大家知道如何求助，才讓小隊分開。他們得到一個答案，也多了兩份責任。明天的工作需要那些本來就有理由憤怒的人保持耐心。至少在這一天結束時，小隊終於有了一個值得追查的方向。工作檔案為「程序」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。安琳詢問小隊能否依安全規則「追查」，答案仍不明朗。有人把警告標成「悲劇的」，梅教練則查證這種描述是否準確。有爭議的通知使用「部族的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。
