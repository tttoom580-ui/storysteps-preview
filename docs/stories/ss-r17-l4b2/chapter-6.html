Hardware Under the Table

At the arena equipment inspection room, the first sign of trouble was not a scream or an alarm. It was a small disagreement about something everyone believed they understood. A technician found an undocumented wireless module beneath the team's official keyboard station. An Lin listened to the surrounding voices before making a judgment. Rumors traveled faster than careful questions, especially when the public wanted a simple villain. The team needed a record that would remain useful even if their favorite explanation proved false. Someone had marked the warning conservative; Coach Mei checked whether that description was accurate. The evidence list included consultant, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for consumer, and An Lin asked who had verified it. Coach Mei found a reference to dye in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

在競技館設備檢查室，麻煩不是從尖叫或警報開始，而是從一件大家自認了解的小事產生分歧。技師在戰隊官方鍵盤座下找到未登記的無線模組。安琳先聽周遭人的說法，沒有急著下判斷。群眾想找到簡單的壞人時，謠言總比仔細提問傳得快。即使最喜歡的推測最後被推翻，小隊也需要留下仍然有用的紀錄。有人把警告標成「保守的」，梅教練則查證這種描述是否準確。證物清單上有「顧問」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「消費者」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。梅教練在舊筆記裡找到關於「染料」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

The oldest document on the table looked less important than the newest screen. That appearance was misleading. Coach Mei compared the module with the leaked microphone channel and found a matching supplier. Coach Mei read the handwritten notes aloud while An Lin checked the sequence of events. Their different skills mattered more than either person's rank. When they found a contradiction, they preserved both versions. Destroying an inconvenient record would only make the next investigation harder. The old manual told its readers to enclose, and Coach Mei wanted to know who had written that rule. The evidence list included goods, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for grace, and An Lin asked who had verified it. The report called the arrangement habitual, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof.

桌上最舊的文件看起來不如最新螢幕重要，但那只是表面。梅教練把模組與洩漏麥克風通道比對，發現供應商相同。梅教練念出手寫備註，安琳逐項核對事件順序。此時兩人的不同專長，比誰的職位更高還重要。找到矛盾後，他們把兩個版本都保存下來。毀掉一份不方便的資料，只會讓下一次調查更加困難。舊手冊要求讀者「封入」，梅教練想知道這條規則由誰制定。證物清單上有「貨品」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「從容」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「習慣性的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。

By afternoon, the discovery had reached the people who could lose the most. Director Luo called the hardware a harmless convenience and demanded it remain connected. Director Luo offered a quick settlement, but it required the group to stop asking questions. An Lin considered the frightened faces around the room. It would be easy to sound heroic and demand that everyone take the same risk. Instead, each person was allowed to describe what they were prepared to do and what they would refuse. Someone had marked the warning ignorant; Coach Mei checked whether that description was accurate. One witness promised to invest if conditions improved, but An Lin would not treat hope as evidence. A written instruction told the staff to isolate before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. Coach Mei found a reference to linen in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

到了下午，這項發現已傳到最可能蒙受損失的人耳中。羅總監說硬體只是無害的便利設備，要求繼續連接。羅總監提出快速和解，條件卻是所有人不准再追問。安琳看著房內不安的臉孔。此時說些英雄話、要求大家冒一樣的風險很容易；他們卻讓每個人自己說明願意做什麼，以及拒絕承擔什麼。有人把警告標成「不了解的」，梅教練則查證這種描述是否準確。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「投資」，安琳卻不能把希望當成證據。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「隔離」，卻沒有人記下結果。梅教練在舊筆記裡找到關於「亞麻布」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

An Lin once believed that being captain meant knowing the next move before anyone else. Working with Coach Mei taught her a harder skill: making room for a teammate to say that the plan was unsafe or simply wrong. Such conversations were less exciting than a winning highlight shown on television. They also saved the team from spending its strength on a strategy that looked good only when nobody dared question it.

安琳曾以為當隊長就是比所有人更早知道下一步。與梅教練合作讓她學到更難的本事：讓隊友有空間說計畫不安全，或根本是錯的。這種對話沒有電視精彩片段刺激，卻能避免全隊把力氣浪費在只有沒人敢質疑時才看似漂亮的戰術上。

The plan looked simple on paper and complicated the moment people began moving. An Lin asked for a witnessed isolation test while preserving the device for investigation. A delay forced An Lin to choose between keeping the schedule and checking the safety condition again. The group checked again. They had lost time, but a false success would have cost them much more. Coach Mei marked the revised sequence where everyone could see it. A witness mentioned luxury during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included microscope, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for occupation, and An Lin asked who had verified it. Coach Mei found a reference to orientation in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

計畫寫在紙上很簡單，人一動起來就變得複雜。安琳要求有人見證的隔離測試，同時保留裝置供調查。一次延誤讓安琳必須在守住時間表與重新檢查安全條件之間選擇。小隊決定再查一次。雖然損失時間，虛假的成功卻可能造成更大代價。梅教練把修改後的順序標在所有人都看得見的位置。見證人在訪談中提到「奢華」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「顯微鏡」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「職業」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。梅教練在舊筆記裡找到關於「職前說明」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

The streets outside the arena equipment inspection room were quieter, but quiet was not proof of safety. The AI's predictions became slower once the unapproved module was disconnected. An Lin checked that everyone knew how to ask for help before the group separated. They had gained one answer and inherited two new responsibilities. Tomorrow's work would require patience from people who had every reason to be angry. For the first time that day, the team had a direction worth following. A witness mentioned rage during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included stereo, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. An inspector described the situation as timid; An Lin wanted measurements instead of adjectives. An Lin asked whether the team could tremble under the safety rules; the answer was not yet clear.

競技館設備檢查室外的街道變安靜了，但安靜不代表安全。未核准模組一斷線，AI的預測就慢了下來。安琳先確認大家知道如何求助，才讓小隊分開。他們得到一個答案，也多了兩份責任。明天的工作需要那些本來就有理由憤怒的人保持耐心。至少在這一天結束時，小隊終於有了一個值得追查的方向。見證人在訪談中提到「盛怒」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「立體聲」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。檢查員把情況形容為「膽小的」，安琳卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。安琳詢問小隊能否依安全規則「顫抖」，答案仍不明朗。
