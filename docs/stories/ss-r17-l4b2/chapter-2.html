A Round Lost Before It Starts

The day began inside a tournament practice booth. An Lin had come expecting an ordinary task, but the room already contained the sort of silence that follows bad news. The AI defeated a private practice lineup using a response that perfectly countered every planned move. Nobody could afford to confuse a confident explanation with a reliable observation. They had made that mistake before, and the cost was still visible in the people waiting outside. An Lin opened the notebook and decided to write down what could actually be tested. A witness mentioned ATOM during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The disputed notice used the word ATOMIC, but its author had not signed the page. The working file contained a separate entry for combination, and An Lin asked who had verified it. The report called the arrangement delicate, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof.

這一天從賽事練習隔間開始。安琳原以為只是處理例行工作，房間卻靜得像剛傳出壞消息。AI完美克制每個預定行動，擊敗隊伍的私人練習陣容。他們不能把說得肯定的解釋誤當成可靠觀察。大家以前犯過這種錯，代價仍寫在門外那些等待者的表情上。安琳打開筆記本，決定只記下真正能夠驗證的事情。見證人在訪談中提到「原子」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。有爭議的通知使用「原子能的」一詞，作者卻沒有在頁面簽名。工作檔案為「組合」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「微弱的／精細的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。

A new message reached Coach Mei while the group was still checking its first result. Coach Mei compared server times and noticed predictions arriving before the match data should have existed. An Lin refused to celebrate too early. They had learned that two records could repeat the same lie when both came from a single office. Before treating the discovery as independent evidence, the group searched for a witness who had nothing to gain by agreeing with them. A witness mentioned extent during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included fort, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for fossil, and An Lin asked who had verified it. Coach Mei found a reference to gaze in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

小隊還在核對第一項結果時，梅教練就收到新的消息。梅教練比對伺服器時間，發現預測早於比賽資料出現。安琳不肯太早慶祝。他們已學到，兩份紀錄如果來自同一辦公室，也可能只是重複同一個謊言。要把發現視為獨立證據前，大家先去尋找一個不會因附和他們而得利的見證人。見證人在訪談中提到「程度」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「堡壘」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「化石」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。梅教練在舊筆記裡找到關於「凝視」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

The easiest choice would have made An Lin popular for a single evening. Director Luo said advanced analysis naturally looked like magic and rejected questions about data access. Coach Mei reminded the group that their decision would still matter tomorrow, after the applause and anger faded. Nobody could offer a guarantee that every person would approve. They could, however, make the reasons public and leave a safe way for a witness to disagree. The old manual told its readers to guarantee, and Coach Mei wanted to know who had written that rule. The evidence list included immigration, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. An inspector described the situation as intimate; An Lin wanted measurements instead of adjectives. Coach Mei found a reference to landscape in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

最省事的選擇，或許能讓安琳當晚受到歡呼。羅總監說高階分析本來就像魔法，拒答資料存取問題。梅教練提醒大家，掌聲與怒氣消失後，決定明天仍然會造成影響。沒有人能保證所有人都滿意，但至少可以公開理由，並留下見證人安全表達反對的管道。舊手冊要求讀者「保證」，梅教練想知道這條規則由誰制定。證物清單上有「入出境／移民」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。檢查員把情況形容為「私密的」，安琳卻想要數據，而不是形容詞。梅教練在舊筆記裡找到關於「風景布景」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。

To the audience, the match was a picture of flashing colors and numbers moving faster than the eye. To An Lin, each number represented a decision made by a person with limited information and a tired body. Coach Mei had taught her to ask what the team could verify before choosing a spectacular move. The machine might calculate more quickly than any human, but a fair contest still required both sides to understand the rules of the room.

對觀眾而言，比賽是閃爍色彩與飛快跳動的數字；對安琳而言，每個數字都代表一個資訊有限、身體會疲累的人所做的選擇。梅教練教她先問隊伍能驗證什麼，再決定要不要做華麗動作。機器可以算得比人快，但公平競賽仍要求雙方都理解這個空間的規則。

They agreed to test a limited part of the plan before touching the main system. An Lin archived the practice replay and asked an independent technician to verify the timing. An Lin assigned roles according to practical skill rather than confidence. Coach Mei recorded each step, including the moments when someone had to stop. If the trial failed, the notes would explain why. If it worked, the notes would help the next group repeat it without depending on a hero who happened to be present. A witness mentioned messenger during the interview, so the team recorded the statement without endorsing it. The evidence list included mill, although nobody had explained why that detail mattered. The working file contained a separate entry for murmur, and An Lin asked who had verified it. The report called the arrangement productive, a judgment the group refused to accept without proof.

他們同意先做小範圍測試，不直接碰主要系統。安琳封存練習重播，請獨立技師驗證時間。安琳依照實際專長分派工作，不看誰說話最有自信。梅教練記錄每個步驟，連必須停止的時刻也不漏掉。如果測試失敗，筆記能說明原因；如果成功，下一批人也能按紀錄重做，不必依賴恰好在場的英雄。見證人在訪談中提到「送件員」，小隊照實記錄，卻沒有直接認同。證物清單上有「磨坊」，卻沒有人解釋這個細節為何重要。工作檔案為「低語」設了獨立項目，安琳詢問是誰查證過。報告說這項安排「有成果的」，小隊不願在沒有證據前接受此判斷。

The result was smaller than the promise they had hoped to make, but it was real. The technician found an unknown microphone channel connected to the team's practice room. Coach Mei closed the notebook only after marking the unresolved questions. An Lin was tired of victories that depended on hiding their cost. This one could be examined in daylight. And the detail they had not solved was already pointing toward the next part of the investigation. Someone had marked the warning psychological; Coach Mei checked whether that description was accurate. One witness promised to retain if conditions improved, but An Lin would not treat hope as evidence. A written instruction told the staff to revise before the deadline, but nobody had logged the result. Coach Mei found a reference to voyage in the old notes, but the page gave no reliable date.

結果比原先希望宣布的成果小，卻是真的。技師發現一條不明麥克風通道連著隊伍練習室。梅教練直到標出所有未解問題，才闔上筆記本。安琳早已厭倦靠隱瞞代價換來的勝利；這次的結果可以在日光下接受查證。那個尚未解開的細節，也已指向調查的下一步。有人把警告標成「心理上的」，梅教練則查證這種描述是否準確。一名見證人答應條件改善就會「保留」，安琳卻不能把希望當成證據。書面指示要求工作人員在期限前「修改」，卻沒有人記下結果。梅教練在舊筆記裡找到關於「航程」的記載，但紙上沒有可信的日期。
