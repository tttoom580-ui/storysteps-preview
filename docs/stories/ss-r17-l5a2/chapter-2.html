一塊麵包的價格

A Price for Bread

異世界銀行不收黃金 · 第 2 / 25 章 · 4000–5000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Su Yao discovers that a baker has forgotten her own daughter after a routine loan. The vault smelled of cold ink and rain-soaked wool, not the treasure Su Yao had expected. Su Yao had come to the City of Glass Ledgers looking for one reliable fact, not a miracle. By midday even the smallest object on the table looked capable of changing someone’s life. Ren Vale asked whether they should tell the others immediately. Instead, Su Yao wrote down what could be proved, what remained uncertain, and who might be hurt by a careless answer. An attractive theory was still only a theory, however badly everyone wanted it to be true.

蘇遙發現一名麵包師在例行借款後忘記了親生女兒。 金庫聞起來是冷墨與濕羊毛味，不是蘇遙原以為的寶藏氣味。 蘇遙來到玻璃帳城，為的是尋找一項可靠事實，而不是奇蹟。到了中午，桌上最小的物品似乎都能改變某人的人生。任維爾問是否該立刻告訴其他人。蘇遙卻先寫下哪些能證明、哪些仍不確定，以及草率回答可能傷害誰。再吸引人的理論仍然只是理論，即使所有人都迫切希望它成真。

The loan contract lacks a witness signature. The first reading made the result seem clear. The second raised questions that neither Su Yao nor Ren Vale could answer alone. They drew a line through every conclusion that depended on an unnamed witness, an unverified device, or a missing date. It was painful to lose so much apparent progress, but an empty space in a record was not permission to invent what belonged there. They would rather move slowly than build their next decision on a false certainty.

貸款契約缺少見證人的簽名。 第一遍讀時結果似乎很明確。第二遍卻提出蘇遙與任維爾都無法獨自回答的問題。他們把依賴匿名證人、未驗證儀器或缺失日期的結論逐一刪去。失去這麼多看似已有的進展令人難受，但紀錄有空白，不代表能隨意編造。他們寧可走慢一點，也不願讓下一個決策建立在虛假的確定性上。

Su Yao left the table to speak with Amara the debt advocate. Public stories rarely mentioned the people who waited, cleaned, repaired, or carried messages while celebrated experts argued. Those people knew which door was normally locked and which official arrived only after an emergency. Ren Vale wrote down their observations without turning them into accusations. By evening the investigation had gained no spectacular revelation, but it had acquired something harder to buy: several people willing to correct the investigators when they were mistaken.

蘇遙離開桌旁去和債務倡議者阿瑪拉談話。公開故事很少提到名家辯論時，那些負責等待、清潔、修理或送訊息的人。這些人知道哪扇門平時上鎖，也知道哪位官員總在事故後才到場。任維爾記下觀察，不把它們直接變成控訴。到了晚上，調查沒有驚人的揭露，卻得到更難買到的東西：幾個願意在調查者犯錯時糾正他們的人。

The message from Director Morcant arrived before the afternoon meeting. It sounded polite and left no room for refusal. Su Yao felt the old temptation to answer an insult with a louder insult, but Ren Vale asked to see the exact wording. They copied it into the record, checked whether it carried legal authority, and responded only to the part that required an answer. Behind the polished language lay a simple threat: stop asking questions or lose the little access they still possessed.

來自摩坎特總監的訊息在下午會議前送達。語氣禮貌，實際上卻不容拒絕。蘇遙一度想用更激烈的言語反擊侮辱，任維爾卻請她先看清楚原文。他們把訊息複製進紀錄，檢查是否有合法權限，只回答必須回應的部分。光鮮詞句背後是簡單的威脅：別再問，否則連目前僅存的查閱權也會失去。

The people outside had started measuring time in a different way. They counted missed meals, quiet nights, hospital visits, or the hours spent waiting for a message. Su Yao carried those numbers into the meeting so that nobody could call the problem merely technical. Ren Vale described what could be repaired immediately and what required another witness. No one applauded. Yet several faces relaxed when they heard a plan that did not demand blind faith or a heroic sacrifice.

外頭的人開始用另一種方式計算時間。他們數漏掉的飯、安靜的夜、就診次數，以及等待訊息的時數。蘇遙把那些數字帶進會議，讓沒人能把問題說成純技術。任維爾說明哪些能立刻修復、哪些還需要另一名見證者。沒有人鼓掌，但聽見不要求盲目信任或英雄犧牲的方案時，幾張臉終於放鬆。

On a second sheet, Su Yao found an old cross-reference index. Its headings were not proof of any theory; they were search terms left by previous workers. Entry 1 listed “absurd”, “adverse”, “architecture”, “donor”, “excessive”. Entry 2 listed “highlight”, “ideology”, “indigenous”, “journalism”, “journalist”. Entry 3 listed “massage”, “outsider”, “persist”, “prosecution”, “quest”. Entry 4 listed “render”, “rip”, “salmon”, “series”, “variation”. Ren Vale insisted that a label could not replace a witness, an observed result, or a decision that people had actually approved. The index remained in the archive as a way to retrace the team's work, not as a shortcut to a verdict.

另一張紙上，蘇遙找到舊交叉索引。標題並不能證明任何理論，只是前人留下的搜尋詞。第1欄列著“absurd”, “adverse”, “architecture”, “donor”, “excessive”。第2欄列著“highlight”, “ideology”, “indigenous”, “journalism”, “journalist”。第3欄列著“massage”, “outsider”, “persist”, “prosecution”, “quest”。第4欄列著“render”, “rip”, “salmon”, “series”, “variation”。任維爾堅持，標籤不能取代證人、實際觀察的結果，或當事人真正同意的決定。這份索引留在檔案中，方便後人回查工作，而非作為跳到結論的捷徑。

The bank’s counters held no coins. Under each brass number sat a cylinder containing a surrendered experience and the sealed receipt that made its trade visible. Su Yao examined the consent box before the balance; a blank signature meant more than a long column of numbers. Ren explained that a deposit could be counted without opening its private images. This distinction became their first defense against a system that confused ownership with permission.

銀行櫃檯沒有錢幣。每個黃銅號碼下方都有一支圓筒，保存交出的經驗及使交易可查的封存收據。蘇遙先看同意欄，再看結餘；空白簽名比長串數字更重要。任解釋，不開啟私人畫面也能核對存款。這項區別成為兩人對抗混淆擁有與允許之制度的第一道防線。

This was still the beginning. Su Yao could have pretended to understand the whole system after one strange discovery, but the ordinary people around her deserved better than certainty performed for applause. She wrote one modest promise at the bottom of the page: nothing would be changed without a way to measure the consequence and a way for those affected to refuse. Ren Vale signed beside it. Neither expected the promise to remain easy.

故事仍在起點。蘇遙原可在一次古怪發現後假裝理解整套系統，但身旁普通人值得比博掌聲的篤定更好的對待。她在頁尾寫下一句謹慎承諾：任何改動都要有量測後果的方法，也要讓受影響者有拒絕之路。任維爾在旁簽名。兩人都不認為承諾會一直容易。

The test did not answer every question. It established one limit, exposed one assumption, and forced the group to revise an earlier prediction. Su Yao found that more reassuring than an impossible perfect result. Ren Vale called the observers back to the table and asked them to describe what they had seen in their own words. The descriptions differed slightly, as honest recollections often do. They kept those differences. A truthful record was allowed to contain uncertainty and disagreement.

測試沒有回答所有問題，卻確認一項界線、揭露一個假設，並迫使大家修正先前的預測。蘇遙反而覺得這比不可能的完美結果更令人安心。任維爾把觀察者叫回桌前，請他們用自己的話說出所見。描述略有差異，正如誠實回憶常有的情況。他們保留那些差異。真實紀錄可以包含不確定和不同意見。

The final visitor departed after signing the updated log. Su Yao listened to the sounds of the City of Glass Ledgers and wondered how many similar stories had ended without anyone checking the dates. Ren Vale closed the case box but did not lock away the remaining questions. They agreed to carry the uncertainty openly into the next stage. It was not a promise of success. It was a promise that success, if it came, would not require them to pretend somebody else had never existed.

最後一名訪客簽完更新的登記冊後離開。蘇遙聽著玻璃帳城的聲音，想著有多少類似故事在沒人核對日期的情況下結束。任維爾合上案件箱，卻沒有把剩餘問題鎖起來。兩人同意坦白帶著不確定進入下一階段。這不是成功的保證，而是承諾若成功來臨，不需要假裝某個人從未存在。
