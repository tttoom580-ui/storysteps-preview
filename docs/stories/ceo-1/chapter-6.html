The First Bad Review

總裁，這份合約我改了｜第一卷：她刪掉的那一條 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The first bad review appeared before we had sold a single hotel bag. Someone wrote that our store used unsafe cloth and bought good reviews from friends. Under the words sat a photograph of a bag with a broken handle. It was blue, like ours, but its inside pocket opened on the wrong side. I knew because I had spent two evenings changing that pocket so Mother could reach it with her sore hand. "We answer with the design drawing," I said. Mother was already reading the next comment. Her face looked smaller than it had yesterday.

我們連一個飯店袋都還沒賣，第一則負評就出現了。有人說店裡用不安全的布，還花錢請朋友寫好評。下面放了一張提把斷掉的袋子照片，和我們的一樣藍，內袋卻開在錯的一側。我知道，因為為了讓媽媽疼痛的手方便伸進去，我花了兩晚改那個口袋。「我們用設計圖回應，」我說。媽媽已經讀起下一則留言，她的臉看起來比昨天更小了。

Owen arrived with breakfast and a printed copy of the photograph. "Do not let them make you argue with strangers all day," he said. He found the time when the picture first appeared and saved it. I posted a short answer showing the pocket difference, then offered to inspect any real bag bought from us. No angry names. No story about Father. It took me forty minutes to write four sentences because every longer version wanted to become a fight. When I finally closed the page, a customer stood outside holding a blue bag.

歐文帶著早餐和印出的照片到店。「別讓他們逼妳整天跟陌生人吵架。」他找出照片最早出現的時間，存下紀錄。我發了一段簡短回覆，指出內袋差異，並表示願意檢查任何真正在店裡購買的袋子。沒有罵人，沒有提爸爸。四句話寫了四十分鐘，因為每個更長的版本都忍不住想吵架。我終於關掉網頁時，一位客人拿著藍袋站在外面。

She was a nurse from the hospital at the end of the street. Her bag really had broken, though it was not the one in the picture. Mother took it gently. The sewing near one handle had come loose. We had made a mistake. I wanted to hide behind the false review, to say someone was trying to hurt us. Instead, I gave the nurse her money back and asked permission to keep the bag for a repair check. She agreed, then bought a smaller bag for her daughter. "You did not tell me it was my fault," she said.

她是街尾醫院的護理師。她的袋子真的壞了，雖然不是照片上的那只。媽媽輕輕接過，一邊提把附近的車縫鬆脫。我們確實犯了錯。我很想躲在假負評後面，說有人故意害我們，最後還是退款，並徵得她同意留下袋子檢查。她答應了，又替女兒買了一只小袋。「妳們沒有說是我的錯，」她說。

Adrian called during lunch. His team could remove the false picture from a company page where it had been shared. I said yes, but asked them to leave our reply visible. "There was also a real mistake," I told him. "That one stays in our records." He was quiet long enough for me to hear voices behind him. "My team says we should delay the collection," he said. I closed my eyes. Then he added, "I asked them to explain which test failed. They have not sent me an answer yet."

午餐時沈予川來電。假照片被轉貼到公司的網頁，他的團隊可以移除。我說好，但請保留我們的回覆。「也有一個真的瑕疵，」我告訴他，「那個必須留在紀錄裡。」他安靜得讓我聽見電話另一頭的人聲。「團隊建議延後商品上市。」他說。我閉上眼睛。他又補了一句：「我請他們說明哪項測試沒過，目前還沒收到答案。」

We spent the afternoon pulling at handles until our fingers hurt. Owen built a simple stand from spare wood and used bottles filled with water as weights. Every bag carried its maker's name and the time it was finished. Three failed. Two had been sewn while Mother's machine was making its strange clicking sound. The third was mine. I had rushed it because I wanted the meeting sample to look perfect. I put all three failures on the front table. A beautiful sample meant nothing if I pretended the other bags did not exist.

整個下午，我們把提把拉到手指發疼。歐文拿剩木料做了簡單架子，用裝水瓶當砝碼。每個袋子都標明製作者與完工時間。三只沒通過：兩只是媽媽的機器發出怪異喀噠聲時縫的，第三只是我做的。為了讓會議樣品完美，我趕了工。我把三只失敗品全放在前桌。如果假裝其他袋子不存在，再美的樣品也毫無意義。

At closing time, Adrian arrived with a written copy of the hotel's actual test rules. They were harder than ours, but they were clear. We could meet them if we had three more days. "Ask the same of every supplier," I said. He nodded. Before leaving, he studied our failed bags. "Most people would put these away when I arrived." "Most people did not grow up watching a blue mark become somebody else's answer," I said. His hand stopped above the table. Outside, a car waited in the dark with its lights turned off.

打烊時，沈予川帶來飯店正式的測試規則。比我們的嚴格，但清清楚楚；多三天，我們就做得到。「每家供應商都要照這個標準，」我說。他點頭。離開前，他研究桌上的失敗品。「多數人在我到時，會把它們收起來。」「多數人沒有從小看著一個藍記號，變成別人交差的答案。」我說。他的手停在桌面上方。外面，一輛沒開燈的車在黑暗中等著。
