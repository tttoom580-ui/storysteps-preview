The envelope contained an invitation to a hotel dinner. It named our store, not me, and asked us to bring a sample of our work. Mother immediately began searching for the blue dress she had kept since my cousin's wedding. I read the small print. Each supplier would receive four minutes to explain a product before a team chose the opening collection. "It is a meeting with soup," I told her. "We need the right bag, not the right dress." She looked at my old shoes with the deep sadness only a mother can bring to shoes.

信封裡是一張飯店晚宴邀請，受邀的是我們的店，不是我個人，並請我們帶作品樣品。媽媽立刻翻找從表姊婚禮後就留著的藍洋裝。我讀了小字：每家供應商有四分鐘介紹產品，再由團隊挑選開幕商品。「這是有湯喝的會議，」我告訴她，「我們需要的是對的袋子，不是對的洋裝。」她看著我的舊鞋，露出一種只有母親看鞋子時才會有的深切悲傷。

Owen caught us outside his shop that afternoon. He had found his own envelope after returning from a job. "They want my repair service," he said. "The rich have finally learned that machines break." I showed him Father's photograph. He studied the date on the back and became quiet. His father had died the year after mine. He had left a large metal box that Owen used to hold tools. "There may be papers under the bottom," he said. "I never looked. I thought I knew everything I needed to know about him."

下午，歐文在自己店外叫住我們。他出門工作回來，也發現了信封。「他們想要我的維修服務，」他說，「有錢人終於知道機器會壞了。」我給他看爸爸的照片。他研究背面日期，安靜下來。他父親在我父親去世的隔年也走了，留下的大鐵箱一直被他拿來裝工具。「底下也許有文件，」他說，「我從來沒翻。我以為，關於他該知道的事，我都知道了。」

At the dinner, a man at the door asked whether Owen was my assistant. "No," I said. "He owns the business beside mine." The man changed his list. It was a small correction, but Owen's shoulders relaxed. We found our table behind a large plant, where nobody could see the screen. I carried the plant to the corner before sitting down. Across the room, Adrian watched me, then came over. "The plant had a better seat," I explained. "I noticed," he said. He did not move our table closer to his.

晚宴入口的人問歐文是不是我的助理。「不是，他是隔壁店的老闆。」那人改了名單。只是個小修正，歐文的肩膀卻放鬆了。我們的桌子藏在大盆栽後面，完全看不到螢幕。我先把盆栽搬到角落才坐下。對面的沈予川看著我，隨後走來。「原本是植物坐得比較好，」我解釋。「我看到了。」他說。他沒有把我們的桌子搬到自己身邊。

Our four minutes began with a broken strap. I had brought it on purpose. "A hotel bag can look beautiful for a photograph and fail on the way to the station," I said. I passed the strap around the table, then showed the stronger stitching on ours. A buyer asked whether our bags could cost less. "Yes, if we remove the inside pocket," I answered. "No, if you want us to hide weaker cloth inside the same design." The buyer leaned forward. For the first time that evening, someone began taking notes.

我們的四分鐘從一條斷背帶開始。那是我故意帶的。「飯店袋在照片上很好看，也可能去車站的路上就壞了。」我把背帶傳給大家，再展示我們更牢靠的縫法。採購問能不能便宜點。「拿掉內袋就可以，」我回答，「如果是要在同樣的設計裡偷換比較差的布，不行。」他往前傾身。那晚第一次，有人開始認真記筆記。

An older man in a gray suit joined us after dessert. Adrian introduced him as his uncle, Victor. Victor remembered our family name immediately. "Your father was a clever man," he said. "It is sad when clever people take the wrong road." I wanted to throw my water at him. Instead, I asked which road he meant. His smile did not change. "This is a happy evening. Let us leave old trouble alone." Adrian said, very clearly, "Then do not bring it to her table." Victor looked at him as though a chair had unexpectedly begun to speak.

甜點後，一個穿灰西裝的長輩加入我們。沈予川介紹他是叔叔維克多。對方立刻認出我們的姓。「妳父親是個聰明人，」他說，「聰明人走錯路，真令人難過。」我很想把水潑向他，最後只問他指哪條路。他笑容不變。「今晚這麼開心，舊麻煩就別提了吧。」沈予川清楚地說：「那就別把它帶到她的桌上。」維克多看著他，神情像是一張椅子突然開口說話。

Outside, Owen carried our samples while I waited for the bus. Adrian stood beside us, his dinner jacket over one arm. "I should have warned you," he said. I agreed. He looked ready to offer an explanation, then stopped. "Next time I will." A message lit Owen's phone. His neighbor had found the tool box open in his shop. Nothing expensive was missing, only the false wooden bottom. We looked at one another. Somewhere between the first course and dessert, somebody else had remembered Owen's father too.

外面，歐文拿著樣品，我在等公車。沈予川站在一旁，晚宴外套搭在手臂上。「我應該先提醒妳。」他說。我同意。他原本像要解釋，卻停住了。「下次我會。」歐文的手機亮起。鄰居發現他店裡的工具箱被打開，貴重東西都沒少，只有那塊假的木箱底不見了。我們互望。在前菜與甜點之間，也有別人想起了歐文的父親。
