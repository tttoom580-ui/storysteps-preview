我不是你的助理

Not Your Assistant

The café beside the station had bright windows and six different kinds of tea. Helen chose a seat facing the door. June sat beside her, one hand around a cup she did not drink from. I placed Mother's small red bag on the table. June touched its edge and smiled before she could stop herself. "She remembered," she said. Helen looked at me with tired, careful eyes. "I used to bring you here when your father worked late," she said. "You always wanted the seat with the yellow picture above it."

車站旁的咖啡店窗戶明亮，有六種茶。海倫選了朝向門口的座位，小君坐旁邊，一隻手圈著她沒喝的杯子。我把媽媽做的小紅袋放在桌上。小君摸摸邊緣，還沒來得及掩飾就笑了。「她還記得。」海倫用疲憊而謹慎的眼神看我。「妳爸爸加班時，我以前會帶妳來。妳總要坐上面有黃色圖畫的位置。」

I could not remember the picture. I said so. Helen nodded as if an honest failure of memory were something she could work with. She had cut cloth at the factory. One evening, two deliveries arrived in boxes carrying the same order number. The better cloth went into samples for the hotel owners. The cheaper cloth went into the bags. Helen hurt her hand when a machine caught a rough edge. Daniel found the difference and stopped the next delivery. "Then someone told the workers he was stopping our wages too," she said. "People wanted somebody to blame."

我不記得那幅畫，便照實說。海倫點頭，彷彿坦承記憶失靈，反而是能合作的基礎。她以前在工廠裁布。某晚兩批貨送來，箱上訂單號相同，好布拿來做給飯店業主看的樣品，便宜布用在袋子裡。粗糙的邊緣捲進機器，弄傷她的手。爸爸發現差異，攔住下一批貨。「後來有人告訴工人，他也攔住了薪水。大家需要一個可以怪的人。」

She would not give me the book. She did not say she still had it, only that handing old papers to a powerful family had not helped anyone before. I showed her the copied test sheets and explained Rose's work. Helen watched my face while I spoke. "Are you his assistant now?" she asked. "No. My store has a contract with his company. That is separate." The answer sounded stronger than I felt. June asked whether my contract allowed me to leave. I promised to check before making another promise to anybody else.

她不肯把本子交給我，甚至沒承認還留著，只說以前把舊文件交給有權勢的家族，沒幫到任何人。我給她看測試表副本，解釋蘿絲的工作。她盯著我的臉。「妳現在是他的助理？」「不是，我的店和他的公司有合約，那是另外一件事。」這個答案聽起來比我的底氣更強。小君問合約允不允許我離開。我答應先核對，再對任何人作新的承諾。

At the hotel that afternoon, a staff member handed me Adrian's schedule and asked me to arrange dinner for two visiting buyers. I gave it back. "I am here about bags." She turned red. Adrian came out of his meeting and began to explain. I stopped him gently. "Please explain to your team, not to me." He did. He introduced our store by its full name and asked a staff member to arrange the buyers' dinner. Then he showed me the meeting room instead of asking me to wait outside his office.

下午在飯店，一名員工把沈予川的行程表交給我，要我替兩個來訪採購安排晚餐。我還回去。「我來談袋子的事。」她紅了臉。沈予川開完會走出來，開始解釋。我輕聲制止：「請向你的團隊解釋，不用向我。」他照做，用完整店名介紹我們，另請員工安排採購的晚餐。接著，他帶我看會議室，沒有再叫我等在辦公室外。

I asked him about leaving the contract. Nina brought the new draft. We could end future work after giving notice, but the company could keep using already delivered designs for the agreed year. We would be paid for finished work and materials bought for approved orders. That sounded fair until I reached the last line. If the hotel delayed opening, the payment date would move with it. "So your delay becomes my debt," I said. Adrian read the sentence twice. "Change it," he told Nina. I asked her to write our reasons beside the change.

我問能否終止合約。妮娜拿來新草案。我們提前通知就可結束未來合作，但公司仍能在約定一年內使用已交設計；完成的工作，和核准訂單已買的材料，都會付款。聽起來公平，直到最後一行：飯店延後開幕，付款日也跟著延。「所以，你們的延誤就變成我的債。」我說。沈予川讀兩遍，請妮娜改。我請她連改動理由一起寫在旁邊。

When I returned to the café for my forgotten umbrella, June was waiting outside. She gave me a small envelope. Inside was one photograph of a page with my father's name beneath a long note. The heading read TEMPORARY HOLD. "My mother needs to know you can say no to him," June said. "Today you did." I asked whether Helen had the rest. June stepped back into the station crowd. "She has something more difficult," she answered. "She knows who asked her to bring it to your house."

我回咖啡店拿忘記的傘，小君在門外等。她交給我一個小信封，裡面是一張頁面照片，爸爸的名字在一段長註記下面，標題是「暫時扣留」。「媽媽需要知道，妳能不能對他說不。今天妳做到了。」我問海倫是不是有其餘文件。小君退入車站人潮。「她有更難處理的東西，」她說，「她知道當初是誰叫她把它帶去妳家。」
