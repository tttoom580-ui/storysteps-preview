開店倒數三天

Three Days to Open

The opening was three days away, not tomorrow. Nina's paper was a planning sheet from five years ago, when the hotel had first been promised. Someone had moved the old date forward in red, then used the sheet again. I stared at the numbers until they stopped looking like numbers. "Why keep an old form?" I asked. Nina said the hotel project had changed owners inside the same group. Many papers had followed it without being checked. The new launch was built on old boxes, old names, and perhaps old mistakes that nobody wanted to count twice.

開幕在三天後，不是明天。妮娜的紙是五年前的計畫表，當時就承諾要蓋這間飯店。有人用紅筆把舊日期往後改，又拿這張表來用。我盯著數字，直到它們幾乎不像數字。「為什麼留著舊表？」妮娜說，飯店計畫在同一集團內換過負責單位，許多文件沒經核對便跟著移交。新的發表會建在舊箱子、舊名字上，也許還建在沒人願意重算一次的舊錯誤上。

I called Adrian and asked him to meet us in the shop. I did not ask him to come alone. Nina needed someone from the company who could record her account properly. He arrived with a quiet woman named Rose, who worked outside Victor's team. She asked Nina for permission before turning on a recorder. We sat around Mother's cutting table. The place where she usually spread cloth became a map of five years: dates on one side, names on the other, and a wide strip of empty wood for everything we still did not know.

我打給沈予川，請他來店裡，沒有要求他獨自來。妮娜需要公司的人正式記錄證詞。他帶來一位安靜的女人蘿絲，她不在維克多的團隊裡。開錄音前，她先取得妮娜同意。我們圍著媽媽的裁布桌坐下。平常鋪布的地方，變成五年的地圖：一邊擺日期，一邊擺名字，中間空出寬闊木桌面，留給還不知道的一切。

Nina explained that Daniel had stopped two boxes after finding that their cloth did not match the order. Helen, a worker from the cutting room, had brought him a second sample. The next morning, Nina received a form with Daniel's name under the word CHECKED. She entered it into the company record. She never saw him sign. When the cloth later failed, her entry was used to show that the company had followed its rules. "I thought it was only a late paper," she said. "I did not ask why his name looked different."

妮娜說，林立安發現布料與訂單不符，攔下兩箱。裁剪室的海倫又帶來第二份樣品。隔天早上，妮娜收到表格，「已檢查」欄下有爸爸的名字，於是輸入公司紀錄。她沒親眼看他簽字。後來布料出問題，這筆紀錄被拿來證明公司遵守規則。「我以為只是晚送來的文件，」她說，「沒有追問，為什麼他的名字看起來不太一樣。」

Adrian wanted to stop our collection immediately. I surprised myself by refusing. "Stop anything that has not passed the new tests," I said. "But do not stop my work because we found something uncomfortable about yours." Rose asked for all the bag test sheets. Owen brought them, along with the three failed samples. They checked each bag against the hotel rules. By six, we had a smaller collection that passed. Half the planned bags would not reach the launch. I could lose money telling the truth. At least I would know which truth had cost it.

沈予川想立刻停止我們的商品上市，我竟然拒絕了。「沒通過新測試的，全部停，」我說，「但別因為查出你的工作有難堪的事，就停掉我的工作。」蘿絲要所有袋子的測試表。歐文連同三只失敗樣品一起拿來，逐一對照飯店規則。六點時，我們得到規模較小、卻全部合格的系列。原訂袋子有一半趕不上發表會。說實話可能讓我損失金錢，至少我知道，是哪個事實讓我付了代價。

We worked until the bakery closed. Mother sewed, Owen checked the machine, and I cut cloth on the floor because the table was still full of papers. Adrian asked what he could do. "Carry these," I said, pointing at the finished bags. He folded his sleeves and carried them upstairs without making a speech about helping. When he returned, his tie was crooked. Mother fixed it before remembering who he was. I looked away before either of them saw me smile. Outside, rain began tapping at the window in quick, uneven beats.

我們一直忙到麵包店關門。媽媽縫袋，歐文檢查機器；桌上還攤滿文件，我只好在地上裁布。沈予川問能幫什麼。「搬這些，」我指著完成的袋子。他捲起袖子搬上樓，沒發表一篇幫忙宣言。再下來時，領帶歪了。媽媽替他整理，動手後才想起他是誰。我先轉頭，免得兩人看到我笑。外面開始下雨，雨點敲窗，急而不規則。

At nine, Rose called. She had found another copy of the old form. Its signature space was empty. Both copies carried the same page number and the same time. One had been placed in Daniel's file. The other had been placed in a folder marked RETURNED GOODS. Adrian stood beside me as I listened. "They cannot both be the final page," I said. "They can," Rose answered, "if somebody removed the page that explained why there were two." I looked at the space on our table. It no longer seemed empty. It seemed deliberately cleared.

九點，蘿絲來電。她找到同一張舊表的另一份副本，簽名欄卻是空白。兩張的頁碼和時間都相同，一張存進爸爸的檔案，另一張在標為「退貨」的資料夾裡。我接電話時，沈予川就站在身邊。「它們不可能都是最終版本。」「有可能，」蘿絲回答，「如果有人抽走了那張解釋為何有兩個版本的頁面。」我看著桌中央的空間。它已不像空白，更像被人刻意清空。
