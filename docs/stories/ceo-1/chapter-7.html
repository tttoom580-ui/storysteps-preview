他的另一支手機

His Other Phone

總裁，這份合約我改了｜第一卷：她刪掉的那一條 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The car belonged to Adrian's driver, not a stranger. That should have made me feel better. It did not explain why the driver looked afraid to step inside. Adrian went out to speak with him. Through the glass, I saw the man hand him a second phone, smaller than the one he used at work. Adrian read its screen and turned away from the window. Mother began pulling down the blind. I stopped her. "If there is something I need to know," I said when he returned, "tell me before I sign anything."

那是沈予川司機的車，不是陌生人。知道這點應該讓我放心，卻不能解釋司機為什麼怕得不敢進門。沈予川出去跟他說話。隔著玻璃，我看到司機交給他另一支手機，比工作用的小。他看完螢幕，轉身背對窗戶。媽媽開始拉下百葉簾，我攔住她。他回來時，我說：「如果有什麼我應該知道的事，請在我簽字以前說。」

He put the little phone on the counter. A family message group filled the screen. Victor had shared the false review and asked why Adrian was risking the hotel opening for a shop with a bad history. Under it, three people had agreed. Adrian's reply said the review was being checked. "You did not mention the real broken bag," I said. "I wanted to protect your business." "Then protect its right to tell the whole story." He looked at the failed samples on our table and slowly nodded. I could see how much he disliked being corrected twice in one day.

他把小手機放上櫃檯，螢幕裡是家族群組。維克多轉貼假負評，質問他為何要為一家名聲不好的店拿飯店開幕冒險，下面有三人附和。沈予川的回覆是：正在查證負評。「你沒提真的壞掉的袋子。」「我想保護妳的生意。」「那就保護它說完整故事的權利。」他看著桌上的失敗樣品，慢慢點頭。我看得出來，他很不喜歡一天被糾正兩次。

He called the driver back inside. The man's name was Ben. For twenty years he had driven members of the Shen family through the city. "Did you drive my father anywhere?" I asked before I could lose my courage. Ben looked at Adrian, who said nothing. I did not help him by asking again. At last Ben answered. He had driven Daniel Lin to the old factory on the evening before the final report. A woman had got into the car beside him. She carried a blue book and kept her left hand inside her coat.

他請司機進來。司機叫班，二十年來都替沈家人開車。「你載過我爸爸嗎？」勇氣還沒退去，我就問了。班看向沈予川，後者沒開口。我也沒有再問一次替他解圍。最後，他說出答案：最終報告完成前一晚，他曾送林立安到舊工廠。有個女人坐在爸爸身旁，抱著藍本子，左手一直藏在外套裡。

"Was she Helen Wu?" I asked. He could not remember her name. He remembered her hand because she had hurt it, and Father had asked him to drive slowly over a rough part of the road. I wrote that down. It was not proof of anything, but it was a detail a story would find difficult to invent by accident. Ben also remembered dropping them at the side gate. Someone waited there with an umbrella. He had thought it was Nina, though the dark and rain had hidden most of the person's face.

「是吳海倫嗎？」我問。他不記得名字，卻記得那隻手，因為她受了傷，爸爸請他行經顛簸路段時開慢點。我把這件事寫下來。它不能證明什麼，卻是很難無意間編造得出的細節。班也記得在側門讓兩人下車，有人撐傘等著。他當時以為那是妮娜，但黑暗和雨遮住了對方大半張臉。

Adrian sent the full account of our tests to his team while we watched. Then he turned off the small phone. "I should keep them separate," he said, pointing at the two devices. "Family and work?" I asked. "No. What they tell me and what I know." I almost smiled. It was the first thing he had said that evening which did not sound practiced. Mother brought fresh tea, then asked Ben whether he had eaten. He looked more surprised by that question than by all of mine.

沈予川當著我們的面，把完整測試紀錄傳給團隊，再關掉小手機。「我應該把它們分開。」他指著兩支手機說。「家庭和工作？」「不是。他們告訴我的，和我真正知道的。」我差點笑了。這是他那晚第一句聽起來沒有排練過的話。媽媽端來新泡的茶，問班吃飯沒。和我剛才所有問題相比，這一句更讓他意外。

When they left, I found a message from Nina. She would meet me tomorrow, alone if I preferred. Below it was a photograph of a younger woman sitting on our shop's front step with a child asleep against her shoulder. The woman wore a white cover over her left hand. The child was me. I took the phone to Mother. She sat down before I asked a question. "Helen used to help when you were little," she said. "Your father told me never to contact her again."

他們走後，我發現妮娜傳來訊息。她可以明天見我，若我希望，也能單獨見面。下面有張照片：一個較年輕的女人坐在我們店門前的台階，孩子靠著她的肩膀睡著。女人左手纏著白色包覆物。那孩子是我。我拿手機給媽媽看。還沒提問，她就坐下了。「妳小時候，海倫常來幫忙。」她說，「後來妳爸爸叫我不要再聯絡她。」
