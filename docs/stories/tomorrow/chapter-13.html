四號桌的女孩

The Girl at Table Four

明天，別來學校 · 第 13 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

June was eating a cheese sandwich when I saw her. I had imagined tears, maybe a long silence, maybe her walking away before I could speak. I had not imagined a sandwich. She looked up, covered her mouth to finish chewing, and pointed at the chair across from her. Her hair was shorter. There was a small spot of yellow paint on her left sleeve.

我看見 June 時，她正在吃起司三明治。我想過眼淚、長久沉默、她在我開口前離開，沒想過三明治。她抬頭，摀嘴嚼完，指向對面椅子。頭髮變短了，左袖有一小點黃色顏料。

Ms Lin sat at the next table with June's mother. They were close enough to help and far enough to let us choose our own words. I sat down and put my phone away. June noticed. “You can keep it out if you want,” she said. “I just don't want to be recorded.” “I won't.” “I know you might want proof of everything now.” She looked at her sandwich.

林老師和 June 的媽媽坐鄰桌，近到能幫忙，又遠到讓我們自己選詞。我坐下收手機。June 注意到了。「想放外面也行，我只是不想被錄。」「我不會。」「我知道妳現在可能什麼都想要證據。」她低頭看三明治。

“I am sorry,” I said. The words came out quickly, like air from a hole. “I voted. I didn't look at the screen. I let people say you took money.” June nodded once. “I know.” I had imagined that telling her would break something open. Instead, it was simply information she already had. There was no surprise left for me to hide behind.

「對不起。」話像破洞洩氣般快速跑出來。「我投了票，沒看螢幕，讓大家說妳拿錢。」June 點一下頭。「我知道。」我以為說出口會打開什麼，結果只是她早就知道的資訊。我再也無法躲在她的驚訝後面。

She said her mother had asked for help from the school six weeks ago. The school told them the money question was settled and suggested a short break. The break grew longer. June stayed with her aunt, where the neighbors did not know her face. She studied at the kitchen table. She learned which shops were quiet after three. She did not spend every day planning a way to get back at us.

她說六週前媽媽向學校求助，學校說錢的問題已解決，建議短暫休息。休息越來越長。她住到阿姨家，鄰居不認得她的臉，在餐桌念書，學會哪些店三點後很安靜。她並沒有每天都計畫如何報復我們。

“Then why the future messages?” I asked. She pressed the sandwich paper flat. “Because when I sent you a normal message, you didn't answer.” I opened my mouth. She held up a hand. “Not after I left. Before. I asked if you could talk that night. You sent me the vote link and asked if it was true.” I remembered. I had pushed that part away too.

「那為什麼傳未來訊息？」我問。她壓平三明治包裝紙。「因為傳普通訊息給妳時，妳沒回。」我張嘴，她抬手。「不是離開後，是之前。我問晚上能不能談，妳傳投票連結，問是不是真的。」我記得，那部分我也推開了。

June had copied Sam's warning because she wanted me to stay away from the new vote. Then anger changed the plan. She used our old class password to enter my Later account. She wrote tomorrow's date in the letter and added the cut voice file. “It wasn't the website saying it came from the future,” she said. “It was me, typing a date. You did the rest.”

June 複製 Sam 的警告，本來想讓我遠離新投票，後來憤怒改了計畫。她用舊班級密碼登入我的 Later，寫上明天日期，加上剪過的語音。「不是網站說它來自未來，是我自己打日期，剩下的是妳想像的。」

I stared at the table. “You scared me.” “I know.” “You could have told me plainly.” “I tried.” The answer was sharp, and both of us heard it. June breathed out slowly. “That doesn't make it right. I shouldn't have used your account. I wanted you to know what it feels like when your own voice stops belonging to you.” She looked at my phone, dark beside my bag.

我盯著桌子。「妳嚇到我。」「我知道。」「可以直接說。」「我試過。」答案尖銳，我們都聽見了。June 慢慢吐氣。「那不代表這樣是對的。我不該用妳帳號。我想讓妳知道，自己的聲音不再屬於自己，是什麼感覺。」她看向包旁暗著的手機。

I wanted to argue. I wanted to hug her. Instead I asked whether she had sent the photo of my handwriting. She nodded. Bell had sent it to her as a threat against me. She had forwarded it without explaining, hoping I would finally understand how easy a cut could be. “I'm sorry for that too,” she said. Then she placed a small memory card between us. “This part isn't a trick.”

我想爭辯，也想抱她，最後只問字跡照片是否她寄的。她點頭。Bell 拿照片威脅她，說要對付我；她沒解釋就轉寄，希望我終於明白剪掉一部分有多容易。「那件事我也道歉。」她把小記憶卡放在我們中間。「但這個不是騙局。」
