我的字跡

My Handwriting

明天，別來學校 · 第 7 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The words in the photograph were mine, but they were not a vote. I pulled an old exercise book from my shelf and found the page. Six weeks ago, our class had written an opinion for a lesson about online news. I had written, I believe June took the money because the video shows her by the box. I remembered feeling proud of the clear sentence.

照片裡的字是我寫的，卻不是投票。我從書架抽出舊作業簿，找到那一頁。六週前，班上上網路新聞課，寫自己的看法。我寫的是：我相信 June 拿了錢，因為影片拍到她站在錢盒旁。我記得自己還為句子寫得清楚而自豪。

Below it, Ms Lin had written a question in green: Does being near something prove that you took it? I had never answered. Someone had cut that green question out of the photograph. My sentence now looked like a note from the secret vote book. I took a new picture with the whole page showing and sent both images to Ms Lin. My hands shook, but I kept them steady enough.

下方，林老師用綠筆問：靠近某樣東西，就能證明你拿了它嗎？我從沒回答。有人把綠色問題裁掉，讓我的句子看起來像秘密投票本上的筆記。我重新拍下完整頁面，把兩張照片都傳給林老師。手在抖，我還是讓它穩到足以拍照。

Leo called when I sent him the pictures. “They want you scared of speaking,” he said. “If you say the video was cut, they'll show this and call you a liar.” “I did vote, though.” There was a long silence. I told him about the blue button. I told him I had watched June's number grow. I did not say that everybody else had done it. That would not help.

Leo 收到照片後打來。「他們想讓妳害怕說話。如果妳說影片被剪過，他們就會用這張說妳撒謊。」「可是我真的投了。」沉默很久。我告訴他藍色按鈕，告訴他我看著 June 的數字上升。我沒有說大家都做了，那沒幫助。

“I voted too,” he said at last. The room seemed to tilt a little. “I voted no,” he added quickly. “But I kept going back. I watched the number.” We sat with our two different wrong things. Neither of us could make the other feel clean. Downstairs, my father laughed at something on television. I wondered how many ordinary nights could sit on top of one terrible day.

「我也投了。」他終於說。房間好像歪了一點。「我投不相信，」他趕緊補充，「但一直回去看數字。」我們各自陪著不同的錯，誰也不能讓對方變乾淨。樓下爸爸看電視笑了。我想，一個可怕的日子上，究竟可以堆著多少普通夜晚。

The sender's email name was J. I wrote one short reply. If you are June, I want to hear you. If you are not, stop using her name. I did not add a heart. I did not ask her to prove who she was by sharing something private. I sent it, put the phone down, and opened my homework. I read the same line six times.

寄件人的名字是 J。我回了一小段：如果妳是 June，我想聽妳說。如果不是，別再用她的名字。我沒加愛心，也沒要求她分享隱私來證明身分。我送出，把手機放下，打開作業。同一行看了六次。

At eleven, J answered. Ask Leo about the copy. I called him again. This time he did not answer. A minute later, he wrote, Can't talk. Brother asleep. I asked what copy meant. Three little dots appeared, disappeared, and appeared again. Then his message came. There was a backup of the film folder. I thought I deleted it. I need to check something tomorrow.

十一點，J 回覆：問 Leo 那份副本。我又打給他，這次他沒接。一分鐘後他寫：不能講，弟弟睡了。我問副本是什麼。三個小點出現、消失，又出現。他終於傳來：電影資料夾有備份。我以為刪掉了，明天得確認一些事。

“Why didn't you say that at Ms Lin's?” I typed. He answered, Because I was scared she would see what I had kept. I stared at those words until the screen became dark. It was easy to think fear belonged to other people: Sam by the tap, June in the old video. But Leo was afraid too. My clever, careful friend was sitting somewhere with a secret he could not carry.

「為什麼在林老師那裡不說？」我打字。他回答：因為我怕她看到我留下的東西。我盯著這句，直到螢幕暗下。很容易以為害怕是別人的事：水龍頭旁的 Sam、舊影片裡的 June。但 Leo 也怕。我聰明又小心的朋友，正獨自背著扛不住的秘密。

I wanted to tell him I trusted him. I also wanted to ask exactly what he had kept. Both feelings were true, and neither made the other leave. I finally wrote, We check it with Ms Lin. Not alone. He sent back one word: Okay. Before I slept, I checked the Zero page once more. The vote was closed. Under Leo's name was a new title: The Boy Who Saved Everything.

我想告訴他我相信他，也想追問他究竟留了什麼。兩種感覺同時存在，誰也趕不走誰。最後我寫：跟林老師一起查，不要獨自去。他回：好。睡前我又看一次 Zero。投票結束了。Leo 的名字下有新標題：把所有東西都留下的男孩。
