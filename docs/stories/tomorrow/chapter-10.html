兩種不同的警告

Two Different Warnings

明天，別來學校 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 1000以下 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Don't come to school. Make sure Mia comes to school. I wrote both sentences on a clean page. For two days, I had treated every strange message as one voice. Now I drew a line between them. The Later letters wanted me away. Bell wanted me here. One used my old voice to pull me toward June. The other used names and numbers to move people around.

別來學校。確保 Mia 來學校。我把兩句寫在乾淨紙上。兩天來，我把所有怪訊息都當成同一個聲音。現在我畫線分開：Later 要我離開，Bell 要我出現。一個用舊語音把我拉向 June，另一個用名字與數字移動大家。

Leo read the page over my shoulder. “Two senders,” he said. “Or one person changing the plan.” “We don't know yet.” It felt new to say that without feeling stupid. We took Ada's messages to Ms Lin. Ada came with us. She asked if her friends would find out she had spoken. Ms Lin explained who needed the information and said she would not promise something she could not control.

Leo 從肩後看。「兩個寄件人，或同一人改計畫。」「我們還不知道。」能說這句而不覺得自己笨，是新的感覺。我們帶 Ada 的訊息去找林老師，Ada 一起來。她問朋友會不會知道她說了。老師解釋誰需要知道，說不會承諾自己無法控制的事。

After school, my mother waited outside the gate. Ms Lin had asked me to tell her what was happening, and this time I had. Mum did not take my phone away. She asked if I wanted to talk in the car or have quiet first. I chose quiet. We bought warm buns on the way home. I held one in both hands until my fingers stopped feeling cold.

放學後媽媽在門口等。林老師要我告訴她發生的事，這次我照做了。媽媽沒收走手機，只問想在車上說話，還是先安靜。我選安靜。回家途中買了熱包子，我雙手捧著，直到手指不再冷。

At six, J wrote again. I heard you went to Lin. Good. Meet me tomorrow after school at the public library. Near the front desk. You can bring her. No empty room. No midnight roof. No secret place. The message felt different from every story I had made in my head. At the bottom was a photograph of two hands holding a badly folded paper bird.

六點，J 又寫信：聽說妳找了林老師，很好。明天放學在公立圖書館見，櫃台附近。可以帶她。不在空房間，不在午夜屋頂，也不是秘密地點。訊息跟我腦中的故事都不同，最底下是一張照片，兩隻手捧著摺得很糟的紙鳥。

I knew those hands. One had a small white mark by the thumb. June had burned it on a hot lunch box in seventh grade. Still, I sent the invitation to Ms Lin instead of going alone. She contacted June's mother to check it. An hour later, she called back. “June sent that invitation,” she said. “Her mother will come too, if both of you still want to meet.”

我認得那雙手，其中一隻拇指旁有小白痕，是 June 七年級被熱便當盒燙的。我仍把邀請傳林老師，沒有獨自赴約。她聯絡 June 的媽媽確認。一小時後來電：「邀請是 June 寄的。如果妳們都仍想見面，她媽媽也會來。」

I sat on the edge of my bed. June was not a ghost inside my phone. She was a girl who could choose a library table and ask her mother to sit nearby. That should have made things easier. Instead it made my throat hurt. A ghost could tell me I was forgiven and disappear. June could look at me and decide she did not want me back.

我坐在床沿。June 不是手機裡的鬼，她是能選圖書館座位、請媽媽坐附近的女孩。這本該讓事情容易，卻讓我喉嚨痛。鬼可以說原諒我，然後消失。June 可以看著我，決定不要我回去。

I wrote, I will come. Then, after a long minute, I added, You do not have to make me feel better. She read it. No answer came. I put my phone beside my English book. The twelve words from Monday were still there. This time I covered the Chinese meanings and tested myself. I remembered return. I also remembered choose. It was a small thing I could finish.

我寫：我會去。過了很久，又加：妳不必負責讓我好過。她已讀，沒回。我把手機放到英文課本旁。星期一的十二個字仍在，這次我蓋住中文測驗自己。我記得 return，也記得 choose。至少有件小事能做完。

At nine, Leo sent me a screenshot from the old film list. The computer teacher had found that the broken video was not the only full copy. Another had been sent to the school show laptop. Its file name was simple: Friday. Then a message arrived from June. Before we meet, there is one thing you should know. I sent the letters. But I did not write the first warning.

九點，Leo 傳來舊影片清單的截圖。資訊老師發現損壞的不是唯一完整版，另一份曾送到校慶用筆電。檔名很簡單：Friday。June 接著傳來訊息：見面前，妳得知道一件事。信是我寄的，但第一個警告不是我寫的。
