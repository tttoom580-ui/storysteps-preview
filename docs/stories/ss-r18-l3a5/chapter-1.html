The First Map

我在地下城賣地圖 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

Niko sells a cheap sketch to a lost porter and learns that the official chart leaves out the workers’ stairs. By sunrise, the rumor had traveled across Stonewell faster than any honest explanation. Niko found Sera beside the survey notebook, making a list of the things they had actually seen. There were fewer items on that list than on the rumor sheet. The difference mattered. A frightened person could mistake a guess for a promise, and a promise could send somebody toward a danger they had never agreed to face. The inspector called the risk acceptable, but Niko asked to see the safety conditions. Sera promised to advise the group only after reading the original instructions. A nervous worker rested a hand beneath his chin and studied the open notebook.

尼可把便宜的草圖賣給迷路的搬運工，發現官方地圖漏掉了工人階梯。 日出時，流言已在石井城傳得比任何誠實解釋都快。尼可看見莎拉守在測繪筆記旁，列出兩人真正親眼見到的事。那張清單比流言單上的項目少得多，而差別至關重要。害怕的人可能把猜測誤認成承諾，承諾又可能把某人帶進他從未同意面對的危險。 督察說風險可以接受，但尼可要求查看安全條件。莎拉答應讀過原始指示後才給團隊建議。一名緊張的工人把手托在下巴下，仔細看著打開的筆記。

The problem grew more complicated when Master Voss asked for a quick decision. Niko understood the attraction of a simple answer, but the guild monopoly had already made simple answers expensive for other people. Sera traced a line in the survey notebook and showed exactly where the available information ended. Neither of them wanted to make a public promise across that blank space. They agreed to measure one small fact before taking the next step. A traveler unpacked a coconut from the supply basket and shared it with the tired guards. Niko tried to connect the earlier warning with the mark beside the doorway. Sera called the order cruel because it would leave the weakest people behind.

沃斯會長要求迅速決定後，問題變得更複雜。尼可理解簡單答案的吸引力，但公會壟斷早已讓別人替這些簡單答案付出高昂代價。莎拉沿著測繪筆記畫出一道線，指出現有資訊到哪裡為止。兩人都不願跨過空白處作公開承諾，因此同意先測量一件小事再前進。 一名旅人從補給籃拿出椰子，分給疲倦的守衛。尼可試著把先前的警告和門邊的記號連起來。莎拉認為這命令殘酷，因為它會把最弱勢的人留在原地。

The first disagreement was small, but it revealed the difference between their habits. Niko tended to notice patterns; Sera noticed who was missing from the room. Together they looked at the survey notebook and named one question each. There was no need for either person to win the argument. A good plan would leave room for both questions and for the people whose answers might be different from theirs. At the map stall, a young miner counted coins twice before buying a route home. Niko remembered the man’s tired face whenever a rich buyer offered him exclusive rights. The medic wrote the correct dose on a card instead of trusting anyone to remember it. A slow drip of water came through the ceiling, marking the path of a hidden pipe. Niko felt a brief envy of people who seemed sure of their answers, then returned to work.

第一次分歧不大，卻顯露兩人的習慣差異。尼可擅長注意規律，莎拉則察覺誰沒有出現在房裡。他們一起查看測繪筆記，各自提出一個問題。爭論不必分出輸贏；好計畫應容納兩個問題，也容納答案可能不同的其他人。地圖攤前，年輕礦工數了兩次錢才買一條回家路。每當富有買家提出獨占權時，尼可便想起他的疲倦面容。 醫護人員把正確劑量寫在卡片上，不要求大家憑記憶用藥。緩慢滴落的水從天花板滲出，標示出隱藏水管的位置。尼可一度羨慕那些看似確定答案的人，隨後繼續工作。

No one cheered when their first attempt failed. Niko marked the failure in the survey notebook, and Sera asked whether the result could be repeated safely. They chose a smaller version of the task, with a clear signal to stop if anything went wrong. The second attempt produced a different sound and a different answer. They still needed another witness, but now they knew what question that witness should be asked. Sera measured the wall with a cord and marked where the chalk had moved. They agreed to explore only the passage that the safety team had already inspected. The weather turned extreme by noon, and the team moved its supplies under cover. Niko refused to gamble with the safety of someone who had not agreed to the risk.

第一次嘗試失敗時，沒有人歡呼。尼可把失敗記在測繪筆記裡，莎拉詢問是否能安全重做。他們選擇規模更小的測試，並約定出錯時立即停止的訊號。第二次嘗試出現不同的聲音與答案。雖然還需要另一名證人，但他們終於知道該問對方什麼。莎拉用繩索測量牆壁，標出粉筆記號移動的地方。 他們同意只探索安全小組檢查過的通道。到了中午天候變得極端惡劣，團隊把物資移到遮蔽處。尼可拒絕拿未同意冒險者的安全來賭。

Sera read the last line aloud. Niko checked the date and kept a copy for the morning team. One quick glance at the old date told Sera that the sheet was not the latest copy. Fresh hay lay in a corner of the stable beside a broken wooden gate.

莎拉朗讀最後一行。尼可核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 莎拉瞥一眼舊日期，就知道這張紙並非最新版本。馬廄一角堆著新鮮乾草，旁邊是一扇壞掉的木門。

A new message arrived before the meeting could end. It mentioned the guild monopoly, then claimed that only Master Voss had the authority to decide what happened next. Niko read it twice. The words sounded certain, but there was no date beside the claim and no name of the person who had checked it. Sera suggested saving a copy and asking for the original. The question was not whether to trust a powerful person. It was what anyone could verify. Water touched the edge of the underground steps. Sera held a string above the current and watched it point toward an unmarked tunnel. Niko drew only what they could verify. Someone had left clean laundry beside the emergency supplies, ready for people who might stay overnight. The apology sounded sincere, but Niko still expected the promised repair to be completed.

會議尚未結束，一則新訊息就送到了。訊息提及公會壟斷，並宣稱只有沃斯會長有權決定下一步。尼可讀了兩遍。文字很肯定，卻沒有日期，也沒有查核者姓名。莎拉建議保留副本並索取原件。問題不是該不該相信有權勢的人，而是任何人能核實什麼。水碰到地下階梯邊緣。莎拉把細繩放在水流上方，見它指向未標記的隧道。尼可只畫能證實的部分。 有人把乾淨衣物放在緊急物資旁，供可能需要過夜的人使用。道歉聽來誠懇，但尼可仍期待承諾的修理真正完成。

Sera read the last line aloud. Niko checked the date and kept a copy for the morning team. Sera sat on a low stool to speak at eye level with a frightened child. A sign banned tobacco near the storage room because a spark could cause a fire.

莎拉朗讀最後一行。尼可核對日期，為早班小組留下副本。 莎拉坐在矮凳上，與受驚的孩子平視交談。儲藏室附近的標誌禁止使用菸草，因為火星可能造成火災。

Niko left the survey notebook open so Sera could add the next finding. Neither could see the whole road yet. A pair of twin lamps stood at the gate, but only one was still working. A guard gave a sharp whistle, the agreed signal for everybody to stop moving.

尼可讓測繪筆記保持打開，好讓莎拉補上新的發現。兩人都還看不見整段路。 大門有一對成雙的燈，卻只剩一盞還能發亮。守衛吹出尖銳口哨，那是大家約定停止移動的訊號。
