地板下的記號

Marks Under the Floor

雲線以上，請聽第三聲 · 第 3 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The station could not afford to lose another day, but Shan refused to rush across an unsafe floor. She put on an old apron from the workshop and used a clean bucket to catch water near the entrance. Noah helped cast a small beam of light through a crack. Dirt covered the wooden boards, and a sweet smell from yesterday's dessert still lingered in the staff kitchen. Under the boards lay three metal tags. Their numbers matched a list of valves that the newer map did not mention. Shan photographed their original positions before moving anything. What appeared to be a small repair had become a careful search for an omitted system.

氣象站承受不起再浪費一天，但珊不願匆忙踩上不安全的地板。她穿上工作間的舊圍裙，用乾淨水桶接住入口附近的水。諾亞協助把一道細光投進裂縫。木板上積著泥土，員工廚房仍飄著昨天甜點的香氣。木板下藏著三片金屬標牌，編號對應新版地圖未列出的閥門清單。珊在移動任何東西前，先拍下標牌原本的位置。看似小小的修理工作，已變成對被遺漏系統的審慎搜尋。

An energetic young volunteer named Ivo offered a dramatic explanation: perhaps Mei had discovered an underground room and had been taken away. Shan thanked him, but asked for the written entry record before considering that idea. On a faded sheet she found the word greenhouse, although the station had no working greenhouse now. An inspector had visited the old plant room two years earlier. His report mentioned a damaged electrical plug, a water pit, and an ongoing plan to close the passage. None of those details sounded supernatural. They suggested that Mei might have entered a dangerous area while trying to check a real problem.

一位精力充沛的年輕志工伊沃提出戲劇性的說法：或許梅發現地下密室，然後被人帶走。珊道謝，卻要求先查書面的出入紀錄。她在褪色表單上看見「溫室」一詞，儘管站內已沒有運作中的溫室。兩年前一位檢查員曾到訪舊植物室，報告記載損壞的插頭、積水坑，以及一項仍在進行的封閉通道計畫。這些細節一點也不超自然，反而顯示梅可能是為了檢查真正的問題，才進入危險區域。

In a locked cabinet they found a lollipop wrapper, a drawing of an ox, and a cartoon penguin pasted on an emergency kit. The objects looked childish, yet they belonged to a senior safety teacher who had used pictures to help volunteers remember equipment locations. A small label marked one compartment “spy mirror,” meaning a simple mirror for seeing behind pipes, not a secret agent's device. Shan held the mirror under the boards. A narrow metal handle appeared beyond the crack. They could not reach it safely, but the mirror confirmed that the floor covered something larger than an old cable. Noah drew the position with a measuring tape.

鎖住的櫃子裡有棒棒糖包裝紙、一張公牛圖，以及貼在急救箱上的卡通企鵝。這些物品看似幼稚，其實是資深安全教師用圖片協助志工記住設備位置的教具。一個小標籤寫著「偵查鏡」，指的是用來看管線背面的簡單鏡子，而不是間諜裝置。珊把鏡子伸到木板下，看見裂縫另一頭有個狹窄的金屬把手。他們無法安全碰到，但鏡子證實地板下不只是舊電纜。諾亞用捲尺記下位置。

The inspection report carried three signatures. Two belonged to workers who still lived nearby. The third was covered by a black stamp, apparently added when the report was copied. Shan asked the office clerk for the original rather than trying to guess the name. The clerk said it had been sent to the district archive. She provided a tracking number, and Shan recorded it on the same page as her photographs. The evidence had begun to form a path: an old plant room, a water system below it, and an inspection whose final action was missing. The next step was to learn what had happened after the warning.

檢查報告上有三個簽名。兩個屬於仍住在附近的工人，第三個被黑色印章蓋住，似乎是在影印時加上的。珊沒有猜名字，而是請職員提供原件。職員說原件已送區域檔案室，並給了追蹤號碼。珊把號碼記在照片同一頁。證據開始形成一條線：舊植物室、它下方的水系統，以及一份缺少後續處置的檢查報告。下一步是查出警告提出後究竟發生了什麼。

That evening, a voice came from the maintenance corridor. It spoke Mei's name twice and called for help. Ivo rushed toward it, but Noah stopped him at the marked boundary. Shan requested the three-note call sign on a closed radio channel. The voice repeated its plea without responding to her question. When Noah shut off a nearby ventilation motor, the sound stopped as well. They did not know whether the motor carried the voice or merely disturbed its signal. Shan wrote both possibilities down. She would not turn an observation into a conclusion simply because one explanation felt more exciting than the other.

當晚，維修走廊傳來一個聲音，連喊兩次梅的名字，還呼救。伊沃朝聲音奔去，諾亞在標示邊界前攔住他。珊在封閉頻道要求對方發出三音呼號。聲音只反覆求救，沒有回答問題。諾亞關掉附近的通風馬達後，聲音也停了。他們尚不知道馬達是在傳送聲音，還是干擾訊號。珊把兩種可能都記下。她不願只因某個解釋較刺激，就把觀察直接當成結論。

Later the weather report predicted heavy snow near the upper ridge. Vale wanted the search suspended until spring. Shan agreed that no one should enter the exposed passage that night, but she asked him to keep the logs and valve plans available for indoor review. The director objected that technical work would confuse the volunteers. Noah offered a simple division of duties: trained staff would handle equipment, while volunteers could sort copies in a warm room. Shan accepted. Protecting people from physical danger did not require hiding information from them. As the station lights dimmed, they began a list of questions that the documents might answer without another risky step outside.

後來氣象報告預測上方山脊將降大雪。維爾要求把搜尋暫停到春天。珊同意當晚不應有人進入暴露的通道，卻要求保留紀錄與閥門圖供室內查閱。站長反對，說技術資料會讓志工混亂。諾亞提出簡單分工：受訓員工負責設備，志工在暖室整理副本。珊接受了。保護人免於實際危險，不代表需要對他們隱瞞資訊。隨著站內燈光轉暗，他們開始列出一些不用再冒險出門、只靠文件就可能回答的問題。

The dark ink on the old greenhouse form had begun to fade, so Shan asked the archivist to scan it before touching the paper again. She marked the image with its date and storage location. The original remained in its folder, available for another reader to examine without relying on her interpretation.

舊溫室表單上的深色墨跡已開始褪色，珊請檔案員先掃描，再碰原紙。她替影像標上日期與存放位置，原件則留在資料夾裡，讓其他人不必依賴她的解讀，也能再檢查。
