提早響起的鐘

The Bell That Rang Early

雲線以上，請聽第三聲 · 第 4 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

Shan's ambition was not to solve a ghost story; it was to make the station safe enough for people to return home. At dawn she visited an old barn used for storing spare cables. A rescue dog's collar hung beside a poster advertising a winter concert. The mixture confused Ivo, until Noah explained that the barn had served several community groups over the years. A dim lamp glowed above a frozen water pipe. Shan checked whether the pipe would freeze deeper overnight, then stepped back from a damaged joint. A bell sounded from the station at 7:45, fifteen minutes before the time printed on the official maintenance chart.

珊的抱負不是解開鬼故事，而是讓氣象站安全到足以讓大家回家。天亮時，她去一座用來放備用電纜的舊穀倉。救援犬的項圈掛在宣傳冬季音樂會的海報旁。這種混搭讓伊沃困惑，諾亞解釋穀倉多年來曾供不同社區團體使用。昏暗的燈照著一根結冰水管。珊確認水管夜裡是否會凍得更深，接著離開受損接頭。七點四十五分，站內鐘聲響起，比官方維修表所列時間早了十五分鐘。

A junior technician tried to hide his worry with humor. He said even a novelist could write a better schedule than the station's staff. A visiting painter showed Shan a drawing of the tower, which included a precious detail: two clocks stood on opposite sides of the stairwell. The painter had added them because the station's response to storms depended on which clock workers watched. One clock controlled an automatic bell; the other appeared only on the public screen. Shan remembered that the strange calls occurred shortly after 2:17 on the public display. If the two clocks disagreed, witness times might be accurate to different systems.

一名初階技師用幽默掩飾擔心，說連小說家都能寫出比本站員工更好的時程。一位來訪畫家拿出塔樓素描，上面有個珍貴細節：樓梯兩側各有一只鐘。畫家特別畫下它們，是因為站內遇到風暴時的應變，取決於工人看的是哪只鐘。其一控制自動鐘聲，另一只只顯示在公開螢幕。珊想到怪聲總在公開時鐘顯示兩點十七分後不久出現。若兩只鐘不一致，證人的時間可能各自對應不同系統。

In the staff kitchen, someone prepared roast vegetables with onion while a child watched a squirrel outside. A garden worker used a spray bottle to clean the window, then showed Shan a wall tap that had once supplied emergency water. The worker was a traveler in her younger days and had visited several mountain stations. Her vision of a safe station was simple: every warning should mean the same thing to every worker. She said the old bell had been moved to digital control last winter, but the notice on the wall had not been replaced. Shan asked her to describe only what she had seen, not what she believed the director intended.

員工廚房裡，有人用洋蔥烤蔬菜，一個孩子則看著窗外的松鼠。園藝員用噴瓶清潔玻璃，接著指給珊看一個曾供應緊急用水的牆上水龍頭。她年輕時是位旅行者，去過多座山中氣象站。她心中的安全願景很簡單：同一個警告對每位員工都應有相同意義。她說去年冬天舊鐘改成數位控制，但牆上的告示沒有更換。珊請她只描述親眼所見，不猜測站長有何意圖。

Noah opened the clock cabinet with permission from the office. The public clock had a correction setting, adjusted by hand after a software update. Its log showed that someone had moved it forward eleven minutes on the night Mei vanished. The mechanical bell, however, kept its own time. Shan did not call the change proof of sabotage. A technician might have been correcting an ordinary error. She saved a copy of the setting history, asked who held the access key, and compared it with the station's written procedures. The gap between the two clocks now had a measurable size and a documented starting point.

諾亞取得辦公室許可後，打開時鐘控制櫃。公開時鐘有一個校正設定，軟體更新後由人工調整。紀錄顯示梅失蹤當夜，有人把時間往前調了十一分鐘。機械鐘卻仍走自己的時間。珊沒有把調整直接稱為破壞，技師可能只是修正普通誤差。她存下設定紀錄副本，詢問誰持有門鑰匙，再和站內書面程序比較。兩只鐘之間的差距，現在有了可測量的大小與有紀錄的起點。

The sound system offered another clue. When the public clock reached a set time, it played a safety recording to an old speaker near the maintenance corridor. Shan and Noah tested the speaker while the corridor was closed and no one stood inside it. The recording carried the same small cough as the voice they had heard the night before. Yet the file name was “Training Call Four,” not “Mei Emergency.” Someone had changed the schedule, but perhaps not the sound file itself. Shan labeled the finding carefully. It explained the repeated voice without explaining why Mei was still missing.

廣播系統提供另一條線索。公開時鐘到指定時間時，系統會把安全錄音送往維修走廊旁的舊喇叭。珊與諾亞在走廊封閉、裡面無人的情況下測試。錄音裡有和前一晚完全相同的輕咳，檔名卻是「訓練呼叫四」，不是「梅的緊急呼救」。有人改了播放時程，但不一定更動過聲音檔本身。珊小心標註發現：它解釋了反覆的人聲，還不能解釋梅為何失蹤。

By nightfall, the three rules began to look different. The radio warning protected people from mistaking a recording for a live guide. The clock discrepancy could make a recorded call arrive when staff expected an emergency message. The red antenna rule still had no explanation, and the south channel was still missing from official maps. Shan wrote the known facts on separate cards and left spaces between them. It would be tempting to draw one long line that connected everything, but a good investigation could survive unanswered questions. A bad one would force every new fact to fit its first story.

夜色降下時，三條規則開始有了不同面貌。無線電警告是為了防止人把錄音誤認成即時引導。時鐘差異又可能讓錄音在員工預期收到緊急訊息時播出。紅色天線規則尚無解釋，南側水道也仍不見於正式地圖。珊把已知事實寫在不同卡片上，卡片之間留著空白。把所有事畫成一條長線很誘人，但好的調查能容忍未解問題；壞的調查則會強迫每項新事實符合最初的故事。

Shan asked the staff not to confuse the mechanical bell with the public clock. She prepared two short labels, each identifying its purpose, its owner, and the latest test date. The labels made an ordinary difference visible, so a future night worker would not have to discover it during a storm.

珊請員工不要把機械鐘聲和公開時鐘混淆。她做了兩張短標籤，分別寫清用途、負責人與最近測試日期。標籤讓普通差異變得看得見，未來夜班員工就不必在風暴中才發現。
