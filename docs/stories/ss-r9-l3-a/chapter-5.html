空著的獎牌櫃

An Empty Medal Case

雲線以上，請聽第三聲 · 第 5 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

The station once hosted a mountain athlete program that offered a real benefit to local schools. Its walls still displayed pictures of young climbers considering a career in science. Shan examined a dusty medal case and found a loose panel behind it. Under the panel lay a clue written in a narrow hand: “Do not drag the lower gate against water pressure.” Ivo was eager to pull the board out, but Shan stopped him. The note might belong to a worker who had since left; removing the panel too roughly could erase tool marks that showed when it was last opened. They recorded the location before asking the caretaker for permission to continue.

氣象站過去辦過山地運動員計畫，讓地方學校真正受益。牆上還掛著年輕登山者考慮以科學為職涯的照片。珊檢查積灰的獎牌櫃，發現後面有塊鬆動木板。板下藏著一條細字線索：「不要頂著水壓拖動下方閘門。」伊沃急著拉開木板，珊阻止了他。便條可能出自已離職的工人；硬拆木板可能抹掉顯示上次開啟時間的工具痕跡。他們先記下位置，才請管理員同意繼續檢查。

On a shelf nearby were a bag of flour, a photograph of a harbor, and a child's drawing of a knight. The harbor was far from the mountains, but the photograph showed the same type of manual lock used on older water gates. Shan found its maker's name in a technical catalog. The company had paved access roads around dams, although the station's route was now rough and mostly abandoned. Another page mentioned a scientific study comparing gate pressure with temperature. A trader had donated the catalog after a journey through several communities, including a mountain tribe. Shan did not assume the donor knew about Mei. The material was useful because its diagrams could be independently checked.

附近架上有一袋麵粉、一張港口照片，以及孩子畫的騎士。港口離高山很遠，但照片裡有和舊水閘相同型式的手動鎖。珊在技術型錄中找到製造商名稱。那家公司曾替水壩周邊鋪路，氣象站的路線如今卻崎嶇且大半廢棄。另一頁提到一項比較閘門水壓與溫度的科學研究。一位商人走訪數個社區、包括某山地部落後，捐贈了型錄。珊並不因此推測捐贈者認識梅。這些資料有用，是因為圖示可以被獨立核對。

The engineer's manual listed a safe water volume for each gate position. Its measurements suggested that the lower passage could fill rapidly if snowmelt entered from above. Noah checked the chest strap of his safety harness and followed the approved route to an indoor inspection window. Through the glass he saw standing water behind a metal gate. The upper tank gauge showed a drop, exactly as Mei's note had predicted. Nothing was moving quickly yet. Shan asked him to return without opening the gate. It was not enough to know where Mei might have gone; they needed a safe way to reach her, if she was still on the other side.

工程手冊列出了閘門每個位置的安全水量。數據暗示若上方融雪流入，下層通道可能很快積滿水。諾亞檢查安全背帶的胸前扣環，沿核准路線走到室內觀測窗。他隔著玻璃看見金屬閘門後方的積水。上方水槽水位計正在下降，正如梅的便條所預測。此時水流尚未快速移動，珊請他不要開閘，先返回。知道梅可能去了哪裡還不夠；如果她還在另一側，他們需要安全抵達的方法。

Vale finally acknowledged that the lower gate had been locked after an accident years ago. He said the budget had never covered a full repair. Shan asked for the accident report, and he promised to find it. His admission changed the case but did not settle it. An old locked passage could explain why staff avoided the area. It did not explain why the map erased the route, why the upper gauge was dropping, or why Mei left an urgent note. Shan sent a request to the district engineer for advice on the water system. She also asked the rescue office to prepare, without sending anyone into the passage before a pressure test.

維爾終於承認，下層閘門在多年前一場事故後就鎖上了。他說預算一直不足以完整修復。珊要求事故報告，他答應尋找。這項承認改變了案情，卻沒有解答一切。舊通道上鎖或許能解釋為何員工避開那裡，卻無法解釋地圖為何刪掉路線、上方水位為何下降，以及梅為何留下緊急便條。珊向區域工程師發出水系統諮詢申請，也通知救援辦公室先行準備，但在水壓測試前不要派人進通道。

That evening Ivo found Mei's last notebook page inside the medal case. It contained a hand-drawn triangle between the south channel, the tank, and the lower passage. At each corner was a time, not a person's name. Shan compared the times with both station clocks and found that one matched the mechanical bell exactly. Mei had been using the older clock to measure a change in water level. “She was doing a test,” Noah said. Shan answered, “Perhaps. We still do not know whether it was authorized.” They filed the page with the others and marked one question for the morning: who had asked Mei to check a closed system?

當晚，伊沃在獎牌櫃裡找到梅筆記的最後一頁。紙上畫了個三角形，連接南側水道、水槽與下方通道。三個角寫著時間，而不是姓名。珊把時間和站內兩只鐘核對，發現其中一個完全符合機械鐘。梅當時似乎用舊鐘測量水位變化。「她在做測試，」諾亞說。珊答道：「也許，但我們還不知道測試是否獲准。」他們把紙頁歸檔，留下隔天要追查的問題：是誰請梅去檢查一套已封閉的系統？

Near midnight, the antenna lamp flashed red. Shan and Noah stepped away from the metal terrace and remained inside, following the first rule without trying to test it recklessly. A sharp crack followed in the clouds. Their instruments registered a sudden electrical surge. They knew now that the red light warned of a real hazard, even though no one had explained its circuit. Shan wrote that finding beside the earlier radio test. Two rules already pointed toward ordinary causes. The third remained dangerous because it involved moving water and a missing person. She slept only after confirming that the rescue team had received her latest report.

午夜將近，天線燈閃起紅光。珊與諾亞照第一條規則遠離金屬平台，留在室內，沒有冒險拿自己做測試。雲裡隨後傳來尖銳巨響，儀器記下突然的電流突波。即使還不清楚電路細節，他們已知道紅燈警告的是實際危險。珊把發現記在先前的無線電測試旁邊。三條規則裡，已有兩條指向普通原因；第三條涉及流動的水與失蹤者，仍然危險。她確認救援隊收到最新報告後，才終於去睡。

The old report described a horrible moment when water had pushed against the gate, but it did not explain the cause. A proposal to pave the service route had been attached to it without approval. Shan separated those facts in her notes: a frightening event had happened, while the proposed road work had never been formally accepted.

舊報告描述水撞向閘門的可怕一刻，卻沒有解釋原因。報告還附了鋪設服務道路的提議，但沒有核准。珊在筆記中分開這些事實：可怕事件確實發生，擬議鋪路工程卻未正式獲准。
