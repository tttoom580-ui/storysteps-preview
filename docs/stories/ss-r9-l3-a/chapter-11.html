What the Receiver Could Hear

雲線以上，請聽第三聲 · 第 11 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A bloody scrape on a rescuer's glove was a warning about the sharp edges around Gate Seven, not evidence of an attack. A cold breeze slipped through the service opening. Shan gave the team credit for stopping before anyone entered, even though the pause felt unbearable. One volunteer called the missing worker a victim of a criminal scheme, while another became emotional and spoke of bad fortune. Shan understood their worry, but she could not let uncertain accusations decide where people walked. She checked the pressure reading, the planned exit, and the amount of food that might remain in the chamber. Rescuers needed facts that could guide their hands, not a label for someone to hate.

救援員手套上一道帶血擦傷提醒大家，七號閘門附近有尖銳邊緣，並非有人攻擊的證據。冷風吹進服務開口。即使暫停令人難受，珊仍肯定團隊在有人入內前就先停下。一位志工稱失蹤工人是犯罪陰謀的受害者，另一位情緒激動地談起厄運。珊理解他們的擔憂，卻不能讓尚未證實的指控決定大家走哪條路。她檢查壓力讀數、預定出口，以及隔間內可能剩下的食物量。救援員需要能指引動作的事實，而不是找一個可以憎恨的人。

In the pantry, the cook placed a loaf of bread in a parcel with pepper soup and dry fruit. An old dog began to lick the wrapping, and the cook gently moved it aside. The rescue coordinator approved only sealed supplies that could travel through the narrow opening. Noah measured the pitch of the metal floor and saw it rose slightly toward the inner chamber. That slope could keep one room dry even when the lower passage flooded. Shan pretended nothing was certain when she spoke to the press, because uncertainty was real, but she refused to pretend that the evidence told them nothing. The chamber's shape was now useful.

廚房裡，廚師把一條麵包、胡椒湯與乾果放進包裹。老狗開始舔包裝，廚師輕輕把牠移開。救援協調員只准使用能穿過狹窄開口的密封物資。諾亞量了金屬地板的傾斜角度，發現它向內側隔間略微升高。這道坡度可能讓一間房在下方通道淹水時仍保持乾燥。珊對媒體不裝作一切確定，因為不確定是真實存在的；她也不假裝證據什麼都沒告訴大家。隔間形狀已經提供有用資訊。

A worker tried to resist the order to retreat after his sleeve caught on a pipe. He was slender enough to fit the gap, but that did not make the passage safe. Shan explained that a sudden twist in the old gate could trap anyone beside it. A workers' union representative supported the stop-work decision and asked for a written account. Noah held the worker's wrist to steady him as he backed away, then let go once they reached firm ground. The team updated the map with the snag point. A good rescue plan had to describe not only the route people hoped to take but also every reason to turn around.

一位工人袖子勾住水管後，仍想抗拒撤退命令。他身材纖細，能穿過縫隙，卻不代表通道安全。珊解釋舊閘門若突然扭轉，可能夾住附近的人。工會代表支持停工決定，要求留下書面紀錄。諾亞握住工人手腕，協助他慢慢退回，站上穩固地面後才放手。團隊在地圖標記了勾住衣物的位置。好的救援計畫不只要描述希望走的路，也要寫明所有必須折返的理由。

The radio contact occurred again at noon. Shan asked a single yes-or-no question using the official tapping code: “Do you have food?” Three taps answered yes. “Do you have a light?” Three more taps. Then she asked whether water was entering the room. One tap meant no. These replies were encouraging but not final proof of Mei's identity. The team checked whether an automatic machine could produce them at those times. Its schedule showed no such messages, and the intervals varied naturally. That made live contact increasingly likely. Shan told the family precisely what was supported and what still needed confirmation.

中午無線電又有聯繫。珊用官方敲擊代碼一次只問一個能回答是非的問題：「你有食物嗎？」三下表示有。「你有燈嗎？」又是三下。接著她問水是否流進房間，一下表示沒有。這些回答令人鼓舞，卻還不能最終證實身分。團隊檢查自動機器是否能在那些時間送出訊息；排程沒有這類訊息，敲擊間隔也自然變化。因此即時聯繫越來越可信。珊把已支持的事實與仍待確認的地方清楚告訴家屬。

Sam proposed sending a narrow camera through a dry inspection tube near Gate Seven. It would not require opening the main door. The team tested the camera in a safe room first, checking its light, cable, and recording time. During the trial it lost power twice, so they replaced the battery rather than hoping it would work underground. Shan asked the crew to keep one person responsible for each measurement and another for recording it. The task sounded dull compared with climbing into darkness, but the safer method might reveal whether the chamber was occupied before anyone risked entry. All hands agreed to the test in writing.

山姆提議把細小攝影機從七號閘門旁的乾燥檢查管送入，不必打開主門。團隊先在安全室測試攝影機，確認燈光、纜線與錄影時間。試驗時它兩次斷電，他們換電池，沒有賭它在地下會自己正常工作。珊要求每項測量都由一人負責，另一人負責紀錄。與攀入黑暗相比，這工作聽來無聊，但較安全的方法也許能在任何人冒險入內前，確認隔間是否有人。全員以書面同意測試。

Hunger was one of the risks that worried the medic, although the camera showed a supply box inside the chamber. Shan asked the pantry to prepare a second sealed food packet and have it weighed before delivery. The medic checked ingredients against the emergency health notes, then approved a small portion suitable for someone who had eaten very little. Noah tested the delivery tube without sending the real parcel first. When Mei later signaled that she had food, the team noted the relief but kept their timetable cautious. A positive reply did not remove the need to plan for exhaustion, cold, and an injured ankle.

飢餓是醫護擔心的風險之一，即使攝影機拍到隔間裡有補給箱。珊請廚房準備第二包密封食物，送入前先秤重。醫護依緊急健康紀錄核對成分，核准少量適合久未正常進食者的份量。諾亞先以測試物通過輸送管，沒有直接送真正食物。梅後來發訊表示已收到食物，團隊感到放心，卻仍謹慎維持時間表。肯定回答不能消除疲憊、寒冷與腳踝受傷等風險。
