山中旅店的信

Letters from the Mountain Inn

雲線以上，請聽第三聲 · 第 6 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Shan hoped to achieve a reliable account of the old water system before the storm arrived. Noah took her to an inn below the station, where a former engineer had left a box of records. A faded menu offered a fruit cocktail and bread with jelly, but they came to read, not eat. The innkeeper called the old pressure gate a devil of a machine, then admitted his description might be doubtful after so many years. The box contained a warning that workers must never dump snow into the drainage entrance. At first Shan thought it an odd rule, until she saw a diagram showing how loose snow could block a narrow channel.

珊希望在風暴抵達前，取得可靠的舊水系統紀錄。諾亞帶她到氣象站下方的旅店，前工程師在那裡留了一箱資料。褪色菜單上有水果雞尾酒和果醬麵包，但兩人是來讀資料，不是用餐。店主把舊壓力閘門稱為「惡魔般的機器」，接著承認多年過去，這個說法可能不太確定。箱裡有一項警告：工人絕不能把積雪倒進排水入口。珊一開始覺得規定古怪，看到圖解才知道鬆雪可能堵住狹窄水道。

A newspaper clipping showed the governor visiting the station ten years earlier. In the picture, a young technician stood beside a pile of spare parts and a measuring pole. His badge showed a low rank, but the article praised his careful work as superior to the hurried plans of his bosses. That technician was now the district engineer, Sam. Shan called him and asked whether the gate had ever been tested after the visit. He said the earliest test results had been lost during an office move. He did, however, remember a letter about the south trail. He promised to look for it, not to guess what it said.

一張報紙剪報顯示十年前州長來過氣象站。照片裡，年輕技師站在一堆備件與測量桿旁。他的職級不高，報導卻稱讚他做事仔細，品質勝過上司倉促的計畫。那名技師如今是區域工程師山姆。珊打電話問他來訪後是否測試過閘門。他說最早的測試結果在辦公室搬遷時遺失了，但記得一封提到南側小徑的信。他答應尋找，不會憑記憶猜內容。

The innkeeper's oldest pal, a retired barber, arrived carrying a small razor case. He knew a family from the suburb that had helped maintain the mountain trail. The family had organized a unity walk years before, and a couple had even chosen to wed beside the observation tower. Photographs from that day showed the lower channel uncovered, with a hand wheel on its outside wall. Shan asked the barber for permission to copy only the photographs relevant to the building. He agreed, but wanted the couple's names kept private. The pictures established that an outside control existed; they did not show whether it still worked.

店主最老的朋友、一位退休理髮師帶著小刮鬍刀盒走進來。他認識來自郊區、曾協助維護山道的一家人。那家人多年前組織團結健行，有對新人甚至選在觀測塔旁結婚。當天照片顯示下方水道還沒被覆蓋，外牆上有一個手輪。珊徵求許可，只複製與建築相關的照片。理髮師同意，卻要求保護新人姓名。照片證明外部控制裝置曾存在，卻不能證明它現在還能運作。

Sam called back in the afternoon. He had found a carbon copy of a letter sent to Vale's office five months earlier. It recommended checking the lower gate before a new tourism path opened, because a pressure sensor had returned an uncertain reading. Someone in the station office had signed for the letter, but the signature did not belong to Vale. Shan photographed the receipt and asked for the mail register. She would not accuse anyone simply because a warning had gone unanswered. Still, the letter made one fact plain: concern about the lower system had existed before Mei disappeared. It could not be dismissed as a story invented by frightened volunteers.

下午山姆回電，說找到五個月前寄給維爾辦公室的信件複寫本。信中建議在新觀光步道開放前檢查下方閘門，因為壓力感測器讀數不確定。氣象站有人簽收，簽名卻不是維爾。珊拍下收據，要求查閱收發登記。她不會只因警告沒有得到回應就指控任何人。不過這封信清楚說明，下層系統的疑慮早在梅失蹤前就已存在，不能說成志工害怕後臨時編出的故事。

That night Shan compared the photographs with Mei's triangle drawing. The hand wheel was on the south wall, not inside the flooded passage. If engineers could test it safely from the outside, they might reduce pressure before opening the door. Noah warned that an old picture was not enough to authorize an operation. Shan agreed. She requested a remote inspection by the district team, with a written plan for stopping if the first measurement proved unsafe. The innkeeper brought warm tea and left them alone. Progress did not look like a dramatic rescue. It looked like a clear question, a responsible plan, and the people willing to check it.

那夜，珊把照片與梅畫的三角圖比較。手輪位於南牆，不在已積水的通道裡。如果工程師能從外側安全測試，或許能在開門前減少水壓。諾亞提醒，一張舊照片不足以授權操作。珊同意，要求區域團隊進行遠端檢查，並先寫好若首次測量不安全就停止的方案。店主送來熱茶後讓他們繼續工作。進展並不像戲劇性的救援，而是一個清楚的問題、一份負責的計畫，以及願意核實的人。

Before dawn, a small signal appeared on the station's old emergency channel. It was not a voice. Three tones sounded in the correct order, then stopped. The station's automatic recording system noted that the signal came from somewhere below the main building. Shan did not answer at once. She asked Noah to compare it with the training file and sent the result to Sam. If the tones were real, someone might know the procedure and still be alive. If they were another recording, the signal could be a trap. Either way, the exact time and direction mattered more than a frightening guess about who had sent it.

天亮前，氣象站舊緊急頻道出現一個小訊號。不是人聲，而是依正確順序響起三個音，隨後停止。自動錄音系統記下訊號來自主建築下方某處。珊沒有立刻回答，而是請諾亞與訓練檔比對，並將結果送給山姆。如果是真實訊號，或許有人懂得程序、仍活著；如果又是錄音，就可能是陷阱。無論如何，確切時間與方向，都比猜測發訊者是誰更重要。

A desirable outcome would be a safe rescue and a record anyone could verify, Shan told Noah. She was not seeking a fast victory over Vale. She wanted the missing warning traced to its actual desk and the gate tested by people qualified to judge it, even if the result delayed the mountain's reopening.

珊對諾亞說，理想結果是安全救援，以及任何人都能核對的紀錄。她不是想迅速打敗維爾，而是要追出那封警告實際停在哪張桌上，讓合格人員測試閘門，即使因此延後山區開放也一樣。
