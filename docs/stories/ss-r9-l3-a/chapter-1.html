雲層上的呼叫

The Call Above the Clouds

雲線以上，請聽第三聲 · 第 1 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照

At twenty-eight, Shan Chen had learned that a broken instrument could tell a more honest story than a confident person. She arrived at North Ridge Weather Station with a repair order marked acceptable, though no one had explained why a junior observer named Mei had disappeared. Her colleague Noah offered to advise her on the mountain path. At the entrance, an old guard rested his chin on his hand beside a carton of coconut milk. Shan tried to connect her radio to the station network, but received only a short tone. The guard looked away when she asked about Mei. That silence was her first reason to doubt the official report.

二十八歲的陳珊早已明白，壞掉的儀器有時比自信滿滿的人更誠實。她拿著一張標示「可以接受」的維修單來到北嶺氣象站，卻沒有人解釋年輕觀測員梅為何失蹤。同事諾亞主動提出要向她說明山路。入口的老警衛托著下巴，桌上放著一盒椰奶。珊試著把無線電連上站內網路，得到的卻只有一聲短音。她問起梅時，警衛移開視線。這份沉默是她第一次懷疑官方報告的原因。

The director, Vale, called the search a cruel waste of time. He said Mei had probably gone downhill without permission. A first-aid card listed the correct dose of mountain medicine, while a slow drip from the ceiling fell onto its plastic cover. Shan did not envy Vale's calm expression. She wanted to explore the equipment room before deciding anything. There she found a torn note in Mei's handwriting: “Check the upper tank when the snow begins to melt.” The paper was damp, but its ink remained readable. If Mei had simply walked away, why had she hidden a warning inside a broken instrument case?

站長維爾把搜尋稱為殘酷的時間浪費，說梅大概沒獲准就自行下山了。急救卡列著高山藥物的正確劑量，天花板的水卻一滴滴落在卡片的塑膠封面上。珊不羨慕維爾平靜的神情；她想先探索設備室，再做判斷。她在那裡找到一張梅手寫的破紙：「雪開始融時，檢查上方水槽。」紙已受潮，字跡仍可辨識。如果梅只是自行離開，為何要把警告藏在故障儀器盒中？

Outside, the forecast warned of extreme winds after midnight. Vale suggested that anyone who climbed toward the antenna would gamble with their life. Shan took one glance at the western slope, where old hay had been placed under a covered supply box to keep tools dry. A laundry line rattled in the wind, although no clothes hung from it. Noah noticed that the line pointed toward a disused path. They photographed the ground without following it. Shan insisted that a frightening weather forecast was not permission to stop thinking. They needed to identify what was dangerous, where the danger began, and which paths remained safe.

外頭的預報警告午夜後會有極端強風。維爾說，任何爬向天線的人都是拿生命下注。珊瞥向西坡，看見有人把舊乾草墊在有蓋的補給箱下，以保持工具乾燥。一條晾衣繩被風吹得作響，上面卻沒有衣物。諾亞注意到繩索正指向一條廢棄小徑。兩人先拍照，沒有貿然前進。珊堅持，令人害怕的天氣預報並不是停止思考的藉口。他們需要確認危險是什麼、從哪裡開始，以及哪些路仍可安全通行。

The guard finally gave a sincere account. He had been sitting on a low stool beside an empty tobacco tin when he heard two people speak over the emergency radio. One voice sounded like Mei's twin sister, who lived far below the mountain; the other ended with a sharp whistle. Shan asked whether he had seen either person. He had not. Instead of guessing, she recorded his exact words and checked the radio log. It listed only one call, made at 2:17 in the morning. In the margin someone had drawn three short strokes. The guard admitted that he had forgotten those marks when he spoke to Vale.

警衛終於坦誠敘述。他當時坐在矮凳上，身旁是一個空菸草罐，忽然聽見緊急無線電裡有兩個人說話。一個聲音像梅住在山下的雙胞胎姊妹，另一個則以尖銳哨聲結尾。珊問他是否看見兩人，他說沒有。珊沒有猜測，而是記下他的原話，再核對通訊紀錄。紀錄只列出凌晨兩點十七分的一通呼叫，旁邊有人畫了三道短線。警衛承認，向維爾報告時忘了提到那些記號。

On a board near the stairs were three rules printed in black. First: when the red antenna lamp flashes, leave the metal terrace. Second: answer no radio voice until it gives the three-note call sign. Third: if the upper water gauge drops while the snow is melting, open the south channel before entering the lower passage. These sounded like ordinary safety instructions, but the station's newer map showed no south channel. Shan copied the board and the map separately. An explanation that mixed them together too early might hide the very difference she needed to understand. Noah agreed to help her compare the two sources in daylight.

樓梯旁的告示板印著三條黑字規則。第一：紅色天線燈閃爍時，離開金屬平台。第二：無線電裡的聲音沒有先發出三音呼號，就不要回答。第三：雪正在融化且上方水位計下降時，進入下層通道前先打開南側水道。這些聽起來像普通安全規定，但新版地圖上根本沒有南側水道。珊分別抄下告示與地圖。她知道，太早把兩份資料混成一個解釋，可能會遮住最重要的差異。諾亞同意白天幫她核對兩個來源。

Before sleeping, Shan tested the radio with a simple request for identification. A woman's voice said, “Shan, please come to the lower room.” It did not give the three notes. She pressed the record button but kept the microphone silent. A second later the same sentence returned, with exactly the same small cough before her name. A person could repeat words; identical background noise was harder to explain. Shan wrote down the time and played nothing back over the open channel. She closed the door, looked once more at Mei's warning, and understood that the station's mystery began with a question of trust, not a ghost.

睡前，珊用簡單的身分確認訊息測試無線電。一個女子的聲音說：「珊，請到下層房間來。」卻沒有三音呼號。珊按下錄音鍵，始終沒有打開麥克風。一秒後，同一句話又傳來，連叫她名字前那聲輕咳都一模一樣。人可以重複話語，但完全相同的背景雜音就比較難解釋。珊記下時間，沒有在公開頻道播放錄音。她關上門，再看一眼梅的警告，明白氣象站的謎團始於「該相信誰」，而不是鬼魂。
