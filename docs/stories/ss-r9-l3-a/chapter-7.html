塔上的紅燈

Red Light on the Tower

The morning inspection began with a test of the automatic antenna alarm. An old automobile had once carried supplies to the tower, and its metal frame still stood beside the workshop. Ivo wanted to clothe the frame in a bright cover so visitors would notice it, but the safety committee refused to let him work outside during a warning. Several years earlier a worker had nearly drowned in the lower passage after ignoring the tank gauge. A child in the staff house offered the team dumpling soup while water ran from a faulty faucet. Shan thanked the child and reported the leak, careful not to turn ordinary kindness into a distraction from danger.

上午的檢查先測試自動天線警報。一輛舊汽車曾把補給品送上塔樓，如今金屬車架仍放在工作間旁。伊沃想替車架穿上亮色罩布，讓訪客看見，但安全委員會不准他在警報期間到室外工作。幾年前有位工人忽略水位計，差點在下層通道溺水。員工宿舍裡的孩子端來餃子湯，身旁故障水龍頭還在流水。珊向孩子道謝，也回報漏水問題，不讓日常善意分散大家對危險的注意。

Beyond the fence, a flock of birds crossed the valley under a dark cloud. Noah offered a juicy orange from his pack, but Shan watched the sky. Lightning flashed near the antenna before the warning lamp turned red. A small delay was enough to matter to anyone standing on metal. Vale said the delay was a mental fear made larger by stories. Shan showed him the measured difference between the electrical surge and the alarm. The staff moved indoors, then used a microwave to heat their lunch while the inspection team checked the circuit remotely. Nobody needed to stand in the storm to prove that the lamp responded too late.

圍欄外，一群鳥從黑雲下飛過山谷。諾亞從背包取出多汁的橘子，珊卻盯著天空。閃電先在天線附近出現，警告燈過了一下才轉紅。對站在金屬上的人而言，短短延遲就很重要。維爾說這只是被故事放大的心理恐懼。珊把電流突波與燈號間的測量差異拿給他看。員工移入室內，午餐用微波爐加熱，檢查隊則遠端檢視電路。沒有人需要站在暴風雨裡，才能證明警報來得太晚。

The wind had stripped a tree bare, leaving its naked branches above an abandoned bird's nest. Meltwater carried dirt toward the ditch, where it could pollute the upper tank. A shovel stood beside a sign about the protection of drinking water. Ivo called the damaged ditch a sin against nature, then laughed at his own dramatic language. Shan asked him to photograph the route instead. In the kitchen, the cook separated an egg yolk for bread and said the water had tasted strange that morning. Shan placed the kitchen sample in a sealed container for testing. She would not assume a bad taste proved chemical pollution, but it deserved attention.

風把樹葉吹光，只留下光禿枝條和一個廢棄鳥巢。融雪夾著泥土流向水溝，可能污染上方水槽。一把鏟子靠在飲水保護告示旁。伊沃把壞掉的水溝稱為「對大自然的罪」，接著笑自己太誇張。珊請他改拍實際路徑。廚師在廚房分離蛋黃做麵包，說早上水喝起來怪怪的。珊把廚房水樣裝入封好的容器送檢。她不會只因味道不好就斷定有化學污染，但仍值得查明。

Sam's engineers confirmed that the red lamp depended on a sensor installed beside the antenna, not on the safe side of the building. Under strong wind the sensor sometimes lost its signal before the alarm reached the hall. The oldest rule was therefore sound advice, but its warning could arrive too late. Shan recommended closing the metal terrace whenever a storm warning was in force, without waiting for red light. Vale asked if she could prove the risk on a sunny day. She said the existing readings were enough to justify a temporary closure, while a full repair would need a documented inspection.

山姆的工程師確認，紅燈仰賴裝在天線旁的感測器，不是建築安全側的裝置。強風時感測器可能先失去訊號，警報才抵達大廳。因此最舊的規則本身有道理，但警告可能來得太遲。珊建議只要暴風警報生效，金屬平台就先關閉，不必等紅燈。維爾問能否在晴天證明風險。她說現有讀數足以支持暫時封閉，而完整修復仍需要有紀錄的檢查。

The three-note message returned at noon. Noah noticed that its final tone varied slightly in length, unlike the old training recording. Shan replied on the protected channel with the station's standard question: “State your location if you can hear us.” There was no voice, only two taps and a long pause. The reply did not prove that Mei sent it, but it suggested someone was controlling the sound. Shan informed the rescue coordinator and asked for a directional test before night. Her duty was not to create an exciting chase. It was to turn a possible human contact into a safe, repeatable method for finding its source.

中午三音訊息再次出現。諾亞發現最後一個音的長度略有變化，和舊訓練錄音不同。珊在受保護的頻道依規定回問：「若聽得見，請報位置。」沒有聲音，只有兩次敲擊和長時間停頓。這不代表一定是梅發的，卻暗示有人在控制聲音。珊通知救援協調員，要求夜前進行方向測試。她的任務不是製造刺激的追逐，而是把可能的人員聯繫變成安全、可重複的定位方法。

The volunteers spent the afternoon replacing the damaged fence on the safe side of the station. They did not touch the exposed metal frame. Shan checked the work permit, praised the team for stopping when wind increased, and made sure the repair was not mistaken for permission to reopen the terrace. The day's result was small but important: the first rule protected people from lightning, yet the system that displayed it needed urgent changes. She wrote the difference in plain language for everyone who might work at the station after her departure. A rule without a reliable way to apply it was not enough.

志工下午在氣象站安全的一側修理受損圍欄，沒有碰暴露的金屬車架。珊核對工作許可，稱讚團隊在風變大時主動停工，並確認修柵欄不等於允許重開金屬平台。當天的結果雖小卻重要：第一條規則能防雷，但呈現警告的系統急需改善。她用淺白文字記下差異，留給自己離開後可能在站內工作的人。只有規則，卻沒有可靠的執行方式，仍然不夠。

Shan reminded the volunteers that a person could drown in a passage that looked shallow from the entrance. The old incident report used that exact word, not because anyone had died, but because the worker had nearly lost the ability to breathe. Their training now required retreat before the water reached the marked depth.

珊提醒志工，從入口看起來很淺的通道也可能使人溺水。舊事故報告正是這樣用詞，並非有人死亡，而是工人差點無法呼吸。現在的訓練要求，水位到達標線之前就必須撤退。
