沒有封條的儀器

The Instrument with No Seal

雲線以上，請聽第三聲 · 第 9 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

用互動版查詞、朗讀與收藏

Shan found a brass plate on a weather recorder that had once been displayed beside a school buffet. Replacing the machine would be costly, but its designer had left a clear repair guide. Noah brought a hammer to loosen the wooden case, then stopped when he saw the inspection seal was missing. “This is hell for a careful mechanic,” he said, trying to sound humorous. Outside, the path was icy and a low mist covered the rail. Shan chose to work indoors. The missing seal did not prove that someone had altered the recorder, but its absence meant they could not assume the readings had remained untouched.

珊在一部氣象記錄器上找到黃銅銘牌。那部機器過去曾在學校自助餐活動旁展出。換新設備成本很高，但設計師留下清楚的維修指南。諾亞帶了槌子來鬆開木箱，看見檢查封條不見，立刻停下。「這對謹慎的技師簡直是地獄，」他試著幽默地說。戶外步道結冰，低霧覆住欄杆。珊選擇在室內作業。封條不見不能證明有人改機器，卻表示他們無法假設讀數從未被動過。

The display shelf held a toy kangaroo, a porcelain cup, and a box of tangerine candy left from school visits. A teacher's note explained that children could skip a dangerous climb by learning to read instruments in a classroom. Beside it was a photograph of the town stadium during a large science fair. Shan looked past the colorful objects to the technical label on the recorder. It mentioned a backup battery that preserved changes when the main power failed. If the battery still worked, a hidden service log might exist. She asked Sam to authorize a read-only copy of the memory before anyone scrubbed or reset the device.

展示架上有袋鼠玩具、瓷杯，以及學校參觀後留下的橘子糖。教師便條說，孩子能在教室學讀儀器，跳過危險的登山。旁邊照片拍到城鎮體育場舉行大型科學展。珊略過那些鮮豔物品，看向記錄器上的技術標籤。上面提到備援電池能在主電源中斷時保存更動。如果電池仍有效，就可能留著隱藏的維修紀錄。她請山姆授權先以唯讀方式複製記憶資料，不讓任何人先清洗或重設裝置。

Someone had tried to threaten an apprentice who asked about the recorder, according to an unsigned note found under the case. Shan could not verify the accusation, but she preserved the note separately. Noah used a small tool to tighten a loose cable without touching the memory unit. When the backup display came alive, it showed several changes to the upper water gauge. One adjustment raised the safe limit on a day when snow had started melting. Shan checked the system time against the mechanical clock and recorded both values. The change was real; the reason for it remained an open question.

箱底一張未署名便條稱，有人曾威脅詢問記錄器的學徒。珊無法核實指控，仍把便條分開保存。諾亞用小工具鎖緊鬆動電纜，沒有碰記憶模組。備用顯示器亮起時，畫面列出數次上方水位計的調整。其中一項在融雪開始那天提高了安全上限。珊把系統時間與機械鐘核對，同時記下兩個數值。更動確實發生過；更動原因仍未確定。

Sam reviewed the copied record by secure connection. He noticed that the changed limit had never appeared in the published safety procedure. Vale claimed a supervisor had approved it by phone. Sam asked for the name and the call record. Vale repeated that the change was necessary for a planned demonstration. Shan suggested that they stop the demonstration until independent engineers could test the gate. She did not accuse Vale in front of the volunteers. She wanted a decision grounded in an identified risk, not in fear of an unpopular director. The station's temporary closure was ordered before the evening weather bulletin.

山姆透過安全連線檢視資料副本。他注意到被提高的上限從未出現在公開安全程序裡。維爾說主管曾電話批准，山姆便索取姓名與通話紀錄。維爾重申，更動是為了預定的示範活動。珊建議在獨立工程師測試閘門前，先停止示範。她沒有在志工面前指控維爾，只想讓決定依據已確認的風險，而不是對不受歡迎站長的恐懼。晚間氣象公告前，氣象站已被命令暫時停止對外開放。

Noah and Shan compared the new log with the old chart from the visitor room. The upper gauge once triggered a warning at a lower water level. After the adjustment, the alarm could remain silent while a dangerous amount of water entered the lower system. That discovery made Mei's note urgent, but did not locate her. Shan sent the rescue office a map of the possible lower compartments, the approximate water height, and the safe access routes that had been checked. She marked everything else uncertain. By treating the missing seal as a question rather than a final answer, they had found evidence strong enough to change the station's operating plan.

諾亞和珊把新紀錄與訪客室的舊圖比較。上方水位計過去在較低水位就會觸發警告。調整後，即使危險水量進入下方系統，警報也可能保持沉默。這項發現讓梅的便條顯得迫切，卻還無法定位她。珊把可能的下層隔間圖、估計水高，以及已確認安全的進出路線交給救援辦公室；其他部分全標成不確定。因為先把缺失封條視為問題而非結論，他們才找到足以改變氣象站運作計畫的證據。

Late that night, the radio carried three authentic tones followed by seven taps. The team did not reply over the public speaker. Shan sent a short question on the protected channel: “Are you in a dry room?” Three taps answered yes, as the emergency codebook defined. Then came a pause and a second set of taps that the codebook did not explain. She recorded the raw sound rather than translating it into words she wished to hear. An unknown person might still be trapped below the station. But now they had a reason to prepare a controlled operation, not an invitation to run toward the first voice in the dark.

深夜，無線電傳來三個正確呼號，接著敲了七下。團隊沒有透過公開喇叭回答。珊在受保護頻道送出簡短問題：「你在乾燥房間裡嗎？」依緊急代碼本，三下代表肯定。接著是一段停頓，還有另一組代碼本沒有解釋的敲擊。珊保存原始聲音，不把它翻譯成自己想聽的話。可能仍有人受困在氣象站下方，但現在他們有理由準備受控行動，而不是受邀在黑暗中奔向第一個聲音。

Noah wanted to scrub the dirt off the recorder's case. Shan stopped him until its surface had been photographed. Dirt might cover a useful mark, and cleaning could erase it. After the evidence was copied, the engineer cleaned only the area necessary for safe access, documenting both the original condition and the work performed.

諾亞想刷洗記錄器外殼上的泥土。珊請他先拍照，因為污泥下可能藏著有用記號，清潔反而會抹掉。證據存檔後，工程師只清潔安全操作所需範圍，並同時記下原狀與處理內容。
