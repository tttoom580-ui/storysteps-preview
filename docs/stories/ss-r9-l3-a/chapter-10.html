下方傳來的七聲敲擊

Seven Taps from Below

雲線以上，請聽第三聲 · 第 10 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

A banker from the valley arrived with papers about the station's proposed tourist project. The map showed a scenic bay hundreds of kilometers away as an example of successful development. Shan was more interested in the beam drawn across the lower service entrance. The project team had hired a former skiing champion as its public face and promised creative lessons for local children. But the new plans would decrease space for maintenance, while the old gate needed more, not less. The banker had no authority over safety, yet his papers explained why some officials were eager to reopen the station before the investigation ended.

山谷裡一名銀行員帶著氣象站觀光計畫文件到訪。圖上以數百公里外的景色優美海灣作為成功開發例子。珊更在意下層服務入口上方畫出的橫樑。計畫團隊聘了前滑雪冠軍擔任宣傳人物，承諾給地方孩子創意課程；但新計畫會減少維修空間，而舊閘門需要的其實更多。銀行員無權決定安全事項，文件卻解釋了為何一些官員急著在調查結束前重開氣象站。

The night signal produced an echo in the old stairwell, which made its direction fearful to judge by ear. A historic photograph showed a metal receiver holder near the lower passage. That part had later been removed. A newspaper claimed a mob had gathered at the mountain after an earlier accident, but station records described a normal public meeting, not violence. Shan preferred the documents with dates and named sources. A brochure compared the tower to a palace in the clouds and advertised entry for a single penny in its first year. None of that grand language could change the need for a safe route into the building.

夜間訊號在舊樓梯間產生回聲，單靠耳朵判斷方向令人不安。歷史照片顯示下方通道旁曾有金屬接收器支架，後來被拆除。一篇報導聲稱舊事故後有暴民聚集山上，但站內紀錄把它描述為普通公開會議，而非暴力事件。珊更相信有日期與具名來源的文件。一本手冊把塔樓比成雲中宮殿，宣傳開幕第一年只收一便士入場費。那些華麗說法都無法改變入內路線必須安全的事實。

Sam asked the station to postpone the tourist opening until the gate test was finished. A technician replaced the damaged radio receiver without touching the backup memory. During the work, he noticed that someone had tried to steal a small access key, leaving a broken zipper from a tool bag beside the lock. Shan documented the broken part but did not decide who owned it. A nearby holder contained a numbered spare key, which allowed authorized staff to open the outer control box. The operation plan now had a legal entry point, trained personnel, and a measured stopping condition. A rescue attempt was finally possible without guessing at the pressure behind the door.

山姆要求在閘門測試完成前延後觀光開放。技師更換損壞的無線電接收器，沒有碰備份記憶體。工作時，他發現有人似乎試圖偷取小型門鑰匙，鎖旁還留著工具袋破掉的拉鍊。珊記錄了破損物，沒有判定它屬於誰。附近鑰匙架上有編號備匙，讓獲准人員能打開外部控制箱。行動計畫現在具備合法入口、受訓人員與可測量的停止條件。終於可以在不猜測門後壓力的情況下準備救援。

The first test began just after noon. Two engineers remained outside the old channel and opened a small relief valve a measured distance. Water level changed slowly, and the pressure meter stayed within the agreed range. When the reading became uncertain, Sam ordered a pause. Shan watched the upper gauge from a safe room and called out the values. No one entered the passage. After a second controlled adjustment, the lower chamber's moisture level began to fall. The team had not reached Mei, but they had shown that the outside control worked. That was the first practical success large enough to change the rescue plan.

第一次測試在中午過後開始。兩名工程師留在舊水道外側，把減壓閥開到量定的距離。水位慢慢變化，壓力表維持在議定範圍。一旦讀數不確定，山姆便下令暫停。珊在安全室內觀察上方水位計，報出每個數值。沒有人進入通道。第二次受控調整後，下層隔間的濕度開始下降。團隊還沒找到梅，卻證明外部控制可用。這是第一項足以改變救援計畫的實際成果。

While they waited, a stronger three-note message came over the radio. Shan asked the sender to use the emergency code for the nearest numbered gate. Seven taps answered, then three for a dry area. Sam pointed to Gate Seven on the original drawing: it opened into a storage chamber above the deepest water. The rescue team could now inspect a specific location after further safety checks. Vale seemed relieved, but he also asked whether the tourist opening could resume immediately. Shan said no. A person might be alive; the equipment still required testing; and the reason the alarms had failed remained unresolved.

等待期間，無線電傳來更強的三音訊息。珊要求發訊者用緊急代碼說出最近的編號閘門。對方敲了七下，再敲三下表示乾燥區域。山姆指出原圖上的七號閘門：它通往高於最深積水處的儲物室。救援隊終於能在進一步安全檢查後，查看一個明確地點。維爾看來鬆了口氣，卻立刻問觀光開放是否能恢復。珊說不行。有人可能還活著、設備仍須測試，而且警報失效的原因尚未釐清。

At the end of the first ten days, Shan gathered the records into three groups: verified machine readings, witness accounts with clear limits, and unanswered questions. She marked the official map as incomplete, the voice call as a training recording, and the three-note signal as possible live contact. Noah added the outside gate test and drew a safer route toward Gate Seven. They sent copies to the rescue office before changing anything else. Shan realized how far they had moved from the station's fearful opening night. The mystery was still urgent, but its pieces could now be checked by other people. That made the next decision less lonely and more responsible.

第十天結束時，珊把資料分成三組：已驗證的機器讀數、有限制的證人敘述，以及尚未回答的問題。她標示官方地圖不完整、人聲呼叫屬於訓練錄音，而三音訊號可能是即時聯繫。諾亞加入外部閘門測試結果，畫出通往七號閘門較安全的路線。他們在進一步更動前先把副本交給救援辦公室。珊發現，大家已從氣象站那個令人恐懼的第一晚走了很遠。謎團仍迫切，但各項線索如今能由其他人重新核對。這讓下一個決定不再那麼孤單，也更負責任。

A small leak in the upper pipe was measured and added to the water balance rather than ignored. Sam also found a scholarship report from a local engineering school that described the station's old valves. The student drawings were not official plans, but they helped identify questions for the licensed team to check against the original measurements.

團隊量測上方管線的小漏水，納入水量平衡，而不是忽略。山姆還找到地方工程學校的獎學金研究報告，描述本站舊閥門。學生圖不是官方工程圖，卻協助合格團隊提出可用原始數值查證的問題。
