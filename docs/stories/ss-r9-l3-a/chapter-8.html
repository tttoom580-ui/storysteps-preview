書房裡的聲音

A Voice in the Book Room

雲線以上，請聽第三聲 · 第 8 / 25 章 · 2000–3000 · 英中對照 · 校訂中

An ache spread through Shan's shoulders after another night of poor sleep. She entered the old book room and found a complaint about the radio system tucked inside a bookcase. The writer had been conscious of the difference between recorded and live messages long before Mei vanished. A stamp on the document offered a discount for a family education day; a grasshopper drawing decorated its corner. Those details helped Shan locate the complaint in the visitor program's history. She informed Noah and asked him not to move the original. Even a small piece of paper could lose its meaning if removed from the place where it was found.

又一夜沒睡好，珊的肩膀隱隱痠痛。她走進舊書房，在書櫃裡發現一份對無線電系統的申訴。寫信者早在梅失蹤前就注意到錄音與即時訊息的差別。文件上的印章提供家庭教育日折扣，角落還畫著蚱蜢。這些細節幫助珊在訪客活動歷史裡找到申訴位置。她通知諾亞，請他先別移動原件。即使只是一張紙，一旦脫離發現時的位置，也可能失去重要意義。

Ivo made a hasty guess that a monster had lived behind the walls and learned to use the speakers. Shan suggested they organize a test instead. A portrait of the first station director stood beside a poster about leisure activities. When they lifted the poster, they found an access panel used to release a small service ladder. An old rocket model had blocked the panel during a school exhibition; a paper swan had become trapped behind it. None of the objects explained the voice alone. Together they showed that the book room connected to a narrow sound duct leading toward the maintenance passage.

伊沃匆忙猜測牆後住著怪物，還學會用喇叭說話。珊建議先安排測試。第一任站長的肖像旁掛著休閒活動海報。他們掀起海報，發現可放下小型維修梯的檢修板。過去學校展覽的一枚火箭模型曾擋住板子，後面還卡著紙天鵝。沒有一件物品能單獨解釋人聲，但合在一起顯示書房與通往維修走廊的狹窄傳聲管相連。

The panel was held by a tight screw with a unique star-shaped head. The maintenance list mentioned violence against equipment after a public protest years ago, when a visitor had tried to break into the service duct. Shan did not use that report to judge the visitor's character. She used it to understand why the cover had been reinforced. Sam's engineer gave permission to inspect the duct through a safe opening. When Noah played a test sound in the book room, Shan heard it clearly by the lower stairwell. The result showed that a voice could travel through walls without anyone standing in the passage.

檢修板由一顆鎖得很緊、頭部呈獨特星形的螺絲固定。維修清單記載，多年前一次公開抗議後有人對設備施暴，試圖闖入傳聲管。珊沒有依此判斷當事人的品格，而是用紀錄理解護蓋為何加固。山姆的工程師允許他們從安全開口檢查。諾亞在書房播放測試聲時，珊在下方樓梯口清楚聽見。結果顯示，聲音能穿過牆壁，而不必有人站在通道裡。

They reviewed the recorded calls frame by frame on a computer. Each contained the same cough, but one had additional clicks after the sentence. Shan compared the clicks with the ventilation motor and found they occurred when a loose cover vibrated. A live person might have added the clicks, yet the simplest explanation was a scheduled file playing through a damaged duct. She marked the calls as recordings until stronger evidence appeared. Then Noah asked an uncomfortable question: if the fake voice was so easy to produce, could someone be using it to draw searchers away from the genuine three-note messages?

兩人在電腦上逐段檢查錄下的呼叫。每段都有同一聲輕咳，但其中一段在句末多了喀噠聲。珊把聲音與通風馬達比對，發現那是鬆動護蓋震動時產生的。一個真人也可能製造喀噠聲，但最簡單的解釋是排程音檔經損壞的傳聲管播放。她暫把那些呼叫標成錄音，等待更強證據。諾亞接著問了個令人不安的問題：如果假聲音這麼容易製造，是否有人故意用它把搜尋者引離真正的三音訊號？

Shan requested the sound system's access history. The list showed that Vale's account had scheduled the file two days after Mei disappeared. Vale said a contractor had used his password to test equipment. Shan asked for the contractor's name and service order. Neither was available that day. The missing paperwork raised a question, not a verdict. She kept the access logs and the director's explanation side by side, with a note that the account might not identify the person at the keyboard. The investigation had moved beyond ghosts and into ordinary responsibility: who could change the system, who reviewed those changes, and who knew the consequences?

珊要求查閱音響系統的登入紀錄。紀錄顯示，梅失蹤兩天後，維爾的帳號排定了該音檔。維爾說承包商借用他的密碼測試設備。珊索取承包商姓名及工作單，當天都拿不到。缺文件產生的是問題，不是判決。她把登入紀錄與站長的解釋並排保存，註明帳號不一定能證明實際操作鍵盤者。調查已離開鬼魂，進入一般責任問題：誰能改系統、誰負責審查，以及誰知道後果？

Before dinner, the three-note signal appeared again. This time it included the number seven tapped slowly against a hard surface. Shan placed a small receiver beside a closed window and noted a stronger signal on the west side of the room. She did not send anyone out at dusk. Instead, she drew a circle on the map indicating a possible source area, with a wide border to show uncertainty. The number seven might refer to a valve, a room, or a count. She resisted choosing an answer that fitted her favorite theory. Morning measurements would help narrow the possibilities without risking another disappearance.

晚餐前，三音訊號又出現了。這次還有在硬物上慢慢敲出的數字七。珊把小型接收器放在關閉的窗邊，記下西側訊號較強。她沒有黃昏時派人外出，而是在地圖上畫出一個可能來源區，用寬邊界表示不確定。數字七可能是閥門、房間，也可能是計數。她克制自己不挑選最符合偏愛理論的答案。隔天早上的測量會幫助縮小範圍，不必再冒另一人失蹤的風險。

The office promised to inform everyone assigned to the lower building before any further radio testing. Shan insisted on a written notice because a spoken warning could reach only the people present. She also required the test recording to be labeled clearly, so no family would hear it later and mistake it for a new call for help.

辦公室答應在進一步無線電測試前通知所有負責下層建築的人。珊堅持使用書面通知，因為口頭警告只能傳給在場者。她也要求清楚標示測試錄音，免得日後家屬聽到時誤認成新的求救。
